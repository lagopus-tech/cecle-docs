## III. Operation Process

1. **Artwork Import & Setup**: Import or design patterns in the supporting laser control software, setting engraving/cutting speeds, laser power, and layer parameters.
2. **Material Placement & Focusing**: Open the top cover, place non-metal materials like leather, wood, or acrylic steadily on the working platform, and set the appropriate focal distance using the 0–300mm lifting range.
3. **Auxiliary System Check**: Confirm that the pure circulating water cooling system, exhaust smoke extraction unit, and air pump are operating normally.
4. **Start Processing**: Close the enclosed top cover, wear protective equipment, and execute the laser cutting and engraving task via the control panel or software with a single click.
5. **Part Unloading**: Once processing is complete, wait a moment for smoke evacuation, then open the cabinet top cover to retrieve the finished product.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**
* **Q: Can the LCM-6040 be used to cut metal?**
  * A: No. This machine utilizes CO2 laser technology, which is specifically designed for non-metal materials such as leather, wood, acrylic, and paper, and cannot directly cut bare metal.
* **Q: Does the enclosed cabinet require an external exhaust duct?**
  * A: Yes. Because CO2 laser processing of non-metals (especially leather and acrylic) generates smoke and odors, the cabinet is designed with an exhaust port. We recommend extending the exhaust pipe outdoors or connecting it to an air purification filtration system.
* **Q: Does the machine support 110V voltage? Can overseas customers use it directly?**
  * A: Yes. We offer configuration options meeting multi-country voltage requirements (including AC220V 50Hz and AC110V 60Hz), allowing overseas customers to plug and play directly without an extra transformer.
