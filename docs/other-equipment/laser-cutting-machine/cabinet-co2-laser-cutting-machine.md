## III. Operation Process

1. **Artwork Preparation & Import**: Create or import your vector/raster designs (fully compatible with CorelDraw formats) into the laser control software. Assign processing speeds and power levels by layer.
2. **Material Loading & Focal Adjustment**: Open the protective cabinet lid, place the paper, plastic, or non-metal sheet onto the cellular platform, and adjust the Z-axis lifting range (0–80mm) to establish proper focal distance.
3. **Auxiliary Systems Startup**: Turn on the water cooling unit, air compressor, and exhaust ventilation system to ensure proper cooling and smoke evacuation.
4. **Execution & Unloading**: Close the safety enclosure, run the job via the USB2.0 control interface, and wait for completion before opening the lid to retrieve finished components.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**
* **Q: Can this machine cut through metal sheets?**
  * A: No. This is a CO2 laser machine specifically engineered for non-metal materials such as paper, plastic, acrylic, wood, and leather. It cannot cut bare metal.
* **Q: How does the enclosed cabinet design protect the operator?**
  * A: The full-cabinet structure with its protective cover shields operators from direct laser exposure and contains smoke and debris, which are safely vented out through the exhaust system.
* **Q: What power should I choose for cutting thick acrylic or wood?**
  * A: For heavier cutting tasks up to 20mm, we recommend selecting higher wattage options such as 80W or 100W on the LCM-9060 or LCM-1080 models.
