## 4. Operating Procedures
1. **Special Hopper Filling:** Pour the special beads (like semi-circles or stars) into the customized heavy-duty vibratory bowl, and pour the matching four-prong nails into the lower nail slot.
2. **Pressure & Pneumatic Debugging:** Turn on the power and air supply, test according to sample thickness. Fine-tune the cylinder stroke and locking force parameters on the digital display to prevent edge chipping on the beads.
3. **Product Positioning:** Place the fabric into the lower mold area equipped with a physical thimble or infrared laser pointer.
4. **Stepping to Form:** Step on the lower cylinder working pedal. The pneumatic transmission will stably bite the special bead onto the fabric via the four-prong nail from the bottom, completing the process.
