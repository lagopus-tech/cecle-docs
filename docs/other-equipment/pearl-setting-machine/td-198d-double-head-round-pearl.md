## 4. Operating Procedures
1. **Material Loading & Startup:** Pour different diameters of round pearls into the left and right plastic tanks respectively, pour four-prong nails into the lower nail track, and turn on the main power and dual-side air valves.
2. **Parameter Setting:** On the control panel, select "Double-head mode" or "Single-head mode" as needed, and adjust the cylinder pressing force.
3. **Fabric Positioning:** Lay the fabric flat on the corresponding side's lower cylinder mold positioning pin, assisted by the infrared positioning light for focus.
4. **Cyclic Nailing:** Step on the foot switch to let the machine quickly drive the four-prong nail to fix the first pearl; to attach the second type, step on the other pedal to complete a one-stop mixed pearl setting.

### FAQ
**Q1: Can I use the same size pearls on both sides?**
A1: Yes. If your order only requires a single specification of round pearls, you can load the same size on both sides and switch to "single-side operation," or use them alternately to share the cylinder's workload and fatigue.

**Q2: Does the machine have requirements for the pearl material?**
A2: The machine is suitable for the most mainstream plastic and ABS resin eco-friendly pearls (4-hole or no-hole). Glass or natural fragile pearls should be tested carefully, as claw extrusion may cause them to break.

**Q3: Can I use the double-head machine to attach special-shaped beads?**
A3: The TD-198D's vibration plate and feeding chute are optimized for the center of gravity of "round pearls." If you want to attach irregular beads like semi-circles or squares, we recommend our dedicated double-head model for special beads, the **TD-168D**.
