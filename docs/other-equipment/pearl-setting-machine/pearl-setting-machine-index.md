# Automatic Nail Pearl Fixing Beading Machine Selection Guide & Product Overview

## I. What is an Automatic Nail Pearl Fixing Beading Machine?
### 1.1 Core Definition
The Automatic Nail Pearl Fixing Beading Machine is specialized automated pneumatic processing equipment widely used in the apparel, footwear, leather goods, and home textile industries. It automatically sorts and feeds beads and four-prong nails via high-frequency vibrating plates. Utilizing the powerful downward pressure generated by pneumatic cylinders, it drives metal four-prong nails to penetrate fabrics from the bottom up, pressing and locking them tightly with round or special beads in the upper mold. This equipment largely replaces traditional manual bead sewing, offering high efficiency, durability, aesthetic appeal, and excellent fabric protection.

### 1.2 Working Principle
The entire machine operates on the synergy of two core systems: **"Pneumatic Pressing + Automatic Feeding"**:
1. **Automatic Feeding:** The operator pours the beads into the upper storage tank/hopper and the four-prong nails into the bottom track. The machine sorts and transports the beads and nails to the working station automatically via frequency-converting vibrating plates.
2. **Infrared Positioning & Pneumatic Pressing:** The operator uses the infrared marker to align the target position on the fabric with the mold head. Upon pressing the foot pedal, the lower cylinder pushes the four-prong nail upward through the fabric, while the upper cylinder (or upper mold) provides a damping pressing force. This bends the nail prongs, making them firmly grip the bead (a single operation takes less than 0.5 seconds).

### 1.3 Core Value
- **Extreme Efficiency:** Fully automatic feeding system, with a single-head beading speed of up to 150-180 pieces/minute, significantly saving labor costs.
- **High Penetration & Fabric Protection:** Industrial-grade pneumatic double cylinders provide smooth and even pressure, easily penetrating multi-layer thick denim and genuine leather while effectively protecting delicate fabrics and fragile beads.
- **Low Operation Threshold:** Equipped with a digital display or PLC smart touch screen, making it easy for beginners to learn. The separated dual/multi-foot pedal control ensures a high safety coefficient.
- **High Precision:** Infrared positioning assists in focusing, eliminating the skewing and misalignment problems common in manual sewing.

### 1.4 Applicable Industries
- **Apparel Manufacturing:** Custom wedding dresses, streetwear denim, heavily embroidered women's wear, and children's clothing.
- **Footwear & Leather Goods:** High-end leather shoe upper embellishments, luxury bags, and motorcycle leather goods processing.
- **Other Peripherals:** Home textile manufacturers, lace factories, and handicraft manufacturing.

## III. Core Selection Advice

### 3.1 By Bead Shape (Top Priority Evaluation)
1. **Pure Standard Round Pearls:** Choose **TD-198** (Single Head) or **TD-198D** (Double Head). They feature centered material-sorting optimization specifically for spherical pearls, offering the fastest speed.
2. **Includes Asymmetrical/Special Shapes (Half-round, square, pentagram, pointed studs):** You must choose **TD-168** (Single Head) or **TD-168D** (Double Head). These models are equipped with dedicated heavy-duty vibrating plates, anti-jamming alloy tracks, and damping protection modules to resolve the pain points of jamming or crushing common in standard machines.

### 3.2 By Process Complexity & Capacity Requirements
- **Single Specification Continuous Work:** For limited budgets or orders primarily using a single size/simple pattern, the **Single Head Series (198/168)** is sufficient.
- **High-Frequency Multi-Spec Alternating / Heavy Splicing:** If the garment frequently requires alternating layouts like "Large + Small Bead" or "Round + Star Bead," the **Double Head Series (198D/168D)** is highly recommended. It not only eliminates the time spent on frequently disassembling and replacing molds/tracks but also reduces labor time by over 50%, significantly boosting delivery efficiency for high-profit orders.

### 3.3 By Fabric Thickness & Material
- The entire series comes standard with a **500W (0.67HP)** power drive and heavy-duty cylinders. Whether working on single-layer sheer chiffon, multi-layer thick denim, uneven hard leather, or canvas, they maintain excellent penetration and stability (significantly outperforming simple electric/manual machines on the market).

## IV. General Technical FAQ

### 4.1 Power & Air Supply
**Q: What voltage does the machine require? Do I need an air compressor?**
A: All models support 220V or 110V (50~60Hz) wide voltage, allowing direct use with standard household or industrial electricity. **You must provide your own air compressor**, as the machine's downward pressure is powered by high-pressure pneumatic cylinders. It requires an external stable air source with a working pressure of 0.5~0.6MPa (a small silent air compressor is recommended).

### 4.2 Mold Replacement & Consumables
**Q: Is changing the mold complicated? Does it require a specialized technician?**
A: Replacement is very simple. Just loosen the fixing screws, and you can change the upper and lower molds for different sizes (4-12mm) in 1-2 minutes. A single operator can easily handle the adjustment.
**Q: Are there requirements for bead materials and four-prong nails?**
A: We recommend using plastic or ABS resin eco-friendly beads. For rhinestones, alloy edges, or fragile natural/glass pearls, the special bead machines (TD-168 series) can prevent crushing by precisely setting the pressing damping and time. Four-prong nails must match the width of the bottom track. If penetrating extremely thick materials requires lengthened nails, please confirm the compatibility of the track's spring clip in advance.

### 4.3 Troubleshooting (Jamming Issues)
**Q: Will the machine frequently experience bead jamming or feeding failures?**
A: As long as standard-sized beads without obvious burrs are used, the round pearl machines (198 series) rarely jam. For special beads, the TD-168 series is equipped with an anti-reverse screener that automatically identifies tilted or incorrectly oriented special beads and pushes them back, effectively ensuring a high continuous operation rate.

## VI. Standard Operating Procedures (Using Double Head Model as an Example)

1. **Loading & Power/Air Connection:**
   - Pour the different sizes/shapes of beads into the left and right large-capacity upper storage tanks (or heavy-duty vibratory hoppers) respectively.
   - Pour the four-prong nails into the corresponding nail tracks below.
   - Plug into the power supply, turn on the air compressor, and open the machine's main switch and dual-side air valves.
2. **Parameter Setting & Testing:**
   - On the PLC control panel (or digital display), fine-tune the downward stroke, pressing time, and damping pressure of the left and right cylinders according to the fabric thickness to prevent crushing fragile beads. Select "Single Head" or "Double Head" working mode.
3. **Infrared Assisted Positioning:**
   - Lay the garment or shoe/bag flat between the upper and lower molds. Use the corresponding infrared cross/dot marker to precisely align the location on the fabric where the bead will be fixed.
4. **Pedal Pneumatic Pressing:**
   - Step on the pearl positioning pedal (on certain models), and the upper track will deliver the bead and nail to the workstation.
   - Step on the working foot pedal on the corresponding side. The downward cylinder instantly activates, driving the four-prong nail up through the fabric and securely locking the bead.
   - To alternate the pattern, simply step on the other side's pedal to complete the fixing of the second type of bead, repeating the cycle as needed.
