1. **Dual-Station Independent Special Shape Feeding:** Features two fully independent high-frequency variable-frequency vibrating feeding mechanisms and dual cylinders. ## 4. Operating Procedures
1. **Dual-Hopper Pre-loading:** According to the sample requirements, pour two types of special beads (e.g., stars and square studs) into the left and right custom large-capacity hoppers respectively, and fill the lower track with four-prong rivets (nails).
2. **Channel Parameter Debugging:** Set the air pressure and stroke parameters for both nailing heads via the PLC display screen.
3. **Infrared Positioning:** Use the corresponding infrared laser pointer on each side to position the leather, denim, or wool garment on the lower punch.
4. **Alternate Pedal Nailing:** Step on the different foot pedals to drive the left or right nailing head to press down, allowing the high-load four-prong nails to penetrate from the bottom up, securely fixing the different styles of beads.

### FAQ
**Q1: Will this double-head machine easily crush special beads with rhinestones or alloy edges?**
A1: No. The TD-168D is upgraded with precise limit valves and damping protection. Users can accurately set the "pressing time" and "pressing force" on the touch screen, ensuring that the four-prong nails bend securely without damaging the upper rhinestones and alloy edges.

**Q2: Can I use four-prong nails of different lengths?**
A2: The feeding track at the bottom corresponds to specific nail sizes. If you need longer nails for very thick materials, compatibility with the lower positioning hole and spring slider must be confirmed in advance. Contact us when purchasing to provide the corresponding track.

**Q3: Can I use it to alternately set "one normal round pearl + one semi-circular special bead"?**
A3: Absolutely. Just change one of the mold channels to a dedicated round pearl track and mold, and you can achieve a high-end splicing style of round pearls on one side and special beads on the other.
