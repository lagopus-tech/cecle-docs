## 4. Operating Procedures
1. **Loading & Machine Prep:** Pour the corresponding round pearls into the upper Plastic Pearl Tank, pour the four-prong nails into the vibratory hopper, plug in the power, and turn on the main switch.
2. **Fabric Positioning:** Use the infrared positioning device to align the exact spot on the clothing or bag, laying it flat between the upper and lower molds.
3. **Pedal Positioning:** Step on the pearl positioning pedal; the upper guide rail will feed a pearl and a nail to the working station.
4. **Pneumatic Nailing:** Step on the pressing pedal; the cylinder automatically drives the four-prong nail from the bottom up through the fabric, securely locking the round pearl to complete one cycle.

### FAQ
**Q1: Do I need to provide my own air compressor for this machine?**
A1: Yes. Because the machine relies on a high-pressure cylinder to provide the pressing power, it requires an external stable air source with a working pressure of 0.5~0.6MPa. We recommend customers equip a small silent air compressor.

**Q2: Is it easy to change molds? Can one person operate it independently?**
A2: Changing molds is very simple. Just loosen the fixing screws, and you can finish replacing the upper and lower molds in 1-2 minutes. A single person can independently debug it.

**Q3: Do round pearls jam often on the track?**
A3: The machine is equipped with a variable-frequency vibratory bowl sorting mechanism specifically designed to sort spherical pearls. As long as you use standard pearls without obvious burrs, jamming rarely occurs.
