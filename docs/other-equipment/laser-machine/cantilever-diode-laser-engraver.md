## III. Operation Process

1. **Unboxing & Placement**:
   * Place the compact cantilever base firmly on a flat, stable tabletop.
2. **Power & Hardware Connection**:
   * Plug in the dedicated DC power supply and connect the controller board to a Windows PC via the included USB cable.
3. **Graphic Import & Mode Setup**:
   * Launch the software (supports English, Chinese, and Japanese), import vector line art or bitmap images, select the engraving mode (Black & White, Discrete, or Grayscale), and set processing speed and laser power percentage.
4. **Material Placement & Manual Focus Adjustment**:
   * Slide the workpiece under the cantilever beam from the open side. Rotate the laser module's focus adjustment dial until the projected laser spot converges to its smallest pinpoint on the material surface.
5. **Framing & Alignment**:
   * Run the low-power perimeter framing preview to ensure the design is centered and aligned with the physical workpiece.
6. **Eye Protection & Engraving Execution**:
   * Ensure the room is well ventilated. Put on the certified laser safety goggles, then click "Start." The machine will execute the job automatically and return to idle upon completion.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):**

* **Q: Does the single-arm cantilever design vibrate or lose accuracy during engraving?**
  * A: No. The machine combines a rigid linear profile with a low-center-of-gravity motor base. Over the compact 20 × 17 cm working envelope, the 0.01 mm positioning accuracy ensures smooth, jitter-free lines and consistent motion.
* **Q: Is assembly complicated for beginners?**
  * A: Not at all. Because of the cantilever design, there is no need to balance dual Y-axis belts. The machine arrives largely pre-assembled and requires only a few screws and cable connections to start engraving.
* **Q: Can this machine engrave metals or transparent glass/acrylic?**
  * A: It can engrave coated metals, anodized aluminum, and painted surfaces. Bare, raw reflective metals cannot be engraved. Transparent materials (such as clear acrylic or glass) cannot directly absorb 450nm blue laser light; they require a water-soluble black coating or laser marking paper applied to the surface before processing.
* **Q: What safety precautions should be followed during operation?**
  * A: Semiconductor blue laser light is concentrated and hazardous to eyes. **All operators must wear the included laser safety goggles at all times.** In addition, operate the engraver in a well-ventilated space to disperse smoke and fumes.
