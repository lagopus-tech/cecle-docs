## III. Operation Process

1. **Assembly & Mechanical Check**:
   * Assemble the modular aluminum frame, guide rails, and timing belts according to the user manual. Check belt tension and adjust eccentric nuts to ensure smooth carriage movement without binding.
2. **Power & Hardware Connection**:
   * Connect the DC power adapter and plug the controller board into a Windows PC via the included USB data cable.
3. **Artwork Import & Parameter Configuration**:
   * Launch the control software, import vector graphics (SVG, DXF) or bitmap images (JPG, PNG, BMP), choose the desired engraving mode (Black & White, Discrete, or Grayscale), and set travel speed and laser power output.
4. **Positioning & Manual Focal Adjustment**:
   * Position the machine or workpiece, loosen the laser module thumb screws or adjust the focal ring to set the laser focal spot to its smallest point on the material surface.
5. **Frame Preview & Boundary Verification**:
   * Use the "Framing" function in the software to trace the rectangular working perimeter with low-power pilot light, confirming safe clearance and precise engraving location.
6. **Safety Eye Protection & Engraving Execution**:
   * Ensure proper room ventilation. All operators and bystanders must wear the provided laser safety goggles. Click "Start" to initiate engraving; the machine will automatically return to home/idle upon job completion.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):**

* **Q: Does this machine require assembly, and is it complicated?**
  * A: Yes. To ensure shipping safety and reduce transit volume, the machine ships as a flat-pack DIY kit. Complete assembly tools, hardware packs, and step-by-step video/text guides are included. Assembly typically takes 30 to 60 minutes.
* **Q: Is the laser beam safe to operate, and what precautions are needed?**
  * A: The high-energy blue laser can cause irreversible eye damage from direct exposure or diffuse reflections. **All operators must wear the certified laser safety goggles included in the box at all times.** In addition, operate the equipment in a well-ventilated space to evacuate engraving fumes.
* **Q: Can it engrave metals or cut transparent acrylic?**
  * A: It can engrave the surface coating of anodized aluminum, painted metal, and treated stainless steel, but it cannot engrave raw, bare reflective metal. Transparent acrylic cannot absorb 450nm blue light directly; you can coat the surface with washable black tempera paint or laser marking paper before engraving.
* **Q: What should I do if engraving patterns shift or miss steps?**
  * A: Pattern shifting is almost always caused by loose timing belts or loose set screws on the stepper motor pulleys. Tighten the belts to moderate tension, tighten the pulley grub screws, and ensure the V-wheels glide smoothly without wobble.
