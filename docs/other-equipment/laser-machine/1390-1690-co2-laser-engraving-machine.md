   * **Optional Dual-Head Configuration (Double Heads)**: Driven synchronously by an advanced controller, two laser heads cut or engrave two identical items at once, doubling batch output per run.
   * **Scalable Laser Power Range (60W – 150W)**:

     * **100W / 130W / 150W**: High-penetration output designed for cutting through thick acrylic (up to 20mm+) and dense wooden boards with clean edges.

4. **Industrial-Grade Structure & Enclosure (Industrial Design)**
   * **Heavy-Duty Floor-Standing Chassis**: Reinforced steel frame minimizes structural vibration during high-speed vector sweeps (engraving up to 1000mm/s), maintaining position repeatability within ≤ ±0.01 mm.

## III. Operation Process

1. **Artwork Import & Parameter Assignment**: Import vector (DXF/AI/PLT) or raster graphics into the control software. Assign cut speed, laser power, air assist delay, and layer processing sequence by color.
2. **Material Placement & Focal Alignment**: Lay the stock flat across the honeycomb or aluminum knife-blade bed. Set nozzle-to-material clearance using the focus gauge and check job boundaries with the red dot pointer.
3. **Auxiliary Systems Verification**: Turn on the industrial water chiller, air assist compressor, and exhaust blower. Ensure steady cooling water circulation and correct operating temperatures before firing the laser.
4. **Enclosed Execution & Extraction**: Lower the safety cover and start the job from the control panel. After processing, keep the exhaust running briefly to clear residual vapors before unloading.

## V. Sales Suggestions & FAQ

### **Sales Suggestions**
* **Application-Driven Wattage Selection**: Guide clients prioritizing fine typography, craft stamps, or thin textiles toward 60W/80W systems for sharper focal spots. Direct signage fabricators and display builders handling heavy acrylic or thick plywood to 130W/150W options.
* **Up-Selling Double Heads**: Highlight the 50% labor and cycle-time savings for high-volume contract manufacturers to justify the dual-head upgrade.
* **Upfront Material Advisory**: Clarify metalworking limitations early. Point clients looking for metal tagging toward anodized aluminum surfaces or laser-marking spray compounds, positioning fiber machines if bare-metal cutting is needed.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**
* **Q: Can the 1390/1690 CO2 laser cut or engrave bare metal plates?**  
  * A: No. CO2 laser wavelengths are optimized for organic and non-metallic materials (acrylic, wood, paper, leather). It cannot directly mark or cut bare metals, but it works cleanly on anodized aluminum coatings or metals treated with specialized thermal marking sprays.
* **Q: How does the Double Heads configuration operate?**  
  * A: The dual-head configuration runs two synchronized laser heads simultaneously across the bed, splitting the horizontal axis to produce two identical components in the time of one.
* **Q: Does a high-power tube (130W/150W) work well for detailed raster engraving?**  
  * A: High-power tubes have higher firing thresholds and slightly wider beam waists optimized for cutting. For intricate miniature lettering, stamp engraving, or high-DPI photo reproduction, 60W or 80W tubes provide tighter, more consistent focal spots.
