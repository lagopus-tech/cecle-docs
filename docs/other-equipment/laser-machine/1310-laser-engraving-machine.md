## 2. Core Advantages

1. **Thickened All-Metal Aluminum Frame**  
   Constructed with heavy-gauge anodized aluminum profiles and solid alloy side gantries, the chassis maintains rigidity during high-speed cutting. It absorbs tool chatter and vibration, ensuring straight toolpaths, crisp relief edges, and a long industrial lifespan compared to plastic or acrylic DIY kits.
2. **Full-Axis T8 Lead Screw Transmission**  
   All three axes (X, Y, and Z) utilize precision T8 metal lead screws instead of stretchable rubber timing belts. This delivers high torque transfer, rigid downforce, zero belt slippage, and minimal backlash, providing superior repeatable positioning accuracy on dense workpieces.
3. **Genuine 3D Carving & Depth Cutting**  
   Equipped with a dedicated 775 high-speed spindle motor and carbide end mills, the machine executes true Z-axis multi-pass milling. It handles 10 mm solid timber slabs with carved reliefs, recessed lettering, and cutouts that surface-only diode lasers cannot achieve.
4. **2-in-1 Spindle & Laser Versatility**  
   Designed with an adaptable mounting carriage and integrated control board headers. The standard spindle cuts deep profiles, while an optional 500mW–15W laser module swaps in for rapid surface engraving and dark marking without requiring a separate machine.
5. **Broad Material Processing Capability**  
   The rigid metal frame and lead screw drives support milling on wood, acrylic, PVC, PCB circuit boards, and engineering plastics, as well as light-pass precision milling on soft non-ferrous metals like copper and aluminum.
6. **Compact Desktop Footprint with Universal Voltage**  
   Measuring 281 × 281 × 290 mm and weighing 8.0 kg, it operates on workbench surfaces and runs on 110V/220V dual-voltage power supplies.

## 3. Operating Workflow

1. **Workpiece Fixturing**  
   Place the material (wood, acrylic, copper plate, or PCB) on the aluminum slot bed. Clamp it securely using the included hold-down plate clamps to prevent vibration or shift during operation.
2. **Tool or Laser Installation**  
   * **CNC Milling**: Insert the selected carbide router bit into the 775 spindle chuck and tighten the collet nut.
   * **Laser Engraving**: Mount the laser module onto the Z-axis carriage, connect the control cable, and adjust the focal length ring using protective eyewear.
3. **Software Connection & File Import**  
   Connect the controller board to the host PC via USB (compatible with Windows XP, 7, 8, and Linux):
   * CNC Mode: Open **Candle**, connect to the GRBL port, and open the `.nc` / `.tap` G-code file.
   * Laser Mode: Open **LiteFire**, connect the COM port, and load the vector or bitmap file.
4. **Work Coordinate Origin Setup (X/Y/Z Zeroing)**  
   Use the manual jog controls to position the bit tip or laser focus spot at the starting point of the material, then click the zeroing buttons in the software to define the X0, Y0, and Z0 origin.
5. **Feed & Speed Verification and Job Execution**  
   Confirm feed rates, plunge rates, and spindle RPM settings, activate the 775 spindle motor, and start the job.
6. **Job Completion & Maintenance**  
   Wait for the spindle to come to a complete stop, unclamp the finished piece, remove chips and dust with a soft brush or vacuum, and apply light machine oil to the T8 lead screws regularly.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

* **Q: Can the CNC1310 machine engrave metals like aluminum and copper?**  
  * A: Yes. With its all-metal aluminum structure and T8 lead screw transmission, the machine has sufficient structural rigidity to engrave soft non-ferrous metals like brass, copper, and aluminum. For best results, use sharp carbide bits, apply shallow depths of cut (0.1–0.2 mm per pass), and maintain moderate feed rates. It is not intended for hard ferrous metals such as steel, iron, or titanium.
* **Q: Is this unit a CNC router or a laser engraver? How do they work together?**  
  * A: The CNC1310 is primarily a high-precision physical CNC router equipped with a 775 spindle motor for 3D cutting, pocketing, and carving. It also features a dual-purpose mounting setup that accommodates optional 500mW–15W laser modules. Swapping the tool allows users to switch between mechanical 3D cutting and non-contact laser surface marking on a single machine.
* **Q: How steep is the software learning curve, and are tutorials included?**  
  * A: The machine runs on standard GRBL firmware controlled through Candle (for CNC toolpaths) and LiteFire (for laser engraving). Both programs feature direct, beginner-accessible interfaces. Each unit ships with software installation files, USB drivers, user guides, and test G-code files to help new users set up quickly.
