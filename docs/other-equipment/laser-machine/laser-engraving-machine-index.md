# Comprehensive Laser Engraving Machine Buying Guide

When choosing a laser engraving and marking machine, the primary deciding factors are: **target materials (metals vs. non-metals vs. precision jewelry)**, **processing depth/method (surface marking vs. 3D physical milling/deep engraving)**, and **working area & budget**.

Below, all mainstream models are categorized into four major technological series to help you quickly and accurately match your needs:

---

## 1. Core Categories & Selection Matrix

### 1. Fiber Laser Series (1064nm)

* **Core Advantages**: Wavelength perfectly suited for metals, ultra-long 100,000-hour lifespan, zero consumables, maintenance-free, and ultra-fast marking speeds (up to 14,000 mm/s).
* **Applicable Materials**: Almost all **metals** (stainless steel, aluminum, copper, gold, silver, titanium, etc.) and certain hard engineering plastics (ABS, PC, etc.).
* **Representative Models**:
* **PDL Mini Fiber Laser**: Ultra-compact (330×460×580mm), ideal for small studios, jewelry shops, and desktop marking in space-constrained environments.
* **LMJ-110SP Split-Type Fiber Laser**: Separates the main control cabinet from the galvo column for superior heat dissipation, engineered for continuous, high-intensity industrial operation.
* **LMJ-110P Handheld Portable Fiber Laser**: Suitcase-style enclosure with a carry handle and a micro XY leveling stage. Designed to "bring the machine to bulky workpieces" (such as automotive engine blocks, large steel structures, and installed pipelines).

### 2. CO2 Laser Series (10.6µm)

* **Core Advantages**: The king of non-metal processing, supporting large working areas, thick-sheet cutting, and intricate graphic engraving.
* **Applicable Materials**: **Organic non-metals** (wood, acrylic, leather, paper, fabric, rubber, glass, crystal, etc.). *(Note: Cannot directly cut bare metals, though it can mark coated/anodized metals or bare metals treated with laser marking spray).*
* **Representative Models**:
* **1080 / 9060 / 1390 / 1690 Series**: Working areas range from medium (900×600mm, 1000×800mm) to medium-large (1300×900mm, 1600×900mm), supporting 60W to 150W power options. Among them, the **1080 model** excels at high-end micro-fracture frosted engraving on glass and crystal.
* **1325 Extra-Large Format Industrial Machine**: Features an oversized 1300×2500mm open flatbed that directly accommodates standard 4ft × 8ft full sheets (such as full acrylic or plywood boards). Its 150W high-power laser enables single-pass cutting with flame-polished edges.

### 3. Ultraviolet (UV) Cold Laser Series (355nm)

* **Core Advantages**: Utilizes "cold processing" photochemical ablation (ultra-short 355nm wavelength) with a minimal heat-affected zone, ensuring zero charring, burning, or cracking around edges.
* **Applicable Materials**: Heat-sensitive materials, **glass, crystal, ceramics, silicon wafers, sapphire**, and high-end plastics (capable of producing ultra-high-contrast black or white marks on white plastics).
* **Representative Models**:
* **High-Precision Desktop Jewelry Laser Engraver (3W)**: Purpose-built for high-end custom jewelry. With an ultra-fine laser spot (0.01mm line width), it handles inside-ring engraving, micro-engraving on gemstones, and nano-scale anti-counterfeit marking with **zero thermal damage or ablation** to surrounding inlaid gemstones and precious metals.

### 4. Diode Laser & Desktop Micro CNC Series (450nm Blue Light & Mechanical Milling)

* **Core Advantages**: Compact size, lightweight, high cost-effectiveness, and open-frame/unbounded base designs.
* **Applicable Materials**: Balsa/light wood, leather, cardboard, dark acrylic, and coated metals.
* **Representative Models**:
* **DLE-001 / DLE-001B Mini Portable Engraver**: Ultra-compact (15×15×14cm) with smartphone Bluetooth wireless control (001B model), making it an ideal tool for retail counters and on-site personalization at craft fairs.
* **Cantilever (DLE-1720) & Open-Frame (DLE-6550) Models**: Feature a three-sided open cantilever arm or bottomless frame design, allowing the machine to be placed directly onto oversized wooden planks, doors, or entire tabletops for localized engraving.
* **CNC1310 Desktop 3-in-1 Engraver**: Built with an all-metal aluminum frame and T8 precision lead screws, equipped with a 775 milling spindle (and optional 500mW–15W laser modules). Beyond laser marking, it performs true 3D deep physical milling on wood, acrylic, PCBs, and **soft metals like copper and aluminum**.

## 2. Quick Machine Selection Matrix

| Core Needs / Application Scenarios | Recommended Series / Model | Key Matched Advantages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Metal marking / nameplates / knives / electronic components** | PDL Mini / Split-Type / Handheld Fiber Laser | 1064nm wavelength, 100,000-hour lifespan, ultra-fast metal marking |
| **Bulky, immovable, or oversized metal workpieces** | LMJ-110P Handheld Fiber Laser | Portable suitcase design to "bring the machine to the workpiece" |
| **Non-metal cutting & engraving (acrylic, wood, leather)** | 9060 / 1080 / 1390 CO2 Laser Machine | 60W–150W power, high non-metal efficiency, smooth cut edges |
| **High-volume 4ft×8ft (1.2m×2.4m) signage / full acrylic sheets** | 1325 Industrial Flatbed CO2 Laser | Extra-large 1300×2500mm bed; fits full sheets without pre-cutting |
| **Fine glass & crystal engraving / glassware / trophies** | 1080 CO2 Laser (or with rotary attachment) | Delivers an exquisite, elegant micro-fractured frosted white finish |
| **Luxury jewelry micro-engraving, ring interiors, gemstone IDs, precision electronics** | Jewelry-Grade 3W UV Laser Machine | 355nm cold laser; no burning, safe for gemstones, 0.01mm precision |
| **Craft fairs, wireless mobile app control, lightweight leather/wood DIY** | DLE-001B Mini Smart / DLE-1720 Cantilever | Direct smartphone app connection; ultra-portable or open 3-sided frame |
| **3D deep milling on wood & soft metals (aluminum/copper) alongside laser engraving** | CNC1310 Desktop CNC Router | All-metal body + T8 lead screws + 775 spindle for true 3D physical milling |
