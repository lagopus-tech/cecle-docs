## 3. Operating Workflow

1. **Machine Placement & Focal Alignment**  
   Position the machine securely on a workbench or directly atop an oversized workpiece; adjust the focal distance ring until the laser spot contracts to its sharpest point on the target surface.
2. **Interface Connection & Software Launch**  
   * **PC Operation**: Connect via USB to a PC running Windows 7, 8, or 10[cite: 4], and launch the host software (selectable in Chinese, English, or Japanese)[cite: 4].
   * **Mobile Operation (DLE-001B only)**: Enable mobile Bluetooth and pair the device via the dedicated iOS/Android control app[cite: 4].
3. **Artwork Import & Mode Selection**  
   Load the target graphics, vector paths, or text into the interface, then select Black & White, Discrete, or Grayscale mode according to the desired visual depth[cite: 4].
4. **Power & Speed Configuration**  
   Set power output percentages and feed rates suited to the substrate (e.g., wood, leather, dark plastics, opaque acrylic, or coated metal).
5. **Frame Boundary Preview & Laser Safety**  
   Run the bounding box preview function to verify marking boundaries on the workpiece, and verify that all nearby personnel are wearing protective laser eyewear.
6. **Execution & Final Inspection**  
   Initiate the marking cycle; once complete, remove the finished part and inspect marking clarity and line depth.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

* **Q: What is the core difference between the DLE-001 and DLE-001B models?**[cite: 4, 5]  
  * A: The primary differences are wireless capability and operating system support[cite: 4]. The DLE-001 is a wired model compatible with Windows 7/8/10 via USB[cite: 4]. The DLE-001B includes an integrated Bluetooth module that enables wireless operation from Android and iOS mobile devices in addition to Windows PCs[cite: 4].
* **Q: Can this portable laser engraver engrave metal surfaces?**  
  * A: It engraves anodized aluminum alloys, coated metallic business cards, painted metal casings, and lacquered surfaces. Because it utilizes a compact diode laser, it is not engineered for bare, untreated reflective metals such as bare stainless steel, raw brass, or titanium without laser marking thermal compounds.
* **Q: Is eye protection required during operation, and how is the laser focused?**  
  * A: Yes. Operators and nearby personnel must wear protective laser safety glasses matching the beam wavelength whenever the unit is powered on. The module features an adjustable manual focus ring that tightens the beam spot to its smallest point for optimal cutting edge and line definition.
