## II. Core Advantages
1. **PLC Smart Programmable Control System:** Equipped with an upgradeable computer programming system, variable frequency speed regulation, and automatic electronic braking. Features full-process touch screen parameter settings and automatic fault alerts for low operational thresholds.
2. **Coreless Rewinding Molding Technology:** Utilizes air-column molding technology for fast rewinding and compact, aesthetic roll formation. Supports both cored and coreless production modes to adapt to diverse product lines.
3. **New Tail-Gliding & Edge-Trimming Process:** Automatically completes edge trimming, gluing, and sealing simultaneously. Leaves a 10-15mm paper tail on the finished product for easy tearing, reducing raw material waste and lowering production costs.
4. **Tight-to-Loose Rewinding Logic:** Implements a progressive tension logic (tight to loose during rewinding) to prevent core loosening or unwinding during long-term storage, ensuring superior product storage stability.
5. **Paper Break Automatic Stop Protection:** Real-time paper monitoring automatically halts the equipment upon paper breakage, minimizing material waste and ensuring high-speed operational stability.
6. **High-Precision Perforation and Embossing:** Features a spiral perforation blade with low noise and adjustable perforation pitch, paired with a steel-to-wool embossing roller for clear, three-dimensional patterns (optional scent-adding device available).
7. **Pneumatic Feeding & Tension Adjustment:** Three-layer synchronous paper-feeding frame with pneumatic jumbo roll lifting. Each paper layer features independent tension adjustment and inching threading modes for simple, safe roll changes.

## IV. Operation Process
1. **Power & Air Supply:** Connect 3-phase 380V power supply and external air compressor, ensuring stable air pressure at 0.5Mpa.
2. **Material Loading & Paper Threading:** Use the pneumatic mechanism to load jumbo parent rolls onto the rear frame, complete paper threading, and configure tension parameters for each layer.
3. **Parameter Setting:** Enter target finished roll diameter, perforation pitch, production speed, and cored/coreless mode on the touch screen; configure embossing and gluing settings.
4. **Automatic Rewinding Production:** The equipment automatically rewinds, perforates, and embosses. Upon reaching the target diameter, it automatically trims edges, glues, seals tails, and discharges the finished roll before resetting for the next cycle.
5. **Post-Processing:** Transfer output logs to the log saw cutter for single-roll slicing, then feed them into the automatic packaging machine for final packaging and warehousing.

### Frequently Asked Questions
**Q1: Can the machine start production immediately upon plugging in?**
A1: No. This is a pneumatically assisted device; customers must supply their own air compressor ensuring 0.5Mpa air pressure, along with jumbo parent rolls.

**Q2: Can the machine produce coreless toilet rolls?**
A2: Yes, it features a built-in coreless rewinding system that allows switching between cored and coreless modes directly on the touch screen without extra modifications.

**Q3: Is the perforation pitch adjustable?**
A3: Yes, the perforation pitch is adjustable within 80‑220mm and 150‑300mm ranges via simple touch-screen parameter adjustments to suit various roll specifications.

**Q4: What other equipment is needed for a complete production line?**
A4: The TD-1575 is the rewinding host that outputs large logs. To get individual rolls, pair it with the TD-BSP01 Log Saw Cutter; to automate packaging, add the TD-JZ Automatic Packaging Machine.
