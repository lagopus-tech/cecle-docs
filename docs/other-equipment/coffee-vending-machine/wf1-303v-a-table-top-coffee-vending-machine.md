## 4. Operation Process
1. Select the appropriate water supply method according to the usage scenario (connect to purified water bucket, water pump, or municipal tap water);
2. Add instant coffee powder or other powder raw materials into the machine's material box, and load paper cups of the adapted size (Φ73-Φ74mm) into the cup compartment;
3. Insert the specified type of coin or select the desired beverage through the operation panel;
4. The machine automatically drops the cup, brews, and heats water. Once completed, take out the fragrant instant coffee.
