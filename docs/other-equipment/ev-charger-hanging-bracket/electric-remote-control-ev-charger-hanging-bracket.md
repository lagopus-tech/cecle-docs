## III. Installation & Operation

### 1. Standard Workflow
1. **Preparation**: Consult property management prior to installation regarding regulations for overhead wiring and electrical setup.
2. **Drilling & Securing**: Designed for easy overhead mounting; installers can drill directly into the concrete ceiling or structural beam using a hammer drill and secure the bracket firmly with matching expansion bolts.
3. **Electrical Connection**: Connect the motor's power wire to the designated parking space power source (conduit protection recommended).
4. **Debugging**: Test the wireless remote controller to ensure smooth chain lifting and proper limit positioning.

### 2. Daily Operation
* **Lowering to Charge**: Upon arriving at the parking space, press the "DOWN" button on the remote control to lower the bracket to a comfortable height and plug in.
* **Retracting for Storage**: After charging is complete and the connector is unplugged, press the "UP" button on the remote control to automatically elevate and secure the charger overhead, keeping the parking space completely clear.
