## 4. Operation Process
1. Manually apply glue to the sole and upper, align them, and place them onto the pressing station of the machine;
2. Turn on the power and air supply, observe the barometer, and adjust to the appropriate working air pressure (0.2–0.6 Mpa);
3. Control the equipment through the simple operation panel to initiate the dual-barrel pressing program for strong bonding;
4. Remove the shoes after pressing, inspect the bonding effect, and proceed to the next production step.
