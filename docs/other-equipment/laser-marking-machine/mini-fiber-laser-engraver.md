## III. Operation Process

1. **Power-on & Setup**: Connect to power, turn on the main power switch and the laser key switch, and wait for system initialization.
2. **Graphic & Parameter Setup**: Import design files (PLT, DXF, AI, BMP, JPG, etc.) into the marking software, then configure output power, marking speed, Q-switching frequency (10kHz–100kHz), and hatch settings.
3. **Positioning & Preview**: Place the workpiece inside the 100*100mm marking area. Activate the red-light outline preview to align focal height and locate the exact engraving area.
4. **Execute Marking**: Wear laser safety glasses, depress the foot switch or click "Mark" in the software to let the high-speed galvanometer execute the marking sequence.
5. **Inspection & Batch Production**: Inspect the initial output for depth and clarity. Once validated, proceed with continuous rapid feeding and marking using the foot pedal.
6. **Safe Shutdown**: Turn off the laser source and main power, clean the work surface, and place the protective cap back onto the f-theta lens.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):**

- **Q: Can the PDL mini fiber laser engraver mark on wood, leather, or acrylic?**
  * A: The 1064nm fiber wavelength is tailored specifically for metals and dense engineering plastics. Most organic materials like natural wood or clear acrylic absorb this wavelength poorly. For pure wood or acrylic, CO2 lasers are recommended; for dark rigid plastics and metals, this machine performs exceptionally well.
- **Q: Are there regular consumables that require replacement?**
  * A: No, it is virtually consumable-free. The fiber laser source is air-cooled, requires no gas refills or lamp replacements, and needs no ongoing optical realignment.
- **Q: Is the marking depth adjustable?**
  * A: Yes. By adjusting the laser power, scan speed, Q-frequency, and repetition passes in the software, the marking depth can be calibrated from ≤0.01mm up to ≤1.0mm.
- **Q: What after-sales and technical training do you provide?**
