# Comprehensive CNC Laser Marking Machine Buying Guide

When choosing a laser marking machine, the primary deciding factors are: **target materials**, **operating scenario (portable vs. stationary)**, and **precision & depth requirements**.

---

## Step 1: Choose the Laser Source by "Target Material" (Core Classification)

The core of a laser marking machine lies in matching the laser wavelength to the material. Choosing the wrong laser type will make effective processing impossible:

1. **Fiber Laser Marking Machine (1064nm)**
* **Applicable Materials**: Almost all metals (stainless steel, carbon steel, aluminum and anodized aluminum, copper, gold, silver, titanium alloys, etc.) as well as certain hard engineering plastics (such as ABS, PVC, PC, PBT, etc.).
* **Features**: Ultra-long 100,000-hour lifespan, zero consumables, maintenance-free, and high electro-optical conversion efficiency.
* **Representative Models**: Cabinet Fiber Laser, Handheld Fiber Laser, and Split-Type Fiber Laser.

2. **CO2 Laser Marking Machine (10.6µm)**
* **Applicable Materials**: Organic non-metallic materials (wood, leather, acrylic, paper, fabric, rubber, glass, ceramics, etc.).
* **Features**: High-frequency galvo scanner for high-speed coding, air-cooled RF metal tube design with a long lifespan, requiring no water cooling. *(Note: Cannot mark directly on bare metals unless the surface is treated with a special laser marking spray or anodized coating).*
* **Representative Models**: TD-CO2 Cabinet Marking Machine.

3. **UV Laser Marking Machine (355nm)**
* **Applicable Materials**: Heat-sensitive materials, glass, crystal, ceramics, silicon wafers, sapphire, and various plastics (capable of achieving high-contrast black or white marking on white plastics).
* **Features**: Utilizes "cold processing" photochemical ablation technology (ultra-short 355nm wavelength) with an extremely small heat-affected zone, ensuring edges do not burn, crack, or deform.
* **Representative Models**: TD-ZW UV Laser Marking Machine (equipped with an independent industrial water chiller).

---

## Step 2: Refine Selection by "Power & Configuration"

### 1. Fiber Laser Machines: How to Choose Power & Lenses?

* **20W Power**: Ideal for standard surface marking, such as routine metal logos, serial numbers, barcodes, and QR codes.
* **30W Power**: Balances faster marking speeds with slightly deeper penetration engraving capabilities.
* **50W Power**: Recommended for deep engraving on metals such as stainless steel, gold, and molds (depths up to 0.5mm), or when paired with a large 200×200mm F-theta field lens.
* **Working Area (Field Lens) Selection**: Common sizes include 110×110mm (highest precision), 150×150mm, 220×220mm, up to 300×300mm *(the larger the field size, the thicker the laser spot becomes, so balance according to your needs)*.

### 2. CO2 & UV Laser Machines:

* **CO2 (30W/50W)**: Suitable for high-speed assembly line flying marking on non-metals, wood engraving, acrylic crafts, and paper packaging marking.
* **UV (3W/5W)**: Ideal for precision electronics, medical packaging, cosmetic bottles, wafers, and other micro-machining applications, with selectable working areas ranging from 100×100mm to 300×300mm.

---

## Step 3: Choose the Model by "Operating Scenario & Structure"

1. **Standard Cabinet Type (Compatible with Fiber / CO2 / UV)**
* **Features**: A sturdy floor-standing enclosure integrating an industrial PC, monitor bracket, lifting column, and worktable.
* **Application Scenarios**: Factory workshops, batch production lines for stationary workpieces, electronic components, hardware tools, and precision mold processing.

2. **Handheld Portable Fiber Laser (LMJ-110P Handheld)**
* **Features**: Built with a rugged suitcase-style housing and an ergonomic top carry handle (includes a built-in bottom micro XY leveling stage).
* **Application Scenarios**: "Bringing the machine to the workpiece"—specifically engineered for oversized, bulky, immovable, or already-installed workpieces (such as automotive engine blocks, heavy machinery bases, large steel structures, pipelines, and large mold nameplates).

3. **Split-Type Fiber Laser (LMJ-110SP Split Type)**
* **Features**: Separates the main control cabinet from the galvo column, optimizing internal airflow and enhancing heat dissipation.
* **Application Scenarios**: Ideal for customers requiring continuous, high-intensity long-term operation with strict heat dissipation demands, serving both commercial customization studios and multi-station manufacturing workshops.

## Quick Selection Decision Matrix

| Core Customer Needs / Pain Points | Recommended Model | Key Advantages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **High-volume metal marking / lettering / serial numbers** | Cabinet Fiber Laser Marking Machine (20W/30W/50W) | 100,000-hour lifespan, zero consumables, 10,000 mm/s high-speed marking |
| **Workpieces too heavy or immovable (e.g., car engines, large steel structures)** | Handheld Fiber Laser Marking Machine (LMJ-110P) | Portable suitcase design with carry handle for direct on-site operation |
| **Continuous, heavy-duty long-term operation with high heat dissipation needs** | Split-Type Fiber Laser Marking Machine (LMJ-110SP) | Separated cabinet and galvo column, low thermal loss, ultra-stable operation |
| **Non-metal processing (wood, leather, acrylic, paper, glass)** | CO2 Laser Marking Machine | 10.6µm wavelength perfectly matches organic materials; much faster than traditional flatbed machines |
| **Burn-free plastic marking, glass/wafer micro-machining, high-contrast black/white on premium plastics** | UV Laser Marking Machine (TD-ZW) | 355nm "cold processing" photochemical ablation; zero thermal deformation or burrs |
