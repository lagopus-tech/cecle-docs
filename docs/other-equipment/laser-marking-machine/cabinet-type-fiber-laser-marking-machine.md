## III. Operation Process

1. **System Power-On**: Turn on the main power switch, computer, laser source, and galvanometer scanning head.
2. **Graphic Design & Import**: Open the dedicated laser marking software on the PC; create text/barcodes directly or import vector/image files (AI, DXF, PLT, BMP, etc.).
3. **Workpiece Positioning & Focusing**: Place the metal or plastic part on the worktable and adjust the Z-axis lifting column using the red-light pointer until the focal distance matches the selected field lens (110×110 mm, 150×150 mm, or 200×200 mm).
4. **Parameter Configuration**: Set the laser power percentage, scanning speed (up to 10,000 mm/s), and frequency according to the material type and required marking depth (≤0.5 mm).
5. **Red-Light Preview**: Activate the red-light contour preview to verify that the marking position on the workpiece is completely accurate.
6. **Start Marking**: Click the "Mark" button in the software or press the foot pedal to execute instant, high-precision laser engraving.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):**
- **Q: How do I choose between 20W, 30W, and 50W laser power?**
    * A: 20W is ideal for standard surface marking of logos, serial numbers, and QR codes. 30W offers higher marking speed and slightly deeper penetration. 50W is recommended if you require deep engraving on stainless steel, brass, or molds (up to 0.5 mm depth) or use a larger 200×200 mm lens.
- **Q: Does the Cabinet Type Fiber Laser Marking Machine require any consumables or gas refills?**
    * A: No. It is 100% air-cooled and solid-state, requiring zero ink, solvents, or gas refills. The laser source has a theoretical lifespan of up to 100,000 hours, and electricity is the only operating cost.
- **Q: Can this machine mark both metals and plastics?**
    * A: Yes. It is compatible with almost all metals (stainless steel, aluminum, copper, carbon steel, titanium, gold, silver) and many hard plastics such as ABS, PVC, polycarbonate, epoxy resin, and painted materials.
- **Q: What graphic file formats does the software support?**
    * A: The control software supports industry-standard vector and bitmap formats including AI, PLT, DXF, DWG, DST, DXP, LAS, and BMP, making it easy to import designs from AutoCAD, CorelDRAW, or Illustrator.
