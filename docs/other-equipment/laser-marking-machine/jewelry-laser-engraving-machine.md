## II. Core Advantages

1. **Nano Micro Engraving Precision**: The 355nm wavelength enables an extremely fine focal spot, allowing a minimum character size of 0.1mm, a minimum line width of 0.01mm, and a repeat accuracy of ±0.002mm. It can engrave high-definition micro-graphics onto tiny surfaces like a gemstone or the inside wall of a ring band.
2. **Cold Marking with Zero Thermal Damage**: Unlike standard fiber lasers that melt metal through high heat, UV cold lasers ablate material photochemically with virtually no heat-affected zone (HAZ). Precious metals such as gold, silver, and platinum will not warp, yellow, or burn, and adjacent mounted gems remain completely safe.
3. **Broad Material Compatibility**: Overcomes the material limitations of standard fiber lasers. In addition to metals, it delivers clean, smooth, touch-free marks on glass, crystal, ceramics, silicone, and various plastics without burning or micro-cracking.
4. **Jewelry Projector & Precision Alignment**: Equipped with high-precision visual red-light preview and alignment capabilities tailored for curved jewelry geometries (such as ring inner/outer circumferences and domed pendants), streamlining setup for jewelers.
5. **Integrated Air Cooling Architecture**: Built with an internal air cooling design that eliminates the bulky external water chillers and plumbing associated with traditional UV lasers, making it clean, quiet, and suitable for boutique counters or design studios.

## III. Operation Process

1. **Power-on & System Check**: Connect to a 220V power supply, turn on the main breaker and laser key switch, and allow the system and built-in air cooling to initialize.
2. **File Import & Parameter Setup**: Import artwork or text files (supports DXF, AI, PLT, BMP, etc.) into the control software, then adjust power (fine-tuned across the 3W range), marking speed, and laser pulse width (0–1600ns).
3. **Focal Alignment & Surface Positioning**: Secure the jewelry piece on the work stage (or attach a rotary chuck fixture for rings), turn on the red-light framing guide, and adjust the focal distance to match the target plane.
4. **Marking Execution**: Put on UV-rated laser safety glasses, verify clearance, and trigger the foot switch to let the digital galvanometers complete the micro-engraving process.
5. **Magnified Inspection**: Examine the engraved detail and edge quality under a magnifying loupe or digital microscope to ensure complete clarity before handling.
6. **System Shutdown & Care**: Power down the laser source and main switch, clean the working surface, and replace the protective cap over the f-theta lens.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):**

* **Q: Why does a 3W jewelry laser engraver cost more than a 20W/30W fiber laser?**
  * A: The difference lies in the laser technology and wavelength. Standard fiber lasers (1064nm) use heat to melt metals, which can scorch fine jewelry or crack stones. This machine uses an advanced 355nm UV cold laser source that ablates material at a molecular level with virtually zero heat impact, producing micro-precision engravings on precious metals, crystal, and glass.
* **Q: Can this machine mark on crystal, glass, and jade?**
  * A: Yes. The 355nm UV wavelength is absorbed readily by non-metallic materials, allowing it to mark glass, crystal, jade, ceramics, and silicone smoothly without fracturing the surface.
* **Q: Does this machine require external water cooling?**
  * A: No. It uses an engineered built-in air cooling system, eliminating external water chillers, tubing, and frequent coolant maintenance.
* **Q: Can it engrave curved jewelry like rings and bangles?**
  * A: Yes. The controller supports an optional precision jewelry rotary fixture, and the software includes cylindrical curve compensation to achieve continuous 360° seamless engraving on ring interiors and exteriors.
