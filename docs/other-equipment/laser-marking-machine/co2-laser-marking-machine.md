# CO2 Laser marking machine

![CO2 Laser marking machine](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/f_6170f25e-8ea7-4784-aa05-e963a16b82aa.jpg?v=1793240489)

The TD-CO2 Laser Marking Machine is an industrial-grade laser system engineered for high-speed, high-volume marking and intricate pattern engraving on non-metal materials. Combining 30W/50W power outputs with an integrated cabinet design, it utilizes a high-speed galvanometer scanning system and a 10.6um wavelength carbon dioxide laser. This wavelength is efficiently absorbed by organic non-metal materials such as wood, leather, acrylic, paper, fabrics, rubber, glass, and ceramics, delivering exceptional marking speeds and superior processing precision.

## I. Product Overview

* **Category**: Industrial Laser Marking Systems / CO2 Laser Engraving and Coding Equipment.
* **Applications**: Widely used in non-metal high-speed production lines, high-volume batch coding (e.g., manufacturing dates, serial numbers, 2D barcodes), woodcraft engraving, leather and apparel processing, acrylic promotional displays, paper packaging, and glass/ceramic marking.
* **Core Function**: Utilizing high-speed galvanometers to steer the laser beam, it achieves non-contact, high-speed, and high-precision marking of text, graphics, barcodes, and QR codes on non-metal surfaces.

## II. Core Advantages

1. **Core Technical Advantages (Speed & Efficiency)**:
   - **High-Speed Marking**: Employs a high-speed galvanometer scanner to control laser beam movement rather than moving the entire laser head like flatbed engravers. Speeds are several to dozens of times faster than traditional flatbed engravers, making it ideal for assembly line production and high-volume rapid marking/coding.
   - **High Precision**: The galvanometer system enables extremely fine text and pattern marking.

2. **Material Compatibility Advantages (Non-Metal Expert)**:
   - **Wide Range of Non-Metal Materials**: As a CO2 laser, its 10.6um wavelength is easily absorbed by organic materials.
   - **Proficient Materials**: Wood, leather, acrylic, paper, cardboard, fabrics, rubber, glass, ceramics, and select plastics. *(Note: Typically cannot mark directly on bare metal unless coated or treated with an oxidation/marking layer).*

3. **Machine Design Advantages**:
   - **Integrated Cabinet Design**: Integrates the laser source, control system, computer (with monitor mount), and work table into a stable floor-standing cabinet. Space-saving, professional appearance, robust structure, and excellent vibration resistance ensure long-term operational stability.
   - **Low Maintenance**: Equipped with an RF metal tube CO2 laser (standard for industrial marking), offering an extremely long service life (refillable/reusable), superior beam quality, and air-cooling that eliminates the need for bulky external water chillers.

## III. Operation Process

1. **Power-On & Preparation**: Switch on the main power, control system, and exhaust ventilation unit; launch the marking control software.
2. **File Import & Parameter Setting**: Import the target text, graphics, or barcodes into the software. Set laser power (30W/50W), frequency, and marking speed according to the non-metal material characteristics.
3. **Workpiece Positioning & Focusing**: Place the non-metal workpiece steadily on the working platform and adjust the focal length using the lifting mechanism.
4. **Red-Light Preview & Alignment**: Activate the red-light pointer preview function to verify that the marking area matches the workpiece position precisely.
5. **Start Marking**: Initiate the marking command; the high-speed galvanometer drives the laser beam to complete engraving or coding instantly.
6. **Inspection & Retrieval**: Retrieve the finished product, inspect marking clarity, and clear any surface residues.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):**
* **Q: Can this machine mark on metal?**
    * A: CO2 lasers are primarily designed for non-metal materials. If the metal surface has special paint coatings, anodized layers, or marking compounds applied, it can mark them, but it cannot etch directly onto bare metal surfaces.
* **Q: Does the machine require frequent water refilling or consumable replacements?**
    * A: The RF metal tube CO2 laser features an air-cooled design with an exceptionally long lifespan, eliminating the need for cooling water and keeping daily maintenance costs minimal.
* **Q: Is it suitable for integration into automated factory assembly lines?**
    * A: Absolutely. The integrated cabinet design combined with high-speed galvanometers perfectly accommodates online high-speed coding and batch production demands for various non-metal products.
