## III. Operation Process

1. **Deployment & Power-on**: Carry the unit to the target job site and position it securely (or align directly against the large workpiece). Connect to power (AC 110V/220V), switch on the main power breaker, and activate the laser key switch to complete system self-checks.
2. **Design Import & Parameter Configuration**: Open the industrial marking software (e.g., EzCad), import vector or bitmap graphics (supported formats include AI, BMP, DST, DWG, DXF, DXP, LAS, PLT), and configure laser power, marking speed (up to 8000mm/s), and repetition frequency (20–80kHz).
3. **Focal Alignment & XY Micro-Adjustment**: Adjust the vertical column handwheel to set the precise focal plane. For small benchtop pieces, turn the dual XY stage knobs to center the workpiece, using the red-light outline preview for positioning.
4. **Marking Execution**: Ensure laser safety goggles are worn, verify the focal zone is clear, and press the foot pedal or click "Mark" in the software to initiate high-speed galvanometer processing.
5. **Quality Inspection & Continuous Batching**: Inspect the initial mark for depth (0.01–1mm) and edge contrast. Once verified, utilize the foot switch to perform rapid continuous batch cycles.
6. **System Shutdown & Pack-up**: Turn off the laser source and main power, clean the work area and guide rails, and replace the protective lens cover onto the f-theta lens.
