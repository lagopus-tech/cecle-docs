## 6. Workflow

### 6.1 Filling Process

1. **Connect the air source** — connect 0.4–0.6 MPa compressed air through the standard water-oil separator filter and pressure regulator, and let the gauge stabilize at the set value.
2. **Fill the tank** — pour perfume / aromatic liquid into the 304 stainless steel tank, close the lid tightly, and confirm the suction tube is in place.
3. **Adjust the filling volume** — turn the top handwheel to adjust the piston stroke, verify by weighing 2–3 bottles, and lock the target volume.
4. **Position the bottle** — place a clean empty bottle under the nozzle, with the bottle mouth aligned to the outlet.
5. **Start by foot pedal** — press the foot valve; the air cylinder drives the piston down, and the measured liquid is injected through the anti-drip nozzle.
6. **Release to reset** — release the pedal; the piston returns and refills automatically, and the nozzle cuts off cleanly without dripping.
7. **Remove and proceed** — take out the filled bottle and move it to capping, labeling, and other downstream steps.
8. **Clean and maintain** — when switching fragrance or color, remove the nozzle and tubing and rinse with clean water or alcohol; check the seals and air fittings regularly.
