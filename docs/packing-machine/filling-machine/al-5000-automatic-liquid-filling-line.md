The AL-5000 is a fully automatic liquid filling line built by Wenzhou T&D Packaging Machinery Factory. It covers five stations — bottle distributing, filling, cap feeding, capping and labeling — and is designed for plastic bottles with plastic screw caps, such as dishwashing liquid, hand wash and household cleaning products. The standard line consists of three units: the A4-500 automatic piston filling machine (2 heads, 50-500ml), the DC-1 automatic screwing capping machine, and the AL-220 automatic round bottle labeling machine. The bottle distributing machine (80cm rotary table) is listed as an optional device in the factory quotation. The filler runs on a Taiwanese-brand PLC with touchscreen control and Airtac pneumatic components, with filling accuracy of plus or minus 1 percent. German SICK photocell sensors deliver no bottle, no fill operation, and the nozzles use a bottom close positive shut-off design to prevent dripping. The capper runs two capping heads simultaneously; the labeling machine runs at 40–200 pieces per minute with a labeling precision of 1.0mm or better, and stops with an alarm when labels run out or break. Eight filling ranges are available from 5-50ml to 500-5000ml, with 2, 4, 6 or 8 filling heads. Lead time is 30–45 working days, shipment from Ningbo port, with a one-year warranty.

**Key Specs at a Glance:** Filling range 50–500ml (standard) | Filling speed 10–30 times/min | Filling accuracy ±1% | Filling heads 2 heads (2 / 4 / 6 / 8 optional) | Air pressure 0.6–0.8 MPa | Air consumption 3–4 m³/h | Voltage 220V 50/60Hz 660W | Labeling speed 40–200 pcs/min | Labeling precision ≥1.0mm.

### Core Functions

1. **Automatic bottle distributing:** rotary table sorts round and square bottles, conveying and counting at 30–200 bottles per minute with infinitely variable speed.
2. **Piston dosing filling:** fill volume and fill speed are set directly on the HMI, with combined pneumatic and electric drive.
3. **Automatic cap feeding and capping:** the DC-1 capper runs two capping heads simultaneously, synchronized with the filling cycle.
4. **Automatic labeling:** the AL-220 round bottle labeling machine applies one or two labels to cylindrical products, using a five-axis positioning label feeding system.
5. **No bottle, no fill:** German SICK photocell sensors skip filling and capping when a bottle is missing.
6. **Automatic alarms:** the labeling machine stops and alarms when labels run out or break. An automatic feeding wheel and an automatic receiving device are optional.
7. **Expansion interface:** can be connected with a semi-auto bottle washer and the PM-168 inkjet printing machine to form a complete packaging line.

## 4. Sales Guidance & FAQ

### Sales Guidance

1. **Establish the fill volume first, then the range:** if the customer's product falls outside 50–500ml, select the matching range from the eight options (5-50 / 10-100 / 30-300 / 50-500 / 100-1000 / 250-2500 / 300-3000 / 500-5000ml). Do not force the A4-500 onto it.
2. **Establish the output target first, then the head count:** 10–30 times/min is the single-cycle speed. Actual bottles per hour = cycles × heads × 60. With 2 heads the theoretical output is 1,200–3,600 bottles/h. For customers needing more, offer 4, 6 or 8 heads.
3. **Establish the product viscosity first:** this line is built for liquids. Confirm suitability with the factory before committing to high-viscosity pastes. Do not assume.
4. **Establish the bottle and cap type first:** bottle diameter must satisfy both the bottle distributing machine (φ20–φ100mm) and the labeling machine (φ32–φ150mm), and the same applies to height (20–150mm / 40–350mm). The capping chuck material and torque for plastic screw caps must be confirmed after the customer sends real cap samples.
5. **Establish the label specification first:** labels of 20–150mm long and 10–250mm wide, with a 76mm core and an outer diameter within 360mm. Self-adhesive label rolls outside this range simply will not load.
6. **Align on the output figure:** 10–30 times/min is the rated filling cycle value. Actual line output depends on bottle feed rate, product foaming and manual loading. Quote conservatively.
7. **Upsell in this order:** filling + capping + labeling is the base configuration. For customers needing batch codes, add the PM-168 inkjet printing machine; for customers with cleanliness requirements, add the glass cover and the semi-auto bottle washer.
8. **Push hygiene and compliance:** lead with 304 contact parts for personal care and food customers, and the 316 upgrade for high-salt or high-acid products. This is the differentiator that supports a price premium.
9. **Manage delivery expectations:** 30–45 working days plus ocean shipping. State the total lead time up front to avoid disputes after the deposit is paid.

### FAQ

**Q1: What products is the AL-5000 suited to?**
This line is built as a liquid filling line, for household and cleaning products such as dishwashing liquid, hand wash, laundry detergent, windshield washer fluid and disinfectant, as well as low to medium viscosity liquids such as edible oil, fruit juice and oral liquids. Suitability for high-viscosity pastes must be confirmed with the factory first.

**Q2: What is the output of this line?**
The filling machine runs at 10–30 cycles per minute. Actual output = cycles × number of filling heads × 60. With the standard 2 heads, theoretical output is 1,200–3,600 bottles per hour (**calculated value, to be confirmed**). The labeling machine runs at 40–200 pieces per minute, and the actual line rate is set by the slowest station.

**Q3: How is filling accuracy guaranteed?**
Filling accuracy is plus or minus 1 percent, guaranteed by the PLC-controlled piston stroke. The nozzles use a bottom close positive shut-off design to prevent stringing and dripping, so no product contaminates the bottle body and affects labeling.

**Q4: What material are the product-contact parts?**
The standard configuration is food-grade 304 stainless steel, with 201 stainless steel for the machine body. Contact parts can be upgraded to 304 or 316 stainless steel. We recommend 316 for highly corrosive products.

**Q5: How many filling heads are available, and what filling ranges can I choose?**
Filling heads are available in 2, 4, 6 or 8 head versions. Eight filling ranges are available: 5-50ml, 10-100ml, 30-300ml, 50-500ml, 100-1000ml, 250-2500ml, 300-3000ml and 500-5000ml. This line ships with 2 heads and a 50-500ml range as standard.

**Q6: Does this line include the bottle distributing machine?**
It is sold as a five-station line covering bottle distributing, filling, cap feeding, capping and labeling, but in the factory quotation the **bottle distributing machine is listed as an optional device**. Confirm whether it is included before signing.

**Q7: What label and bottle sizes does the AL-220 labeling machine handle?**
Labels: 20–150mm long, 10–250mm wide, with a 76mm core and an outer diameter within 360mm. Bottles: 32–150mm in diameter, 40–350mm in height. One or two labels can be applied to the same product.

**Q8: Can I buy only the filling machine, without the capper and labeling machine?**
Yes. The machine configuration is freely selectable, and we can supply a single unit to suit your existing line.

**Q9: How long is the warranty and what does it cover?**
One year on the whole machine, starting from start-up and acceptance in the buyer's factory. Parts are supplied free of charge for quality problems during the warranty period; the customer covers freight from China to the destination. If an engineer visit is required, round-trip travel costs are borne by the customer. Lifetime repair service continues after the warranty expires.

**Q10: What power and air supply does the customer need to prepare?**
Power: 220V 50/60Hz (the labeling machine also supports 220V/100V). Air: 0.6–0.8 MPa at 3–4 m³/h. The customer needs to provide an air compressor and a regulated power supply to match.

**Q11: Are the values in the specification tables final?**
The parameters above are for reference only; the actual machine is the standard.

## 6. Workflow

| Station | Action | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Loading | Operator pours empty bottles onto the bottle distributing machine turntable | Operator |
| 2. Bottle distributing | Turntable sorts and orients round/square bottles, 30–200 bottles/min, infinitely variable speed | Automatic |
| 3. Conveying | Conveyor (2 meters as standard) carries bottles to the filling station; fixing device clamps and locates them | Automatic |
| 4. Filling | Piston filling; fill volume and speed set on the PLC; SICK photocell detection, no bottle no fill | Automatic |
| 5. Cap feeding | Caps are fed and placed in position automatically, synchronized with the capping station | Automatic |
| 6. Capping | DC-1 runs two capping heads simultaneously with consistent torque | Automatic |
| 7. Labeling | AL-220 five-axis positioning label feeding, labeling of round bottles at 40–200 pcs/min | Automatic |
| 8. Optional stations | Semi-auto bottle washing / PM-168 inkjet coding | Automatic |
| 9. Output | Finished bottles discharge to the collection table for manual cartoning | Operator |

**Pre-operation Setup**

1. Connect the 220V 50/60Hz power supply and the 0.6–0.8 MPa air supply (air compressor to be provided by the customer).
2. Set the fill volume (50–500ml) and fill speed on the touchscreen.
3. Adjust the bottle distributing machine turntable speed and conveyor guide rails to suit the bottle diameter and height.
4. Load the caps and trial-cap 3–5 bottles to confirm torque and sealing.
5. Load the label roll (76mm core, outer diameter within 360mm) and trial-label 3–5 bottles to confirm label position and seam.
6. Inspect the fill volume of the first articles (accuracy ±1%), then move to continuous production.
