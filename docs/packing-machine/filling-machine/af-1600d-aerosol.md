AF-1600D is a semi automatic aerosol filling machine built around three stations — vacuum evacuation, valve crimping and gas filling — equipped with two gas filling units and two booster pumps. It runs at 3000–4000 cans per day per filling head, with a gas fill weight of 150–300 g and repeat filling accuracy within ±1%. A semi-automatic water bath leak detector with temperature control completes in-house leak testing. It handles aerosol cans with the international standard 1-inch (25.4 mm) valve, can diameters of 35–65 mm and can heights of 70–330 mm, making it a practical crimping and gas filling solution for small-to-medium batches of butane and propellant cartridges such as portable camping stove gas cartridges.

## 2. Key Advantages

1. **Dual filling stations plus dual booster pumps:** Supplied with two gas fillers and two booster pumps, so both filling units can run in parallel and output beats comparable semi-automatic machines with a single filling head.
2. **Three stations in one line:** Vacuum evacuation, valve crimping and gas filling are integrated. The operator only loads and unloads cans, and the full vacuum → crimping → gas filling cycle runs on one machine with a small footprint and fast transfer.
3. **Consistent fill weights:** Gas fill weight of 150–300 g with repeat accuracy ≤±1%, reducing the gas loss and safety risk that come with overfilling.
4. **Built-in leak testing:** A semi-automatic water bath leak detector with temperature control is supplied as standard, so finished cans can be temperature-controlled water bath tested in-house to verify seal quality before shipment.
5. **Broad can compatibility:** Suitable for can diameters of 35–65 mm and can heights of 70–330 mm, both customisable to the customer's can.
6. **Universal valve:** Works with the international standard 1-inch (25.4 mm) aerosol valve, so customers need no extra tooling or modification for valve format.
7. **Transparent air requirements:** The air source specification is stated up front — 0.8–1.2 MPa with maximum air consumption of 0.8 m³/min at 0.6 MPa — so customers can verify their compressor and gas supply before ordering.
8. **Customized:** Can diameter, can height and fill weight can all be tailored to the customer's can and medium.

## 4. Sales Tips & FAQ

### Sales Tips

- **Positioning:** Suited to customers running pilot or small-batch production of portable butane and gas cartridges. This machine is gas filling only — customers who also need liquid filling on the same frame should be offered four-station machines such as the AF4-1600A.
- **Must-ask before quoting:** ① the medium to be filled (butane, LPG, DME, etc.) and its required filling pressure; ② target fill weight per can (to confirm it falls within 150–300 g); ③ can diameter and height (to confirm they fall within 35–65 mm / 70–330 mm or need customising); ④ whether the valve is standard 1 inch; ⑤ whether the semi-automatic water bath leak detector is required; ⑥ whether on-site installation is required.
- **Be clear about output:** The 3000–4000 cans/day figure in the specification table is the **per-head** rate. Twin filling units raise theoretical output, but actual throughput depends on how fast the operator loads and unloads cans and on the leak testing rhythm — do not quote double output without factory confirmation.

- **Differentiation pitch:** Lead with the two points — dual gas filling plus dual booster pumps, and the supplied water bath leak detector with temperature control. Most semi-automatic machines at this price point use a single filling head and ship without any leak testing step.

## 6. Workflow

Semi-automatic sequence (manual can loading, stations in series):

```
Operator loads an empty can
    ↓
① Vacuum evacuation station — air is drawn out of the can
    ↓
② Valve crimping station — the 1" aerosol valve is crimped in place
    ↓
③ Gas filling station ×2 — metered fuel gas / propellant fill (150–300 g, accuracy ≤±1%)
    ↓
④ Semi-automatic water bath test — temperature-controlled water bath leak testing (dedicated detector included)
    ↓
Operator removes the finished can
```

> Station order follows the equipment list in the factory quotation (vacuum evacuation → valve crimping → gas filling → water bath testing); the actual on-site sequence should follow the factory SOP.

**Air circuit:** gas source (butane / LPG / DME, etc.) → booster pump ×2 → gas filling station ×2. The twin booster pumps hold both filling lines steady at 0.8–1.2 MPa, keeping parallel filling smoother and safer.

**Power/air supply:** compressed air source pressure 0.8–1.2 MPa, max air consumption 0.8 m³/min at 0.6 MPa.
