## VI. Workflow

### Step-by-Step Workflow

1. **Connect utilities**: Connect the air compressor (0.4–0.6 MPa) and plug in the 220 V power supply for the mixer motor.
2. **Load material**: Add the paste or liquid into the U-type mixing hopper.
3. **Mix continuously**: The electric stirrer keeps the material homogeneous and prevents settling or separation throughout the whole filling run.
4. **Set parameters**: Adjust the filling volume and filling speed as required.
5. **Choose the operation mode**: Step on the foot pedal for single-shot semi-automatic filling, or switch to the automatic timer for continuous cyclic filling.
6. **Quantitative filling**: The piston draws the exact amount of paste and the nozzle fills the container (on multi-head machines, all heads fill simultaneously).
7. **Anti-drip cut-off**: The bottom close positive shutoff nozzle cuts off instantly, leaving zero drips.
8. **Next cycle**: The machine resets automatically and the next filling cycle begins.
