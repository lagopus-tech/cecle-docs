## 6. Workflow

### 6.1 Filling Process

1. **Connect air source** — connect 0.4–0.6 MPa compressed air (consumption 0.6–1.1 m³/h); no power supply needed.
2. **Prepare empty bottles** — place clean empty bottles (bottle diameter 4.5–35 mm, bottle height ≤350 mm) into the 4 filling stations in sequence.
3. **Align with nozzles** — manually or via a robotic arm, raise the 4 empty bottles simultaneously to align with the 4 filling nozzles.
4. **Foot-pedal start** — step on the foot pedal to trigger the 4 nozzles to descend synchronously and insert into the bottle mouths.
5. **Vacuum suction + filling** — continuously vacuum-suck the perfume into the bottles; excess material is automatically pumped back to the tank.
6. **Liquid-level positioning complete** — the liquid level stabilizes at the set height of 15–50 mm; the insertion depth of the nozzle determines the liquid level.
7. **Nozzles rise + bottle removal** — the 4 nozzles rise synchronously; the operator removes the 4 filled bottles and proceeds to the next batch.
8. **Cleaning and maintenance** — disassemble nozzles and pipelines when switching fragrance or color; 304 stainless steel contact surfaces can be rinsed with clean water.
