## VI. Workflow

### Step-by-Step Workflow

1. **Bottle Feeding**: Empty bottles are automatically conveyed into the machine.
2. **Counting & Positioning**: The inlet photoelectric sensor counts bottles; once 6 bottles are reached, the bottle stop cylinder extends for precise positioning.
3. **Filling**: The filling heads descend and the pistons push material, achieving synchronized 6-head high-precision filling.
4. **Anti-Drip Handling**: When filling ends, the positive shutoff valve closes and the heads rise, ensuring zero dripping.
5. **Bottle Discharge**: The stop cylinder retracts and the filled bottles move on to the capping/labeling process.
