The AF3-1600A is a semi-automatic three-head aerosol filling machine that combines liquid filling, crimping, and gas charging into a single machine frame, supplied with a matched booster pump for stable air supply. Output is 100–300 cans per hour per machine. It handles tinplate and aluminium aerosol cans with the international standard 1-inch (25.4 mm) valve, with liquid and gas filling volumes of 40–500 ml at an accuracy of plus or minus 2 percent (±2%). It fills a wide range of media, including corrosive liquids, and is widely used for industrial maintenance sprays, car care products, and household spray products.

## 2. Key Advantages

1. **Three stations in one machine:** Liquid filling, crimping and gas charging are integrated into a single frame — a small footprint and fast workflow, ideal for small and medium batches with a wide product mix.
2. **Proven crimping technology:** The crimping unit draws extensively on the structural design of Pamasol (Switzerland) filling machines. The guided crimping head delivers fast crimping, reliable crimp quality, and easy operation, adjustment and maintenance.
3. **Twin-cylinder crimping structure:** The lower cylinder (clamping cylinder) drives the crimping head down to clamp the aerosol valve, while the upper cylinder performs the crimp — stable and reliable in action.
4. **Corrosion-resistant design:** Liquid contact parts are made of 316L stainless steel with PTFE (polytetrafluoroethylene) seals, handling different liquids including corrosive ones.
5. **Reliable key components:** Seals are supplied by Hansheng Sealing Technology Co., Ltd. (Taiwan); cylinders are AirTAC brand, giving a clean, modern appearance.
6. **Long service life:** No frequent seal replacement required; service life is longer than comparable filling machines in the industry.
7. **Booster pump included:** The added booster pump keeps the air supply stable, making production more consistent and safer.
8. **Customisable:** Suitable for can diameters of 35–65 mm and can heights of 70–330 mm, both customisable on request.

### FAQ

**Q1: Can this machine complete liquid filling, crimping and gas charging in one pass?**
A: Yes. The AF3-1600A is a three-station integrated design: cans are loaded and unloaded manually, and liquid filling → crimping → gas charging are completed in sequence on the same machine, supported by the booster pump.

**Q2: Which can types and valves is it suitable for?**
A: Tinplate and aluminium aerosol cans with the international standard 1-inch (25.4 mm) can opening, can diameters of 35–65 mm and can heights of 70–330 mm. Sizes are customisable.

**Q3: Can it fill corrosive liquids (such as certain cleaners and maintenance agents)?**
A: Yes. Liquid contact parts are made of 316L stainless steel with PTFE seals, handling different liquids including corrosive ones.

**Q4: What production scale is it suitable for?**
A: Output is 100–300 cans per hour per machine, ideal for small and medium batches with a wide product mix. For higher output, upgrade to a fully automatic multi-head model.

**Q5: How is crimp quality ensured?**
A: The crimping head uses guided crimping (drawing on Pamasol structural design) with a twin-cylinder structure: the clamping cylinder clamps the valve first, then the crimping cylinder completes the internal expansion crimp — fast and reliable.

**Q6: What are the air supply requirements?**
A: Compressed air pressure of 0.65–1.0 MPa and air consumption of approximately 0.8 m³/min; the machine is supplied with a booster pump for a stable air supply.

## 6. Workflow

Semi-automatic operation sequence (cans loaded manually, three stations working in sequence):

```
Load empty can manually
    ↓
(1) Liquid filling station — volumetric liquid filling (40-500 ml, accuracy ±2%)
    ↓
(2) Crimping station — clamping cylinder clamps the 1" aerosol valve → crimping cylinder performs internal expansion crimping
    ↓
(3) Gas charging station — volumetric propellant charging (40-500 ml, accuracy ±2%)
    ↓
Remove finished can manually
```

**Air circuit:** LPG / compressed air supply → booster pump (360 × 320 × 750 mm) → each station of the machine. The booster pump keeps the supply pressure stable, making production smoother and safer.
