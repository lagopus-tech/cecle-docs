### 4.2 FAQ

**Q1: Does this machine need electricity?**
A: **No.** The F6 series is a full-pneumatic design with no motor and no circuits — every action is driven by compressed air. However, it **must be paired with an air compressor**: air supply pressure 0.4–0.6 MPa, air consumption approx. 0.55–1.1 m³/h.

**Q2: What are the requirements for the air compressor?**
A: An ordinary small piston-type air compressor (with air tank) is sufficient — set the output pressure to 0.4–0.6 MPa. Check that the compressor's airflow covers the machine's air consumption (approx. 0.55–1.1 m³/h). We recommend following the factory-recommended models; you can also purchase one together with the machine.

**Q3: Is the machine explosion-proof?**
A: Yes. Full pneumatic drive with no electricity makes it a **safety explosion-proof type** machine suitable for workshops filling flammable liquids such as alcohol and solvents — a core advantage over electric filling machines.

**Q4: What about filling accuracy and speed?**
A: Accuracy is **within ±1%** (piston-type metering, stable with no drift); speed is **10–40 cycles/min** (10–30 cycles/min on some models) and is adjustable.

**Q5: What liquids can it fill? What about high-viscosity or paste products?**
A: It is designed for **free-flowing liquids**: water, edible oil, juice, milk, liquor, medicinal liquids, liquid detergents, etc. For high-viscosity products or pastes (sauces, creams, ointments), we recommend the factory's paste filling machine series.

**Q6: Are the product-contact materials food-grade?**
A: The frame is 201 stainless steel and **product-contact parts are food-grade 304 stainless steel** compliant with food-safety requirements; **custom 316 stainless steel is available** (better acid/alkali and corrosion resistance, suitable for pharmaceutical, chemical, and corrosive products). Seals are food-grade silicone (heat-resistant to 200 ℃).

**Q7: Does it drip or string?**
A: No. The filling head uses a **bottom-close positive-shutoff** structure that closes the outlet the moment filling ends, guaranteeing drip-free operation. Nozzle diameters are selectable from 3–12 mm.

**Q8: Is it troublesome to change the fill volume?**
A: No. Within the model's range, simply **adjust the piston stroke** to change the fill volume — no parts to replace, done in a few minutes. Ideal for multi-format, small-batch production.

**Q9: Can it run fully automatically? What if capacity is not enough?**
A: Yes. The machine supports both **foot-pedal (semi-automatic) and automatic-timer (automatic) modes** and can switch between them at any time. For higher capacity, **custom 2-head, 4-head, or 6-head** models (one head per cylinder) are available — no equipment replacement needed.

**Q10: What are the MOQ, lead time, and warranty?**
A: **MOQ is 1 unit**; the factory keeps **goods in stock**; thickened export carton packing with tools and spare parts included; **1-year warranty** on the whole machine (free repair or parts replacement for non-man-made damage within the warranty period; international shipping costs borne by the customer), plus **lifetime maintenance service** after the warranty period.

### 6.1 Machine Operation Flow

```
① Setup
   Connect the air compressor and set the pressure to 0.4–0.6 MPa
   (air consumption approx. 0.55–1.1 m³/h)
   ※ The machine uses no electricity (full pneumatic)
        │
② Feed
   Pour the liquid to be filled into the material cylinder
        │
③ Set Volume
   Adjust the piston stroke → set the fill volume per cycle
   (stepless within the range); adjust the filling speed as needed
        │
④ Place Bottle
   Position the container directly under the filling head
        │
⑤ Trigger Filling
   Foot pedal (semi-automatic mode) or automatic timer (automatic mode)
        │
⑥ Filled (accuracy ±1%)
   Bottom-close positive-shutoff nozzle → no dripping, no stringing
        │
⑦ Repeat
   Repeat steps ④–⑥ at 10–40 cycles/min
        │
⑧ Clean
   Shut off the air supply; disassemble and wash the
   material cylinder, piston, and filling head.
   After cleaning, the machine is ready for a different product.
```
