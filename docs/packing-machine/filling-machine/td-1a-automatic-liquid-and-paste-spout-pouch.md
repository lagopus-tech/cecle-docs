## Core Abstract

The TD-1A is an **automatic liquid and paste spout pouch filling and capping machine** built by Wenzhou T&D Packing Machinery Factory. It is designed for stand up pouches, spout pouches, doypack bags and other flexible packages with a spout, and completes **both filling and capping in a single machine**: once the product is filled into the pouch, the machine automatically screws the cap onto the spout. No separate capping machine and no manual cap tightening are needed - the pouch is sealed in one step. The machine combines **Airtac (Taiwan) pneumatic components with electrical control**: pneumatics handle the filling action (piston dosing, valve opening and closing) while the electrical side handles control (timer, automatic cycle). The purely pneumatic actuating section does not rely on electric drive, giving a **safety explosion-proof** structure with simple operation and high filling accuracy. **The number of filling heads is customizable to the required output** (one filling head as standard, two filling heads available). Filling volume is adjustable from 100–1000ml at 800–1000 pouches/hour with accuracy ≤1%; **piston dosing plus a bottom close positive shutoff nozzle** with an optional 3–12mm nozzle diameter eliminates dripping. Product contact parts are **food grade 304 stainless steel** (316 optional), and sealing uses **food grade silicone O-rings rated to 200℃** (rubber sealing rings available for corrosive products), meeting food safety requirements. It handles free-flowing products - water, oil, juice (no particles), liquid detergent, sauce, wine, liquid cream and more - one machine for both liquids and pastes. The machine weighs 900kg, ships in a single case measuring 1700×1100×1920mm. MOQ 1 set, T/T 100%, delivery within 10 days; one-year machine warranty plus lifetime maintenance.

## 6. Workflow

```
Pouch loading (manual hanging / automatic feeding)
   │
   ▼
① Filling head pushes forward and descends
   Specially configured drive → filling head lowers into the pouch mouth / to the pouch bottom
   │
   ▼
② Piston dosing and filling
   The piston cylinder delivers the set volume of 100–1000ml
   The bottom close positive shutoff nozzle closes in sync: no dripping, no stringing
   │
   ▼
③ Filling head rises and resets
   Rises automatically after filling; the pouch moves to the capping station
   │
   ▼
④ Automatic capping
   Cap fed to the spout automatically → capping head descends and aligns → cap tightened at the set torque
   │
   ▼
⑤ Finished pouch discharge
   Discharged automatically after capping; moves on to coding / labeling / cartoning
```

**Operating notes:**
1. Start-up: connect the 220V power supply and the 0.5–0.7MPa air supply, check the oil level and pressure of the air service unit
2. Parameter setting: set the filling volume (steplessly adjustable 100–1000ml), cycle speed and capping torque on the control panel; test-fill 3–5 pouches to calibrate accuracy and cap tightness
3. Changeover procedure: when changing pouch types, adjust the filling head height and push stroke → select the right nozzle diameter for the product (3–12mm) → adjust the capping head height and torque to the cap type → re-calibrate the filling volume
4. Material change procedure: clean the hopper, piston and pipeline; always clean thoroughly after filling pastes to avoid residue hardening
5. Routine maintenance: clean the filling head and piston assembly every shift; check silicone O-rings regularly for ageing (rated to 200℃; rubber sealing rings recommended for corrosive products); drain the air service unit regularly
