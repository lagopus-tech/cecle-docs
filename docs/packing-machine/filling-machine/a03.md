## VI. Workflow
1.  **Assembly**: Mount the hopper onto the top of the machine, ensuring the sealing ring is properly positioned.
2.  **Set Volume**: Adjust the screw rod at the bottom of the machine to lock in the desired filling volume (2.5–50 ml).
3.  **Load Material**: Pour the product into the hopper.
4.  **Purge/Test Fill**: Press the handle down a few times to purge air from the pump cylinder and verify stable dispensing.
5.  **Batch Production**: Place bottles under the nozzle, press the handle down to complete filling, and release for the handle to automatically spring back.
6.  **Cleaning**: After production, pour warm water or cleaning solution into the hopper and repeatedly press the handle to flush the interior pump system clean.
