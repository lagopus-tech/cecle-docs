### 4.2 FAQ

**Q1. What is the difference between the DF8 series and the DF4 series? Which one is right for me?**

A. The biggest difference is the **drive mode**: DF8 is **full pneumatic drive**, no electric motor on the main body; DF4 is **pneumatic operation + electric control (electric-pneumatic integrated)**, with an electronic control module handling timing and counting. DF4 has 4 nozzles as standard (vs. DF8's 2 nozzles), and DF4 reaches ≤0.5% accuracy (vs. DF8's ±1%). If you want **pure pneumatic, intrinsically safe, low-maintenance** — choose DF8. If you want **higher accuracy, 4 nozzles, more flexible control** — choose DF4.

**Q2. Does the DF8 series require an air compressor?**

A. Yes. The DF8 series is full pneumatic drive and **must be used with an air compressor**. Air pressure 0.4–0.6 MPa, air consumption 100 L/min. A general 1.5–2.2 kW / 100–150 L silent oil-free air compressor is sufficient.

**Q3. Why does only the DF8-1000 specify a voltage?**

A. The DF8 series is full pneumatic, so the main body has no motor and requires no power supply. The DF8-1000 model's pneumatic circuit uses an **electric control valve**, which requires 220 V 50–60 Hz. The other five models are fully pneumatic and voltage-free.

**Q4. Can the DF8 series fill high-viscosity pastes?**

A. The DF8 series is **piston-type volumetric filling** designed for **free-flowing liquids** (water, juice, oil, liquid detergent, etc.). For high-viscosity pastes (cream, ointment, ketchup), please consult our **paste filling machine** category — those use a different filling principle (auger / pressure) and are not in the DF8 scope of application.

**Q5. Does the filling accuracy of ±1% include the pouch itself?**

A. ±1% refers to **filling volume accuracy of the machine itself**, not including the pouch tolerance, pouch expansion, or operator handling. If you require higher overall accuracy, please use a calibrated sample pouch for verification and refer to DF4 (≤0.5%) or contact the factory for higher-accuracy customization.

**Q6. Can the filling speed 10–40 times/min be reached?**

A. 10–40 times/min is the factory-spec speed range. Actual speed depends on **liquid viscosity, pouch size, operator proficiency, and air source stability**. For high-viscosity liquids or large pouches, the speed will be lower than the maximum value.

**Q7. What pouch types does DF8 support?**

A. DF8 supports **stand-up pouch, spout pouch, Doypack, and regular flexible pouch with pre-made spout**. Switching pouch types only requires changing the matching pouch clamp / fixture; the main machine does not need to be changed.

**Q8. What is the minimum order quantity? What is the delivery time?**

A. **To be confirmed with factory.** Please refer to the latest quotation for MOQ, payment terms, and lead time.

**Q9. What is the warranty period? Are consumables included?**

A. **To be confirmed with factory.** Standard packaging machinery industry practice is a 1-year whole-machine warranty + lifetime maintenance, but please confirm with the factory for the specific terms.

**Q10. Can the material be upgraded to 316 stainless steel?**

A. The standard material for the DF8 series is 201/304 stainless steel. **316 stainless steel upgrade is to be confirmed with the factory.** 316 is more expensive, typically used for highly corrosive liquids or pharmaceutical applications.

## 6. Workflow

### 6.1 Operator Workflow (Per-Shift)

1. **Air source check** — confirm the air compressor is on, air pressure is stable in 0.4–0.6 MPa, air consumption ≥ 100 L/min
2. **Pouch preparation** — pre-open the stand-up pouch / spout pouch spout, arrange the pouches in the matching clamp / tray
3. **Machine startup** — connect the air source, switch on the pneumatic circuit, set the semi-auto / automatic mode
4. **Volume adjustment** — adjust the piston stroke according to the pouch spec to set the target filling volume
5. **Nozzle diameter selection** — replace the matching nozzle diameter according to liquid viscosity and pouch-mouth size
6. **Trial fill** — perform 3–5 trial fills, weigh and verify the filling volume is within the ±1% target
7. **Production run** — start formal production; the operator inserts the pouch, triggers filling (foot pedal or auto timer), and removes the filled pouch
8. **Mid-shift check** — every 30–60 minutes, re-weigh to ensure filling accuracy has not drifted; clean the nozzle if dripping is observed
9. **End-of-shift cleanup** — flush the contact parts with clean water (for food applications), wipe the machine body, shut off the air source

### 6.2 Buyer Decision Workflow (Reference)

1. **Confirm application** — pouch type (stand-up pouch / spout pouch / Doypack), capacity range, liquid type (water / juice / oil / detergent), daily output target
2. **Select capacity tier** — match against the 6 capacity tiers in §3.2 (DF8-100 to DF8-5000)
3. **Confirm drive mode preference** — full pneumatic (DF8) vs. electric-pneumatic (DF4); if 4 nozzles / ≤0.5% accuracy is required, choose DF4
4. **Request quotation** — provide model, capacity tier, optional upgrades; request EXW / FOB / CIF quote
5. **Confirm plug & voltage** — for DF8-1000, confirm 220 V 50–60 Hz plug standard (Chinese / European / US / UK); the rest of the series is voltage-free
6. **Confirm shipment details** — packing method, fumigation / wooden crate, destination port, trade terms
7. **Place order & pay deposit** — refer to the latest quotation for MOQ and payment terms
8. **Production & inspection** — factory production, buyer-side video inspection or third-party inspection (recommended)
9. **Ship & clear customs** — EXW Wenzhou buyer self-pick-up, or FOB / CIF shipping arranged
10. **On-site installation & training** — buyer-side on-site installation and commissioning; video remote training is supported (recommended to confirm with factory)
