**Model**: FF5-1000 (series also includes FF5-100 / FF5-300 / FF5-500)

## Overview

The FF5 electric vertical liquid and paste filling machine is an electric piston-type semi-automatic filler produced by Wenzhou T&D Packing Machinery Factory. This document focuses on the FF5-1000 model (100–1000 mL filling range), which is powered by 220 V / 50–60 Hz electricity with a rated power of 500 W. The machine frame is built from 201/304 stainless steel, and product-contact parts are available in 304 or 316 stainless steel. It can fill low- to high-viscosity fluids including water, edible oil, juice, milk, yogurt, sauces, cream, honey, shampoo, butter, margarine, ketchup, jam, and detergent liquid, and is suitable for small-batch and pilot production in the food, daily chemical, and chemical industries. Unlike the pneumatic FF4, the FF5 only needs a power outlet and requires no air compressor.

### 4.2 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Does the FF5 series require electrical power or compressed air?**
A1: The FF5 is fully electric driven; it only needs a 220 V / 50–60 Hz power supply and does not require compressed air.

**Q2: Can it fill high-viscosity paste products?**
A2: Yes. The FF5 uses piston volumetric filling, suitable for water-like liquids to high-viscosity pastes such as ketchup, honey, butter, and face cream. For ultra-high viscosity materials, a heated/agitated hopper or the FF5B series is recommended.

**Q3: How is the filling volume adjusted?**
A3: The filling volume is adjusted by a hand wheel that changes the piston stroke; both filling volume and filling speed are steplessly adjustable.

**Q4: Does it support automatic filling?**
A4: Yes. The machine switches between foot-pedal mode and automatic timed mode, with automatic mode filling at preset intervals.

**Q5: What materials contact the product? Are they food-grade?**
A5: The standard configuration uses 304 stainless steel product-contact parts; 316 stainless steel is optional. All materials are food-safe. The sealing ring is a silicone O-ring rated to 200 °C; a corrosion-resistant rubber sealing ring is also available.

**Q6: Can one machine fill several different sizes?**
A6: Each model covers a specific filling range that can be covered by adjusting the piston stroke; for a large range span, switching to the matching model is recommended (for example, from FF5-100 to FF5-500).

**Q7: What is the packaging method and shipping size?**
A7: Each unit is packed in a plywood case measuring approximately 49 × 45 × 155 cm; weight varies by model. The case is suitable for LCL sea freight or air cargo.

**Q8: What is the warranty period?**
A8: The whole machine is covered by a one-year warranty, with lifetime repair service available thereafter.

### 6.1 Purchase & Delivery Workflow

6. **Shipment**: Ship under the confirmed terms, providing commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and other documents.
7. **After-Sales Support**: One-year warranty on the whole machine, with lifetime repair service.

### 6.2 Machine Operation Workflow

1. **Connect Power**: Connect the machine to a 220 V / 50–60 Hz power supply and ensure proper grounding.
2. **Load Material**: Open the hopper lid and pour in the material to be filled (pre-filtering impurities is recommended).
3. **Set Filling Volume**: Turn the hand wheel to set the piston stroke for the target filling volume.
4. **Set Filling Speed**: Set an appropriate filling speed and interval based on material flowability.
5. **Test Filling**: Test-fill with empty bottles, check the filling volume and sealing, and fine-tune until compliant.
6. **Batch Filling**: Place bottles under the filling nozzle, then press the foot pedal or switch to automatic mode for continuous filling.
7. **Cleaning**: After operation, remove the filling nozzle and feed pipe, clean them with water or solvent, and dry.
