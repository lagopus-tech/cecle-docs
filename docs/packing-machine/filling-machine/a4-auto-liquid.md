#### Model Recommendation

- Recommend the model based on the upper limit of the customer's bottle capacity.
- **Example**: For bottles from 200 ml to 800 ml, recommend the **A4-1000** for the best accuracy coverage — its 100–1000 ml range covers the full SKU spectrum without switching models.

## 6. Workflow

### Step-by-Step Operation

```
Step 1                Step 2              Step 3                Step 4              Step 5
Bottle Feeding  →   Counting & Stop  →   4-Head Synchronized  →   Anti-Drip    →   Bottle Discharge
                                    Filling                           Handling
```

#### Step 1 — Bottle Feeding

- **Equipment**: Infeed conveyor
- **Operation**: Empty bottles enter the machine on the conveyor automatically.
- **Key Point**: Ensure bottles are oriented correctly and spaced evenly on the belt before they reach the counting station.

#### Step 2 — Counting & Positioning

- **Equipment**: Inlet photoelectric sensor + bottle-stop cylinder
- **Operation**: The inlet photoelectric sensor counts bottles as they pass. Once 4 bottles are detected, the bottle-stop cylinder extends to lock the bottles in precise filling positions.
- **Key Point**: Sensor sensitivity must be calibrated to the bottle diameter; miscounts will trigger the no-bottle-no-fill protection.

#### Step 3 — 4-Head Synchronized Filling

- **Equipment**: 4 filling heads + servo/pneumatic piston cylinders
- **Operation**: The 4 filling heads descend simultaneously; the pistons push material to fill all 4 bottles in one synchronized cycle.
- **Key Point**: Filling volume is set on the touchscreen; Airtac pneumatic components ensure consistent pressure across all 4 heads for **≤ ±1% accuracy**.

#### Step 4 — Anti-Drip Handling

- **Equipment**: Positive shutoff valve + filling head lift cylinder
- **Operation**: At the end of each filling cycle, the positive shutoff valve closes and the filling heads lift, ensuring **zero dripping** and no residue on the bottle mouth.
- **Key Point**: The cut-off valve design eliminates dripping at high cycle speeds — a clear upgrade over spring-return valves.

#### Step 5 — Bottle Discharge

- **Equipment**: Outlet conveyor + next-stage equipment (capping / labeling)
- **Operation**: The bottle-stop cylinder retracts and the filled bottles move forward on the conveyor to the capping or labeling station.
- **Key Point**: For a fully-automatic line, sync the discharge conveyor speed with the downstream capping machine to avoid jams.

### Production-Line Recommendations

- **Operators**: 1 – 2 (1 monitoring the HMI, 1 doing visual quality check and bottle supply)
- **Cycle Time**: 10 – 40 cycles/min (10 – 30 cycles/min for A4-2500)
- **Floor Space**: Reserve a 3 × 2 m² area for the host machine, plus line space for conveyors and downstream equipment
- **Supporting Equipment**: Air compressor ≥ 0.8 MPa, ≥ 400 L/min
- **Recommended Line Layout**: Infeed conveyor → A4 filling machine → Capping machine → Labeling machine → Collection table
