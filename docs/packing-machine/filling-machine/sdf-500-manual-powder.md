The SDF-500 is a compact small powder filling machine built on stepper-motor control technology, electronic weighing technology, and microcomputer control. It delivers a filling range of 5 g to 5000 g (screw replacement required across the range) at a speed of about 20 fills per minute with accuracy within 1%. The operator manually presents bags, cans, or bottles while the machine automatically doses and corrects errors, and it can be matched with a spiral feeder for automatic feed control. It handles powders, superfine powders, and small granules — such as milk powder, seasonings, flour, white sugar, solid beverages, washing powder, and feed additives. One-year warranty with lifetime maintenance service.

## 1. Product Overview

### 1.1 Product Category

The SDF-500 is a **small manual powder filling machine with auger (spiral) dosing, electronic weighing, and microcomputer control**, designed for quantitative dosing and filling of free-flowing and non-free-flowing dry powders and small granules at small scale. The operator manually presents the container; the machine automatically measures and fills.

### 1.2 Application Scenarios

Typical materials and industries:

- **Food:** milk powder, compound seasoning, natural seasoning, white sugar, flour, solid beverage powder, monosodium glutamate (MSG)
- **Daily chemical:** washing powder, detergent powder
- **Agriculture & pharmaceutical:** pesticides, veterinary medicine, feed, enzyme preparations, additives
- **Container types:** bags, cans, bottles, and other rigid or flexible containers

### 1.3 Core Functions

- Automatic quantitative dosing and automatic filling
- Automatic error tracking and correction (compensates for material density and hopper level changes via photoelectric switch control)
- Automatic counting and parameter memory
- Automatic material cleaning function
- Spiral (auger) feeding control with photoelectric sensor measurement
- Keyboard-adjustable target weight, continuously adjustable across the range by changing blanking screws of different specifications
- Manual container handling (operator presents bag, can, or bottle)
- Optional spiral feeder for automatic feed control

## 6. Workflow

1. **Material feeding** — powder is fed to the hopper (manually, or automatically via the optional spiral feeder with photoelectric level control).
2. **Dosing setup** — the operator sets the target weight on the keyboard; the microcomputer control system drives the stepper motor and auger (blanking screw).
3. **Container presentation** — the operator manually places the bag, can, or bottle under the filling head.
4. **Auger filling & weighing** — spiral feeding control with photoelectric sensor measurement dispenses the set quantity; electronic weighing technology verifies each fill, and errors caused by material proportion or material level changes are automatically tracked and corrected.
5. **Counting & memory** — the system counts filled units and memorizes parameters for repeat runs; the cleaning function clears residual material.
6. **Sealing** — the clean filling mouth makes the bag easy to seal; the operator moves to the next container.
