## Overview
The F2 series is Wenzhou T&D's **semi-automatic horizontal electric-pneumatic liquid filling machine (Pneumatic & Electric Integrated)**. It covers 8 models (F2-50/100/300/500/1000/2500/3000/5000) with filling ranges from **5–50 ml** up to **500–5000 ml**. The machine uses an **electric-pneumatic integrated design — pneumatic actuation with electric control**: it requires 220V/50–60Hz power (approx. **100W**) plus an air compressor supply of **0.4–0.6 MPa**. It is fitted with **Airtac pneumatic components** and fills at **10–40 cycles/min**; the electric control keeps cycle timing stable with excellent batch-to-batch consistency. The frame is **201 stainless steel**, and all product-contact parts are **food-grade 304 stainless steel (316 stainless steel available on request)**. Piston-type volumetric filling delivers **±1% accuracy** with **bottom-close positive shutoff drip-free nozzles** (orifice 3–12 mm selectable). Two trigger modes — foot pedal or automatic timer — let the operator switch between semi-automatic and automatic operation. The filler comes with a single filling head as standard; **dual-head, 4-head or 6-head versions can be customized**. Suitable for free-flowing liquids such as water, edible oil, juice, milk, alcoholic beverages, liquid medicine and detergents.

### 4.2 FAQ

**Q1: Does this machine need electricity?**
A: **Yes.** The F2 series is an electric-pneumatic integrated unit: the electric control section requires a **220V / 50–60Hz** supply (power draw only ~**100W**), while the filling motion is powered by compressed air, so an **air compressor (0.4–0.6 MPa)** must also be connected.

**Q2: Do I need an air compressor?**
A: Yes. The machine uses pneumatic actuation with electric control, so it must be connected to an air compressor supplying **0.4–0.6 MPa**. An ordinary small piston-type compressor (with an air receiver/tank) is sufficient; one can also be added to the order.

**Q3: Is the machine explosion-proof?**
A: The F2 is an electric-pneumatic model (it contains electric control parts) and is **not intended as an explosion-proof unit**. If the workshop has explosion-proof requirements, or the customer needs to fill flammable liquids such as alcohol or solvents, please recommend the **F6 fully-pneumatic series** (no electricity at all; intrinsically safe, explosion-proof).

**Q4: What are the filling accuracy and speed?**
A: Accuracy is **within ±1%** (piston-type metering — stable, no drift); speed is **10–40 cycles/min**, and the filling speed is adjustable.

**Q5: What liquids can it fill? Can it handle high-viscosity liquids or pastes?**
A: It suits **free-flowing liquids**: water, edible oil, juice, milk, alcoholic beverages, liquid medicine, liquid detergents, etc. For high-viscosity products or pastes (sauces, creams, ointments) we recommend our paste filling machine series.

**Q6: Are the product-contact materials food grade?**
A: The frame is 201 stainless steel and **all product-contact parts are food-grade 304 stainless steel**, meeting food-safety requirements; **316 stainless steel can be customized** (greater acid/alkali and corrosion resistance — suited to pharmaceutical, chemical and corrosive products). Seals are food-grade silicone (rated 200 °C).

**Q7: Will it drip or string?**
A: No. The nozzle uses a **bottom-close positive shutoff** design — the discharge closes the moment filling ends, guaranteeing drip-free operation. Nozzle orifices can be selected within a 3–12 mm range.

**Q8: Is changing the fill volume troublesome?**
A: Not at all. Within the model's range you simply **adjust the piston stroke** to change the fill volume — no parts replacement needed; a changeover takes only a few minutes. Ideal for multi-specification, small-batch production.

**Q9: Can it run fully automatically? What if I need more output later?**
A: Yes. The machine supports **both foot-pedal (semi-automatic) and automatic timer (automatic) modes** and can be switched at any time. For higher output you can order a **dual (2-head), 4-head or 6-head** multi-head version (one cylinder per head) — no machine replacement needed.

**Q10: What are the MOQ, delivery time and warranty?**
A: **MOQ is 1 unit**; the factory keeps **goods in stock** for fast dispatch; export packing is a heavy-duty carton, one machine per case, with tools and spare parts included. The machine carries a **1-year warranty** (free repair or part replacement for non-human damage during the warranty period; international freight borne by the customer), with **lifetime maintenance service** after the warranty period.

### 6.1 Filling Operation Flow

```
① Setup
 Connect power 220V/50–60Hz (approx. 100W)
 Connect air compressor; regulate supply to 0.4–0.6 MPa
 │
② Feed
 Add the liquid to be filled into the hopper/tank
 │
③ Set Volume
 Adjust piston stroke → set the fill volume per cycle (stepless within range)
 Adjust filling speed if needed
 │
④ Place Bottle
 Position the container directly under the filling nozzle
 │
⑤ Trigger Filling
 Foot pedal (semi-automatic mode) or automatic timer (automatic mode)
 │
⑥ Filled (accuracy ±1%)
 Bottom-close positive shutoff nozzle → no dripping, no stringing
 │
⑦ Next Bottle — Repeat
 Repeat ④–⑥ at 10–40 cycles/min
 │
⑧ Cleaning & Shutdown
 Stop the machine; disconnect power and air;
 disassemble and rinse hopper/piston/filling nozzle;
 clean before switching to a different product
```
