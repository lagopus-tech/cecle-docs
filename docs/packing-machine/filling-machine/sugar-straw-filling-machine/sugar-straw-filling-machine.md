## VI. Workflow

1. **Automatic Straw Feeding**: Empty straws are neatly loaded and automatically fed into the machine's conveying station one by one.
2. **First-End Ultrasonic Sealing**: The machine automatically performs an ultrasonic seal on the bottom end of the straw to prepare for material filling.
3. **Quantitative Filling**: The precise filling system automatically meters and pours sugar, powder, or flavor granules into the open-ended straw.
4. **Second-End Ultrasonic Sealing**: Once filled, the machine automatically executes ultrasonic sealing on the top end of the straw, securely locking the contents inside.
5. **Finished Output**: Fully sealed candy-filled or sugar-filled straws are automatically discharged and outputted for collection and packaging.
