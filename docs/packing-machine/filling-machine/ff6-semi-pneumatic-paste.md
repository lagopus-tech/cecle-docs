## Overview

The FF6 series is Wenzhou T&D's **semi-automatic horizontal fully-pneumatic paste filling machine (All-Pneumatic / No Electricity)** — designed for customers with **explosion-proof duty**, **flammable product filling**, or **no on-site electricity**. It covers 8 models (FF6-50/100/300/500/1000/2500/3000/5000) with filling ranges from **5–50 ml** up to **500–5000 ml**. The machine uses a **fully-pneumatic architecture — pneumatic drive AND pneumatic control**: it needs **only compressed air (0.4–0.6 MPa)** and **no electrical power whatsoever**, so it is intrinsically safe for explosion-proof zones and ideal for **flammable, alcohol-based or solvent-based products** (alcohol-based cosmetics, perfumes, essential oils, solvent-based adhesives, paints, inks, alcohol tinctures, flammable lubricants, etc.). It is fitted with **Airtac pneumatic cylinders and air valves** and fills at **5–30 cycles/min** (paste-tuned — slower than liquid-only fillers because paste requires longer per-stroke settling). The frame is **201 stainless steel**, and all product-contact parts are **food-grade 304 stainless steel (316 stainless steel available on request)**. Piston-type volumetric filling delivers **±1% accuracy** with **bottom-close positive shutoff drip-free nozzles** (orifice 5–25 mm selectable, sized for paste flow). The feed system comes with a **30L hopper as standard** — hopper footprint **40 × 40 × 48 cm**, empty weight **5 kg** (compact enough for tabletop or line mounting); **40L, 60L or 80L** larger hoppers are optional (larger-hopper dimensions and weight to be confirmed with the factory). Because the machine has no electrics, the **stirring hopper** (pneumatic-driven) and **pressurized hopper** (compressed-air-assisted — actually ideal for fully-pneumatic machines) are natural matches; a **heating hopper** can be configured with steam / hot-water jacket or intrinsically-safe electric heating to suit explosion-proof duty. The single trigger mode is the **foot pedal** (fully-automatic timer mode is unavailable because there is no electric timer). The filler comes with a single filling head as standard; **dual-head, 4-head or 6-head versions can be customized**. The FF6 is the **cross-sell partner to the FF2 electric-pneumatic series**: offer FF2 to customers with stable power and non-flammable products; offer FF6 to customers without power, with explosion-proof duty, or filling flammable / alcohol-based / solvent-based products.

### 1.2 Applications

Designed for **paste and high-viscosity products** as the primary use case, with **free-flowing liquids (including flammable / alcohol-based / solvent-based liquids)** as a secondary capability — one machine, two product categories, **no electricity required**. Typical products and industries:

**Secondary — Free-Flowing Liquids (including flammable liquids):**

**Typical Customers:** paste / cosmetic / food producers filling flammable or alcohol-based products; workshops in **explosion-proof zones** (no electrics allowed); plants **without on-site power** (remote sites, mobile workshops, certain export regions with unstable grids); pharmaceutical and chemical producers filling flammable solvents; customers who prioritize the lowest possible running cost (no electricity bill at all); businesses filling both paste SKUs and liquid SKUs (including flammable SKUs) and wanting one machine for both.

### 1.3 Key Functions

- **Piston-type volumetric filling optimized for paste:** cylinder-driven piston with adjustable stroke — handles thick products (sauce, cream, ointment, solvent-based adhesive) without splashing or pulling apart; volume is steplessly adjustable within the model range; change specs without changing parts
- **Liquid-capable as a bonus (incl. flammable liquids):** switch from paste to liquid (including alcohol-based / solvent-based liquids) with a nozzle change and a piston-stroke adjustment; one machine covers both product categories with full explosion-proof safety
- **Fully-pneumatic drive and control — no electricity:** pneumatic actuation governed by pneumatic valves; no electrics anywhere on the machine; no fuses, no relays, no timers, no PLC — intrinsic safety by construction
- **Single trigger mode — foot pedal:** operator triggers each cycle with a foot pedal; **fully-automatic timer mode is not available** on the fully-pneumatic architecture (this is the trade-off vs. FF2's electric control: lose auto-timer, gain explosion-proof safety)
- **Drip-free filling:** bottom-close positive shutoff nozzles — discharge seals the moment filling ends; **no stringing, no dripping, no paste tail on bottle mouths** (critical for cosmetic, food and flammable-product applications)
- **Customizable filling heads:** single head as standard; **dual (2-head), 4-head or 6-head configurations available** for proportionally higher output
- **Adjustable fill volume and speed:** match different container formats (small jars, large buckets) and line paces
- **Flexible hopper / feed options:** standard **30L hopper**; optional **40L / 60L / 80L** larger hoppers reduce refill stops. Because the machine is fully pneumatic, the **stirring hopper** (pneumatic-driven) and the **pressurized hopper** (compressed-air-assisted feeding) are natural matches; a **heating hopper** can be fitted with a steam / hot-water jacket or intrinsically-safe electric heating for explosion-proof duty — match the hopper option to the product characteristics.

It runs on compressed air only (0.4–0.6 MPa). Because there are no electrics to spark, the machine is intrinsically safe and **suited to explosion-proof zones and flammable / alcohol-based / solvent-based product filling** — opening markets that the FF2 electric-pneumatic series simply cannot serve.

6. **Airtac pneumatic cylinders and air valves.** Taiwan Airtac cylinders and pneumatic valves deliver stable action and long service life. Spare parts are available worldwide and long-term maintenance cost stays low. No electrics means no electric spare parts, no relay boards, no sensor failures.
7. **±1% filling accuracy + drip-free nozzles.** Piston metering is stable and reliable for paste (no under/over-fill); bottom-close positive shutoff nozzles eliminate dripping, stringing and paste tailing, keeping bottle mouths and the workbench clean — essential for cosmetic jars, food containers, and especially for flammable products where residue on a bottle mouth is a fire hazard.

9. **8 models cover 5–5000 ml; changeover at zero cost.** Adjust the piston stroke to change the fill volume — no parts replacement needed; a spec change takes just 2–3 minutes. Covers everything from sample-size cosmetic jars to large buckets.
10. **201 stainless steel frame + food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts; 316 available.** The all-stainless frame and food-grade product-contact parts meet food-safety requirements as standard (304); **product-contact parts can be customized in 316 stainless steel** for stronger acid/alkali and corrosion resistance — suited to pharmaceutical, chemical and corrosive products. 11. **Flexible hopper — 30L standard, 40L/60L/80L optional, stirring/pressurized/heating configurable.** Standard **30L** hopper; **40L, 60L or 80L** larger sizes cut refill stops on high-volume lines. For hard-to-handle products, fit a **stirring hopper** (pneumatic-driven, no electrics), a **pressurized hopper** (compressed-air-assisted feeding — native fit) or a **heating hopper** (steam / hot-water / intrinsically-safe electric heating for explosion-proof duty) — match the hopper to the product.
12. **Horizontal, compact and lightweight.** The horizontal body has a small footprint and weighs only 22–32 kg (net), so it fits on a tabletop, workbench or in an existing line and is easy to move.
13. **Food-grade silicone seals (rated 200 °C).** Food-safe silicone O-rings as standard; corrosion-resistant rubber seals can be fitted when filling corrosive or sticky products.
14. **Multi-head expandable (2-head / 4-head / 6-head).** Start with a single head; when volume grows, upgrade to a **dual/4-head/6-head** machine (one pneumatic cylinder per head) — no equipment replacement, no wasted investment.
15. **Cross-sell partner to the FF2.** The FF2 (electric-pneumatic, with auto-timer) and the FF6 (fully-pneumatic, no electricity, explosion-proof) form a complementary pair: offer FF2 to customers with stable power and non-flammable products; offer FF6 to customers without power, with explosion-proof duty, or filling flammable / alcohol-based / solvent-based products. ### 4.2 FAQ

**Q1: Does this machine need electricity?**
A: **No.** The FF6 series is a **fully-pneumatic unit (All-Pneumatic Drive and Control)**: the machine contains no electric components whatsoever — no motors, no solenoids, no timers, no relays, no PLC. It runs entirely on compressed air and requires only an **air compressor (0.4–0.6 MPa)**. **There is no electricity involved, no electricity bill, and no ignition source — making it intrinsically safe for explosion-proof duty.**

**Q2: Do I need an air compressor?**
A: Yes. The FF6 is fully pneumatic, so it must be connected to an air compressor supplying **0.4–0.6 MPa**. An ordinary small piston-type compressor (with an air receiver/tank) is sufficient; one can also be added to the order.

**Q3: Is the machine explosion-proof? Can it fill alcohol-based or solvent-based products?**
A: **Yes — this is the FF6's biggest selling point.** The FF6 contains no electric components, so there is no ignition source on the machine. It is **intrinsically safe / explosion-proof by construction** and is **suited to filling flammable, alcohol-based or solvent-based products** (alcohol-based hand sanitizer, perfume, essential oil, alcohol tincture, solvent-based adhesive, paint, ink, thinner, flammable lubricant, etc.) and to operation in explosion-proof zones. If the customer needs to fill flammable products, the FF6 is the only option in this product line.

**Q4: What are the filling accuracy and speed?**
A: Accuracy is **within ±1%** (piston-type metering — stable, no drift); speed is **5–30 cycles/min** (paste-tuned; slower than liquid-only fillers because paste requires longer per-stroke settling), and the filling speed is adjustable.

**Q5: Can it fill both paste and liquids? Can it handle flammable products?**
A: **Yes — the FF6 is optimized for paste and high-viscosity products, and also fills free-flowing liquids, including flammable / alcohol-based / solvent-based products in both categories.** Primary use: sauce, ketchup, jam, honey, peanut butter, chocolate paste, mustard, mayonnaise; flammable or alcohol-based cosmetic creams and gels; ointments; flammable or solvent-based adhesives, paints, inks; flammable lubricants. Secondary use: alcohol-based liquids (perfume, essential oil, alcohol tincture, sanitizer base liquid), solvents, plus water, juice, milk, edible oil. **One machine covers both paste and liquid categories with full explosion-proof safety — change the nozzle, adjust the piston stroke, switch product categories in minutes.**

**Q6: Can it run fully automatically with an electric timer?**
A: **No.** Because the FF6 is fully pneumatic with no electric components, **the automatic electric-timer mode is not available** — the machine supports **foot-pedal triggered filling only**, i.e. **semi-automatic mode**. Each cycle is initiated by the operator pressing the foot pedal. If the customer needs fully-automatic filling cycles with no operator, they should choose the **FF2 electric-pneumatic series** (which adds the electric timer) — provided the workshop is not in an explosion-proof zone and the product is not flammable.

**Q7: How is the FF6 different from the FF2 paste filler?**
A: Both are piston-type paste fillers covering 5–5000 ml with the same hopper options and same nozzle range. The differences:

- **FF2** is **electric-pneumatic integrated** — needs 220V/50–60Hz (~100W) **plus** air (0.4–0.6 MPa). Electric control keeps cycle timing steady and enables **automatic timer mode**. Better for long continuous runs and stable cycle rhythm. **Not** suited to explosion-proof zones or flammable products.
- **FF6** is **fully pneumatic** — needs **only** air (0.4–0.6 MPa); **no electricity whatsoever**. Intrinsically safe for **explosion-proof duty and flammable / alcohol-based / solvent-based products**. Trade-off: loses the auto-timer mode (foot pedal only) and the electric-controlled cycle stability of the FF2.

Choose **FF2** when: workshop has stable power, product is non-flammable, customer wants the steadiest cycles and auto-timer mode. Choose **FF6** when: workshop has no power, is in an explosion-proof zone, or the product is flammable / alcohol-based / solvent-based.

**Q8: Are the product-contact materials food grade?**
A: The frame is 201 stainless steel and **all product-contact parts are food-grade 304 stainless steel**, meeting food-safety requirements; **316 stainless steel can be customized** (greater acid/alkali and corrosion resistance — suited to pharmaceutical, chemical and corrosive products). Seals are food-grade silicone (rated 200 °C).

**Q9: Will it drip, string or paste-tail?**
A: No. The nozzle uses a **bottom-close positive shutoff** design — the discharge closes the moment filling ends, guaranteeing drip-free operation. **For paste applications this is critical:** no stringing, no paste tail, no residue on the bottle mouth. **For flammable products this is also a fire-safety benefit** — no flammable residue on the bottle mouth. Nozzle orifices can be selected within a 5–25 mm range (sized for paste flow).

**Q10: Is changing the fill volume or switching between paste and liquid troublesome?**
A: Not at all. Within the model's range you simply **adjust the piston stroke** to change the fill volume — no parts replacement needed; a changeover takes only a few minutes. **Switching between paste and liquid** is also simple: change the nozzle (paste nozzle vs. liquid nozzle) and adjust the piston stroke and speed — a few minutes, no tools required.

**Q11: Can the hopper be customized for hard-to-feed pastes? What about the heating hopper on an explosion-proof machine?**
A: Yes. The hopper comes standard at **30L**, with **40L, 60L or 80L** larger options for high-volume lines. Because the FF6 is fully pneumatic, the **pressurized hopper** (compressed-air-assisted feeding) and the **stirring hopper** (pneumatic-driven) are natural fits and need no extra power source. The **heating hopper** can be configured with a **steam / hot-water jacket** or **intrinsically-safe electric heating** to maintain explosion-proof duty — please confirm the exact heating method with the factory when ordering.

**Q12: What are the MOQ, delivery time and warranty?**
A: **MOQ is 1 unit**; the factory keeps **goods in stock** for fast dispatch; export packing is a heavy-duty carton, one machine per case, with tools and spare parts included. The machine carries a **1-year warranty** (free repair or part replacement for non-human damage during the warranty period; international freight borne by the customer), with **lifetime maintenance service** after the warranty period.

## 6. Workflow

### 6.1 Filling Operation Flow

```
① Setup
 Connect air compressor; regulate supply to 0.4–0.6 MPa
 (NO electrical power connection needed)
 │
② Feed
 Add the paste (or liquid — incl. flammable / alcohol-based / solvent-based
 products) into the hopper (standard 30L; 40/60/80L optional)
 (stirring / pressurized / heating hopper for products that need it)
 │
③ Set Volume
 Adjust piston stroke → set the fill volume per cycle (stepless within range)
 Adjust filling speed if needed (paste: typically 5–30 cycles/min)
 │
④ Place Container
 Position the jar / bottle / bucket directly under the filling nozzle
 (paste: use paste nozzle 5–25 mm; liquid: switch to liquid nozzle 5–25 mm)
 │
⑤ Trigger Filling
 Foot pedal (semi-automatic mode — the only mode on fully-pneumatic FF6)
 │
⑥ Filled (accuracy ±1%)
 Bottom-close positive shutoff nozzle → no dripping, no stringing,
 no paste tail (and no flammable residue on bottle mouths)
 │
⑦ Next Container — Repeat
 Repeat ④–⑥ at 5–30 cycles/min (paste)
 │
⑧ Cleaning & Shutdown
 Stop the machine; disconnect air;
 disassemble and rinse hopper / piston / filling nozzle;
 clean before switching to a different product
```
