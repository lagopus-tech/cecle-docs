## core Abstract
The **FF3 series** is Wenzhou T&D's **semi-automatic horizontal paste filling machine with an integrated stainless steel stand** — the floor-standing, line-ready version of the FF2/FF6 platform. It covers 8 models (FF3-50/100/300/500/1000/2500/3000/5000) with filling ranges from **5–50 ml** up to **500–5000 ml**. **Two drive versions are available, selected at order time:** the **electric-pneumatic version** (220V/50–60Hz, ~100W + 0.4–0.6 MPa air; electric timer enables automatic filling mode — same architecture as the FF2) or the **fully-pneumatic version** (compressed air only, no electricity, intrinsically safe and explosion-proof — same architecture as the FF6, for flammable / alcohol-based / solvent-based products and explosion-proof zones). Both versions share the same core filling specs: **Airtac pneumatic components**, piston-type volumetric filling with **±1% accuracy**, **bottom-close positive shutoff drip-free nozzles** (orifice **5–25 mm** selectable), filling speed **5–30 cycles/min**, standard **30L hopper** (same hopper dimensions and weight as FF2/FF6; 40L/60L/80L optional; heating/stirring/pressurized hoppers optional), 201 stainless steel frame with food-grade **304 stainless steel contact parts (316 optional)**. Filling heads are fully configurable: **single head, dual head, 4-head or multi-head**. **The FF3's single key difference vs. FF2/FF6 is the integrated 201 stainless steel floor stand — dimensions 80 × 46 × 24 cm, weight 11 kg** (with adjustable leveling feet and a side container tray): the machine arrives mounted at floor-standing working height, drop-in for production lines with no separate bench required. The machine body, hopper volume and weights are otherwise identical to the FF2/FF6. Suitable for cosmetics (creams, lotions, gels), food (sauce, jam, honey, ketchup, peanut butter), daily chemicals, pharmaceuticals (ointments), chemicals (lubricants, adhesives) and free-flowing liquids — with the fully-pneumatic version additionally covering flammable and explosion-proof applications.

### 4.2 FAQ

**Q1: Does this machine need electricity?**
A: **It depends on the version you order.** The FF3 is available in two drive versions: the **electric-pneumatic version** requires a **220V / 50–60Hz** supply (power draw only ~**100W**) plus an air compressor (0.4–0.6 MPa); the **fully-pneumatic version** contains no electric components whatsoever and requires **no electricity at all** — only an air compressor (0.4–0.6 MPa).

**Q2: Do I need an air compressor?**
A: Yes — **both versions** use pneumatic actuation and must be connected to an air compressor supplying **0.4–0.6 MPa**. An ordinary small piston-type compressor (with an air receiver/tank) is sufficient; one can also be added to the order. (Choosing the fully-pneumatic version removes the electricity requirement, not the compressed-air requirement.)

**Q3: Is the machine explosion-proof?**
A: **The fully-pneumatic FF3 version is** — it contains no electric components, so there is no ignition source; it is intrinsically safe and suited to explosion-proof zones and to filling flammable, alcohol-based or solvent-based products (hand sanitizer gel, perfume, alcohol tinctures, solvent-based adhesives, paints, inks). **The electric-pneumatic version is not intended for explosion-proof duty** — order the fully-pneumatic version for those applications.

**Q4: What are the filling accuracy and speed?**
A: Accuracy is **within ±1%** (piston-type metering — stable, no drift); speed is **5–30 cycles/min** (paste-tuned; slower than liquid-only fillers because paste requires longer per-stroke settling), and the filling speed is adjustable.

**Q5: Can it fill both paste and liquids? What products can it handle?**
A: **Yes — the FF3 is optimized for paste and high-viscosity products, and also fills free-flowing liquids** (on the fully-pneumatic version, including flammable / alcohol-based / solvent-based liquids). Primary use: sauce, ketchup, jam, honey, peanut butter, chocolate paste, mustard, mayonnaise; face cream, body lotion, hand cream, shampoo (thick), shower gel, hair conditioner; ointments; detergents (thick); lubricants and adhesives. Secondary use: water, juice, milk, edible oil, liquid seasonings, liquid medicine, liquid detergents. **One machine covers both paste and liquid categories — change the nozzle, adjust the piston stroke, switch product categories in minutes.**

**Q6: How is the FF3 different from the FF2 and FF6?**
A: The **FF3 shares its machine body, hopper, filling specifications and weights with the FF2 and FF6 series** — same piston metering (±1%), same 5–30 cycles/min paste-tuned speed, same 8-model lineup covering 5–5000 ml, same 30L standard hopper (40L/60L/80L optional; heating/stirring/pressurized options), same 201 SS frame + 304 SS contact parts (316 optional), same nozzle/drip-free/seal configuration. **The FF3's single difference: it ships on an integrated 201 stainless steel floor stand — dimensions 80 × 46 × 24 cm, weight 11 kg** — with adjustable leveling feet and a side container tray, turning the benchtop FF2/FF6 into a ready-to-use floor-standing production station. The FF3 can be ordered with either drive: electric-pneumatic (FF2 architecture, with auto-timer mode) or fully-pneumatic (FF6 architecture, explosion-proof). **Choose FF3** for line-ready floor-standing operation; **choose FF2/FF6** if you already have your own bench/table and want a tabletop unit.

**Q7: What are the stand's specifications?**
A: The FF3's integrated stand measures **80 × 46 × 24 cm and weighs 11 kg**, and provides:

- **201 stainless steel construction** — matches the machine's corrosion-resistance profile; easy to wipe clean.
- **Adjustable leveling feet** — fine-tune the height and level the machine on slightly uneven workshop floors; important for paste lines where the cylinder and nozzle must be aligned vertically over the container mouth.
- **Built-in side-mounted container tray/platform** — a stable landing spot right beside the filling nozzle; the operator slides the jar onto the tray, slides it under the nozzle to fill, then slides it back. **Reduces spillage and speeds up small-format loading.**
- **Floor-standing working height** — operator fills while standing; no need to procure or build a separate bench; one-shipment delivery.

**Q8: Are the product-contact materials food grade?**
A: The frame is 201 stainless steel and **all product-contact parts are food-grade 304 stainless steel**, meeting food-safety requirements; **316 stainless steel can be customized** (greater acid/alkali and corrosion resistance — suited to pharmaceutical, chemical and corrosive products). Seals are food-grade silicone (rated 200 °C). The FF3's integrated stand and tray are also 201 stainless steel.

**Q9: Will it drip, string or paste-tail?**
A: No. The nozzle uses a **bottom-close positive shutoff** design — the discharge closes the moment filling ends, guaranteeing drip-free operation. **For paste applications this is critical:** no stringing, no paste tail, no residue on the bottle mouth — keeping cosmetic jars, food containers and pharma packaging clean (on flammable products this is also a fire-safety benefit). Nozzle orifices can be selected within a 5–25 mm range (sized for paste flow).

```
① Setup
   Position the FF3 on the production line floor.

   Electric-pneumatic version: connect power 220V/50–60Hz (approx. 100W)
                                + connect air compressor, regulate to 0.4–0.6 MPa

        │

        │
④ Place Container
   Set the jar / bottle on the side-mounted tray, slide it under the filling nozzle
   (paste: use paste nozzle 5–25 mm; liquid: switch to liquid nozzle)
        │
⑤ Trigger Filling
   Foot pedal (both versions, semi-automatic mode)
   Electric-pneumatic version: or automatic timer (automatic mode)
        │
⑥ Filled (accuracy ±1%)
   Bottom-close positive shutoff nozzle → no dripping, no stringing, no paste tail
        │
⑦ Next Container — Repeat
   Slide the filled container back to the tray, repeat ④–⑥ at 5–30 cycles/min (paste)
        │
⑧ Cleaning & Shutdown
   Stop the machine; disconnect air (and power on the electric-pneumatic version);
   disassemble and rinse hopper / piston / filling nozzle;
   clean before switching to a different product
```
