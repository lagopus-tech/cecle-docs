## Overview

The ADF-500 automatic powder filling machine is a fully automatic auger-type dosing and filling machine built on stepper-motor control, electronic weighing technology, and microcomputer control. It delivers a filling range of 5 g to 5000 g (screw replacement required across the range) at speeds of 1800–4000 fills per hour with accuracy within 1%. It handles powders, superfine powders, and small granules — such as milk powder, seasonings, flour, white sugar, solid beverages, washing powder, and feed additives — into bags, cans, bottles, and other containers. One-year warranty with lifetime maintenance service.

The ADF-500 is a **fully automatic auger (spiral) powder filling machine** with electronic weighing and microcomputer control, designed for quantitative dosing and filling of free-flowing and non-free-flowing dry powders and small granules.

### 1.2 Application Scenarios

Typical materials and industries:

- **Food:** milk powder, compound seasoning, natural seasoning, white sugar, flour, solid beverage powder, monosodium glutamate (MSG)
- **Daily chemical:** washing powder, detergent powder

- **Container types:** bags, cans, bottles, and other rigid or flexible containers

### 1.3 Core Functions

- Automatic quantitative dosing and automatic filling
- Automatic error tracking and correction (compensates for material density and hopper level changes via photoelectric switch control)
- Automatic counting and parameter memory
- Automatic material cleaning function
- Spiral (auger) feeding control with photoelectric sensor measurement
- Keyboard-adjustable target weight, continuously adjustable across the range by changing blanking screws of different specifications
- Optional spiral feeder for automatic feed control

## 6. Workflow

1. **Material feeding** — powder is fed to the hopper (manually, or automatically via the optional spiral feeder with photoelectric level control).
2. **Dosing setup** — the operator sets the target weight on the keyboard; the microcomputer control system drives the stepper motor and auger (blanking screw).
3. **Auger filling** — spiral feeding control with photoelectric sensor measurement dispenses the set quantity of powder into the container (bag, can, or bottle).
4. **Weighing & auto correction** — electronic weighing technology verifies each fill; errors caused by material proportion or material level changes are automatically tracked and corrected.
5. **Counting & memory** — the system counts filled units and memorizes parameters for repeat runs; the cleaning function clears residual material.
6. **Container handling & sealing** — the operator feeds bags manually; the clean filling mouth makes sealing easy.
