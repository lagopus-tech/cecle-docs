The DF4-1000, manufactured by Wenzhou T&D Packing Machinery Factory, is a **semi-automatic electric-pneumatic stand-up and spout pouch liquid filling machine (spout pouch filling machine)** designed specifically for filling liquids into spouted flexible packaging such as stand-up pouches, spout pouches, and Doypacks. The machine uses a hybrid scheme of **Taiwan Airtac pneumatic components for actuation plus electric control** — the pneumatic side handles the filling action (piston push and valve open/close), while the electric side handles control (timer, foot-pedal signal, semi-auto/automatic switching). 2. **Piston-type volumetric filling** — one cylinder per head, filling volume determined by piston stroke, adjustable within 20–1000 ml
3. **Four simultaneous filling nozzles** — 4 anti-drip positive shut-off nozzles as standard; 1 head / 2 head / 4 head selectable to order; nozzle diameter 3–12 mm optional

### 4.2 FAQ

**Q1: What liquids is the DF4-1000 suitable for?**
Free-flowing liquids — water, oil, juice (no particles), liquid detergent, sauce, wine, liquid cream, etc. Liquids with particles, high-viscosity pastes, and strongly corrosive liquids are not suitable (strongly corrosive liquids require rubber sealing rings + a 316 stainless steel configuration; please confirm with Andrea before ordering).

**Q2: Does the machine need a power supply? Is the control mode electric or pneumatic?**
The machine **requires a 220 V 50/60 Hz power supply** and **must be connected to an air compressor**. The DF4 series uses a hybrid **pneumatic operation + electric control** scheme: the air source drives the piston and valves, while the electronic control module handles timing, counting, and foot-pedal signals. It offers more stable timing and finer adjustment than purely pneumatic models, and is more resistant to rough use and longer-lasting than purely electric models.

**Q3: How many nozzles as standard? Can I choose 1 or 2?**
**4 nozzles as standard.** **1 head / 2 head / 4 head** are available to order — fewer heads means a lower unit price, and multi-nozzle models deliver a clear capacity boost for small pouches.

**Q4: How to choose the nozzle diameter?**
**Optional within 3–12 mm.** A 6 mm nozzle head is common for general liquids (such as water, oil, juice, detergent); 8 mm for thin sauces, wine, and cosmetics; 10–12 mm for thicker liquids. Confirm the pouch-mouth size and liquid viscosity before ordering, and Andrea will recommend the diameter.

**Q5: How accurate is the filling? What can it achieve?**
**Filling accuracy ≤ 0.5%**, typical for piston-type volumetric models. We recommend weighing 2–3 pouches for calibration before mass production.

**Q6: Will the nozzles drip or leave liquid on the pouch mouth?**
No. The standard **bottom anti-drip positive shut-off nozzles** — after filling, a cylinder drives a positive shut-off at the nozzle bottom, leaving no residual drops or clinging liquid. The pouch mouth stays clean and can proceed directly to the next process.

**Q7: What material are the product-contact parts? What hygiene grade?**
Contact parts are **food-grade 304 stainless steel** (316 optional), with a stainless steel valve system; seals are **food-grade silicone O-rings rated to 200°C**. The whole machine is food-safety compliant and can be used directly for food pouch filling.

**Q8: Is it troublesome to change pouch specifications? Do I need to change parts?**
Not at all. The machine supports switching between **stand-up pouch, spout pouch, Doypack, and regular flexible pouch bag** on the same machine; changing pouch type usually only requires changing the corresponding fixture / pouch clamp, and the filling volume is adjusted via the piston stroke.

**Q9: Is cleaning troublesome when changing liquids?**
Not at all. Tubing uses quick-fit connections and the contact parts are easy to disassemble; regular liquids can be rinsed with clean water or hot water; for oils and sauces, rinse with hot water first and then with clean water. **For strongly corrosive liquids, soak with the appropriate cleaning agent before rinsing when changing products.**

## 6. Workflow

### 6.1 Pouch Filling Process

1. **Connect air and power** — confirm the 220 V 50/60 Hz power supply is in place; connect the air compressor and set the air pressure to the standard range (**MPa to be confirmed**); check that the pressure gauge, filter, and solenoid valve signals are normal.
2. **Load material / feed** — pour the liquid to be filled into the feed tank (or feed it from the upstream pipeline), and confirm the contact parts have the correct seal selection for the liquid type (silicone O-ring / rubber sealing ring).
3. **Load / place pouches** — fix the stand-up pouch / spout pouch / Doypack onto the corresponding fixture or pouch clamp, with the pouch mouth aligned to the filling head; the automatic head lifting device adapts to the pouch-mouth position.
4. **Select mode / start** — choose "foot pedal (semi-auto)" or "auto timer (automatic)" mode according to the day's production needs; the two modes can be switched instantly on the same machine.
5. **Volumetric filling** — once started, the air source drives the piston and the 4 anti-drip positive shut-off nozzles fill simultaneously; single-nozzle flow approx. 20 ml/s, completing the dose automatically within the 20–1000 ml range.
6. **Positive shut-off / drip stop** — when the target volume is reached, a cylinder drives a positive shut-off at the nozzle bottom, eliminating dripping and liquid clinging to the pouch mouth.
7. **Unload / move downstream** — remove the filled pouch and move it to the next process (sealing / capping / labeling / inkjet coding / packing / cartoning).
8. **CIP cleaning / product changeover** — when changing liquids, remove the nozzles and contact parts and rinse with clean water or hot water; for strongly corrosive liquids, soak with the appropriate cleaning agent before rinsing. **Regularly check the silicone O-rings** for aging, deformation, or air leaks.
