## Overview
The FF2 series is Wenzhou T&D's **semi-automatic horizontal electric-pneumatic paste filling machine (Pneumatic & Electric Integrated)** — optimized for **paste and high-viscosity products** while remaining capable of filling **free-flowing liquids**. It covers 8 models (FF2-50/100/300/500/1000/2500/3000/5000) with filling ranges from **5–50 ml** up to **500–5000 ml**. The machine uses an **electric-pneumatic integrated design — pneumatic actuation with electric control**: it requires 220V/50–60Hz power (approx. **100W**) plus an air compressor supply of **0.4–0.6 MPa**. It is fitted with **Airtac pneumatic components** and fills at **5–30 cycles/min** (paste-tuned — slower than liquid-only fillers because paste requires more time per stroke). The frame is **201 stainless steel**, and all product-contact parts are **food-grade 304 stainless steel (316 stainless steel available on request)**. Piston-type volumetric filling delivers **±1% accuracy** with **bottom-close positive shutoff drip-free nozzles** (orifice 5–25 mm selectable, sized for paste flow). The feed system comes with a **30L hopper as standard**; **40L, 60L or 80L** larger hoppers are optional, and the unit can be fitted with a **heating hopper** (keeps temperature-sensitive products flowing), a **stirring hopper** (prevents settling and separation) or a **pressurized hopper** (assists feeding of high-viscosity pastes) to match product characteristics. Two trigger modes — foot pedal or automatic timer — let the operator switch between semi-automatic and automatic operation. The filler comes with a single filling head as standard; **dual-head, 4-head or 6-head versions can be customized**. Suitable for cosmetics (creams, lotions, gels), food (sauce, jam, honey, ketchup, peanut butter), daily chemicals (shampoo, hand cream), pharmaceuticals (ointments), and chemical products (lubricants, adhesives), as well as free-flowing liquids.

## 2. Key Advantages

> **Electric-pneumatic integration (Pneumatic & Electric Integrated) is the biggest selling point of this series — the first five advantages below all center on it. Versatility (paste + liquid in one machine) is the second key advantage.**

1. **Electric-pneumatic drive — more precise control, steadier cycles.** Electric control combined with pneumatic actuation keeps the filling cycle precisely timed by the electrical system. In long continuous runs the cycle stays consistent with excellent batch repeatability; filling speed is **5–30 cycles/min** and can be adjusted to the production pace.
2. **Electric control timer — finer automatic mode settings.** In automatic mode the fill interval is governed by the electric timer: fine adjustment, fast response — ideal for rhythm-based continuous paste filling.
3. **Airtac pneumatic components.** Taiwan Airtac cylinders and valves deliver stable action and long service life — important for paste applications where the cylinder works against higher back-pressure. Spare parts are easy to source globally and long-term maintenance cost stays low.
4. **Ultra-low power — minimal running cost.** Total power draw is only approx. **100W (0.1 kW)** and it runs on any standard 220V/50–60Hz outlet; electricity cost is almost negligible. Air consumption is on par with comparable pneumatic fillers.
5. **Simple to operate — connect and start.** Connect power (220V) and air (0.4–0.6 MPa), and production can begin. The electric control panel is intuitive and new operators learn quickly.
6. **201 stainless steel frame + food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts; 316 available.** The all-stainless frame and food-grade product-contact parts meet food-safety requirements as standard (304); **product-contact parts can be customized in 316 stainless steel** for stronger acid/alkali and corrosion resistance — suited to pharmaceutical, chemical and corrosive products. A stainless steel valve assembly resists corrosion and is easy to keep clean — critical for paste where residue is harder to remove.
7. **±1% filling accuracy + drip-free nozzles.** Piston metering is stable and reliable for paste (no under/over-fill); bottom-close positive shutoff nozzles eliminate dripping, stringing and paste tailing, keeping bottle mouths and the workbench clean — essential for cosmetic jars, food containers and pharma packaging.
8. **Paste + liquid in one machine — versatility and value.** The FF2 is optimized for paste but **also fills free-flowing liquids**: change the nozzle, adjust the piston stroke and switch product categories. **One machine replaces two single-purpose fillers** (paste-only and liquid-only).
9. **8 models cover 5–5000 ml; changeover at zero cost.** Adjust the piston stroke to change the fill volume — no parts replacement needed; a spec change takes just 2–3 minutes. Covers everything from sample-size cosmetic jars to large buckets.
10. **Flexible hopper — 30L standard, heating/stirring/pressurized options.** The hopper comes standard at **30L**, with **40L, 60L or 80L** larger sizes available to cut refill stops on high-volume lines. For hard-to-handle products, fit a **heating hopper** (keeps honey, chocolate paste and other temperature-sensitive products flowing), a **stirring hopper** (prevents settling, separation and surface skinning in particulate sauces) or a **pressurized hopper** (assists feeding of very viscous pastes for a steadier supply) — one machine adapts to different paste characteristics.
11. **Horizontal, compact and lightweight.** The horizontal body has a small footprint and weighs only 22–32 kg (net), so it fits on a tabletop, workbench or in an existing line and is easy to move.
12. **Food-grade silicone seals (rated 200 °C).** Food-safe silicone O-rings as standard; corrosion-resistant rubber seals can be fitted when filling corrosive or sticky products.
13. **Semi-auto ⇄ auto switchable and multi-head expandable.** Start with foot-pedal semi-auto operation; when volume grows, switch to automatic timer mode or upgrade the single head to a **dual/4-head/6-head** machine — no equipment replacement, no wasted investment.

### 4.2 FAQ

**Q1: Does this machine need electricity?**
A: **Yes.** The FF2 series is an electric-pneumatic integrated unit: the electric control section requires a **220V / 50–60Hz** supply (power draw only ~**100W**), while the filling motion is powered by compressed air, so an **air compressor (0.4–0.6 MPa)** must also be connected.

**Q2: Do I need an air compressor?**
A: Yes. The machine uses pneumatic actuation with electric control, so it must be connected to an air compressor supplying **0.4–0.6 MPa**. An ordinary small piston-type compressor (with an air receiver/tank) is sufficient; one can also be added to the order.

**Q3: Is the machine explosion-proof?**
A: The FF2 is an electric-pneumatic model (it contains electric control parts) and is **not intended as an explosion-proof unit**. If the workshop has explosion-proof requirements, or the customer needs to fill flammable liquids such as alcohol-based solvents, please recommend the **FF6 fully-pneumatic paste-filling series** (no electricity at all; intrinsically safe, explosion-proof).

**Q4: What are the filling accuracy and speed?**
A: Accuracy is **within ±1%** (piston-type metering — stable, no drift); speed is **5–30 cycles/min** (paste-tuned; slower than liquid-only fillers because paste requires longer per-stroke settling), and the filling speed is adjustable.

**Q5: Can it fill both paste and liquids? What products can it handle?**
A: **Yes — the FF2 is optimized for paste and high-viscosity products, and also fills free-flowing liquids.** Primary use: sauce, ketchup, jam, honey, peanut butter, chocolate paste, mustard, mayonnaise; face cream, body lotion, hand cream, shampoo (thick), shower gel, hair conditioner; ointments; detergents (thick); lubricants and adhesives. Secondary use: water, juice, milk, edible oil, liquid seasonings, liquid medicine, liquid detergents. **One machine covers both paste and liquid categories — change the nozzle, adjust the piston stroke, switch product categories in minutes.**

**Q6: How is the FF2 different from the F2 liquid filler?**
A: Both use the same electric-pneumatic integrated architecture (220V/50–60Hz, ~100W + 0.4–0.6 MPa air). The differences:

- **F2** is liquid-only — faster (10–40 cycles/min), smaller nozzle (3–12 mm), optimized for free-flowing liquids.
- **FF2** is paste-primary, liquid-secondary — slower (5–30 cycles/min), larger nozzle (5–25 mm), piston geometry tuned for thick products, drip-free nozzle designed to handle paste residue.

If the customer fills **only liquids**, recommend F2. If the customer fills **paste (or both paste and liquid)**, recommend FF2.

**Q7: Are the product-contact materials food grade?**
A: The frame is 201 stainless steel and **all product-contact parts are food-grade 304 stainless steel**, meeting food-safety requirements; **316 stainless steel can be customized** (greater acid/alkali and corrosion resistance — suited to pharmaceutical, chemical and corrosive products). Seals are food-grade silicone (rated 200 °C).

**Q8: Will it drip, string or paste-tail?**
A: No. The nozzle uses a **bottom-close positive shutoff** design — the discharge closes the moment filling ends, guaranteeing drip-free operation. **For paste applications this is critical:** no stringing, no paste tail, no residue on the bottle mouth — keeping cosmetic jars, food containers and pharma packaging clean. Nozzle orifices can be selected within a 5–25 mm range (sized for paste flow).

**Q9: Is changing the fill volume or switching between paste and liquid troublesome?**
A: Not at all. Within the model's range you simply **adjust the piston stroke** to change the fill volume — no parts replacement needed; a changeover takes only a few minutes. **Switching between paste and liquid** is also simple: change the nozzle (paste nozzle vs. liquid nozzle) and adjust the piston stroke and speed — a few minutes, no tools required.

**Q10: Can it run fully automatically? What if I need more output later?**
A: Yes. The machine supports **both foot-pedal (semi-automatic) and automatic timer (automatic) modes** and can be switched at any time. For higher output you can order a **dual (2-head), 4-head or 6-head** multi-head version (one cylinder per head) — no machine replacement needed.

**Q11: What are the MOQ, delivery time and warranty?**
A: **MOQ is 1 unit**; the factory keeps **goods in stock** for fast dispatch; export packing is a heavy-duty carton, one machine per case, with tools and spare parts included. The machine carries a **1-year warranty** (free repair or part replacement for non-human damage during the warranty period; international freight borne by the customer), with **lifetime maintenance service** after the warranty period.

**Q12: Can I choose the hopper capacity? Can I get a heating, stirring or pressurized hopper?**
A: Yes. The hopper comes standard at **30L**, with **40L, 60L or 80L** larger options for high-volume lines that need fewer refill stops. Three hopper types can also be fitted to suit the product: a **heating hopper** (temperature control keeps honey, chocolate paste, syrup and other products that thicken when cool free-flowing); a **stirring hopper** (continuous agitation prevents settling, separation and surface skinning — ideal for fruit-piece jams/sauces and settling-prone products); and a **pressurized hopper** (compressed air assists stable feeding of very viscous pastes for a steadier supply). Choose according to your product characteristics and output.

 │

③ Set Volume

 Adjust filling speed if needed (paste: typically 5–30 cycles/min)
 │
④ Place Container
 Position the jar / bottle / bucket directly under the filling nozzle
 (paste: use paste nozzle 5–25 mm; liquid: switch to liquid nozzle 3–12 mm)
 │
⑤ Trigger Filling
 Foot pedal (semi-automatic mode) or automatic timer (automatic mode)
 │
⑥ Filled (accuracy ±1%)
 Bottom-close positive shutoff nozzle → no dripping, no stringing, no paste tail
 │
⑦ Next Container — Repeat
 Repeat ④–⑥ at 5–30 cycles/min (paste)
 │
⑧ Cleaning & Shutdown
 Stop the machine; disconnect power and air;
 disassemble and rinse hopper / piston / filling nozzle;
 clean before switching to a different product
