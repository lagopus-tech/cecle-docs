### VI. Workflow
1. **Feeding**: Place the product inside the folded shrink film and push it into the sealing area.
2. **Sealing & Cutting**: Press down the transparent cover/sealing frame. The equipment automatically performs sealing and cutting actions, creating a preliminary wrap.
3. **Shrinking**: After sealing is completed, the product enters the internal shrink chamber. Under the action of the set constant-temperature hot air, the film rapidly shrinks to wrap the product.
4. **Extraction**: Once shrinking is finished, the transparent cover automatically resets or opens manually, allowing you to remove the perfectly packaged finished product and proceed to the next packaging cycle.
