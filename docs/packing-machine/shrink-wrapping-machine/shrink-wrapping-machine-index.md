## II. Detailed Breakdown of the 5 Series

### 1. FM-5540 Semi-Automatic 2-in-1 Shrink Packaging Machine
* **Product Positioning**: A cost-effective, compact entry-level shrink packaging solution.
* **Core Advantages**: Integrates sealing, cutting, and shrinking into a single platform, eliminating separate machines to save floor space and reduce costs. Equipped with mobile casters for flexible relocation.
* **Technical Specifications**: Max packing size 550 × 400 × 300 mm, power 3.8 kW, weight 132 kg.
* **Selection Recommendation**: Best for boutique shops, e-commerce sellers, and R&D labs with space constraints and medium output needs without continuous lines.

### 2. FQL-450 + BSE-4525 Semi-Automatic L-Sealer & Shrink Tunnel Combination
* **Product Positioning**: Engineered for small-to-medium factories needing higher efficiency than 2-in-1 systems without a massive automatic footprint.
* **Core Advantages**: Separates sealing and shrinking so tasks happen in parallel—while one item shrinks, you can seal the next, greatly boosting throughput.
* **Technical Specifications**: FQL-450 max wrapping size 45×55cm, height 15cm, speed 6–12 times/min. BSE-4525 conveying speed 1–10m/min, tunnel size 900×450×250mm.
* **Selection Recommendation**: Ideal for businesses upgrading from 2-in-1 machines to overcome bottlenecks without requiring full automation budgets.

### 3. FQA & BSE Series Fully Automatic L-Bar Sealer & Shrink System
* **Product Positioning**: Industrial-grade automated solution for modern factories pursuing high-efficiency production lines.
* **Core Advantages**: Achieves closed-loop unattended production from feeding to output. Available in model sizes FQA-450 and FQA-550, with custom options for PE film, tight-belt conveyance for small items, and optional front/rear conveyors.
* **Technical Specifications**: Packaging speed 15–25 pcs/min. FQA-450 supports length 100–450mm, width ≤300mm, height ≤150mm. FQA-550 supports length 100–550mm, width ≤400mm, height ≤150mm.
* **Selection Recommendation**: Premium choice for medium-to-large factory assembly lines and high-capacity warehousing.

### 4. FQS Series Automatic Side Seal Thermal Shrink Packaging System
* **Product Positioning**: High-performance industrial solution designed for packaging products of unlimited length.
* **Core Advantages**: Side-sealing mechanism permits unrestricted product length (>100mm with no upper limit). Supports POF or PE films, with optional front/rear conveyors and a tight-belt feature for small items (<100mm).
* **Technical Specifications**: Packaging speed 15–30 ppm. Standard models FQS-450, FQS-550, FQS-650, with larger custom sizing available.
* **Selection Recommendation**: Perfect for long-format goods like carpets, textiles, aluminum profiles, and automotive components.

### 5. HFQ Series Fully Automatic Flow Packing Shrink Machine
* **Product Positioning**: Industrial-grade solution for high-efficiency, high-precision packaging with superior retail aesthetics.
* **Core Advantages**: Delivers speeds up to 50–100 pieces per minute, tighter film fit, and saves 3%-10% in film material waste compared to traditional sealers. Features customizable automatic feeding for items like bath bombs.
* **Technical Specifications**: Available in HFQ-590 (film width ≤590mm) and HFQ-720 (film width ≤720mm).
* **Selection Recommendation**: Ideal for small boxes, phone boxes, cosmetic boxes, bath bombs, and instant noodles requiring ultimate efficiency and appearance.
