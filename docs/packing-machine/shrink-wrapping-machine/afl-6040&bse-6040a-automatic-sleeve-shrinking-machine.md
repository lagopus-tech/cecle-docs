### VI. Workflow
1. **Automated Infeed**: Products (bottles/cans) are placed onto the conveyor. The conveyor carries them forward into the grouping area.
2. **Automatic Grouping**: An internal intelligent pusher and sensor system automatically counts and organizes products into the desired bundle format.
3. **Pneumatic Sleeve-Wrapping**: The gathered bundle is pushed into the PE film. The pneumatic sealer cuts and bonds the film ends with high precision.
4. **Heat Shrinking**: The wrapped bundle is automatically pushed into the BSE-6040A high-temperature shrink tunnel.
5. **Cooling & Discharge**: The film tightens firmly around the product. The completed bundle exits the tunnel, cools, and is ready for palletizing.
