> **Core Abstract**
> The FQS series automatic side seal thermal shrink packaging system is a high-performance industrial solution designed for packaging products of unlimited length. Unlike traditional L-bar sealers, the side-sealing mechanism allows for continuous packaging of exceptionally long items. Standard models accommodate product lengths over 100mm and heights up to 25cm (customizable to 30cm), making them ideal for long-format goods like carpets, textiles, aluminum profiles, and electronics. The system features a modular design supporting various widths, robust automation capabilities, and compatibility with POF or PE films. This solution is the preferred choice for factories seeking to maximize efficiency, reduce labor costs, and achieve superior, professional-grade product presentation.

### IV. Sizing Calculation Guide
To ensure your product fits correctly, please use this formula for the **POF Center-folded film**:

* **Film Width Calculation Formula**: `Film Width = Product Width (W) + Product Height (H) + 150mm (Allowance)`

**Important Size & Configuration Guidelines**:
* **FQS-450 Limits**: Max width 300mm, length >100mm (unlimited), max height 250mm, W+H <= 400mm.
* **FQS-550 Limits**: Max width 400mm, length >100mm (unlimited), max height 250mm, W+H <= 500mm.
* **FQS-650 Limits**: Max width 500mm, length >100mm (unlimited), max height 250mm, W+H <= 600mm.
* **Height Customization**: Standard maximum height is 250mm, but it can be custom-built to 300mm if required.
* **Small Products (<100mm)**: If your product length is under 100mm, standard belt spacing can cause items to fall or jam. We offer an optional "Tight-Belt Feature" (extra cost) to resolve this.

### VII. Workflow
1. **Feeding**: Products are placed on the optional front infeed conveyor (or fed manually) and moved via the pushing device one by one into the side-sealing zone.
2. **Side Sealing**: The side-seal unit moves continuously along the length of the product, sealing the open edge.
3. **End Sealing**: The transverse sealing unit creates the front and rear seals, cutting the product cleanly from the continuous film stream.
4. **Shrink Tunnel**: The sealed package is transported into the heat shrink tunnel for uniform shrinking under constant temperature.
5. **Collection**: Finished packages exit the tunnel onto an optional rear conveyor or non-powered roller table, ready for collection or palletizing.
