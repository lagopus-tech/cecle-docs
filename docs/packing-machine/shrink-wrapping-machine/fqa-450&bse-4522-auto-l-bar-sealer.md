### II. Core Advantages
1. **Fully Automated Operation**: Achieves closed-loop production from feeding to finished output, greatly improving production efficiency (15–25 pcs/min) and replacing heavy manual operation.
2. **Flexible Model Selection**:
   * **FQA-450 + BSE-4522**: Tailored for medium-sized products, supporting a product length of 100–450mm, max product width of 300mm, and max height of 150mm.
   * **FQA-550 + BSE-5530**: Tailored for large-sized products, supporting a product length of 100–550mm, max product width of 400mm, and max height of 150mm.
3. **Targeted Customization Services**:
   * **PE Film Support**: Standard is center-folded POF film. If you require tougher PE film packaging (e.g., bulky beverages, heavy items), we can replace the cutter and provide a custom PE-dedicated shrink machine.
   * **Precision Packaging Customization**: If your product length is less than 100mm, standard belt spacing may cause jamming or dropping. We offer a "close-belt integration feature" customization service to ensure smooth conveyance of small-sized products (additional charges apply).
   * **Optional Front Infeed Conveyor System**: An optional conveyor belt with a pushing mechanism can be added in front of the L-bar sealer. Workers simply place products onto the conveyor belt, and the pushing device automatically feeds them into the sealing machine one by one (requires an extra charge).
   * **Optional Rear Discharge Conveyor / Roller Table**: An additional conveyor belt or non-powered roller conveyor can be added at the exit of the shrink tunnel to facilitate easy collection and handling of packaged products by workers (requires an extra charge).

### IV. Sizing Calculation Guide
To ensure your product perfectly fits the machine, please calculate the required **center-folded POF film width** using the following formula:

* **Film Width Calculation Formula**: `Film Width = Product Width (W) + Product Height (H) + 150mm (Allowance)`
* **Precautions**:
    * **FQA-450 Rigid Specifications**: Product length must be between **100–450mm**, max product width is **300mm**, and max height is **150mm**.
    * **FQA-550 Rigid Specifications**: Product length must be between **100–550mm**, max product width is **400mm**, and max height is **150mm**.
    * If your product length is less than 100mm, be sure to consult us about adding the "close-belt integration feature," otherwise products may jam in the conveyor gaps.
    * If your product material is special or dimensions are at critical thresholds, please provide sample product dimensions so we can evaluate if non-standard customization is required.

### V. Sales Strategy & FAQ
#### **Sales Strategy**
* **PE vs. POF Selection**: POF is the mainstream retail packaging choice, offering high transparency and eco-friendliness. If your product is a heavy industrial item or bundled drinks requiring PE film, please specify when ordering so we can reconfigure the cutter and tunnel.
* **Size Evaluation**: This machine is a high-precision automated equipment with strict size requirements. Customers are advised to provide the three dimensions (width, height, length) before ordering so we can pre-audit if it falls within the machine's packaging range.
* **Line Extension Value**: Recommend the optional front infeed and rear discharge conveyors to clients looking for ergonomic workflow upgrades, minimizing manual handling bottlenecks.

* **Q: Can we add extra conveyors for feeding and collecting products?**
    * A: Yes! We offer optional front infeed conveyor belts with a pushing mechanism to feed products one by one, as well as rear discharge conveyor belts or non-powered roller conveyors to help workers collect finished items easily (available for an extra charge).

    * A: Standard belt spacing carries a jamming risk for excessively small products. We can custom-add a "close-belt integration" component to specially solve the conveyance of tiny products (extra fee required).

    * A: Simply calculate: `Product Width (W) + Product Height (H) + 150mm`. This formula provides enough sealing allowance to guarantee optimal packaging results.

### VII. Workflow
1. **Feeding**: Products are placed onto the optional front infeed conveyor (or fed manually) and pushed automatically into the sealing zone by the pushing device, where photoelectric sensors accurately position them.
2. **Sealing & Cutting**: The L-bar sealing blade automatically presses down and cuts the film to achieve a precise seal.
3. **Automatic Conveying**: Sealed products are automatically pushed into the rear heat shrink tunnel.
4. **Constant Temperature Shrinking**: Products run smoothly through constant-temperature hot air, causing the film to evenly shrink and tightly wrap the product.
5. **Finished Output**: Beautifully packaged and cooled products exit from the discharge end onto the optional rear conveyor or roller table, ready for subsequent carton packaging or palletizing.
