### VI. Workflow
1. **Manual/Automatic Feeding**: Place small products onto the infeed conveyor (or via a bath bomb dedicated automatic feeding system).
2. **Wrapping and Sealing**: As products follow the conveyor into the film former, the machine automatically wraps the film around them and seals the bottom and ends.
3. **High-Speed Cutting**: High-speed rotating blades precisely cut the package units, ensuring secure and neat seals.
4. **Constant Temperature Shrinking**: The wrapped products automatically enter the shrink tunnel, where circulating hot air under a constant temperature quickly shrinks the film tightly around the product surface.
5. **Finished Product Output**: Finished items are conveyed out of the discharge port after cooling, presenting a smooth and aesthetic appearance, ready for boxing.
