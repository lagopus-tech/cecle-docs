> The FQL-450 + BSE-4525 system is a professional semi-automatic packaging combination, specifically engineered for small-to-medium-sized factories that need higher efficiency than 2-in-1 machines without the massive footprint of fully automatic industrial lines. This system pairs an efficient manual L-Bar sealer with a high-performance heat shrink tunnel, facilitating a continuous, professional workflow. Ideally suited for wrapping soap, photo frames, tableware, and cosmetics, it utilizes center-folded POF film to deliver a clear, tight, and aesthetically pleasing wrap. ### II. Core Advantages
1. **Higher Efficiency (Continuous Flow)**: Unlike 2-in-1 machines that require waiting for a cycle to finish, this system separates sealing and shrinking. While one item is shrinking in the tunnel, you can already seal the next, significantly increasing daily throughput.
2. **Compact Industrial Footprint**: Designed to be smaller than fully automatic high-speed lines, this system fits perfectly into smaller factories while maintaining professional-grade output capabilities.
3. **Versatile Film Compatibility**: Fully compatible with center-folded POF, PVC, PE, and PP films, allowing you to choose the material that best suits your product protection needs.
4. **Professional Finish**: The controlled heat tunnel ensures uniform shrinking, providing a crystal-clear, tight, and professional look that enhances shelf appeal.

### VI. Workflow
1. **Preparation**: Load the center-folded film onto the FQL-450 sealer.
2. **Sealing & Cutting**: The operator places the product inside the folded film, then pulls down the L-sealing bar. The machine cuts and seals the edges instantly.
3. **Automatic Conveyance**: Once sealed, the product is automatically carried by the belt into the BSE-4525 Shrink Tunnel.
4. **Shrinking**: The product passes through the temperature-controlled tunnel, where hot air causes the film to shrink tightly around the product.
5. **Completion**: The finished, professionally wrapped product exits the other end of the tunnel, ready for cooling and packaging.
