## IV. Operation Process
1. **Power and Air Connection:** Connect to a 110V, 220V, or 380V power supply and attach an external air compressor (ensure the air supply pressure is stable at 0.6-0.8 MPa).
2. **Material Placement:** Place the items to be compressed (e.g., pillows/cushions) inside their packaging bags and lay them flat on the 1000*700mm working desk. Thanks to the double-position design, two items can be placed simultaneously.
3. **Start Compression:** Turn on the switch. Driven by the pneumatic cylinder, the upper pressing plate smoothly presses down, squeezing out all the air from inside the items.
4. **Sealing:** Seal the packaging bags while the pressing plate is still holding the items down. Afterward, the pressing plate rises, and you can remove the flat, non-rebounding finished products.

A1: No, it cannot be used directly. Since this is a pneumatic compression packing machine, the power source is a pneumatic cylinder. Therefore, customers must equip it with their own external air compressor (air pump).

**Q2: What are the external air pressure requirements for the machine?**
A2: The machine requires a working air pressure range of 0.6 - 0.8 MPa. Standard industrial air compressors can easily meet this requirement.
