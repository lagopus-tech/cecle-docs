# Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP) Machine Selection Guide & Product Overview

Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP) replaces the air inside the packaging with specific preservation gases (such as a mixture of nitrogen, carbon dioxide, and oxygen), effectively inhibiting microbial growth, extending the shelf life of food, and locking in its original color and taste.

To meet the needs of enterprises of different scales, we offer three core machine models: **Drawer Type**, **Rotary Type**, and **Continuous Type**.

## II. Detailed Differences Between Styles

### 1. Drawer Type - The Choice for Flexibility
* **Operating Mode**: Features a drawer-style design where the operator manually pushes the tray with materials into the sealing chamber and pulls it out after packaging.
* **Advantages**:
    * **Cost-Effective**: The preferred choice for entry-level investment.
    * **Space-Friendly**: Extremely small footprint, suitable for retail stores or back-of-house operations with limited space.
    * **Quick Mold Change**: Suitable for multi-spec, small-batch production; mold changes take only a few minutes.
* **Applicable Scenarios**: Braised food chain stores, laboratory research, start-up fresh food e-commerce.

### 2. Rotary Type - Balance of Efficiency and Space
* **Operating Mode**: Utilizes a rotary turntable to achieve dual-station synchronous operation. While the machine is sealing on one side, the operator can place the next batch of trays on the other, eliminating waiting time.
* **Advantages**:
    * **Uninterrupted Operation**: Sealing and loading are performed simultaneously, doubling the output compared to the Drawer Type.
    * **Operational Safety**: The loading area is physically isolated from the internal sealing area, ensuring operator safety.
    * **High Stability**: Suitable for medium-sized factories with specific production speed requirements.
* **Applicable Scenarios**: Medium-sized cooked food processing centers, cold-chain fresh-cut vegetable suppliers, chain meal distribution centers.

### 3. Continuous Type - The Industrial Benchmark for Efficiency
* **Operating Mode**: Full-automatic assembly line operation. Trays enter the sealing chamber via a conveyor belt/chain, and are automatically discharged after vacuuming, gas flushing, and sealing.
* **Advantages**:
    * **Extremely High Output**: Multi-cavity molds combined with continuous feeding achieve large-scale mass production.
    * **Labor Saving**: Can be directly integrated with upstream automatic weighing/filling and downstream labeling/coding lines, significantly reducing labor costs.
    * **Servo-Driven**: Extremely precise positioning with uniform sealing pressure, resulting in high aesthetic quality.
* **Applicable Scenarios**: Large ready-made meal production bases, large-scale meat processing plants, food export enterprises.

## III. Selection Decision: Which Model is Best for You?

Choose according to your core business needs:

1. **Based on Production Budget (Output Focus)**:
    * **Daily Output < 2,000 trays**: Choose **Drawer Type (MAP-2)**.
    * **Daily Output 2,000 - 6,000 trays**: Choose **Rotary Type (RMAP-4634)**.
    * **Daily Output > 6,000 trays**: Choose **Continuous Type (AMAP-5827)**.

2. **Based on Site Conditions (Space Constraints)**:
    * Extremely narrow space or retail store use: **Drawer Type** is the only choice.
    * Have a formal production workshop and pursue automation: Prioritize **Continuous Type**.

3. **Based on Product Variety**:
    * If tray shapes are complex and frequently changing, **Drawer Type** or **Rotary Type** offer more convenient mold changes.
