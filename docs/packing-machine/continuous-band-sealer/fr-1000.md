## IV. SOP & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Pouches to Seal**: Pre-filled, ready-to-seal flexible bags.
* **Power Supply**: 110V / 220V mains.
* **Solid-Ink Roller / Ink Wheel**: One piece is included as standard and pre-fitted on the machine.
* **Type Blocks**: The corresponding type blocks must be installed for printing.

### 2. Startup & Operation
1. **Pre-Start Check**: Twist and lift the **【Emergency Stop】** button, then turn on the **【Power】** switch.
2. **Switch On Cooling Fan**: Turn on the **【Fan】** switch to activate internal heat dissipation and the air-cooling duct.
3. **Set Heating**:
   * Turn on the **【Heat Seal】** switch and set the target seal temperature on the **【Digital Temperature Controller】**;
   * Turn on the **【Print】** switch, then slowly rotate the **【Ink-Wheel Temperature】** knob clockwise to heat and soften the ink roller.
4. **Warm Up & Melt Ink**: Wait 3–5 minutes until the digital readout stabilizes and the ink roller is fully softened.
5. **Adjust Conveyor**: Rotate the **【Sealing Speed】** knob to the speed that best fits your production pace; the conveyor begins to run.
6. **Feed & Seal with Fine Adjustment**:
   * Place the pouch mouth flat against the guide rail into the inlet; the machine automatically completes heat sealing, air cooling, printing, and discharge;
   * Inspect the first finished piece. If the printed date sits too far forward or too far back, directly rotate the panel-mounted **【Print Position】** knob to fine-tune until the imprint is perfectly placed.

### 3. Shutdown SOP (Critical to Prevent Heat-Damage Belt Failure)
* **Step 1**: When production ends, **always switch off both the 【Heat Seal】 and 【Print】 switches first** (stop all heating);
* **Step 2**: **Leave the 【Fan】 on and the conveyor running idle for 3–5 minutes** to perform forced air-cooling and heat dissipation;
* **Step 3**: Only after the digital temperature readout drops **below 100 °C** may you switch off the **【Fan】** and **【Power】** switches.

## V. Sales Tips & FAQ

### Sales Tips
* **Lead With "Solid-Ink Printing vs Steel Printing"**: Solid-ink printing produces **full-color, clearly visible, non-piercing imprints**, perfect for food and daily-chemical customers who require **clearly traceable** dates/batch numbers. The FR-900's steel printing produces **heat-pressed indentations with zero ink and zero consumables**.
* **Recommend 110V / 60Hz for North American Buyers**: Confirm the voltage version with customer service before order placement to prevent buyers from receiving equipment they cannot power on.

### FAQ
* **Q: What's the essential difference between the FR-1000 and the standard FR-900? How should I choose?**
  * **A**:
    * **FR-1000 (Solid-Ink Model)**: Uses a hot-melt solid-ink roller to print **clearly visible black text** (e.g., `EXP: 2026/08/18`). Instantly dry, highly visible, and instantly recognizable — the top choice for supermarket and regulatory-compliant sales;
    * **FR-900 (Steel-Print Model)**: Uses inkless heat pressing to create **indented impressions (no color)**. Best for internal turnover, where high visibility of the date is not required and zero-consumable operation is preferred.
* **Q: Is the ink roller a consumable? How often does it need to be replaced?**
  * **A**: The ink roller is a **normal consumable**. The higher the daily output and print frequency, the faster it wears. We recommend keeping **2–3 spare ink rollers** on hand and judging replacement by the actual clarity of the printed imprints.
