An optional nitrogen-flow adjustment valve is available for fine-tuning across different preservation needs.

## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Pouches to Seal**: Pre-filled soft-packaging bags ready for nitrogen-flushed sealing (vacuum bags, laminated bags, aluminum-foil bags, etc.).
* **Power Supply**: 220V / 50Hz or 110V / 60Hz mains.
* **Nitrogen Source**: **Customer-supplied** nitrogen cylinder/tank × 1, plus 8 mm OD (8 mm OD / 5 mm ID) gas tubing. Note: As long as the cylinder can supply nitrogen and has an external outlet, the brand and capacity are not restricted.
* **Type Blocks & Consumables**: Install the corresponding date type blocks according to the purchased model (for the ink-wheel version, ensure the solid-ink roller is fitted).

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Connect Gas Supply**: Connect the external nitrogen cylinder line securely. Open the cylinder main valve and set the pressure regulator to a safe micro-pressure state.
2. **Power On**: Plug in the power and switch on the panel main power switch.
3. **Set Heating**: Switch on the heating switch and set the seal temperature on the temperature controller. For the ink-wheel model, also switch on the print pre-heating.
4. **Start Conveyor**: Adjust the speed knob to make the conveyor run smoothly.
5. **Bag Loading & Sealing**:
   * Align the pouch mouth with the gas nozzle and slip it on;
   * As the conveyor advances, the gas nozzle injects gas into the pouch, inflating it;
   * The moment the pouch mouth leaves the gas nozzle, the sealing band clamps and fuses the film → pressing and setting → synchronous coding → discharge.
6. **Check Hermeticity**: Lightly press the finished inflated pouch to verify seal strength, gas-tightness, and adequate inflation.
7. **Proper Shutdown (Safety First)**:
   * **Step 1**: Close the external nitrogen cylinder main valve first;
   * **Step 2**: Switch off the heating switch on the machine;
   * **Step 3**: Let the conveyor continue to idle for 3–5 minutes for cooling;
   * **Step 4**: Only switch off the main power once the temperature has dropped below 100 °C.

## V. Sales Tips & FAQ

### Sales Tips
* **Lead With "Preservation + Anti-Crush", Not Price Wars**: The value of a nitrogen-flushing sealer is not in "sealing tighter", but in **extending shelf life + reducing transit breakage**. For puffed-food, baked-goods, and nut customers, talk directly about **"nitrogen in the pouch = air-cushion anti-crush + anti-oxidation preservation"** — far more persuasive than spec sheets.
* **Prepare a Script for the "Adjustable Nitrogen Flow" Question**: Customers often ask "can the nitrogen flow be adjusted?". Answer: **Manual adjustment is standard; an optional dedicated adjustment valve (+USD 100)** can be added for finer nitrogen-flow control to suit different pouch types and preservation requirements.
* **⚠️ Always Emphasize "This Machine Does NOT Have a Nitrogen Generating Funtion"**: The machine only has a gas nozzle. **The nitrogen cylinder / tank must be purchased locally by the customer.** Clarifying this upfront prevents 100% of cross-border misunderstandings.

### FAQ
* **Q: Does the machine come with a nitrogen cylinder? What else do I need to buy?**
  * **A**: The machine **does NOT include a nitrogen cylinder/tank, nor does it have nitrogen-generation capability**. The customer must purchase their own nitrogen cylinder or tank — **any model with an external outlet will work**. An 8 mm OD (8 mm OD / 5 mm ID) gas tube is also required.
* **Q: Can the nitrogen flow be adjusted?**
  * **A**: Yes. The standard model supports manual nitrogen-flow adjustment; an **optional dedicated adjustment valve (+USD 100)** can be added for finer, valve-based control of the nitrogen flow to suit different pouch types and preservation requirements.
* **Q: What's the difference between the steel-print and ink-wheel versions? How should I choose?**
  * **A**:
    * **Steel Printing Model**: Uses metal type blocks to heat-press **indented impressions (no color)** — zero consumable cost, exceptional value-for-money;
    * **Ink Wheel Model**: Uses a solid hot-melt ink roller to print **clearly visible black text** with bold, eye-catching characters. The price difference between the two complete machines is approximately USD 100.
