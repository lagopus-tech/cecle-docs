# Continuous Band Sealer — Selection Guide & Product Overview

## I. What Is a Continuous Band Sealer?

A Continuous Band Sealer (also called a band sealing machine / bag sealing machine) is an automated packaging device that clamps the pouch mouth between two constant-speed conveyor belts. The heat-seal film is fused by a heated copper block, cooled and set, then discharged continuously. Combined with different coding modules, it can simultaneously emboss production dates, batch numbers, QR codes, and other information onto the seal while sealing.

*   **Core Value:** It upgrades "manually pressing the bag mouth" into a "continuous, non-stop inline sealing" process. A single operator can handle both filling and collecting. The seal pattern is uniform and strong, with excellent airtightness, significantly extending the shelf life of food / products. It is standard equipment on soft-packaging lines across the food, daily-chemical, pharmaceutical, agricultural, and hardware industries.

*   **Applicable Industries:** Food factories, bakeries and tea, pharmaceuticals and health products, daily-chemical and cosmetics, agricultural products, chemicals, hardware and auto parts, and virtually every other sector.

### Core Working Procedure

The sealing operation is accomplished collaboratively by the main unit, the temperature controller, and the coding module. The standardized process is divided into four stages:

1. **Bag Prep & Filling (Pre-Process)**
   * Place the product into a heat-sealable flexible pouch (aluminum-foil bag, laminated bag, plastic bag, etc.), leaving sufficient space at the mouth and avoiding material sticking to the sealing zone.

2. **Preheat & Speed Adjustment (Preparation)**
   * After powering on, turn on the cooling fan (air cooling) and the heating switch, and set the temperature on the digital temperature controller according to the film material and thickness;
   * **Ink-Roller models** must additionally turn on the printing heater to soften the solid ink (wait 3–5 mins). **Inkjet models** simply need to boot up the touchscreen system.

3. **Sealing & Coding (Core Process)**
   * Feed the pouch mouth into the guide plate; it then passes through, in order: **Heated copper block fusion → Cooling & setting → Synchronized coding (steel print / ink roller / inkjet) → Smooth discharge**;
   * Nitrogen-flush models inject nitrogen into the bag through a dedicated nozzle before the film fuses, displacing oxygen before sealing to achieve "nitrogen preservation + anti-crush."

4. **Collecting & Shutdown (Post-Process)**
   * **Proper shutdown (the key to preventing belt burn-out):** First switch off the heating / coding switch, keep the fan and conveyor running empty for 3–5 minutes to force cooling, and only cut the main power once the temperature drops below 100 °C. Inkjet models must also remove the cartridge and snap it back into its sealed clip to prevent nozzle dry-out and clogging.

## III. Technical Q&A & Common Issues (FAQ)

### 1. Quick Selection Decisions

*   **Q1: Horizontal or Vertical?**
 *   **A:** It depends on the filled material form —
    * **Solid dry goods** (nuts, coffee beans, biscuits, hardware parts, etc. that lie flat without leaking): choose **Horizontal** — easiest bag loading, fastest speed;
    * **Liquids, pastes, powders, stand-up pouches** (chili sauce, shampoo, flour, etc.): must choose **Vertical** (FR-900 Vertical), with bags conveyed upright to eliminate leaks and spills.

*   **Q2: How to choose among steel printing, solid-ink roller printing , and inkjet printing?**
 *   **A:**
    * **Steel Printing (FR-900):** Metal type slugs hot-press an embossed mark — **no color, zero ink consumables**; for internal transfers, where date visibility is not critical, and customers pursuing the lowest running cost;
    * **Solid-Ink Roller Printing (FR-1000):** A solid hot-melt roller prints **clear black solid text**, instant-dry and scratch-resistant — **the first choice for supermarket sales and compliant traceability**;
    * **Inkjet Printing (PM-1800):** Free touchscreen editing, **can print QR code / barcode / graphic LOGO**, meeting "one-code-per-item, full traceability, content changeable anytime" — something neither steel print nor ink roller can do; the trade-off is needing spare ink-cartridge consumables.

### 2. Nitrogen Models & Daily Maintenance

*   **Q3: Does the nitrogen sealer come with its own nitrogen cylinder?**
*   **A:** **No, and it has no nitrogen-generating function.** The machine only includes the inflation nozzle; the customer must supply their own local nitrogen cylinder / tank (any brand/capacity with an external interface will do) and provide their own gas hose with an 8 mm outer diameter (8 mm outer / 5 mm inner).
