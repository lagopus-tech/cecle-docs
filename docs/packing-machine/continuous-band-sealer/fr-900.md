The FR-900 is a continuous band sealer available in horizontal and vertical configurations, engineered for hands-free, high-throughput heat sealing of pre-filled pouches across food, cosmetic, pharmaceutical, chemical, and agricultural industries. The horizontal model excels at sealing dry goods such as coffee beans, nuts, tea leaves, snacks, baked goods, and hardware parts laid flat on the conveyor, while the vertical model is purpose-built for liquids, pastes, powders, and self-standing pouches, eliminating leaks and spills. Both variants share digital temperature control from 0–300 °C, a 12 mm seal width with a thickened copper heating block for airtight, shelf-life-extending seals, integrated steel-print coding for one-step batch/date embossing, and a 5 kg conveying load. The vertical stand features a height-adjustable head-to-conveyor spacing (120–300 mm) via hand-tightened knobs, covering virtually every short or tall bag type. A reliable, versatile packaging workhorse.

## IV. Model Selection & Operation

### 1. Horizontal / Vertical Model Selection
* **Choose the 【Horizontal Model】**: For dry-goods fills such as nuts, biscuits, coffee beans, and hardware parts. The sealing head lies flat, allowing the easiest pouch loading and the fastest throughput.
* **Choose the 【Vertical Model】**: For liquids, sauces, powders/granules, or tall self-standing pouches, the elevated vertical stand is required. The pouches stand upright, ensuring that the fill cannot leak or spill during sealing.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Set Bag Height (Vertical Model Only)**:
   * Stand the pouch upright on the conveyor and loosen the knobs on both sides of the vertical column;
   * **Fine-tune the height within the 12 cm – 30 cm range** until the pouch mouth feeds smoothly into the inlet, then re-tighten the knobs.
2. **Power On**: Connect mains power and switch on the main power switch and the cooling-fan switch.
3. **Set Parameters**: Switch on the heating switch. Set the temperature-controller value according to the film material, then adjust the conveyor belt to the appropriate speed.
4. **Warm Up**: Wait 3–5 minutes until the actual temperature stabilizes at the set value.
5. **Load & Seal**: Guide the pouch mouth into the guide plate. The machine automatically heat-fuses the film, cools and sets the seal, applies the steel-print coding, and discharges the finished pouch.
7. **Proper Shutdown (Key Maintenance to Prevent Belt Burn-Out)**:
   * **Step 1**: When production is finished, **always switch off the heating switch first**.
   * **Step 2**: Keep the cooling fan and conveyor running for 3–5 minutes to forcibly dissipate heat.
   * **Step 3**: Only cut the main power once the temperature has dropped below 100 °C.
