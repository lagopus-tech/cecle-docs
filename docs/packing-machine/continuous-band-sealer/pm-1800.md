## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials

- **Pouches to Seal**: Pre-filled, ready-to-seal soft-packaging bags (aluminum-foil bags, kraft paper bags, etc.).
- **Power Supply**: 110V / 220V mains.
- **Ink Cartridge**: Installed and confirmed to have ink. When prints become faint, simply replace the cartridge — **no type blocks required**.

### 2. Standard Workflow

1. **Power On**: Connect mains power and switch on the main power switch and the cooling-fan switch.
2. **Set Sealing Parameters**: Switch on the heating switch. Set the temperature-controller value according to the film material and thickness, and adjust the conveyor belt to the appropriate speed.
3. **Install Ink Cartridge & Boot Up**: Remove the cartridge's protective clip, insert the cartridge into the print-head slot at a 45° angle, and clip it firmly into place. Switch on the inkjet system screen.
4. **Edit Print Information**: Edit the production date, expiration date, batch number, or call up a stored template on the touch screen. Confirm font size and number of lines.
5. **First-Piece Sample & Fine-Tuning**: Feed a sample pouch for a trial run. Check: ① Seal strength and flatness; ② Print clarity and print position.
6. **Continuous High-Efficiency Operation**: The operator places the pouches flat on the conveyor. The machine automatically performs: **copper-block heat sealing → cooling & setting → inkjet coding → clean, smooth discharge**.

### 3. Core Maintenance & Shutdown Specifications (⚠️ Critical to Prevent Nozzle Clogging and Heat-Damage Belt Failure)

- **【Ink Cartridge Anti-Dry Protection (Extremely Important)】**:
  - When the operation ends or is paused for over 15 minutes, **the ink cartridge must be removed immediately and clipped back into its original sealed clip**!
  - Never leave the cartridge exposed to air for an extended period, otherwise the print nozzle will dry out and crust over, causing broken lines or no ink output.
- **【Band Sealer Proper Shutdown】**:
  - First switch off the heating switch;
  - Keep the cooling fan and conveyor running idle for 3–5 minutes until the machine body has cooled below 100 °C, then switch off the main power.

### FAQ

- **Q: What's the difference between the PM-1800 and the FR-900 / FR-1000?**
  - **A**: The core difference lies in the **coding technology**. The FR-900 uses **steel printing** (physical metal indentation, no color); the FR-1000 uses **solid-ink roller printing** (black solid-ink text) — **both require type blocks to be installed and replaced**. The PM-1800, on the other hand, uses **smart inkjet printing** (cartridge-based inkjet). The print content is **set directly on the touch screen**, requires no type blocks, and can also print **QR codes / barcodes / graphics**.
- **Q: Can it print QR codes and barcodes?**
  - **A**: Yes. The inkjet system supports **QR codes, barcodes, and graphic logos**, ideal for **one-product-one-code, scan-to-trace, and e-commerce barcode** scenarios. **Neither steel-print nor solid-ink roller models can achieve this.**
- **Q: How often does the ink cartridge need to be replaced? How do I replace it?**
  - **A**: The cartridge is a **normal consumable**. The higher the print frequency, the faster it wears. The actual replacement interval depends on print clarity. When the ink runs low, **simply replace the cartridge** — no type-block adjustment or ink-circuit cleaning required. We recommend keeping 1–2 spare cartridges on hand.
