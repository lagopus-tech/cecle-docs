* **Dual Separate Guide Slots:** Features a split-loading design where the bottom strap is seated into the base cutter guide slot and the upper strap is fed between the tension wheel and vibrating teeth, ensuring uniform tension and precise feeding alignment.
* **Auto Cut-Off:** Upon triggering the welding cycle, the embedded pneumatic cutter synchronizes to cleanly trim the upper excess strap without jagged edges.

## IV. Strap Selection & Operation

### 1. Strap Selection Guide
* **Strap Recommendation:** Always use standard **13–19 mm wide and 0.6–1.2 mm thick** PET strapping (recommendations: 1608 or 1909 specs). Straps thinner than 0.6 mm may tear under high tension.
* **Air Supply Requirements (Critical for Service Life):**
  * The air compressor downstream must be equipped with an **"Air Filter-Regulator-Lubricator (FRL) Unit"** to remove moisture, maintaining stable operating pressure at 0.6–0.7 MPa;
  * Apply 2–3 drops of dedicated pneumatic oil (sewing machine oil) into the air inlet weekly. Running dry without lubrication is strictly prohibited.

### 2. Standard Workflow (⚠️ Strict Separate Upper & Lower Loading Required)
1. **Wrap Package:** Loop the PET strap around the package, holding the overlap intersection with your left hand.
2. **[Core Protocol] Separate Strap Loading:**
   * Firmly **push down the handle on the right side of the machine** with your right hand to open the feed channel;
   * **Bottom Strap (Lower Layer):** Thread through the **cutter's bottom guide slot** and lay it flat on the base anvil;
   * **Top Strap (Upper Layer):** Smoothly feed it **between the tension wheel and vibrating tooth plate**;
   * **⚠️ Never load both overlapping straps into the same layer together!** Ensure correct vertical separation and horizontal alignment before releasing the handle to allow the tension wheel to clamp the upper strap.
3. **Pneumatic Tensioning:** Press and hold the "Tension Button (Air Throttle Lever)" with your right thumb. The pneumatic motor rotates at high speed to powerfully draw the strap tight. Release the lever once desired tension is reached.
4. **Pneumatic Welding & Cutting:** Immediately press and hold the "Welding Button". The vibration cylinder performs high-frequency friction sealing and automatically shears off the excess strap tail. Hold for 2–3 seconds until the weld cures, then release.
5. **Release & Remove:** Push down the right-hand handle again to release the clamping jaws, then smoothly slide the machine off to the right, completing an efficient strapping cycle.

### FAQ
* **Q: Does this machine need to be plugged into electricity?**
  * **A:** No. The A19 is a purely pneumatic tool driven by compressed air. It requires an external **air compressor** operating at 0.5–0.8 MPa. Customers without an air compressor are recommended to choose the JD-1316 cordless electric strapping machine.
* **Q: Are there any special operational precautions?**
  * **A:** **Manual separate strap loading is mandatory.** The A19 lacks an automatic guiding slot for overlapped straps; operators must manually split the overlapping upper and lower straps (short end on the bottom, long end on top) before feeding them individually into the tool. Skipping this step will result in welding failure. Additionally, allow a few seconds for the weld to cool before removing the tool.
