The TDAG-12 and TDAG-20 are fully-automatic OPP/paper banding machines specifically designed for printing, pharmaceutical, and delicate retail industries. Utilizing either OPP plastic film or eco-friendly paper bands, they bundle lightweight items via instant thermal heat sealing without leaving any adhesive residue or pressure marks. Featuring photoelectric automatic triggering and dual-model specifications, they provide a damage-free, eco-friendly bundling solution for small, delicate goods.

## II. Core Advantages

* **Damage-Free Bundling (Core Value):** While standard PP strapping bands possess high tension that crushes and leaves indentations on medicine or gift boxes, banding machines utilize a narrow band with gentle binding pressure, keeping product surfaces pristine and undamaged—an absolute necessity for high-end printed goods and cosmetic boxes.
* **No Glue, No Residue:** Thermal sealing operates entirely without glue, leaving zero adhesive residue after band removal and requiring zero subsequent cleaning steps—the primary reason food and pharmaceutical customers choose this system.
* **Extremely Low Learning Curve:** One-touch power-on operation with zero complex debugging, allowing new employees to operate the machine proficiently without prolonged training.

## IV. Operation Guide

### 1. Preparation
* **Power Connection:** Connect to a **110V / 220V** power supply and turn on the power switch.
* **Load Band Roll:** Mount the OPP film band / paper band roll onto the dispenser holder, threading it through the guide path to the machine head according to directional diagrams.
* **Confirm Band Material:** This machine is compatible with **0.12 mm thick** OPP film bands or paper bands. Band width must match the machine model (TDAG-12 uses 12 mm bands; TDAG-20 uses 20 mm bands).

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Preheat:** Connect power, turn on the master switch, and allow the heating element to warm up for 1–2 minutes.
2. **Position Package:** Push the items to be bundled (**Note: Single item dimensions must not be less than 30×10 mm, and must fall within arch dimensions**) into the photoelectric sensing zone on the tabletop.
3. **Auto Banding:** The machine automatically executes: tension ➔ heat-seal ➔ cut ➔ band-reset upon sensing the package.
4. **Remove Product:** Take away the banded finished product and proceed to the next cycle.

## V. Sales Tips & FAQ

### Sales Tips
* **The Primary Qualifying Question ("What goods are being bundled?"):** For customers bundling **medicine boxes, cards, printed goods, currency, shoe boxes, or gift boxes** → Recommend the Banding Machine; for **cartons and heavy goods** → Recommend Strapping Machines (JD-1316, KZB-08AL, MH-101, etc.). These two equipment categories are complementary, not interchangeable.
* **Natural Product Line Extension:** When a customer purchases a carton strapping machine, they often still need to bundle internal small boxes and cards. Use the cross-selling pitch: *"Carton Strapping for outer boxes, Banding Machines for inner small items."*

### FAQ
* **Q: What is the difference between a banding machine and a strapping machine?**
  * **A:** Strapping machines utilize thicker PP/PET bands for **heavy goods**, offering high tension that **leaves indentations on soft boxes**; Banding machines utilize a narrow ~0.12 mm band (OPP film or paper) to loop **light, small items** with gentle tension, leaving **surfaces undamaged and residue-free**. Use banding machines for medicine boxes, cards, currency, and printed goods; use strapping machines for cartons and pallets.
* **Q: Does the banding machine use glue?**
  * **A:** No. It utilizes **thermal heat-sealing**, melting the band itself via heat without involving any glue. Consequently, it **leaves zero adhesive residue, doesn't dirty boxes**, and requires no post-cleaning—making it the preferred choice for the food and pharmaceutical industries.
* **Q: What type of bands can be used? Are paper bands supported?**
  * **A:** Yes. Compatible with **OPP film bands and paper bands** with a thickness of 0.12 mm, provided widths match the machine model (TDAG-12 uses 12 mm bands; TDAG-20 uses 20 mm bands).
