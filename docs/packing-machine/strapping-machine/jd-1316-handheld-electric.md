## IV. Strap Selection & Operation

### 1. Strap Selection Guide
* **Strap Selection Based on Load Weight:**
  * **Light cartons, apparel, courier packages:** Prioritize PP straps for high flexibility;
  * **Heavy pallets, steel, stone, building materials:** Must use **PET straps** for high tensile strength, weather resistance, and strong elastic recovery.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Insert Battery & Check Power:** Push a fully charged battery into the base slot until a "click" sound is heard. Power on and verify the head indicator light turns on normally.
2. **Thread Strap:** Loop the strap around the package. Use your left hand to flatly overlap the bottom strap (leaving a ~30cm tail) and the top strap together.
3. **Open Jaw & Load:** Press down the bottom release lever with your right hand, fully push the overlapping straps into the guide slot (top strap on top, bottom strap on the bottom), and release the lever to engage the teeth.
4. **Tension:** Press and hold the "Tension Button." The machine rapidly draws the strap tight, stopping automatically once preset tension is reached (for fragile cartons, you can release the button early at any time).
5. **Weld & Cut:** Immediately press the "Welding Button." The device automatically performs high-frequency friction welding and cuts the excess strap (finishing automatically when motor rotation ceases).
6. **Release & Remove:** Firmly push down the bottom release lever again to open the head, and smoothly slide the strapping tool off to the right to complete the cycle.

### FAQ
* **Q: Can this machine use PP straps, or is it restricted to PET straps?**
  * **A:** Both are supported. The JD-1316 is a universal model compatible with both PET and PP straps in widths from 13 to 16 mm (shipped standard for PET, adjustable spring setup easily adapts to PP).
* **Q: Are metal clips required? Is the joint secure?**
  * **A:** Absolutely no metal clips are needed. Utilizing friction welding technology, the strap fuses with itself, achieving a joint strength of approx. 75% of the parent strap—fully meeting standard transport and warehouse stacking requirements while leaving smooth, scratch-free joints.
* **Q: How tight can it pull? Will it crush cardboard cartons?**
  * **A:** Maximum tension force is 2800 N, and the tension knob allows adjustable tightening (approx. 600–2800 N). Adjust to lower settings for fragile cartons to prevent crushing, and higher settings for heavy pallets and steel.
* **Q: How do I adjust the welding time?**
  * **A:** Welding time is adjustable from 1 to 3 seconds. Increase time if the weld is loose, or shorten it if the strap surface scorches or melts through. PP straps generally require slightly shorter welding durations than PET straps.
