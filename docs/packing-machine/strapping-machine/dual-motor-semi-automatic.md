## IV. Model Selection & Operation Guide

### 1. Model Selection (High-Table vs. Low-Table)
* **Choose [High-Table (73 cm)]:** Ideal when packages are primarily small and light, and operators stand during work. The elevated tabletop prevents back bending, ensuring fast pacing and low fatigue, making it the top choice for **e-commerce fulfillment stations**.
* **Choose [Low-Table (45 cm)]:** Ideal for heavy or bulky cargo. The low tabletop allows heavy items to be **pushed straight onto the surface** without lifting.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Power On:** Plug into a 110V/220V outlet and turn on the power switch to enter standby mode.
2. **Warm-Up:** Allow the sealing head to preheat for approx. 10–30 seconds (extend appropriately in winter). Insufficient preheating leads to **weak seals**.
3. **Parameter Check:** The machine is **adjustment-free** for 9–14 mm strap widths. If switching to a different thickness or strap grade, fine-tune the **tension and temperature knobs** based on sealing results (lower tension for light cartons to prevent crushing; higher tension for heavy bundles to prevent loosening).
4. **Position Package:** Place the package onto the tabletop (keep load within approx. 30 KG).
5. **Manual Insert:** Hold the strap with your right hand, wrap it around the package, and **insert the strap tip into the right-hand channel with your left hand**. Once inserted, the machine automatically executes **draw ➔ tension ➔ heat-seal ➔ cut**.
6. **Remove Package:** Upon hearing the cutting sound, the strapping cycle is complete. Remove the package and inspect the joint (a qualified joint should feature complete fusion without voids).
7. **Continuous Operation:** Repeat steps 4–6. Power down and clear residual strap tracks before ending your shift.

### FAQ
* **Q: What is the difference between this machine and a handheld electric strapping tool?**
  * **A:** This unit is a **semi-automatic tabletop device**: packages are placed on the table, and operators manually insert the strap into the slot, while the machine handles tensioning, heat sealing, and cutting automatically. Handheld tools require operators to carry and maneuver the tool around the package. For fixed stations with high daily volumes, tabletop machines offer higher speed and less fatigue.
* **Q: Do I need to adjust the machine when switching different PP strap widths?**
  * **A:** No. Strap widths from 9 to 14 mm and thicknesses from 0.6 to 0.9 mm are **supported without adjustment**, allowing mixed use of different strap specifications without stopping production.
* **Q: What is the maximum load capacity of the tabletop?**
  * **A:** This model features a 1.5 mm spray-molded iron tabletop panel supporting approx. 30 KG. For heavier loads, we recommend models equipped with 2.5 mm iron plates or 2.0 mm stainless steel tops.
