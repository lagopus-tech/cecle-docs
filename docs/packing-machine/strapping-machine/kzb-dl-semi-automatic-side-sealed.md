## IV. Operation Guide

### Standard Workflow
1. **Power On & Preheat:** Connect power, turn on the master switch, and allow the heating element to warm up for 1–2 minutes.
2. **Position Package:** Push the item to be strapped onto the side strapping tabletop.
3. **Manual Feed:** Wrap the PP strap around the package and insert the end into the side mechanism port.
4. **Auto Strapping:** The machine senses the strap and automatically executes the full cycle of tensioning, heat-sealing, cutting, and strap resetting.
5. **Remove Package:** Take away the strapped goods and prepare for the next packaging cycle.

### FAQ
* **Q: What is the difference between a side-sealed machine and a standard semi-automatic strapping machine?**
  * **A:** The difference lies in the **mechanism placement**. Standard machines have their core mechanism positioned directly beneath the tabletop; when bundling wet or debris-laden goods, water droplets and fragments slide down tabletop gaps directly into the core, causing rust, corrosion, and jams. The side-sealed machine relocates the mechanism to the **side of the machine body**, preventing liquid and debris entry. This is a structural difference, not merely a spec difference.
* **Q: Do I really need a side-sealed machine for seafood and aquatic products?**
  * **A:** Highly recommended. Wet goods such as seafood, fresh frozen foods, and ice blocks are the prime operating scenarios for side-sealed machines. While standard machines might work temporarily, long-term internal moisture and rust lead to extremely high failure rates, where production downtime losses far outweigh the equipment price difference.
* **Q: Are metal clips required? Is the joint secure?**
  * **A:** Absolutely no clips are needed. The unit utilizes thermal heat-sealing, fusing the strap to itself for a flat, secure, and hand-safe joint while saving procurement and threading time.
