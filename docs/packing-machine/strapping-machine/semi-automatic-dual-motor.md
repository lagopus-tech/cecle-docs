## II. Core Advantages

* **Dual-Motor Control for Ultimate Durability:** Main operation powered by one motor, while strap feeding and tensioning are managed by a separate motor. Sharing the mechanical load produces lower coil heat during long continuous shift operations, preventing motor burnout and delivering a service life substantially longer than single-motor units.
* **Zero Buckle Cost:** Thermal heat sealing eliminates the procurement and manual threading of metal clips, leaving flat, clean, and aesthetically pleasing joints that will not scratch outer packaging.
* **Under 3 Seconds per Strap:** Completes a single strapping cycle in less than 3 seconds, leaving operators only responsible for strap insertion and loading/unloading, offering a pace drastically faster than manual handheld tools.

## IV. Operation Guide

### Standard Workflow
1. **Power On & Preheat:** Turn on the power switch to activate sealing head heating; quick-heat models can operate immediately upon boot-up (extend preheating appropriately in winter or cold environments to prevent weak seals).
2. **Position Package:** Place the package onto the tabletop (monitor load limits to avoid overloading and tabletop deformation).
3. **Manual Feed:** Hold the strap with your right hand, wrap it around the package, and **insert the strap tip into the right-hand channel**. Once inserted properly, the machine automatically executes **draw ➔ tension ➔ heat-seal ➔ cut**.
4. **Remove Package:** Upon hearing the cutting sound, the strapping cycle is complete. Remove the package and inspect the joint (a qualified joint should feature complete fusion without voids).
5. **Continuous Operation:** Repeat steps 2–4. Power down and clear residual strap tracks before ending your shift.

### FAQ
* **Q: What is the difference between this machine and a handheld electric strapping tool?**
  * **A:** This unit is a **semi-automatic tabletop device**: packages are placed on the table, and operators manually insert the strap into the slot, while the machine handles tensioning, heat sealing, and cutting automatically. Handheld tools require operators to carry and maneuver the tool around the package. For fixed stations with high daily volumes, tabletop machines offer higher speed and less fatigue.
* **Q: Are metal clips required? Is the joint secure?**
  * **A:** Absolutely no metal clips are needed. Utilizing thermal heat-sealing technology, the strap fuses with itself, leaving a flat, secure, and hand-safe joint while saving procurement and threading time.
