## IV. Strap Selection & Operation

### 1. Strap Selection Guide
* **PET Strap Only:** This model is custom-engineered exclusively for PET straps and **is not suitable for PP straps**.
* **Parameter Settings:** For thicker straps (0.9 mm and above), it is recommended to appropriately increase the welding time setting; for thinner straps (approx. 0.6 mm), suitably reduce the welding duration to prevent overheating or burning through the strap.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Battery Check:** Push a fully charged 14.8V lithium battery into the machine tail slot until a clicking sound confirms it is securely locked in place.
2. **Wrap & Align:** Loop the strapping band around the package. Use your left hand to flatly overlap the bottom strap (leaving approx. 20–30 cm tail end) and the top strap over the top of the package.
3. **Open Jaw & Load:** Firmly press down the release lever on the machine body with your right hand to open the jaw, and push the overlapping straps into the gear slot (top strap on top, bottom strap on the bottom). Release the lever to clamp tightly.
4. **Auto-Tension:** Press and hold the "Tension Button." The high-power motor will rapidly draw the strap tight, automatically stopping once the preset tension is reached. Release the button.
5. **Weld & Cut:** Immediately press the "Welding Button." High-frequency friction welding activates and simultaneously cuts the outer excess strap (finishing automatically when motor rotation ceases).
6. **Release & Remove:** Firmly push down the release lever again to release the clamping jaws, then smoothly slide the machine off to the right, completing a high-strength strapping cycle.
