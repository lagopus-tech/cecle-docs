## II. Core Advantages
1. **Four‑column Double‑cylinder Hydraulic Drive**: Equipped with a four-column automatic balancing connecting rod structure. The cutting force is evenly distributed across the entire machine, ensuring consistent cutting depth at all positions and neat, burr-free edges.
2. **Slow Cutting on Contact**: Automatically decelerates upon contact with the material, minimizing impact wear and extending the service life of both the die cutter and the workbench cutting pad.
3. **Adjustable Stroke for Multi-molds**: The cutting stroke is infinitely adjustable, allowing for quick adaptation to dies of different heights, making mold changes and debugging simple and convenient.
4. **Safety Operation Design**: Dual-hand push buttons + emergency stop button. The machine requires both hands to be triggered simultaneously to execute the cut, effectively preventing industrial injury risks caused by misoperation.
5. **Centralized Auto-lubrication System**: Key moving parts receive centralized lubrication to reduce component wear, ensure long-term machine precision, and lower maintenance workload.

## IV. Operation Procedure
1. **Install the Die Cutter**: According to the product contour, fix the customized steel die cutter onto the workbench cutting pad and lock the positioning clamps.
2. **Debug Stroke and Pressure**: Turn on the power to start the hydraulic system and run it idling; adjust the cutting stroke and set the appropriate hydraulic pressure based on the die height.
3. **Place Materials**: Place the blister semi-finished products flatly on top of the die cutter, align with the positioning benchmarks, and ensure hands are completely away from the cutting area.
4. **Test Run**: Punch a sample piece first to check the cutting effect; if the cut is incomplete or burrs are too large, finely adjust the hydraulic pressure and stroke height.
5. **Batch Production**: Once the test run is approved, press the start buttons with both hands simultaneously to complete a single cut; take out the finished product, clear the waste borders, and repeat the cycle.

## V. Sales Suggestions & FAQs
### Sales Suggestions
1. **Clarify Application Scenarios (Offline Post-cutting)**: This equipment is an **offline post-processing cutting machine** used only for trimming already heat-formed blister sheets. It cannot perform blister forming or heat-sealing processes. Be sure to confirm process requirements with the customer.
2. **Emphasize Die Matching**: The machine does not include product die cutters. Customers need to provide product drawings/samples to customize steel die cutters. Remind them to source die cutters simultaneously when placing an order.
3. **Consumables Recommendation**: It is recommended to bundle workbench cutting pads. Cutting pads are high-frequency consumable parts that will develop dents over long-term cutting and need to be replaced periodically.

### FAQs
**Q1: Can this machine directly perform blister forming and heat sealing?**
A1: No. This machine is only responsible for cutting and trimming. Blister forming and aluminum foil heat sealing require a blister packaging main machine; this machine is used for the subsequent finished product separation process.

**Q2: The product edges are not thoroughly cut and remain attached after punching?**
A2: This is generally caused by insufficient cutting pressure or inadequate stroke. You can appropriately increase the hydraulic pressure and enlarge the cutting stroke. Also, check if the die cutting edge is dull; dull edges need to be reground.

**Q3: Can different sizes of blister products share the same machine?**
A3: The machine can be shared, but each product requires a dedicated custom steel die cutter, and the cutting area must not exceed the maximum cutting range of the machine.

**Q4: Can it be upgraded to a fully automatic feeding version?**
A4: Yes, an optional automatic feeding mechanism is supported to realize automatic feeding and cutting, suitable for mass production. The basic model comes standard with a semi-automatic dual-hand operation mode.
