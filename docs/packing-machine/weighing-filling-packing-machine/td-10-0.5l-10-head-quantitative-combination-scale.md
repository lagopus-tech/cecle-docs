## 4. Operation Process
1. Material is fed into the central top funnel of the combination scale via a matching bucket elevator.
2. The main vibrator pan vibrates, distributing the material evenly to 10 linear vibrator pans.
3. The linear pans feed the material into buffer hoppers, which then drop it into 10 independent weighing hoppers below.
4. The computer mainboard instantly reads the weights of the 10 hoppers and performs high-speed permutation and combination calculations.
5. The system selects the combination of hoppers whose sum is closest to the preset target weight (e.g., 500g).
6. The selected hoppers open simultaneously, discharging the material into the collection chute, which then drops into the packaging machine below.
