## 4. Operation Process
1. Load bulk granular materials (like rice, seeds) into the top storage hopper via a bucket elevator or manually.
2. Set the target weight (e.g., 25KG / 55.1 lbs) and vibration feeding speed on the control panel.
3. Manually place an empty bag under the discharge outlet; after machine induction or stepping on the pedal, it automatically begins high-precision weighing and discharging.
4. Once the set weight is reached, materials drop into the bag, which then lowers onto the conveyor belt below.
5. The conveyor transports the heavy filled bag smoothly to the side pedestal sealing/sewing machine.
6. Manually assist to complete the final sealing operation, yielding a perfectly sealed 25KG (55.1 lbs) finished bag.

## 5. Sales Suggestions & FAQ
### Sales Suggestions
1. This machine is designed exclusively for free-flowing **granules and crystals** (rice, beans, seeds, sugar, salt). If the customer needs to pack **fine flour or milk powder**, do not recommend this; pitch the "Auger Filler Scale" instead.
2. The machine height reaches 2.8 meters (110.2 in / 9.2 ft). Always have the customer confirm their factory ceiling height before quoting.
3. Ideal for mid-to-large agricultural processors and feed mills. It can be sold as a complete assembly line with a sewing machine and sealer to increase order value.

### FAQ
**Q1: Can this machine pack powder?**
A1: It is only suitable for free-flowing crystalline coarse powders (like sugar, salt, MSG). For fine powders that clump and create heavy dust (like flour, fine milk powder), this vibratory model is not suitable; an auger metering machine is required.

**Q2: Does the machine use single-phase or three-phase power?**
A2: It supports both 220V single-phase and 380V three-phase (50Hz) and can be configured at the factory according to the customer's actual electrical environment.

**Q3: Are wearing parts covered by warranty?**
A3: The whole machine has a 1-year warranty. Wearing parts (like heating tubes, conveyor belts) are not covered, but lifetime maintenance support is provided.

**Q4: Can it accommodate different bag sizes?**
A4: Yes, the machine is equipped with an adjustable sealing height range of 600-1100mm (23.6-43.3 in) to adapt to bags of varying heights and capacities.

**Q5: Do I need an air compressor?**
A5: Typically, pneumatic bag-clamping devices require a small amount of compressed air. It is recommended that customers prepare a small air compressor to ensure smooth and strong clamping action (depending on the chosen clamp type).
