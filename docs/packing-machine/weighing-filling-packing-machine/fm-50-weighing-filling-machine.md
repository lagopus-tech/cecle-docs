## 4. Operation Process
1. **Feeding**: After confirming the power connection is normal, turn on the switch and pour the materials to be filled (such as tea, granules, or powder) into the top stainless steel hopper.
2. **Setting weight**: Input the target dispensing weight (between 1g and 50g) on the intelligent control panel.
3. **Automatic weighing**: The internal vibration plate of the machine starts working, transporting the materials to the weighing hopper, and automatically stops vibrating when the set weight is reached.
4. **Foot pedal discharging**: Place the packaging bag or container under the stainless steel discharging outlet, step on the foot switch, the baffle will open, and the material will accurately fall into the package, completing one filling cycle.

### FAQ
**Q1: What materials can this machine pack?**
A1: Suitable for dry, non-sticky materials such as nuts, corn, beans, tea, powders, and small hardware like screws. Not suitable for moist, highly sticky, or extremely poorly flowing materials.

**Q2: Is the filling error large?**
A2: The error range of the machine is between ±0.2g and 2g. The specific error depends on the particle size, uniformity, and fluidity of the material (the more uniform and flowable the material, the higher the precision).

**Q3: How does the discharging work?**
A3: When the panel indicates that the weighing is complete, the operator simply steps on the provided foot switch, and the machine will automatically open the valve to discharge the material.

**Q4: Is the machine easy to clean?**
A4: The main channels and hopper of the entire machine are made of stainless steel. The channel structure is relatively open and can be easily cleaned with a brush or wiped with a dry cloth, making cleaning very convenient.

**Q5: Is it plug-and-play? Does it require an air compressor?**
A5: Yes, the machine can be used directly by plugging it in (supports 110V/220V standard mains power). It is purely electrically controlled and does not require an air compressor.
