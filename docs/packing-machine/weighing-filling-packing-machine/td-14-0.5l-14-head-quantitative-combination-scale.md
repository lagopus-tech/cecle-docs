## 4. Operation Process
1. Start the equipment and recall the corresponding product recipe (weight, vibration amplitude, etc.) via the touch screen.
2. Material drops into the central main vibrator pan via the bucket elevator and is evenly distributed to the outer 14 linear vibrator pans.
3. Passing through buffer hoppers, the material is temporarily stored in 14 high-precision weighing hoppers.
4. The modular computer mainboard computes the permutations and combinations of the 14 hoppers' weights in milliseconds.
5. The system automatically selects the hoppers whose sum is the most precise (error controlled within 0.1~1g).
6. The selected hoppers instantly open their gates to discharge, completing one incredibly precise dose, and automatically loop back.
