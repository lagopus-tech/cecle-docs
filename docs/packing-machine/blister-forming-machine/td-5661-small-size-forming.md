## 4. Operation Process
1. Install matched copper mold according to product size, fix mold station and debug vacuum pipelines;
2. Cut plastic sheets to required size, lay flat on the loading platform and clamp for positioning;
3. Set heating temperature, heating duration and vacuum holding time on control panel, start heating preheating;
4. Move heated softened sheet down to fit mold and turn on vacuum negative pressure for forming;
5. Keep vacuum for cooling & shaping, then switch off vacuum and lift heating assembly;
6. Manually take out formed blister trays, trim burrs, and repeat feeding for continuous production.

### FAQs
Q1: Can this machine process eco-friendly APET and PETG sheets?
A1: Yes. Its heating and vacuum system is fully compatible with APET & PETG, delivering stable forming results.

Q2: Can the forming height be increased?
A2: Standard max height is 200mm. Deepened molds and longer strokes can be customized as required.

Q3: Does the machine come with forming molds?
A3: Molds are not included. Copper or aluminum molds need to be customized separately based on your products.

Q4: Is it suitable for mass high-volume production?
A4: 200~300 molds per hour fits sampling and small & medium batches. Fully automatic continuous blister machines are recommended for mass production.

Q5: What is the limit of sheet thickness?
