## 4. Operation Process
1. Install customized forming copper mold according to product specification, fix the mold plate and inspect vacuum & heating pipelines;
2. Cut plastic sheets to required size, place flat on feeding station and complete positioning & clamping;
3. Set heating temperature, preheating duration and vacuum holding time on control panel;
4. Machine automatically starts heating; sheets will move down to fit molds after softening, then vacuum suction activates for molding;
5. Maintain vacuum pressure for cooling and shaping, then heating assembly lifts automatically;
6. Manually take semi-finished products, cut finished goods off waste edges with the standard mold plate;
7. Clean waste materials, reload sheets and repeat cyclic mass production.

### FAQs
Q1: Can this machine process eco-friendly APET and PETG materials?
A1: Yes. Its heating and vacuum system is specially adapted for eco-plastics without whitening or cracking during forming.

Q2: Can the forming height be customized to be higher?
A2: The standard max forming height is 200mm. Extended stroke and deepened molds can be customized based on product depth requirements.

Q3: Does the machine come with forming copper mold and cutting plate?
A3: Standard manual cutting mold plate is included; forming copper molds need to be customized separately according to your products.

Q4: Is it suitable for 24-hour non-stop mass production?
A4: The heavy stable structure supports long-hour continuous production with output of 200–300 molds per hour.

Q5: Are there limits on sheet thickness?
A5: Standard applicable thickness range is 0.2–1.5mm. Sheets beyond this range will suffer uneven heating and defective forming.
