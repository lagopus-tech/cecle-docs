## IV. Operation Guide & Safety

### 1. Standard Workflow
1. **Power Connection**: Plug the machine power cord into a compliant 110V/220V power outlet.
2. **Set Heating Time**: Adjust the heat-sealing time knob according to the thickness and material of the packaging bag.
3. **Position Bag**: Align the mouth of the bag flat and smooth, placing it centered across the lower sealing jaw.
4. **Press Foot Pedal**: Gently press the foot pedal all the way down. The indicator light will illuminate to signal heating. Hold for 1–3 seconds, then release the pedal to complete the seal.
