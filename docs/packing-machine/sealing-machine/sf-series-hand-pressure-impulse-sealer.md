## IV. Operation Guide & Safety

### 1. Standard Workflow
1. **Power Connection**: Plug the power cord into a 110V/220V electrical outlet.
2. **Set Timer Gear**: Rotate the timer knob on the panel according to bag thickness (set to gears 2–4 for thin bags, gears 5–7 for slightly thicker bags).
3. **Position Bag**: Place the mouth of the bag flat and smooth across the silicone sealing strip.
4. **Hand Press Seal**: Press the handle down. The red indicator light will illuminate to begin heating. Once the red light turns off, hold the handle down for an additional 1–2 seconds to ensure cooling and setting, then lift the handle to complete the seal.

### FAQ
* **Q: Why does the seal appear burned or fail to seal properly?**
  * **A**:
    * **If the seal is scorched or torn**: The timer setting is too high; please turn the gear down (e.g., from gear 6 to gear 3).
    * **If it fails to seal / pulls apart easily**: The timer setting is too low or heating time is insufficient; please adjust the gear up appropriately.
* **Q: Can this machine seal kraft paper bags?**
  * **A**: **No.** This plastic shell hand sealer is designed primarily for various **plastic film bags**. Kraft paper bags are too thick for its heating power and pressure capacity.
