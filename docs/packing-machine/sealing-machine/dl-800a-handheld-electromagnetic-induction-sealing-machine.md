## IV. Operation Guide & Safety

### 1. Standard Workflow
1. **Preparation**: Ensure the **aluminum-foil induction liner** is properly placed inside the bottle cap (aluminum foil side facing outward, paper backing facing the inside of the cap), and tighten the cap securely onto the bottle.
2. **Power On**: Plug the power cord into a compliant power outlet and switch on the power switch.
3. **Set Parameters**: Adjust the **sealing time and power** on the control panel according to the bottle mouth diameter and plastic material.
4. **Start Sealing**: Place the sealing head flat and centered over the bottle cap. Press the start button on the handle; the machine will emit an audible beep (typically 1–2 seconds). Remove the sealing head once the beep finishes.
5. **Inspect Seal**: Allow it to cool, then unscrew the cap to verify that the aluminum foil liner is evenly and firmly fused to the bottle rim.

### FAQ
* **Q: Can this machine seal plastic bags?**
  * **A**: **No.** The DL-800A is an electromagnetic induction sealing machine **strictly designed for sealing aluminum foil liners onto the mouths of bottles (plastic or glass)**. It cannot be used to seal plastic film bags.
* **Q: Why is the aluminum foil not sticking to the bottle mouth after sealing?**
  * **A**: There are three common causes:
    1. The sealing time or power settings are too low; please increase the parameters appropriately.
    2. The bottle cap was not tightened securely, preventing tight contact between the foil liner and the bottle rim.
    3. The aluminum foil liner was inserted upside down (the foil side must face downward toward the bottle mouth).
* **Q: Can it be used on metal bottle caps?**
  * **A**: **Absolutely not.** The electromagnetic induction principle heats up metal caps directly, which will cause severe overheating and burn out the machine. It is strictly for plastic or non-metal caps.
