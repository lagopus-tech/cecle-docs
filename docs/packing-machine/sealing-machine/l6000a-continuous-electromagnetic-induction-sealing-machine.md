Purpose-built for medium-to-large scale automated packaging lines, it features an adjustable conveyor belt and high-frequency induction heating to instantly bond aluminum-foil liners onto plastic and glass bottles. A reliable, high-capacity packaging workhorse for automated production.

## IV. Operation Guide & Safety

### 1. Standard Workflow
1. **Preparation**: Ensure that the **aluminum-foil induction liner** is placed inside the bottle cap and the cap is tightened securely onto the bottle.
2. **Model Selection & Height Adjustment**: Select either the horizontal or vertical model based on your facility layout, and adjust the height of the induction head to match the bottle height.
3. **Power On**: Connect the power cord to a 110V or 220V power supply and turn on the main power switch.
4. **Parameter Setting**: Set the appropriate conveyor belt speed according to the bottle material and caliber.
5. **Start Operation**: Turn on the conveyor belt and place filled bottles onto the belt sequentially. The bottles will automatically pass through the induction zone to complete the aluminum foil seal.
