## VI. Workflow

1. **Automatic Feeding**: The matching Z-type elevator automatically lifts and feeds granular materials from the floor storage hopper into the packaging machine's weighing/rotary cup hopper.
2. **Film Unwinding**: Load the roll film onto the machine spindle, feeding the packaging material smoothly through tension guide rollers into the bag-making zone.
3. **UV Sterilization (Optional)**: Turn on the optional UV germicidal lamp to continuously irradiate and sterilize the roll film surface prior to forming.
4. **Film Forming & Vertical Sealing**: The flat film is folded into a tubular shape by the former, and the vertical sealing mechanism heats and seals the overlapping edges of the film.
5. **Quantitative Dosing & Filling**: The rotary cup system or combination scale system accurately doses the target weight of granular materials, smoothly dropping them into the formed film tube.
6. **Horizontal Sealing & Coding**: The horizontal sealing jaws heat-seal the top of the pouch and the bottom of the next pouch, while the steel-stamp or ribbon coder imprints the batch number.
7. **Cutting & Collection**: The integrated cutter cuts the sealed pouch according to the chosen flat or serrated cutting style, dropping finished granule sachets into the collection area automatically.
