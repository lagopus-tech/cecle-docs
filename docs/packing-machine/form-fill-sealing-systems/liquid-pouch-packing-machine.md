## IV. Sales Recommendations & FAQ

### Sales Recommendations
* **How to choose between the SJ Series and SJZF Series?**
  * If your packaging film is plain color or single-layer film without printing alignment requirements, and you pursue ultimate cost-effectiveness, the standard **SJ-1000** or **SJ-2000** is recommended.
  * If your packaging film features exquisite brand logos or patterns requiring uniform image placement on every sachet, or if you plan to use composite film, choosing the **SJZF-1000** or **SJZF-2000** equipped with a photoelectric sensor and stepping motor is strongly recommended.
* **How to choose between the 1000 and 2000 Models?**
  * For package volumes between 50ml and 500ml with high-speed production demands (e.g., water sachets, small milk pouches), choose the **1000 model**.
  * For larger capacity requirements between 200ml and 1000ml, choose the **2000 model**.

## VI. Workflow

1. **Film Unwinding**: Mount the packaging roll film onto the machine holder, and feed the film smoothly through guide rollers.
2. **UV Sterilization (Optional)**: Turn on the optional UV germicidal lamp for real-time irradiation and sterilization of the film surface.
3. **Film Drawing & Registration**: The film-drawing mechanism (adopting a stepping motor and photoelectric eye on the SJZF series) accurately pulls and positions the film into the bag former.
4. **Vertical Sealing**: The film is formed into a tube shape by the former, and the vertical sealer heats and seals the overlapping edge.
5. **Liquid Filling**: Material is injected through the filling pipe into the bottom-sealed film tube.
6. **Horizontal Sealing & Coding**: The horizontal sealing mold heat-seals the top of the sachet, while the steel-stamp or ribbon coder imprints the batch number.
7. **Cutting & Output**: The cutter cuts and separates the sealed packaging bags, and finished sachets slide out automatically for collection.
