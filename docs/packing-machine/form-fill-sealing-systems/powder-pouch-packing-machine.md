## I. Product Overview

* **Product Category**: Vertical Form Fill Seal Machine for Powder / Automatic Auger Powder Packaging Machine
* **Application Scenarios**: Widely used for automatic sachet and pouch packaging of various fine and granular powders, such as milk powder, coffee powder, flour, starch, protein powder, spice powders, medical powders, and chemical additives.
* **Core Functions**:
  * **Automatic Roll-Film Forming**: Automatically draws and wraps flat film into a tubular shape, combining vertical sealing, volumetric auger dosing, and horizontal sealing.
  * **Auger Screw Dosing System**: High-precision screw metering system designed specifically for free-flowing and non-free-flowing powders, ensuring consistent fill weights.
  * **Flexible Sealing & Cutting Options**: Supports 3-side seal, 4-side seal, or back seal configurations based on client specifications, with options for flat or serrated cutting.
  * **Batch Coding & Hygiene Control**: Standard steel-stamp coding for batch numbers/dates (with optional ribbon coder upgrade), and optional UV sterilization for film sanitization.

## VI. Workflow

1. **Automatic Material Feeding (Optional)**: The optional screw feeder automatically lifts and feeds powder material from the floor bin into the machine hopper.
2. **Film Unwinding**: Load the roll film onto the spindle, guiding the packaging material smoothly through tension rollers.
3. **UV Sterilization (Optional)**: Turn on the optional UV germicidal lamp to sterilize the film surface continuously prior to forming.
4. **Film Forming & Vertical Sealing**: The flat film is shaped into a tube by the former, and the vertical sealing mechanism seals the overlapping film edges.
5. **Auger Screw Dosing & Filling**: The high-precision auger screw rotates to accurately dose and drop the target weight of powder into the formed bag.
6. **Horizontal Sealing & Coding**: The horizontal sealing jaws heat-seal the top of the pouch, while the steel-stamp or ribbon coder imprints the batch number.
7. **Cutting & Collection**: The integrated cutter cuts the sealed package (flat cut or serrated cut), dropping the finished powder sachet into the collection zone.
