### 2. Series Models & Application Scenarios Details

1. **Liquid Vertical Form Fill Seal Machine Series (SJ / SJZF Series)**
   * **Features**: Engineered for low-viscosity liquids. The SJ series offers economical standard performance (single-layer film only), while the SJZF series features photoelectric sensors and stepper motors for precise image registration and composite film compatibility.
   * **Best For**: Water bottling plants, dairies, and liquid seasoning packagers.

2. **Paste Vertical Form Fill Seal Machine Series (PA Series)**
   * **Features**: Designed for high-viscosity and semi-fluid items with anti-drip paste pumps. Supports custom molding for 3-side, 4-side, or back-sealing styles.
   * **Best For**: Condiment manufacturers, cosmetic sachet producers, and honey/ketchup packaging lines.

3. **Powder Vertical Form Fill Seal Machine Series (P Series)**
   * **Features**: Equipped with a precision auger screw metering mechanism for fine powder control, with options to integrate screw feeders for dust-free automated feeding.
   * **Best For**: Milk powder, coffee, spice blends, and pharmaceutical/chemical powder packaging.

4. **Granule Vertical Form Fill Seal Machine Series (G Series)**
   * **Features**: Utilizes high-efficiency rotary cup systems or upgradeable multi-head combination scales, perfectly paired with Z-type elevators for automated material handling.
   * **Best For**: Nut processors, snack food factories, seed packaging, and tea distribution.

### 3. Key Differences & Selection Recommendations

When recommending models to customers, guide them through three main criteria:

1. **Selection by Material State:**
   * **Liquids (Water, Milk, Juice)**: Choose the **Liquid VFFS Machine** (SJ/SJZF series). Choose **SJZF** if graphic alignment or composite film is required.
   * **Pastes & Sauces (Ketchup, Shampoo, Honey)**: Choose the **Paste VFFS Machine** (PA series).
   * **Powders (Milk powder, Flour, Coffee)**: Choose the **Powder VFFS Machine** (P series) alongside an optional screw feeder.
   * **Granules (Nuts, Candies, Seeds)**: Choose the **Granule VFFS Machine** (G series), upgrading to a combination scale for strict weighing accuracy.

2. **Selection by Filling Range & Capacity:**
   * **Small Sachets / Sample Packs (5ml-100ml or 5g-100g)**: Select compact entry models like Liquid SJ-1000/SJZF-1000, Paste PA100, Powder P100, or Granule G100.
   * **Large Pouches / Bulk Packaging (200ml-2000ml or 250g-5000g)**: Select heavy-duty industrial models like Liquid 2000 series, Paste PA500, Powder P500, or Granule G500.
