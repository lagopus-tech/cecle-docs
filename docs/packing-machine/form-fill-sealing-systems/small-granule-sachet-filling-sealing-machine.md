This product is an efficient, fully automatic small granule vertical packaging machine designed specifically for small-scale factories, startup businesses, and small-batch production lines. ## VI. Workflow

1. **Film Loading & Setup**: Install the packaging roll film onto the film frame, thread it through the bag former, and configure the target bag length and sealing temperature on the control panel.
2. **Material Loading**: Pour the granular product into the top sub-packaging hopper.
3. **Automatic Dosing & Bag Forming**: The sub-packaging unit automatically weighs/dispenses the preset weight, while the machine pulls and forms the film into a bag shape.
4. **Filling & Sealing/Cutting**: Granules drop into the formed bag; the heat-sealing mechanism completes the top seal of the filled bag and the bottom seal of the next bag, followed by cutter separation.
5. **Coding & Alignment (Optional)**: If equipped with a ribbon coder and optical sensor, the machine automatically prints the production date and aligns with printed designs during sealing and cutting.
