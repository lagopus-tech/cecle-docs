## VI. Workflow

1. **Film Unwinding**: Load the roll film onto the machine spindle, feeding the film material smoothly through guide pulleys.
2. **UV Sterilization (Optional)**: Turn on the optional UV germicidal lamp to sterilize the film surface continuously prior to bag forming.
3. **Film Forming & Sealing**: The flat film is smoothly shaped into a tube by the former, and the vertical sealing mechanism seals the overlapping edges.
4. **Quantitative Paste Filling**: The paste pump precisely meters and injects the target amount of sauce, honey, or cream into the formed film tube.
5. **Horizontal Sealing & Coding**: The horizontal sealing jaws heat-seal the top of the current bag and bottom of the next bag, while the steel-stamp or ribbon coder imprints the batch number.
6. **Cutting & Collection**: The integrated cutter cuts the sealed bag (flat cut or serrated cut) to drop the finished paste sachet into the collection area.
