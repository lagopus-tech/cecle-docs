## IV. Sales Recommendations & FAQ

### Sales Recommendations
* **Material Viscosity & Flowability Confirmation**: Before recommending a model to a customer, make sure to understand whether they are filling thin liquids or thick pastes, and evaluate whether heating, stirring, or U-shaped hoppers are needed based on the viscosity.
* **Bag Type & Film Specification Verification**: Confirm the required packaging bag style (back-seal, three-side seal, or four-side seal) and roll film width to recommend the most matching model from S2-100 to S2-1000.
* **Optional Feature Tips**: If the customer uses printed film with trademark patterns, recommend the registration mark sensor; if compliant date printing is required, recommend pairing it with a ribbon coder.

## VI. Workflow

1. **Film Loading & Setup**: Place the packaging roll film onto the film holder and thread it through the forming mechanism correctly.
2. **Parameter Configuration**: Power on the machine, set the target bag length, filling volume, and temperature parameters via the control panel.
3. **Material Feeding**: Pour the liquid or paste material into the feeding hopper (utilizing heating or stirring functions if required).
4. **Automatic Forming & Filling**: The machine automatically pulls the film into a tube, forms the bag shape, seals the vertical/bottom edges, and dispenses the exact volume of product into the bag.
5. **Final Sealing & Cutting**: The machine executes top sealing, imprints date/batch numbers (if code printer is equipped), and cuts the finished bag off for output collection.
