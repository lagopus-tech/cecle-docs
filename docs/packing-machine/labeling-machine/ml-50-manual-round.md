### VI. Workflow
1. **Bottle Placement**: Place the cylindrical bottle onto the roller supports and adjust the positioning baffle.
2. **Handle Adjustment**: Align the label starting edge with the bottle surface.
3. **Manual Operation**: Gently rotate the hand crank or roll the bottle.
4. **Label Application**: The label is peeled from the backing paper and smoothly pressed onto the rotating bottle.
5. **Finished Output**: Remove the perfectly labeled bottle from the machine.
