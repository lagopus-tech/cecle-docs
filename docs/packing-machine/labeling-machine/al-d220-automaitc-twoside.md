### VI. Workflow
1. **Automatic Feeding & Spacing**: Containers (round or square) enter the conveyor line and are arranged at equal intervals by the spacing mechanism.
2. **Clamping & Precise Positioning**: The synchronous positioning and pressing device securely clamps the container to ensure the labeling position is accurate.
3. **Label Peeling & Coding**: Sensors trigger label dispensing, while the ribbon coder (optional) simultaneously prints batch or date information on the label.
4. **Smooth Application**: The application roller and pressing mechanism firmly bond the label to the container surface, completely eliminating air bubbles and wrinkles.
5. **Finished Product Output**: Labeled finished products are automatically discharged to the collection area.
