### 2. Core Advantages
1. **High Customizability**:
    * **Label Detection**: Can be equipped with a **transparent label sensor** to ensure precise detection even with high-clarity labels.
    * **Ribbon Coding**: Optional **ribbon coder** allows for printing of production dates or batch numbers directly onto the label during the application process.
    * **Precision Positioning & Dual Labeling**: For products requiring specific orientation (such as aligning to a specific spot on the bottle) or **dual-label application** (front and back), a high-precision **pneumatic positioning system** can be added to ensure uniform labeling angles.
    * **Line Expansion**: Optional **bottle spacing wheel** for smooth feeding, and can be integrated with an automatic **round bottle unscrambler** to build a fully automated packaging line.
2. **Multi-Mode Labeling**: Supports single and dual-label modes to meet diverse product presentation and regulatory labeling requirements.
3. **Compact Design**: The tabletop structure occupies minimal space, making it ideal for small-to-medium factories, studios, or laboratories for efficient mass production.
4. **Stable & Efficient**: Equipped with a high-performance servo drive system, it ensures extremely high consistency in labeling position, effectively avoiding tilting, wrinkles, and bubbles common in manual labeling.

### 6. Workflow
1. **Automatic Feeding**: Bottles are placed onto the conveyor manually or via an unscrambler.
2. **Positioning & Spacing**: If equipped with a spacing wheel, it maintains intervals; for positioning/dual-label needs, the pneumatic system automatically grabs the bottle for precise alignment.
3. **Label Peeling & Coding**: Sensors trigger labeling, while the ribbon coder simultaneously prints the date or code on the label.
4. **Smooth Application**: The bottle rotates, and the label is applied by the application unit, ensuring a smooth, wrinkle-free, and bubble-free finish.
5. **Finished Product Collection**: Labeled products are automatically discharged via the exit plate.
