> **Core Abstract**
> The Automatic Horizontal Round Bottle Labeling Machine is an advanced industrial packaging solution specifically engineered for unstable cylindrical products that cannot stand steadily on standard conveyors, such as medical vials, ampoules, oral liquid bottles, lipstick tubes, and crayons. Ideal for the pharmaceutical, cosmetic, and stationery industries, this specialized horizontal system offers flexible throughput options ranging from a standard 60-150 pcs/min to an optional high-speed 150-300 pcs/min configuration utilizing an elevator and centrifugal feeder setup, all while maintaining $\pm 1\text{mm}$ precision. Featuring customizable automatic feeding mechanisms, transparent label sensors, and on-line ribbon coders, it ensures reliable, high-efficiency, and bubble-free horizontal rolling application.

#### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**
* **Q: Why do I need a horizontal machine instead of a standard vertical labeling machine?**
    * A: Products like ampoules, small vials, and crayons are too narrow or round to stand steadily on a vertical conveyor belt. The horizontal design rolls them sideways, ensuring complete stability and flawless label adhesion.
* **Q: What is the speed difference between standard and high-speed feeding configurations?**
    * A: The standard automatic feeding device delivers 60-150 pieces per minute. For high-volume production, our customized elevator and centrifugal pump feeder setup achieves 150-300 pieces per minute.

### VI. Workflow
1. **Bulk Feed & Sorting**: Products are loaded into either the standard automatic feeder or the optional elevator + centrifugal pump feeding system for smooth orientation.
2. **Horizontal Transport**: The rolling conveyor system holds and rotates the cylindrical items smoothly along the horizontal plane.
3. **Label Peeling & Coding**: Sensors trigger label dispensing while the optional ribbon coder prints batch data onto the label in real-time.
4. **Rolling Application**: The label is firmly and evenly rolled onto the horizontal surface of the moving cylinder, eliminating air bubbles and wrinkles.
5. **Finished Output**: Labeled items are automatically discharged to the collection area.
