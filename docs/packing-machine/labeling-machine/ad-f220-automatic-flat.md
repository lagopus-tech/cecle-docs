### VI. Workflow
1. **Manual/Automatic Placement**: Place the box or bag at the conveyor starting position.
2. **Sensor Trigger**: Sensor detects the object in place and triggers the labeling command.
3. **Label Peeling & Coding**: The dispensing head simultaneously peels the label, while the optional ribbon coder prints date information instantly.
4. **Smooth Pressing**: The pressing mechanism firmly secures the label onto the flat surface, preventing air bubbles.
5. **Finished Product Output**: Completed labeled products automatically flow into the collection area.
