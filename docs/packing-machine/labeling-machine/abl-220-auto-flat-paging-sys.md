### VI. Workflow
1. **Automatic Paging**: Place stacked bags or cartons into the paging machine inlet; they are automatically separated and fed sequentially onto the conveyor belt.
2. **Sensor Trigger**: Sensors precisely capture the material entry position and trigger the labeling command.
3. **Label Peeling & Coding**: The labeling head peels the label while the ribbon coder simultaneously prints production information directly onto the film surface.
4. **Pressing Application**: Pressure devices smoothly apply and secure the label onto the surface of the bag or carton.
5. **Finished Product Output**: Labeled items automatically enter the collection area, completing the automated packaging loop.
