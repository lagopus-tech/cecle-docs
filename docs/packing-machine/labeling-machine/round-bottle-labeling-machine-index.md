### III. Selection Guide: How to Choose the Right Labeling Machine for Your Business?

1. **Choose the Base Model Based on Capacity & Budget**:
   * Daily output within hundreds of pieces with a very low budget: Choose the **Manual Model**.
   * Medium-low daily output, looking to eliminate manual inefficiency: Choose the **Semi-Automatic Model**.
   * Seeking automation with limited space but requiring continuous production: Choose the **Desktop Fully Automatic Model**.
   * Factories owning or planning to build large-scale automated lines, pursuing ultra-high capacity or special customization: Choose the **Vertical Fully Automatic Model**.

2. **Choose Optional Features Based on Special Process Requirements**:
   * **Label Type**: If you use transparent labels, except for the manual model, the other three models can be equipped with a **transparent label sensor**.
   * **Batch & Date Printing**: If compliant production date or batch printing is required, semi-automatic and fully automatic models can be equipped with a **ribbon coder**.
   * **Efficiency & Positioning Upgrade**: Both fully automatic models (desktop and vertical) can be paired with a **bottle unscrambler** to reduce labor; for high precision in label placement (such as specified angle alignment or front/back dual labeling), a custom **cylinder positioning model** can be selected.
