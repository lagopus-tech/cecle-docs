> **Core Abstract**
> The Automatic Vertical Round Bottle Labeling Machine is an advanced industrial solution engineered for high-precision cylindrical and vertical bottle labeling across the daily chemical, pharmaceutical, and food industries. Ideal for hand sanitizer bottles, spray cans, and cosmetic vials, this versatile system delivers a throughput of 30-60 bottles per minute with high servo-driven precision ($\pm 1\text{mm}$). ### VI. Workflow
1. **Automatic Feeding**: Bottles are fed onto the conveyor system manually or via an optional bottle unscrambler.
2. **Spacing & Positioning**: The bottle spacing wheel regulates intervals; if equipped with a pneumatic cylinder, it clamps and rotates the bottle for precise orientation or dual labeling.
3. **Label Peeling & Coding**: Sensors trigger the label dispensing mechanism while the optional ribbon coder prints batch data onto the label.
4. **Smooth Application**: The container rotates as the labeling unit presses the label down securely, ensuring a wrinkle-free and bubble-free finish.
5. **Finished Product Output**: Labeled bottles are smoothly discharged to the collection area.
