### VI. Operating Workflow
1.  **Height Adjustment**: Manually adjust the tray lifting mechanism according to the container height to ensure the lid fits tightly against the indentor.
2.  **Place Container**: The operator securely places the pre-filled container with its lid onto the bottom tray.
3.  **Initiate Operation**: Start via foot pedal or manual switch; the tray automatically rises to clamp the container in place.
4.  **Automatic Seaming**: The indentor rotates the container, and the rollers automatically approach to complete the curling and pressing process.
5.  **Cycle Completion**: Upon completion of sealing, the tray lowers, and the operator removes the finished product to prepare for the next cycle.
