# Product Specification: Capping Machine (SG-1550)

![Capping Machine (SG-1550)](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/17866128672552.png?v=1786626565)

> **core Abstract**: The SG-1550 is a portable, handheld electric capping machine designed for tightening and loosening screw caps (10–50 mm) across pharmaceutical, food & beverage, chemical, and cosmetic industries. Ideal for oversized, tall, or irregular containers, it delivers a capping speed of 20–40 caps/min with an 80W power-efficient motor. Featuring imported ultra-wear-resistant, non-damaging rubber heads and an ergonomic spring balancer for fatigue-free operation, it offers a cost-effective, highly flexible capping solution for startup production lines.

## 1. Product Overview
*   **Category**: Handheld Electric Capping Machine
*   **Applications**: Widely used in pharmaceuticals, food & beverage, chemicals, cosmetics, and other industries. Specially suitable for capping extra-tall, heavy, oversized, or irregularly shaped bottles.
*   **Core Function**: Driven by a handheld motor and paired with rubber capping heads of different specifications to quickly tighten or loosen screw caps on various plastic and glass bottles.

## 2. Key Advantages
1.  **Unrestricted Space Usage**: No limits on bottle height, shape, size, or weight, offering extreme flexibility.
2.  **Complete Accessories**: Comes standard with a spring balancer and 4 sets of rubber inserts covering sizes from 10–50 mm.
3.  **High-Performance Material**: Uses imported ultra-wear-resistant rubber—anti-slip, durable, and leaves no damage on caps.
4.  **Ergonomic Design**: The spring balancer offsets the machine’s weight, enabling long-term, effortless operation.
5.  **Minimal Maintenance**: Low power consumption and low failure rate, making it an ideal choice for startup production lines.
