# Capping Machine - Model: PC-88

![PC-88 Capping Machine](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/1786004487309.png?v=1786004547)

> **core Abstract**: The PC-88 is a high-precision semi-automatic pneumatic capping machine designed for non-abrasive, leak-proof crimping of aluminum and spray-type caps (13–22mm). Built with durable, high-grade pneumatic components and a stable stainless-steel worktable, it reaches operating speeds up to 200 cycles/min, making it an ideal sealing solution for perfume, cosmetics, pharmaceutical, and beverage packaging lines.

## I. Product Overview
* **Product Category**: Semi-automatic Pneumatic Capping Machine.
* **Applications**:
  * Widely used in **perfume**, **pharmaceutical** (oral liquids, ampoules), **cosmetics**, and **beverage** production lines.
  * Suitable for crimping and sealing various spray-type bottle caps and aluminum caps.
* **Core Function**: Utilizes pure pneumatic power via a retractable crimping mechanism to securely and neatly seal caps onto bottle rims.

## II. Key Advantages
1. **Pure Pneumatic Control**: No electrical power required, explosion-proof and safe, ideal for humid or specialized production environments.
2. **Cap Protection**: Precision positioning and crimping technology ensure zero surface abrasion during sealing, maintaining a flawless product appearance.
3. **High Sealing Quality**: Delivers tight, smooth, and firm seals with exceptional leak-proof performance.
4. **Easy Operation**: Equipped with a height-adjustment handwheel to quickly adapt to bottles of various heights, with extremely low maintenance costs.
5. **High Compatibility**: Supports various mainstream cap diameters ranging from 13mm to 22mm.

## VI. Operation Process
1. **Setup**: Connect the air supply and adjust the air pressure to the standard range (0.4–0.6 MPa).
2. **Mold Installation**: Install the corresponding capping mold based on the cap diameter (e.g., 15mm).
3. **Height Positioning**: Place the bottle and turn the handwheel to set the capping device at the appropriate sealing height.
4. **Execution**: Position the bottle with its cap on the base, trigger the pneumatic switch, and the machine will automatically crimp and release.
5. **Quality Check**: Remove the bottle, inspect for a smooth and tight seal, and proceed with continuous production.
