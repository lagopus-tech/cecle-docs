*   **Q: Can this machine cap plastic caps?**
    *   A: **Yes.** The DK-50 series includes models specifically designed for metal caps (aluminum caps) as well as models specifically for plastic pilfer-proof caps.
*   **Q: Can I buy one machine to cap both metal and plastic caps?**
    *   A: **No.** Although they look similar externally, **plastic cap models and metal cap models have significant differences in capping mechanisms and internal construction, and cannot share the same machine.** You need to choose the corresponding model based on the actual bottle cap material used.
*   **Q: Is the capping head prone to wear and tear?**
    *   A: We use high-hardness alloy capping molds, which are extremely durable under normal operation. A necessary spare parts kit is also included in the package for convenient long-term maintenance.

### VI. Workflow
1.  **Height Adjustment**: Manually adjust and lock the machine head position according to bottle height, ensuring the capping head descends to accurately cover the cap.
2.  **Manual Bottle Placement**: The operator places the bottle with the cap on directly under the positioning seat in the working station.
3.  **Capping & Forming**: The machine drives the capping mold downward at a constant speed, locking the cap tightly through mechanical force and engaging the pilfer-proof ring onto the bottle neck.
4.  **Cyclic Production**: After capping is completed, the machine head rises back up. The operator removes the finished product and inserts the next product to be sealed.
