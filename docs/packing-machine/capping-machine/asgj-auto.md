# Product Specification: ASGJ Capping Machine (Automatic Capping Machine)

![ASGJ Capping Machine](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/17861781873056.png?v=1786178222)

> **core Abstract**: The ASGJ Automatic Inline Capping Machine is a high-efficiency packaging solution designed for daily chemical, pharmaceutical, food, and beverage production lines. Featuring a durable 201 stainless steel frame, intelligent PLC touchscreen control, and high-precision German SICK photoelectric sensors, it handles bottle heights from 80–400mm and capping speeds of 30–45 bottles/minute. Specifically optimized for complex closures such as pump heads, duckbill caps, and spray nozzles without cap damage.

## I. Product Overview
*   **Category**: Automatic Inline Capping Machine
*   **Application Scenarios**: Suitable for automated production lines in the daily chemical, food, pharmaceutical, and beverage industries. Especially ideal for the following bottle and cap types:
    *   **Personal Care Products**: Pump bottles for shampoo, body wash, etc.
    *   **Pharmaceuticals & Beverages**: Oral liquid bottles, various screw-cap beverage bottles, spray bottles.
    *   **Bottle Compatibility**: Handles a wide range of bottle types with heights between 80–400mm.
*   **Core Function**: Automatically feeds bottles via conveyor belt, utilizes photoelectric sensors to ensure "no bottle, no cap feeding/capping," and uses PLC control to precisely execute screw-cap sealing.

## II. Core Advantages
1.  **Smart Control System**: Uses a Taiwan-brand PLC touch screen with digital settings, making operation intuitive, simple, and exceptionally stable.
2.  **High-Sensitivity Sensing**: Equipped with German SICK photoelectric sensors for precise bottle detection, ensuring no capping occurs without a bottle present.
3.  **Durable & Sturdy Build**: The main frame and body are constructed from 201 stainless steel, meeting hygiene standards and allowing easy cleaning.
4.  **High Adaptability**: Capping speed, conveyor belt speed, and capping head height are all adjustable for seamless integration into existing production lines.
5.  **Modular Customization**: Comes standard with a 2-meter conveyor belt; capping heads can be customized to single-head, double-head, or multi-head setups based on customer needs.

## VI. Working Process

1.  **Infeed**: Bottles enter the capping station smoothly via the front-end conveyor belt.
2.  **Photoelectric Sensing**: German SICK sensors detect bottles entering the capping area.
3.  **Cap Placement/Sorting**: Caps are placed automatically (optional cap dispenser) or manually pre-placed before moving under the capping head.
4.  **Capping & Sealing**: A pneumatic bottle clamping mechanism holds the bottle in place while the capping head presses down and rotates according to preset torque to complete sealing.
5.  **Outfeed**: Sealed bottles continue down the conveyor belt to the next process (e.g., labeling or inkjet coding).
