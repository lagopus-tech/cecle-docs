> **core Abstract**: The DDX-450 is a semi-automatic electric desktop capping machine designed for tightening plastic screw caps (10–50 mm diameter) on bottles ranging from 40 to 200 mm in height. ### VI. Operational Workflow
1.  **Equipment Adjustment**: Manually adjust the stroke height and positioning base according to the bottle height and cap size to be processed.
2.  **Positioning the Bottle**: The operator manually places the bottle (with cap pre-placed) at the positioning block on the work table.
3.  **Capping Operation**: The machine moves up and down at a constant speed; the capping head descends to engage the cap and tightens it via friction.
4.  **Manual Turnover**: During continuous operation, the operator manually removes the sealed finished product and loads a new bottle according to the machine's operational rhythm.
