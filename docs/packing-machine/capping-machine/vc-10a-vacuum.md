# Product Specification: VC-10A Vacuum Capping Machine

![VC-10A Capping Machine](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/17861716985709.png?v=1786171731)

> **core Abstract**: The VC-10A is a tabletop semi-automatic vacuum capping machine designed for glass bottles with tinplate caps. Featuring a dual-workstation design for enhanced production efficiency (0–600 bottles/hour) and 1:1 custom-fit molds, it integrates pneumatic and electrical operation to deliver reliable vacuum sealing for food processing and condiment industries like jams, sauces, honey, and pickles.

## 1. Product Overview
*   **Category**: Tabletop Semi-automatic Vacuum Capping Machine
*   **Applications**: Specially designed for glass bottles and tinplate caps requiring high vacuum sealing. Widely applied in:
    *   **Food Processing**: Jams, chili sauces, canned peaches, honey, pickles, etc.
    *   **High Vacuum Demand Scenarios**: Removes air inside the bottle to effectively prevent oxidation and extend shelf life.
*   **Core Function**: Combining mechanical, electrical, and pneumatic integration, the machine completes the "vacuuming + capping" action within the vacuum chamber, ensuring a preset high vacuum level inside the bottle and achieving a tight seal.

## 2. Key Advantages
1.  **Dual-Workstation Efficient Design (2 Workstations)**: Equipped with two operating stations for alternating cycle operation, offering higher production efficiency than standard single-station models.
2.  **Precision Customized Molds**: 1:1 mold customization based on customer-provided bottle and cap samples to guarantee 100% fit, preventing cap slipping or damage.
3.  **Compact and Lightweight Body**: Tabletop structural design with dimensions of only 950*650*400 mm, saving space and making installation and transport extremely convenient.
4.  **Stable and Reliable Performance**: Key pneumatic and electrical components are selected from world-renowned brands, ensuring low failure rates and long-term stable operation.
5.  **Simple Operation & Maintenance**: Optimized through years of R&D, the operating logic is intuitive. Operators can easily run it with basic training, resulting in low maintenance costs.

## 6. Working Process

1.  **Preparation**: Place the filled bottles with caps loosely placed on top into the dual-workstation molds.
2.  **Chamber Closing & Vacuuming**: Press the start switch; the vacuum chamber automatically lowers to seal under cylinder drive. The vacuum pump starts simultaneously, extracting air from the vacuum chamber.
3.  **Capping & Sealing**: Once the preset vacuum level is reached, the capping unit lowers to rapidly rotate and lock the tinplate cap tightly.
4.  **Pressure Relief & Chamber Raising**: After capping, the system automatically releases pressure, and the vacuum chamber rises back to its reset position.
5.  **Finished Product Output**: The operator removes the sealed bottles and prepares for the next set of operations.
