### II. Core Advantages
1.  **Full Material Compatibility**: The precision-engineered capping head adapts to both **rigid aluminum caps** and **flexible plastic caps**. It applies even torque without damaging caps or leaving scratches.

3.  **Sensor-Driven Precision Operation**: Features **German SICK photoelectric sensors** to achieve "No Bottle, No Cap Feeding/No Capping," effectively reducing aluminum cap deformation and plastic cap waste.
4.  **Durable High-Quality Frame**: Full stainless steel frame (body built with 201 stainless steel), rust-proof and corrosion-resistant, meeting industrial hygiene standards.
5.  **Customizable Design**:
    *   Standard configuration includes 6 capping heads, supporting dual capping heads operating simultaneously for high efficiency and easy adjustment.
    *   The number of capping heads (single-head, double-head, etc.) and conveyor specifications can be customized based on client requirements.

### VI. Workflow
1.  **Automatic Bottle Feeding**: Bottles smoothly enter the workstation via the 2-meter stainless steel conveyor belt.
2.  **Material Sensing**: German SICK sensors accurately identify bottle positions to prevent misfires.
3.  **Precise Cap Placement**: Guides aluminum or plastic caps to ensure they are placed upright on the bottle mouth.
4.  **Synchronous Capping**: Dual capping heads move down vertically at the same time to secure the caps according to the preset torque.
5.  **Finished Product Discharge**: After capping is complete, the positioning mechanism releases, and finished products move along the conveyor to the next packaging process.
