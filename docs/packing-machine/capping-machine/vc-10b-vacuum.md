## 6. Working Process

1.  **Manual Bottle Placement**: The operator places the glass jar/bottle (filled and capped) into the mold.
2.  **Start / Vacuum Chamber Closing**: Press start; driven by the air cylinder, the vacuum chamber lowers automatically to form a sealed space.
3.  **Vacuuming**: The vacuum pump starts, rapidly extracting air from the chamber and jar to reach the preset vacuum level.
4.  **Capping**: The capping head lowers driven by pneumatic components and tightens the cap according to the preset torque.
5.  **Reset / Bottle Removal**: The vacuum chamber automatically rises, completing one working cycle, and the operator removes the finished product.
