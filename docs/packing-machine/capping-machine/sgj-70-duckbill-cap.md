### VI. Operating Procedure
1.  **Height Adjustment**: Adjust the vertical position of the capping head bracket according to the height of the bottle to be processed.
2.  **Parameter Setup**: Set the capping time, speed, and torque level on the control panel based on the cap material.
3.  **Bottle & Cap Loading**: Manually pre-screw the cap onto the bottle neck and place the bottle into the positioning area on the workbench.
4.  **Press & Tighten**: Trigger the sensor or foot switch. The pneumatic cylinder presses the capping head down, and the dual motors start simultaneously to complete the tightening.
5.  **Reset & Unload**: Upon reaching the preset torque, the capping head automatically retracts upward. Remove the finished bottle and proceed to the next cycle.
