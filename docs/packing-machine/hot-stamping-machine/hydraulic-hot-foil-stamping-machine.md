### VI. Workflow
1. **Initial Preparation**: Ensure the machine is filled with HM46 anti-wear hydraulic oil and connected to the correct power supply (220V/380V).
2. **Die & Foil Setup**: Mount the custom hot stamping die onto the heating plate (using an optional honeycomb plate if required) and thread the hot stamping foil through the feed path.
3. **Parameter Setting**: Adjust the operating temperature, dwell time, and hydraulic pressure based on your standard model or custom-built specifications.
4. **Product Placement**: Place the target product (metal, steel, aluminum, soap, or rigid packaging) onto the worktable (or trigger the optional pneumatic/servo sliding table to load).
5. **Safety Activation & Stamping**: Engage the dual-button start while the photoelectric protection and light curtain ensure safety. The hydraulic press descends to execute high-tonnage stamping.
6. **Finished Product Removal**: The hydraulic plate retracts automatically, allowing you to safely retrieve the finished high-precision stamped product.
