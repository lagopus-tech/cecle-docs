> **Core Abstract:** The Plastic Seal Serial Number Hot Foil Stamping Machine (Model: SHS-10) is a specialized pneumatic stamping system engineered exclusively for high-efficiency sequential numbering and logo branding on plastic security seals. Perfectly tailored for security seal manufacturers and logistics tagging industries, it features a dual-head configuration that allows simultaneous hot stamping of logos and consecutive serial numbers (typically processing 10 seals per batch). ### VI. Workflow
1. **Air & Power Connection**: Connect the machine to an external air compressor and plug into a 220V single-phase power source.
2. **Die & Foil Setup**: Ensure the custom seal mold and dual stamping heads (numbering machine + logo die) are securely installed, and thread the hot stamping foil through the path.
3. **Seal Placement**: Place a batch of plastic seals (typically 10 pieces) neatly into the dedicated custom mold on the worktable.
4. **Pneumatic Stamping**: Press the pneumatic push button or step on the foot switch to trigger the automated stamping cycle. The dual heads descend to imprint both the logo and sequential numbers crisply onto the seals using the foil.
5. **Collection & Inspection**: Retrieve the finished batch of serialized plastic seals, inspect the imprint clarity, and proceed with packaging.
