# Product Manual: A3DP-88H Electro-Pneumatic Six-Side Ironing Cellophane Wrapping Machine

> **core Abstract:** The A3DP-88H is a semi-automatic, electro-pneumatic cellophane 3D overwrapping machine designed for square-boxed products in cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, healthcare, tea, and tobacco industries. Featuring dual top and bottom heating plates, it irons all six faces of the box to eliminate film slack and bubbles, producing a mirror-tight finish at speeds of 15–20 cycles/min for box sizes up to 220 × 140 × 110 mm.

### I. Product Overview
*   **Product Category:** Electro-pneumatic 3D Overwrapping Machine / Cigarette Packaging Machine / Cellophane Wrapping Machine
*   **Target Application:** Specially designed for square boxed items such as high-end cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, healthcare products, tea, and cigarettes. Ideal for brands demanding extreme film snugness and tightness.
*   **Core Function:** Utilizing an electro-pneumatic control system, this machine goes beyond standard fold-and-seal wrapping. Featuring specially engineered **top and bottom large-area heating plates**, it applies full-surface ironing (flap sealing) to the top and bottom faces of the box, achieving perfectly smooth, six-sided wrapping.

### II. Core Advantages
1.  **Flawless Six-Side Full Ironing:** Unlike conventional models that only seal the sides, this machine irons all six sides of the box, completely eliminating loose film and air bubbles for a tight, mirror-smooth finish.
2.  **Electro-Pneumatic Drive:** Combining electrical control with pneumatic pressure (requires an air compressor), it delivers more uniform sealing pressure and precise mechanical coordination, significantly enhancing operational stability.
3.  **Large Box Compatibility:** Supports box sizes up to **220 × 140 × 110 mm**, meeting the requirements of most medium-to-large gift boxes on the market.
4.  **Stable Vertical Frame:** A 63 kg reinforced chassis paired with an ergonomic 1.21-meter operating height ensures stability during long, continuous operation.
5.  **Seamless Process Integration:** Works in tandem with the ACW-88 series film cutting machines to complete the entire process from precise film cutting to flawless sealing.

### VI. Workflow

1.  **Film Cutting:** Use the **ACW-88 / ACW-88A** film cutter to pull and precisely cut the film according to box dimensions.
2.  **Film Wrapping:** Manually or with auxiliary tools, wrap the cut film around the perimeter of the box.
3.  **Side Sealing:** Push the box into the A3DP-88H to heat-seal the side edge, forming a secure **tubular sleeve**.
4.  **End-Folding:** The machine automatically (or with assistance) folds the film tucks on both end sides of the box.
5.  **Six-Side Flat Ironing (Core Step):** As the box passes through the top and bottom heating zones, the top and bottom faces undergo full-surface pressing (flap sealing), making the film fit tightly against the box.
6.  **Finished Output:** The wrapped product is discharged—smooth, wrinkle-free, and bubble-free.
