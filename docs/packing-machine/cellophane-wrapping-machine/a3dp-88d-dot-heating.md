---

### II. Core Advantages: Point Heat Sealing Technology
1.  **Perfect Match for Square End-Faces:** Point heat sealing is the core feature for wrapping **cubic boxes**. It ensures the film forms a perfectly symmetrical "X" or diamond-shaped fold pattern at the center of the square end-face.
2.  **Enhanced Visual Appeal:** Compared to standard flat plate ironing, point heat sealing applies precise heat only at the central fold point. This avoids film wrinkling or stretch marks caused by large-area heating, resulting in a cleaner, more premium packaging finish.
3.  **Protects Box Artwork:** Localized heating effectively safeguards the original delicate printing on the box, preventing discoloration or damage caused by excessive heat exposure.
4.  **Versatile Configurations:** Supports switching between point heat sealing and flat ironing plates, allowing it to easily handle both conventional rectangular boxes and high-precision square box sealing.
5.  **Precise Pneumatic Control:** A stable power drive ensures consistent point sealing position for every cycle, raising product standardization levels.

### VI. Workflow

1.  **Initial Film Wrapping:** Pull out the film to wrap around the box, then heat-seal the side seam to form a tubular sleeve.
2.  **Pre-stage Cutting:** Use **ACW-88/88A** to cut the film to the exact required length.
3.  **Pneumatic Folding:** Turn on the air compressor and place the wrapped box into the station. Pneumatic cylinders automatically execute left, right, top, and bottom folding.
4.  **Point Sealing & Shaping:** For cubic boxes, the point sealing head applies instantaneous localized heat at the center of the end-face to lock folds in place.
5.  **Finished Output:** The wrapping is complete, presenting a tight, symmetrical, art-grade 3D overwrapped product.
