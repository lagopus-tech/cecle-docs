# Product Manual: A3DP-88A Cellophane Wrapping Machine
**Model:** A3DP-88A (Customized Pneumatic Version)  
**Name:** Cellophane Wrapping Machine

> **core Abstract (Core Summary)**  
> The A3DP-88A is a customized pneumatic-electric cellophane overwrapping machine designed for mold-free 3D fold wrapping of square box products using transparent BOPP film. Operating at speeds up to 20 cycles/min, it is ideal for cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, premium tea, and gift box packaging. Its standout mold-free adjustable design supports box height variations up to 70mm, eliminating expensive tooling costs for manufacturers with diverse product lines. Featuring efficient aluminum heat sealing and optional top/bottom ironing plates, this unit provides tight, wrinkle-free, tamper-evident packaging.

---

### I. Product Overview
*   **Category:** Pneumatic-Electric Integrated Cellophane Wrapping Machine / Mold-Free Overwrapping Machine / 3D Packaging Machine (Cellophane Wrapping Machine)
*   **Application Scenarios:** Suitable for the transparent film overwrapping of square products such as cosmetic boxes, medicine boxes, premium tea, gift boxes, etc. Ideal for production environments with diverse product specifications where frequent mold changes are undesirable.
*   **Core Function:** This equipment must be used with the ACW-88/88A film cutting machine. It uses pneumatic power to automatically fold and seal the film. Its key highlight is **requiring no fixed molds**, adjusting easily to accommodate various product specifications within a certain range.

---

### II. Core Advantages
1.  **No Molds Needed Design:** Completely eliminates expensive mold costs typical of traditional wrapping machines and allows fast production line adjustment, significantly reducing packaging costs for multi-specification products.
2.  **Highly Customizable:** Professionally customized according to box height ranges, with a maximum-to-minimum height span of up to **70mm** (applicable height range: 20mm - 110mm).
3.  **Pneumatic-Electric Integrated Drive:** Must be connected to an air compressor to operate. Sealing pressure is stronger and more uniform, resulting in tighter packaging compared to traditional mechanical designs.
4.  **Optional Expandability:** Can be equipped with an optional **"spot ironing device"** or **"top & bottom ironing plates"** to achieve smooth, wrinkle-free packaging across all six sides of the box.
5.  **High-Efficiency Aluminum Plate Heating:** Employs aluminum heat transfer technology for rapid temperature ramp-up and precise control, ensuring smooth, neat seals.

### VI. Workflow

1.  **Film Cutting:** Use the **ACW-88 or ACW-88A** film cutting machine to accurately pull out and cut the film to length according to the box size.
2.  **Film Wrapping:** Manually or with assistance, wrap the cut film evenly around the perimeter of the box.
3.  **Feeding:** Feed the pre-wrapped product into the A3DP-88A workstation.
4.  **Sealing:** The machine uses pneumatic pressure to complete side heat-sealing (forming a tubular sleeve) and automatic folding at both ends.
5.  **Fine Ironing (Optional):** The product passes through the optional top & bottom ironing plate area for 6-side flat ironing, achieving a perfect, tight-fitting seal.
6.  **Discharge:** Delivers high-quality finished packaging.
