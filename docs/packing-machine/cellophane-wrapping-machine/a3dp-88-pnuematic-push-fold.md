### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
* **Q: Why must it be used with a film cutting machine?**
  * A: The cellophane wrapping machine itself does not cut large rolls of film; it requires the film cutting machine to pre-cut the film into sheets matched to the box dimensions.
* **Q: Can I prepare my own air compressor?**
  * A: Yes, as long as your air compressor can stably supply clean air at a pressure of 0.4–0.8 MPa.
* **Q: What are the benefits of "four-side sealing"?**
  * A: Four-side heat sealing distributes heat more evenly around the box, eliminating wrinkles and giving the product a tight, premium finish similar to a high-end cigarette box.
* **Q: Do we need to make molds for the A3DP88?**
  * A: Yes, the molds need to be customized according to the box dimensions.
* **Q: Is it convenient to change molds on the A3DP88?**
  * A: Yes, mold changing will take no more than half an hour. Our technicians will provide professional mold replacement videos.
* **Q: What if the box size exceeds the maximum limit of A3DP-88?**
  * A: We can customize and enlarge the machine according to customers' box dimensions.
* **Q: How to achieve better packing results when using the A3DP88 for boxes?**
  * A: The dot hot sealing function is recommended.

## VI. Workflow & Operating Steps
1. **Film Cutting & Preparation**: Use the **ACW-88/88A Film Cutting Machine** to pull out the BOPP film, wrap the box, and perform side heat sealing to form a tubular film sheath.
2. **Air Supply Connection**: Turn on the **air compressor** and ensure the pressure gauge on the A3DP-88 reaches the operating range (0.4–0.8 MPa).
3. **Preheating**: Set the required temperature and wait for the aluminum heating plates to reach the set temperature.
4. **Placement & Folding**: Feed the pre-wrapped box into the inlet; the pneumatic system automatically completes the 3D fold-wrapping.
5. **Heat Sealing & Shaping**: Perform thermal setting on the seals via spot sealing or four-side heating plates to ensure a tight, flawless finish.
6. **Finished Product Output**: The packaged product is automatically ejected with a smooth, premium finish, enhancing overall product value.
