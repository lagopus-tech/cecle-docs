## 2. Core Advantages
1.  **Key Highlight: Precision Spot Sealing System**  
    For square boxes or items with large overlapping side areas, conventional large-area heating often causes wrinkles at the film overlaps. **The ACW-88D is equipped with a dedicated spot sealer** that applies heat precisely at the side center point. This design ensures the film fits tightly around the box, effectively preventing edge curling, eliminating redundant wrinkles, and delivering a smoother, more refined finish.
2.  **High-Precision Aluminum Heating Plate:** Made of premium-grade aluminum for even heat distribution, fast heating, and precise temperature control, ensuring flat, large-area sealing without damaging the film.
3.  **Integrated Hot Wire Cutting System:** Built-in hot wire cutter provides quick, clean film cuts and automatically seals the edges to prevent fraying.
4.  **Tension Control System:** Professional tensioning mechanism allows flexible adjustment of film feeding tightness based on film thickness, giving the wrapped film a firm, sleek texture.
5.  **Low Maintenance Cost:** Robust all-mechanical structure combined with modular circuit design ensures durability. Routine maintenance requires only simple cleaning of the heating surface, offering a long service life.

### FAQ:
*   **Q: Why do square box sides often wrinkle during wrapping?**
    *   A: Square box side folds result in a larger film overlap area. The ACW-88D's spot sealer uses targeted point heating, avoiding thermal shrinkage caused by large-area heating and thus eliminating wrinkles.
*   **Q: Does the machine require assembly upon arrival?**
    *   A: The machine is shipped fully assembled. You only need to attach the tension bracket and preheat the unit before use.
*   **Q: How should I set the aluminum plate temperature?**
    *   A: Generally set between 130°C and 160°C. Fine-tuning should be done based on the material and thickness of the film.

## 6. Workflow

1.  **Preheating Setup:** Turn on the power, set the temperatures for the main heating plate and the spot sealer head, and wait about 3–5 minutes until the set temperatures are reached.
2.  **Film Cutting:** Pull out the film to the desired product size and cut it cleanly using the hot wire cutter.
3.  **Bottom Sealing:** Place the product in the center of the film, fold the overlapping long edges, and smoothly glide it across the main heating plate to complete the bottom seal.
4.  **Side Folding & Spot Sealing:**
    *   Manually fold both side envelop-style folds.
    *   **Align the center of the side fold with the spot sealer heating head and press lightly for 1–2 seconds.**
    *   The film is instantly secured at the center point, creating a smooth side finish without excess wrinkles.
5.  **Quality Check:** Ensure the seal is tight and aesthetically pleasing before completing the packaging process.
