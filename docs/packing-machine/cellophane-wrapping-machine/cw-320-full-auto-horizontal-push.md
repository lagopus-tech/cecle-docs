> **Core Abstract (Core Overview):**
> The **CW-320** is a fully automatic flat-push 3D cellophane overwrapping machine designed for high-speed, skin-tight BOPP film packaging of boxed items. Ideal for pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, food, healthcare, and stationery industries, it delivers elegant, bubble-free seals with optional anti-counterfeiting tear tape. Featuring robust heavy-duty cam transmissions and quick 20-30 minute mold changeovers, the CW-320 offers superior stability, high production output, and versatile multi-specification packaging for growing enterprises.

## 6. Workflow
1.  **Power-On Self-Test**: Connect 220V power supply and air compressor, then turn on the main switch.
2.  **Temperature Setting**: Set the ironing plate temperatures on the touchscreen and wait for the system to preheat to the target values.
3.  **Film Installation**: Install the film roll and tear tape, ensuring the film passes through all tension rollers properly aligned.
4.  **Material Feeding**: Place boxes onto the conveyor belt; the sensor triggers pushing once a box is detected.
5.  **Cyclic Operation**: Flat-push wrapping -> Film cutting -> Side fold sealing -> Shape spot-ironing -> Six-sided ironing.
6.  **Finished Output**: Beautifully wrapped finished products are automatically pushed out to the discharge port.
