### FAQ

**Q1: Are the ACW-88 and A3DP-88A a single machine?**
A: No. They are a combo set. The ACW-88 is a front-end manual film cutter (cuts + wraps the film). The A3DP-88A is a back-end pneumatic sealing machine (auto-folds + heat-seals). They work together as a complete line.

**Q2: Why can't the A3DP-88A handle a height differential greater than 70 mm?**
A: This constraint guarantees the folding arm achieves the most precise folding angle and pressure without changing molds. If you need a wider height range, we recommend ordering two machines or requesting a custom build.

**Q3: Does the A3DP-88A require an air compressor?**
A: Yes. The pneumatic A3DP-88A relies on cylinder pressure to seal the package — delivering stronger force and more consistent packaging. The customer should provide their own air compressor (recommended ≥ 0.6 MPa).

**Q4: Is the ACW-88 durable?**
A: Yes. The machine has a robust structure, industrial-grade aluminum on all key heating components, and a long design life. It comes with a 1-year warranty and lifetime maintenance support.

**Q5: How long until shipment?**
A: Standard ACW-88 units ship within 7 days of deposit. The A3DP-88A requires a sample box for customization — typical lead time is 7 – 10 days after we receive your sample.

**Q6: What about warranty and after-sales?**
A: 1-year warranty on the whole machine (consumables excluded). Lifetime online video support is included. Warranty-period replacement parts are free; shipping is borne by the customer.

**Q7: Do you support voltage customization?**
A: Yes. We can customize to 110 V 60 Hz (North American standard) or 220 V 60 Hz (South African standard) at no extra charge.

## 6. Workflow

### Combo-Line Production Flow (6-Step Standard Operation)

```
Step 1           Step 2           Step 3           Step 4           Step 5           Step 6
Cut Film   →    Wrap Film   →     Feed In    →     Seal       →     Ironing    →    Output
(ACW-88)       (Manual/Aid)      (A3DP-88A)        (A3DP-88A)       (Optional)       (Finished)
```

#### Step 1 — Film Cutting
- **Equipment**: ACW-88 Manual Film Cutting Machine
- **Operation**: Pull BOPP transparent film to the exact length and width per your box size, and cut via the hot-wire cutting device.
- **Key Point**: Use the built-in adjustable cutter to freely control film width — no need to swap narrow film rolls.

#### Step 2 — Film Wrapping
- **Method**: Manual or assisted
- **Operation**: Evenly wrap the cut film around the outer surface of the box.
- **Key Point**: Keep the film flat with no visible wrinkles; cover fully and neatly.

#### Step 3 — Feeding
- **Equipment**: A3DP-88A Feed-In Station
- **Operation**: Place the initially wrapped product into the A3DP-88A folding & sealing station.
- **Key Point**: Center the product and keep the orientation consistent.

#### Step 4 — Sealing
- **Equipment**: A3DP-88A Pneumatic Folding Module
- **Operation**: The machine uses pneumatic pressure to perform side heat sealing (forming a sleeve) and automatic two-end folding.
- **Key Point**: Cylinder pressure ensures tight, uniform seals; aluminum-plate heating ensures flat, clean seal lines.

#### Step 5 — Premium Ironing (Optional)
- **Equipment**: Optional spot ironing device / top & bottom full-coverage ironing plates
- **Operation**: The product passes through the ironing-plate zone for six-side flat ironing.
- **Key Point**: Eliminate all wrinkles for a perfectly flat, skin-tight finish (all six sides flat).

#### Step 6 — Output
- **Output**: High-quality three-dimensional transparent-film wrapped finished product.
- **Downstream**: Directly carton and warehouse, or move on to the next process (labeling / inkjet coding).

### Production-Line Recommendations
- **Operators**: 1 – 2 (1 for ACW-88 film cutting, 1 for A3DP-88A feeding and quality check)
- **Cycle Time**: 15 – 20 times/min (paced by the A3DP-88A)
- **Floor Space**: Reserve a 3 × 2 m² work area (including operation space and WIP buffer zone)
- **Supporting Equipment**: Air compressor ≥ 0.6 MPa (required for A3DP-88A)
