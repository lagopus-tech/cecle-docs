> The **CW-230 Automatic Rotary Cellophane Wrapping Machine** is a high-speed overwrapping solution engineered for large-batch, single-specification packaging in industries like pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, health supplements, and cigarettes. Capable of reaching speeds up to 120 boxes/minute, it uses BOPP film and precision custom molds to deliver tight, wrinkle-free, and anti-counterfeiting film packaging with optional six-side ironing for premium product aesthetics.

### I. Product Overview
*   **Product Category:** Automatic Rotary Cellophane Wrapping Machine / High-Speed Transparent Film Overwrapping Machine / Cigarette Box Overwrapper.
*   **Application Scenarios:** Specially designed for large-batch, single-specification production lines in pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, health supplements, playing cards, cigarettes, etc. Ideal for industrial plants requiring high stability and high output.
*   **Core Functionality:** Utilizes a high-efficiency rotary wrapping structure to automatically perform film feeding & cutting, tear tape application, box wrapping, folding, and heat sealing. Features an electro-pneumatic integrated design, utilizing pneumatic power to ensure tight seal integrity during high-speed operation.

### II. Core Advantages
1.  **Rotary High-Speed Wrapping:** Unlike traditional reciprocating structures, the rotary dial operates smoother with lower noise, achieving wrapping speeds up to 120 boxes/minute.
2.  **Superior Visual Presentation:** Utilizes customized precision molds tailored for 1–2 fixed box sizes, ensuring seamless, wrinkle-free film wrapping with industrial-grade perfection.
3.  **Six-Side Smooth-Pressing Support:** Optional **six-side full ironing (overall press)** or **spot ironing devices** to re-flatten box surfaces, making the film ultra-tight and flush, with a visual finish comparable to premium cigarette packaging.
4.  **Cost Optimization:** Uses BOPP roll film with automatic feeding and supports automatic tear tape application (easy-open strip), providing both anti-counterfeiting functionality and easy opening for consumers.
5.  **Electro-Pneumatic Reliability:** **Requires an air compressor for operation.** Pneumatic cylinder pressure assists mechanical hands during film folding, ensuring uniform sealing pressure on every package during high-volume, continuous high-speed runs.

### VI. Workflow

1.  **Preparation:** Install the BOPP roll film and tear tape, connect the **air compressor**, turn on the power, and preheat the heating plates.
2.  **Infeed:** Boxes automatically and orderly enter the rotary infeed station via the front-end conveyor line.
3.  **Rotary Cut & Wrap:** During rotation, the machine precisely cuts film, attaches the tear tape, and completes initial wrapping.
4.  **Folding & Side-Sealing:** Rotary mechanical arms, assisted by pneumatic components, execute side folding and side heat-sealing in continuous motion.
5.  **Ironing & Shaping (Optional):** Boxes pass through six-side or spot ironing units to press top and bottom surfaces, ensuring a tight and smooth film finish.
6.  **Finished Output:** High-quality wrapped products are automatically conveyed to the buffer area or the next packaging line.
