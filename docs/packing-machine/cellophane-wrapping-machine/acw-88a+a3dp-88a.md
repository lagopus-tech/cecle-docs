### FAQ

**Q1: Are the ACW-88A and A3DP-88A a single machine?**
A: No. They are a combo set. The ACW-88A is a front-end automatic film cutter (cutting + pre-wrapping). The A3DP-88A is a back-end pneumatic folding & sealing machine (auto-folding + heat sealing). Both machines must work together to form a complete line.

**Q2: What is the difference between the ACW-88A and the ACW-88?**
A: The ACW-88A is the automatic model — 15 – 20 times/min, 35 kg, 64 × 56 × 29 cm. The ACW-88 is the manual model — 5 – 10 times/min, 21 kg, 58 × 58 × 28 cm. The ACW-88A's cycle time matches the A3DP-88A exactly, making it the better partner for a high-throughput line; the ACW-88 suits small-batch, budget-first scenarios.

**Q3: Why can't the A3DP-88A handle a height differential greater than 70 mm?**
A: This constraint guarantees the folding arm achieves the most precise folding angle and pressure without changing molds. If you need a wider height range, we recommend ordering two machines or requesting a custom build.

**Q4: Does the A3DP-88A require an air compressor?**
A: Yes. The pneumatic A3DP-88A relies on cylinder pressure to seal the package — delivering stronger force and more consistent packaging. The customer should provide their own air compressor (recommended ≥ 0.6 MPa).

**Q5: Can the machine complete the full packaging action on its own?**
A: The ACW-88A handles film cutting and pre-wrapping. A complete six-side flat fold requires pairing with an A3DP-88A (pneumatic, high efficiency) or 3DP-88 (manual, economical) folding machine. This ACW-88A + A3DP-88A combo is the complete solution.

**Q6: Can a tear tape (gold tear strip) be added?**
A: Yes. The ACW-88A has a built-in tear-tape device, improving anti-counterfeiting and opening convenience.

**Q7: Is the ACW-88A durable?**
A: Yes. The machine has a robust structure, industrial-grade aluminum on all key heating components, and a long design life. It comes with a 1-year warranty and lifetime maintenance support.

**Q8: How long until shipment?**
A: Standard ACW-88A units ship within 7 days of payment. The A3DP-88A requires a sample box for customization — typical lead time is 7 – 10 days after we receive your sample.

**Q9: What about warranty and after-sales?**
A: 1-year warranty on the whole machine (consumables excluded). Within the warranty period, defective parts are supplied free of charge (shipping borne by the customer). Beyond the warranty period, we provide lifetime technical support and parts at cost.

**Q10: Do you support voltage customization?**
A: Yes. We can customize to 110 V 60 Hz (North American standard) or 220 V 60 Hz (South African standard) at no extra charge.

## 6. Workflow

### Combo-Line Production Flow (6-Step Standard Operation)

```
Step 1              Step 2              Step 3              Step 4              Step 5              Step 6
Preparation    →   Cut & Pre-Wrap  →    Feed In       →   Fold & Seal    →   Ironing (Opt.)  →    Output
(ACW-88A)          (ACW-88A)          (A3DP-88A)          (A3DP-88A)          (Optional)          (Finished)
```

#### Step 1 — Preparation & Preheating

- **Equipment**: ACW-88A Automatic Film Cutting Machine
- **Operation**: Load the BOPP film, adjust the film-width cutter and carrier-plate height to suit the box size, then switch on the power and wait for the aluminum plate to reach the set temperature.
- **Key Point**: Ensure the film roll is seated correctly with even tension; never feed product before the temperature is reached.

#### Step 2 — Film Cutting & Pre-Wrapping

- **Equipment**: ACW-88A Automatic Film Cutting Machine
- **Operation**: Pull and cut the exact film length required by the box size using the hot-wire cutting device, and wrap the film around the product.
- **Key Point**: Use the built-in adjustable cutter to freely control film width — no narrow-roll swaps needed. If tear tape is used, feed the gold strip at the same time.

#### Step 3 — Feeding

- **Equipment**: A3DP-88A Feed-In Station
- **Operation**: Place the pre-wrapped product into the A3DP-88A folding & sealing station.
- **Key Point**: Center the product and keep the orientation consistent so the fold is symmetrical.

#### Step 4 — Folding & Sealing

- **Equipment**: A3DP-88A Pneumatic Folding Module
- **Operation**: The machine uses pneumatic pressure to perform side heat sealing (forming a sleeve) and automatic two-end folding.
- **Key Point**: Cylinder pressure ensures tight, uniform seals; aluminum-plate heating ensures flat, clean seal lines without damaging the box.

#### Step 5 — Premium Ironing (Optional)

- **Equipment**: Optional spot ironing device / top & bottom full-coverage ironing plates
- **Operation**: The product passes through the ironing-plate zone for six-side flat ironing.
- **Key Point**: Eliminate all wrinkles for a perfectly flat, skin-tight finish (all six sides flat).

#### Step 6 — Output

- **Output**: High-quality three-dimensional transparent-film wrapped finished product with a tight, skin-tight film appearance.
- **Downstream**: Directly carton and warehouse, or move on to the next process (labeling / inkjet coding).

### Production-Line Recommendations
