## IV. Operation Process
1. Inspect electrical lines prior to startup; clean stains on heat-sealing blades and rubber rollers; verify that optional punching components are installed correctly.
2. Mount the PE film roll onto the unwinding mechanism, threading the film sequentially through guide rollers, tension systems, the heat-sealing station, and the zipper bonding station.
3. Configure bag length, heat-sealing temperature, production speed, and counting parameters on the touch screen HMI.
4. Once the heating station reaches preset process temperatures, start the equipment at low speed to produce trial sample bags.
5. Inspect trial samples for seal strength, zipper bonding quality, dimensions, and cutting performance to confirm compliance.
6. Upon sample approval, gradually ramp up to full production speed for automated batch manufacturing, conducting periodic quality checks.
7. After production, shut down and let heaters cool, clear residual material from the machine bed, and complete routine daily maintenance.
