# Automatic Bag Making Machine Selection Guide & Product Overview

## I. What is an Automatic Bag Making Machine?
### 1.1 Core Definition
An Automatic Bag Making Machine is a piece of automated processing equipment dedicated to the flexible packaging industry. It continuously converts rolls of plastic film (single-layer or laminated) into finished packaging bags of specific sizes and shapes through an automated process of unwinding, tension control, folding, heat sealing (or high-frequency welding), punching, and cutting. This equipment largely replaces semi-automatic or manual bag-making processes and is indispensable for packaging material manufacturers, garment accessory factories, and food packaging enterprises.

### 1.2 Working Principle
The machine operates primarily through the coordination of two major systems: **"Tension Unwinding + PLC-Controlled Heat Sealing/Cutting"**:
1. **Automatic Unwinding & Guiding (EPC):** The film roll is mounted on the unwinding stand. Through a magnetic powder tension controller and an EPC (Edge Position Control) system, the film is fed forward smoothly, consistently, and without deviation.
2. **Heat Sealing & Cutting:** A photocell sensor tracks the printed color marks on the film for precise positioning. Based on the parameters set on the touch screen, the heat sealing knife (or high-frequency mold) instantly heats and presses the film to seal it (or attach a zipper). Finally, the cutting knife accurately severs the bag, and the machine automatically counts and outputs the finished product.

### 1.3 Core Values
- **High-Efficiency Production:** Fully automatic continuous operation yields 100 to 170 bags per minute (depending on the model), significantly boosting production capacity.
- **High Compatibility:** Capable of processing OPP, BOPP, PE, PP, PVC, and various laminated materials like PET/PA/Aluminum-foil.
- **Extreme Precision:** Automatic photocell tracking ensures printed patterns are perfectly centered and dimensions are highly accurate. The machine stops automatically if the mark is lost.
- **Modular Expansion:** Supports the addition of various hole punchers (hanger holes/air exhaust holes), zipper modules, and gusset formers for multi-purpose use.

### 1.4 Applicable Industries
- **Apparel & Home Textiles:** Transparent clothing/underwear packaging, towel/bedding bags, jewelry storage bags.
- **Food & Daily Chemicals:** Small bread packaging, snack bags, meat vacuum pouches, toiletry bags.
- **Stationery & Sundries:** Pencil cases, folder insert bags, hardware parts ziplock bags, garbage bags.

## III. Core Selection Advice

### 3.1 Selection by "Film Material & Bag Type" (Top Priority)
1. **Single-layer transparent plastics (OPP/BOPP/PE):** If the bag only needs side sealing (e.g., clothing bags, toast bags), choose the **OPP Series**.
2. **Requires a Zipper/Ziplock:** Choose the **TD-ZB700 or PE800/900/1000 Series**. The machine features built-in zipper guides for one-step forming, perfect for apparel storage bags.
3. **Food-grade / Vacuum / Laminated Materials (Alu-foil, Paper-plastic, Nylon):** You must select the **TDZD Series**. Only the high-pressure sealing mechanisms and multi-servo systems of this series can process rigid laminated films to create airtight central-seal, 3-side seal, and gusset pouches.

### 3.2 Selection by "Bag Size" (Width & Length)
- The machine model usually indicates the **Maximum Bag Width** it can process (e.g., OPP-600 makes bags up to 600mm wide).
- **Golden Rule:** A large machine can make small bags, but a small machine cannot make large bags. We recommend choosing a model that is 50-100mm wider than your daily maximum bag size.
- **Pro Tip:** Unless you specifically need ultra-large bags, avoid blindly choosing the largest model (like the 1000 series), as they consume more power and space. The **OPP-700/800 or TDZD-500 are often the best-balanced mainstream choices**.

### 3.3 Selection by "Add-on Functions"
- If you need to produce supermarket hanging bags with round/butterfly holes, or garment bags with air exhaust holes, please inform us when purchasing so we can **equip the machine with the corresponding pneumatic punchers**.

## IV. General Technical FAQ

### 4.1 Power & Air Supply
**Q: What power supply is required? Do I need an air compressor?**
A: Standard equipment requires Industrial 3-Phase 380V (50Hz/60Hz). We offer **customized voltages** (e.g., 220V 3-phase) to meet your local standards.
**An air compressor is mandatory and must be prepared by the customer.** The pneumatic cutters, punchers, and feeding mechanisms require a stable air supply (ranging from 3HP to 6.5HP depending on the model).

### 4.2 Printed Bags & Photocell Tracking
**Q: Can the machine process bags with color printed patterns?**
A: Yes. All our models are equipped with a **Photocell Color Mark Tracking System**. Simply align the photocell sensor with the printed eye-mark on the film before operation. The machine will automatically track and cut accurately. If a mark is missed, the machine will automatically alarm and stop, preventing waste.

### 4.3 Consumables & Maintenance
**Q: What are the consumable parts? Is operation difficult?**
A: Core consumables include **heat sealing/cutting blades, high-temperature Teflon tape, and silicone rollers**. These are wear-and-tear items not covered by the 1-year warranty; we recommend purchasing spare parts with your machine.
Operation is managed via a **PLC + Touch Screen (Multi-language supported)**. However, as this is heavy industrial automated equipment, **operators must be trained by factory technicians** to handle threading, temperature tuning, and length setting before working independently.

## VI. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Regardless of the series you purchase, the basic operational logic remains highly consistent:

1. **Pre-Startup Check (Power & Air):**
   - Verify power and air compressor connections. Clean any residual glue or dirt from the sealing knives and rollers.
   - Ensure optional punchers or modules are securely installed.
2. **Loading Roll & Threading Film:**
   - Mount the film roll (and zipper roll, if applicable) onto the rear unwinding stand.
   - Strictly follow the "Threading Diagram" to guide the film through the rollers, tension dancing rollers, EPC sensor, photocell, and finally into the sealing/cutting stations.
3. **Parameter Setting & Heating:**
   - Input the target bag length, production speed, and batch count on the touch screen.
   - Turn on the heaters and set the temperature (usually 150℃-220℃, depending on film material and thickness). Wait for the temperature to stabilize. For printed film, activate and calibrate the photocell sensor.
4. **Low-Speed Testing & QC:**
   - Jog the machine at a low speed (e.g., 30 pcs/min) to produce a few samples.
   - **Perform the 3-Point QC Check:** Pull the seal to test strength (Does it tear?), measure the bag (Is the length accurate?), and inspect the cut (Is it clean and wire-free?).
5. **High-Speed Mass Production:**
   - Once samples pass QC, gradually increase to normal production speed (e.g., 100 pcs/min) on the screen and switch to fully automatic mode.
   - The operator should monitor the machine and spot-check products periodically. The machine will automatically alarm/stop when the set batch count is reached. Shut off heaters and clean remnants after production ends.
