## IV. Operation Process
> Operating workflow is identical across TDZD-400 / 500 / 600 models.
1. Check electrical and pneumatic circuits prior to startup, ensuring normal air compressor supply; clean stains on guide rollers and heat-sealing blades; install matching heat-sealing knives, cutting blades, and gusset molds for the target pouch specification.
2. Mount the laminated roll material onto the unwinding unit, threading material sequentially through guide rollers, tension mechanisms, EPC web guide, photoelectric sensors, and sealing stations.
3. Configure pouch length, skip-feeding multiplier, sealing temperature, production speed, and counting parameters on the HMI touch screen; calibrate photoelectric print marks and select the desired operating language.
4. Once sealing stations reach operating temperatures, start the equipment at low speed to produce trial sample pouches, checking seal strength, dimensions, gusset formation, and cutting performance.
5. Upon sample approval, ramp up to full production speed for automated batch manufacturing, conducting routine periodic quality checks.
6. After production, shut down and let heaters cool, turn off the air compressor, clear residual materials and scrap, and complete routine lubrication and maintenance.

1. TDZD-400: Ideal for small-to-medium vacuum pouches, central-seal pouches, and three-side seal bags; top choice for small/medium flexible packaging plants.
2. TDZD-500: Highest versatility, covering mainstream market pouch formats; recommended primary selection for most clients.
3. TDZD-600: Tailored for wide-width packaging pouches, producing large vacuum bags, large gusset pouches, and oversized paper-plastic laminates.
4. Suited for high-volume laminated pouch mass production; preferred by food, meat, snack, and tea packaging enterprises (not suitable for micro-batch sampling).
5. Raw materials should prioritize PET/PE, PA/PE, aluminum, and paper-plastic laminates; unlaminated raw paper cannot be processed directly.
6. For stand-up pouches, handle punches, or rounded corners, recommend selecting the corresponding optional modules; air compressor is customer-supplied.

### FAQ
**Q1: What power supply is required for the equipment?**
A1: Standard 3-phase 380V, supporting 50Hz/60Hz, with custom voltage available upon request.

**Q2: Can molds be interchanged across the three models?**
A2: Smaller molds within the maximum bag width of a given model can be shared; exceeding the maximum width requires model-specific tooling.

**Q3: Can this machine produce vacuum pouches?**
A3: Yes. It is fully compatible with PA/PE and other laminated films to produce food vacuum packaging pouches.

**Q4: Are wearing parts covered under warranty?**
A4: The whole machine carries a 1-year warranty; heat-sealing blades and cutting knives are excluded.

**Q5: Is operation difficult, and is training required?**
A5: Featuring a multilingual touch screen interface, this is an advanced high-speed packaging machine; on-site manufacturer technical training is mandatory before operation.

**Q6: Is an air compressor included?**
A6: The air compressor is customer-supplied; a 5HP compressor is recommended for TDZD-400/500, and 6.5HP for TDZD-600.

**Q7: Can it produce extra-long pouches?**
A7: Yes, supporting up to 6x skip feeding with maximum bag lengths reaching 2100mm via touch-screen multiplier settings.
