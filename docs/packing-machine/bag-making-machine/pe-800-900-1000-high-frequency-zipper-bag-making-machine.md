## IV. Operation Process
> Operating workflow is identical across PE800 / PE900 / PE1000 models.
1. Check electrical and pneumatic lines before starting; clean stains on thermal welding and cutting blades; ensure the zipper feeding mechanism and optional punching components are installed correctly.
2. Mount the plastic film roll onto the unwinding stand, threading the film sequentially through the tension mechanism, web guide, photoelectric sensor, zipper thermal welding station, and cutting station.
3. Load the zipper material and adjust the zipper feeding position; configure bag length, sealing temperature, production speed, and counting parameters on the touch screen; calibrate photoelectric marks for printed film.
4. Once the thermal welding station reaches the preset process temperature, start the machine at low speed to produce trial sample zipper bags.
5. Inspect samples for zipper bonding strength, sealing effectiveness, bag dimensions, and cutting quality, confirming compliance.
6. Upon sample approval, ramp up to full production speed for automated batch manufacturing, conducting routine quality checks.
7. After production, turn off heating, let the machine cool down, clear residual film and zipper waste, and perform routine daily maintenance.
