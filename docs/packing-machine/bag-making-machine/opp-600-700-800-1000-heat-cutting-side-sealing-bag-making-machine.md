## IV. Operation Process
> Operating workflow is identical across OPP-600 / OPP-700 / **OPP-800** / OPP-1000 models.
1. Check electrical and pneumatic lines before starting; clean residual glue/stains on the heat-sealing and cutting blades; ensure optional punching components are installed securely.
2. Mount the film roll onto the unwinding stand, threading the film sequentially through the tension mechanism, EPC web guide, photoelectric sensor, and heat-sealing/cutting station.
3. Configure bag length, heat-sealing temperature, production speed, and counting parameters on the touch screen; calibrate the photoelectric color mark position for printed bags.
4. Once the sealing blades reach the preset process temperature, start the equipment at low speed to produce trial sample bags.
5. Inspect the samples for seal strength, cutting quality, bag dimensions, and color mark registration, ensuring compliance.
6. Upon sample approval, gradually ramp up to full production speed for automated batch manufacturing, conducting periodic quality checks.
7. After production, turn off heating, let the machine cool down, clear waste film and debris, and perform routine daily maintenance.

### Sales Suggestions
1. OPP-600: Focuses on small-to-medium OPP garment and jewelry bags; ideal for small and medium packaging plants.
2. OPP-700: Highest versatility, covering mainstream OPP side-sealing bag demands; recommended as the primary selection for most clients.
3. **OPP-800: Tailored for 700–800mm medium-large specification bags, ideal for medium-large garment and home textile towel bags; recommended for clients whose product dimensions fall between 700 and 1000mm to avoid oversized machine energy waste.**
4. OPP-1000: Targeted at large wide-width packaging bag production for oversized garments and textile items.
5. Best suited for high-volume continuous OPP/BOPP/PE side-sealing bag production; not ideal for ultra-small batch sampling frequencies.
6. For bags requiring hanging holes or exhaust holes, recommend bundling the corresponding punching attachments; enable photoelectric tracking when running printed film.
7. Pneumatic cylinder mechanisms require an air compressor (supplied by the customer).

### FAQ
**Q1: What power supply is required for the equipment?**
A1: Standard 3-phase 380V, supporting 50Hz/60Hz, with custom voltage available upon request.

**Q2: Can molds and accessories be interchanged among the four models?**
A2: Accessories within the maximum bag width of a specific model can be shared; accessories exceeding the maximum width require model-specific matching parts.

**Q3: Can it produce printed colorful OPP bags?**
A3: Yes. Enable photoelectric color mark tracking and calibrate the mark position for stable printed bag production with automatic error stopping.

**Q4: Are wearing parts covered under warranty?**
A4: The whole machine carries a 1-year warranty; wearing parts such as heat-cutting blades and rubber rollers are excluded.

**Q5: Is operation difficult? Is training required?**
A5: Featuring a touch screen HMI, this is an automated bag-making system. Operators can start working independently after receiving manufacturer technical training.

**Q6: Is an air compressor necessary?**
A6: Yes, punching and pneumatic mechanisms require an external air compressor provided by the customer.
