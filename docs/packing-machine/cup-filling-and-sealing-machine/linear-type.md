### VI. Workflow
1. **Automatic Cup Dropping**: Cups are stacked and loaded into the machine, which automatically places them into custom mold stations.
2. **(Optional) Sterilization & Nitrogen Flushing**: UV disinfection inside the station, with nitrogen injected before and after filling to displace air.
3. **Synchronized Precise Filling**: Multi-head filling pumps simultaneously inject materials, achieving high-speed and high-precision operation.
4. **Film/Lid Placement & Coding**: Automatic film application (pre-cut/reel) or capping; if film reels are used, online coding is completed simultaneously.
5. **High-Speed Heat Sealing**: Constant temperature pressing to complete a flat, tight seal on the cup rim.
6. **Automatic Finished Product Output**: Finished products are automatically ejected along the track into subsequent packaging or boxing stages.
