## II. Detailed Analysis of Core Model Differences

### 1. Rotary Filling & Sealing Machine (Rotary Series)
* **Core Positioning**: A flexible and efficient tool for small-to-medium production.
* **Working Logic**: Integrates cup dropping, filling, sealing, and other stations through a high-precision rotary indexing table.
* **Model Differences**:
    * **CF-1**: Fills 1 cup per cycle, suitable for laboratory testing or precision small-batch production of high-value products.
    * **CF-2**: Fills 2 cups per cycle, suitable for growing enterprises steadily scaling up production capacity.

### 2. Linear Filling & Sealing Machine (Linear Series)
* **Core Positioning**: Standard configuration for industrialized, large-scale assembly lines.
* **Working Logic**: Adopts linear synchronized conveying technology, supporting ultra-high-density multi-lane filling.
* **Model Differences**: Offers **4/6/8/10/12/14/16 lane** filling configurations. As the number of lanes increases, capacity multiplies, making it the industrial choice of preference for pursuing ultimate economies of scale.

## III. Highly Customizable Optional Solutions (Standard Capabilities Across Series)

We understand the special packaging requirements of different products, therefore we provide comprehensive in-depth customization services:

* **Mold Customization**: All molds are precisely built 1:1 according to the cup dimensions you provide, ensuring a perfect fit.
* **Sealing Flexibility**: Supports **pre-cut lid** (automatic placement) or **film reel** (stretching and cutting) sealing solutions.

    * **Secondary Capping**: Supports adding plastic snap-on caps on top of the sealing film to enhance product presentation.

## IV. Selection Guide: How to Make a Decision?

When evaluating and selecting, please consider the following factors:

1. **Evaluate Capacity Targets**:
   * Moderate daily capacity demand with a desire for single-machine flexibility -> **Choose Rotary Series (CF-1/2)**.
   * Requirement for thousands of cups per day with large-scale assembly line planning -> **Choose Linear Series (CF-4~16)**.

2. **Consider Material Form**:
   * Our equipment supports high customization; whether you are filling coffee capsule powders, liquids, tea leaves, or granules, we will match you with the most suitable filling and metering system (such as piston pumps, auger dosing, etc.) based on the rheological characteristics of the material.
