### 6. Workflow
1. **Automatic Tube Loading:** The tubes in the hopper automatically enter the conveying track and are accurately inserted into the rotary table molds.
2. **Automatic Registration:** The high-precision photoelectric eye recognizes the color mark on the tube body, driving the motor to automatically calibrate the sealing orientation.
3. **Precision Filling:** The pneumatic piston pump precisely injects material according to the preset volume, featuring a cut-off and anti-drip function at the end.
4. **Sealing Treatment:**
    * **Metal Tubes:** Crimped and pressed through multiple mechanical folds.
    * **Plastic Tubes:** Internally heated followed by pneumatic cold-press sealing.
5. **Date Printing:** While sealing, the mold automatically embosses clear manufacturing dates or batch numbers.
6. **Tail Trimming:** An automatic cutter trims away excess tail material.
7. **Finished Product Discharge:** The finished product automatically detaches from the mold and is output to the collection bin via a chute.
