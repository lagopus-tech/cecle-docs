# Tube Sealing Machine Selection Guide & Product Overview

## I. What is a Tube Sealing Machine?
A tube sealing machine is a packaging equipment designed to seal the tail, emboss batch numbers, and trim the edges of tubes (plastic tubes, composite tubes, aluminum-plastic tubes) after they are filled with liquid, paste, or viscous materials.

* **Working Principle:**
    1. **Ultrasonic / Hot Air Principle (For Plastic/Composite Tubes):** Melts the inner wall of the tube mouth via high-frequency vibration friction or hot air, which is then pressed and fused together by mechanical jaws.
    2. **Pneumatic Folding Principle (For Aluminum-Plastic Tubes):** Mechanically folds and crimps the tail of the aluminum-plastic tube multiple times (such as 3-fold or 5-fold) to achieve a tight physical seal.
* **Core Value:** Ensures products are leak-proof and oxidation-free, delivers smooth and aesthetic seals, prints clear batch numbers, and significantly enhances the premium look of the finished product.
* **Applicable Industries:** Cosmetics (toothpaste, facial cleanser), pharmaceuticals (ointments, gels), food (jam, chocolate sauce), adhesives, and fine chemical industries.

## III. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. **Q: What is the fundamental difference between the Aluminum-Plastic Tube Sealing Machine (TS-80) and Plastic Tube Sealing Machines?**
   **A:** * **Hot Air / Thermal Conduction Type (e.g., Plastic Tube Machines):** Relies on external heating (usually hot air or heating blocks) to bake the inner wall of the tube mouth directly, softening the plastic before pressing it shut.
   * **Ultrasonic Type (QDFW-125):** Relies on high-frequency vibration to generate friction heat. It uses an ultrasonic horn to make the plastic molecules at the tube mouth collide violently, generating heat internally to fuse the tube.

2. **Q: What are the advantages of the Ultrasonic Tube Sealing Machine (QDFW-125)?**
   * **A:** Ultrasonic sealing is a "cold sealing" method. It does not generate continuous high temperatures at the seal, making it friendlier to heat-sensitive materials (such as certain biological gels and high-end skincare products). Furthermore, the sealed and trimmed edges are neat and beautiful, without damaging the printing on the tube body.

3. **Q: Can the machine automatically print batch numbers or production dates?**
   * **A:** Yes. All of our models are equipped with a date character matrix/font library. While sealing the tail, the character molds leave a clear embossed steel stamp on the tube tail via mechanical pressure, which will never fade.

4. **Q: How is the filling accuracy guaranteed? (For FW-006 / HZNF-60B)**
   * **A:** We utilize a high-precision piston pump volumetric filling system, controlling the error within ≤±1%. By adjusting the piston stroke, users can easily adapt the machine to different filling volumes.
