# Product Specification Sheet: Tube Sealing Machine (TS-80)

![TS-80 Tube Sealing Machine](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/17834930212181.png?v=1783493300)

> **Core Abstract:**  
> The **TS-80** is a semi-automatic pneumatic tube sealing machine specifically engineered for aluminum tube cold-folding and tail-crimping in the cosmetic, pharmaceutical, food, and chemical industries. Ideal for processing pastes, ointments, toothpastes, and creams, it features an efficient dual-station setup (left for crimping/folding, right for date/batch code embossing) operated via a convenient pneumatic foot pedal. With a production capacity of 10-20 pcs/min and customizable horizontal or vertical sealing, it delivers durable, professional packaging for small-to-medium factories and laboratories.

### 1. Product Overview
* **Category:** Packaging Machinery / Pneumatic Tube Sealing Machine
* **Application:** Widely used for sealing aluminum tubes in the chemical, cosmetic, pharmaceutical, and food industries. It is particularly suitable for processing products like ointments, toothpastes, and cosmetic creams.
* **Core Function:** Specifically designed for aluminum tubes to achieve automatic tube-tail folding/sealing and manufacturing date/batch number embossing.

### 2. Core Advantages
1. **Efficient Dual-Station Division:** Features independent left and right workstations. The left station handles tail crimping/folding, while the right station handles date embossing, ensuring a clear and streamlined process.
2. **Pneumatic Foot Pedal Control:** Triggered simply by pressing the pneumatic foot switch. It requires less effort than purely manual models and offers greater operational flexibility than fully automatic ones.
3. **Optional Sealing Directions:** Supports custom horizontal or vertical sealing directions to meet different product packaging aesthetic requirements.
4. **Digital Intelligent Monitoring:** Equipped with high-precision digital display instruments to monitor working parameters in real-time, ensuring consistent sealing quality.
5. **Industrial-Grade Durability:** Robust structure and simple maintenance make it the preferred equipment for small-to-medium-sized factories and laboratories.

#### 4.2 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
* **Q: Can this machine seal plastic tubes?**
    * A: No, it cannot. The TS-80 is a cold-folding sealing machine specifically designed for aluminum tubes. Plastic tubes require heating or ultrasonic models.
* **Q: Can the embossing characters be changed?**
    * A: Yes, the mold character blocks are detachable and replaceable, making it easy to adjust production dates and batch numbers.

### 6. Workflow
1. **Preparation:** Connect the power and air source. Adjust the working parameters on the digital display according to the material and thickness of the aluminum tube.
2. **Material Placement:** The operator places the filled aluminum tube into the corresponding mold slot.
3. **Processing Trigger:**
    * **Step 1:** Press the foot switch to trigger the left cylinder, completing the **tail crimping and folding** action (multi-layer folding) of the aluminum tube.
    * **Step 2:** Move the folded tube to the right side and trigger the switch again. The right station will complete the **date code embossing**.
4. **Finished Product Collection:** Remove the finished product, check the flatness of the seal and the clarity of the embossed characters, and proceed to the next cycle.
