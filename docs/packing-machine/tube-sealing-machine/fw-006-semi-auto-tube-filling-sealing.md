## II. Core Advantages
* **High-Precision Intelligent Control**: Adopts a PLC control system combined with a touchscreen human-machine interface (HMI) for intuitive operation, controlling the filling accuracy deviation within ±1%.
* **Premium Brand Core Configurations**:
  * **Orientation System**: Utilizes a highly sensitive **Panasonic (Japan)** photoelectric eye to ensure precise positioning of the tail sealing pattern.
  * **Pneumatic Components**: Uses **AirTAC** or Xinchen cylinders, ensuring smooth operation and a long service life.
  * **Electrical Protection**: Equipped with **Omron** relays and **Delta** inverters to guarantee system stability.
* **Ultrasonic Cold Sealing Technology**: 20KHz high-frequency ultrasonic waves instantly fuse the tail, creating a firm and aesthetic seal. The sealing quality remains unaffected even if a small amount of material adheres to the tube mouth.
* **GMP Standard Compliant**: The entire machine body is made of 304 stainless steel. The hopper and parts contacting the material are easy to disassemble and clean, meeting hygienic production requirements.
* **Multi-functional Integration**: Beyond sealing, it features automatic tail trimming (cutting off excess edge material) and date/batch number embossing.

## VI. Workflow
1. **Manual Loading**: Operators manually insert the tubes into the turntable fixtures.
2. **Marking (Orientation)**: The sensor detects the tube color code, and the rotary motor automatically calibrates the sealing direction.
3. **Filling**: The piston pump automatically executes quantitative filling; the filling head features an anti-drip design.
4. **Sealing**: The ultrasonic system excites high-frequency vibrations to instantly weld the inner wall of the tube tail.
5. **Coding**: The sealing mold synchronously embosses the production date or batch number.
6. **Cutting**: The automatic cutter trims off the excess, uneven residual material at the tube tail.
7. **Discharge**: The finished product automatically ejects from the fixture and enters the collection chute.
