### 6. Workflow
1. **Preparation:** Connect the 220V power supply and 0.4-0.6 MPa air source, and adjust the ultrasonic frequency to the normal range.
2. **Manual Tube Loading:** Place the filled tube into the working fixture of the machine.
3. **Trigger Sealing:** Press the start button or step on the foot switch, and the sealing mold closes.
4. **Ultrasonic Fusion:** The machine instantly emits ultrasonic waves to melt the inner wall of the tube opening, while simultaneously embossing the date block into it.
5. **Cooling & Discharging:** The mold dwells for about 0.5 - 1 second to complete setting, then automatically opens, and the finished product can be taken out.
