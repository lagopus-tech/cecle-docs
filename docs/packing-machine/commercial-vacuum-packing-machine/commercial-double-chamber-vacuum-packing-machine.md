### 2. Core Functions & Working Principles

* **Seamless Alternating Loading**: After the operator arranges the products in Chamber A, they press the lid down onto Chamber A. Chamber A immediately and automatically begins the vacuum, seal, and cool cycle. During this time, the operator continues to arrange products in Chamber B.
* **Instant Impulse Heat Sealing**: Each vacuum chamber is equipped with **two** parallel heat-sealing bars (one in the front, one in the back). A single lid closure can seal two rows of packaging bags simultaneously, vastly expanding the throughput of a single working cycle.
* **Pressure Restoration & Lid Popping**: Once Chamber A finishes cooling, normal atmospheric pressure is restored inside, and the lid automatically releases (or pops up with assistance). The operator then smoothly pushes the lid over to Chamber B, where the products are already loaded, and Chamber B starts working. This cycle repeats for continuous production.

### 3. Core Value: Why Must You Upgrade to a "Double Chamber" Vacuum Sealer?

* **Eliminate Labor Waiting Costs**: With a single-chamber machine, the operator can only stand and wait during the vacuuming phase (usually 15-30 seconds). A double-chamber machine turns these 30 seconds into "prep time" for the other chamber, squeezing every second of productivity out of your labor.
* **Massive Single-Cycle Throughput**: Taking the DZ-500/2L as an example, the two chambers have a total of four 500mm sealing bars. A single cycle can simultaneously seal a dozen or even dozens of small packaging bags.
* **Extremely Low Consumable Costs**: Like all chamber vacuum sealers, the double-chamber machine is compatible with the cheapest smooth commercial vacuum bags (Nylon/PE) on the market. The higher your capacity, the more astonishing your savings on packaging bags will be.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Production Workflow

To maximize the capacity of the double-chamber machine and prolong equipment life, please follow these continuous operation protocols:

### 1. Bag Preparation & Left/Right Chamber Allocation

* **Match Sealing Bar Length**: Use standard smooth commercial vacuum bags. Arrange the filled bags neatly on both the **front and back** sealing bars in Chamber A.
* **Liquid/Sauce Spill Prevention**: When packaging foods containing a lot of broth, it is recommended to leave 3-5 cm of buffer space at the top of the bag. You can also utilize the depth of the vacuum chamber to place the bags at a slight angle.
* **Clean the Sealing Zone**: Before closing the lid, quickly check that the bag opening is flat and wipe away any grease or water stains from the sealing area.

### 2. Microcomputer Panel Settings (Synchronized for Both Chambers)

1. **Power On**: Turn on the main power switch.
2. **Set Parameters**: The double-chamber machine shares the same control motherboard.
   * **Vacuum Time**: Usually set to **15-25 seconds**.
   * **Sealing Time**: Usually set to **1.5-2.5 seconds** (fine-tune based on bag thickness).
   * **Cooling Time**: Usually set to **2.0-3.0 seconds**.

### 3. "Left-Right Alternating" High-Efficiency Packaging Operation

1. **Chamber A Start**: The operator arranges the bags on the left side (Chamber A), swings the lid from right to left, and gently presses down. The machine automatically engages the lid, and Chamber A begins to vacuum.
2. **Chamber B Prep**: During the 20+ seconds Chamber A is working, the operator quickly arranges the next batch of packaging bags on the front and back sealing bars on the right side (Chamber B).
3. **Switch Cycle**: Once Chamber A finishes working, the lid automatically releases. The operator pushes the lid to the right (Chamber B) and presses down. Chamber B starts working. The operator then immediately clears the finished products from Chamber A and preps the next batch.

### 4. Preventative Maintenance & Vacuum Pump Care

* **Large-Capacity Vacuum Pump Oil Change**: Double-chamber machines have a high workload intensity, making **oil changes extremely important**. The oil must be changed after the first 100 hours of operation. After that, it should be promptly replaced with dedicated **Vacuum Pump Oil (ISO VG 32/68)** every **300 hours** of operation, or whenever the oil appears emulsified or milky white.
* **Teflon High-Temperature Tape Replacement**: If the Teflon heat insulation cloth on the four sealing bars becomes scorched, blackened, or torn, it must be replaced immediately. Otherwise, it will cause air leaks in the seal or burn out the heating wire.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why do large food factories choose a double-chamber vacuum machine instead of buying two single-chamber machines?**
  * **A**: The "swinging" design of the double-chamber machine naturally fits human ergonomics. One worker stands perfectly in the middle of the machine: loads bags on the left, pushes the lid, loads bags on the right, pushes the lid. The movements flow smoothly without any waiting time. Operating two single-chamber machines not only requires more floor space but walking back and forth also increases worker fatigue.
* **Q: Can this machine package liquids (e.g., hot pot soup base, broth)?**
  * **A**: Absolutely. The double-chamber machine features a deep-concave vacuum chamber chassis. Because the pressure inside and outside the bag is balanced across the entire chamber during the vacuum phase, the liquid inside the bag will not boil or splash out. It is the perfect solution for packaging liquid foods like hot pot bases, sauce packets, and fish soup pouches.
* **Q: There are four sealing bars. Will it be troublesome to replace the consumables?**
  * **A**: Not at all. CECLE's sealing bars feature a quick-release design. You simply pull the entire sealing bar straight up, replace the heating wire and Teflon high-temperature tape on your workbench, and then snap it back into the machine. Maintenance is simple and fast.
