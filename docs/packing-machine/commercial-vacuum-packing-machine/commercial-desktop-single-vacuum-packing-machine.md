## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Production Workflow

To ensure sealing quality and extend the service life of your desktop vacuum machine, please follow these operating protocols:

### 1. Preparation & Placement

* **Stable Placement**: Place the machine on a dry, well-ventilated, and flat horizontal workbench.
* **Check Power**: Confirm that the input voltage is 220V and that a reliable ground wire is connected.
* **Machine Warm-Up**: After powering on, perform a no-load test run to check if the lid sealing gasket fits snugly and properly.

### 2. Operating Steps

1. **Adjust Parameters**: Based on the thickness and material of the packaging bag, set the vacuum time, sealing time (typically adjustable within 1-8 seconds), and cooling time on the control panel.
2. **Position the Product**: Neatly place the vacuum bag containing the item (e.g., tea caddies, dried goods, meat products) into the vacuum chamber. The bag opening must lie completely flat across the sealing line directly above the silicone pressure bar without any wrinkles.
3. **Initiate Vacuum Extraction**: Close the transparent organic glass lid. The machine's micro-switch will trigger, automatically starting vacuum extraction. Operators can monitor the vacuum level via the panel pressure gauge.
4. **Automatic Sealing & Retrieval**: Once extraction concludes, the machine automatically performs sealing and cooling, followed by exhaust and lid release. Remove the packaged finished product and inspect the seal to ensure it is flat, bubble-free, and unburned.

### 3. Daily Maintenance

* **Keep Clean**: After each use, wipe down the interior vacuum chamber, sealing bar, and organic glass lid with a clean, slightly damp cloth to prevent residues from corroding components.
* **Regular Oil Changes**: Periodically check the oil level and color of the built-in vacuum pump. If the oil turns dark or emulsified, replace the vacuum pump oil promptly to maintain extraction efficiency.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: What are the main differences between desktop vacuum machines (DZ-T series) and large floor-standing machines?**
  * **A**: The main differences lie in size, vacuum chamber volume, and single-cycle capacity. Desktop units (DZ-260T/300T/400T) emphasize portability, compactness, and plug-and-play capability (220V), making them ideal for small shops or homes with limited space and small-to-medium daily yields. Floor-standing machines feature higher power and deeper chambers, tailored for high-intensity, industrial-scale mass production.
* **Q: How should the sealing time (1-8s) be adjusted?**
  * **A**: Sealing time depends on the thickness of the vacuum bag you use. Generally, thinner composite bags require about 2-3 seconds; if the bags are thicker or contain aluminum foil, extend the time to 4-6 seconds appropriately. Do not blindly set it too long to avoid melting through the plastic bag or burning out the heating wire.
* **Q: Can this machine be used to package tea?**
  * **A**: Absolutely ideal. Tea (especially loose leaf, compressed tea cakes, or canned/bagged tea) has extremely high demands for moisture and oxidation prevention. The DZ series desktop vacuum machine perfectly removes oxygen, and with appropriate sealing times, keeps tea fragrant and green for extended periods.
