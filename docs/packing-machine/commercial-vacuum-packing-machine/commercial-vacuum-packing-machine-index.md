## 3. Buyer's Selection & Decision Matrix

Choose the machine series that matches your workflow and product portfolio:

### Choose the DZ-T Desktop Series if:
1. **Space and budget are tightly constrained:** You operate a boutique tea store, small cafe, or home lab where a compact footprint is mandatory.
2. **You only need standard 220V power:** You do not have industrial three-phase wiring and need a true plug-and-play solution.
3. **Daily yields are moderate:** Your packing volume fits comfortably within a portable desktop workflow.

### Choose the DZ-S Single Chamber Series if:
1. **You package liquids or high-moisture items:** Your menu or products feature soups, marinades, broths, or herbal extracts that require safe chamber vacuuming.
2. **You want lower bag operating expenses:** You prefer cheap, smooth commercial flat vacuum bags over expensive textured alternatives.
3. **You need robust floor-standing reliability:** You want a durable, oil-lubricated industrial pump system for daily commercial prep.

### Choose the DZ-2L Double Chamber Series if:
1. **Your production line faces severe bottlenecks:** Your packaging volume is so high that a single-chamber machine cannot keep up with output demands.
2. **You want to maximize labor efficiency:** You want a single operator to continuously prep one chamber while the other vacuums, cutting idle wait time to zero.
3. **You need massive single-cycle capacity:** Four sealing bars running simultaneously are required to handle your commercial distribution scale.

### Choose the YUNNA External Series if:
1. **Your items are too big for a chamber:** You process oversized blocks, long strips, or bulky goods that physically cannot fit inside an enclosed box chamber.
2. **You need ultimate packaging versatility:** You frequently switch between standard heat sealing and external vacuum extraction.
