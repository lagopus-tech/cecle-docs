### 2. Core Functions & Working Principles

* **Atmospheric Evacuation**: Close the lid to form an airtight seal. The vacuum pump starts, quickly pumping out the air in the entire vacuum chamber (including the inside of the unsealed bag containing the product).
* **Impulse Heat Sealing**: After reaching the target vacuum degree, the pneumatic airbag pushes the heat-sealing bar tight against the packaging bag. An electrical impulse heats the Teflon-coated nichrome wire, fusing the bag layers together to form a permanent airtight seal.
* **Pressure Restoration & Cooling**: Normal air pressure is instantly restored in the vacuum chamber. This pressure difference makes the bag shrink tightly around the product. The pneumatic hydraulic rod automatically releases and pops open the lid.

### 3. Core Value: Why Choose DZ Series Chamber Sealers?

* **Complete Oxygen Elimination (Shelf Life Extended 5x)**: Removes oxygen to prevent oxidation, discoloration, and mold. Medical herbs retain their essential oils and efficacy; meat avoids freezer burn; dry goods stay crisp for months or years.
* **Universal for All Mediums (Dry, Wet, Liquid, Powder)**: External suction sealers struggle to handle liquids and fine powders. The DZ series handles them effortlessly.
* **Massive Savings on Consumable Costs**: Chamber sealers use standard smooth commercial vacuum bags (flat bags). These smooth bags are much cheaper than the textured bags that household machines must use, drastically reducing long-term operating costs.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Production Workflow

To ensure perfect sealing airtightness and prolong the service life of the vacuum pump and heating elements, please strictly adhere to the following protocols:

### 1. Bag Preparation & Product Loading

* **Select the Right Packaging Bag**: Use standard smooth commercial vacuum bags. Ensure the bag is wide enough for the product but must be within the sealing bar length limit of the machine (e.g., 400mm for DZ-400S).
* **Leave Top Space**: When packaging liquids, sauces, or large pieces of herbs, leave at least 2 to 3 inches (5-8 cm) of empty space at the top of the bag. This ensures the sealing area remains clean and flat.
* **Clean the Sealing Area**: Wipe away any moisture, grease, or herbal dust from the inside top opening of the bag. Contaminants can cause weak or leaking seals.

### 2. Programming the Digital Control Panel

1. **Power On**: Turn on the main power switch.
2. **Set Vacuum Time**: Usually between **15 to 30 seconds**, depending on the size of the product and packaging bag. *(Note: For fragile herbs or soft bread, please reduce the time to avoid crushing).*
3. **Set Sealing Time**: Usually between **1.2 to 2.5 seconds**, depending on the thickness of the vacuum bag.
4. **Set Cooling Time**: Usually set to **2.0 to 3.0 seconds**, allowing the heated and melted plastic to solidify under pressure before opening the vacuum chamber.

### 3. Preventative Maintenance & Vacuum Pump Care

* **Vacuum Pump Oil Change (Crucial)**:
  * **First Oil Change**: Must be performed after the first **100 hours** of operation.
  * **Routine Oil Change**: Change the oil every **250 to 300 hours** of operation, or whenever the oil in the sight glass appears cloudy, emulsified, or dark.
* **Sealing Bar Maintenance**: If the high-temperature Teflon tape turns brown, tears, or sticks to the bags, please replace the Teflon tape and heating wire immediately to prevent short circuits.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Can this machine vacuum package liquids like soups or medical herbal extracts?**
  * **A**: Absolutely. Chamber vacuum packaging machines are the only devices that can truly vacuum package liquids. Because the pressure drops uniformly across the entire vacuum chamber, liquids will not boil over or get sucked out of the bag during the vacuum stage.
* **Q: Why does this machine require pump oil? What kind of oil should I use?**
  * **A**: The machine is equipped with a 0.9KW industrial-grade rotary vane vacuum pump (displacement 20m³/h) to achieve maximum air extraction and commercial longevity. Pump oil is used to lubricate the vanes and dissipate heat. You should use dedicated **vacuum pump oil (usually ISO VG 32 or 68, subject to the manual)**.
* **Q: Will it crush my fragile medical herbs or soft foods?**
  * **A**: If set to maximum vacuum, atmospheric pressure might crush fragile items. To prevent this, you simply need to reduce the "Vacuum Time" on the digital panel, thereby creating a "partial vacuum" that preserves the product without destroying its physical structure.
