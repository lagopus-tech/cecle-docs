## IV. Workflow

### Standard Workflow
1. **Adjust Position**: Adjust the wrapping limit poles on the turntable according to the carton width.
2. **Placement**: Place the carton in the center of the turntable.
3. **Attaching Film**: Pull out the stretch film and press it under the goods.
4. **Start**: Set the required number of turns through the panel and step on the foot pedal switch.
5. **Finish**: The machine stops automatically upon reaching the preset turns (or stops manually), cut the film, and remove the goods.
