## IV. Workflow

### Standard Workflow
1. **Positioning Goods**: Use a forklift to smoothly place the palletized goods in the center of the turntable. At this time, the weighing indicator will automatically display the gross weight of the goods.
2. **Attaching Film**: Manually pull out one end of the stretch film and tie it to the bottom of the goods.
3. **Start**: Operate on the touch screen and press the start button.
4. **Wrapping Process**: The machine rotates automatically, and the film carriage moves up and down according to the program to complete the wrapping.
5. **Finish**: After wrapping is completed, the turntable automatically resets, record the final data on the weighing indicator. Manually cut the film, and the forklift transports the goods away.
