## IV. Operation & Requirements

> ⚠️ **Mandatory Reading for Purchase: Strict size restrictions for the customer's manual forklift (hand pallet jack)!**
> To ensure your manual forklift can smoothly push into the M-shaped chassis, please be sure to measure your forklift size before placing an order:
> 1. **Inner distance of forklift legs**: Must be **> 230mm**
> 2. **Outer distance of forklift legs**: Must be **< 680mm**
> 3. **Chassis ground clearance**: After the forklift is raised, the height of the bottom of the pallet from the ground must be **> 100mm**
> *(Note: This machine is not suitable for special goods where the bottom surface is lower than the forklift load-bearing surface)*

### Standard Workflow
1. **Hand Truck Entry**: Workers use a manual forklift to push the pallet directly into the center of the M-shaped opening of the turntable.
2. **Unloading the Pallet**: Lower the manual forklift so the pallet rests steadily on the turntable, then pull out the forklift and exit the work area.
3. **Attaching Film & Start**: Manually pull out the stretch film and fix it to the bottom of the pallet, then press the start button on the touch screen.
4. **Automatic Wrapping**: The turntable rotates, automatically detects the height of the goods, and automatically packages according to the parameters set by the program.
5. **Film Cutting & Shipping**: After packaging is complete, the machine automatically resets and stops. Manually cut the film, and the worker pushes the manual forklift in again to transport the goods away.

### FAQ
* **Q: How to choose between the standard model (TP1650F-L) and the M-type opening model (TP1650F-M)?**
  * **A**: It depends on your loading tools. If you have a motorized forklift, choose the TP1650F-L standard model (load capacity up to 2000KG); if you only have a manual forklift (hand pallet jack), you must choose the TP1650F-M type. Because it is extremely difficult to push a 1-ton load up the ramp of the standard model with a manual forklift, while the M-type allows you to push straight in on flat ground.
* **Q: Why is the load capacity of the M-type machine only 1000KG?**
  * **A**: Because the chassis is made into an "M" shaped open structure, the physical stress area has changed. To ensure long-term structural stability and safety, we limit the safe load-bearing capacity to 1000KG, which already fully meets the daily handling limits of the vast majority of manual forklifts.
* **Q: What about warranty and after-sales services?**
  * **A**: We provide a 1-year warranty service. During the warranty period, if damage occurs under normal use according to the manual, we provide free replacement parts (the buyer is responsible for the international shipping costs from China to their local area).
