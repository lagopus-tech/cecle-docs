## IV. Workflow

### Standard Workflow
1. **Loading Cargo**: Place the small luggage or carton steadily between the two metal guide bars in the center of the turntable.
2. **Lateral Clamping**: Adjust the position of the vertical guide bars on the turntable to securely clamp and fix the cargo from both sides.
3. **Attaching Film**: Pull out the stretch film and secure it at the bottom of the cargo or around the guide bar.
4. **Start Wrapping**: Press the start button on the panel, and the machine will automatically complete the wrapping according to the preset number of circles.
5. **Film Cutting & Removal**: After the machine stops, manually cut the film, release the guide bars, and remove the packaged cargo.
