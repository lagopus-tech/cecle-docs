## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Stretch Film**：Use 15~35μm specification pre-stretch type machine stretch film (25μm is recommended).
* **Power Supply**：110V/220V voltage.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Loading**：A forklift places the palletized goods in the center of the turntable.
2. **Attaching Film**：Manually pull out one end of the stretch film and tie it to the bottom of the goods.
3. **Start:**：Operate the touch screen and press the start button.
4. **Wrapping Process**：The machine rotates automatically, and the film carriage moves up and down according to the program to complete the wrapping.
5. **Finish**：After wrapping is completed, the turntable automatically resets, manually cut the film, and a forklift moves the goods away.
