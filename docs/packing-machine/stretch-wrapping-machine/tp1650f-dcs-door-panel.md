> **Core Abstract**
> The TP1650F-DCS Door Panel Stretch Wrapping Machine is a specialized packaging solution designed for narrow, tall, flat, and non-freestanding goods such as door panels, wooden boards, and window frames. Featuring an exclusive dual-clamping system (a slot-type top pressure device and a detachable bottom clamp), it ensures long, flat materials remain absolutely vertical and stable during high-speed rotation. With a self-locking safety design, a 250% powered pre-stretch system, this machine maximizes operational safety, material savings, and packaging efficiency. Furthermore, its detachable base allows it to double as a standard pallet wrapper, offering exceptional equipment utilization for manufacturers.

## I. Product Overview

### 1. Product Category
* **Equipment Name**：Door Panel Stretch Wrapping Machine
* **Product Model**：TP1650F-DCS 
* **Equipment Positioning**: Designed specifically for narrow, tall, flat, and non-freestanding goods such as door panels, wooden boards, and window frames. It adopts a top slot-type pressure clamping device and a bottom clamping device to ensure long, flat materials remain absolutely vertical and stable during rotating wrapping.

### 2. Applications
* **Industry Applications**: Stretch wrapping for flat materials like wooden doors, security doors, furniture boards, and window frames.
* **Solving Pain Points**: Completely solves the industry pain points of flat, towering goods being unable to stand on ordinary stretch wrappers, prone to tipping, and having extremely low manual packaging efficiency.
* **Production Scale**: Turntable speed 0-10 rpm (variable frequency adjustable), suitable for large-scale assembly line operations of door and board manufacturers.

### 3. Core Functions
* **Exclusive Board Clamping System**: The top adopts a "slot-type pressure clamping device", and the bottom uses a screw-fixed "detachable clamping device". Working together top and bottom, they firmly lock the door panel in place.
* **Over-top Wrapping Function**: The PLC supports over-top time setting, which can achieve about 30% packaging coverage on the top for better dustproof and moisture-proof effects.
* **Film System**: Uses special rubber guide rollers to maximize the protection of the film from damage, ensuring smooth dispensing and uniform stretching.

## II. Core Advantages

* **Self-locking Safety Design**: The top pressure system is chain-driven, independently controlled, and features a self-locking function. Even if the power is cut off during the packaging process, the pressure plate will not slip and damage the door panel, operating quietly and extremely smoothly.
* **Flexible Detachable Base**: The bottom door panel clamping device is fixed with screws and features a detachable structure. This means that after removing the bottom clamp, the equipment can also handle standard pallet stretch wrapping, serving dual purposes in one machine.

## IV. Workflow

### Standard Workflow
1. **Loading and Fixing**: Manually place the door panel upright into the "special clamping bracket" at the bottom of the turntable and tighten the screws to secure it.
2. **Top Locking**: Operate on the touch screen/panel to lower the top slot-type pressure device, firmly embedding and locking the top of the door panel.
3. **Automatic Wrapping**: Pull out the film, attach it to the door panel, and start the machine. The equipment will automatically complete wrap based on the set over-top time and film carriage lifting speed.
4. **Safe Unloading**: After wrapping, the turntable automatically resets. Raise the top pressure plate, loosen the bottom clamp, and remove the packaged door panel.

### FAQ
* **Q: Can the top of the door panel be wrapped? Does it provide moisture protection?**
  * **A**: Yes. Our PLC control system has a built-in "over-top time setting" function. When wrapping reaches the top, the film carriage will continue to rise to a certain height above the top of the goods, achieving about 30% film coverage on the top. This effectively provides dustproof and moisture-proof protection.
* **Q: What are the benefits of the film carriage's pre-stretch function?**
  * **A**: The machine is equipped with a 250% powered pre-stretch system, allowing 1 meter of film to be stretched to 2.5 meters. For large-area products like door panels, long-term use can save you 30%-50% on packaging consumable costs.
