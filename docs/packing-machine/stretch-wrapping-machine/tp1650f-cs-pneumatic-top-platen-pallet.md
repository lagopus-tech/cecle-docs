## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Stretch Film**: Use 15~35μm specification pre-stretch machine stretch film (25μm recommended).
* **Power Supply**: 110V/220V voltage.
* **⚠️ Air Source**: Must connect to an external air compressor to provide a stable air pressure of 0.4-0.7Mpa.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Loading**: A forklift places the lightweight palletized goods in the center of the turntable.
2. **Pneumatic Top Platen**: Press the start switch, the cylinder pushes the platen down, steadily pressing the top of the goods.
3. **Attaching Film & Wrapping**: After manually attaching the film, the machine begins wrapping according to the parameters set by the program.
4. **Automatic Reset**: After the top and bottom are wrapped for the set number of turns, the cylinder automatically rises, and the turntable automatically resets to the original position.
5. **Film Cutting & Unloading**: Manually cut the film, and remove the packaged finished product.
