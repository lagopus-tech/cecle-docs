### 1. Product Category
* **Equipment Name**：Fully Enclosed Cylindrical Roll Stretch Wrapping Machine
* **Product Model**：HYP2000F-AU 
* **Equipment Positioning**: Specialized equipment designed specifically for stretch wrapping cylindrical objects. By utilizing the rolling friction of rollers to drive the cargo's rotation, it achieves six-sided packaging of cylindrical goods, greatly ensuring product quality and safety during storage and logistics.

### 2. Applications
* **Industry Applications**: Widely used in industries requiring stable transport and storage of cylindrical products, such as paper mills, textiles, building materials, plastic films, metal processing, etc.
* **Pain Points Solved**: Completely resolves industry pain points such as the difficulty of manually packaging heavy cylindrical objects, the inability of standard wrapping machines to cover both end faces, and the danger of unloading heavy rolls.
* **Production Scale**: Specially designed for high-intensity, heavy-duty industrial continuous production, helping enterprises significantly reduce overall operating costs.

### 3. Core Functions
* **Pneumatic Auto-Ejection Unloading**: The automatic ejection mechanism uses airbag-type pneumatic cylinders. After packaging is completed, the mechanism smoothly flips up to push the cylindrical cargo out of the machine. After unloading, the mechanism automatically retracts and resets, realizing automated packaging.
* **Fully Enclosed Moisture-Proof Wrapping**: The rollers' self-rotation drives the cargo to rotate, cooperating with the turntable's revolution to achieve 360° dead-angle-free wrapping on both the cylindrical side (radial) and the two end faces (axial).
* **PLC Programmable Control**: Wrapping layers, over-top time, and over-top height can be set directly on the control panel.

## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Machine Stretch Film**: Use LLDPE pre-stretch machine-grade film with a width of 500mm, a paper core of Φ76.2mm, and a thickness of 20~30μm.
* **Power Supply**: 380V 3-Phase 5-Wire
* **⚠️ Air Source**: Must be connected to an external air compressor providing a stable pressure of 0.6-0.8 Mpa. Without an air source, the pneumatic ejection function cannot be used.

### 4.2 Standard Workflow
1. **Loading and Fixing**: Manually or via an overhead crane, center the cargo on the rollers and fix the film head to one end of the cargo.
2. **Automatic Wrapping**: Input the cargo's diameter and width parameters on the screen, then press the auto-run button. The turntable starts the wrapping process while the rollers begin rotating the cargo. Based on the set diameter, it automatically calculates the number of wrapping cycles.
3. **Cutting Film**: Upon completion, the turntable automatically resets and stops, after which the film is manually cut.
4. **Auto-Ejection**: The roller arm mechanism (airbag cylinder) automatically and smoothly pushes the cargo out, then automatically retracts, completing the packaging workflow.
