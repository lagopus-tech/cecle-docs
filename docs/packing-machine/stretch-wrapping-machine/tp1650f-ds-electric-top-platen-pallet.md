> **Core Abstract**
> The TP1650F-DS Electric Top Platen Pallet Stretch Wrapping Machine is a specialized packaging solution designed for unstable or lightweight pallet loads under 100KG. It is highly suitable for industries handling empty bottles, lightweight cartons, and foam products. Its defining feature is the independent electric top platen system, which securely holds the load in place during high-speed rotation, preventing scattering or shifting. Combined with a 250% powered pre-stretch mechanism to minimize film costs, and a PLC programmable control system, it offers unparalleled stability and efficiency for medium to large-scale manufacturing operations.

### 1. Product Category
* **Equipment Name**：Electric Top Platen Pallet Stretch Wrapping Machine
* **Product Model**：TP1650F-DS

## IV. Workflow

### Standard Workflow
1. **Loading**: A forklift places the lightweight palletized goods in the center of the turntable.
2. **Top Platen Start**: After turning on the switch, the top platen automatically descends to press the top of the goods.
3. **Attaching Film & Wrapping**: After manually attaching the film, the machine automatically senses the height of the goods and begins wrapping, and the film carriage automatically moves up and down.
4. **Automatic Reset**: After wrapping ends, the top platen rises, and the turntable returns to its initial position.
5. **Film Cutting & Unloading**: Manually cut the film, and the forklift takes away the finished product.

### FAQ
* **Q: Will the top platen crush my cartons?**
  * **A**: No. The top platen stroke and pressure can be fine-tuned through the PLC to ensure that the goods are firmly pressed without damaging the outer packaging.
* **Q: If the goods' heights are not uniform, can the top platen work automatically?**
  * **A**: Yes. The machine is equipped with a photoelectric switch that can automatically sense the actual height of each pallet of goods, achieving intelligent start and stop.
