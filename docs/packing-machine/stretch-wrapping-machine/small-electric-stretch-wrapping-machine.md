## IV. Workflow

### Standard Workflow
1. **Loading Cargo**: Place the carton to be packed flat in the center of the black turntable.
2. **Manual Film Attaching**: Pull out one end of the stretch film and secure it to the bottom or edge of the cargo.
3. **Starting Machine**: Turn on the power switch on the side of the machine; the turntable begins rotating at a constant speed.
4. **Stretch Wrapping**: The operator presses the hand-grip top turntable onto the top of the cargo. As the bottom turntable rotates the carton, hold the top turntable handle with one hand to stabilize the cargo, stretching the film tighter and denser.
5. **Film Cutting & Removal**: After reaching the desired number of wrapping layers, turn off the power, manually cut the film, and remove the parcel.
