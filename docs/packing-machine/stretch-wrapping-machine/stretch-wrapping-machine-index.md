# Stretch Wrapping Machine Selection Guide & Product Catalog Overview

## I. What is a Stretch Wrapping Machine?

A Stretch Wrapping Machine is a piece of logistics packaging equipment that uses the tension and elastic recovery of stretch film to wrap and bundle goods into a secure load.

* **Core Value**: Binds scattered items into a unified whole, preventing shifting, scattering, or collapsing during handling and transit. Simultaneously provides excellent dust, moisture, and tamper resistance while significantly reducing manual packing labor and consumable costs.
* **Applicable Industries**: Widely used in foreign trade exports, food & beverages, chemicals & plastics, electronics & appliances, papermaking & textiles, airport logistics, cross-border e-commerce, cable & auto parts, and many other sectors.

---

### Core Operational Workflow

Stretch wrapping is more than just rotating a turntable; it is a systematic process designed for efficiency and cost control:

#### 1. Preparation & Loading (Pre-process)
* **Cargo Positioning**: Using a motorized forklift, manual pallet jack, or manual labor, place the goods steadily in the center of the turntable or packaging station.
* **Attaching Film**: The operator pulls the leading edge of the stretch film and simply fixes it to the bottom of the pallet or edge of the cargo.

#### 2. Wrapping (Core Process)
* **Start & Sensing**: The machine starts and the turntable rotates. Most mainstream models feature a photoelectric sensor that automatically detects cargo height.
* **Stretching & Wrapping**: The film wraps according to preset programs. Standard pallet wrapping models feature a Powered Pre-stretch system to maximize film savings; orbital wrapping machines pass film through the inner ring to achieve 360° wrapping.

#### 3. Film Cutting & Unloading (Post-process)
* **Cutting Film**: Upon completion, the turntable automatically resets to its original position. The operator manually cuts the film (high-end models like QTP1800F-L feature automatic film cutting).
* **Unloading**: The packaged goods are moved out and loaded directly into shipping containers or high-density storage racks.

## III. Technical FAQ & Common Questions

### 1. Selection Advice

* **Q1: How do I select the most suitable stretch wrapper for my warehouse?**
  * **A**: 
    1. *Check loading tools*: If you only have manual pallet jacks, choose **TP1650F-M**; if you have motorized forklifts, choose **TP1650F-L**.
    2. *Check cargo weight & stability*: For extremely light or easily tipped items, choose **TP1650F-CS** or **TP1650F-DS** top-pressure models; for flat door panels, choose **TP1650F-DCS**.
    3. *Check functional needs*: If integrated weighing is required, choose **CTP1650F-L**; for unmanned automation, choose **QTP1800F-L**.

* **Q2: If I want to buy an "Ring Stretch Wrapping Machine" for cable coils, can I order directly?**
  * **A**: No, direct ordering is not recommended. Please send your cable coil's inner diameter, outer diameter, width/thickness, and weight to our sales engineers first so we can tailor the exact machine dimensions for you.

---

### 2. Operation & Calibration

* **Q3: Why does the stretch film break frequently during wrapping?**
  * **A**: Film breakage is typically caused by:
    1. *Sharp burrs on pallets*: Protruding nails or wood splinters on pallet corners puncture the film.
    2. *Excessive tension*: Film output tension can be reduced on the operation panel.
    3. *Substandard film quality*: Using low-quality film unsuited for powered pre-stretch. We recommend using uniform pre-stretch machine-grade film.

* **Q4: How much money can 150% or 250% pre-stretch actually save? (For pallet wrappers)**
  * **A**: Pre-stretch refers to two internal rollers in the film carriage rotating at different speeds, physically elongating the film inside the machine before it touches the cargo. A 250% pre-stretch ratio means 1 meter of film is stretched into 3.5 meters. Compared to manual wrapping or mechanical tension wrappers, this can save approx. 30% to 50% in film purchasing costs over time.
