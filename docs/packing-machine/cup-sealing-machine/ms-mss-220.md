## 4. Operation Flow
1. **Power-On & Preheat:** Connect the power supply, turn on the machine, and wait for the heating plate to reach the designated sealing temperature.
2. **Place the Cup:** Place the filled PP cup into the cup holder tray beneath the machine.
3. **Push & Seal:** Push the cup holder tray into the machine. The electronic eye sensor will automatically recognize the film pattern, and the machine will lower the heating plate to complete precise heat-sealing and film-cutting.
4. **Retrieve Cup:** Once sealing is complete, pull out the tray and remove the securely sealed beverage cup.

### FAQ
**Q1: What is the difference between MS-220 and MSS-220?**
A1: The primary difference lies in the outer casing material. **MS-220** features a black plastic shell, which is stylish and lightweight. **MSS-220** features a stainless steel shell, offering superior rust resistance and durability for damp, high-use bar environments. **Q2: What is the maximum cup height this machine can seal? Are there limits on cup diameters?**

**Q3: Will the sealing pattern align incorrectly or look crooked?**
