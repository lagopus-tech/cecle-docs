# VS-600 Table-top Semi-automatic Tray Sealing Machine

## 1. Product Overview

The VS-600 is a table-top tray sealer designed for small and medium-sized food service operations, central kitchens, and supermarket fresh-food departments. Its compact body takes up minimal space and is built for countertop operation.

* **Flexible Customization**: Molds are custom-built 1:1 to match your tray shape (square, round, or multi-compartment).
* **Easy Operation**: Switchable between manual and semi-automatic modes; a single operator handles tray loading, sealing, and discharge.
* **Clean Seal Finish**: Automatic film cutting leaves neat seal edges with no excess trim.

## 3. Key Selling Points

* **High-Precision Mold Control**: Uniform sealing pressure ensures a tight, leak-proof and air-tight seal even when a small amount of oil is present on the tray rim.
* **Quick Mold Changeover**: If you work with trays in several sizes, a mold can be changed in only 5-10 minutes, allowing one machine to serve multiple products.

## 4. FAQ

**Q1: Will sealed trays leak?**
A1: No. High-precision digital temperature control and evenly distributed sealing pressure deliver a 100% tight seal, even for takeaway meals containing a large amount of liquid.

**Q2: Can the machine seal special materials such as pulp or aluminum foil trays?**
A2: Yes. We match the optimal heating mold and temperature parameters to your tray and film materials so that every material achieves an ideal seal.

**Q3: Does the machine work with a fixed tray size?**
A3: All molds are custom-made to the customer's tray dimensions. Customers need to send us tray samples for mold production, and we test the machine before shipment.

**Q4: We use trays in several different sizes. Can one machine handle all of them?**
A4: Different tray sizes require different molds. The machine price includes one mold set; additional molds can be purchased for other tray sizes at $2,000.00 per set. We also provide a mold-changeover video for reference.

**Q5: What after-sales support is provided?**
A5: A 1-year warranty is provided. A set of spare wear parts is supplied free with the machine, together with video installation guidance and online technical support.
