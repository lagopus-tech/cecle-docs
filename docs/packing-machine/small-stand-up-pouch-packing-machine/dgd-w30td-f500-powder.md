## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Conditions
* **Consumables**: Premade bags, powdered materials to be packed.
* **Air Supply**: An air compressor must be prepared by the user, providing a stable air pressure of 0.6-0.8 MPA.
* **Power Supply**: 110V/220V voltage.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Feeding**: Pour the powdered material into the 16L stainless steel large silo at the top of the machine.
2. **Load Bags**: Place the premade bags neatly into the machine's bag bin.
3. **Setup**: Input the target weight (e.g., 200g) on the touchscreen.
4. **Auto-run**: The machine automatically fetches the bag -> automatically opens the bag -> the auger nozzle extends into the bag opening for precise quantitative powder filling -> automatically thermal seals -> outputs the finished product.
