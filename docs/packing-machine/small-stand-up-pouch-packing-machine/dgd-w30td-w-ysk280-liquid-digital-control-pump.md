## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Conditions
* **Consumables**: Premade bags, pure liquid materials placed in ground-level buckets.
* **Air Supply**: An air compressor must be prepared by the user, providing a stable air pressure of 0.6-0.8 MPA.
* **Power Supply**: 110V/220V voltage.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Preparation**: Insert the machine's self-suction tube directly into your liquid raw material bucket, eliminating the need to climb up for pouring.
2. **Load Bags**: Place the premade bags neatly into the machine's bag bin.
3. **Setup**: Input the target milliliter volume (e.g., 250ml) on the touchscreen.
4. **Auto-run**: The machine automatically fetches the bag -> automatically opens the bag -> self-priming pump injects the liquid -> automatically heat seals -> outputs the finished product.

### FAQ
* **Q: Can this machine pack red oil with sesame seeds, or sauces containing broad bean particles?**
  * **A**: Absolutely not. This model is designed specifically for pure liquids. It requires materials to be completely free of impurities and viscosity. Any particulates such as sesame seeds or Sichuan peppercorns will clog the suction tubing and potentially damage the pump body. If you need to package sauces with particulates, please contact us for a pneumatic plunger pump model (such as DGD-W30TD-W-J500).
