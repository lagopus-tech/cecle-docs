## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Conditions
* **Consumables**: Premade bags, block/strip materials to be packaged.
* **Air Supply**: An air compressor must be prepared by the user, providing a stable air pressure of 0.6-0.8 MPA.
* **Power Supply**: 110V/220V voltage.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Positioning**: 3-4 operators stand ready on both sides of the bucket conveyor belt.
2. **Load Bags**: Place the premade bags neatly into the machine's bag bin.
3. **Manual Feeding**: The machine starts, and the bucket belt moves at a uniform speed. Workers sequentially drop materials into passing empty buckets.
4. **Auto-bagging & Sealing**: The bucket travels above the main unit and automatically dumps, dropping materials into the expanded pouch, after which the machine completes thermal sealing and outputs the finished product.

### FAQ
* **Q: Can the bucket running speed be adjusted? What if workers can't keep up with feeding?**
  * **A**: Yes, the running speed of the buckets is adjustable. When you lower the bucket speed, the main unit's bag picking, opening, and sealing speeds will also automatically synchronize and slow down. You can fine-tune the speed based on material complexity and worker proficiency to find the most comfortable human-machine coordination rhythm.
* **Q: My products are sausages (or nuts), and I want better freshness and a longer shelf life. Are there any optional customization options?**
  * **A**: We offer a rich array of optional devices.
    * If you need to extend the shelf life of meat products like sausages, we can install a "Vacuum Packaging Device" or a "Nitrogen Flushing Device";
    * If your materials are light and prone to stacking (like dried fruits), we recommend adding a "Bag Bottom Tapping Device", which taps the bottom of the bag after filling to settle the materials and ensure a clean, seal-friendly opening.(For stand-up pouches only.)
* **Q: What about warranty and after-sales services?**
  * **A**: We provide a 1-year warranty service. During the warranty period, if damage occurs under normal use according to the manual, we provide free replacement parts (the buyer is responsible for the international shipping costs from China to their local area).
