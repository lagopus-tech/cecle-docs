## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Conditions
* **Consumables**: Premade bags, compound sauces to be packaged.
* **Air Supply**: An air compressor must be prepared by the user, providing a stable air pressure of 0.6-0.8 MPA.
* **Power Supply**: 110V/220V voltage.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Loading & Mixing**: Pour the sauce into the 80L U-shaped hopper at the top of the machine, and turn on the automatic stirring function to thoroughly blend the sauce.
2. **Load Bags**: Place the premade bags neatly into the machine's bag bin.
3. **Setup**: Directly input the target filling volume (e.g., 350ml) on the touchscreen.
4. **Auto-run**: The machine automatically fetches the bag -> automatically opens the bag -> the plunger pump precisely and quantitatively fills it -> automatically heat seals -> outputs the finished product.

### FAQ
* **Q: Why does the hopper require "U-shaped stirring"? Are regular round hoppers insufficient?**
  * **A**:Regular round hoppers suffer from uneven gravity, causing sauces to accumulate at the bottom with dead zones in mixing. Our 80L U-shaped stirring hopper provides 360-degree dead-zone-free tumbling and mixing, ensuring the "oil and meat particle" ratio is uniform across every package, eliminating scenarios where the first bag is all oil and the last bag is all residue.
* **Q: Sauce packaging is prone to leakage; can your seals guarantee safety?**
  * **A**: Absolutely. Our equipment features a standard "500ml blow-filling head," which uses a pneumatic air burst at the end of filling to eliminate dripping and prevent sauce from staining the sealing area. Combined with standard mesh-patterned heating seal dies, this ensures even heat distribution, zero material pinching, and a strong, secure seal.
* **Q: What about warranty and after-sales services?**
  * **A**: We provide a 1-year warranty service. During the warranty period, if damage occurs under normal use according to the manual, we provide free replacement parts (the buyer is responsible for the international shipping costs from China to their local area).
