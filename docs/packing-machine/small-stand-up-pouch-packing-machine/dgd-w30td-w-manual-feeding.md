## I. Product Overview

### 1. Product Category
* **Equipment Name**：Manual Feeding Stand Up Pouch Packing Machine
* **Product Model**：DGD-W30TD-W 
* **Equipment Positioning**：A premade pouch packing main machine specifically designed for "hard-to-pack materials". It is extremely suitable for materials with irregular shapes, those that cannot be automatically and quantitatively discharged, or items that easily scatter or misalign during the dropping process (solving blind spots through manual feeding).

### 2. Applications
* **Industry Applications**：Widely used in the food, daily chemical, and hardware industries. Examples include disposable face towels, casual snacks, hardware accessories, etc.
* **Applicable Materials**：The premier choice for manually feeding irregular materials. Meanwhile, this machine features strong expandability; when paired with different automatic feeders, it can also pack conventional granules, liquids, pastes, etc.
* **Packaging Bag Types**：Suitable for premade pouches, such as flat pouches, stand-up pouches, zipper bags, etc. Not suitable for bags that are too soft, as they cannot be normally picked up by the machine.

### 3. Core Functions

* **Optional Universal Interface**：If you have plans to upgrade to a fully automated production line in the future, please inform us before placing your order. We can customize a "universal signal interface" for you before leaving the factory (including signals for material readiness, discharging, foot pedal, photoelectric sensor, etc.), making it convenient to connect with other external weighing/filling equipment later.
* **Bag Compatibility**: Compatible with multiple bag sizes, easily adjustable to fit bag specifications ranging from 5×7cm to 20×30cm.

## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Conditions
* **Consumables**: Premade bags.
* **Air Supply**: An air compressor must be prepared by the user, providing a stable air pressure of 0.6-0.8 MPA.
* **Power Supply**: 110V/220V voltage.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Load Bags**: Place the premade bags neatly into the machine's bag bin.
2. **Setup**: Set parameters on the touchscreen.
3. **Feeding**: The machine automatically fetches and opens the bag, and the operator manually feeds the materials into the bag.
4. **Sealing**: The machine automatically completes the thermal sealing and outputs the finished product.
