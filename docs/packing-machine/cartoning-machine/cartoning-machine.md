## VI. Workflow

1. **Material Feeding & Unscrambling**: Products are automatically fed into the machine via an automatic conveying device or matched bottle unscrambler from the main production line.
2. **Leaflet Insertion (Optional)**: If configured, the leaflet folding and insertion unit automatically places the paper manual into or alongside the product.
3. **Carton Feeding & Opening**: The carton magazine automatically feeds flat paper boxes, and the vacuum suction system opens and squares up the carton.
4. **Product Pushing into Carton**: The mechanical pusher accurately inserts the product (along with the leaflet) into the opened carton.
5. **Flap Folding & Sealing**: The machine folds the carton flaps and executes either tuck-in mechanical sealing or hot-melt glue sealing (via optional glue applicator).
6. **Finished Product Output**: The fully sealed and inspected paper box is automatically discharged from the machine outfeed.
