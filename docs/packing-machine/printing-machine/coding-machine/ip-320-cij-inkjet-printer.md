## IV. Sales Recommendations & FAQ

### Sales Recommendations
* **Substrate & Environment Assessment**: Evaluate the customer's production environment, line speed, and substrate material (glass, plastic, metal, or cable) to recommend the appropriate ink type (compatible with 630, B600, 601, A630, 680, 619, 669 inks).
* **Line Integration Accessories**: Confirm if the customer requires external shaft encoders for unsteady conveyor speeds or dual photocells for precise product detection.
* **Maintenance & Safety Training**: Advise operators to wear protective glasses and resistant gloves when handling makeup solvents and to utilize the automated nozzle flush procedures regularly.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
* **Q1: How do I start up and shut down the IP-320 CIJ printer properly?**
  * A1: To start, turn on the side power switch, press "F1" to feed the ink line, and after 3 minutes press "F2" to turn on high pressure and enter ready status. To shut down, press "F1" while the ink line is fed; the printer will automatically flush and enter stop status after about 3 minutes before powering off.
* **Q2: What should I do if the print quality becomes poor or incomplete?**
  * A2: First, check the distance between the printhead and the object, verify character height/width settings, and run the automated nozzle flush or purge function to clean the nozzle and gutter.
* **Q3: Can the printer handle unsteady production line speeds?**
  * A3: Yes. You can connect an external shaft encoder (synchronizer) and select the external synchronizer source in the print control menu to synchronize printing with variable conveyor speeds.

## VI. Workflow

1. **Power-Up & System Check**: Verify cable connections, ensure the printhead is clean and dry, turn on the side power switch, and press "F1" to feed the ink line.
2. **High-Pressure Activation**: Once the ink stream is stable, press "F2" to turn on high pressure (EHT/300V) and achieve the "Ready" status indicated by the green LED.
3. **Message Selection & Parameter Setup**: Select or create the print message (including text, clocks, counters, or barcodes) and adjust parameters such as character height, width, product delay, and raster settings.
4. **Inline Triggering & Printing**: As products pass the photocell sensor on the conveyor line, the printer automatically applies the high-definition ink jet code at the specified delay time.
5. **Shutdown & Maintenance**: At the end of the production run, initiate the shutdown sequence by pressing "F1" to flush the nozzle and safely power down the system.
