### VI. Workflow
1. **Die & Foil Setup**: Secure the custom hot stamping plate (die) onto the 400x250mm heating plate and thread the hot stamping foil through the guide mechanism.
2. **Bag Positioning**: Place the paper shopping bag onto the customized fixture board, ensuring it is flat and correctly aligned.
3. **Safety & Activation**: Initiate the stamping cycle (with optional light curtain and acrylic guard active for operator protection).
4. **Pneumatic/Hydraulic Stamping**: The heated plate descends under 1T pneumatic pressure (or optional 2T hydraulic pressure), pressing the foil onto the bag surface.
5. **Finished Product Removal**: The plate retracts automatically, and the finished custom-branded shopping bag is removed for packaging.
