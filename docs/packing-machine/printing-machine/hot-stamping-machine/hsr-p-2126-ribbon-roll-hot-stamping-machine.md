### VI. Workflow
1. **Material Loading**: Mount the roll of ribbon, lanyard, or label material onto the unwinding spindle of the roll-to-roll system.
2. **Foil Setup**: Thread the hot stamping foil through the precision tension and feeding guides over the stamping zone.
3. **Die Installation & Calibration**: Secure the custom hot stamping plate (die) onto the heating plate, set the desired temperature, and calibrate positioning (using the optional photoelectric sensor for label rolls if applicable).
4. **Automatic Stamping & Embossing**: The machine automatically lowers the heated plate, pressing the foil onto the ribbon surface at a speed of 10-30 times/min to form a permanent, crisp design.
5. **Roll Rewinding**: The finished stamped ribbon or lanyard is automatically pulled through and neatly wound back into a roll on the rewinding spindle.
