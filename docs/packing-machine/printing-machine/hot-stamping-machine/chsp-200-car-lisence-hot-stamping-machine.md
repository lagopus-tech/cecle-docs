3. **Wide Material Adaptability**: Perfectly adapts to aluminum license plates while also being widely applicable to plastic rulers, PVC, metal strips, wood products, and other long-strip materials.
4. **Superior Stamping Texture & Durability**: Stamped logos or lines feature full colors, sharp edges, wear-scratch resistance, UV resistance, and long-lasting colorfastness.
5. **Robust Industrial-Grade Structure**: Solid machine body structure ensuring stable operation with low noise, suitable for long-term continuous high-intensity production tasks.

### VI. Workflow
1. **Loading & Positioning**: Place the workpieces to be processed (such as car license plates, plastic rulers, etc.) stably onto the workbench or feeding positioning station.
2. **Parameter Setup**: Set the appropriate stamping temperature and operating speed according to the workpiece material.
3. **Foil Installation**: Thread the hot transfer stamping foil correctly through the rolling hot stamping head mechanism and secure it.
4. **Initiate Rolling Stamping**: Trigger the semi-automatic working cycle; the roller drives the heating roller to roll and press continuously, transferring the foil precisely onto the workpiece surface.
5. **Finished Product Unloading**: Remove the finished product after single or continuous stamping is complete, inspect the surface quality, and proceed with packaging.
