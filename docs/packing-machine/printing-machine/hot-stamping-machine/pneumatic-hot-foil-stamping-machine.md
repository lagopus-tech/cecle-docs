### I. Product Overview

* **Application Scenarios**: Widely used for hot stamping and embossing on wallets, leather goods, leather logos, embossed labels, paper, cards, and various flat plastic or paper products.
* **Core Functions**: Semi-automatic pneumatic control, offering four different heating plate sizes and pressure specifications (GP-180 to GP-350, pressures from 0.24T to 1T). Precise temperature and pneumatic pressure control achieve clear and long-lasting flat hot stamping or foil embossing.

1. **Multiple Models and Specifications**: Covers GP-180 (14×18cm), GP-210 (21×26cm), GP-300 (30×40cm), and GP-350 (35×50cm) to meet diverse production needs from small wallets and labels to large-format flat surfaces.

5. **User-Friendly Operation & Maintenance**: Simple and intuitive operation interface with perfect stamping results; customers only need to connect an external air compressor upon receiving the machine to start production.

### VI. Workflow
1. **Air and Power Connection**: Connect the machine to an air compressor to ensure stable air pressure, and plug into the corresponding power supply.
2. **Mold and Foil Installation**: Install the custom hot stamping plate onto the heating plate (or honeycomb plate) and thread the hot transfer stamping foil properly.
3. **Parameter and Positioning Adjustment**: Set the operating temperature and stamping time, and use the micro-adjustment worktable to adjust the product and stamping plate to the optimal alignment state.
4. **Dual-Button Safety Start**: Place the product onto the worktable (or feed it in via the optional pneumatic/servo sliding table) and press both buttons simultaneously to start.
5. **Pneumatic Stamping and Product Removal**: The pneumatic pressing head automatically descends to complete the stamping and then retracts, allowing you to remove the finished product for quality inspection and subsequent packaging.
