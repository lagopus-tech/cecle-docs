### 2. Series Models & Application Scenarios Details

1. **AGP-S-210 (Flat & Round Dual-Use Semi-Auto Stamping Machine)**
   * **Features**: Combines flat and curved surface stamping. By installing or removing custom circular molds, it can process cylindrical items (mugs, bottles) as well as flat items (labels, jewelry boxes).
   * **Best For**: Craft gift processing workshops and growing businesses handling mixed product shapes.

2. **PHS-320 (Automatic Plastic Plates Stamping Machine)**
   * **Features**: Utilizes gas-liquid pressurization (3T pressure) and a high-precision servo sliding table with standard safety light curtains. Supports quick mold changes for various plate sizes and optional robotic unloading.
   * **Best For**: Large-scale tableware manufacturing factories and high-volume plastic plate production.

3. **HSR-P-2126 (Fully Auto Roll-to-Roll Ribbon Stamping Machine)**
   * **Features**: Integrated automatic unwinding and rewinding system. Standard single-lane processing with options to customize 2, 3, or more lanes simultaneously, plus optional photoelectric sensors.
   * **Best For**: Ribbon manufacturing factories, garment accessory suppliers, and lanyard printers.

4. **HSB-4025 (Semi-Auto Paper Shopping Bag & Logo Stamping Machine)**
   * **Features**: 400×250mm heating plate with 1T pneumatic pressure (optional 2T hydraulic upgrade) and customizable bag-fixing boards. Optional safety acrylic guards and light curtains for US/EU compliance.
   * **Best For**: Paper bag manufacturers, retail packaging customization studios, and gift box makers.

5. **DGP-210 (Desktop Pneumatic Stamping Machine)**
   * **Features**: Compact tabletop footprint combined with an advanced booster cylinder delivering a robust 2T maximum pressure.
   * **Best For**: Small studios, retail counters, and leather workshops requiring high industrial pressure without large floor space.

6. **CHSP-200 (Car License Plate Continuous Rolling Stamping Machine)**
   * **Features**: Semi-automatic rolling (roll-on) technology designed to prevent crushing or ghosting defects typical when stamping long-strip items with flat presses.
   * **Best For**: License plate manufacturing plants, stationery ruler makers, and PVC/metal strip processors.

7. **HGP-300 / HGP-350 (Heavy-Duty Hydraulic Hot Stamping Press Series)**
   * **Features**: Industrial-grade hydraulic systems ranging from 3T to 15T. Specially designed for large-size and high-density stamping on paper, plastic parts, and leather. Supports custom engineering for oversized heating plates.
   * **Best For**: Large-area high-density branding, heavy industrial packaging, and premium leather or heavy plastic processing.

8. **GP Series 180 / 210 / 300 / 350 (General Pneumatic Flat Stamping Series)**
   * **Features**: Features lighter pressure, offering a classic pneumatic flat stamping series ranging from small to large formats. Specifically tailored for paper, plastic parts, or small-size leather hot stamping, with upgrade options for micro-adjustment tables and sliding worktables.
   * **Best For**: Standard paper goods, plastic components, and small-format leather workshops.

9. **SHS-10 (Plastic Seal Serial Number Hot Foil Stamping Machine)**
   * **Features**: Specialized dual-head pneumatic system allowing simultaneous logo stamping and consecutive serial numbering (typically processing 10 security seals per batch).
   * **Best For**: Security seal manufacturers and logistics supply chain tagging enterprises.

### 3. Key Differences & Selection Recommendations

1. **Classification by Product Shape:**
   * **Cylindrical/Curved + Flat Dual-Use**: Choose **AGP-S-210**.
   * **Long-Strip Workpieces / License Plates / Rulers**: Choose **CHSP-200** (Continuous Rolling).
   * **Plastic Plates / Tableware Edges**: Choose **PHS-320** (Servo & Gas-Liquid Automation).
   * **Roll-to-Roll Ribbons / Lanyards**: Choose **HSR-P-2126** (Roll-to-Roll Automation).
   * **Plastic Security Seals (Sequential Numbering)**: Choose **SHS-10** (Dual-Head Stamping).
   * **General Flat Items (Paper Bags, Leather, Cards, Boxes)**: Choose from **GP Series**, **DGP-210**, **HSB-4025**, or heavy-duty **HGP Series** depending on size and tonnage.

2. **Classification by Pressure & Substrate Hardness:**
   * **Light Pressure Flat Work (Paper, plastic parts, or small-size leather: 0.24T - 1T)**: Recommend **GP Series (180/210/300/350)**.
   * **Medium-Format High Pressure (Leather, tough plastics, heavy cardstock: 2T)**: Recommend space-saving **DGP-210** (Desktop Booster Cylinder).
   * **Large Shopping Bags & Retail Boxes (1T - 2T)**: Recommend **HSB-4025** (with optional pneumatic/hydraulic upgrades).
   * **Large-Size, High-Density Stamping (Large-area paper, plastic parts, and leather heavy-duty pressing: 3T - 15T+ Hydraulic)**: Recommend **HGP-300 / HGP-350**.
