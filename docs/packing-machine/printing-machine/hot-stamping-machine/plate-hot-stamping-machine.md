### VI. Workflow
1. **Pneumatic & Electrical Connection**: Connect the machine to an external air compressor (supplying 0.4~0.6mpa air pressure) and plug into a 380V industrial power supply.
2. **Mold & Consumables Installation**: Install the corresponding fixed mold matching the plate size, mount the hot stamping die, and properly thread the hot stamping foil.
3. **Manual Loading**: Under the protection of safety light curtains, the operator manually places the plate needing hot stamping into the feeding position.
4. **Startup & Servo Sliding**: Press the dual-button start or step on the foot switch, and the servo sliding table smoothly carries the plate into the interior of the machine.
5. **Automatic Stamping & Unloading**: The gas-liquid pressurization head applies 3T pressure to complete precision stamping; subsequently, the sliding table slides out, and the finished plate is retrieved manually or by the customized automated robotic arm to enter the next cycle.
