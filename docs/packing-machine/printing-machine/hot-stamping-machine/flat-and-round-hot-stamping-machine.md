Perfectly suited for promotional gift processing, custom packaging, and craft industries, it features an adaptable design that allows quick switching between flat and round stamping modes via custom circular molds. ### I. Product Overview
* **Product Category**: Flat and Round Dual-Use Semi-Automatic Hot Foil Stamping Machine / Dual-Purpose Pneumatic Stamping Press

### VI. Workflow
1. **Air & Power Connection**: Connect the machine to an external air compressor (supplying 0.6–0.8mpa air pressure) and plug into the 110V/220V power source.
2. **Die & Foil Setup**: Mount the hot stamping die onto the heating plate and thread the hot stamping foil through the feeding path.
3. **Mode Configuration (Flat or Round)**: 
   * *For Round Stamping*: Install the custom circular mold designed for your cylindrical product (e.g., mug or bottle).
   * *For Flat Stamping*: Remove the circular mold and fixture to use the standard flat working surface.
4. **Stroke & Parameter Adjustment**: Adjust the heating plate descent distance and set the operating temperature and stamping time according to your material.
5. **Stamping Operation**: Place the product onto the mold/worktable, trigger the pneumatic control to execute the stamp, and retrieve the finished item.
