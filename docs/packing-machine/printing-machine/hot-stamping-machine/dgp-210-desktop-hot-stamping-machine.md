### VI. Workflow
1. **Die & Foil Setup**: Secure the custom hot stamping plate (die) onto the 210x260mm heating plate and thread the hot stamping foil through the feeding mechanism.
2. **Parameter Adjustment**: Set the target operating temperature and adjust the air pressure (0.6–0.8 MPa) according to the material specifications.
3. **Material Positioning**: Place the target product (paper, leather piece, or plastic part) securely onto the workbench, aligning it with the stamping area.
4. **Pneumatic Stamping**: Trigger the semi-automatic cycle; the booster cylinder activates to deliver up to 2T of pressure, pressing the heated die and foil onto the material surface.
5. **Finished Product Retrieval**: The heating plate automatically returns to its upper position, allowing you to safely remove the neatly stamped or embossed product.
