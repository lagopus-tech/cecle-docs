### 3. Core Functions
* **Auto-Scanning (Cup Contour Scanning)**: Automatically scans the bottle shape to seamlessly achieve uninterrupted printing on cylindrical, conical, and special-shaped products.
* **White + Color + Varnish Simultaneous Printing**: Supports simultaneous output of 6 color inks, white ink, and varnish in a single pass, delivering a stereoscopic embossed effect and high glossiness.
* **High Drop Fine Print**: Ink drops as small as 3.5PL, with a maximum printing drop height of up to 25mm, perfectly handling various drops and special-shaped products.
* **Auto Height Measurement & Anti-Collision**: Uses infrared radiation to automatically set a safe printing height, preventing printhead scratches.
* **UV Light Leakage Protection**: Automatically turns off the UV light when there is a risk of radiation exposure to the printhead, comprehensively protecting core components.

## IV. Operation Guide & Tips

### Standard Workflow
1. **Power On & Self-Check**: Turn on the main power supply, check air pressure, ink negative pressure, and cooling systems, and wait for the machine to auto-zero.
2. **Import Files**: Import the target cylindrical design artwork into the RIP software (RIIN) (supports TIFF, JPG, EPS, PDF, PNG, AI, PSD formats), and adjust dimensions along with white/color/varnish layers.
3. **Load Workpiece & Auto-Scan**: Place the cylindrical/conical product onto the rotary fixture, and initiate **Auto-Scanning** to let the system automatically recognize the bottle contour.
4. **Height Measurement & Positioning**: Use the infrared sensor to automatically set the safe printing height, ensuring the optimal distance between the printhead and the workpiece.
5. **Start Printing**: Initiate printing; the machine will automatically execute rotary inkjet printing and simultaneous LED UV curing.
6. **Unload & Quality Check**: Take out the finished product and inspect the color adhesion and varnish effect.
