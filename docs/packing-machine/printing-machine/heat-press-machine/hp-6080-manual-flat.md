## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Media**: Flat items to be heat-pressed such as T-shirts/jerseys/yoga mats, heat-transfer paper.
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V power source.
* **Placement Space**: The machine is large; place it on a sturdy, level tabletop or floor with enough clearance around it for operation.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Preheat**: Connect the power, turn on the machine switch, set the target temperature and time on the digital controller, and wait for the aluminum heating plate to reach the set value.
2. **Lay the Material**: Lay the workpiece flat on the bottom high-temperature-resistant silicone pad, place the transfer paper pattern-side down and align it in position.
3. **Apply Pressure**: Hold both pressure handles with both hands and press the heating plate down smoothly; the machine begins the automatic countdown.
4. **Finish**: When the buzzer sounds, pull the pressure handles upward, lift the heating plate, peel off the transfer paper—printing is complete.

* **Q: How do I adjust the pressure knob, and how do I judge if the pressure is right?**
  * **A**: Adjust the pressure via the machine's pressure knob. After pressing, if the pattern edges are sharp, with no missing color and no sliding, the pressure is appropriate; if the transfer looks faded or the silicone pad shows deep imprints, fine-tune it.
