## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Media**: Blank heat-resistant 3D phone cases (must have a sublimation coating), blank mugs/ceramic plates, etc.
* **Transfer Paper**: Paper printed with sublimation patterns, high-temperature-resistant tape.
* **Auxiliary Tools**: Phone-case molds, mug fixtures, etc.
* **Power Supply**: 220V or 110V power source.

### 2. Standard Workflow (3D phone case as example)
1. **Preheat**: Power on, set the temperature and time on the control panel (typically 190℃, 360 seconds), and wait for the machine to heat up.
2. **Wrap Pattern**: Fix the sublimation-paper pattern onto the blank 3D phone case using high-temperature-resistant tape.
3. **Load Chamber**: Open the 108° flip-top, place the wrapped and molded phone case into the chamber, and close the lid.
4. **Vacuum Transfer**: Press the **`Vacuum`** key; the machine automatically starts suction and vacuuming, then press the countdown key and the machine automatically begins printing.
5. **Vent & Unload**: When the countdown ends, **the machine automatically vents** and emits a "beep" alarm. Open the lid, remove the phone case, peel off the paper, and the transfer is complete.

### FAQ
* **Q: Does this machine come with a built-in vacuum pump? Do I need to buy an air pump separately?**
  * **A**: **No separate purchase needed.** Our HP-3042 already has a **built-in high-stability vacuum pump** inside the machine. You only need to plug it in to use it—no need to connect an external air compressor as with pneumatic heat presses.
* **Q: Can it wrap ordinary phone cases, or ordinary ceramic mugs?**
  * **A**: **No.**
    1. All cups and plates must have a **"sublimation coating"** on the surface for the pattern to transfer;
    2. Phone cases must be **heat-resistant 3D phone cases with a sublimation coating**. If an ordinary cheap phone case is placed in the 190℃ chamber, it will directly melt and deform.
