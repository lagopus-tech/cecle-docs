> **Core Abstract**
> The HP-3838 / HP-4060 is a cost-effective, compact flatbed heat press machine built for small-batch heat transfer onto flat substrates such as T-shirts, canvas bags, and mouse pads. It is best suited to startup printing studios, DIY entrepreneurs, apparel shops, cross-border e-commerce sellers, school clubs, and advertising signage production. Its core advantages include an M-type densely arranged cast-aluminum heating plate that delivers uniform, edge-to-center heat without color cast; a manual top-mounted pressure knob for quick adjustment across different substrate thicknesses; and a smart digital controller with accurate ±1℃ temperature control and automatic timing. Available in two platen sizes (38×38 cm and 40×60 cm) with 1400W and 2400W power, it offers light weight (23 kg / 35 kg) and space-saving clamshell operation for flexible studio use.

### FAQ
* **Q: How do I switch the temperature display between Fahrenheit (℉) and Celsius (℃)?**
  * **A**: Press and hold the red **`SET`** key on the upper row of the controller for **3 seconds** until the upper display shows **`LOY`**, then press the plus key on the lower row until it shows **`88`**. Press **`SET`** again to enter the setting mode, and keep pressing **`SET`** until the upper display shows the letter **`CF`** for the Fahrenheit/Celsius conversion. When the upper row shows **`CF`** and the lower row shows **`0`**, the controller is in Celsius (℃); when the upper row shows **`CF`** and the lower row shows **`1`**, the controller is in Fahrenheit (℉).
* **Q: What is the warranty and after-sales service policy?**
