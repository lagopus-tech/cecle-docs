## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Media**: Garments/fabric to be printed, heat-transfer labels, heat-transfer paper, etc.
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V power source.
* **Air Compressor**: Recommended working pressure 0.4 MPa (factory standard setting).

### 2. Standard Workflow
**Step 1: Connect the air source and adjust pressure**
1. Connect the air hose to the pneumatic machine, ensuring the fitting is secure and leak-free.
2. Lift the pressure valve and rotate clockwise to increase pressure, counterclockwise to decrease it. The factory standard pressure is set to 0.4 MPa and usually needs no frequent adjustment; after adjustment, press the pressure valve down to lock it.
3. Adjust the solenoid valve (set at the factory, generally no need to adjust): the upper regulator raises the pressure setting to control the heating plate lift force; the lower regulator sets the downward pressure to control the heating plate pressing force. When correctly adjusted, the heating plate should lift and lower smoothly at the set pressure.

**Step 2: Controller Setting**
1. Press the "SET" button; the display shows SP—press the up/down keys to set the desired temperature.
2. Press "SET" again; the display shows St—press the up/down keys to set the desired pressing time.
3. Finally, hold the "SET" button for about 3 seconds to save the setting; the machine begins to heat up.
4. To reset the counter, hold the "RESET" key for about 3 seconds to zero the COUNTER.

**Step 3: Pressing Operation**
1. Once the temperature reaches the set value, place the workpiece on the high-temperature-resistant silicone pad of one of the fixed aluminum worktables. (The two lower worktables are fixed and do not move left/right.)
2. Push the moving handle to slide the upper heating-plate carriage to the designated position; the micro-switch is triggered, the heating plate presses down automatically, and the countdown begins.
3. When the countdown ends, the heating plate lifts automatically.
4. Push the moving handle to slide the upper carriage to the other worktable, remove the finished product, and load the next item—alternate between the two sides and repeat the process.
