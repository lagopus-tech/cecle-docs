## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Media**: Sublimation/transfer paper and the item to be printed (cotton, linen, chemical fiber, metal, ceramic, glass, etc.).
* **Power Supply**: 110V/220V power source.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Inspection**: Check the wiring and the machine; confirm there are no obvious signs such as appearance damage or broken wires.
2. **Power-on Preparation**: Lift the handle and open the heating plate so the machine is in an open position.
3. **Pressure Adjustment**: Place the item to be printed on the worktable and test whether the pressure is appropriate. If not, rotate the pressure knob to adjust (clockwise to increase pressure, counterclockwise to decrease pressure). After adjusting, press the handle down to test whether the pressure is appropriate.
   *Note: Pressure adjustment should be done before placing the transfer product on the worktable; different products require separate pressure adjustment.*
4. **Prepare the Item**: Prepare the item to be printed. Do not place the item on the worktable during heating to avoid scorching.
   *PS: Use sublimation paper (transfer paper) and sublimation ink (pigment ink) to mirror-print the desired pattern with an inkjet printer (except for dark transfer paper). Do not transfer until the transfer-paper ink is dry.*
5. **Parameter Setting**: Turn on the power switch; the temperature controller begins self-check and enters working mode after about 10 seconds.
   * Press the **SET** button to display **SP**, then press ▼▲ to adjust the target temperature.
   * Press **SET** again to display **SL**, then press ▼▲ to adjust the required time.
   * Finally, press and hold **SET** for about 3 seconds to complete the setting; the machine begins to heat up.
6. **Preheat**: After setting the temperature and time, wait for the machine to heat up (heating to 200℃ takes about 15 minutes; heat-up time varies by power).
7. **Transfer**: When the machine reaches the required temperature, place the item to be transferred in the center of the worktable (do not touch the heating plate to avoid burns).
8. **Pressing**: Press the handle down; the machine begins the countdown. When the countdown ends, the buzzer sounds; lift the handle, the buzzer stops, remove the product, peel off the sublimation paper and high-temperature tape—printing is complete.
