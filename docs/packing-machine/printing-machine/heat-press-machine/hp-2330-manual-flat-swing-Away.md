## II. Core Advantages

* **Ultimate Safety Design**: The swing-away structure allows operators to fully move the scorching heating platen aside when placing garments and patterns. **This avoids the major hazard of burning the back of the hand that traditional clamshell presses easily cause.**
* **Extremely Even Heat & Pressure**: The high-pressure swing-away structure applies vertical downward pressure from top to bottom. Combined with the mechanical force distribution of the plum-blossom pressure plate, it ensures that when transferring large patterns, the edges and center show equally vivid colors without missing tones.
* **Dual Electrical Safety Protection**: An **external fuse switch** is added on the side of the machine body. It automatically trips during unstable voltage or overload, protecting the life of the core heating plate and preventing burnout and electric leakage.

## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Media**: T-shirts/flat items to be heat-transferred, sublimation paper.
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V power source.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Preheat**: Connect the power, set the target temperature and time on the dual-display panel, and wait for the heating platen to warm up.
2. **Load Material**: Move the heating platen aside horizontally (swing away), lay the T-shirt flat on the silicone pad of the base plate, and place the transfer paper with the pattern facing down.
3. **Apply Pressure**: Rotate the heating platen back into position, press down firmly on the black handle with both hands, and the machine starts the automatic countdown.
4. **Finish**: When the buzzer sounds, pull up the handle, swing the heating platen horizontally aside (burn protection), peel off the transfer paper, and complete the heat transfer.
