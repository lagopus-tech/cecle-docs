## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Media**: The webbing / lanyard / ribbon and other long-strip substrates to be printed.
* **Consumables**: Sublimation ink, sublimation paper, printed patterns.
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V power source.
* **Tools**: High-temperature-resistant tape (to fix the pattern and prevent shifting) and gloves (to prevent burns when removing items).

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Preheat**: Connect the power and turn on; set the target temperature and countdown time on the controller; wait 10–15 minutes for it to heat up to working temperature.
2. **Adjust Pressure**: Rotate the top pressure knob to a suitable gear (a small trial press is recommended before formal production).
3. **Print Pattern**: Design the pattern on a computer and print it with an inkjet printer + sublimation paper + sublimation ink. **Use only after the ink is completely dry.**
4. **Load Material**: Lay the lanyard flat on the heating plate, align the pattern paper face-down precisely, and fix both ends with high-temperature-resistant tape to prevent shifting.
5. **Apply Pressure**: Press the handle down firmly with both hands; the machine starts the automatic countdown.
6. **Finish**: When the buzzer sounds, lift the handle, take out the lanyard, and peel off the transfer paper—the pattern is firmly attached.
7. **Power Off & Reset**: After work, turn off the power and wait for the heating plate to cool before storing or moving the machine.

### FAQ
* **Q: How do I switch between Celsius and Fahrenheit?**
  * **A**: Long-press the **`SET`** key for a few seconds until **`LOY`** appears on the display; press the ▼ key until **`88`** appears; long-press **`SET`** again for a few seconds until **`CF`** shows; press ▲ or ▼ to switch **0 (Celsius) / 1 (Fahrenheit)**; long-press **`SET`** for 3 seconds to confirm and exit.
* **Q: Can this machine be used with 110V voltage in the United States?**
