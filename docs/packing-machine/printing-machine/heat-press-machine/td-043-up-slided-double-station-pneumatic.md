## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Media**: Textiles to be printed, sublimation/transfer paper, patterns printed with sublimation ink.
* **Air Supply**: Air compressor and air hose; recommended working pressure 3–6 kg/cm².
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V power source.

### 2. Safety Notes
* Before powering on, check that all parts are secure and confirm the air and power connections are normal.
* The machine must be connected to a safe, reliable ground wire.
* Do not touch the heating plate during heating or operation to avoid burns.
* Do not place hands on the worktable while the machine is running, to prevent crushing or burns.
* Before mass production, always test the optimal time, temperature, and pressure with a sample first.
* The temperature controller is complex; except for temperature and time settings, do not adjust other parameters to avoid program errors or equipment damage.

### 3. Standard Workflow
1. **Connect air and power**: Connect the air compressor, switch on the 220V power, open the air valve, and adjust the air pressure to the required range (about 3–6 kg/cm²).
2. **Parameter Setting**: Turn on the power switch.
   - Set temperature: press ▲ to increase, ▼ to decrease.
   - Set time: press ▲ to increase, ▼ to decrease.
   - Once set, the machine begins to heat up.
3. **Preheat**: Wait for the machine to heat up to the target temperature (about 15–30 minutes).
4. **Place the workpiece and switch stations**: The machine alarms when the set temperature is reached.
   - Hold the black moving handle below the control panel and slide the upper heating-plate assembly along the top rail directly above the target station.
   - Lay the workpiece to be printed (textile + transfer paper) on the other fixed station.
5. **Pressing**:
   - **Auto mode**: The heating plate presses down automatically once in position; it lifts automatically after the countdown ends, completing one transfer.
   - **Manual mode**: Press the manual button on the control panel; the heating plate presses down and lifts automatically after the countdown ends.
6. **Remove the finished product**: Take out the workpiece, peel off the transfer paper—printing is complete.
