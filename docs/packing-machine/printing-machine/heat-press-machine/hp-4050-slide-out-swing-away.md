## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Media**: T-shirts/hoodies/canvas bags/flat items to be heat-transferred; sublimation paper or hotfix rhinestone stickers.
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V power source.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Preheat**: Connect the power, set the target temperature and countdown time on the intelligent digital control panel, and wait for the heating platen to reach the target temperature.
2. **Slide-Out & Load**: **Slide** the base plate outward along the rails to outside the machine, lay the T-shirt/hoodie/substrate flat, and align the transfer paper with the pattern facing down.
3. **Return to Machine**: Push the loaded base plate back inside the machine along the rails to the locating hole.
4. **Swing Back + Pressurize**: Rotate the heating platen 360° back into position above the base plate; press down the anti-slip foam handle, and the machine automatically starts the countdown.
5. **Finish & Retrieve**: When the buzzer sounds, pull up the handle; rotate the heating platen horizontally 360° aside (heat avoidance); pull the base plate out again, retrieve the finished product, peel off the transfer paper, and complete the heat transfer.
