## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Media**: Small-area items to be printed such as T-shirts, toys, shoe tongues; sublimation paper or heat transfer film.
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V power source.
* **Accessories**: Heat-insulating pad, spray bottle, storage pouch.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Preheat**: Place the machine flat on the anti-scald base, plug in and turn on, set the temperature and time via the buttons, and wait until the LED screen shows the working temperature is reached.
2. **Load Material**: Lay the substrate (e.g., a T-shirt section, a pillow corner) flat on the heat-insulating pad, with the heat transfer paper pattern facing down and aligned.
3. **Apply Pressure**: Hold the machine and press the aluminum alloy heating plate steadily above the pattern; the dual high-frequency motors automatically provide continuous, even pressure, held for the set time.
4. **Finish**: When the timer ends, move the machine away, peel off the transfer paper, and after cooling the pattern is firmly attached.
5. **Store**: After work and once the machine has cooled, put on the transparent protective cover and pack it into the storage pouch to carry with you or put it away.
