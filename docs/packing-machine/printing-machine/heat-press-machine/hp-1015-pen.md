> **Core Abstract**

Its core advantages include simultaneous 6-pen printing on a 10×15 cm platen that multiplies throughput versus single-pen presses, a groove silicone pad that locks pens firmly to prevent rolling and ghosting, and an automatic countdown sensor that starts timing the moment the handle is pressed down—no manual Start button needed. * **Countdown Sensor**: Press the handle down and the machine automatically starts the countdown; lift the handle and the buzzer alarm stops automatically—no repeated manual button pressing.
* **Grooved Silicone Pad**: The base is made of premium molded silicone with grooves, making pen placement extremely easy.

## II. Core Advantages

* **High ROI**: Advertising pens are among the highest-demand B2B custom items. With 350W low energy consumption, 6 units per run, and extremely simple operation, the machine cost is recovered within just a few orders.
* **Anti-Rolling Design**: Ordinary flat presses cannot print pens (they roll). This machine's grooved silicone pad locks pens firmly in place; combined with the precise downward pressure of the upper heating plate, patterns and text never double-image or blur.
* **Dual-Display Digital Controller**: Dual-screen display makes temperature and time settings clearer. After transfer completes, a "beep" alert sounds so workers need not stand by the machine and can perform other layout work simultaneously.
