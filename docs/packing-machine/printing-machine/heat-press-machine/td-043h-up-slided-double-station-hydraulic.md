## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Media**: Textiles to be printed, sublimation/transfer paper, patterns printed with sublimation ink.
* **Hydraulic Oil**: The machine must be filled with hydraulic oil before use; for first use, fill to the standard oil level per the instructions.
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V power source; the machine must be reliably grounded.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Power on and check oil**: Connect the 220V power, confirm the hydraulic oil is filled to the standard level, and turn on the power switch.
2. **Parameter Setting**:
   * Set temperature: press ▲ to increase, ▼ to decrease.
   * Set time: press ▲ to increase, ▼ to decrease.
   * Once set, the machine begins to heat up.
3. **Preheat**: Wait for the machine to heat up to the target temperature.
4. **Place the workpiece and switch stations**: The machine alarms when the set temperature is reached.
   * Hold the moving handle below the control panel and slide the upper heating-plate assembly along the top rail directly above the target station.
   * Lay the workpiece to be printed (textile + transfer paper) on the other fixed station.
5. **Pressing**: Once the heating plate is in position, the hydraulic system presses down automatically; it lifts automatically after the countdown ends, completing one transfer.
6. **Remove the finished product**: Take out the workpiece, peel off the transfer paper—printing is complete.

### Sales Tips
* **Promote Dual-Station Efficiency**: Emphasize to customers the two fixed stations and the moving handle that slides the upper heating plate between sides—one side presses while the other is prepared simultaneously, greatly increasing daily output.

### FAQ
* **Q: Does this machine require an air compressor?**
  * **A**: No. The TD-043H is a hydraulic model with a built-in hydraulic system; it only needs hydraulic oil and a 220V power connection to work, eliminating the purchase, air-hose routing, and daily maintenance of an air compressor.
* **Q: How does the dual-station design improve efficiency?**
  * **A**: The machine has two fixed stations; the upper heating plate is slid between left and right stations via the moving handle. While one side is pressing, the operator can simultaneously load material or take finished products on the other side, reducing idle waiting.
* **Q: What is the difference from the pneumatic TD-043?**
  * **A**: The core difference is the drive method—the TD-043H is hydraulically driven, with greater, more stable pressure and no air compressor needed, while the TD-043 is pneumatic and requires an air compressor. Both share the same 40×60 cm up-slide dual-station structure.
