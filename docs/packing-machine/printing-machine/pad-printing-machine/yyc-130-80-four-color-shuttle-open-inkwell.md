## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Substrate**: Concave/convex workpieces such as stationery, toys, gifts, appliances, electronics, and more.
* **Consumables**: Steel plate, fixture, rubber head, ink, thinner, doctor blade.
* **Air Pressure**: 5–7 bar. An air compressor is required.
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V, factory-configured per the customer's destination voltage.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Air & Power Connection**: Connect 5–7 bar compressed air and power; check for leaks, then power on.
2. **Steel Plate & Rubber Head Installation**: Load the four etched steel plates into their corresponding inkwell bases and mount the four matching pad-printing rubber heads.
3. **Ink Filling**: Pour the properly prepared ink of each color into the four open inkwells and install the doctoring mechanism.
4. **Workpiece Clamping**: Fix the workpiece onto the shuttle-table fixture, calibrate, and complete the first-color base alignment.
5. **Trial Printing & Adjustment**: Run a test print, observe registration accuracy, and fine-tune the worktable and print-head positions.
6. **Mass Production**: After adjustment, begin formal printing; the shuttle worktable automatically transfers the workpiece beneath each head to complete four-color printing in sequence.
7. **Shutdown & Maintenance**: After work, shut off air and power, clean residual ink from each inkwell, wipe the rubber heads, steel plates, and blade with cleaning agent, and keep the machine dry.
