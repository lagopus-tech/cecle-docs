- **Built-in Digital Counter**: Integrated digital-display counter precisely tracks printing cycles in real time, facilitating order-based shipment and piece-rate accounting.

## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials

- **Substrate**: Equal-diameter cylindrical small parts to be printed (diameter ≤20 mm, e.g., straight-wall knobs, small bottle caps).
- **Consumables**: Steel plate, fixture, rubber head, ink, thinner, doctor blade
- **Air Pressure**: 4–6 bar. An air compressor is required.
- **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V, factory-configured per the customer's destination voltage.

### 2. Standard Workflow

1. **Air Supply Connection**: Connect 4–6 bar compressed air, check for leaks, then power on.
2. **Steel Plate & Rubber Head Installation**: Load the etched steel plate into the open inkwell base, mount the dedicated long-bar rubber head, and pour a proper amount of mixed ink into the open inkwell.
3. **Fixture Assembly & Position Calibration**: Install the workpiece locating fixture on the shuttle table, place the product to be printed (the fixture is for fixing product position); adjust the table height so the rubber head presses the product surface with appropriate force when descending.
4. **Trial Printing & Adjustment (Linear Rolling Calibration)**: Press the foot pedal to start a single trial print; observe the motion — the shuttle table translates, the rubber head presses on the product surface, and the product is pressed by the head and rolls to transfer during translation; after printing, the head lifts and the shuttle table stops; observe the clarity of the side print, fine-tune the table height (rubber head press force) to ensure the print is full without slipping and the start/end close flatly.
5. **Mass Production**: After calibration, enter formal batch operation; the operator quickly loads/unloads workpieces, and the digital counter automatically accumulates the output quantity.
6. **Shutdown & Maintenance**: After work, shut off the air and power, clean residual ink from the open inkwell, wipe the rubber head and steel plate with cleaning agent, and keep the machine dry.
