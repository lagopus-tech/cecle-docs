## II. Core Advantages

* **Floor-Standing Cast-Aluminum Body, Stable Printing**: The entire machine adopts a floor-standing vertical body structure with a low center of gravity and stable support — no shaking or deformation during prolonged high-speed printing, with low operating noise.
* **Double-Head Productivity**: Compared with single-head models, the double-head layout transfers two workpieces (or two print positions) per cycle, especially suited to small-part batch printing; it significantly shortens the per-piece production cycle and multiplies throughput.
* **Open Inkwell Ink Supply**: Uses an independent open inkwell plus doctor blade for ink supply — an extremely simple structure that is easy to disassemble and clean, with quick color changes. It adapts to a wide range of ink viscosities, making it the most economical solution for small-batch, multi-variety sampling and changeover, with clearly lower maintenance cost than sealed cup systems.

## III. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Substrate**: Fabric, metal, automotive parts, stationery, daily goods, electronic components, toys, instruments, bottle caps, pen barrels, and other small-to-medium flat / slightly curved / irregular workpieces.
* **Consumables**: Steel plate, fixture, rubber head, ink, thinner, doctor blade.
* **Air Pressure**: 4–6 bar. An air compressor is required.
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V, factory-configured per the customer's destination voltage; the demo unit is 220V 50Hz.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Air Supply Connection**: Connect 4–6 bar compressed air, check the air line for leaks, then power on.
2. **Steel Plate & Rubber Head Installation**: Load the two etched steel plates into their respective open inkwell bases, mount the corresponding pad printing rubber heads, and pour a proper amount of mixed ink into the open inkwells.
3. **Trial Printing & Adjustment**: Place a sample substrate, press the control-panel start button to test print, and fine-tune the inkwell doctoring stroke, the two head positions, and the worktable forward/backward & up/down positions according to pattern sharpness.
4. **Mass Production**: After adjustment, proceed to formal printing; the counter automatically accumulates the print count for easy production management.
5. **Shutdown & Maintenance**: After work, shut off the air and power, clean residual ink from the open inkwells, wipe the rubber heads and steel plates with cleaning agent, and keep the equipment dry.

## IV. Sales Tips & FAQ

### Sales Tips
* **Two-Model Selection Guidance**: Recommend by print area — choose **YYD2-125-100** (steel plate 100×100 mm, up to 2300 cycles/h, faster and more flexible plate change) for small parts with high cycle rates and large batches; choose **YYD2-200-150** (steel plate 150×100 mm, 1500 cycles/h, heavier body, more stable downforce) for slightly larger print areas.
* **Highlight "Double-Head Doubling Throughput"**: For high-volume customers processing bottle caps, pen barrels, and electronic small parts, emphasize **"one press, two parts produced simultaneously"** — the per-piece cycle is halved, greatly improving per-capita output and machine turnover.
* **Bundle Sales Suggestion**: Bundle with consumables such as steel plates, fixtures, rubber heads, and doctor blades to raise average order value and customer stickiness.

### FAQ
* **Q: Can this machine use 110V voltage in the United States?**
 * **A**: Yes. We provide both 110V (North American standard) and 220V (European/Asian standard) versions, factory-preset to the customer's destination voltage, and equipped with power plugs that comply with local standards.
* **Q: Is an air compressor required?**
 * **A**: Yes, the machine needs to be connected to an air compressor — you can prepare your own or purchase one from us. The required air pressure is **4–6 bar**.
* **Q: What is the difference between a double-head single-color machine and a single-head single-color machine?**
 * **A**: The core difference lies in throughput and station count: a single-head machine prints only one product per cycle, whereas a double-head model is equipped with two rubber heads and can transfer two products (or two positions on one workpiece) in a single press. In small-part batch printing, double-head efficiency is close to twice that of a single-head machine, greatly saving labor and man-hour costs; the ink-supply principle and cleaning/maintenance methods are essentially the same for both.
