## III. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Substrate**: The workpiece to be printed and its matching positioning fixture.
* **Consumables**: Steel plate, fixture, rubber head, ink, thinner, doctor blade.
* **Air Pressure**: 4–6 bar. An air compressor is required.
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V, factory-configured per the customer's destination voltage.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Air Supply Connection**: Connect 4–6 bar compressed air, check for leaks, then power on.
2. **Steel Plate & Rubber Head Installation**: Load the two etched steel plates into their respective open inkwell bases, and mount the two corresponding pad printing rubber heads above.
3. **Fill Two-Color Ink**: Pour the prepared first-color and second-color ink into the left and right open inkwells respectively, and install the doctoring mechanisms.
4. **Clamp Workpiece & Reference Alignment**: Fix the sample on the shuttle-table fixture, and manually align the first-color (reference color) head with the worktable position first.
5. **Trial Printing & Registration Calibration**: Step on the foot pedal to start a single two-color trial print; observe the registration precision of the second color against the first, and precisely align via the second head's micro-adjustment knob until the two colors are perfectly aligned.
6. **Mass Production**: After locking the setup, switch to automatic/semi-automatic mode for continuous production; the shuttle table automatically reciprocates to transport the workpiece and complete two-color transfer, while the digital counter automatically accumulates the finished count.
7. **Shutdown, Maintenance & Cleaning**: After production, cut power and air, pour out residual ink from the open inkwells, and thoroughly clean both steel plates, rubber heads, inkwells, and doctor blades with cleaning agent.

### FAQ
* **Q: Can this machine use 110V voltage in the United States?**
 * **A**: Yes. We provide both 110V (North American standard) and 220V (European/Asian standard) versions, factory-preset to the customer's destination voltage, and equipped with power plugs that comply with local standards.
* **Q: Is an air compressor required?**
 * **A**: Yes, the machine needs to be connected to an air compressor — you can prepare your own or purchase one from us. The required air pressure is **4–6 bar**.
* **Q: What is the difference between the YYC shuttle 2-color machine and the YYD2 double-head single-color machine? How should I choose?**
 * **A**: Their production purposes are completely different: the YYD2 Series (double-head single-color) presses once to print 2 products (single color) simultaneously, ideal for small-part high-volume fast runs where per-piece throughput is doubled; the YYC Series (shuttle 2-color) clamps one product per cycle, but the workpiece automatically shuttles between two print heads to complete two-color overprinting in sequence, designed specifically for high-precision two-color patterns.
