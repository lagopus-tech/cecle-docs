The YYD-125-100Y is a dedicated single-head, single-color floor-standing open inkwell pad printing machine engineered specifically for eyeglass-frame printing. Ideal for sunglasses, optical, reading, and protective eyewear manufacturers as well as surface-treatment and foreign-trade workshops, it prints both outer temple brand logos and inner-side model numbers, CE/FDA markings, and material codes. Its core advantages include an extended clearance head with a lengthened downward structure that safely reaches inside the frame to print the inner side without striking the front rim — solving the "inner-side collision" problem standard machines cannot; an open inkwell system enabling minutes-fast color changes between white, gold, silver, and black inks at lower cost than sealed cups; and a 98 KG one-piece floor-standing body with foot-pedal control delivering stable, high-yield runs up to 2300 pcs/hr.

Developed specifically for inner-temple printing, the rubber head easily reaches deep inside the frame to print downward without ever scraping the front rim or folding parts.
* **Fine Micro-Adjustment Alignment System**: The worktable supports up/down and front/back micro-adjustment, and the print head supports front/back and left/right micro-adjustment, ensuring miniature English lettering stays perfectly level and aligned.
* **Digital Intelligent Control**: Equipped with a microcomputer integrated control panel and digital counter with clear stroke logic and real-time output statistics.

## II. Core Advantages

* **Inner & Outer Dual Printing — Completely Solves the "Inner-Side Collision" Problem**:
 * **Standard machine**: Only suitable for the outer temple (flat, no interference), but when printing the inner side, its wide flat head inevitably strikes and damages the front frame on descent.
 * **YYD-125-100Y dedicated machine**: The head adopts a lengthened downward structure — it prints the outer logo and, more importantly, safely reaches into the frame cavity to print inner parameters, completing the entire eyeglass printing process with a single machine.
* **Open Inkwell Ink Supply, Zero Burden for Frequent Color Changes (Economical Open Inkwell)**: Eyewear marking often requires frequent switching between white, gold, silver, black, and other inks; the open inkwell disassembles and changes color in just minutes, with consumable and maintenance costs far lower than sealed cup systems.
* **Floor-Standing Body & Foot-Pedal Efficiency (Floor-Standing & Foot Pedal)**: One-piece body with 98 KG self-weight stays stable at high speed; the standard foot switch lets workers hold and align the frame with both hands, greatly reducing defect rates.

## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Substrate**: Various eyeglass frames, temple arms, etc.
* **Consumables**: Steel plate, fixture, rubber head, ink, thinner, doctor blade.
* **Air Pressure**: 4–6 bar. An air compressor is required.
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V, factory-configured per the customer's destination voltage.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Connect Air & Power**: Connect 4–6 bar compressed air and check for leaks, then power on and open the control panel.
2. **Plate Loading & Ink Mixing**: Install the etched steel plate, mount the dedicated pad printing rubber head, and pour a proper amount of mixed ink into the open inkwell.
3. **Fixture Setup & Adjustment**: Fix the corresponding eyewear fixture on the worktable according to whether you are printing the **inner** or **outer** side.
4. **Foot-Pedal Single Trial Print**: Place the sample glasses into the fixture, press the foot switch to complete one trial print, and fine-tune until the print edge is sharp and the lettering clear.
5. **Mass Production**: After confirming the print is clear and complete, move into batch production; clean the open inkwell, steel plate, and rubber head promptly when production ends.

### FAQ
* **Q: Why must YYD-125-100Y be selected for printing the inner side of eyeglass temples instead of a standard pad printer?**
 * **A**: When printing the inner side, the glasses are in an open 3D state with the temple surrounded by the front frame. A standard machine's square head is too wide and will strike and damage the frame on descent; the **YYD-125-100Y print head adopts a lengthened downward structure** that effectively avoids the metal interference on both sides, directly reaching inside the frame to print safely.
* **Q: Can one YYD-125-100Y print both the outer and inner sides?**
 * **A**: Absolutely. The YYD-125-100Y is fully downward-compatible — it not only safely prints the high-difficulty inner parameters, but also prints outer logos and ordinary flat hardware and plastic workpieces with equal ease and efficiency.
