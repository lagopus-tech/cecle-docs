## IV. Operation & Workflow

### 1. Required Materials
* **Substrate**: Small workpieces such as automotive parts, stationery, daily goods, electronic components, toys, instruments, and garment labels.
* **Consumables**: Steel plate, fixture, rubber head, ink, thinner, doctor blade.
* **Air Pressure**: 5–6 bar. An air compressor is required.
* **Power Supply**: 110V or 220V, factory-configured per the customer's destination voltage.

### 2. Standard Workflow
1. **Air Supply Connection**: Connect 5–6 bar compressed air, check for leaks, then power on.
2. **Steel Plate & Rubber Head Installation**: Load the etched steel plate into the open inkwell base, mount the pad printing rubber head, and pour a proper amount of mixed ink into the open inkwell.
3. **Trial Printing & Adjustment**: Place a sample substrate, press the foot pedal or the control-panel start button to test print, and fine-tune the print-head position according to pattern sharpness.
4. **Mass Production**: After adjustment, start formal printing; the digital counter automatically accumulates the print count for easy production management.
5. **Shutdown & Maintenance**: After work, shut off the air and power, clean residual ink from the open inkwell, wipe the rubber head and steel plate with cleaning agent, and keep the machine dry.
