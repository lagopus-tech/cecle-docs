> ### core Abstract
> The **HT-C-200 Cone Heat Transfer Machine** is a semi-automatic, high-precision surface decoration printer specially engineered for tapered and truncated cone-shaped containers, such as milk tea cups, chemical buckets, cosmetic caps, and pen barrels. Featuring a 200mm transfer width, high-precision foil feeding system, and full pneumatic drive (0.6–0.8 MPa), it eliminates wrinkling and missed printing on irregular surfaces. With durable chromate-treated components and an intuitive digital control interface, it delivers vibrant, high-adhesion color transfers for customized packaging production.

### I. Product Overview

*   **Product Category**: Packaging Machinery / Surface Treatment Equipment / Heat Transfer Equipment
*   **Application Scenario**: Specially designed for surface graphic decoration of **conical and truncated cone-shaped** containers. Widely used in industries such as plastic cups (e.g., milk tea cups, cold drink cups), chemical buckets, cosmetic bottle caps, and office stationery (pen barrels).
*   **Core Function**: Utilizes the heat generated by the heating roller combined with cylinder pressure to transfer patterns from the heat transfer film onto conical object surfaces with high precision, achieving full-wrap or localized high-definition color printing.

### VI. Workflow

1.  **Startup & Preheating**: Connect the power supply and air pump, then set the heating temperature and transfer time.
2.  **Film Loading & Positioning**: Install the heat transfer film onto the feeding bracket and adjust the photoelectric sensor to align with the pattern.
3.  **Workpiece Placement**: Manually place the conical product to be printed onto the mold of the rotating station.
4.  **Initiate Transfer**: Step on the foot switch; the machine head will automatically press down, and the rubber roller will drive the product to rotate and synchronously transfer the pattern.
5.  **Automatic Rewinding**: Once the transfer is complete, the machine head lifts, and the machine automatically winds the waste film to prepare for the next cycle.
6.  **Unloading Finished Product**: Manually remove the completed item.
