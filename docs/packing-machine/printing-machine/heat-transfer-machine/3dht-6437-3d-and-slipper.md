## VI. Workflow
1. **Debugging & Preparation**: Connect the 220V power supply and air source, and set the required transfer temperature (usually set between 180-210°C depending on the product material).
2. **Film Loading & Positioning**: Install the 3D heat transfer film onto the film roller, and use the photoelectric sensor to lock the pattern position.
3. **Manual Loading**: Place the product to be transferred (such as a shoe sole) onto the base tooling/fixture.
4. **Start Operation**: Trigger the switch, and the machine will automatically complete the cycle: "pressing down - heat transfer - returning to position."
5. **Waste Film Rewinding**: After the transfer is complete, the machine automatically rewinds the waste film via the electric drive and pre-positions the next pattern.
6. **Finished Product Inspection**: Manually remove the finished product and inspect the transfer completeness on the edges and curved sections.
