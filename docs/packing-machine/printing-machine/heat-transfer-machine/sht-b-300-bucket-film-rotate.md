## II. Key Advantages
1. **Precision Mold Customization (Core Advantage):** The tooling fixtures for each machine are custom-developed at a 1:1 ratio based on the **actual bucket samples** provided by the customer. This ensures stable support and uniform force distribution on the bucket body during transfer, completely eliminating pattern ghosting or wrinkling issues.
2. **Rocking Contour-Following Transfer:** Designed specifically for tapered bucket bodies, the rocking mechanism automatically compensates for angular deviations, achieving seamless adhesion between the pattern and the bucket wall.
3. **Industrial-Grade Heavy-Duty Framework:** With a machine weight of 600kg, it exhibits excellent anti-vibration performance during high-speed operation, delivering industrial reliability that ordinary lightweight equipment cannot match.
4. **Precision Film Feeding & Recoiling:** Equipped with a high-precision foil-feeding system and photoelectric switch positioning, it ensures accurate film stepping, significantly reducing consumable costs.
5. **Durable Components:** All key components of the machine undergo chromate treatment, offering corrosion resistance and high hardness to meet the long-term operational demands of chemical workshops.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
* **Q: Why do we need to customize the mold based on samples?**
    * A: Because even minor differences in the height, diameter, and taper of the bucket body can affect the transfer results. Customized molds ensure perfect pressure distribution and prevent the design from shifting during the transfer process.
* **Q: How long does it take to customize the mold?**
    * A: After receiving the samples, an additional testing and debugging period is usually required to ensure perfect synchronization between the fixture and the rocking mechanism.
* **Q: Can this machine print flat products?**
    * A: This model is developed specifically for the curved surfaces of buckets. If you need a machine that can handle flat surfaces as well, we recommend inquiring about our flat/round dual-use models, or retrofitting this machine with specific custom fixtures.

## VI. Workflow / Operation Steps
1. **Sample Confirmation:** Receive the customer's bucket sample, customize the exclusive positioning mold, and install it.
2. **Parameter Presetting:** Set the transfer temperature, rocking stroke, and downward pressure.
3. **Film Loading & Calibration:** Install the film roll and align the pattern using the photoelectric sensor.
4. **Manual Placement:** Place the bucket onto the customized mold.
5. **Automatic Transfer:** Activate the switch; the machine head presses down and rocks along with the bucket body to complete the pattern transfer.
6. **Waste Film Recoiling:** The system automatically winds up the waste film, preparing for the next transfer cycle.
