### IV. Sales Suggestions & FAQ
* **Sales Suggestions:**
    * **Bundling Reminder:** Always inform customers that this machine is pneumatically driven and **requires an external air compressor**. It is highly recommended to recommend a model of 3HP (2.2KW) or above to ensure stable pressure output.
    * **Targeted Marketing:** Highlight the excellent adaptability of the 45° silicone roller to skateboard curves, as this is the ultimate selling point for solving the industry pain point of poor edge transfer.
* **FAQ:**
    * **Q: Is the 230kg weight stable enough under high-pressure operations?**
        * A: Absolutely. The 230kg weight is scientifically optimized. With a low and evenly distributed center of gravity, the machine remains rock-steady without any vibration or shifting, even under 0.6T of pressure.
    * **Q: Why does the silicone roller need to keep spinning after turning off the heat?**
        * A: If the silicone roller stops spinning while still at a high temperature, gravity and localized residual heat will cause permanent flat spots or deformation. Keeping it rotating during the cooling phase greatly extends the lifespan of the 45° silicone roller.

### VI. Workflow
1. **Air Supply Connection:** Connect the air compressor hose and adjust the pressure to the standard working range of 0.6–0.8Mpa.
2. **Power Up:** Turn on the main Power Switch, and the dashboard display will light up.
3. **Speed Control:** Turn on and adjust the Speed Controller to ensure the silicone roller starts rotating for uniform heat distribution.
4. **Temperature Preset:** Set the required transfer temperature on the thermostat and wait for the heating roller to reach the target value.
5. **Execute Transfer:** Manually place the skateboard deck and the transfer film. Once aligned, press the dual Operate Buttons simultaneously.
6. **Safety Monitoring:** In case of any abnormality during operation, immediately hit the red Emergency Stop button.
7. **Cooling & Maintenance:** After production is finished and the heating power is turned off, **the roller must continue to rotate for approximately 30 minutes** to dissipate residual heat safely and prevent roller deformation.
