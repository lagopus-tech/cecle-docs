Featuring dual film feeding modes (automatic feeding for high-speed output at 15 prints/min and manual pulling for fine calibration), a high-precision foil controller, and a durable chromate-treated frame, it offers footwear manufacturers an efficient, low-cost solution for standardized surface decoration.

## I. Product Overview
* **Product Category**: Heat Transfer Machinery / Specialized Equipment for Slipper Processing
* **Application Scenarios**: Specially designed for multicolor pattern heat transfer on slipper surfaces, PVC/EVA fabrics, craft gifts, and other similar products.
* **Core Function**: Utilizes precise temperature and pneumatic pressure control to high-definition transfer patterns from the heat transfer film onto the slipper surface, achieving mass-produced and standardized surface decoration.

## II. Core Advantages
1. **Dual Film Feeding Modes**: **Supports both automatic film feeding and manual film pulling**. The automatic mode is ideal for highly efficient mass production, significantly reducing labor intensity; the manual mode allows for ultra-precise secondary alignment for specialized patterns.
2. **High-Precision Feeding System**: Equipped with a high precision foil feeding control system to ensure consistent feeding length each time and accurate pattern positioning, significantly reducing film wastage.
3. **Premium Component Craftsmanship**: All core metal parts undergo chromate treatment for corrosion and wear resistance, substantially extending the machine's lifespan under high-intensity production environments.
4. **Powerful Hybrid Drive**: Combines electric control with a pneumatic drive, delivering uniform and stable stamping pressure to ensure vibrant colors and sharp edges for the transferred patterns.
5. **User-Friendly Design**: Features a semi-automatic logic design where manual loading and unloading are paired with automated stamping and film feeding. Regular operators can master the machine with just 5 minutes of training.

## VI. Workflow
1. **Startup Preparation**: Connect the power and air supplies, and set the temperature of the heating plate (depending on the film material, typically between 150-180°C).
2. **Mode Selection**: Select the **Automatic Feeding** mode on the control panel and set the desired feeding length.
3. **Manual Loading**: The operator precisely places the slipper surface onto the positioning mold of the workbench.
4. **Automatic Film Feeding & Alignment**:
   * *Under Automatic Mode*: Upon completion of the previous press, the machine automatically drives the motor to advance the film to the next pattern position.
5. **Pressing & Transfer**: Step on the foot switch; the pneumatic system drives the stamping head downward for high-temperature heat pressing and automatically resets based on the dwell time.
6. **Finished Product Removal**: Remove the printed finished product, inspect the color saturation, and the machine will cycle into the next operation.
