> The **HT-S-300 Flat and Round Heat Transfer Machine** is a semi-automatic industrial printer designed for dual-surface printing on flat, cylindrical, and conical workpieces, making it ideal for stationery, cosmetic packaging, and gift manufacturing. ## VI. Workflow / Operation Steps
1. **Startup & Debugging:** Connect the power and air pump, and set the heating plate temperature (usually 150°C - 200°C, adjusted according to the film material).
2. **Installing the Film Roll:** Load the heat transfer film into the feeding system and adjust the position of the electric eye sensor.
3. **Placing the Workpiece:** Place the product to be printed onto the positioning fixture.
4. **Initiating Transfer:** Press the start button, and the machine will automatically complete the "Press down - Transfer - Film feed - Reset" cycle.
5. **Quality Inspection:** Remove the finished product to check the completeness of the pattern, and fine-tune the pressure, time, and temperature based on the results.
