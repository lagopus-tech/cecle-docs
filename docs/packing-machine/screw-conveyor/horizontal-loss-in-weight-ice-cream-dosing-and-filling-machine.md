## III. Operation Process

1. **Material Loading**: Pour the prepared or pre-chilled ice cream base into the 55L stainless steel refrigerated hopper and secure the transparent sanitary lid.
2. **Refrigeration & Agitation Pre-start**: Start the refrigeration system (powered by the Secop SC15CL unit) and engage the variable-frequency agitator to keep the product homogenous and cold.
3. **Parameter Setting**: Configure target batch weight (e.g., 500 g) or continuous dosing rate on the PLC touchscreen, setting two-stage (fast/fine) cut-off parameters.
4. **Loss-in-Weight Metering**: Press the foot pedal or initiate auto cycle; the horizontal feeding auger and vertical discharge screw operate synchronously while load sensors track hopper weight reduction in real time.
5. **Precision Cut-Off**: When the target loss-in-weight value is reached, the servo motors halt instantly, closing off product flow with no dripping to complete the cycle.
6. **Cleaning & Maintenance**: Release the quick-clamp fittings to remove the auger assemblies and hopper for routine washdown or CIP sanitization.

## V. Sales Suggestions & FAQ

### **Sales Suggestions:**
* **Targeting Industry Pain Points (Melting & Weight Drift)**: Focus on the built-in compressor refrigeration system and dynamic loss-in-weight servo control. Highlight that regular paste fillers allow product to melt and separate during pauses, whereas jacketed cooling keeps the mixture stable and maintains quality.
* **Dual Servo Precision Advantage**: Compared to volumetric piston fillers, loss-in-weight measurement is unaffected by overrun (air content) variations, delivering accurate gravimetric output and cutting ingredient giveaway.
* **Versatile Integration**: Operates reliably as a standalone unit with foot pedal dosing, or integrates directly into downstream automated cup/tub packaging lines.

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):**
* **Q: Why is an active refrigeration system essential for ice cream dosing?**
  * A: Ice cream is temperature-sensitive; room temperature quickly melts structure and causes overrun loss. The onboard Secop 565W compressor and R404A cooling maintain a cold boundary layer, preserving texture and overrun even during production breaks.
* **Q: How easy is it to disassemble and sanitize the machine?**
  * A: All contact components—including augers, tubes, and the hopper—feature sanitary quick-release clamp designs, enabling complete disassembly and sanitation within minutes without specialized tools.
* **Q: Can the machine handle inclusions like fruit pulp or nut pieces?**
  * A: The Ø76mm horizontal tube and Ø48mm vertical discharge accommodate soft purees and small particulates smoothly. Custom augers with enlarged pitch are available for larger inclusions.
