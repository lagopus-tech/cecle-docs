## II. Production Process & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Below is the core operating and parameter adjustment guide compiled from actual on-site commissioning:

**1. Preparing the Bottom Dusting System**

* First, add dry flour (dusting powder) to the flour hopper located underneath.
* Pull out the baffle pin so that the flour can fall onto the conveyor belt that will soon transport the dough sheet.
* Loosen the side screws: pushing the slide plate inward will spray a double thin layer of flour, while pulling it outward will spray a single layer, preventing the dough sheet from sticking.

**2. Filling Pre-processing & No-Load Testing**

* **Filling Requirements**: Ingredients in the filling must be finely chopped, and the mixture must have some binding cohesion (it should not be too loose). Place the filling into the injection inlet and the filling hopper.
* **Filling Feed Test**: The switch on the right side of the control panel controls filling injection. Turn on the switch to adjust the speed, and pay close attention to air venting and filling output. **Tip: The speed should not be too fast; you can hold the hose to feel its fullness.** Once the filling flows smoothly from the forming end, temporarily turn off the filling switch.

**3. Dough Sheet Conveying & Precision Thickness Adjustment**

* **Dough Sheet Preparation**: Pre-roll the dough into a sheet about 1cm thick, with a width close to that of the conveyor belt.
* **Start Dough Conveying**: Turn on the dough conveying switch on the left. Start with a slow speed, then adjust it to around **10**.
* Lay the dough sheet flat onto the rollers, and sprinkle a small amount of dry powder on the surface to prevent sticking.
* **Thickness Adjustment (Critical)**: Adjust the dough thickness using the gear knobs on both sides. Rotating upward increases thickness, while rotating downward decreases it. An ideal dough thickness of around **1.6mm** is recommended.

**4. Dough Threading & Waste Reclamation**

* Once the dough sheet emerges from the rollers, catch it by hand and guide it into the forming roller molds below. **Note: Do not let the dough sheet pile up here.**
* Pull up the excess trim dough left over after cutting, and hang it onto the white recycling roller to achieve automatic waste reclamation.

**5. Synchronous Forming & Parameter Matching**

* Turn the filling switch back on. Once the filling comes out and fills the dough sheet, slowly adjust the filling speed.
* **Parameter Reference**: For initial startup testing, it is recommended to set the left speed (dough sheet running) to around 10, and the right speed (filling) to around 8.
* **Fine-Tuning Tips**:
  * If excess dough accumulates in the middle, slightly tighten the knobs on both sides.
  * If the dough skin bursts at the forming outlet, it indicates that the dough is too firm and needs to be kneaded a bit softer.
* **Emergency Stop Protection**: The red button in the center of the control panel is the emergency stop switch. Pressing it instantly halts the entire system; rotating it clockwise releases the emergency stop and resumes operation.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Flour recipes (flour-to-water ratio) vary greatly among customers. Can the machine adapt?**
  * **A**: Absolutely. The dough pressing host has excellent compatibility with doughs of different shear resistances and rheological characteristics. By fine-tuning the roller gaps or conveying speed, it can perfectly match your exclusive recipe.
* **Q: Can we switch between meat and vegetable fillings at any time?**
  * **A**: The filling system is compatible with the vast majority of meat and vegetable fillings. However, before switching to a different flavor, the filling hopper and pumping pipes must be thoroughly cleaned. For ultimate efficiency, we recommend purchasing an extra set of spare filling components for quick replacement.
* **Q: Are there any specific limits on the weight and size of the wontons?**
  * **A**: The YT-10 automatic wonton machine is designed with dedicated machines and dedicated molds. Molds are configured according to your standards at the factory. If you need to change the weight or shape of the finished product later, you will need to customize and replace the entire set of molds and feeding mechanisms.
