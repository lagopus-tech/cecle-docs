## II. Production Process and Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

The following is a guide to core operation and parameter setting based on real machine startup and debugging:

**1. Preparation of the Bottom Powder Dusting System**

* First, add dry flour to the lower flour hopper.
* Pull out the baffle pin to allow the flour to fall onto the conveyor belt that will transport the dough sheet.
* Loosen the side screws. Pushing the insert plate inward will spray two thin layers of powder; pulling it outward will spray a single layer of powder to prevent the dough sheet from sticking.

**2. Filling Pre-treatment & Emptying Test**

* **Filling Requirements**: Granules need to be broken up, and the filling needs to be slightly sticky (not too loose). Put the filling into the filling injection hole and filling storage hopper.
* **Filling Supply Test**: The switch on the right side of the control panel controls filling injection. Turn on the switch to adjust the speed, and pay attention to the exhaust and filling output. **Tip: The speed should not be too fast; you can hold the hose with your hand to feel it.** After the filling is smoothly discharged from the forming end, turn off the filling switch.

**3. Dough Sheet Conveying & Precise Thickness Adjustment**

* **Dough Pre-treatment**: First, press the dough into a dough sheet about 1 cm thick, with a width similar to the conveyor belt.
* **Start Dough Sheet Conveying**: Turn on the dough sheet conveying switch on the left. Slow down the initial speed, and then adjust it to a value of around **10**.
* Lay the dough sheet flat on the roller, and sprinkle a little dry powder on the surface to prevent sticking.
* **Thickness Adjustment (Crucial)**: Adjust the thickness of the dough wrapper through the gear knobs on both sides. Twist upwards to thicken, and twist downwards to thin. The recommended ideal dough wrapper thickness is about **1.6mm**.

**4. Dough Threading & Waste Recovery**

* After the dough sheet comes out from rolling, hold it with your hand and guide it into the forming roller mold below. **Be careful not to let the dough sheet pile up here**.
* Pull up the excess waste dough sheet (scraps) after cutting and hang it on the white recovery roller.

**5. Synchronous Forming & Parameter Matching**

* Turn on the filling switch again. When the filling comes out and fills the dough wrapper, slowly adjust the filling speed.
* **Parameter Reference**: In the early stage of machine testing, it is recommended to set the left side (wrapper running) speed to around 10, and the right side (filling) speed to around 8.
* **Fine-tuning Tips**:
  * If there is excess accumulation of the dough sheet in the middle, you can tighten the knobs on both sides.
  * If the dough wrapper breaks (Pops) at the forming port, it means the dough is too hard and needs to be kneaded slightly softer.
* **Emergency Stop Protection**: The red button in the middle of the control panel is the emergency stop switch. After pressing it, all systems will immediately stop working; rotate it to the right to release the emergency stop and resume work.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: The dough recipe (flour + water) varies greatly among different customers. Can the machine adapt?**
  * **A**: Absolutely. The dough pressing host has excellent compatibility with the different rheological properties of dough. By fine-tuning the roller gap or dough feeding speed, it can adapt to your exclusive recipe.
* **Q: Can meat filling and vegetarian filling be switched at any time?**
  * **A**: The filling supply system is compatible with most meat and vegetarian fillings, but before switching fillings with different flavors, the filling storage hopper and pumping pipes need to be thoroughly cleaned. In the pursuit of ultimate efficiency, it is recommended to purchase spare filling supply components for quick replacement.
* **Q: Are there any special instructions regarding the weight and specifications of the dumplings?**
  * **A**: The regular forming weight of the ADM series is fixed at 10-35g. This is a standard specification range that balances efficiency and quality. If you need to exceed this range, you will need a completely new exclusively customized mold and feeding system.
