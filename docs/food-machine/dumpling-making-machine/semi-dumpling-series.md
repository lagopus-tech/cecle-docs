## 2. Production Process and Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Core operating guidelines and parameter settings compiled based on real machine commissioning and operational experience:

### 1. Filling Pre-treatment and Exhaust Test

* **Filling Requirements**: Filling ingredients/particles must be finely chopped or ground uniformly; the filling should possess a certain cohesiveness (must not be too loose). Place the prepared filling into the feeding hopper.
* **Feeding Test**: Turn on the feeding control switch, adjust the feeding speed from slow to fast, and observe pipeline exhaust and filling output. **Tip: Keep speed low in the initial stage and feel the flow velocity by touching the feeding pipe.** Once filling output at the forming end becomes smooth and even, proceed to the pressing stage.

### 2. Wrapper Placement and Forming Operation

* **Wrapper Preparation**: Select pre-made dumpling/gyoza wrappers with uniform thickness.
* **Wrapper Placement & Forming**: Place the wrapper flat in the center of the forming mold tray, then trigger the filling & pressing switch (or step on the foot pedal switch).
* **Thickness and Filling Amount Adjustment (Key)**: Precisely control the filling weight per injection via the filling adjustment knob to perfectly match the wrapper size, ensuring wrapped dumplings are full and plump without breaking the skin.

### 3. Synchronous Forming and Parameter Matching

* Enable continuous or single-operation mode, and gradually fine-tune the forming rhythm and filling speed as filling is precisely injected into the wrapper.
* **Fine-Tuning Tips**:
  * If the dumpling seal is not tight, adjust the mold pressing stroke or clean debris from the mold edges.
  * If dumpling wrappers burst, it indicates excessive filling or dry/hard wrappers; reduce filling quantity appropriately or adjust wrapper formula.
* **Emergency Stop Protection**: A prominent red emergency stop button is located at the center of the control panel. In case of emergency, pressing the button immediately stops all systems; turn clockwise to reset and unlock.
