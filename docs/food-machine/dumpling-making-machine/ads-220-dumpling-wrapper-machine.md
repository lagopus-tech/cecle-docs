## II. Production Process & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Below are the standard operating procedures for initial setup, cutting operations, and daily maintenance of the ADS-220:

**1. Pre-Operation Preparation & Inspection**

* Inspect the leveling feet to ensure the 330 kg machine sits flat and stable, with the grounded power source securely connected.
* Fill the upper dusting hopper with dry cornstarch or tapioca starch and adjust the dusting brush dispensing rate.
* Check the mold rollers and output conveyor belt to ensure they are clean and free of hardened dough debris.

**2. Dough Sheet Feeding & Thickness Calibration**

* Turn on the main power and conveyor switches; the pressing rollers and rotary mold will begin rotating smoothly.
* Place the prepared dough band onto the intake tray and feed the leading edge into the primary pressing rollers.
* Adjust the side thickness adjustment handwheel and check the output wrapper thickness until it matches your process standard.

**3. Rotary Roll-Cutting & Scrap Collection**

* As the dough sheet passes through the rotary mold roller, formed dumpling wrappers automatically separate and drop onto the discharge conveyor.
* Guide the leading end of the scrap dough lattice upward onto the overhead automatic take-up spool; the machine will continuously wind up the scrap during operation.

**4. Shutdown Cleaning & Routine Maintenance**

* After completing a production run, press the stop/emergency stop button and disconnect the main power supply.
* Remove the collected dough scrap roll from the take-up spool (ready for re-kneading).
* Use a dedicated cleaning brush and a dry soft cloth to remove residual flour from rollers, mold crevices, and machine surfaces.
* **Safety Warning**: **Never spray water directly onto internal motors or electrical control cabinets!**
