## II. Production Process & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Below is a summary of core operations and parameter settings based on machine commissioning and operational experience:

### 1. Filling Pre-treatment & Degassing Test

* **Filling Requirements**: Ingredients/particles must be finely chopped or ground uniformly; filling should possess cohesion (not overly loose). Load prepared filling into the hopper.
* **Filling Feed Test**: Turn on the filling control switch, adjusting speed from slow to fast, and observe pipeline air purge and filling output. **Tip: Keep speed low initially, sensing flow rate by touching the feed tube.** Once filling outputs smoothly and uniformly at the forming end, proceed to the pressing phase.

### 2. Wrapper Placement & Forming Operation

* **Wrapper Preparation**: Select pre-made Gyoza wrappers of uniform thickness.
* **Placement & Forming**: Place wrapper flat in the center of the forming mold tray, triggering the filling and pressing switch (or stepping on the foot pedal).
* **Thickness & Filling Quantity Adjustment (Critical)**: Use the filling adjustment knob to precisely control filling weight per piece, matching wrapper size to ensure full, well-shaped Gyoza without skin breakage.

### 3. Synchronous Forming & Parameter Matching

* Enable continuous or single-cycle mode. As filling is precisely injected into wrappers, gradually fine-tune forming rhythm and filling speed.
* **Fine-Tuning Tips**:
  * If Gyoza seals are loose, adjust mold pressing stroke or clear residual debris from mold edges.
  * If wrappers burst, filling is excessive or dough is too dry/hard; reduce filling amount or adjust wrapper recipe.
* **Emergency Stop**: A prominent red emergency stop button is located in the center of the control panel. In emergencies, press it to halt all operations instantly; turn clockwise to reset.

## II. Production Process & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Core operating procedures and parameter setup based on actual machine commissioning:

### 1. Bottom Flour Dusting System Preparation

* Add dry flour to the lower dusting hopper.
* Pull out the baffle pin to let flour fall onto the conveyor belt carrying dough sheets.
* Loosen side screws. Push insertion plate inward for double-layer dusting; pull outward for single-layer dusting to prevent dough sheet sticking.

### 2. Filling Pre-treatment & Purging Test

* **Filling Requirements**: Ingredients should be finely chopped with good cohesion (not loose). Load filling into the injection port and storage hopper.
* **Feeding Test**: The switch on the right side of the control panel operates filling injection. Turn on the switch to adjust speed, checking air evacuation and output flow. **Tip: Keep speed low initially; hold the hose to feel flow consistency.** Turn off filling once output flows smoothly at the forming end.

### 3. Dough Sheet Transport & Precision Thickness Adjustment

* **Dough Preparation**: Press dough into sheets approx. 1 cm thick, matching conveyor width.
* **Start Transport**: Turn on left dough transport switch. Start at low speed, then adjust to a value around **10**.
* Lay dough sheet flat over rollers, dusting lightly with dry flour to prevent sticking.
* **Thickness Adjustment (Critical)**: Turn gear knobs on both sides to adjust wrapper thickness. Turn upward for thicker sheets, downward for thinner sheets. Recommended target thickness is approx. **1.6 mm**.

### 4. Dough Threading & Scrap Recycling

* As the dough sheet exits rollers, guide it by hand into lower forming mold rollers. **Ensure dough does not pile up here**.
* Pull residual dough scraps upward and hook onto the white recycling spool.

### 5. Synchronous Forming & Parameter Matching

* Turn on filling switch again. As filling flows and injects into dough sheets, gradually adjust filling speed.
* **Parameter Reference**: During initial setup, set left speed (dough sheet) around 10 and right speed (filling feed) around 8.
* **Fine-Tuning Tips**:
  * If dough accumulates in the middle, tighten side knobs.
  * If wrappers tear at the forming outlet, dough is too hard; knead slightly softer dough.
* **Emergency Stop**: The central red button serves as the emergency stop. Press to stop all systems immediately; twist right to release and reset.

## II. Production Process & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Operation guidelines and key parameter settings based on real-machine commissioning:

**1. Bottom Flour Dusting System Preparation**

* Fill lower dusting hopper with dry flour.
* Pull baffle pin to allow flour to fall onto the dough sheet conveyor.
* Loosen side screws. Push insertion plate inward for double-layer dusting; pull outward for single-layer dusting to prevent dough sticking.

**2. Filling Pre-treatment & Purging Test**

* **Filling Requirements**: Ingredients must be finely chopped with sufficient cohesion (not loose). Load filling into the injection port and storage hopper.
* **Feeding Test**: Turn on filling switch on the right side of the control panel to adjust speed, checking air evacuation and output flow. **Tip: Maintain low speed initially; hold the hose to check flow.** Turn off filling switch once flow becomes consistent at the forming end.

**3. Dough Sheet Transport & Precision Thickness Adjustment**

* **Dough Preparation**: Press dough into sheets approx. 1 cm thick, matching conveyor width.
* **Start Transport**: Turn on left dough transport switch. Start at low speed, then adjust to a value around **10**.
* Lay dough sheet flat over rollers, dusting lightly with dry flour to prevent sticking.
* **Thickness Adjustment (Critical)**: Turn gear knobs on both sides to adjust skin thickness. Turn upward for thicker sheets, downward for thinner sheets. Target thickness is approx. **1.6 mm**.

**4. Dough Threading & Scrap Recycling**

* As dough sheet leaves rollers, hand-guide it into lower forming mold rollers. **Prevent dough accumulation at this point**.
* Pull residual dough scrap upward and secure onto the white recycling spool.

**5. Synchronous Forming & Parameter Matching**

* Turn on filling switch again. As filling flows into dough sheets, gradually adjust filling speed.
* **Parameter Reference**: During initial setup, set left speed (dough sheet) to approx. 10 and right speed (filling feed) to approx. 8.
* **Fine-Tuning Tips**:
  * Tighten side knobs if dough bunches in the center.
  * If wrappers burst at the outlet, dough is too firm; knead slightly softer dough.
* **Emergency Stop**: Press central red button to halt operations immediately; turn clockwise to release and reset.
