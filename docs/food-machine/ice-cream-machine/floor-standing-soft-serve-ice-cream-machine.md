## III. Operation Workflow
1.  **Mix Preparation**: Thoroughly blend ice cream powder with purified water in the recommended ratio (typically 1:2.5–3) and let it rest for 10–15 minutes.
2.  **Hopper Filling**: Pour the liquid mix into the top hoppers (if equipped with the pre-cooling option, the mix remains fresh at low temperatures inside the hoppers).
3.  **Start Freezing**: Power on the machine and activate the Freeze mode. The digital control panel monitors and displays the freezing progress and product hardness in real time.
4.  **Dispensing**: Once the hardness reaches the optimal serving point (around 80%–95%), place a cone or cup under the dispensing nozzle and pull the handle down to extrude professional soft serve ice cream.
5.  **Cleaning & Maintenance**: At closing time, pour clean water into the hoppers and run the auto-clean cycle to flush out residual mix. Periodically disassemble the front valve assembly for thorough sanitation.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q: What are the main advantages of a floor-standing ice cream machine compared to a countertop model?**
A: Floor-standing machines offer significantly higher production capacity, larger hopper and freezing cylinder volumes, better heat dissipation for continuous operation, built-in lockable casters for easy mobility, and an ergonomic height that requires no extra table support.

**Q: What does the "Pre-cooling" option do, and is it worth choosing?**
A: The pre-cooling function adds independent refrigeration to the upper hoppers, keeping the liquid mix chilled at 2–8°C. This allows you to safely leave the mix inside overnight without spoilage (saving cleanup time and ingredients) and speeds up the freezing process when business picks up. 

**Q: Are floor-standing machines difficult to move and clean?**
A: Not at all. Despite their larger capacity, they feature heavy-duty universal casters with brakes so one person can easily reposition them. They also include automated wash modes for quick daily rinsing.
