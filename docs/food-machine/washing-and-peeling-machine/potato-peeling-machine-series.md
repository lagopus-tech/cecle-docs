### 2. Core Functions & Working Principle

The machine operates on a combined mechanism of centrifugal vortex tumbling friction and hydrodynamic flushing:

* **Top-Loading Bulk Feeding**: Unpeeled root vegetables (even with surface mud) are poured directly into the spacious top hopper drum, and the top water inlet valve is opened.
* **Centrifugal Rotation & Multi-Dimensional Tumbling**: The motor drives the corrugated abrasive emery baseplate at high speed, initiating a powerful centrifugal vortex and upward/downward tumbling motion. Vegetables constantly rub against the inner abrasive wall, the spinning baseplate, and each other, shearing and sloughing off the skin gently and evenly.
* **Synchronous Peeling & Washing**: Continuous water injection generates a circular vortex rinse, instantly flushing ground peel fragments, soil, and starch residues into the bottom drainage chute to prevent peel residue from re-adhering to the vegetables.
* **Rapid Centrifugal Hatch Discharge**: Once peeling is complete, **there is no need to turn off the machine**. Simply release the front door latch and swing open the hatch. The centrifugal force from the spinning baseplate instantly propels the clean, peeled vegetables down the discharge chute into a receiving crate.

## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To ensure optimum peeling cleanliness, reduce vegetable waste, and extend the lifespan of the abrasive lining, strictly follow these operational guidelines:

### 1. Raw Material Preparation & Pre-Treatment

* **Sorting & Foreign Object Removal**:
  * **Carefully inspect and remove large stones, nails, gravel, bricks, and hard metal debris mixed in with the produce!** Hard foreign materials entering the high-speed rotating drum can chip the abrasive emery lining or damage the disc.
  * For best results, process vegetables of relatively uniform size in each batch to ensure even contact and avoid over-peeling smaller produce.
* **Moistening Dry Soil**: Vegetables with heavily dried, caked-on mud (such as sweet potatoes or taro) should be lightly sprayed with water beforehand to soften the soil, speeding up the peeling cycle and protecting the abrasive lining.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Water Connection

1. Place the machine on a flat, stable surface (ensure all four feet are balanced) and connect it to a properly grounded **110V or 220V power outlet**.
2. Firmly attach a clean running water supply hose to the top water inlet nozzle and secure it with a hose clamp.
3. Direct the bottom wastewater drainage hose into a floor drain, gutter, or sedimentation tank.
4. **Lock the Front Discharge Door**: Ensure the front hatch plate is tightly shut and latch the locking handle securely to prevent the door from swinging open under centrifugal force during processing.

### 3. Standard Peeling Operation Guide

1. **Start Before Loading**: Press the green push button to start the motor, then turn on the water tap to let water spray and lubricate the inner drum.
2. **Batch Loading**: Pour the vegetables evenly into the drum (**do not overload beyond capacity**; recommended batch size is 10–15 kg for CPP-350 and 20–30 kg for CPP-450; product level must not exceed the internal fill line).
3. **Monitor Peeling (Approx. 60–120 Seconds)**: Observe the tumbling process through the top opening. When the skin is stripped clean and the drain water runs clear, the batch is ready to unload.
4. **Automatic Centrifugal Discharge**: **While the motor continues running**, place a receiving crate under the discharge chute with one hand, and release the front door latch with the other. The centrifugal force will automatically eject the peeled produce into the crate in seconds. Close and latch the door immediately once empty.
5. **Safety Warning**: **Never insert hands, spatulas, metal rods, or tools into the top drum or discharge door while the machine is running or coming to a stop!**

### 4. Cleaning & Routine Maintenance

* **Flushing Residue**: After processing, let the machine idle with the water running for 1–2 minutes to rinse out any remaining starch, peelings, or mud from beneath the disc and in the drain trough. Turn off the power once clear water flows out.
* **Post-Use Cleaning**: Press the red stop button and unplug the unit. Clean hard-to-reach corners and the discharge door using a soft brush, and wipe down the exterior stainless steel casing with a clean dry cloth.
* **Electrical Moisture Warning**: **Never spray high-pressure water directly at the motor base, ventilation louvers, or waterproof switch box!**
* **Bearing Lubrication**: Grease the main drive bearing underneath the machine with high-grade food-safe bearing grease every 6 months to maintain quiet, smooth operation.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: How do I gauge the optimal peeling time? Will running the machine too long waste potato flesh?**
  * **A:** Under normal conditions, each batch takes only **60–120 seconds**. When first using the machine, observe the peeling progress through the top opening; once the peel is removed and the drain water runs clear, immediately open the discharge door. Running the cycle excessively long (over 3–5 minutes) will continually abrade the vegetable surface and increase flesh loss. Keeping the cycle strictly within 1–2 minutes ensures maximum efficiency and minimal product loss.
* **Q: Can the machine effectively peel irregularly shaped vegetables like sweet potatoes and taro?**
  * **A:** Yes. The CPP Series features a corrugated rotary disc that imparts a three-dimensional tumbling motion, allowing irregular sweet potatoes and hairy taro to make full contact with the abrasive surfaces. It achieves an overall peeling efficiency exceeding 95%. Any deeply recessed eyes can be quickly removed with a paring knife afterward, still saving over 90% of manual labor.
* **Q: How durable is the abrasive emery lining? Will it shed grit or rust during daily use?**
  * **A:** The interior uses a high-temperature food-grade bonding resin to firmly secure industrial emery grit to the stainless steel backing. It provides high hardness and adhesion. Under normal operating conditions (free of stones and metal), the commercial service life is typically **2–3 years or more**. The stainless steel base substrate prevents rust, and replacement rotary discs can be ordered directly from CECLE when wear occurs.
