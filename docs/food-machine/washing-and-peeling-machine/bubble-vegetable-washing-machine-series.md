## I. Product Overview

### 1. Basic Equipment Information

* **Product Name**: Commercial Industrial Bubble Fruit and Vegetable Washing Assembly Line / Multifunctional Continuous Produce Washer (Commercial Bubble Water Washer Multi-Functional Fruit and Vegetable Assembly Line)
* **Product Models**: VAL-200 / VAL-300 / VAL-400
* **Equipment Type**: Continuous water bath bubble surging and inclined mesh-belt secondary high-pressure rinse integrated assembly line
* **Market Positioning**: Targeted at modern agricultural industrial parks, university and corporate central kitchens, fresh-cut salad processing facilities, and frozen/dehydrated food manufacturers to overcome high daily throughput demands, escalating manual washing costs, ineffective sand/floating debris removal, and persistent pesticide residue buildup.

**Engineering & Manufacturing Highlights**:

* **High-Power Industrial Air Blower & Micro-Porous Aeration Array**: Multi-channel stainless steel aeration pipes are set across the tank floor. The high-pressure blower forces air through micro-perforations, creating a dense surge of rising bubbles. These bubbles simulate a vigorous rolling boil, using fluid dynamics to gently envelope and scrub the produce from all angles, stripping trapped mud and debris without bruising fragile leaves or soft fruit skins.
* **VFD-Driven Food-Grade SUS304 Mesh Belt Conveyor**: Driven by a high-torque speed-reducing motor and variable frequency drive (VFD), conveying speeds can be smoothly adjusted via the control panel. The mesh belt carries produce steadily forward and up the discharge incline, preventing inter-product crushing while eliminating belt wear to deliver continuous automated loading and discharging.
* **Secondary High-Pressure Fresh Water Spray on Elevating Incline**: As produce ascends out of the wash tank, it passes through an independent clean-water rinse zone. A high-pressure booster pump delivers fresh water through multi-angle fan spray nozzles, thoroughly removing residual tank water and loose debris. This fresh rinse water flows directly back into the main tank, maintaining clean water levels and creating an efficient cascade recirculation system.
* **Dual-Layer Sludge Settling Hopper & Overflow Filtration Sub-Tank**: The main tank floor features an angled conical settling hopper where heavy sand and gravel settle out of the active washing zone. An external overflow filtration sub-tank with removable multi-stage fine screens and scum skimmers continuously traps floating husks, loose leaves, and fibers. Recirculated clean water is pumped back to the main tank, cutting overall water consumption by over **50%–60%**.

### 2. Core Functions & Working Principle

The line operates continuously through four hydrodynamic mechanisms: high-pressure bubble micro-bursting, turbulent propulsion, buoyant separation, and cascade spray rinsing:

* **Infeed & Turbulent Water Propulsion**: Raw produce is fed into the front infeed section. Bottom aeration instantly disperses compacted clusters, preventing product clumping. Simultaneously, directional circulating water currents tumble produce gently while propelling it toward the discharge end at a steady speed.
* **Bubble Burst Micro-Shockwave Dirt Removal**: Millions of dense microbubbles continuously strike and burst against product surfaces. The resulting microscopic shear forces penetrate leaf folds and stem indentations, dislodging sand, dust, insect eggs, and surface chemical residues far more effectively than static manual soaking.
* **Heavy Impurity Settling & Floating Debris Overflow**: High-density grit, stones, and sand fall through the wire mesh belt into the bottom sediment hopper and remain isolated from turbulent flow. Lightweight floating debris, dry leaves, and froth are swept toward lateral overflow ports into the filtration sub-tank.
* **Incline Elevation & Secondary Clean Water Rinse**: Clean produce travels up the inclined mesh belt out of the bath, passing under overhead fresh-water fan spray nozzles that provide a final, spotless rinse, completely eliminating dirty water carryover.
* **Seamless Downstream Line Integration**: Products discharge smoothly from the exit chute to connect directly with **high-flow air knife dewatering dryers, commercial vegetable cutters, metal detectors, or automated packaging lines**, creating a complete, untouched processing flow.

## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Follow these standardized procedures to ensure optimal cleaning performance, prevent plumbing blockages, and maintain electrical safety:

### 1. Raw Material Preparation & Pre-Sorting

* **Pre-Sorting Foreign Objects**:
  * **Never dump unbundled twine, baling wire, broken plastic bags, or large stones into the line!** Long strings can wrap around internal shafts and drive chains.
  * For vegetables with hard, clumped root mud (such as spinach or celery roots), trimming root tips or loosening clumps beforehand significantly accelerates washing efficiency.
* **Water Supply Check**: Ensure the incoming clean water supply provides a minimum pressure of **0.2 MPa** to guarantee full, uniform fan spray patterns across the incline rinse zone.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Utility Connections

1. Verify the assembly line is leveled securely on a reinforced non-slip concrete floor with balanced support legs, and ensure the main electrical panel is grounded to a reliable 3-phase power supply.
2. Ensure the bottom sludge drainage butterfly valves and sand purge valves are **fully closed and locked**.
3. Open the main water supply valve to fill the main tank until water reaches the overflow port (a slight overflow into the sub-tank indicates a full tank).
4. Verify all multi-stage removable stainless steel filter screens in the auxiliary sub-tank are clean and locked in place.

### 3. Standard Operating Guide

1. **Sequential Startup**:
   * Turn on the main electrical isolator switch;
   * Start the **water circulation pump** and confirm steady directional surface flow;
   * Start the **high-pressure vortex air blower**; observe the main tank to confirm vigorous, uniform white-water bubble aeration;
   * Start the **mesh belt conveyor motor** and set the VFD potentiometer to the target processing speed (typically 15–35 Hz depending on product type);
   * Open the **secondary rinse water valve** and verify that all fan spray nozzles across the elevating section are spraying clean water evenly.
2. **Continuous, Even Infeed**: Feed produce evenly and steadily into the infeed section (manually or via an automated infeed elevator). **Avoid dumping dense crate-loads in one spot**, which can overload the aeration bed and hinder bubble penetration.
3. **Operational Monitoring**: Adjust mesh belt speed as needed based on incoming soil loads (slow down belt speed for heavy mud loads to extend soak time; increase belt speed for lightly soiled produce to boost hourly output).
4. **Regular Scum Removal**: Periodically inspect the collection basket in the filtration sub-tank during production. Lift and empty collected floating leaves and froth as needed to maintain unimpeded recirculation.

### 4. Shutdown, Cleaning, Drainage & Daily Maintenance

* **Clearing Material**: Stop feeding produce at the front end while keeping the line running until all products have exited the discharge incline.
* **Shutdown & Drainage**: Press the stop buttons in sequence: air blower, spray pump, circulation pump, and conveyor drive. Shut off main power. Open the primary bottom drainage gate valves to discharge wastewater and accumulated sand into the floor drain trench.
* **Internal Tank Washdown**: Use a low-pressure washdown hose to rinse inner tank walls, under-belt wear tracks, and the sludge hopper, clearing all vegetable scraps and sediment.
* **Drive & Chain Lubrication**: Inspect the conveyor drive chain, bearings, and take-up sprockets weekly. Apply food-grade water-resistant grease and adjust the tail take-up tension bolts if slack develops.
* **Electrical Safety Warning**: **Never direct high-pressure spray wands at the main control enclosure, VFD digital display, or motor casings!**

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why is bubble water bath cleaning particularly suited for delicate items like strawberries, tomatoes, and spinach? Will it crush leaves or soft fruits?**
  * **A:** Traditional rotary drum or abrasive brush washers use hard mechanical impact and friction, which tears leafy greens, dislodges strawberry calyxes, and bruises tomato skins, leading to rapid spoilage and unappealing produce. In contrast, the bubble washing line keeps products fully suspended in a buoyant water bath. Micro-shockwaves from collapsing bubbles gently dislodge dirt and surface debris without rigid mechanical contact, completely eliminating bruising and tearing.
* **Q: When washing heavily soiled vegetables, will accumulated mud and floating leaves foul the wash water and cause recontamination?**
  * **A:** No. The line features a triple-action separation design:
    1. **Bottom Sediment Isolation**: Dense sand and stones fall through the mesh belt into the bottom conical hopper—a dead-flow zone where water turbulence cannot stir them back up.
    2. **Lateral Overflow Skimming**: Lightweight floating weeds and leaf fragments are guided by surface current into lateral overflow ports and caught by fine filter screens in the external sub-tank.
    3. **Secondary Fresh Spray Rinse**: As vegetables emerge on the discharge incline, high-pressure fan nozzles deliver a final rinse of fresh, clean water, flushing away any residual dirty water before products exit the machine.
* **Q: How can this washing line be integrated with cutters, dewatering machines, or packaging units to build a complete central kitchen processing line?**
  * **A:** The line is engineered with standardized heights and incline angles to connect directly into automated processing lines:
    * **Upstream Infeed**: Connects to an automated bin tipper or sorting inspection conveyor for continuous infeed.
    * **Downstream Outfeed**: The exit chute discharges directly into a high-flow ambient air-knife dewatering machine, which blows away surface moisture before vegetables enter a commercial multi-functional slicer/dicer or modified atmosphere packaging (MAP) system. A complete line requires only 2 to 3 operators to process tons of produce daily.
