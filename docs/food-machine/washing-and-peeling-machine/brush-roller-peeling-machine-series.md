### 2. Core Functions & Working Principle

The equipment combines horizontal multi-axis brush rotation friction with high-pressure fluid flushing:

* **Trough Bulk Loading**: Whole crates of muddy potatoes, soil-coated lotus roots, cassava, or oysters covered in algae/sand can be loaded directly into the horizontal cleaning trough, followed by opening the water spray valve.
* **Multi-Roller Tumbling & Surface Rubbing**: The motor drives the brush rollers in synchronized rotation, gently rolling the materials in a wave-like pattern. Millions of resilient nylon bristles continuously rub against every angle and groove of the product surface, rapidly stripping away stubborn mud and epidermal layers.
* **Non-Destructive Protection for Elongated Produce**: Unlike vertical centrifugal peelers that cause violent wall impacts, the horizontal multi-roller layout allows long produce like lotus roots, yams, and cassava to lie flat and rotate gently, preventing snapped tips, cracks, or bruising discoloration.
* **High-Pressure Spray Flushing**: The continuous top spray cleans both the produce and the brush bristles, instantly flushing detached sludge, peelings, and grit down through roller gaps into the bottom sediment hopper.
* **Side-Hatch Quick Discharge**: Once washing or peeling is complete, simply release the end discharge gate handle while the rollers continue spinning. The rotational motion smoothly propels the produce out into receiving crates in seconds.

## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

### 1. Raw Material Preparation & Pre-Treatment

* **Sorting Foreign Objects**:
  * **Carefully inspect and remove nails, large stones, bricks, and hard debris mixed in with dirt!** Trapped stones can jam between rollers, stall the motor, or damage bristles.
  * For produce with thick, dried-out mud (like field sweet potatoes or cassava), pre-wetting or spraying with water helps soften soil, significantly accelerating processing speed.
* **Seafood Inspection**: When washing oysters or shellfish, discard dead cracked shells beforehand to prevent sharp fragments from lodging between the bristles.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Water Connection

1. Place the machine on a level, solid floor, ensuring all support legs are firmly positioned, and connect it to a safe, properly grounded **110V or 220V power supply**.
2. Firmly connect the clean water inlet hose to the top spray pipe coupling and secure it with a stainless steel hose clamp.
3. Direct the bottom discharge drain toward a floor drainage channel or sediment settling tank to prevent sand clogging municipal piping.
4. **Secure the Discharge Gate**: Shut the side discharge hatch and check that the locking clamp handle is fastened securely.

### 3. Standard Washing & Peeling Guide

1. **Start Water and Machine Before Loading**: Press the green push button to idle the machine, then turn the water valve fully open to wet and lubricate the brush rollers.
2. **Uniform Loading**: Pour products into the cleaning trough evenly according to the machine rating (Model 45: 10–12 kg; Model 60: 15–20 kg; Model 80: 35–40 kg; Model 100: 40–50 kg; never load past the top roller height line).
3. **Processing Duration**:
   * **Washing Mode (Soft Bristles)**: Approx. **1–3 minutes**; unload once the drain water runs clear.
   * **Peeling Mode (Hard Bristles/Emery Rollers)**: Approx. **3–5 minutes**; unload once skins are uniformly peeled.
4. **Automatic Discharge**: **While the machine remains running**, place a perforated collection crate under the side chute, flip open the discharge latch, and allow the tumbling rollers to automatically sweep the produce out. Re-close and latch the door immediately to process the next batch.
5. **Safety Warning**: **Never put hands, iron shovels, or metal rods between the rollers while the unit is running!**

### 4. Shutdown, Cleaning & Maintenance

* **Flushing the Trough**: After each shift, keep the unit running empty with water spraying for 2 minutes to rinse out any residual peelings, mud, or fibers between the bristles.
* **Sediment Tank Cleaning**: Periodically open the bottom clean-out access cover to flush accumulated sediment and prevent bacterial growth or foul odors.
* **Electrical Water Warning**: Wipe down the stainless steel exterior with a damp cloth; **never spray high-pressure water directly at the motor, chain drive compartment, or electrical control box!**
* **Chain Lubrication**: Open the side drive guard monthly to check chain tension and apply food-grade water-resistant grease to sprockets and drive chains.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: What is the main difference between a brush roller machine and a vertical cylinder abrasive peeler? Why must lotus root and yam use a brush roller machine?**
  * **A:** Vertical cylinder abrasive peelers rely on high-speed centrifugal rotation that flings products against an abrasive abrasive lining. This works well for round or oval items (like regular potatoes and taro). However, elongated and fragile items like lotus roots, yams, and cassava will snap and break immediately upon high-speed impact, resulting in catastrophic loss. The brush roller machine uses a horizontal multi-roller bed where items lie flat and roll gently over soft or medium bristles. It cleans stubborn mud out of deep joints without breaking or bruising elongated crops.
* **Q: What is the difference between soft and hard brush rollers? Can they be interchanged based on our processing needs?**
  * **A:** They differ in bristle stiffness and target applications:
    * **Soft Bristles (Fine undulating filaments)**: Designed for "mud and grime removal while preserving outer skin," perfect for crisp lotus root, new potatoes, carrots, and water chestnuts where peeling is unwanted.
    * **Hard Bristles / Emery Rollers (Thickened stiff filaments or abrasive grit)**: Designed for "aggressive peeling and scouring," suitable for aged potatoes, tough sweet potatoes, mature ginger, and cassava requiring skin removal.
    * Customers can specify roller configurations directly with CECLE according to their processing requirements.
* **Q: Can this machine wash raw shellfish like oysters and scallops? Will it crack the shells?**
  * **A:** Yes, oyster and shellfish cleaning is one of this machine's most celebrated commercial applications. Shellfish often have encrusted mud, barnacles, and marine algae that are difficult and hazardous to scrub by hand. The dense, high-elasticity nylon bristles combined with pressurized water jets thoroughly clean the deep shell ridges from all angles. Because the bristles provide natural cushioning, they will not crack the shells, delivering spotless oysters for high-end raw bars, oyster barbecues, and seafood supply hubs.
