# CECLE Commercial Fish Back & Belly Opening Machine Series - Technical Comparison & Selection Guide

> 💡 **Series Overview:** The **CECLE Commercial Fish Dressing & Slitting Series** provides mechanized solutions for seafood wholesale processors, grilled fish restaurant chains, pickled fish franchise commissaries, and commercial kitchens. Both the **FFK-18 Continuous Conveyor Model** and the **FK-17 Vertical Cabinet Model** eliminate the safety risks, high labor costs, and uneven knife cuts of manual butchery. Engineered with **SUS304 food-grade stainless steel**, **cryogenically treated alloy circular blades**, and **calibrated spinal depth control**, both units achieve a **gallbladder rupture rate of less than 0.1%** while offering dedicated factory configurations for **"Precision Open Back"** (butterfly cut for grilling) and **"Precision Open Belly"** (gutting/filleting prep).

## 3. Buyer's Selection & Decision Matrix

Choose the model that fits your layout and throughput:

### Choose the FFK-18 if:
1. **Output is your #1 priority:** Your team handles large wholesale crates or continuous shifts where processing 40–50 fish per minute saves hours of prep time.
2. **You process smaller or uniform fish:** You frequently process fish in the 100g to 2.5 kg range (e.g., standard grilled tilapia, snakehead, or yellow croaker).
3. **You have room for a 1.6-meter conveyor line:** Your layout has adequate floor space for dedicated infeed and discharge bins.

### Choose the FK-17 if:
1. **Floor space is strictly limited:** You need an industrial butcher replacement that fits into a standard 60 × 60 cm kitchen station.
2. **You process heavier fish:** Your menu features large grass carp, common carp, or bighead carp weighing between 2.5 kg and 4.0 kg.
3. **Hygiene and containment are critical:** You operate in a clean commercial kitchen where an enclosed cabinet prevents splash hazards and keeps prep stations dry.

## 4. Shared Engineering & Quality Standards

Regardless of the model chosen, every CECLE fish processing machine includes the following manufacturing baselines:

1. **Intact Gallbladder Guarantee (<0.1% Rupture Rate):** Calibrated depth control ensures incisions run along the bone line, eliminating bitter bile contamination and wasted product.
2. **Dedicated Functional Calibration:** Both models are supplied as either a **Dedicated Open Back** or **Dedicated Open Belly** unit with factory-locked guides to ensure repeatable accuracy.
3. **Food-Grade SUS304 Construction:** Thickened brushed stainless steel repels salt corrosion, fish blood, and moisture, supporting washdown sanitation.
4. **Worldwide Door-to-Door Logistics:** Available with full-service door-to-door ocean delivery options, handling export documentation, sea freight, customs clearance, and prepaid duty logistics.
5. **Factory Warranty & Technical Support:** Covered by a **1-year warranty** on core motors and drive assemblies, backed by video technical guidance and express spare parts dispatch.
