## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

To maintain electrical safety, protect alloy cutting tools, and preserve commercial food hygiene, strictly observe the following protocols:

### 2.1 Pre-Start Inspection & Utility Connection

1. **Placement & Leveling**: Install on a level, slip-resistant floor with adequate floor drainage. Adjust the 4 heavy-duty stainless steel leveling feet so that all corners bear weight evenly with zero chassis play.
2. **Electrical Safety Connection (⚠️ Crucial Safety Point)**:
   * Rated motor power is 1.5 kW; operational voltage supports **220V 1-phase or 380V 3-phase (50/60Hz optional)**;
   * Must be wired directly to a dedicated **industrial circuit breaker with leakage protection**, ensuring the protective earth ground wire (PE) is securely grounded. Never plug into loose, worn extension cords.
3. **Discharge Catch Basin**: Place a clean, food-grade perforated plastic bin beneath the lower exit chute to receive dressed fish smoothly.

### 2.2 Blade Depth & Fish Sizing Adjustment

1. **Pre-Calibrate Blade Depth by Fish Weight**: Before running, calibrate the top micrometer dial according to the batch weight (e.g., 1.5 kg grass carp vs. 3.0 kg bighead carp):
   * **Smaller Fish (0.5–1.5 kg)**: Rotate clockwise to extend blade stroke so dorsal flesh is fully slit;
   * **Larger Fish (2.0–4.0 kg)**: Rotate counter-clockwise to retract blade stroke slightly, preventing over-penetration into abdominal visceral pockets.
2. **Idle Power-On Test**: Press the green "START" button. Let the machine idle for 10–15 seconds to verify smooth, quiet mechanical movement without metallic scraping or jamming before loading fish.
3. **Standardized Infeed Technique**:
   * Wear food-safe, cut-resistant, non-slip processing gloves;
   * Hold the fish firmly by the tail end, align the head downward with the spine (Open Back) or belly (Open Belly) centered into the guide channel, slide it in, and release;
   * **Absolute Safety Red Line (⚠️)**: **Never insert fingers into the infeed chute while the machine is operating! If a fish jams, immediately slap the red EMERGENCY STOP palm button, disconnect the power, and use a specialized hook tool to clear the jam!**

### 2.4 End-of-Day Sanitation & Maintenance

* **Daily Washdown Procedure**:
  1. Once daily cutting concludes, press the red STOP button and shut off the main circuit breaker;
  2. Open the quick-release top inspection access hatch;
  3. Use a low-pressure water hose to flush away accumulated scales, blood, and slime from the clamping jaws and rotary blade housing;
  4. Spray all contact surfaces with neutral food-grade foaming sanitizer, let soak for 3 minutes, rinse thoroughly with fresh water, and leave the canopy open to air dry.
* **Blade & Drive Lubrication (Weekly Requirement)**:
  * Regularly inspect the circular blade edge. If heavy bones cause minor dulling, remove the blade and hone both bevels with a fine diamond whetstone;
  * After drying, wipe a light coating of **food-grade white mineral oil (anti-rust oil)** onto the blade edge and mechanical slide linkages to prevent oxidation.
