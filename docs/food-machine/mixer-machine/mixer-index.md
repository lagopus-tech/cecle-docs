## ❓ Comprehensive FAQ Index

### Category 1: Equipment Selection & Baking Technology

* **Q1: If my bakery produces both bread and cakes/desserts, which machine should I buy?**
  * **A**: If your main products are **bread, toast, or pizza**, we strongly recommend prioritizing the **N Series Spiral Dough Mixer** (fast gluten development without temperature rise). If you mainly produce **cakes, puffs, egg tarts, fillings, or light batters**, the **EB Series Planetary Mixer** is the ideal choice. Commercial bakeries with higher production volumes usually equip one of each (N Series dedicated to dough, EB Series dedicated to whipping cream and egg wash).

* **Q2: How does the N Series Spiral Mixer perform with high-hydration doughs (e.g., Ciabatta, Sourdough 70%-80%+ hydration)?**
  * **A**: Exceptionally well. The dual-action spiral system rapidly builds the gluten network to trap moisture. When running at high speed (200 r/min), it quickly incorporates water into the flour, making it ideal for high-hydration or artisan bread doughs.

* **Q3: Can the EB Series Planetary Mixer be used to knead stiff doughs (e.g., bagels, ramen noodles)?**
  * **A**: **Not recommended.** The EB Series uses a planetary transmission. Consistently kneading heavy, low-hydration stiff doughs with the dough hook places heavy strain on the gearbox. For stiff or heavy doughs, always choose the **N Series Dual-Action Spiral Mixer**.

* **Q4: How does the N Series "Dual-Action" design prevent dough from overheating?**
  * **A**: Traditional mixers only rotate the hook, forcing the dough to rub against a static bowl and center column, causing rapid friction heat that degrades yeast activity. The N Series features **simultaneous rotation of both the bowl (16 r/min) and the spiral hook**. This ensures uniform force distribution, eliminates dead spots, and keeps dough temperature 3-5°C lower than traditional mixers.

### Category 2: Operation, Maintenance & Troubleshooting

* **Q5: Why is it strictly forbidden to shift gears while the machine is running?**
  * **A**: Commercial mixers use **heavy-duty mechanical gearboxes**. Shifting gears while the motor is spinning at high speeds causes severe gear collisions and tooth stripping. **Correct procedure: Press Stop ➔ Wait for the rotation to stop completely ➔ Shift gear ➔ Restart.**

* **Q6: How should the machine be cleaned? Can I use a high-pressure water gun on the whole machine?**
  * **A**: **Never spray high-pressure water directly onto the control panel, electrical components, or motor housing!** Food-contact parts (stainless steel bowl, spiral hook, whisk) are made of 304 stainless steel and can be wiped or removed for washing. For the machine body housing internal wiring, wipe down using a damp cloth.

* **Q7: What happens if the safety guard is raised during operation?**
  * **A**: Both series are equipped with **micro-switch safety interlocks**. Raising the safety guard during operation causes the machine to **stop instantly** to protect the operator's hands. Lowering the guard and pressing Start resumes operation.

* **Q8: What should I do if the machine suddenly stops during operation?**
  * **A**: Follow this troubleshooting checklist:
    1. Check if the red **Emergency Stop button** was pressed (if so, rotate clockwise to reset).
    2. Ensure the **safety guard** is fully lowered into place.
    3. Check for power phase loss (for 380V 3-phase units) or low voltage that may have tripped the thermal overload protection.
