## 2. Production Flow & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Core operating steps and parameter guidelines derived from actual machine commissioning and operational testing:

### 1. Pre-Operation Inspection & Attachment Installation

* Inspect the power cable and grounding protection to ensure a stable **110V** power source.
* Choose the proper attachment (whisk, beater, or dough hook) based on production needs, insert it into the mixing shaft socket, push upwards, and rotate to lock in place.
* Place the stainless steel bowl on the cradle, raise the bowl, and lock the securing handles.

### 2. Ingredient Loading & Speed Selection

* **Ingredient Loading**: Pour egg liquid, cream, or flour into the bowl. Do not exceed the maximum rated capacity (e.g., 3.5kg dough max for EB-15, 8kg max for EB-40).
* **Speed Selection**:
  * **Egg Whisking / Cream Whipping**: Use the whisk wire whip at **High Speed** (e.g., 390 rpm for EB-15, 408 rpm for EB-20/30).
  * **Batter / Filling Mixing**: Use the flat beater at **Medium Speed** (e.g., 160–180 rpm).
  * **Dough Kneading**: Use the dough hook at **Low Speed** (e.g., 80–110 rpm).

### 3. Bowl Elevation & Operation

* **Elevation Control**: Turn the handwheel or pull the lift lever to raise the bowl to its highest operating position.
* **Start Equipment**: Close the safety guard and press the Start button. The machine will start operating at the selected speed.
* **Speed Shift Warning (CRITICAL)**: **NEVER switch speed gears while the machine is running at high speed!** Always stop the machine completely, wait for the shaft to come to a complete stop, adjust the speed lever, and then restart.

### 4. Monitoring & Unloading

* Monitor the mixing/whipping state. Once egg whites reach soft/stiff peaks or dough achieves proper gluten development, press the Stop button.
* Lower the bowl cradle, unlock and remove the attachment, and remove the bowl to unload or proceed to the next baking process.

### 5. Emergency Stop & Safety Features

* A red **Emergency Stop Button** is located in the center of the control panel. In an emergency, press the button to immediately cut off all power. Twist the button clockwise to reset.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Can the machine handle different recipes for egg liquid or dough (e.g., high/low gluten flour, varying hydration levels)?**
  * **A**: Absolutely. The EB Series features 3-speed adjustment and three specialized attachments. By adjusting the speed setting and attachment, it easily adapts to recipes ranging from highly fluid egg mixes and light batters to high-viscosity doughs.
* **Q: Can speed gear levels be changed while the machine is running?**
  * **A**: No. To protect the gear transmission system and prolong machine service life, you must stop the machine first. Wait until the mixing shaft comes to a complete standstill before changing speed gears, then restart.
* **Q: What are the capacity limits for cream whipping and dough kneading?**
  * **A**: The maximum rated dough kneading capacities for each model are 3.5kg (EB-15), 5kg (EB-20), 6kg (EB-30), and 8kg (EB-40). Please operate within the specified rated capacity to ensure optimal mixing performance and prolong machine life.
