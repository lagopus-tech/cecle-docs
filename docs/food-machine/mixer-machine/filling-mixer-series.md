## II. Production Process & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

The following core operation and parameter setting guide is summarized from actual machine commissioning and operation:

### 1. Equipment Inspection & Preparation

* Check the power cord and grounding protection to ensure stable voltage (220V/380V), and lock the caster brakes.
* Verify that the bottom discharge valve (red handle valve) is fully closed and locked.
* Install the stainless steel agitator shaft and blades properly, ensuring all fasteners are secured.

### 2. Material Loading & Speed Selection

* **Material Loading**: Pour diced meat, chopped vegetables, water, and seasonings into the bowl, taking care not to exceed the maximum rated capacity (e.g., MI-30 output limit 20kg, MI-60 output limit 90kg).
* **Start Operation**: Close the top stainless steel safety guard mesh, press the start button, and begin mixing.

### 3. Process Monitoring & Discharge

* Observe the mixing status. When the meat filling reaches uniform water absorption and binding, or when the vegetable filling is evenly mixed, press the stop button.
* Position a receiving container (such as a filling pan or trolley bucket) directly beneath the bottom discharge valve, open the red handle valve, and the material will rapidly discharge assisted by gravity and mixing motion.

### 4. Emergency Stop & Safety Protection

* A prominent red **Emergency Stop Button** is located on the control panel. In an emergency, pressing this button immediately cuts power to all drive systems; rotate clockwise to reset.

  * **A**: It will not clog. The discharge valve utilizes a large-diameter food-grade quick-release valve designed specifically for viscous meat pastes and sauces with particles. For cleaning, simply fill with clean water, run the mixer, and open the valve to flush without hygiene dead spots.
