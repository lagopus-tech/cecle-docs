## 2. Production Process & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Core operating steps and parameter guidelines compiled from actual equipment commissioning and running tests:

### 1. Pre-Operation Inspection

* Check the power cord and grounding protection to ensure a stable **220V/380V** power connection.
* Ensure the mixing bowl and spiral hook are clean with no residues, and the safety guard opens and closes smoothly.
* Confirm the safety guard is lowered to the working position.

### 2. Loading Ingredients & Speed Selection

* **Loading Steps**: Pour flour, water, yeast, and other raw ingredients into the bowl. Ensure total weight does not exceed the machine's maximum rated dough capacity (e.g., 8kg for N-20, 20kg for N-50).
* **Gear/Speed Selection (Two-Speed)**:
  * **Initial Mixing**: Select **slow speed** (101 r/min) to gradually combine water and flour, preventing dry flour from splashing.
  * **Kneading/Windowpane Stage**: Once dough forms a cohesive mass, switch to **fast speed** (200 r/min) for high-speed stretching and kneading to rapidly develop gluten.

### 3. Operation & Safety

* **Start Equipment**: Close the safety guard, select slow speed, press the Start button; the mixing bowl (16 r/min) and spiral hook begin synchronous rotation.
* **Speed Switching Warning (Important)**: **When the machine is running at high speed, it is recommended to press the Stop button or wait for the machine to slow down before switching gears** to best protect the two-speed motor and mechanical gear transmission system.
* **Emergency Stop**: A large red Emergency Stop button is located at the center of the control panel. Press in an emergency to immediately cut off all power; rotate clockwise to reset.

### 4. Monitoring & Unloading

* Observe dough elasticity. Once dough achieves the desired gluten structure (e.g., passing the windowpane test), press the Stop button.
* Lift the safety guard and manually remove the dough from the bowl to proceed to fermentation and shaping stages.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Can this machine handle low-hydration/stiff doughs (such as ramen dough or bagel dough)?**
  * **A**: Yes. The N Series is specifically designed for heavy doughs. The robust spiral hook and double-action system easily handle low-hydration doughs. However, for exceptionally stiff doughs, we recommend limiting the batch size to approximately 70% of maximum rated capacity to reduce machine load and prolong service life.
* **Q: Can I switch between high and low speeds directly while the machine is running?**
  * **A**: Yes, but to provide optimal protection for the two-speed motor and mechanical gear transmission, it is strongly recommended to slow down or briefly press Stop before switching from slow speed (101 r/min) to fast speed (200 r/min), adjust the gear, and restart.
* **Q: What are the benefits of a double-action system (bowl and hook rotating simultaneously)?**
  * **A**: The double-action system ensures that every part of the dough is evenly kneaded. It prevents the dough from merely wrapping around the center column, significantly reduces friction heat (preserving yeast activity), and cuts kneading time in half compared to fixed-bowl dough mixers.
