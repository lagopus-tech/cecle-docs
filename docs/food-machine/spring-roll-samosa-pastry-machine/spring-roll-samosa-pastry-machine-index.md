## III. Frequently Asked Questions (Series FAQ)

* **Q: Can these machines make wrappers of different thicknesses?**
  * **A**: Yes. The thickness of the wrapper (ranging from 0.3mm to 2.0mm) can be easily adjusted by modifying the batter concentration (water-to-flour ratio) and fine-tuning the heating drum rotation speed via the microcomputer control panel.
* **Q: What is the difference between the pancake machine and the samosa/spring roll wrapper machine?**
  * **A**: While both utilize advanced automated drum-baking assembly line technology, they can be optimized with specific shaping nozzles, cutting blades, and mold configurations tailored to the specific dimensions and geometry required for round roast duck pancakes versus square samosa pastry sheets.
* **Q: How difficult is it to clean the equipment after daily production?**
  * **A**: Extremely easy. The entire body and food-contact parts are made of 304 food-grade stainless steel. The batter delivery pipeline supports water-pumping circulating self-cleaning, and the drum surface can be wiped clean after cooling.
