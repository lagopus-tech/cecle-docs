## II. Production Process and Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

The following is a core operation and parameter setting guide based on real machine startup debugging:

**1. Pre-startup Preparation**

* Check whether the surface of the machine drum is clean, and whether the conveyor belt and batter pump pipeline are connected correctly without foreign objects.
* Set the baking temperature of the drum according to the wrapper requirements, and turn on the heating switch to preheat (usually preheated to 120°C - 160°C).
* Before preheating is complete, a very small amount of edible demolding oil can be evenly applied to the drum surface.

**2. Feeding and Operation**

* **Batter Pre-processing**: Mix flour and water according to a strict ratio to make a batter, let it stand to remove bubbles (the batter needs to have good fluidity and no lumps), and pour it into the machine's stainless steel batter bucket.
* **Start Forming**: After the drum temperature reaches the set value, turn on the drum rotation motor, and then turn on the batter delivery pump. The batter will automatically and evenly adhere to the drum surface.

**3. Forming and Collection**

* **Thickness and Speed Adjustment**: Observe the first few formed wrappers to check their doneness and thickness. If the wrapper is too thick or undercooked, appropriately slow down the drum rotation speed or increase the temperature; the thickness can also be fine-tuned by adjusting the batter concentration.
* The machine will automatically complete the process of batter baking, maturation, and demolding.
* The finished wrappers will automatically fall onto the conveyor belt along the peeling blade, and employees can directly stack them at the end or proceed to the next packaging step.

**4. Shutdown, Cleaning, and Maintenance**

* After work, be sure to turn off the batter pump first and let the remaining batter on the drum finish baking.
* Turn off the heating system switch, and cut off the main power/close the gas valve. **Note: Keep the drum idling for a period of time to help dissipate heat and prevent local high-temperature deformation.**
* After the drum has completely cooled naturally to a safe temperature, wipe the drum surface with a damp cloth (strictly prohibit using hard objects to scratch the drum).
* Put the batter pipeline into a bucket of clean water and turn on the pump for water circulation flushing until there is no batter residue inside the pipeline.
