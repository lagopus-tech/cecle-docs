## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Maintenance

Follow these guidelines for optimal slicing performance, extended blade longevity, and safe operation:

### 1. Material Preparation & Pre-Processing (Critical Factor)

* **Sorting & Cleanliness**:
  * Raw materials must be washed and trimmed. **Never load unwashed materials containing sand, gravel, soil clumps, or metal staples**, as these will damage the alloy blade edges.
  * Sort roots by diameter to match the appropriate guide mold tubes.
* **Hardness & Moisture Conditioning (For Hard Medicinal Roots)**:
  * Hard, dried medicinal roots (e.g., Astragalus, Ginseng, Licorice) must undergo standard softening or gentle steaming per processing protocols. Slicing when materials reach a "thoroughly conditioned, pliable, non-crumbly" state maximizes qualified yield and blade life.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Pneumatic Connection

1. **Air Supply Requirement (Mandatory)**:
   * **The unit does not include an air compressor. The user must provide an air compressor with ≥50 L tank capacity and 0.5 – 0.8 MPa operating pressure.**
   * Connect clean air to the machine's FRL unit (Filter-Regulator-Lubricator) using a quick-connect hose, ensuring leak-free connections.
2. **Electrical & Mechanical Checks**:
   * Position the unit on a firm, level surface ensuring all support feet bear weight evenly.
   * Connect to the designated power supply (2.2 kW power unit) with verified electrical grounding.
   * Open the safety guard, inspect blade mounting screws, turn the cutter head by hand to confirm free rotation, remove any debris, and secure the access door.

### 3. Standard Slicing Operation

1. **Set Slice Thickness**: Adjust the blade protrusion using the micro-metric adjustment mechanism to set thickness within **0.5 – 5 mm**, then secure all locking handles.
2. **Start Main Motor**: Press the power start button and allow the cutter head to run unloaded for 3–5 seconds to verify smooth, quiet operation.
3. **Load Materials**: Place root materials into the guide mold tubes.
4. **Activate Pneumatic Feed**: Open the pneumatic control valve. The pneumatic push rod automatically advances material into the spinning disc; cut slices slide out continuously.
5. **Safety Warning**: **Never insert fingers, scrapers, or metal tools into the feed tubes or discharge chute while the machine is powered on!**

### 4. Shutdown, Cleaning & Daily Maintenance

* **Power & Air Isolation**: After operation, shut down the motor, disconnect main power, shut off the air compressor supply valve, and release residual pneumatic pressure.
* **Sanitization**: Open the slicing chamber, remove feed molds, clean out residue using a soft brush and clean warm water, and wipe dry.
* **Blade Care**: Inspect blade sharpness regularly. Hone gently with a fine diamond whetstone if slight dulling occurs, or replace with genuine spare blades. Apply a light layer of food-grade mineral oil during extended downtime to prevent oxidation.
* **Pneumatic Water Drainage**: Inspect the air filter bowl regularly and drain accumulated moisture to prevent water from entering pneumatic cylinders.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why does the XP-380 require a customer-supplied ≥50L air compressor? What are the benefits over manual or mechanical feeding?**
  * **A**: During high-speed slicing (1,792 cuts/min), raw materials experience intense cutting resistance. Manual feeding is uneven, causes vibration, and poses severe hand injury risks; mechanical gears lack adaptive buffering for varied root diameters. The XP-380 utilizes pneumatic cylinder pushing for uniform, constant, and cushioned feeding. This eliminates work hazards and guarantees slice uniformity. A ≥50L compressor ensures sustained working pressure (0.5–0.8 MPa) during continuous slicing without stalling.
* **Q: Can the XP-380 cut extremely hard dried medicinal herbs? Will it chip blades or produce excessive powder?**
  * **A**: The machine is purpose-built for root crops and herbs, utilizing cryogenic-treated wear-resistant alloy blades. Slicing properly conditioned/softened roots (e.g., Astragalus, American Ginseng, Angelica, Notoginseng) yields clean slices with scrap rates under 2%. For bone-dry, rock-hard materials, we strongly recommend pre-conditioning (steaming or moistening) per standard herbal processing practices to protect blade edges and minimize powdering loss.
* **Q: How is the 0.5 – 5 mm thickness adjusted? Is it difficult?**
  * **A**: Adjustment is fast and straightforward. The slicing mechanism features micro-metric adjustment bolts and scale markings without requiring disassembly of the entire disc. Simply loosen the locking handle, adjust to the desired thickness (from 0.5 mm paper-thin cuts up to 5 mm stewing cuts), retighten the lock, and resume production.
