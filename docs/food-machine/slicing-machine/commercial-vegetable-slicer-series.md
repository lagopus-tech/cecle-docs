### 2. Core Working Principle

The machine operates on high-speed rotary centrifugal cutting and adjustable mechanical transmission principles:

* **Continuous Steady Feeding**: Peeled and washed onions, scallions, peppers, and other vegetables are dropped into the cutting chamber via the wide top infeed hopper.
* **Dual-Speed Rotary Slicing**: Depending on the vegetable texture, either high or low speed is selected. The motor drives the rotary cutter disc at high speed, using ultra-sharp cutting edges to slice through ingredients with high linear velocity.
* **Centrifugal & Gravity Discharge**: Sliced, sectioned, or shredded vegetables slide smoothly down the discharge ramp into the collection bin below under centrifugal force and gravity, achieving instant, continuous "feed-to-discharge" output.
* **Clean Cuts that Lock in Freshness**: The crisp, instant mechanical cutting ensures neat cross-sections, preserving internal moisture and aromatic flavor compounds while significantly extending the refrigerated shelf life of prepped ingredients.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) & Production Workflow

To ensure optimal cutting quality, prolong blade service life, and ensure operator safety, strictly follow these operating guidelines:

### 1. Ingredient Preparation Requirements

* **Pre-processing**: Large root vegetables or whole onions should be peeled and trimmed. If ingredient dimensions exceed the feed opening, pre-cut them in half.
* **Remove Hard Foreign Objects**: Always inspect and remove hard debris such as stones, wire, pits, hard seeds, and bones before feeding. Hard foreign objects entering the blade chamber can severely damage cutting edges.

### 2. Pre-Operation Inspection & Setup

1. Place the machine securely on a flat, dry, and solid countertop, ensuring all rubber suction feet adhere firmly.
2. Inspect the power cable and plug for damage, then connect to a matching **110V or 220V grounded outlet**.
3. Inspect the cutting chamber and ensure the cutter disc retaining nut is tightly locked with no foreign debris inside.
4. Place a clean stainless steel basin or food-grade crate beneath the discharge chute.

### 3. Standard Cutting Operation

1. **Select Speed Based on Ingredients**:
   * **Low Speed**: Best for scallions, cilantro, leeks, spinach, and delicate greens to prevent bruising and excessive moisture release.
   * **High Speed**: Best for onions, bell peppers, carrots, cucumbers, potatoes, and firm root vegetables.
2. **Start the Machine**: Turn on the power switch and let the machine idle for 2–3 seconds to verify smooth, balanced rotation.
3. **Feed Evenly & Continuously**: Feed ingredients steadily through the infeed opening (never reach deep into the hopper; elongated greens slide in naturally).
4. **Collect Finished Products**: Monitor the discharge chute and replace collection bins promptly once full.
5. **Safety Warning**: **While the machine is powered and running, NEVER use fingers, chopsticks, or metal utensils to push ingredients inside the infeed hopper!**

### 4. Shutdown, Cleaning & Routine Maintenance

* **Power Safety**: Always unplug the power cable and ensure the cutter disc has come to a complete stop before cleaning or maintenance.
* **Quick-Release Blade Cleaning**: Open the front cover, remove the cutter disc, wash the blade and cutting chamber with clean water and mild food-safe detergent, dry thoroughly, and apply a light coating of food-grade oil to prevent surface oxidation.
* **Protect Electrical Components from Water**: Wipe the exterior casing with a damp cloth. **NEVER use high-pressure spray guns or running water directly over motor louvers and power switch panels!**

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: What is the practical advantage of the "Two Speeds Adjustable" feature?**
  * **A**: Different vegetables vary substantially in fiber density and moisture levels. Firm vegetables like onions and carrots require high-speed blades to slice through fibers instantly without releasing excess moisture. Delicate greens such as scallions, cilantro, and leeks can easily bruise or get blown around at high speeds; setting the machine to low speed preserves their delicate texture, crisp shape, and natural moisture balance.
* **Q: Does it really slice onions without tearing eyes or crushing out juice?**
  * **A**: Yes. Tearing while cutting onions is caused by blunt blades crushing cell walls and releasing volatile sulfur compounds. The OC-20 uses an ultra-sharp, food-grade rotary blade paired with a high-torque motor to slice onions in milliseconds. This minimizes cell breakage, leaving neat, dry slice surfaces and substantially reducing pungent tear-inducing fumes.
* **Q: Is it easy to clean? Do I need special tools to replace or clean the blades?**
  * **A**: Very easy. The front casing and blade attachment mechanism feature a user-friendly quick-release design. After disconnecting the power, simply open the chamber, remove the cutter disc, and wash it under running water with a soft brush. The stainless steel body will not harbor residual odors.
