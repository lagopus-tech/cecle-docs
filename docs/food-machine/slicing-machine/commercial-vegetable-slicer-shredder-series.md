### 2. Core Working Principle

The machine utilizes high-speed centrifugal rotary cutting combined with geometric gravity-assisted feeding:

* **Gravity-Assisted Infeed:** Washed and peeled potatoes, carrots, or quartered cabbage pieces are dropped through the infeed hopper into the cutting chamber.
* **Centrifugal Rotary Cutting:** The motor drives the cutter disc at high speed. Centrifugal force holds the produce firmly against the internal cutting reference surface, where fast-moving slicing or shredding blades slice cleanly through the vegetable.
* **Smooth Discharge & Moisture Retention:** Sliced chips or shreds drop instantly through the discharge chute into a receiving bin under gravity and centrifugal airflow. High-speed, razor-sharp shearing protects cellular structures, preventing the crushing, bruising, and juice leakage typical of dull blades, keeping processed vegetables crisp and fresh longer.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) & Production Workflow

Follow these operational guidelines to maintain cut quality, extend blade service life, and ensure operator safety:

### 1. Ingredient Preparation Requirements

* **Cleaning & Pre-Processing:** Wash root vegetables (potatoes, sweet potatoes, carrots) to remove soil and peel as needed. Cut oversized cabbage heads into halves or quarters to fit the hopper opening.
* **Prevent Hard Foreign Objects:** Check produce thoroughly to ensure stones, metal wires, utensils, pits, and hard debris do not enter the feed hopper, as hard objects will damage the high-speed rotating blades.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Setup

1. Place the machine on a flat, dry, level floor or sturdy workbench, ensuring all rubber feet make balanced contact with the surface.
2. Check the power cord and plug for damage, and connect to a properly grounded **220V (or optional 110V)** outlet.
3. Verify that all internal fastening bolts inside the cutting chamber are tight and that the cutter disc rotates freely by hand without abnormal noise or interference.
4. Place a large stainless-steel bin or perforated food-grade crate beneath the discharge chute.

### 3. Standard Cutting Operation

1. **Idle Start:** Turn on the power switch and run the machine empty for 2–3 seconds to ensure smooth, balanced operation.
2. **Continuous, Uniform Feeding:** Feed ingredients steadily into the hopper in batches (maintain a steady pace; avoid forcing oversized amounts in all at once).
3. **Collect Processed Produce:** With rapid discharge, replace full receiving bins promptly to prevent buildup and blockages inside the chute.
4. **Safety Warning:** **NEVER insert fingers into the infeed hopper while the machine is powered on! Always use the provided safety pusher when feeding ingredients.**

### 4. Shutdown, Cleaning & Routine Maintenance

* **Power Safety:** Always unplug the power cord and ensure the motor has come to a complete stop before cleaning or servicing.
* **Cutter Disc & Chamber Cleaning:** Open the cutting chamber cover, wash away residue and starch from the cutter disc and interior walls using clean water and a soft brush, wipe dry with a clean cloth, and apply a thin layer of food-grade mineral oil to the blade edges for rust protection.
* **Keep Electrical Enclosure Dry:** Wipe the exterior with a damp cloth. **NEVER spray water directly into motor ventilation louvers, the electrical housing, or the power switch!**
