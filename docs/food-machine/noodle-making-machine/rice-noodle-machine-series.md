## II. Production Workflow and Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Below are the standard operating procedures for startup debugging, slurry mixing, steaming, cutting, and daily maintenance:

**1. Pre-startup Preparation and Inspection**

* Place the machine steadily on the ground of a solid and flat commercial operation workshop or back kitchen.
* Check whether the water pipe connections are normal (for steam generation and cooling), and plug in the power supply of the corresponding voltage.
* Check whether there is any dried residue inside the conveyor belt, slurry inlet, and cutting blade port to ensure the work area is clean and hygienic.
* Start the heating system to preheat until the steam temperature reaches the set production standard.

**2. Slurry Mixing and Automatic Paving Operation**

* **Slurry Pre-treatment**: According to the standard golden water-to-rice ratio, grind the soaked rice into a delicate, grain-free rice slurry (or prepare the starch slurry).
* **Start Equipment and Feeding**: Turn on the main transmission switch of the machine, and the conveyor belt begins to run smoothly; open the hopper valve to let the slurry flow evenly into the slurry distributor on the conveyor belt.
* **Thickness Adjustment (Core)**: By adjusting the size of the slurry inlet valve and the running speed of the conveyor belt, fine-tune the noodle sheet to the desired perfect thickness.

**3. Steaming, Setting, Cooling, and Cutting**

* The rice slurry enters the high-temperature steam box along with the conveyor belt. Observe the maturation degree of the noodle sheet (it should be translucent and shiny when fully cooked).
* The matured noodle sheet automatically enters the cooling zone (Cooler) to cool down rapidly, making the surface of the sheet smooth.
* **Automatic Cutting**: The noodle sheet is smoothly fed into the high-speed cutting blade inlet, and the machine will instantly cut distinct strips of Ho Fun or cold noodles.
* Use a turnover box or tray under the discharge port to neatly collect the finished products.

**4. Shutdown Cleaning and Daily Maintenance**

* After production is completed, first close the slurry inlet valve and the heating system, keep the conveyor belt running, and thoroughly rinse the residual slurry off its surface with clean water.
* After the machine has cooled down, press the stop button and disconnect the power supply.
* **Safety Warning**: **There is still a high residual temperature inside and around the steam box after shutdown. Please be sure to wear anti-scald gloves when cleaning!** It is strictly forbidden to use a faucet to directly rinse the internal motor and electronic control distribution box of the machine. The external stainless steel body can be wiped clean with a damp cloth.
