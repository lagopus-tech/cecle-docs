## II. Production Process & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Below is a guide to core operations and parameter settings based on real-machine startup and debugging:

**1. Automatic Flour Dusting System Preparation**

* First, add dry flour (starch or cornstarch works better to prevent sticking) into the top dry powder hopper.
* Adjust the dusting brushes and baffles to ensure that a thin layer of flour is evenly sprinkled on both sides of the dough sheet while the machine is running.

**2. Dough Sheet Conveying & Precise Thickness Adjustment**

* **Dough Pre-treatment**: Slightly flatten the prepared dough to make it easier for the machine rollers to pull it in.
* **Starting the Machine**: Connect the 220V power supply, press the start switch, and the pressing rollers and conveyor belt will begin to run synchronously.
* Place the flattened dough on the feeding tray and guide it into the pressing rollers.
* **Thickness Adjustment (Crucial)**: Adjust the thickness of the dough sheet through the handwheel knobs on both sides. Fine-tune clockwise/counterclockwise until the desired thickness is reached. **Tip: It is recommended to test the thickness with a small piece of dough first.**

**3. Cutting, Forming, and Scrap Recycling**

* The pressed dough sheet will be automatically conveyed to the cutting mold area.
* After the mold rolls over, the formed dumpling wrappers will fall into the collection tray or conveyor belt below.
* **Scrap Recycling**: Pull the excess dough sheet (scrap edges) after cutting upwards, guide it, and wrap it around the recycling roller at the top. The machine will automatically roll up all the scraps while running. These scraps can be re-kneaded into the dough for secondary use, achieving zero waste.

**4. Collection & Shutdown Cleaning**

* Employees simply collect the neatly stacked dumpling wrappers at the outlet.
* **Shutdown Cleaning**: After work is finished, press the red stop button. Use a dry cloth or brush to clean the residual flour on the machine. **Warning: It is strictly forbidden to wash the motor area inside the machine body directly with a water hose!**

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Can the machine adapt to doughs with different recipes (different water content)?**
  * **A**: Yes. This machine has strong adaptability to dough. However, to achieve the best anti-sticking and forming effect, it is recommended that the water content of the dough should not be too high (not too soft). A slightly harder dough produces better-shaped wrappers and is less likely to stick.
* **Q: Can it make wrappers of different sizes and shapes? Like square wonton wrappers?**
  * **A**: Yes. The forming mold equipped on the machine is detachable. If you need to make round dumpling skins of different diameters or square wonton wrappers, you only need to purchase the corresponding backup molds from us for replacement.
* **Q: Is the 250W power of this machine enough? Will the dough get jammed?**
  * **A**: It is more than enough. The SK-080 uses a transmission system with a special deceleration and torque-increasing design. The 250W motor provides powerful pressing torque, which is not only very energy-saving but also maintains extremely smooth operation at the rated output (3800 pcs/hour) without jamming.
