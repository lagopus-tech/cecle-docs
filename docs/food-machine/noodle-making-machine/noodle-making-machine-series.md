## II. Production Process & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Below is the standard operating procedure for setup, dough rolling, noodle slicing, and daily maintenance:

**1. Pre-Operation Preparation & Safety Inspection**

* Place the machine securely on a level, sturdy commercial workbench.
* Ensure the safety guard is fastened tightly and plug the machine into a grounded 220V power outlet.
* Inspect rollers and cutter combs to ensure they are clean, dry, and free of dried dough residue.

**2. Dough Sheeting & Dual-Handwheel Calibration**

* **Dough Pre-treatment**: Flatten the prepared dough slightly by hand to a width that fits the roller intake.
* **Start the Machine**: Press the power switch; the rollers will begin rotating smoothly.
* **Thickness Adjustment (Crucial)**: Initially, loosen both handwheels symmetrically (gap of approx. 4–5 mm) and feed the dough block through.
* **Folding & Pressing**: Fold the ejected dough sheet in half and re-feed it 3–5 times. Gradually tighten both handwheels evenly to bring the sheet down to the target thickness (e.g., 1.0–2.5 mm).

**3. Cutter Installation & Noodle Slicing**

* Insert the desired noodle cutter assembly into the drive slot and secure it in place.
* Lightly dust both sides of the dough sheet with dry flour (or starch) to prevent sticking.
* Feed the leading edge of the sheet into the cutter inlet; the machine will instantly slice and eject uniform noodle strands.
* Collect the sliced noodles underneath the outlet using a tray or hanging rod.

**4. Shutdown Cleaning & Routine Maintenance**

* After slicing is complete, press the stop button and disconnect the power plug.
* Use a dry cleaning brush or wooden scraper to remove residual dough bits from rollers, gears, and cutter combs.
* **Safety Warning**: **Never wash or spray the internal motor and electrical components directly with water!** Wipe the stainless steel exterior with a damp cloth and dry thoroughly.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why is dual-handwheel adjustment superior to single-knob adjustment?**
  * **A**: Under sustained load, single-knob mechanisms tend to apply uneven force, causing the roller ends to tilt slightly and producing sheets that are thicker on one side. Dual-handwheel systems allow independent calibration on both sides, ensuring absolute roller parallelism and perfectly uniform sheet thickness.
* **Q: Can this machine be used strictly as a dough sheeter without slicing noodles?**
  * **A**: Absolutely. The noodle cutter features a modular quick-release design. Removing the cutter turns the unit into a high-performance commercial dough sheeter for dumpling skins, wonton wrappers, baozi sheets, layered flatbreads, pizza crusts, and pastry dough.
* **Q: How can I prevent the fresh noodles from sticking together during cutting?**
  * **A**: Two main factors: first, control dough hydration (keep water content around 38%–42% for a firmer dough); second, dust a thin layer of dry flour or cornstarch over the dough sheet before feeding it into the cutting blades.
