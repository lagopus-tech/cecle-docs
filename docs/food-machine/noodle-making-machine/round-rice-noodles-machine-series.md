**2. Production and Forming Process**

This equipment completely subverts the slow traditional processes of manual dough mixing and boiling water for steamer cooking:

* **Automatic Feeding and Propulsion**: The pre-soaked and ground rice slurry (or well-proportioned dough/powder mixture) is fed into the hopper, and the internal screw shaft of the machine runs at high speed to propel the material forward.
* **Instant Heat Curing**: The material generates high temperature under the powerful screw propulsion pressure and friction, and the starch instantly cures and sets without the need for external steam or boilers.
* **One-Step Extrusion and Discharging**: The cured dough mass is pushed to the front mold and instantly extruded into fresh rice noodles or pasta of uniform thickness. After collection, it can be dried, directly cooked, or weighed and packaged.

## II. Production Workflow and Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Below are the standard operating procedures for startup debugging, feeding, forming, discharging, and daily maintenance:

**1. Pre-startup Preparation and Inspection**

* Place the machine steadily on the ground of a solid and flat commercial operation workshop.
* Check if the 380V three-phase power connection is correct and safely grounded.
* Check whether the front extrusion mold is locked tightly and whether the internal screw shaft sleeve is clean and free of dried residue.

**2. Feeding and Self-Cooking Extrusion Operation**

* **Material Pre-treatment**: Prepare the fine, grain-free rice slurry or mixed powder material according to the standard water content ratio.
* **Start the Equipment**: Turn on the main unit through the instrument monitoring panel, and the main motor will drive the screw shaft to run smoothly at high speed.
* **Continuous Feeding**: Pour the material evenly and continuously into the upper hopper to avoid material interruption which causes discontinuous noodle output.

**3. Forming, Discharging, and Collecting**

* After entering the chamber, the material automatically heats up and cures through friction.
* Observe the shape of the rice noodles extruded from the front mold. Fully cooked rice noodles should appear translucent with a smooth and shiny surface.
* Use a turnover box, conveyor belt, or noodle stick under the discharge port to neatly collect the extruded fresh noodles.

**4. Shutdown Cleaning and Daily Maintenance**

* After production is completed, stop feeding and let the machine continue to run for a few minutes to extrude all the remaining material in the chamber.
* Press the stop button and cut off the main power supply.
* **Cleaning Requirements**: While the machine is still warm, remove the front mold and screw sleeve, and thoroughly wash away the residual starch inside with clean water to prevent it from condensing, hardening, and damaging the parts. The external stainless steel body can be simply wiped with a damp cloth.
