   * Choose **SK-080** for tabletop space-saving requirements in small restaurants needing up to 3,800 wrappers/hour.
   * Choose **ADS-220** for high-volume central kitchens or frozen food factories requiring heavy 330kg stability, rotary cutter speed, and continuous scrap spooling.
2. **For Fresh Noodle Bars & Canteens:**
   * Choose **Dual-Adjustment Noodle Machine** for standard daily production (30–60 kg/h) of cut wheat noodles with high thickness precision.
3. **For Large-Scale Rice Noodle & Asian Specialty Production:**
   * Choose **MK Series Self-Cooking Maker** for extruding round/flat rice noodles or pasta directly from rice slurry/starch without external boilers.

   * Choose **PRE Series (PRE-350 / PRE-500)** for primary heavy-duty dough rolling (5–20mm gap) to build gluten structure for steamed buns, bread, or pie dough blocks.
