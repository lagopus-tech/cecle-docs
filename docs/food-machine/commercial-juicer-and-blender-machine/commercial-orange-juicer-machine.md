The equipment operates on a physical cold-press principle of **"gravity feeding + midline halving + spherical biomimetic rolling compression"**:

* **Automatic Continuous Feeding**: The top stainless steel storage basket holds several kilograms of fresh oranges, feeding them one by one into the cutting chamber via an inclined guide track.
* **Precision Centerline Halving**: Entering oranges are instantly halved by a vertical fixed razor-sharp blade, exposing the pulp of both halves.
* **Biomimetic Spherical Squeezing**: Opposing rolling balls with curved rims rotate in synchrony, pressing into the interior pulp to express juice and pulp fibers. Dedicated stainless peel scrapers strip the rinds, ejecting them into collection buckets on either side.
* **Gravity Sieve Juice Collection**: Raw juice flows through an inclined stainless micro-perforated filter plate, straining out seeds and coarse membrane fibers before pouring directly into collection pitchers below.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

To maintain electrical safety, prevent fruit jams, and ensure optimal juice flavor, strictly adhere to the following protocols:

### 2.1 Pre-Start Inspection & Installation Setup

1. **Placement & Clearance**: Place the machine on a level, sturdy, load-bearing countertop or display bar. Maintain at least **10 cm (4 inches)** of clearance around the rear and sides for airflow, ensuring clear pathways beneath both sides for the peel collection buckets.
2. **Component Assembly Check (Crucial Safety Protocol)**:
   * Verify the stainless steel halving blade is securely mounted onto the center shaft;
   * Ensure all 4 plastic squeezing balls are tightened with their knurled retaining nuts;
   * Install the peel scrapers, filter screen, and juice drip tray;
   * Seat the transparent front cover and hand-tighten the thumb screws on both sides. If the cover is misaligned, the magnetic safety switch will prevent the motor from starting.
3. **Electrical Specifications (⚠️ Crucial Rule)**:
   * Confirm the outlet voltage matches the machine rating plate (110V/60Hz or 220V/50Hz);
   * Connect directly to an independent, grounded wall outlet. Never share unrated power strips with high-draw equipment like commercial induction cookers or water boilers.

### 2.2 Fruit Selection & Operational Protocols

1. **Fruit Sizing & Selection (Optimal Extraction Key)**:
   * **Applicable Diameter Range**: Select citrus fruits with a diameter between **40 mm and 80 mm (1.6″ to 3.1″)**; standard 60–75 mm oranges yield the best results;
   * Never insert oversized fruit exceeding 85 mm to prevent fruit from wedging in the infeed chute;
   * Wash fruit thoroughly and discard soft, overripe, or spoiled fruit before juicing.
2. **Power-On & Juicing**:
   * Load clean, dry oranges along the top stainless feeder rail;
   * Turn on the green power switch (POWER ON); the machine will begin running with a quiet, smooth hum;
   * Oranges will automatically feed into the slicing blade, clean juice will pour from the dispensing spout, and rinds will eject into the side bins.
3. **Clearing Fruit Jams**:
   * If an abnormally dense or misshapen fruit stalls the rollers, **immediately press the red power switch (OFF)**;
   * Loosen the cover thumb screws, remove the front cover, and clear the jammed fruit using the included safety tool. Inspect the blade, re-seat the cover, and restart.

* **Daily Closing Cleaning Procedure (Mandatory)**:
  1. Turn off the power switch and disconnect the main power plug;
  2. Unscrew the front cover thumb knobs and remove the transparent front shield;
  3. Loosen the 4 retaining nuts and pull the 4 plastic squeezing balls straight off;
  4. Slide off the stainless blade assembly, pulp filter, and juice tray;
  5. Wash all removed plastic and stainless components in a sink using warm water and neutral food-grade detergent, rinse thoroughly, and air dry;
  6. Wipe down the stainless steel front housing with a damp microfiber cloth. (**Never spray high-pressure water directly against the motor chassis!**)
* **Drive Shaft Seal Care (Weekly Requirement)**:
  * After cleaning and drying the shafts, apply a small dab of **food-grade white mineral grease** around the rubber seals of the 4 drive shafts to keep the seals supple and maintain waterproof protection.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why does juice from this machine taste sweet without bitterness, while regular blenders produce bitter juice?**
  * **A**: Bitterness in orange juice comes from essential oils and limonin contained in the outer yellow peel. Blenders crush the entire peel and pith, releasing these oils into the juice. The JEM-2000E-2 halves the fruit and **presses only the internal pulp using spherical balls, while peel scrapers eject the intact rinds into waste bins**. Because the outer oil glands are never crushed, the juice remains clean, aromatic, and sweet.
* **Q: Why won't the machine turn on after installing the front cover?**
  * **A**: This indicates the **magnetic safety interlock has not fully engaged**:
    1. Verify the magnetic sensor on top of the transparent cover aligns directly with the sensor notch on the chassis;
    2. Ensure both side thumb screws are threaded down completely tight;
    3. Confirm both peel waste buckets are seated flush against the base. If any component is misaligned, the machine stays locked out to protect the operator.
* **Q: Can this machine juice lemons, pomegranates, or grapefruits?**
  * **A**:
    * **Lemons & Small Grapefruits**: Yes, as long as fruit diameter falls within 40–80 mm and the shape is relatively round;
    * **Pomegranates**: Not recommended. Pomegranate seeds are extremely hard and can scratch the plastic squeezing balls or release bitter tannins. The unit is optimized specifically for citrus fruits (oranges, lemons, limes, mandarins, and small grapefruits).
