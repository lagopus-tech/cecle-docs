### 1.2 Core Functions & Working Principle

The machine utilizes a heavy-duty cold-press mechanical process of **"progressive clearance compression + continuous self-feeding traction"**:

* **Self-Feeding Initial Crushing**: The primary textured roller teeth grip the stalk under high torque, instantly fracturing the rigid outer rind.
* **High-Pressure Progressive Extraction**: The secondary and tertiary precision rollers compress the sugarcane through progressively tighter gaps, rupturing the internal cell walls and pressing every drop of sucrose and mineral-rich juice from both nodes and pith.
* **Separation & Collection**: De-juiced, flattened bagasse flakes eject automatically through the rear discharge chute, while high-purity raw juice filters directly through the stainless sieve into collection pitchers or serving taps, achieving maximum yield in a single pass.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

To maintain operator safety, prevent jams, and prolong battery, roller, and bearing service life, strictly observe the following protocols:

### 2.1 Pre-Start Setup & Charging Safety

1. **Countertop Placement & Caster Braking**:
   * **Countertop Models (T-80)**: Place on a sturdy, level, weight-bearing folding table or bar counter, ensuring a dry and stable base.
   * **Floor Cart Models (SF-80)**: After wheeling into your stall position, **firmly depress the brake levers on all 4 swivel casters** to prevent the cart from shifting during feeding.
2. **Electrical Connection & Battery Charging (⚠️ Crucial Rule)**:
   * **AC Use**: Connect to an independent, grounded 110V/220V power receptacle.
   * **Battery Recharging**: After business hours, connect the included smart pulse charger to the charging port. The LED indicator turns red during charging and switches to green once fully charged (typically 6–8 hours). **Never use unapproved third-party chargers, and never store battery models in a completely discharged state!**
3. **Juice Catch Basin Check**: Place a clean food-grade pitcher beneath the dispensing spigot, ensure the valve seal is tightened, and verify the filter drawer is fully seated.

### 2.2 Feeding, Extraction & Jam Clearing

1. **Sugarcane Pre-Trimming (Essential Anti-Jam Protocol)**:
   * Select fresh sugarcane stalks with a diameter ≤ 45 mm (1.75 inches);
   * **Crucial Bevel Cut**: **Before feeding, always use a cleaver to trim the front root tip into an angled "duckbill wedge"**. This tapered leading edge allows the textured rollers to grab the cane instantly, preventing blunt-end resistance that can cause motor stall or breaker trips.
2. **Startup & Normal Extraction**:
   * Turn on the master power switch and toggle the direction switch to **"FORWARD / EXTRACT"**. The rollers will begin counter-rotating smoothly;
   * Once running quietly, hold the stalk by the rear end and gently insert the tapered tip into the infeed chute. Once the rollers bite, release your grip and let the machine draw it through.
3. **Emergency Jam Handling (Reverse Gear Clearance)**:
   * If a thick, fibrous node causes the rollers to stall, **immediately switch the toggle to "STOP"**;
   * Then flick the switch to **"REVERSE"**; the rollers will reverse rotation and back the stalk out of the infeed opening. Trim away the hard knot or split the cane lengthwise before resuming forward extraction.

**Safety Red Lines (⚠️ Strictly Prohibited)**:
* Rollers exert massive crushing pressure. **Never insert fingers, gloves, or rags past the infeed safety guard opening!**
* Never feed foreign objects such as wooden dowels, wires, metal bars, or rocks into the rollers to prevent damaging the teeth and gearbox.

### 2.4 End-of-Day Cleaning & Roller Maintenance

* **Quick Fresh Water Washdown (Mandatory at Closing)**:
  1. Turn off power and disconnect the plug (or power down battery switch);
  2. Pull out the bagasse chute and stainless filter screen, rinsing away fiber pulp under a tap;
  3. Direct a low-pressure water hose into the stainless roller chamber while scrubbing roller grooves with a long-handled soft nylon brush until runoff water is clear and bubble-free;
  4. Wipe dry with a clean cloth and allow to air-dry. (**Note: Never spray water directly into motor cooling vents, electrical toggles, or battery enclosures!**)
* **Roller Anti-Stick Lubrication (Monthly Requirement)**:
  * After cleaning and drying, wipe a light coat of **food-grade white mineral oil (anti-rust lubricating oil)** over the roller surfaces and drive chains to prevent sugar caramelization and keep rotation smooth.

* **Q: How do I choose between the T-80 (Countertop) and SF-80 (Floor Cart)?**
  * **A**: Match your selection to your **transport vehicle and vending environment**:
    * **Choose T-80 (Countertop)**: If you transport your setup in a standard passenger car, SUV, or compact van. Weighing ~60 kg, it is easily handled by two people and sits neatly on a folding table or counter.
    * **Choose SF-80 (Floor Cart)**: If you operate in pedestrian streets, night markets, or food plazas. Built-in heavy-duty casters and push handles allow single-person maneuvering, while the lower cabinet stores extra cane stalks, cups, and supplies.
* **Q: How long does the battery last on a full charge? What if I run out of power outdoors?**
  * **A**: The high-capacity battery pack provides continuous extraction for **400 to 600 standard sugarcane stalks** per charge—comfortably covering a 4–6 hour peak rush. Under normal traffic, it can operate for 1–2 days between charges. We highly recommend the **Dual-Use Hybrid model**, which runs on wall power when available and switches seamlessly to battery when off-grid.
* **Q: What should I do if a cane stalk jams the machine during operation?**
  * **A**: Jams occur primarily if stalks are too thick or inserted without a tapered wedge:
    1. **Stop Immediately**: Do not force the motor forward; switch the toggle to "STOP";
    2. **Reverse Out**: Flick the toggle to "REVERSE" briefly; the rollers will push the stalk back out;
    3. **Trim & Re-feed**: Chop the leading end into an angled wedge, switch back to "FORWARD", and resume smooth extraction.
