## 4. Shared Engineering & Quality Standards

Every machine in the CECLE commercial beverage series is manufactured according to strict food-safety and durability standards:

1. **Food-Grade Contact Certification:** Contact surfaces feature certified SUS304 acid-resistant stainless steel, BPA-free Tritan/PC, or food-safe POM polymers to ensure chemical safety and zero flavor transfer.
2. **Industrial Pure Copper Motors:** High-gauge pure copper winding provides sustained torque, thermal stability, and prolonged service lives far exceeding consumer-grade appliances.
3. **Safety Interlock Systems:** Every unit incorporates active safety mechanisms (interlock micro-switches, magnetic safety sensors, or mechanical reverse gears) to prevent accidental contact injuries.
4. **Worldwide Logistics & Support:** Supported by factory-direct door-to-door DDP sea and express air delivery options, a comprehensive **1-year factory warranty**, and immediate spare parts dispatch.
