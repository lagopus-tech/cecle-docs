## II. Production Flow & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

The following are the standard startup and floss-making operational specifications based on this series of commercial cotton candy machines:

**1. Pre-startup Preparation**

* Place the machine stably on a solid, dry countertop, ensuring the bottom suction cups are firmly attached.
* Check whether the stainless steel sugar bowl buckles are securely locked and ensure there is no foreign residue in the middle of the rotary spinner head.
* Connect to the corresponding voltage power supply (110V or 220V) and turn on the main power switch.

**2. Preheating & Feeding**

* **Preheating**: Turn on the heating switch, let the machine idle and preheat for about 2-3 minutes, and observe that the sugar spinner head rotates smoothly without abnormal noise.
* **Quantitative Sugar Addition**: Once the machine rotation is stable, use the dedicated stainless steel sugar scoop to take an appropriate amount of white granulated sugar or colored fruit-flavored sugar (about 15-20g) and pour it steadily into the central rotating dispensing head.

**3. Flossing & Shaping**

* Within seconds after adding sugar, fine white sugar threads will spray out from all around the spinner head.
* Hold a bamboo stick and rotate it horizontally at a constant speed clockwise or counter-clockwise inside the bowl. Once the sugar threads fill the stick, slightly tilt the stick and gradually pull it upward out of the bowl to make a full and fluffy cloud cotton candy.
* You can add different colors of flavored sugar powder in batches to create layered rainbow cotton candy or creative floral shapes.

**4. Shutdown Cleaning & Maintenance**

* After work is finished, **turn off the heating switch first**. While the motor is still rotating, pour a small amount of clean water into the rotary head. Utilize the centrifugal force and residual heat to automatically flush out and discharge the residual sugar in the spinner.
* Wait until the spinner head stops spraying water mist, then cut off the main power supply and wait for the machine to cool down naturally.
* Unfasten the buckles, remove the stainless steel bowl, wash it clean with warm water, wipe it dry, and put it back on the machine body.
* **WARNING: It is strictly forbidden to submerge the main machine body in water for rinsing. The electrical base can only be wiped with a slightly damp cloth!**

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: How should I choose between the TD-530 and TD-550?**
  * **A**: If you are running a car-trunk stall, frequently changing locations, and have high requirements for portability and space-saving, the **TD-530** (compact, 2kg lighter) is recommended. If you are operating in a fixed shop, large amusement park, or pedestrian main street with massive foot traffic, and you pursue higher visual appeal and an anti-flying sugar experience, the **TD-550** with a larger bowl is recommended.
* **Q: Can I use regular white granulated sugar with this machine, or do I have to use special sugar?**
  * **A**: Commercially available coarse-grained white granulated sugar, rock sugar granules, or various dedicated colored fruit hard candies can be used. Coarse sugar yields better flossing results when heated, is less prone to sticking, and has a significant cost advantage.
* **Q: What should I do if the sugar outlet becomes clogged or stops producing floss?**
  * **A**: This is usually caused by residual sugar carbonizing and clogging the holes because the water cleaning process was skipped before shutting down. After turning on the machine and preheating, slowly drip a small amount of hot water into the center of the spinner head, letting the high-temperature steam naturally soften the sugar scale to restore flow. If severely clogged, you can remove the spinner head and thoroughly soak and wash it in hot water.
