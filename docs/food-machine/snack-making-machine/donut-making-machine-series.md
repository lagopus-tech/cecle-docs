**2. Core Process and Working Principle**

The system breaks through the tedious process of traditional manual batter squeezing and long-term manual guarding in front of the frying pan for flipping, adopting industry-leading automated frying assembly line technology.

* **Smooth Feeding and Quantitative Cutting**: Pour the prepared liquid batter into the upper hopper, and the cutter at the bottom of the machine will continuously and accurately cut the batter rings according to the set frequency, allowing them to fall smoothly into the high-temperature oil tank below.
* **Automatic Frying and Intelligent Flipping**: After the batter falls into the constant-temperature oil tank, it will slowly float forward driven by the internal mechanical paddles. When one side is fried to a golden set shape, the paddle device will automatically flip it to ensure even heating on both sides. Finally, it is automatically pushed out at the end and enters the oil drain tray.

## II. Production Process and Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Below is a guide to core operations and parameter settings based on actual machine debugging:

**1. Pre-startup Preparation**

* Check whether the oil drain valve at the bottom of the machine's oil tank is completely closed.
* Pour in an appropriate amount of special shortening or soybean oil, noting that the oil level must reach between the minimum and maximum level marks on the equipment.
* Set the required frying temperature and turn on the heating switch to preheat (usually preheated to 170°C - 190°C).

**2. Feeding and Operation**

* **Batter Pre-treatment**: Pour the prepared batter (neither too thin nor too thick, requiring good fluidity) into the conical stainless steel hopper at the top.
* **Start Dropping**: Once the oil temperature reaches the set value and the indicator light is on, turn on the automatic dropping switch. The forming valve will begin regular operation, cutting off the batter rings and dropping them into the fryer.

**3. Forming and Collection**

* **Automatic Flipping Observation**: Observe the first few fried donuts and check if their color is uniform. If the color is too dark, slightly lower the temperature or speed up the conveyor chain.
* The machine will automatically complete the process of bottom heating, mid-stage flipping, and thorough frying on both sides of the batter rings.
* The finished products will automatically fall along the end slide into the receiving tray. Employees can sprinkle powdered sugar while hot or proceed to the chocolate glazing process.

**4. Shutdown Cleaning and Maintenance**

* After work, be sure to turn off the heating switch and cut off the main power/close the gas valve.
* Wait for the hot oil to naturally cool down completely to a safe temperature, then open the bottom oil drain valve to discharge and recycle the filtered waste oil.
* Disassemble the hopper and internal paddles, wash with neutral detergent and warm water, dry them, and then reassemble.
* **WARNING: It is strictly forbidden to perform washing operations without draining the hot oil or powering off the machine!**
