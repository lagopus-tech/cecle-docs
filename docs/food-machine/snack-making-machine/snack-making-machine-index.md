  2. **Preheat & Load Meat**: Preheat to 180°C–200°C. Temporarily pause carousel rotation, open the doors with heat-resistant gloves, and hang glazed, air-dried poultry evenly using double-prong hooks.
  3. **Thermostatic Roasting**: Re-engage rotation and roast at 200°C–240°C for 40–55 minutes. Turn on the internal display lighting prior to completion to inspect browning and showcase the poultry to customers.
  4. **Grease Discharge & Cleanup**: Allow the unit to cool down below 50°C. Open the external drainage ball valve to discharge the accumulated oil-water mixture into a waste container, then wipe interior surfaces with a soft damp cloth.

  ### Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Summary
  1. **Preheating Rollers**: Switch on roller rotation. Set both thermostats to 150°C–180°C and preheat for 5–8 minutes. Lightly brush rollers with edible oil to prevent initial sticking.
  2. **Placing & Roasting**: Place thawed sausages horizontally between the roller grooves. Allow them to tumble continuously for 10–15 minutes until plump, sizzling, and golden.
  3. **Dual-Zone Holding**: Move cooked sausages to the front rows and lower the front thermostat to 60°C–80°C. Load fresh sausages into the rear rows on high heat for continuous service.
  4. **Slide-Out Drip Tray Cleaning**: Turn off the power at closing. Wipe down warm rollers using a soft damp cloth along the roller axes. Pull out the bottom stainless steel drip tray, empty grease, wash, and reinstall.
