## II. Production Flow and Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Below are the standard start-up, roasting, and cleaning/maintenance procedures based on this series of electric sausage roasters:

**1. Pre-startup Preparation**

* Place the machine steadily on a level and heat-resistant operating countertop, leaving at least 10cm of heat dissipation space around it.
* Check if the curved glass cover is securely assembled and if the bottom pull-out drip tray is pushed firmly into place.
* Connect to a power outlet that meets the power requirements and ensure good grounding protection.

**2. Preheating and Loading**

* **Preheating**: Turn on the rotary switch to let the rollers start rotating. Turn the temperature control knobs clockwise to set the front and rear temperatures to 150°C - 180°C, and preheat for about 5-8 minutes.
* **Placing Sausages**: Once the surface is slightly hot, you can lightly brush a thin layer of cooking oil on the rollers to prevent sticking. Then, evenly place the chilled or thawed sausages horizontally in the grooves between the rollers.

**3. Rolling Roasting and Warming**

* Sausages tumble and heat up on the rotating rollers, usually roasting for 10-15 minutes to reach a golden-brown, juicy, and fully cooked state.
* **Zoned Warming Operation**: Turn the rear row temperature control down to the 60°C - 80°C warming zone, and keep the front row temperature high to roast newly added sausages. This ensures customers can always get piping hot food while avoiding over-roasting which can cause casings to burst and dry out.

**4. Shutdown Cleanup and Maintenance**

* At the end of business hours, first reset the front and rear temperature control knobs to zero, turn off the main power, and unplug the power cord.
* While the rollers still have some residual heat, use a soft damp cloth with a neutral detergent to wipe cleanly from both ends inwards along the rollers (**WARNING: Do not use steel wool to scrub, as it may scratch the surface**).
* Pull out the bottom drip tray, dump the waste oil, wash it thoroughly, dry it, and put it back in place.
* **WARNING: It is strictly forbidden to spray water directly with a hose or flush the entire machine with water!**

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: How should I choose between TD-7 and TD-10?**
  * **A**: The core of selection lies in the countertop depth space and customer flow. If your operating counter depth is narrow (like a regular convenience store checkout), the **TD-7** is recommended (only 330mm wide, lightweight and space-saving); if you are in cinemas, amusement parks, or scenic spots with explosive concentrated foot traffic, and need to simultaneously roast and display multiple varieties (such as pure meat sausages, black pepper sausages, corn sausages), the **TD-10**, which has nearly 50% more capacity, is highly recommended.
* **Q: What should I do if the sausages don't rotate or slip on the rollers?**
  * **A**: If it's a sausage just taken out of the freezer, the icy surface might be slippery. It is recommended to let it thaw slightly or brush a tiny bit of cooking oil on the rollers to increase friction so it can grip and rotate normally. Additionally, please regularly check if the gears at the end of the rollers are meshed tightly.
* **Q: Can it be used to roast food other than sausages?**
  * **A**: Yes. Any cooked food that is cylindrical and can roll smoothly between the axes can be made, such as hot dogs, meat rolls, fish tofu sticks, glutinous rice sausages, small corn cobs, etc. The application scenarios are highly diverse.
