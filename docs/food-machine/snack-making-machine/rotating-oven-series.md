## II. Production Flow & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

The following outlines standard startup, roasting, and maintenance protocols for the 850 Series Commercial Rotary Roaster:

**1. Pre-Operation Preparation**

* Roll the machine onto a flat, well-ventilated surface and engage the caster wheel brakes.
* Check that the internal hanging rack and top drive couplings are securely locked. Pour a shallow layer of clean water into the bottom drip pan (the water seal traps drippings, prevents grease from scorching, and minimizes smoke).
* **Electric Model**: Verify secure connection to a dedicated 380V industrial power line. **Gas Model**: Connect the 220V auxiliary cord, connect the gas line and pressure regulator, and perform a leak check on all gas fittings.

**2. Preheating & Loading**

* **Preheating**: Turn on the heating switch (or ignite the gas burners) and set the thermostat to 180°C–200°C. Allow the empty roaster to preheat for 8–10 minutes to stabilize the thermal chamber.
* **Loading Meat**: Temporarily pause the rotation switch. Wearing heat-resistant safety gloves, open the glass door and hang the prepped, sugar-glazed, and air-dried ducks or chickens evenly onto the top carousel using stainless steel double-prong hooks. Ensure adequate spacing between carcasses.

**3. Roasting & Temperature Control**

* Close and securely latch the glass door. Switch on both the carousel rotation motor and heating systems.
* Roast at 200°C–240°C for 40–55 minutes depending on the weight and type of poultry. Monitor skin caramelization and fat dripping through the 360° glass panels, adjusting the thermostat as needed during the final stages.
* Turn on the internal spotlight 5 minutes prior to completion to inspect color uniformity and showcase finished items to storefront patrons.

**4. Shutdown, Cleaning & Maintenance**

* When roasting is complete, **turn off the heating system first**. With the ventilation/lighting remaining on, open the door with thermal gloves and unload the poultry into holding warmers.
* Allow the chamber to cool to a safe handling temperature (below 50°C), place a collection bucket under the drain valve, and open the ball valve to discharge the grease-water mixture.
* Clean interior stainless steel surfaces and glass panels using warm water and a neutral degreaser with a soft microfiber cloth. Never use steel wool or abrasive scourers on the tempered glass.
* **WARNING: Never spray pressurized water hoses directly at the top motor housing, electrical control box, or gas control valves to prevent short circuits and component failure!**

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: How should I choose between the TD-850 (Electricity) and TD-850 (Gas)?**
  * **A**: Both models share identical external dimensions (850 × 1000 × 1610 mm) and batch capacities. If your facility is located inside a modern commercial mall, office building atrium, or any space with strict zero-open-flame fire codes and has 380V three-phase power available, select the **TD-850 (Electricity)**. If your shop is a street-side deli, outdoor food stall, or has limited electrical capacity (cannot support 12KW electric load) with only standard 220V outlets, choose the **TD-850 (Gas)** for rapid heat output and minimal power draw.
* **Q: What is the advantage of having central and side heating elements/burners operating together?**
  * **A**: Conventional roasting cabinets generally rely on unidirectional heat, often leaving the internal carcass undercooked while scorching the exterior facing the heat source. The TD-850 series uses a coordinated "central + side" dual heat source architecture paired with a 360° continuous rotation system. This forms an enveloping thermal convection zone, ensuring uniform caramelization and consistent core cooking for every single bird.
* **Q: With high-volume daily roasting, is grease removal complicated?**
  * **A**: Not at all. The base features a funnel-shaped stainless steel drainage pan connected to an external ball valve. Adding a shallow layer of water before cooking stops falling grease from burning and smoking. At the end of the shift, simply place a waste receptacle under the valve and turn the lever to drain the pan completely.
