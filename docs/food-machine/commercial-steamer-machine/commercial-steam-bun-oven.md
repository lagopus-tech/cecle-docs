## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To maintain optimal food quality, protect heating elements against dry burning, and extend equipment life, please follow these guidelines:

### 1. Pre-Startup Inspection & Connection

1. **Level Placement**: Place the machine on a flat, dry, heat-resistant floor in a well-ventilated area (ensure adequate exhaust hood ventilation for gas units).
2. **Gas & Power Verification**:
   * **Gas Model**: Confirm whether the unit is configured for **LPG** or **Natural Gas (NG)**. Never mix fuel types! Use a certified low-pressure regulator and oil-resistant high-pressure hose. Secure all clamps and conduct a soap-bubble test on all fittings to verify that there are zero leaks.
   * **Electric Model**: Verify that electrical lines meet required amp capacity. Never use power strips. Connect directly to a dedicated **220V or 380V air circuit breaker** with reliable grounding.
3. **Water Supply**: Open the inlet valve to fill the water tank up to the designated water-level gauge (or ensure the automatic ball-float valve is properly connected and operating). **Never ignite or heat the unit while the tank is dry!**

### 2. Ignition & Preheating

* **Gas Ignition**: Open the main supply valve, slightly open the control valve, and ignite the burner using an extended electronic lighter. Adjust the air damper until the flame burns steady and pure blue (if yellow flame or smoke occurs, increase damper opening).
* **Electric Heating**: Switch on the dedicated breaker, turn the temperature/heating dial, and confirm that the heating indicator turns on to engage full load.
* **Preheating**: Cover the top or place the initial steamer base on the hole, preheating for 3–5 minutes until dense white steam erupts steadily from the vents.

### 3. Standard Steaming Operations

1. **Loading**: Once steam is active, stack the loaded baskets in sequence. For rice noodle rolls or cold skin, slide the batter-coated flat tray securely onto the steam rack.
2. **Steaming Times**:
   * **Soup Dumplings / Dim Sum / Shaomai**: High steam for **6 to 8 minutes**;
   * **Large Meat Buns / Steamed Bread**: Medium-high steam for **12 to 15 minutes**;
   * **Rice Noodle Rolls / Cold Skin**: High steam rapid cook for **60 to 90 seconds** (remove when batter bubbles up translucently).
3. **Unloading & Reloading**: Wear heat-resistant gloves, transfer cooked baskets to the holding area, and immediately place fresh dough trays onto the steamer.
4. **Water Level Monitoring**: During peak continuous operation, check the water level gauge and float valve regularly to maintain sufficient water depth and prevent dry-burn element damage.

### 4. Shutdown, Cleaning & Descaling Maintenance

* **Shutdown**: Turn off the gas valve (or cut main electrical power). Allow residual steam to disperse before opening the drain valve to discharge wastewater.
* **Surface Cleaning**: Clean tabletop surfaces and rims using warm water and a neutral food-grade detergent; wipe dry with a soft cloth. **Never spray high-pressure water directly into the electrical control enclosure or gas burner compartment!**
* **Routine Descaling (Critical)**: In hard water environments, scale buildup insulates heating pipes, which can cause heat-pipe rupture. **Every 7 to 15 days**, soak and flush the tank with food-grade citric acid or commercial descaler to clear mineral deposits and maintain 95%+ thermal transfer efficiency.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Should I choose the Gas model or the Electric model for my breakfast shop?**
  * **A**: This depends on your site infrastructure:
    1. **Choose Gas**: If you operate a street-level restaurant, mobile food truck, night-market stall, or a venue lacking 380V three-phase industrial power. Gas runs independently with standard LPG tanks without requiring blowers or cords.
    2. **Choose Electric**: If you operate in shopping malls, subway plazas, or commercial office basements where open flames are strictly prohibited, and where dedicated 380V/220V 9 kW electrical service is available.
* **Q: How should I choose between Single Hole (BO-1), Dual Holes (BO-2), and Triple Holes (BO-3)?**
  * **A**: Base your choice on your primary menu items:
    * **Large Steamed Buns, Bread & Mantou**: Choose **BO-1 (38 cm Large Aperture)**. It accommodates 40–70 cm stacked large bamboo baskets, producing hundreds of items per batch;
    * **Soup Dumplings (Xiaolongbao), Dim Sum & Stew Cups**: Choose **BO-2 (Dual)** or **BO-3 (Triple)**. Individual openings allow on-demand batch steaming without losing steam across the whole unit.
* **Q: Can this machine steam Cantonese rice noodle rolls and cold skin (Liangpi) as well?**
  * **A**: Yes. The unit is designed specifically with "Rice Noodle Roll" and "Cold Skin" production in mind. By placing flat square roll drawers or circular cold skin trays over the apertures, the dense saturated steam cooks batter into elastic, tender sheets in just 60 to 90 seconds.
