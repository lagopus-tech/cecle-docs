> 💡 **Series Overview:** The **CECLE Commercial Food Steaming Equipment Lineup** provides specialized, high-capacity thermal steaming solutions engineered for institutional canteens, commercial catering kitchens, breakfast diners, and dim sum restaurants. While the SRC-12 focuses on high-volume batch staples (steamed rice, bulk dim sum, and proteins), the BO Series excels at continuous front-of-house service for stacked bamboo baskets, Cantonese rice noodle rolls, and fresh buns.

## 2. In-Depth Model Profiles

### Model A: SRC-12 Commercial 12-Tray Rice Steamer Cabinet
* **Core Identity:** The High-Volume Staple Food & Catering Workhorse
* **Key Mechanism:** A heavy-gauge double-walled cabinet insulated with high-density polyurethane foam. Micro-pressure saturated steam circulates across 12 horizontal tiers to cook dry grains, proteins, or pastries simultaneously under progressive door-lever pressure.

* **Best For:** Enterprise & university canteens, hotel banquet halls, catering delivery centers, and frozen food central commissaries.
* **Core Strengths:**
  * Massive output: Cooks 45–50 kg of fluffy rice in 35–45 minutes, satisfying 250–350 diners in a single batch;
  * Outstanding thermal efficiency: Polyurethane foam keeps food hot for 4–6 hours after power-off, slashing reheating overhead;
  * Automated safety: Float valve auto-refill, low-water dry-burn cutoff, and mechanical pressure-relief valves ensure fail-safe operation.

* **Core Identity:** The Fast-Service Front-Counter Dim Sum & Noodle Roll Station
* **Key Mechanism:** An immersion heat exchanger where water directly envelopes the combustion flues/heating pipes. Delivers intense, continuous steam through standardized top openings (Single 38 cm, Dual 13 cm, or Triple 13 cm apertures) to support live customer service.

## 3. Buyer's Selection & Decision Matrix

Match your primary menu requirements, kitchen space, and service model to the right steaming system:### Quick Selection Guidelines:
1. **Choose the SRC-12 Cabinet if:** Your operation revolves around serving fixed-time bulk meals (such as lunch rushes at schools, corporate canteens, or central delivery kitchens) where standardized large trays of rice, meats, and seafood must be prepared simultaneously.
2. **Choose the BO Steam Bun Oven if:** You operate an open-display breakfast shop or street counter where customers order individual baskets of dim sum, buns, soup dumplings, or freshly steamed Cantonese rice noodle rolls throughout morning service.

## 4. Shared Engineering & Quality Standards

Every steaming machine in the CECLE food processing lineup is engineered to meet rigorous commercial foodservice benchmarks:

1. **Food-Grade Stainless Steel Build:** Heavy-gauge food-safe stainless steel shells and internal water tanks repel waterborne minerals, acids, and grease for effortless daily washdowns.
2. **Dual-Energy Flexibility:** Both lines offer interchangeable configurations for **Commercial Electric (220V/380V industrial heating elements)** or **Gas Operation (LPG bottled gas or pipeline Natural Gas)**.
3. **Automated Water Management:** Integrated mechanical brass float valves maintain stable water levels, preventing dry-burn heating failures during peak cooking periods.
4. **Worldwide Logistics & Warranty:** Packed in fumigation-free reinforced export wooden crates, supported by door-to-door DDP ocean freight, a **1-year factory warranty**, and direct video engineering support.
