## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To guarantee consistent steaming results, prevent high-temperature steam burns, and maximize component service life, strictly observe the following steps:

### 1. Pre-Startup Environment & Utility Verification

1. **Level Placement**: Position the cabinet on a solid, level floor with at least 15 cm of clearance behind the unit for ventilation. Keep the area free of flammable or volatile substances.
2. **Water Supply Connection**: Connect the water inlet to your potable water supply line and open the inlet valve. Confirm that the float valve automatically stops filling once the reservoir is full (water must cover heating elements by at least 3 cm). Ensure the manual bottom drain ball valve is firmly closed.
3. **Power / Gas Verification**:
   * **Electric Model**: Must be connected to a dedicated **380V Three-Phase (or 220V Single-Phase) circuit breaker** sized for a 12 kW load with a secure ground connection. Never use temporary extension cables.
   * **Gas Model**: Confirm that the gas supply matches the machine's rating plate (**LPG 2.8 kPa** or **Natural Gas 2.0 kPa**). Use certified high-pressure gas hoses secured with heavy-duty clamps and a matching low-pressure regulator. Perform a soap-bubble leak check on all joints before initial ignition.

### 2. Food Preparation & Loading Standards

1. **Steamed Rice Specifications**:
   * Spread washed raw rice evenly across each tray, keeping the batch to **approx. 3.0–3.5 kg of dry rice per tray**;
   * Add clean water at an approximate **rice-to-water ratio of 1 : 1.2 to 1 : 1.3** (water depth roughly half a finger-knuckle above the rice surface);
   * Slide each tray horizontally along the guide rails until fully seated, ensuring trays are not tilted or misaligned.
2. **Steamed Buns & Pastry Specifications**:
   * Lightly brush the tray with cooking oil or use food-grade silicone steaming mats;
   * Leave 2–3 cm of space between dough portions to allow for natural expansion during steaming without sticking.

### 3. Standardized Steaming Operation

1. **Door Latching**: Turn the safety door handle clockwise and pull tight, verifying that the silicone gasket makes complete, flush contact with the cabinet rim.
2. **Startup**:
   * **Electric Model**: Switch on the main electrical breaker, then engage the cabinet power switch; the heating indicator will illuminate, initiating full-power heating;
   * **Gas Model**: Open the main gas supply valve, trigger the ignition switch, inspect the flame through the viewing port, and adjust the air damper until a stable, pure blue flame is achieved.
3. **Reference Cooking Times**:
   * **Steamed Rice (Full 12 Trays)**: Approx. **35 to 45 minutes**;
   * **Soup Dumplings (Xiaolongbao), Dumplings, Shaomai**: Approx. **15 to 20 minutes**;
   * **Large Buns (Baozi), Mantou, Flower Rolls**: Approx. **25 to 30 minutes**;
   * **Pork Ribs, Chicken, Large Meat Cuts**: Approx. **35 to 50 minutes**;
   * **Fresh Seafood (Whole Fish, Shrimp, Crab)**: Approx. **10 to 18 minutes**.
4. **Safety Protocols for Opening Doors (Critical Caution ⚠️)**:
   * When cooking time is complete, shut off the power or gas supply first;
   * **Never stand directly in front of the door! Stand strictly to the hinge side (behind the door swing path)**;
   * Unlatch the handle halfway to release the primary seal, allowing pressurized steam to vent upward and away through the gap for 10–15 seconds;
   * Only open the door fully once heavy steam clouds have dispersed, and always wear heat-resistant thermal gloves when sliding out trays.

### 4. Cleaning, Shutdown & Descaling Maintenance

* **Daily Draining**: After daily service and once the water in the reservoir cools to room temperature, open the bottom drain valve to empty dirty water and food debris, then flush the basin with fresh water.
* **Gasket Care**: Wipe the silicone door gasket with a damp, soft cloth to remove grease and starch residues. Keep the door unlatched or slightly ajar during non-operating hours to prevent gasket compression fatigue.
* **Periodic Descaling (Essential Maintenance)**: Mineral scale deposits insulate heating elements and burners, leading to premature element burnout and inefficient heat transfer. **Every 7 to 15 days**, dissolve 200–500 g of food-grade citric acid in the reservoir, heat to a gentle simmer for 20 minutes, let soak for 2 hours, then drain and flush thoroughly to keep heating elements scale-free.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Should I choose the Electric model or the Gas model for a 12-tray steamer?**
  * **A**: Make your decision based on your kitchen utility infrastructure:
    1. **Electric Model**: Best for enclosed commercial malls, office basement food courts, and school cafeterias where open flames or gas bottles are restricted, provided your electrical panel supports a 12 kW electrical load (preferably 380V 3-phase).
    2. **Gas Model**: Ideal for street-front restaurants, standalone industrial canteens, or regions where commercial electricity rates are high and high-power connections are difficult to install. Gas heats up quickly and offers lower operating fuel costs.
* **Q: Can this 12-tray cabinet steam rice and buns/pastries at the same time?**
  * **A**: Yes. The multi-tier design allows for mixed steaming. Place rice trays (which require more water and longer cooking times) in the lower and middle tiers, and place faster-cooking buns, dumplings, or dim sum in the upper tiers so they can be removed early when finished.
* **Q: Why does condensation sometimes drip onto cooked rice or buns?**
  * **A**: This is caused by natural steam condensation inside a sealed saturated environment. To minimize dripping, ensure the door handle is fully locked to create balanced micro-pressure circulation. When steaming delicate pastries, use perforated trays with breathable silicone mats so condensation flows naturally down to the drain channels rather than pooling on food surfaces.
