### 2. Core Working Principle

The unit operates on high-speed frequency-driven centrifugal separation and gravity drainage principles:

* **Ingredient Loading:** Washed leafy vegetables, shredded/diced vegetables, or freshly fried hot foods are loaded into an optional food-grade mesh liner or placed directly into the perforated stainless-steel spinning basket.
* **Smooth Centrifugal Acceleration:** Close the top lid and start the machine. The VFD system accelerates the basket smoothly to the preset speed. Under immense centrifugal force, free surface moisture or hot oil droplets are instantly propelled outward through the basket perforations against the outer casing wall.
* **Drainage Discharge & Automatic Braking:** Discharged liquid runs down the interior wall and exits through the bottom drain spout. Once the preset operating time expires (typically just **30–90 seconds**), power disengages and electromagnetic assisted braking brings the basket to a smooth, complete stop.
* **Preserving Texture and Freshness:** Uniform centrifugal force ensures vegetables emerge crisp, fresh, and free from crushing or wilting. Fried foods lose excess surface grease, emerging crispier, lighter, and with a significantly longer shelf life.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) & Production Workflow

Follow these operational guidelines to maximize dewatering/deoiling efficiency, maintain mechanical balance, and ensure safety:

### 1. Ingredient Preparation Requirements

* **Even Loading & Dynamic Balance (Crucial for Success):**
  * When placing ingredients into the basket, **spread them evenly across the perimeter of the basket wall. Never pile everything onto one side!**
  * Even distribution is vital to maintain smooth dynamic balance at high speeds and avoid violent chassis vibration.
* **Capacity Limit:** Do not load produce above the upper rim of the spinning basket; leave adequate clearance for free centrifugal motion.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Setup

1. Place the machine on a flat, dry, level, and solid floor, ensuring all rubber anti-vibration feet make balanced contact with the ground.
2. Check the power cord and plug for damage, and connect to a dedicated, grounded **110V or 220V outlet**.
3. Check the inner basket for loose hardware or foreign objects, and spin the basket by hand to confirm smooth rotation without rubbing noises.
4. Place a collection bucket or heat-resistant oil bin directly below the side drain port.

### 3. Standard Dewatering / Deoiling Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Load and Close Lid:** Distribute ingredients evenly inside the inner basket and securely close the safety lid.
2. **Set Timer & Speed:**
   * **Leafy Greens Dewatering:** Use low-to-medium speed with a timer setting of **30–45 seconds** (prevents high-speed tearing of tender leaves).
   * **Vegetable Fillings / Diced Root Produce:** Use medium-to-high speed with a timer setting of **45–60 seconds**.
   * **Fried Food Deoiling:** Load immediately while foods are still hot out of the fryer, spin at medium-to-high speed for **30–60 seconds** (hot oil flows and extracts most easily).
3. **Start the Cycle:** Press the start button. The VFD smoothly ramps up speed. Monitor liquid extraction from the drain port.
4. **Stop and Unload:** When the timer finishes, the basket stops spinning smoothly under assisted braking. Open the lid and remove the processed food.
5. **Safety Rule:** **NEVER force open the lid or reach inside the spinning basket while it is rotating!**

### 4. Cleaning & Routine Maintenance

* **Post-Shift Cleanup:** Unplug the power cable. Rinse the inner basket thoroughly using fresh water, mild food-grade detergent, and a soft brush (especially after deoiling hot fried foods with warm soapy water). Effluent drains through the bottom spout.
* **Electrical Protection:** Wipe the exterior clean with a damp cloth. **NEVER spray high-pressure water directly into motor vents, louvers, or the electrical control panel!**
* **Routine Checks:** Periodically check spindle bearings for smooth rotation and verify the tightness of shock-absorbing mounts to ensure ongoing quiet operation.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Can this machine handle both vegetable dewatering and fried food deoiling without cross-flavor contamination?**
  * **A:** Yes, absolutely. The SC Series is engineered specifically for dual-purpose dewatering and deoiling. The entire chassis and inner basket are manufactured from premium food-grade SUS304 stainless steel with smooth, non-porous surfaces that do not absorb oils or odors. After completing a fried food deoiling run, simply flush the basket with warm soapy water, rinse clean, and wipe dry. You can immediately switch to vegetable dewatering with zero odor transfer.
* **Q: Will high-speed centrifugal dewatering crush or tear delicate leafy greens like lettuce and spinach?**
  * **A:** No. The unit features precise frequency conversion technology. For tender greens, operators can lower the rotational speed and set the timer to around 30 seconds. The gentle, uniform centrifugal force removes only surface water droplets without damaging delicate plant cell walls, leaving greens crisp, fresh, and whole.
* **Q: Why does the unit vibrate slightly on startup? How do I ensure maximum running stability?**
  * **A:** Any high-speed centrifugal equipment relies on proper rotational balance. **Avoid loading everything on one side; spread produce evenly around the basket's inner perimeter.** The machine is built with an industrial suspended shock-absorption system. As long as ingredients are distributed relatively evenly, the machine runs smoothly, stably, and quietly once up to speed.
