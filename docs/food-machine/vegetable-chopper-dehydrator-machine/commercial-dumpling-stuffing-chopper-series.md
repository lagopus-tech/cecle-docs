### 2. Core Working Principle

The machine functions through synchronized differential relative motion:

* **Rotary Bowl Conveyance:** Produce (cabbage leaves, celery, onions, carrots, pre-ground meat) is loaded into the **60 cm rotating bowl**. As the bowl slowly rotates, it continuously guides the produce into the cutting zone beneath the safety hood.
* **High-Speed Shearing:** High-velocity rotary curved knives spin perpendicularly across the moving bowl bed, slicing through the ingredients hundreds of times per minute at clean, optimized cutting angles.
* **Uniform Stuffing Formation:** Due to the relative motion, every part of the ingredient batch passes through the knife set evenly. Within **1–3 minutes**, the produce reaches the desired granular consistency (from coarse diced bits to fine dumpling stuffing) without turning into an unappetizing watery mush.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) & Production Workflow

Follow these standardized procedures to maintain peak chopping performance, ensure operator safety, and prolong blade sharpness:

### 1. Ingredient Preparation Requirements

* **Washing & Drainage:** Wash all vegetables thoroughly. Ensure excess surface wash water is drained (using a centrifugal dehydrator such as the SC Series is recommended) prior to chopping to prevent watery fillings.
* **Pre-Cutting Oversized Ingredients:** Quarter whole cabbages or large root vegetables so they feed smoothly beneath the knife hood.
* **Strict Foreign Object Screening:** Inspect ingredients carefully to remove hard bones, stone fragments, toothpicks, or metal debris that could nick or dull the precision blades.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Setup

1. Place the machine on a flat, solid, level floor. Ensure all four base feet make solid, balanced contact with the floor.
2. Verify that the power cord and plug are intact, and connect to a dedicated **220V or 380V grounded outlet**.
3. Lower the safety cover and verify that the bowl and blade assembly rotate freely without interference or abnormal friction.
4. Prepare clean food-grade collection bins and plastic scrapers.

### 3. Step-by-Step Chopping Operation

1. **Load Produce into the Bowl:** Place the prepared vegetables into the 60 cm stainless steel bowl (do not overload beyond the bowl rim).
2. **Lower Safety Hood & Start:** Close the safety lid securely and press the start button.
3. **Monitor Granule Size:** Observe the chopping progress through the safety hood. Chopping typically takes only **60 to 120 seconds** per batch:
   * **Coarser Cut (e.g., Cabbage rolls, potstickers):** Stop after approx. 60–90 seconds.
   * **Finer Stuffing (e.g., Steamed buns, delicate dumplings):** Run for approx. 120–180 seconds.
4. **Shutdown & Unload:** Press the stop switch. Once the blades and bowl come to a complete standstill, lift the cover and unload the finished stuffing using a food-grade scraper or scoop.
5. **Safety Rule:** **NEVER reach into the rotating pot with hands, scrapers, or spoons while the machine is running! Always stop the machine before unloading.**

### 4. Cleaning & Routine Maintenance

* **Disconnect Power:** Always unplug the electrical supply before performing any cleaning or maintenance.
* **Wash the Pot & Blade Assembly:** Wipe and rinse the stainless steel pot, blades, and inner safety cover with warm water, mild food-grade detergent, and a soft brush. Wipe dry with a clean cloth.
* **Keep Motor Housing Dry:** Wipe the outer stainless steel casing with a damp towel. **NEVER spray pressurized water directly into the lower motor compartment, ventilation louvers, or power switches!**
* **Lubrication Maintenance:** Periodically check gearbox oil levels and grease transmission bearings to ensure ongoing quiet, durable operation.

  * **A:** No. Unlike conventional blenders or vertical blade food processors that puree ingredients through violent impact, the CP-30 utilizes the relative motion of the rotating bowl and razor-sharp curved shearing blades. It neatly slices through fibers with minimal cellular crushing, keeping internal juices locked inside each vegetable morsel.
* **Q: Can this machine mix meat and vegetables together at the same time?**
  * **A:** Yes. You can load pre-ground meat along with vegetables, seasoning, and oil directly into the rotating pot. The synchronized rotation of the bowl and blades chops the vegetables while thoroughly blending them with the meat, delivering an evenly integrated, ready-to-wrap filling.
* **Q: How difficult is it to clean the 60 cm stainless steel pot and blades?**
