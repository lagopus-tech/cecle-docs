## II. Production Process and Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Below is the core operation and parameter setting guide based on real machine startup and debugging:

### 1. Material Feeding and Preliminary Preparation

* First, add the prepared dough into the dough hopper (Note: For ABM-2600, the recommended flour-to-water ratio is flour:water=2:1).
* Add the prepared filling (requires granules to be crushed and have some stickiness) into the filling storage hopper.

### 2. No-Load and Discharge Testing

* **Filling and Dough Feeding Test**: Turn on the dough feeding and filling feeding switches independently to observe the extrusion of the dough tube and filling. Speeds are adjustable; it is recommended to slow down the speed during initial testing.
* **Confirm Status**: Once the dough tube is smoothly extruded with uniform thickness, and the filling is evenly discharged from the center tube, you can stop and prepare for combined forming.

### 3. Synchronous Forming and Parameter Matching

* Guide the dough tube with filling into the forming cutter block area below.
* **Start Forming**: Turn on the forming switch, and the cutter disk will automatically close, cut, and pleat the dough tube.
* **Fine-Tuning Tips**:
  * By adjusting the ratios between the dough feeding speed, filling feeding speed, and forming cutter speed, you can control the final weight and skin-to-filling ratio of the baozi.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: How to adjust for producing different sizes of baozi with ABM-2600 and ABM-3000?**
  * **A**: Both models support a wide forming weight range from 20g to 180g. You need to change the corresponding mold cutter block based on the target weight and adjust the dough and filling feeding speeds accordingly.
* **Q: Can meat filling and vegetarian filling be switched at any time?**
  * **A**: The filling supply system is compatible with most meat and vegetarian fillings, but before switching fillings with different flavors, the filling storage hopper and internal auger need to be thoroughly cleaned.
* **Q: Can the production speed be changed?**
  * **A**: Yes, the production speed of the ABM series is adjustable. ABM-2600 reaches up to 2600pcs/h, and ABM-3000 reaches up to 3000pcs/h.
