## II. Production Process and Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Below is a guide to core operations and parameter settings based on real-machine commissioning:

**1. Pre-startup Preparation**

* Check for foreign objects inside the machine, ensuring the cutting disk and dough extruding spiral are installed correctly and fixed securely.
* Prepare dry flour on the receiving tray or end conveyor belt to prevent the formed dough from sticking.

**2. Feeding and Operation**

* **Dough Pre-treatment**: Cut the fermented or kneaded dough into a size suitable for the hopper (the dough should not be too hard or too dry; it needs to retain a certain amount of moisture and ductility).
* **Start the Machine**: Connect to the 220V/380V power supply, press the green start switch, and the machine motor will start running.
* Continuously feed the dough blocks into the hopper; the pushing spiral will automatically draw them in and extrude them.

**3. Forming and Collection**

* **Discharge Observation**: Observe the first few extruded bun blanks to check if their shape and weight meet requirements. If the surface is not smooth enough, adjust the dough's water ratio or the amount of dusting flour appropriately.
* The formed original cut buns or round buns will automatically fall onto the tray below.
* Employees can collect them in an orderly manner at the discharge port and directly place them into steamers or proofing boxes to prepare for subsequent processes.

**4. Shutdown Cleaning and Maintenance**

* After work is finished, press the red stop button and be sure to cut off the main power supply.
* Open the front cover and remove the residual dough inside. Use a dedicated scraper and a damp cloth to clean the residues on the spiral propeller and cutting disk.
* **WARNING: It is strictly forbidden to use a high-pressure water pipe to wash the internal motor and circuit control box areas directly!**
