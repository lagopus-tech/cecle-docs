## II. Production Workflow and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Below is the core operation and parameter setting guide based on real machine startup debugging:

### 1. Feeding and Preparation

* First, add the mixed dough into the left dough hopper.
* Add the prepared filling (requiring crushed particles and a certain cohesiveness) into the right filling storage hopper.

### 2. No-Load and Discharge Testing

* **Filling and Dough Supply Test**: Independently turn on the dough feeding and filling switches, observing the extrusion of the dough tube and filling. Both speeds are adjustable; it is recommended to slow down the speed during initial testing.
* **Confirm Status**: Once the dough tube is smoothly extruded with uniform thickness, and filling is evenly discharged from the center tube, you can stop and prepare for combined forming.

### 3. Synchronized Forming and Parameter Matching

* Guide the dough tube containing filling into the lower forming cutter block (mold) area.
* **Start Forming**: Turn on the forming switch, and the cutter disk will automatically close, cut, and pleat the dough tube.
* **Fine-Tuning Tips**:
  * Control the final weight and skin-to-filling ratio of the baozi by adjusting the ratio among dough feeding speed, filling feeding speed, and forming cutter speed.
  * Select and install the correct mold according to the target weight (20-50g, 50-80g, 80-120g, 120-160g).

  * **A**: The machine supports a wide forming weight range from 20g to 160g. You need to change the corresponding mold based on the target weight (20-50g / 50-80g / 80-120g / 120-160g) and adjust the dough and filling feeding speeds accordingly.

  * **A**: The filling supply system is compatible with most meat and vegetarian fillings, but before switching fillings with different flavors, the filling storage hopper and auger need to be thoroughly cleaned.
