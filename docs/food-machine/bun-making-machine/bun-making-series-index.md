        ## In-Depth Comparison: Which Series is Right for You?

        To help you choose the perfect equipment for your business, here is a detailed comparison of the three series across core operational dimensions:

        ### 1. Space Efficiency vs. Industrial Power
        * **DBM-3600 (Desktop):** The ultimate space-saver. It fits directly on a standard stainless steel prep table. Despite its small footprint, it delivers an impressive 3,600 pcs/h, making it ideal for high-rent urban central kitchens or restaurants transitioning to automation without extra floor space.
        * **ABM Series (Vertical):** Offers a floor-standing vertical design that maximizes height rather than floor width. It provides a heavier, more stable chassis (up to 300kg) for continuous, rigorous industrial running without vibrating, acting as the perfect middle ground for growing factories.
        * **BZ-290A (Intelligent):** A heavy-duty, horizontal industrial powerhouse. It requires the most floor space (268cm long) but houses the most powerful motor system (3.3kw) designed for relentless, high-volume production lines.

        ### 2. Dough Process Compatibility (The Deal-Breaker)
        * **DBM-3600 & ABM Series:** Designed primarily for **Proofed Dough (醒面)**. This means the dough is mixed, formed into baozi by the machine, and *then* sent to a proofer for fermentation before steaming.
        * **BZ-290A (Intelligent):** Features proprietary **Dual Dough Compatibility**. It can handle both Proofed Dough AND **Fermented Dough (发面)** (dough that is fermented *before* being put into the machine). If your traditional recipe strictly requires pre-fermented dough to maintain a specific fluffy texture, the BZ-290A is the only choice.

        ### 3. Product Size & Flexibility
        * **ABM Series:** Offers the widest single-machine capability range (**20g to 180g**). If your business produces extra-large jumbo buns alongside small ones, this series provides the most extreme size flexibility.
        * **DBM-3600:** Covers **20g to 160g**, but relies on 4 distinct mold brackets. It's highly versatile but requires physical mold swapping when jumping between drastically different weight classes.
        * **BZ-290A:** Focuses on the golden standard of commercial baozi (**25g to 130g**). While it doesn't do 180g jumbo buns, its intelligent extrusion system ensures the absolute highest precision and aesthetic quality for standard sizes.

        ## IV. Unified Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

        * **Q: How to adjust for producing different sizes of baozi across the series?**
          * **A**: All our machines (DBM, ABM, and BZ series) support a wide forming weight range. You need to change the corresponding mold cutter block based on your target weight and adjust the dough and filling feeding speeds on the control panel accordingly.
        * **Q: Can meat filling and vegetarian filling be switched at any time?**
          * **A**: Yes, the filling supply systems across all series are compatible with most meat, vegetarian, and mixed granular fillings. Before switching flavors, simply ensure the filling storage hopper and internal auger are thoroughly cleaned.
        * **Q: What is the difference in operation between fermented dough and proofed dough (BZ-290A exclusive)?**
          * **A**: The BZ-290A is perfectly compatible with both. Since fermented dough is fluffier, you may need to fine-tune the dough feeding speed to achieve the optimal skin-to-filling ratio; proofed dough has better extensibility. Adjust the speed on the control panel based on the dough's condition.
