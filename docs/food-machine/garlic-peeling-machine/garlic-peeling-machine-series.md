### 2. Core Functions & Working Principle

The machine operates through a combination of aerodynamic peeling and mechanical rotation:

* **Dry Separated Clove Feeding**: Whole garlic bulbs are split into individual dry cloves and poured into the top hopper chute, descending naturally into the internal peeling chamber.
* **Air-Assisted Rubbing & Stripping**: Upon activation, the required external air compressor delivers high-pressure airflow (0.28 to 0.9 m³/min depending on the model) into the chamber. The aerodynamic vortex tears and strips the outer husks instantly while protecting the garlic flesh.
* **Gravity & Airflow Discharge**: Stripped papery skins and polished cloves exit smoothly via rotational momentum, airflow, and gravity into a collection crate, achieving rapid continuous feeding and discharge.
* **100% Waterless Dry Peeling**: Operating completely without water prevents cloves from absorbing moisture, swelling, or losing volatile aromatic oils. Dry-peeled cloves remain firm and can be stored safely in refrigerated containers.

## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To maintain a consistent 98% peeling rate and prevent clogging, strictly adhere to these operating guidelines:

### 1. Raw Material Preparation & Pre-Treatment (Critical Success Factor)

* **Must Use Thoroughly Dry Garlic (Crucial)**:
  * **Never attempt to peel fresh, wet garlic straight from harvest or damp, refrigerated garlic!** Fresh garlic skins have high moisture content, adhere tightly to the flesh, and cannot be mechanically rubbed off dry.
  * **Dryness Test**: Skins should feel crisp and crackle audibly when squeezed. If garlic is damp, spread bulbs in a ventilated area to air-dry for 1–2 days, or warm cloves in a food dehydrator/low-temp oven at 40°C–50°C (104°F–122°F) with fan ventilation for 10–20 minutes before processing. Peeling efficiency will instantly exceed 98%.
* **Bulbs Must Be Separated into Cloves**:
  * Before feeding, bulbs must be broken into individual cloves (by hand or using a mechanical garlic clove splitter). Hard stem bases and thick outer bulb sheaths should be discarded.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Workstation Setup

1. Place the unit on a flat, dry, and stable floor or heavy-duty commercial countertop.
2. **Air Compressor Connection**: Ensure the correct external air compressor (see specs in Section III) is securely connected to the machine's pneumatic inlet and that the air pressure/delivery is stable.
3. Inspect the power cord and connect to a dedicated **grounded 220V electrical outlet**.
4. Position a perforated plastic collection crate or stainless steel bowl directly beneath the discharge chute.

### 3. Standard Operating Guide

1. **Start Compressor & Motor**: Turn on the external air compressor and wait for it to reach operational pressure. Press the machine's power switch.
2. **Even, Continuous Feeding**: Feed prepared dry cloves into the hopper opening in steady batches (**avoid dumping massive quantities at once to prevent choking the inlet**).
3. **Rapid Peeling & Discharge**: Once cloves drop into the chamber, skins are stripped in seconds. Peeled cloves and papery husks slide continuously down the exit chute.
4. **Safety Warning**: **Never insert fingers, chopsticks, spatulas, or metal utensils into the hopper throat while the machine is running!**

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why does freshly purchased garlic peel poorly in the machine? What are the specific requirements for garlic?**
  * **A:** The machine relies on the aerodynamic shearing force between compressed air and dry papery husks. **Freshly harvested garlic contains high internal moisture, and the skin adheres tightly to the flesh with sticky natural allicin gum; no mechanical dry peeler can peel fresh wet garlic effectively**. For best performance, air-dry whole garlic bulbs in a shaded, ventilated space or under sunlight for 1–2 days until the outer skins feel crisp and papery. For immediate use, dry separated cloves in a food dehydrator or oven at 45°C (113°F) for 10 minutes. Once skins are brittle, peeling efficiency instantly exceeds 98%.
* **Q: Do I really need an air compressor for this machine to work?**
  * **A:** Yes, the GP-30 and GP-150 models operate using an advanced pneumatic peeling system. The machine's motor handles rotation and feed, while the external air compressor provides the crucial high-pressure air blast that strips the skins away without bruising the cloves. Please refer to Section III to match the required Air Delivery (m³/min) for your specific model.
* **Q: Peeled cloves and light papery skins exit together. What is the fastest way to separate them?**
  * **A:** Because dry peeling uses zero water, the stripped skins are featherlight while the peeled cloves are solid and dense. Separation is effortless: Place a small desktop fan beside the discharge chute. Cloves drop straight into the collection bin while skins are blown clear into a collection box or bag. Alternatively, shake the mixture gently in a perforated kitchen colander.
