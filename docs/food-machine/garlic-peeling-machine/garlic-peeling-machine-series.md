### 2. Core Functions & Working Principle

The machine operates through bionic contact friction, high-speed centrifugal rubbing, and aerodynamic separation:

* **Dry Separated Clove Feeding**: Whole garlic bulbs are split into individual dry cloves and poured into the top hopper chute, descending naturally into the internal peeling chamber.
* **High-Speed Bionic Gentle Rubbing**: Upon activation, the bottom silicone impeller spins at an optimized velocity, propelling cloves against the interior stainless steel drum walls in a 3D tumbling vortex. High frictional resistance between the resilient silicone and dry papery skin tears and strips the outer husks instantly.
* **Gravity & Airflow Discharge**: Stripped papery skins and polished cloves exit smoothly down the front discharge chute via rotational momentum and gravity into a collection crate, achieving rapid continuous feeding and discharge in seconds.
* **100% Waterless Dry Peeling**: Operating completely without water prevents cloves from absorbing moisture, swelling, or losing volatile aromatic oils. Dry-peeled cloves remain firm and can be stored in sealed refrigerated containers for weeks without turning sticky or spoiling.

## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To maintain a consistent 98% peeling rate and prevent clogging, strictly adhere to these operating guidelines:

### 1. Raw Material Preparation & Pre-Treatment (Critical Success Factor)

* **Must Use Thoroughly Dry Garlic (Crucial)**:
  * **Never attempt to peel fresh, wet garlic straight from harvest or damp, refrigerated garlic!** Fresh garlic skins have high moisture content, adhere tightly to the flesh, and cannot be mechanically rubbed off dry.
  * **Dryness Test**: Skins should feel crisp and crackle audibly when squeezed. If garlic is damp, spread bulbs in a ventilated area to air-dry for 1–2 days, or warm cloves in a food dehydrator/low-temp oven at 40°C–50°C (104°F–122°F) with fan ventilation for 10–20 minutes before processing. Peeling efficiency will instantly exceed 98%.
* **Bulbs Must Be Separated into Cloves**:
  * Before feeding, bulbs must be broken into individual cloves (by hand or using a mechanical garlic clove splitter). Hard stem bases and thick outer bulb sheaths should be discarded.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Workstation Setup

1. Place the unit on a flat, dry, and stable countertop, ensuring all four rubber suction feet grip firmly.
2. Inspect the power cord and plug for damage; connect to a dedicated **grounded 110V or 220V electrical outlet**.
3. Inspect the hopper and discharge chute for foreign debris or loose screws; spin the silicone impeller by hand to confirm free rotation.
4. Position a perforated plastic collection crate or stainless steel bowl directly beneath the discharge chute.

### 3. Standard Operating Guide

1. **Start Motor First**: Press the power switch and allow the motor to idle smoothly for 2–3 seconds.
2. **Even, Continuous Feeding**: Feed prepared dry cloves into the hopper opening in steady, moderate handfuls (**avoid dumping massive quantities at once to prevent choking the inlet**).
3. **Rapid Peeling & Discharge**: Once cloves drop into the chamber, skins are stripped in **10–15 seconds**. Peeled cloves and papery husks slide continuously down the exit chute.
4. **Quick Skin Separation**:
   * *Method A*: Place a small desktop fan beside the discharge chute to blow light skins away into a collection bag while heavier cloves fall directly into the bin.
   * *Method B*: Toss peeled cloves gently in a perforated colander; loose skins will fly out, leaving clean cloves.
5. **Safety Warning**: **Never insert fingers, chopsticks, spatulas, or metal utensils into the hopper throat while the machine is running!**

### 4. Shutdown, Cleaning & Routine Maintenance

* **Post-Use Dry Cleaning**: Unplug the power cord. Use a soft brush to sweep loose skins and garlic dust from the hopper, drum walls, and silicone impeller slots.
* **Washing the Silicone Impeller**: If residual garlic gum accumulates after processing sticky varieties, unscrew the top retaining nut, remove the silicone impeller, wash with warm water and mild detergent, **dry thoroughly**, and reinstall.
* **Electrical Moisture Warning**: Wipe the stainless steel exterior with a damp cloth; **never submerge the machine in water or spray running water directly into the base motor housing and switch assembly!**
* **Storage**: Store in a dry, well-ventilated space when not in use to protect motor windings from humid air.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why does freshly purchased garlic peel poorly in the machine? What are the specific requirements for garlic?**
  * **A:** The machine relies on the frictional shearing force between resilient silicone paddles and dry papery husks. **Freshly harvested garlic contains high internal moisture, and the skin adheres tightly to the flesh with sticky natural allicin gum; no mechanical dry peeler can peel fresh wet garlic effectively**. For best performance, air-dry whole garlic bulbs in a shaded, ventilated space or under sunlight for 1–2 days until the outer skins feel crisp and papery. For immediate use, dry separated cloves in a food dehydrator or oven at 45°C (113°F) for 10 minutes. Once skins are brittle, peeling efficiency instantly exceeds 98%.
* **Q: How durable is the silicone impeller? Will long-term friction crush cloves or impart chemical odors?**
  * **A:** The impeller is cast from premium food-grade vulcanized silicone compliant with FDA and European food-contact hygiene standards. It is completely odorless, non-toxic, and free of plasticizers. The flexible silicone yields gently against firm clove surfaces, peeling only the brittle skin without crushing flesh or squeezing out garlic juice. Under typical restaurant use, an original silicone impeller lasts **1–2 years**, and replacements are inexpensive and easy to install via a single retaining bolt.
* **Q: Peeled cloves and light papery skins exit together. What is the fastest way to separate them?**
  * **A:** Because dry peeling uses zero water, the stripped skins are featherlight while the peeled cloves are solid and dense. Separation is effortless:
    1. *Air Separation (Fastest)*: Place a small desktop fan beside the discharge chute. Cloves drop straight into the collection bin while skins are blown clear into a collection box or bag.
    2. *Manual Sifting*: Shake the mixture gently in a perforated kitchen colander; light skins float to the top and blow away easily.
