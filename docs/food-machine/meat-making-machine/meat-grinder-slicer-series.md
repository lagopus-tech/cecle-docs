## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

To maintain machine stability, operator safety, and extended blade life during continuous commercial operation, adhere strictly to the following procedures:

### 1. Raw Material Preparation & Pre-Treatment

* **Suitable Raw Materials**:
  * **Slicing Station (Right)**: Chilled fresh meat, semi-frozen meat (optimal cutting firmness between -2°C and 4°C), boneless skinless poultry, cooked beef, cooked offal, etc.
  * **Mincing Station (Left)**: Fresh meat chunks, fat/lean blends, trimmed meat with light sinew, and drained aromatic vegetables.
* **Bone-Free Inspection**: **Never feed hard bones (such as beef marrow bones or pork femur bones), rock-hard frozen block meat, or metal objects into the machine.** Slicing meats must be verified bone-free prior to loading to avoid chipping rotary blades or jamming the mincer auger.
* **Input Sizing**: Trim meat for the right slicing chute to a width within 8–10 cm; portion meat for the left grinder into continuous strips suitable for smooth intake.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Safety Verification

1. Place the machine on a flat, solid floor and engage the pedal brakes on all swivel casters.
2. Verify connection to a properly grounded **110V or 220V power supply** with an intact power cable.
3. Confirm that the right slicer safety guard and front window are securely latched; ensure the left mincer front retaining collar (locking ring) is evenly hand-tightened.
4. Press the green start switch to test-run the machine for 10–15 seconds, listening for smooth motor rotation and verifying that there is no abnormal scraping noise.

### 3. Dual-Station Standardized Operating Guide

1. Position food-grade stainless steel collection trays or lugs beneath both the left and right discharge outlets.
2. **Slicing / Shredding / Dicing (Right Side)**:
   * Power on the machine and feed trimmed fresh meat strips evenly down the right hopper. The self-drawing rotary blades will pull the meat down and produce clean slices instantly.
   * **Shredding**: Gather sliced sheets, stack them neatly, and feed them through a second time to produce uniform meat strips.
   * **Dicing**: Gather strips, align them horizontally, and feed them through a third time to produce uniform diced cubes.
3. **Mincing / Grinding (Left Side)**:
   * Place meat chunks into the left tray and feed them into the throat. **Always use the supplied safety pusher** to lightly press meat downward; ground meat will continuously extrude from the front hole plate.
4. **Safety Red Line**: **Never insert fingers or hands into either the left or right feed ports during operation!** If a blockage occurs, always power down and unplug the machine before clearing.

### 4. Post-Shift Sanitation & Rust-Prevention Maintenance

* Always **turn off the main power and disconnect the plug** prior to cleaning.
* **Cleaning the Left Mincer**: Unscrew the front locking ring, remove the hole plate, cross blade, and feed screw auger. Wash thoroughly in a sink with warm water and neutral food-grade detergent.
* **Cleaning the Right Slicer**: Open the top safety guard. Using warm water with detergent, spray and brush down the disc cutters from top to bottom with a long-handled brush, letting runoff drain out the slanted discharge chute.
* **Drying & Lubrication**: Thoroughly wipe or blow dry all metal parts; **apply a thin coating of food-grade white mineral oil or vegetable oil to the rotary disc cutters, mincer auger, cross blade, and hole plate** to protect against oxidation and keep cutting edges sharp.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Can the slicing and grinding stations operate at the same time? Does it require constant tool swapping?**
  * **A**: Yes, they can run simultaneously without changing tools. The VMG-2200 features an integrated dual-station head driven by a robust single transmission system. Both zones are physically separated, allowing one operator to slice fresh stir-fry meat while an assistant simultaneously grinds burger or dumpling mince on the other side, greatly accelerating multi-dish prep workflows.
* **Q: Can this machine handle meat with light sinew? Does the ground meat heat up or turn pale?**
  * **A**: Yes. Powered by a 2.2 kW high-torque motor combined with hardened rotary blades and a razor-ground cross cutter, the VMG-2200 cleanly cuts through light connective tissue and trimmed cuts without dragging or stalling. The mincing barrel features progressive flighting that extrudes product quickly, keeping dwell time low and limiting temperature rise to a negligible range. This locks in moisture and fat integrity, keeping the meat fresh, bright, and springy.
* **Q: Weighing 92 kg, is the machine difficult to move and clean after shifts?**
  * **A**: Not at all. The base is fitted with 4 heavy-duty industrial swivel casters, allowing a single person to roll the unit across flat kitchen floors easily. When cleaning is needed, release the wheel brakes to roll it aside. For teardown, the left grinding components (ring, plate, knife, auger) detach by hand without tools; the right slicing section cleans rapidly using a spray wand and long-handled brush. Routine maintenance is straightforward and fast.
