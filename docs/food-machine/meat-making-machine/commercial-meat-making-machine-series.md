* **Precision High-Speed Slicing / Shredding / Dicing (Top Station)**: Fresh meat fed into the top chute is drawn in and sheared by circular interlocking blades, producing **uniform 3.5 mm slices** in a single pass. * **High-Torque Continuous Mincing & Stuffing (Side Station)**: The side auger conveys meat chunks toward the cutting zone, shearing them against the perforated plate to output consistent mince. Installing the sausage funnel attachment allows direct, uniform casing filling.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Follow these standardized procedures to ensure operational safety and maximize component lifespan:

### 2.1 Raw Material Preparation & Pre-Processing

* **Inspection & Deboning**: Never feed hard bones, frozen rock meat, or foreign metal objects into the machine. Bone fragments must be removed completely to avoid damaging the slicing blade set or the mincer auger.
* **Meat Sizing**: Chilled, slightly firmed boneless meat provides the cleanest slicing cuts. Trim meat cuts to fit within the feeding chute (recommended width: under 8–10 cm).

### 2.2 Assembly & Pre-Start Inspection

1. Place the machine on a level, sturdy worktable and plug it into a grounded 220V commercial power outlet.
2. Confirm the top slicing safety guard is secured and the side mincing retaining collar is tightened hand-tight.
3. Switch on the unit for a brief dry run. Verify smooth mechanical sound and correct rotational direction.

### 2.3 Slicing, Shredding & Dicing Operation

* **Slicing**: Start the slicing unit. Drop prepared boneless meat cuts into the top feed chute. The rotary blades will self-feed and cut the meat into 3.5 mm slices into the receiving tray.
* **Shredding (Strips)**: Take the sliced meat cuts, stack them flat, and feed them through the chute a second time to produce 3.5 mm meat strips.
* **Dicing (Cubes)**: Gather the meat shreds crosswise and feed them through a third time to produce uniform dices.
* **Safety Warning**: Never insert fingers into the chute. Always use the included safety food pusher when guidance is required.

### 2.4 Mincing & Sausage Stuffing Operation

* **Mincing**: Place cut meat strips into the side hopper tray, turn on the mincing drive, and gently push meat down using the pusher. Extruded mince discharges steadily from the hole plate.
* **Sausage Stuffing**: Remove the cross blade and hole plate, install the sausage nozzle and support flange, and hand-tighten the front collar. Slide soaked casing over the nozzle tube, feed seasoned mince into the hopper, and guide the filled casing by hand before tying off links.

### 2.5 Shutdown, Disassembly & Maintenance

* Always **disconnect the power plug** before maintenance or cleaning.
* Unscrew the side collar nut and remove the auger, blade, and hole plate for cleaning with warm water and mild detergent.
* For the top slicing blade group, flush with warm water and neutral detergent using a wash brush, or unmount the assembly for deep cleaning.
* After drying completely, coat all blade edges, hole plates, and circular cutters with a thin layer of food-grade mineral or vegetable oil to prevent surface oxidation and rust.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: How does the MG-120 achieve dual-function slicing and mincing without frequent part changes?**
  * **A**: The MG-120 features a dual-channel design. The top inlet powers circular rotating cutters dedicated to slicing, shredding, and dicing, while the side station houses an independent auger extrusion chamber dedicated to mincing and sausage stuffing. Both systems operate from the same machine without swapping internal drive assemblies.
* **Q: What dish applications work best with the standard 3.5 mm slice thickness? How are shreds and dices made?**
  * **A**: The 3.5 mm thickness is a kitchen standard ideal for stir-fries, hotpot cuts, and barbecue preparations. A single pass yields slices; stacking and re-feeding slices yields 3.5 mm shreds for noodle toppings or stir-fried meat strips; feeding shreds crosswise produces dices for bun fillings, stew toppings, or pizza toppings.
* **Q: With a 40 kg net weight, how should routine cleaning and rust prevention be handled?**
  * **A**: The smooth stainless steel body cleans easily with a damp cloth and mild degreaser. The side grinding parts (auger, blade, plate) unthread by hand for sink washing. The top blade set can be rinsed using warm water and a wash brush while powered off. Always dry parts thoroughly and apply a light wipe of cooking oil over the blades to maintain sharpness and corrosion resistance.
