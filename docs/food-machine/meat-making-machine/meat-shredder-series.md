### 2. Core Functions & Working Principle

The DK-2 utilizes a 2.2 kW high-power motor coupled to a heavy-duty synchronized gearbox driving two vertically aligned rotary cutter shafts simultaneously:

* **Synchronized Dual-Blade Action (One-Step Shredding)**: Fresh meat fed through the top hopper is immediately sliced into uniform sheets by the upper blade set. Gravity and precision baffles guide the sliced meat directly into the lower perpendicular blade set, which instantly cuts the sheets into straight, uniform, clean-cut strips—**all accomplished in a single continuous pass**.
* **Secondary Feeding for Rapid Dicing (Two-Step Dicing)**: Taking the freshly cut strips and spreading them horizontally across the feed tray for a second pass instantly produces neat, uniform meat cubes/dice. Perfect for dumpling and bun fillings, braised pork rice toppings, and pizza toppings.
* **Smooth Self-Drawing Intake Without Compression**: The circular blades are vacuum heat-treated and razor-ground. The rotating discs draw raw meat through by traction without bruising cellular walls or squeezing out natural juices, locking in natural tenderness and freshness.

## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

To maintain operator safety and maximize blade life during continuous commercial operation, adhere strictly to the following procedures:

### 1. Raw Material Preparation & Pre-Treatment

* **Suitable Raw Materials**: Chilled fresh pork, beef, lamb, boneless skinless chicken breast, duck, cooked beef, cooked offal, and firm root vegetables.
* **Optimal Temperature**: Best processing temperature is **-2°C to 4°C** (lightly chilled/tempered meat yields the firmest, cleanest strips).
* **Bone-Free Requirement**: Thoroughly debone all meats. Large beef/pork/lamb bones, hard bone fragments, poultry femur bones, and foreign metal objects must be completely removed to prevent blade chipping or gear damage.
* **Portioning Dimensions**: Trim raw primal cuts into strips 8–12 cm wide to ensure smooth gravity feeding through the top hopper opening.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Safety Verification

1. Position the machine on a level, hard floor and engage the pedal brakes on all swivel casters.
2. Confirm connection to a properly grounded **110V or 220V power supply**.
3. Verify that the blade comb scrapers, safety hopper, and front access door are securely latched with safety bolts.
4. Jog the green power switch to verify smooth blade rotation, correct directional travel, and absence of abnormal scraping or rattling sounds.

### 3. Slicing, Shredding & Dicing Operation

1. Place a food-grade stainless steel collection pan or tub directly under the discharge chute.
2. Press the start button and let the unit reach full operating speed.
3. **Meat Shredding (Single Pass)**: Feed prepared meat strips smoothly into the top hopper. The blades will grip and pull the meat through, instantly depositing uniform meat strips into the collection pan below.
4. **Meat Dicing (Secondary Pass)**: Gather the shredded meat strips, arrange them horizontally on a tray, and feed them into the hopper a second time. The lower blade set cross-cuts the strips into neat, distinct meat cubes.
5. **Safety Warning**: **Never insert hands, fingers, or metal implements into the hopper opening during operation.** If a feed jam occurs, stop the machine and disconnect power before clearing.

### 4. Blade Spacing Specifications & Selection Guide

* **3.0 - 3.5 mm Spacing**: Ideal for fine stir-fry shreds (e.g., shredded pork with sweet bean sauce, shredded pork with garlic sauce) and fine meat dice.
* **5.0 mm Spacing**: Standard commercial width for beef noodle toppings, wok-fried meat strips, and general delivery meal prep.
* **7.0 mm Spacing**: Ideal for breaded chicken tenders, boneless chicken strips, fish fillet strips, and barbecue skewer cubes.
* **10.0 mm Spacing**: Perfect for hearty meat strips, braised pork cubes, stew chunks, and coarse dicing.

### 5. Post-Shift Sanitation & Rust-Prevention Maintenance

* Always **disconnect the main power plug** before performing cleaning or sanitation.
* Open the front inspection door and top safety shield. Spray warm water combined with a neutral food-grade detergent through the upper and lower blade assemblies using a low-to-medium pressure spray nozzle and a long-handled brush (avoid spraying electrical components directly).
* Rinse thoroughly and dry completely using clean towels or food-safe compressed air.
* Once dry, **apply a generous coat of food-grade mineral oil or vegetable oil over both upper and lower blade sets** to prevent surface oxidation and preserve cutting edge sharpness.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: How does the DK-2 "One-Step Double-Blade" design outperform standard single-blade slicers?**
  * **A**: Standard single-blade meat slicers contain only one cutting shaft, meaning fresh meat can only be cut into slices on the first pass. To make strips, operators have to manually catch, organize, flatten, and re-feed those slices into the machine a second time. This is labor-intensive and often causes jams when soft meat slips. The DK-2 stacks two precision cutting sets vertically: the top set slices, and the meat immediately falls directly into the lower set to be cross-cut into uniform strips in a single pass, tripling prep speed.
* **Q: Can this machine process meats with sinew, soft connective tissue, or semi-frozen cuts? Will it tear or connect strips?**
  * **A**: Yes. It easily handles boneless meats with light connective tissues and tempered semi-frozen meats (-2°C to 4°C). Powered by a 2.2 kW high-torque motor and razor-ground hardened alloy blades, the DK-2 provides clean shearing action that cuts cleanly through soft sinew and fascia, eliminating the "stringy, half-cut" problem common with underpowered small tabletop cutters.
* **Q: Is cleaning and maintaining a dual-blade assembly complicated?**
  * **A**: Not at all. The DK-2 is built specifically for commercial kitchen sanitation. It features a wide front-opening waterproof access door. Once the machine is powered off, operators can rinse both blade sets from top to bottom with a water hose and neutral detergent; meat trimmings and water flow out through the bottom discharge chute. Drying and wiping the blades with food-safe oil takes just a few minutes and keeps the blades sharp and rust-free.
