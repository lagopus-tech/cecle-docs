## II. Operating Procedures and Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

The following guide outlines the core operation steps for the HMG-120 (RED / BLACK) in everyday home use:

### 1. Preparation & Ingredient Pre-Treatment

* **Meat Preparation**: Remove bones, skin, and excessive gristle from fresh meat. Cut into strips or cubes that easily fit into the feeding tube (approx. 2 cm wide). *Note: Never insert bones, shells, or unthawed rock-hard frozen meat.*
* **Sausage Preparation**: Soak dry casings in lukewarm water until soft and pliable. Pre-mix the ground meat with salt, herbs, and desired spices.

### 2. Meat Grinding Operation

* Attach the grinder head housing to the motor base, insert the feed auger, cross blade, and desired grinding plate, then firmly tighten the front locking cap.
* Connect to a 220V–240V / 50Hz power supply and turn on the power switch. Place meat pieces onto the food tray and gently guide them into the tube using the included food pusher. Uniform minced meat will extrude smoothly.

### 3. Sausage Stuffing Operation

* Remove the front locking cap and cross blade, install the specialized sausage stuffing funnel, and retighten the cap securely.
* Slide the prepared casing over the stuffing funnel tube and tie a knot at the far end.
* Turn on the machine and feed the seasoned meat mixture into the hopper. The meat will evenly fill the casing. Once finished, tie off sections with cotton twine to create sausage links of your preferred length.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Can the HMG-120 (RED / BLACK) really make sausages besides grinding meat?**
  * **A**: Yes, absolutely! The HMG-120 (RED / BLACK) comes with a dedicated sausage stuffing funnel attachment. After grinding and seasoning your meat, simply install the funnel to effortlessly extrude the meat filling into casings for delicious, preservative-free homemade sausages.
* **Q: Is the machine difficult to clean after use?**
  * **A**: Not at all. The entire grinding head assembly (including the food chute, feed auger, cutting blade, and hole plate) can be easily disassembled. Simply rinse them thoroughly with warm water and mild dish detergent.
* **Q: What does 800W locked power mean? Can it grind bones?**
  * **A**: The 300W rated power is for normal continuous running, while 800W locked power represents the peak motor torque delivered when encountering tougher fibers or sinew to prevent jamming. This machine is designed for fresh boneless meat and soft ingredients. Do not attempt to grind bones or hard nuts, as this will damage the blades and gears.
