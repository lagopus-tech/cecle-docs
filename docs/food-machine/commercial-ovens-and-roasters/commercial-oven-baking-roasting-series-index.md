* **Core Identity:** The High-Volume Artisan Bakery & Pizza Workhorse
* **Key Mechanism:** Heavy-duty modular baking chambers lined with dense aluminum-silicate insulation. Each deck features dedicated top and bottom far-infrared heating tubes with individual thermostats, accommodating standard 400 × 600 mm commercial sheet pans.

## 3. Buyer's Selection & Decision Matrix

Match your menu structure, service format, and kitchen utility infrastructure to the optimal equipment:### Quick Selection Guidelines:
1. **Choose the OVE Deck Oven if:** You operate a dedicated bakery, pastry shop, or pizzeria where multi-tray batch capacity (400 × 600 mm sheets) and separate top/bottom deck thermal adjustments are required.
2. **Choose the TD Roller Grill if:** You manage a high-traffic snack bar, cinema, convenience store, or food truck needing a low-footprint, plug-and-play visual merchandising grill for sausages and skewers.
3. **Choose the JC / C Steam Combi Oven if:** You run a restaurant kitchen or upscale bistro requiring versatile, space-saving technology that roasts, steams, combi-bakes, and sous-vides with automated consistency.
