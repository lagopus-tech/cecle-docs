### 2. Core Functions & Working Principles

The combi oven combines four essential commercial thermal cooking methods:

* **Pure Steam Mode (50°C–130°C)**: High-purity, saturated steam envelops food, cooking rapidly while locking in natural nutrients, juices, and vibrant colors without condensation dripping or waterlogging. Ideal for seafood, nourishing broths, dim sum, and tender vegetables.
* **Hot Air Convection Bake Mode (30°C–300°C)**: Rapid convective air streams sweep over ingredient surfaces, quickly evaporating surface moisture to trigger the Maillard reaction. Yields golden, crispy exteriors and moist interiors for pizzas, egg tarts, roast poultry, pastries, and cookies.
* **Combi Steam-Bake Mode (100°C–250°C)**: Convection heat operates simultaneously with pulsed micro-steam injections regulated by the microcomputer. Steam forms a protective moisture film on the food surface, **producing crispy skins while retaining internal juices without drying out meat fibers**—the gold standard for tomahawk steaks, roast lamb, crispy duck, and crusty sourdough expansion.
* **Low-Temperature Proofing & Defrost Mode (30°C–60°C)**: Provides a gentle, constant-temperature environment with delicate humidity, allowing bread dough to proof evenly into an optimal honeycomb structure, or thawing thick-cut steaks gently without moisture loss.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To ensure reliable long-term performance, electrical safety, and optimal culinary results, adhere strictly to the following guidelines:

### 1. Pre-Startup Environment & Utility Connections

1. **Clearance & Ventilation**: Place the unit level on a sturdy, heat-resistant countertop or commercial stainless steel equipment stand. Maintain at least **10–15 cm** of clearance along the back and sides for ventilation. Never cover the top exhaust outlet.
2. **Electrical Hookup**:
   * The 70L (6.0 kW) and 90L (8.0 kW) models are commercial heavy-duty units that **must be connected directly to a dedicated circuit breaker with ground leakage protection (dedicated 380V Three-Phase or properly rated 220V industrial line)**. Ensure the chassis is reliably grounded; never use domestic power strips or extension cords.
3. **Water Supply Connection (Critical Quality Requirement)**:
   * Connect the inlet hose to a **potable water supply fitted with a commercial water softener or RO filtration system** (operating water pressure should be maintained between 0.15–0.35 MPa).
   * **Strict Prohibition**: **Never connect directly to untreated hard tap water or well water!** Calcium and magnesium ions rapidly calcify inside steam generators at high temperatures, clogging injection nozzles and burning out heating elements.

### 2. Smart Panel Settings & Standard Operation

1. **Powering On & Self-Test**: Switch on the external breaker, tap the power icon on the front panel, and the LCD screen will light up as the unit automatically self-checks the water lines and fills the internal reservoir.
2. **Selecting a Cooking Mode**:
   * **Preset Program Method**: Touch the corresponding ingredient icon (e.g., "Roast Chicken", "Steamed Fish", "Baguette", "Steak"), and the system will automatically execute the optimized multi-stage time, temperature, and humidity curve.
   * **Manual Custom Method**: Select the core mode (Steam / Bake / Combi), then use the touch dial to adjust target temperature (30°C–300°C), steam humidity percentage (0%–100%), and timer duration.
3. **Preheating**: Always preheat the empty oven for 3–5 minutes before loading chilled food. Once the target chamber temperature is reached, a chime will prompt you to slide in trays.

**Door-Opening Safety Protocol (⚠️ Critical Caution)**:
* During cooking, the cavity is filled with pressurized steam and hot air reaching 100°C–300°C.
* **Never stand directly in front of the door when opening!** Always stand to the handle side, open the door **3–5 cm** to vent accumulated steam for 3–5 seconds, and then swing the door fully open. Always wear heat-resistant commercial oven mitts when handling hot trays.

### 4. Cleaning & Descaling Maintenance

* **Daily Steam Self-Clean**: At the end of daily service, run the built-in "Steam Clean" cycle for 5 minutes to soften cavity grease. Once warm to the touch, wipe the interior with a damp microfiber cloth and neutral detergent, then dry the bottom condensation trough.
* **Door Gasket & Glass Maintenance**: Gently wipe the silicone door seal to remove grease residues, preventing acidic fats from prematurely aging the rubber.
* **Periodic Descaling (Mandatory Monthly)**: Depending on local water hardness, add food-grade citric acid solution into the reservoir **every 15–30 days** and run the automated "Descaling Program." This flushes calcified scale from heating elements and injection nozzles to ensure continuous, high-volume steam output.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: What is a Combi Oven, and how does it fundamentally differ from standard ovens or steamers?**
  * **A**: Standard ovens rely strictly on dry radiant or convection heat, which can easily dehydrate meats and dry out dough. Standard steamers max out at 100°C and cannot brown or crisp food. The CECLE Combi Oven integrates convective hot air baking (up to 300°C) with high-pressure direct-injection micro-steam. It operates as a pure steamer, a pure convection oven, or a combined steam-baking unit that roasts food crispy on the outside while maintaining tender, juicy interiors—all while significantly reducing cook times.
* **Q: How should I choose between the 70L (4-Tray) and 90L (6-Tray) models, as well as Economy vs. Deluxe?**
  * **A**: Match your selection to your kitchen capacity and recipe requirements:
    * **70L Models (JC-4 / C-4)**: Compact countertop footprint; ideal for boutique cafes, salad bars, small bakeries, or bistros with 50–80 seats.
    * **90L Models (JC-6 / C-6)**: Larger interior volume with 6 tiers for higher volume throughput; suited for restaurants with 100+ seats, central kitchens, or high-volume hotel lines.
    * **Economy vs. Deluxe**: The primary difference lies in the controller and recipe presets (Economy includes 12 standard one-touch menus; Deluxe includes 18 advanced multi-stage chef programs with finer humidity regulation).
* **Q: Is it mandatory to connect the unit through a water softener or purification system?**
  * **A**: Yes, **using softened, filtered water is strongly recommended**. The combi oven relies on precise internal steam generation nozzles. Tap water with mineral hardness will deposit calcified scale when boiled, clogging fine nozzles and degrading heating elements. Softened water prevents limescale buildup, extends component life, and ensures pure steam quality.
