## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

To ensure electrical safety, protect heating elements, and maximize baking consistency, strictly adhere to the following guidelines:

### 2.1 Pre-Start Inspection & Electrical Installation

1. **Placement & Clearance**: Install on a level, structurally sound surface supporting 150–350 kg (330–770 lbs). Maintain at least **20 cm (8 inches)** of ventilation clearance around the rear and side panels. Never place cardboard, towels, or plastics on top of the oven.
2. **Electrical Connection (⚠️ Crucial Safety Notice)**:
   * This is a high-power commercial appliance (6.6 kW for OVE-01, 13.2 kW for OVE-02, 19.8 kW for OVE-03).
   * **Never connect to regular household power strips or standard wall outlets!** Must be hardwired by a licensed electrician to a dedicated **industrial circuit breaker with leakage protection**.
   * OVE-01 supports 220V single-phase or 380V 3-phase wiring; OVE-02 and OVE-03 require **380V 3-phase 5-wire commercial power**. Ensure the equipment casing is reliably grounded.
3. **Mandatory Initial Burn-In (Burn-Off Protective Oil)**:
   * Factory heating elements carry a light coating of food-safe anti-corrosion oil. **Before first-time food use, set top and bottom heat to 200°C–220°C and run the empty oven for 30–45 minutes.**
   * Slight white smoke and odor are completely normal during this initial burn-off. Keep the kitchen well-ventilated until all odor clears, let the oven cool, and wipe the interior with a damp cloth before loading food.

### 2.2 Independent Thermostat & Timer Operation

1. **Activate Selected Deck**: Turn on the main circuit breaker, then switch on the power toggle for the specific deck you intend to use. The interior indicator light will illuminate.
2. **Adjust Temperature Parameters**:
   * **Top Heat Dial**: Rotate clockwise to your target setting (e.g., 200°C);
   * **Bottom Heat Dial**: Rotate clockwise to your target setting (e.g., 180°C);
   * The heating indicator lamps will light up. When the set temperature is reached, the lights click off as the oven enters its energy-saving holding cycle.
3. **Preheating & Loading**: Allow 12–18 minutes for the chamber temperature to stabilize. Using high-heat baking gloves, quickly slide the loaded 400 × 600 mm pans onto the deck and latch the door securely.
4. **Set Countdown Timer**: Turn the timer dial to your desired baking duration (e.g., 25 minutes). Once the countdown reaches zero, an audible buzzer sounds continuously to alert staff.

**Door Opening & Safety Warning (⚠️)**:
* Chamber air and baking sheets reach 200°C–300°C (392°F–572°F).
* **Never stand directly in front of the door when opening!** Step to the side, open the door 2–3 inches to let the initial hot steam vent for 2–3 seconds, then swing it fully open.
* Always wear **elbow-length heat-resistant baking gloves (rated for 300°C+)** and use tray pullers when handling baking sheets.

### 2.4 End-of-Day Cleaning & Maintenance

* **Daily Shutdown Procedure**:
  1. Turn all thermostats to 0°C, turn off all deck switches, and disconnect the main breaker;
  2. Crack the doors open to allow the interior to cool to a warm 40°C;
  3. Remove baking trays and use a long-handled soft brush to sweep out fallen flour, seeds, and crumbs;
  4. Wipe stainless steel door trims and glass interiors with a damp microfiber cloth and mild neutral food-safe detergent, then dry with a clean cloth.
* **Cleaning Hazards Warning**:
  * **Never spray the oven with a hose or high-pressure washer!** High-voltage heating connections and control boards inside will short-circuit.
  * **Never use wire brushes, steel wool, or abrasive scouring powders on heating tubes or glass!** Scratches can create hot-spot tube failures or fracture the glass.
* **Periodic Electrical Inspection**: Have a certified electrician inspect all main terminal block connections every 1–2 months to ensure thermal expansion hasn't loosened any wires.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: How do I choose between OVE-01 (1-deck), OVE-02 (2-deck), and OVE-03 (3-deck)?**
  * **A**: Your decision depends on **available electrical supply, floor space, and peak production volume**:
    * **Choose OVE-01 (1-Deck 2-Tray)**: Best for compact cafes, specialty pizza shops, or boutique pastry studios running single-recipe menus and standard 220V single-phase electrical supply;
    * **Choose OVE-02 (2-Deck 4-Tray)**: The industry sweet spot for artisan bakeries and busy bistros, enabling simultaneous multi-temperature baking (e.g., bread on one deck, cookies on another) for venues serving 100–300 customers daily;
    * **Choose OVE-03 (3-Deck 6-Tray)**: Essential for wholesale production bakeries and central kitchens requiring vertical space efficiency and massive simultaneous tray output.
* **Q: Can this deck oven bake authentic Italian thin-crust (Neapolitan) pizza directly?**
  * **A**: Yes, absolutely. With a chamber depth of 630 mm and width of 870 mm, it accommodates multiple 10″ to 12″ pizza screens. By placing optional commercial cordierite pizza baking stones directly on the deck and setting the bottom heat between 280°C and 300°C, the stone delivers intense conductive heat to produce an authentic crispy crust with characteristic leopard-spot char in 5–8 minutes.
* **Q: What should I do if baking coloration is slightly uneven between the front and back?**
  * **A**: While heating tube geometry is optimized for even distribution, opening the door naturally introduces minor temperature differences near the entrance. To ensure perfection:
    1. Leave a 3–5 cm gap between adjacent trays for convective air movement rather than jamming them against the chamber walls;
    2. For delicate items like chiffon cakes or macarons, rotate the baking trays 180° once the dough has set (around 60% through the baking cycle).
