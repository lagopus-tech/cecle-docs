## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

To guarantee electrical safety, prolong the service life of roller bearings and drive gears, and maintain food hygiene standards, strictly observe the following procedures:

### 2.1 Pre-Start Inspection & Electrical Installation

1. **Placement & Clearance**: Place the unit on a flat, sturdy, heat-resistant countertop. Maintain at least **10 cm (4 inches)** of ventilation clearance around the unit, away from flammable items and water splash zones.
2. **Glass Sneeze Guard Assembly**: Carefully insert the curved glass canopy and side panels into the body mounting slots, tightening the thumb screws securely.
3. **Electrical Specifications**:
   * TD-7 (1050 W) and TD-10 (1500 W) operate on standard 220V/50Hz (or optional 110V/60Hz export customization). Connect directly to an independent, grounded wall outlet rated for the load. Avoid using cheap or overloaded multi-plug extension cords.
4. **Drip Tray Position**: Ensure the slide-out stainless steel drip tray is fully inserted into the bottom base before turning on the power.

### 2.2 Dual-Zone Temperature Settings & Operation

1. **Power-Up**: Plug in the machine, switch on the Power and Roller/Motor switches. The rollers will begin rotating smoothly, and the indicator lights will illuminate.
2. **Pre-Greasing (Anti-Stick Precaution)**: While cold or slightly warm, apply a light, even layer of food-grade cooking oil across all stainless steel rollers using a silicone basting brush.
3. **Preheating the Zones**:
   * Set the **Rear Temperature Dial** to **160°C–180°C** for grilling;
   * Set the **Front Temperature Dial** to **60°C–80°C** for holding;
   * Allow the machine to preheat for 3–5 minutes until the heating indicator lights click off.
4. **Loading Food**: Use clean, stainless steel food tongs to place thawed or chilled sausages evenly between the rear roller grooves.

### 2.3 Cooking & Temperature Reference Chart

| Food Product                 | Recommended Grilling Temp | Grilling Time | Optimal Holding Temp  | Quality Highlights & Pro Tips                                |
| :--------------------------- | :------------------------ | :------------ | :-------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pork Sausages / Hot Dogs** | Rear Zone: 160°C–180°C    | 12–15 mins    | Front Zone: 65°C–75°C | Crisp, taut casing with juicy bite; move forward once cooked |
| **Cheese-Stuffed Sausages**  | Rear Zone: 140°C–150°C    | 15–18 mins    | Front Zone: 60°C–70°C | Moderate heat prevents casing burst and cheese leakage       |
| **Beef Frankfurters**        | Rear Zone: 170°C–190°C    | 10–12 mins    | Front Zone: 70°C–80°C | Glossy, aromatic, perfect pairing for hot dog buns           |
| **Fish Tofu Skewers**        | Uniform Zone: 120°C–140°C | 8–10 mins     | Uniform: 60°C         | Golden exterior, tender center, non-stick performance        |
| **Sweet Corn Cobs**          | Rear Zone: 180°C–200°C    | 15–20 mins    | Front Zone: 75°C      | Brush with light butter to caramelize and lock in sweetness  |

**Safety & Burn Prevention Warning (⚠️)**:
* Roller surfaces reach temperatures between 150°C and 250°C during grilling. **Never touch the rollers or metal casing with bare hands!**
* Always use **long-handled food tongs** to load or serve sausages.
* If a sausage casing expands excessively, reduce the temperature or prick small pinholes to release steam and prevent casing rupture.

### 2.4 End-of-Day Cleaning & Maintenance

* **Power Off & Cool Down**: Turn both temperature dials to "OFF", switch off the master power, and disconnect the plug. Allow the rollers to cool to a warm 40°C–50°C.
* **Roller Cleaning (Non-Abrasive)**:
  * While rollers are warm, wipe along each roller length with a damp microfiber cloth and mild dish soap to remove grease and carbon residues;
  * **Strict Warning**: **Never use steel wool, abrasive scouring pads, metal scrapers, or harsh acid/alkali chemicals on the rollers!** Scratching the mirror-finish stainless steel will cause food to stick permanently.
* **Drip Tray & Glass**: Slide out the drip tray, discard waste oil, wash in a sink, dry thoroughly, and slide back into place. Wipe the glass cover with a clean damp cloth.
* **Water Hazard Warning**: **Never immerse the unit in water or spray it with a high-pressure hose!** Internal motors and electrical wiring will short-circuit.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: How do I choose between the TD-7 (7-roller) and TD-10 (10-roller)?**
  * **A**: The primary decision factors are **countertop depth and customer volume**:
    * **Choose TD-7**: If counter space is tight (such as convenience store register counters, small tea/juice shops, or trunk markets). Its 330 mm depth saves vital space while the 20-sausage capacity easily handles regular pedestrian flow.
    * **Choose TD-10**: If you operate in high-traffic peak venues like cinema lobbies, tourist scenic spots, or large cafeterias where you need to display multiple flavors (pork, beef, cheese, corn) simultaneously. The 10-roller capacity prevents supply shortages during rushes.
* **Q: Why are some rollers not spinning, or why is there a squeaking sound during operation?**
  * **A**: Rollers are synchronized via internal heavy-duty gears and chain links. Squeaking or sluggish rotation typically results from cooking grease penetrating the gear chamber or dried carbon buildup jamming gear teeth. Disconnect power, clean the gear compartment, and apply a small amount of food-grade high-temperature grease to restore whisper-quiet performance.
* **Q: How can I prevent sausage casings from bursting and losing juices?**
  * **A**: Casings burst primarily due to **excessive heat or grilling frozen sausages directly at high temperatures**. We recommend:
    1. Fully thawing sausages before placing them on the grill;
    2. Starting the rear heating zone at a gentle 150°C–160°C rather than turning it immediately above 200°C;
    3. Moving cooked sausages to the front 65°C holding zone immediately once done.
