**Engineering & Manufacturing Highlights**:

* **Independent Dual-Plate Temperature Control**: Both the upper and lower baking plates are equipped with commercial-grade step-less thermostat knobs and independent heating indicators. Operators can choose dual-sided heating, lower-plate-only heating, or set distinct temperatures for each plate to achieve layered textures such as "crispy base with a soft top."
* **Floating Self-Adjusting Suspension Lid**: The upper heating plate features a mechanical floating suspension mechanism. * **Heavy-Duty Heat-Retaining Non-Stick Plates**: Large-diameter (typically 50 cm – 80 cm class) deep pans are treated with a specialized food-grade non-stick surface. The **Floor-Standing model** includes multi-directional locking casters for smooth repositioning around commercial kitchens or street-side pop-ups, while the **Countertop model** provides a compact, stable footprint on standard stainless steel worktables.

### 2. Core Functions & Operating Principles

* **Double-Sided Energy-Gathering Contact Baking**: Place prepared dough onto the lower plate and close the upper lid. Dual heating currents penetrate the dough simultaneously from top and bottom, cutting baking cycle times by over 50% compared to traditional single-sided griddles while instantly sealing in natural moisture to achieve a crisp shell and tender center.
* **Automatic Thermostatic Cycling**: Once the desired temperature (e.g., 200°C / 392°F) is reached, the unit automatically switches to an insulated holding state. As the surface temperature dips, heating elements automatically re-engage, completely eliminating the risk of scorching or undercooking.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Production Workflow

To ensure optimal baking performance, extend the non-stick surface lifespan, and safeguard kitchen personnel, strictly adhere to the following procedures:

### 1. Pre-Operation Inspection, Wiring & Pan Seasoning

1. **Electrical Safety**: Due to high commercial power draw, ensure the unit is connected to a dedicated ground-fault circuit breaker matching its rated voltage (220V commercial single-phase, 110V custom dedicated circuit, or 380V 3-phase). Never use light-duty extension cords. Lock the caster brakes on floor-standing models.
2. **Initial Pan Seasoning**: Before first use, wipe both plates thoroughly with a damp microfiber cloth. Heat the plates to 100°C (212°F), apply a thin, even coat of high-smoke-point edible oil across both surfaces to season the non-stick layer, allow it to bake empty for several minutes, then wipe clean with paper towels.
3. **Independent Preheating**: Turn on the power and activate both the top and bottom switches. Set the thermostat knobs to your target operating temperature (typically 180°C–220°C / 356°F–428°F for flatbreads). When the heating indicator lamps turn off, preheating is complete, and the plates are thermally stabilized.

### 2. Standardized Baking Guide (e.g., Thousand-Layer Spiced Sauce Pancake)

1. **Base Oiling**: Lightly brush a thin layer of cooking oil onto the heated lower plate.
2. **Loading Dough**: Spread the rolled large-diameter flatbread dough evenly onto the lower plate.
3. **Double-Sided Press Baking**: For pastries requiring dual-sided baking, pull down the top handle to close the lid. The floating suspension mechanism will automatically adapt to the dough's initial thickness.
4. **Baking Cycle**: Allow the flatbread to bake for 2 to 4 minutes (depending on dough hydration and thickness). The automatic thermostat handles temperature regulation continuously.
5. **Saucing & Removal**: Lift the top lid once the cycle is complete. The top will be golden and flaky. Brush savory sauce and sprinkle chopped scallions/sesame seeds directly on the plate, then lift the entire flatbread out with a wide wooden or food-grade silicone spatula for slicing.

### 3. Shutdown, Cleaning & Daily Preventive Maintenance

* **Warm Cleaning Only (Never Quench with Cold Water)**: At the end of each shift, shut off all controls and disconnect the main power. While the pan retains a mild residual heat of approximately **50°C–60°C (122°F–140°F)**, wipe away surface oil and crumbs with a damp soft towel. **NEVER pour cold water directly onto hot baking plates! Thermal shock will crack heavy-duty cast metal plates and cause catastrophic peeling of non-stick coatings.**
* **No Sharp or Abrasive Tools**: For stubborn baked-on debris, pour a small amount of warm water or cooking oil onto the spot to soften it, then gently scrape it off using a silicone or wooden spatula. Never scrub with steel wire balls, metal spatulas, or sharp blades.
* **Electrical Housing Ingress Prevention**: Wipe the stainless steel exterior with a neutral detergent and a damp cloth. **Never spray or pressure-wash the unit with water hoses**, as liquid ingress will short-circuit internal wiring and thermostatic controls.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Can this machine be used with the top heating off as a standard single-sided flat griddle / teppanyaki?**
  * **A**: Absolutely. The top and bottom baking plates operate on completely independent heating circuits. When preparing food items that require only bottom-contact heat—such as pan-fried soup dumplings (Shengjianbao), fried eggs, or grilled street cold noodles—you can simply leave the top switch off (or tilt the lid upright) and operate the lower plate as a large-capacity commercial electric griddle.
* **Q: What is the benefit of a "floating suspension lid"? Will it crush thick, yeast-leavened flatbreads?**
  * **A**: The floating suspension system is engineered specifically for leavened doughs that puff up while baking. The lid is not locked into a rigid gap; instead, it has flexible vertical travel. As yeast flatbreads or flaky pastries expand from internal steam, the lid gently rises with the bread's volume, guaranteeing a fluffy, airy interior crumb rather than a dense, compressed slab.
* **Q: If batter or sauce accidentally burns onto the surface, how can it be cleaned without damaging the non-stick coating?**
  * **A**: Never scrub with wire mesh or sharp knives. Wait until the unit is powered off and cooled down to a warm touch (around 50°C), pour a small pool of warm water or cooking oil over the burnt residue, let it soak for a few minutes to soften, and then push it away effortlessly using a wooden or silicone spatula before wiping dry with a microfiber cloth.
