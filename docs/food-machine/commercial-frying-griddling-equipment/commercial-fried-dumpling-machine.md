### 2. Core Functions & Operating Principles

The equipment operates on a proven **Steam-Frying Composite Cooking Principle**:

* **Bottom Contact Searing**: Raw dumplings or stuffed buns are aligned on the preheated, lightly oiled griddle plate; high-power heating elements quickly sear and set the dough base to a light golden crisp.
* **Enclosed High-Temperature Steam Braising**: Clean water or premixed starch slurry (for crispy lace skirts) is injected, and the heavy hood is sealed immediately. Intense saturated steam envelops the food, thoroughly cooking the wrapper and meat filling within minutes while locking in natural juices.
* **Moisture Evaporation & Bottom Crust Crisp-Up**: Once the timer elapses and the hood is lifted, residual steam evaporates rapidly within 20–30 seconds. The base re-engages with residual cooking oil, producing an enticing, crackling golden-brown crust.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Production Workflow

To achieve the signature crispy lace skirt and prolong the non-stick pan lifespan, adhere strictly to the following steps:

### 1. Preparation & Preheating

* **Dough & Slurry Preparation**: Prepare fresh or frozen dumplings, potstickers, or raw stuffed buns. If creating a classic "crispy lace skirt" (Binghua), premix a starch slurry (standard ratio: Water : Starch : Cooking Oil ≈ 10 : 1 : 0.5).
* **Electrical Connection Check**: Place the unit on a level, heat-resistant worktable and connect to a dedicated 110V or 220V circuit equipped with leakage protection.
* **Setting Preheating Parameters**:
  1. Switch on the main power and set the thermostat dial to **160°C–200°C (320°F–392°F)** (adjusting slightly for frozen versus room-temperature dough).
  2. Set the digital countdown timer (typically 4–5 minutes for thin-skinned gyoza; 6–8 minutes for thick-skinned yeast buns).
  3. The indicator light turns off once the pan reaches target temperature.

### 2. Standardized Frying Operation

1. **Light Base Oiling**: Use a food-grade silicone brush to spread a thin, uniform layer of cooking oil across the hot griddle.
2. **Arranging Dough Portions**: Place raw buns or dumplings neatly into the pan (leave slight expansion gaps between yeast-leavened buns). Sear dry for 30–60 seconds to firm the base.
3. **Water Injection & Hood Sealing**: Pour in clean water or your prepared lace slurry (submerging approximately 1/3 of the dumpling height), then close the stainless steel lid immediately.
4. **Initiating Countdown**: Press the timer start button. The machine executes the enclosed steam-braising cycle automatically while kitchen staff prepare side dishes or packaging.
5. **Acoustic Buzzer & Crisp-Up**: When the buzzer sounds, cancel the alarm and lift the lid wearing heat-resistant gloves. Allow residual steam to evaporate for 20–30 seconds until sizzling resumes and the pan dries. Slide a wide flat spatula underneath to lift out a solid sheet of golden dumplings.

### 3. Shutdown, Cleaning & Preventive Maintenance

* **Warm Cleaning Only (Never Quench Hot Plates with Cold Water)**: Power off and disconnect the plug at shift close. While the pan retains gentle residual heat (**around 50°C / 122°F, safe to touch**), wipe away grease and crumbs with a damp microfiber cloth or soft sponge. **NEVER pour cold or iced water onto a hot dry pan—thermal shock will warp the cast alloy plate and cause the non-stick coating to delaminate!**
* **No Sharp Metal Implements**: Never use steel wire wool, rigid metal scrapers, or sharp knives on the non-stick surface. Soften stubborn residue by soaking with warm water for several minutes, then push off gently with a silicone or wooden spatula.
* **Protect Electrical Enclosures**: Wipe the exterior stainless steel casing clean with a damp towel. **Never spray the unit with pressure washer wands or splash water directly onto digital control dials or rear cooling vents!**

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Can the two wells on the dual-head model (FD-2) run at different temperatures and timings independently?**
  * **A**: Yes, completely. The FD-2 incorporates fully isolated dual operating systems. Each cooking well features its own dedicated power switch, analog thermostat dial, and digital countdown timer. You can set the left pan to 180°C for 6 minutes for thick-crusted Shengjian buns, while running the right pan at 200°C for 4 minutes for thin-skinned Japanese gyoza. During slow business hours, simply leave one side powered down to cut electrical consumption.
* **Q: Why is it necessary to inject water and close the lid during cooking? What is the culinary benefit?**
  * **A**: Searing dry in an open pan easily burns the bottom before the raw meat filling cooks through. Injecting water and sealing the lid traps high-temperature steam that rapidly cooks the dough wrapper and raw meat filling while locking in natural broth. As the water evaporates, residual oil re-sears the bottom, yielding the signature texture: a soft, juicy steamed top with a crackling, golden crust.
* **Q: Can this machine cook frozen raw dumplings and unthawed buns directly?**
  * **A**: Yes. Raw frozen dumplings can be loaded straight into the pan without prior thawing. Simply add approximately 10%–20% more water and extend the digital timer by 1–2 minutes. Thanks to the heavy thermal inertia of the cast alloy pan, frozen items will not cause sudden temperature drops, ensuring a uniform, crispy finish every time.
