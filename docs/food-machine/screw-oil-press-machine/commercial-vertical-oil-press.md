Encased in food-grade thickened stainless steel with flexible 220V / 380V electrical configurations, it is a high-visibility, high-return asset for modern oil retailers seeking high output, minimal waste, and compact shop-floor footprints.

### 2. Core Functions & Operating Principles

The system operates on **Continuous Mechanical Volumetric Compression** and **Microscopic Cell Wall Rupture**:

* **Gravity Infeed & Screw Propulsion**: Raw seeds feed by gravity from the large-capacity stainless steel conical hopper into the infeed throat. The rotating alloy screw drives the material forward continuously along a deepening helical channel.
* **Progressive Compression & High-Temperature Hot Pressing**: As seeds advance, the helical pitch narrows, drastically reducing channel volume. Combined with controlled heat conducted from the external heating band, seeds experience intense mechanical shear, friction, and multi-megapascal compressive forces that rupture oil-bearing cellular structures to expel crude virgin oil.
* **Multi-Directional Drainage & Automated Cake Extrusion**: Extracted virgin oil trickles through precision drainage slots along the circumference of the barrel, draining into a catch pan and passing through a multi-tier stainless steel mesh strainer. Thoroughly de-oiled dry meal is continuously extruded through the front conical cake discharge nozzle as thin, crisp wafers, automating continuous oil-slag separation.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Production Workflow

To ensure reliable, high-load commercial operation, protect working components, and prevent foreign-object damage, strictly adhere to the following protocol:

### 1. Raw Ingredient Preparation & Quality Standards

* **Moisture Content Standards (The Decisive Factor for Oil Yield)**:
  * **Use ONLY thoroughly dried oilseeds! NEVER load damp, freshly harvested, or moisture-laden crops!** Excessive moisture causes seeds to slip and churn inside the barrel without building compressive resistance, leading to cloudy, emulsified oil and chamber packing.
  * **Dryness Testing Standard**: Peanut skins should rub off crisply between fingers; sesame seeds should rustle dryly; soybeans must be bone-hard. Damp crops must be sun-dried, roasted, or pan-dried and allowed to cool to ambient temperature before pressing.
* **Foreign Object Screening**:
  * Screen all seeds through a fine mesh prior to filling the hopper. Sift out rocks, dirt clods, tramp iron, nails, and bolts. **Hard metallic debris will crack or chip the precision alloy screw and barrel!**

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Preheating

1. Wheel the unit onto a level, firm concrete or tile floor and **lock all caster wheel brakes securely** to prevent machine movement.
2. Verify that the screw drive keyway is firmly seated and that the cake-thickness adjustment sleeve is set to its calibrated baseline.
3. Connect the power cable to a dedicated industrial power switch matching the rated voltage (**220V 50Hz or 380V 50Hz**), ensuring the ground line is properly bonded.
4. **Initiate Barrel Preheating**:
   * Turn on the digital heating control switch. Set target temperatures according to seed variety (Peanuts: 180°C–200°C; Sesame: 160°C–180°C; Soybeans: 200°C–220°C).
   * Allow the machine to preheat for **10 to 15 minutes** until the temperature indicator stabilizes, ensuring thermal equilibrium throughout the barrel before introducing ingredients.

### 3. Standardized Commercial Pressing Operation

1. **Start Drive Motor**: Press the **"Start / Forward"** button. Confirm that the drive shaft rotates in the correct direction and runs smoothly without metallic rubbing sounds.
2. **Initial Gradual Feeding**: Drop a small handful of seeds into the hopper throat. Observe the front discharge nozzle. Once the machine begins steadily extruding firm cake strips and oil trickles from the barrel slots, compressive backpressure is established.
3. **Continuous Full-Hopper Pressing**: Fill the hopper with raw material. The unit operates in fully automatic continuous mode; staff simply monitor cake clearance and container levels.
4. **Oil Catching & Straining**: Place a clean stainless steel container fitted with a fine-mesh strainer beneath the drainage apron to capture sediment fines.
5. **Anti-Jamming Reverse Protocol**: If a foreign object or aggressive feeding causes a sudden motor stall, immediately press **"Stop"**, then press and hold **"Reverse"** for 3 to 5 seconds to back the material out of the compaction zone. Clear the obstruction before resuming forward operation.
6. **Shift-End Purge**: Once the hopper empties, let the motor idle for 2 to 3 minutes until no further cake extrudes and the barrel is completely empty. Turn off the motor switch, then power down the heating system.

### 4. Teardown, Cleaning & Preventative Maintenance

* **Warm Disassembly Routine**:
  * **Safety Priority: Operators MUST wear heavy heat-resistant gloves!** Disassemble while the barrel retains mild residual heat. Loosen the cake adjustment head and locking collar, then withdraw the screw shaft. Scrub the helical grooves thoroughly with a brass-wire cleaning brush. (*If allowed to cool completely, hardened oil and meal residue will lock the screw in place, making extraction extremely difficult.*)
* **Cleaning & Corrosion Protection**: Wipe all alloy components clean with dry shop towels, apply a thin protective film of clean vegetable cooking oil across surfaces to prevent oxidation, and reassemble for the next shift.
* **Gearbox Lubrication**: Inspect the gearbox oil sight glass every 300 hours of continuous operation. Top up or replace with designated industrial gear lubricant as needed.
* **Protect Electrical Enclosure**: Wipe the stainless steel exterior with a damp towel. **NEVER wash the motor housing, switch gear, or electrical cabinet with high-pressure hoses or running water!**

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: What are the main operational differences between the S02 Standard and S02 Plus? Which should I choose?**
  * **A**: Both models feature all-stainless steel commercial construction. The primary differences lie in processing throughput, machine mass, and heating power:
    1. **S02 Standard**: Fitted with an 1800W heating band, processes 15–25 kg/h, weighs approx. 100 kg, and features a compact square footprint—ideal for supermarket fresh corners and community retail counters;
    2. **S02 Plus**: Equipped with a 2200W high-output heating collar and an extended barrel assembly, processes 20–30 kg/h, and weighs 150 kg. The heavier structural mass delivers greater rigidity under heavy continuous loads, making it ideal for non-stop processing runs and dense crops like soybeans.
* **Q: Can this machine extract oil from raw soybeans? What are the operating requirements?**
  * **A**: Yes. Soybeans are low in oil content (approx. 16%–20%) and have an extremely dense, hard texture that requires high compressive torque and mechanical backpressure. The S02's 1500W industrial drive and hardened alloy screw handle soybeans with ease. Ensure soybeans are completely dry and engage the high-temperature hot-press mode (preheat past 200°C). Deep cell rupture expels available oils, while the residual soybean cake discharges as thin, toasted wafers with high commercial feed value.
* **Q: Does a brand-new machine require a running-in / seasoning period before full production?**
  * **A**: Yes, a brief running-in procedure is recommended. Factory-fresh screws and barrels carry protective machining oil and slight manufacturing micro-burrs. Before initial food production, prepare 5–10 kg of low-cost seeds (such as rapeseeds or oily cake meal), heat the barrel to 180°C, and run the material through for 15–20 minutes. This polishes the alloy contact surfaces and cleans out residue. Drain and discard the test batch; the machine is then fully seasoned for maximum extraction yields.
