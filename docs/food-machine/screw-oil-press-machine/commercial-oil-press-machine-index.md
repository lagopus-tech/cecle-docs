> 💡 **Series Overview:** The **CECLE Multifunctional Screw Oil Press Machine Series** provides chemical-free physical extraction solutions for health-conscious homes, organic grocery fresh-pressing counters, community grain and oil mills, agricultural cooperatives, and commercial food processors. ## 3. Buyer's Selection & Decision Matrix

Choose the model that aligns with your production volume, available electrical service, and operational footprint:

### Choose the ZYJ Series if:
1. **Your daily demand is under 20 kg:** You need fresh, daily batches for household nutrition, baby food, or specialty restaurant menu items.
2. **Space and portability are your primary constraints:** The machine must fit onto a domestic kitchen counter or pack into a travel crate easily.
3. **Budget and clean-up speed are critical:** You want an affordable entry point with a single-barrel assembly that washes clean in minutes.

### Choose the Model B04 if:
1. **You need commercial-scale output on residential power:** You must process 12–25 kg/h (up to 100 kg/day) but only have standard 110V or 220V household outlets available.
2. **You operate live-pressing retail counters:** The dynamic spectacle of four heads extruding oil and crisp cakes simultaneously attracts retail foot traffic in grocery stores and markets.
3. **You require multi-channel redundancy:** You want uninterrupted operation where individual channels can be serviced or reversed without halting entire production.

### Choose the S02 Series if:
1. **Your operation processes dense, low-oil grains:** You frequently press rock-hard soybeans, cottonseeds, or unhulled rapeseeds that demand 1500W industrial reduction-gear torque.
2. **You run all-day commercial processing shifts:** You require continuous processing throughput of 15–30 kg/h to fulfill bulk wholesale contracts or toll-milling services.
3. **Floor-standing industrial ergonomics are preferred:** You prefer an upright, wheeled unit with large-capacity hopper feeding and integrated high-mass vibration damping.

## 4. Shared Engineering & Quality Standards

Regardless of model, every CECLE screw oil press incorporates the following core manufacturing benchmarks:

1. **100% Physical Mechanical Extraction:** Pure mechanical screw compression without chemical solvents, bleaches, or artificial additives, preserving natural vitamins and unsaturated fatty acids.
2. **Food-Grade SUS304 Product Contact:** Infeed hoppers, exterior housings, collection trays, and strainers are built from certified food-grade stainless steel for washdown sanitation.
3. **High-Frequency Quenched Alloy Screws:** Helical screws and barrels are heat-treated with high-frequency carburizing to resist extreme compressive wear and abrasive seed friction.
4. **Worldwide Electrical & Logistics Support:** Configurable with 110V, 220V, or 380V power systems, supported by DDP ocean freight and express air door-to-door delivery with customs clearance included.
5. **Manufacturer Warranty & Technical Support:** Covered by a **1-year commercial warranty** on core motors, gearboxes, and heating elements, backed by video technical guidance and expedited spare parts dispatch.
