## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Production Workflow

To ensure balanced extraction across all four channels, prolong alloy screw working life, and prevent barrel clogging, strictly adhere to the following protocol:

### 1. Raw Ingredient Preparation & Moisture Control

* **Oilseeds Must Be Thoroughly Dry**:
  * **NEVER feed damp, freshly harvested, or wet raw materials directly into the barrels!** Excessive moisture causes the screws to slip and idle without generating internal pressure, resulting in zero oil flow and packing wet meal inside the chambers.
  * **Dryness Testing Standard**: Peanut skins should rub off crisply between palms; walnuts should snap cleanly; sesame seeds should rustle dryly. If slightly damp, gently roast seeds in a pan or dry them in an oven at 100°C (212°F) for 5–10 minutes and cool to room temperature before pressing.
* **Pre-Processing for Specialty Crops**:
  * **Coconut (Copra)**: Use matured, dehydrated coconut meat, diced into small 5–8 mm cubes and thoroughly dried.
  * **Olive & Palm Fruit**: **Must be 100% destoned and pitted!** Feed only the dried or dehydrated flesh. Hard stones will chip or jam the hardened alloy screws.
  * **Shelled Nuts (Walnuts, Hazelnuts, Almonds)**: Fully remove hard outer wooden shells; press kernels only.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Preheating

1. Position the machine on a flat, dry, and stable work surface.
2. Confirm that all 4 sets of screw rods, barrels, and quick-release locking pins are fully seated and latched.
3. Plug the power cord into a dedicated, grounded **110V or 220V electrical outlet** (avoid sharing circuits with other heavy thermal appliances).
4. **Initiate Preheating (Hot Press Mode)**:
   * Turn on the master heating switch and allow the 4 barrels to preheat for **5 to 8 minutes** until operating temperatures stabilize (approx. 150°C–180°C / 302°F–356°F).
   * *(Note: For unheated cold pressing, leave the heating switch off, ensuring seeds are brittle and dry).*

### 3. Standardized 4-Channel Pressing Operation

1. **Engage Drive Motors**: Turn on the main squeezing switch and verify that all 4 screws rotate forward smoothly without metallic rubbing sounds.
2. **Gradual Staggered Feeding**: Pour prepared seeds into the upper hopper. Feed small quantities initially until all 4 front discharge ports steadily extrude dry cake strips, then fill the hopper for continuous operation.
3. **Primary Filtration & Oil Catching**: Crude oil continuously drains into the lower catch pan, passing through the high-density stainless steel strainer to remove suspended meal fines.
4. **Anti-Jamming Reverse Operation**: If an irregular feed causes a motor to slow or stall, immediately turn off the squeezing switch and press and hold the **"Reverse"** button for 3 to 5 seconds to back the material out of the pinch point. Clear the restriction, then resume forward rotation.
5. **Shift-End Purging**: Once the hopper empties, let the motor idle empty for 1 to 2 minutes to evacuate residual cake from the barrels, then turn off both the motor and heating switches.

### 4. Teardown, Cleaning & Preventative Maintenance

* **Warm Disassembly Rule (Never Allow Residue to Cool & Lock)**:
  * **Safety Priority**: Always **wear heat-resistant gloves** after shutdown. While the barrels retain gentle residual heat (**approx. 50°C–60°C / 122°F–140°F**), pull the locking pins and slide out the 4 barrels and screws. If left to cool completely, hardened oil and meal will cement the screws inside the barrels, making disassembly extremely difficult.
* **Washing & Protective Oiling**: Wash the disassembled screws and barrels with warm water, neutral detergent, and the included wire cleaning brush. After cleaning, **dry parts thoroughly with a towel or dry heat**, wipe a thin film of edible cooking oil over the metal surfaces to prevent surface oxidation, and reassemble.
* **Keep Drive Enclosure Dry**: The chassis houses electric motors and circuit boards. **NEVER wash the main body with high-pressure sprayers or running faucets!** Clean the stainless steel exterior using only a damp microfiber cloth.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: What is the primary operational advantage of a 4-head press over a single-head machine?**
  * **A**: The core advantage is **delivering commercial-scale throughput within a compact, countertop-friendly footprint**:
    1. **Multiplied Hourly Throughput**: While single-head units extract only 3–5 kg per hour, Model B04's four parallel channels process 12–25 kg per hour—a 4x to 5x increase in operational efficiency;
    2. **Accessible Household Power**: High output without demanding 380V industrial three-phase power; it runs on standard single-phase 110V or 220V outlets with a total draw of just 1.2 kW;
    3. **Space-Saving Portability**: Measuring just 56 × 39 cm and weighing 23.5 kg, it eliminates the need for massive factory floor space or permanent structural tables.
* **Q: Why does fresh-pressed oil appear slightly hazy or cloudy? Is an expensive centrifugal filter required?**
  * **A**: No expensive commercial filter is needed. Fresh, unrefined virgin oil naturally carries micro-particles of plant fiber and natural phospholipids immediately after extraction. Simply pass the oil through the included fine-mesh screen into a clean container and let it settle in a cool, dark location for **12 to 24 hours**. Gravitational settling precipitates all botanical fines to the bottom, leaving crystal-clear, aromatic virgin oil on top.
* **Q: When all 4 channels run concurrently, can a single clogged channel burn out the motor?**
  * **A**: No. The B04 power control system includes integrated thermal overload protection. The drive channels feature isolated dynamic load management. If foreign material causes a channel to stall, the motor protection safeguards against thermal damage. Operators can easily clear feed obstructions using the dedicated one-touch "Reverse" function.
