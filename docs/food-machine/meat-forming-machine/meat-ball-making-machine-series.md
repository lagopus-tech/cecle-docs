### 2. Core Working Principle & Functional Modules

Powered by an industrial pure copper motor driving a dual-reduction internal gearbox:

* **Extrusion Feeding Mechanism**: Well-beaten, emulsified meat paste loaded into the top hopper is continuously driven downward and compacted by an internal variable-pitch screw auger. This eliminates internal voids and air pockets, ensuring a dense, fine-textured interior.
* **Mold Forming & High-Speed Reciprocating Cut**: The meat paste is continuously extruded through the brass forming die sleeve into a spherical shape. The top reciprocating cutter instantly shears it off, achieving synchronized "one-extrude, one-cut, one-step forming." Dropping immediately into warm water beneath the outlet, the meatballs set rapidly into smooth, round, and springy spheres.
* **Extensive Ingredient Versatility**: Compatible with lean or fat/lean mixed pork paste, dense Chaozhou beef puree, high-viscosity seafood pastes (cuttlefish balls, fish tofu), chicken paste, shrimp paste, and blended meat pastes loaded with finely minced water chestnuts or shiitake mushroom bits.

## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

To maintain consistent forming quality, operational longevity, and operator safety, adhere strictly to the following procedures:

### 1. Raw Material Preparation & Pre-Treatment

* **Paste Requirements**: Raw meat must be pre-ground and emulsified using a commercial meat grinder, bowl cutter, or meat beating machine, thoroughly blended with water, starch, and seasoning.
* **Zero Hard Foreign Objects**: **Never feed hard bone fragments, bone splinters, unthawed rock-hard ice chunks, or metal objects into the hopper!** Hard foreign materials will jam the auger screw and permanently chip or damage the brass die and cutting blades.
* **Forming Water Bath Preparation**: Position a hot water bath tank or continuous setting line containing **50°C–60°C warm water** directly underneath the discharge chute, allowing meatballs to set rapidly into a spherical shape upon dropping.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Safety Verification

1. Place the machine on a level, solid surface and lock all caster brakes.
2. Confirm connection to a properly grounded, compatible **220V (or custom 110V / 380V) power supply**, and verify that the power cord is intact without cuts or wear.
3. Ensure the installed forming mold size matches the scheduled production batch and verify that all retaining bolts and collars are securely tightened.
4. Jog the start button to test-run the machine empty for 10 seconds. Check that the reciprocating blade and screw auger operate smoothly without abnormal friction or grinding sounds.

### 3. Standardized Meatball Forming Operating Guide

1. Power on the machine, bringing the reciprocating cutter and feeding screw auger up to operating speed.
2. Slowly load the emulsified meat paste into the hopper in steady batches (do not overload the throat opening all at once).
3. Inspect the initial batch of meatballs: If they appear slightly flat or oblong, adjust the material feed gate to balance the flow, or fine-tune the cutting cycle timing until meatballs drop uniformly spherical and plump.
4. As cutting continues, meatballs drop continuously into the lower water bath, where they blanch to retain their shape before transfer to the final cooking line or blast freezer.
5. **Safety Warning**: **Never place hands, fingers, or unauthorized rigid tools inside the hopper or near the cutting die while the machine is operating!** In case of feeding issues or blockages, immediately strike the emergency stop button and disconnect power before inspection.

### 4. Post-Shift Sanitation & Anti-Oxidation Maintenance

* **Electrical Safety**: Always switch off the main control switch and disconnect the power plug prior to any cleaning or disassembly.
* **Disassembly & Washing**: Loosen the hopper retaining nuts, pull out the feeding screw auger, brass mold core, discharge tube, and reciprocating cutter assembly. Wash thoroughly in a cleaning sink using warm water and food-grade neutral detergent to remove all residual oils and meat paste.
* **Chassis Cleaning**: Wipe down the stainless steel exterior surfaces using a warm, damp cloth. Never spray pressurized water jets directly into the motor housing or electrical control box.
* **Drying & Lubrication**: Thoroughly dry all metal parts after washing, and **apply a thin layer of edible vegetable oil or food-grade white mineral oil** to the cutting blade and brass mold parts to prevent oxidation, then reassemble for the next shift.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Can the meatball diameter be adjusted? Is mold changeover difficult?**
  * **A**: Yes, meatball diameter is adjusted by swapping the quick-change mold core. The machine accommodates multiple standard die diameters (16 mm, 18 mm, 22 mm, 26 mm, or 40–60 mm on large models). Built with a quick-release collar mechanism, operators can change out the mold set in 3–5 minutes by loosening the retaining collar and sliding the module in or out.
* **Q: How consistent is the sphericity and texture? Can it handle paste mixed with vegetable bits (water chestnuts, mushrooms)?**
  * **A**: Sphericity and texture are consistent and repeatable. The variable-pitch auger compresses the paste to remove internal voids, and the reciprocating blade shears clean edges that settle into round spheres upon entering the water bath. As long as vegetable bits or mushroom dices are smaller than the die aperture and free of hard bones or pits, the machine runs smoothly without clogging.
* **Q: Is the machine easy to clean? Will meat residue build up and cause odor issues?**
  * **A**: Daily cleaning is simple and fast. The hopper, feed tube, extrusion auger, and mold cutting modules are designed for tool-free disassembly. Operators can dismantle the food-contact path in a few minutes and wash components in a standard pot sink, leaving no blind hygiene corners.
