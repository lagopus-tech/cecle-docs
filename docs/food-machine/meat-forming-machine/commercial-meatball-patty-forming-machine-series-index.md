## 2. In-Depth Model Profiles

### Model A: M-160 / SX Series Commercial Meatball Forming Machine
* **Core Identity:** The High-Volume Spherical Meat & Seafood Ball Workhorse
* **Key Mechanism:** Emulsified meat paste loaded into the hopper is driven downward by a variable-pitch auger screw, extruded through a precision wear-resistant brass forming mold, and sheared cleanly by a high-speed reciprocating blade into an immediate hot water setting bath.

* **Best For:** Hot pot restaurant suppliers, Chaozhou beef ball specialty plants, fish ball and cuttlefish ball manufacturers, institutional dining halls, and meat packaging centers.
* **Core Strengths:**
  * Ultra-high throughput: Yields 200–300 meatballs per minute, replacing 8 to 10 manual prep workers;
  * Preserves texture: High-speed cutting minimizes temperature rise, keeping meat proteins bouncy and succulent;
  * Tool-free disassembly: Auger, brass die, and cutter dismantle in minutes for thorough washdown in standard pot sinks.

### Model B: MPS-30L Series Commercial Automatic Hamburger Patty Machine
* **Core Identity:** The Precision Volumetric Burger Patty & Cutlet Press
* **Key Mechanism:** Blended ground meat in a 30L hopper is fed into a reciprocating forming slide. Once compressed within the closed cavity, a timed 6 Bar pulse of air demolds the patty onto a stainless steel wire-mesh conveyor belt.

* **Best For:** Fast-food burger franchises, frozen ready-meal plants, central commissaries, chicken nugget processing lines, and plant-based protein manufacturing facilities.
* **Core Strengths:**
  * Clean pneumatic release: Food-grade polymer molds paired with 6 Bar air blow-off prevent meat sticking and edge tearing;
  * Full dimensional control: Diameter adjustable from 28 to 120 mm with stepless thickness adjustment from 8 to 20 mm;
  * Automated transfer: Finished patties exit on a wire-mesh conveyor, ready for continuous battering, breading, or freezing lines.

## 3. Buyer's Selection & Decision Matrix

Match your menu profiles, product geometry, and back-of-house utility requirements to the appropriate forming system:### Quick Selection Guidelines:
1. **Choose the M-160 / SX Meatball Machine if:** Your core product is spherical (beef balls, pork balls, fish balls, chicken balls) and you need high-volume continuous output dropped directly into a blanching or cooking water bath without needing an external air compressor.
2. **Choose the MPS-30L Patty Machine if:** You supply commercial burger patties, sliders, nuggets, or cutlets that require precise thickness control (8–20 mm), uniform weight, clean edges, and automatic delivery onto a transfer conveyor belt.

## 4. Shared Engineering & Quality Standards

Both machines in the CECLE commercial meat forming lineup are manufactured according to strict food-safety and durability standards:

1. **Food-Grade Stainless Steel Build:** Heavy-gauge SUS304 stainless steel housings, hoppers, and product pathways resist organic meat acids, animal fats, and sanitation chemicals.
2. **Standardized Portion Uniformity:** Mechanical compression and precision volumetric chambers eliminate density variances, preventing product shrinkage and breakage during boiling, grilling, or frying.
3. **Quick-Change Modular Tooling:** Both units feature tool-less or quick-release die mounts, enabling recipe or size changeovers in 5 to 10 minutes.
4. **Worldwide Logistics & Support:** Shipped in fumigation-free reinforced export wooden crates via DDP sea or express air freight, backed by a **1-year factory warranty** and direct remote video engineering assistance.
