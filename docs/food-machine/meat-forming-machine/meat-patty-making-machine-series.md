* **Pneumatic Demolding & Conveyor Transfer**: Once the die reaches the discharge station, the ejection mechanism releases an instantaneous pulse of compressed air to drop the patty smoothly onto the wire-mesh conveyor, maintaining clean, hands-free operation.

## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

To maintain consistent forming quality and extend the service life of internal pneumatic cylinders and molds, adhere strictly to the following procedures:

### 1. Raw Material Preparation & Pre-Treatment

* **Mixture Texture & Temperature**: Meat mixtures should be properly blended and chilled; **recommended working temperature is between -2°C and 4°C**. Slightly chilled meat with fine ice crystals yields the cleanest edges and lowest mold adhesion.
* **Zero Hard Foreign Objects**: **Never load bone splinters, hard cartilage fragments, rock-hard frozen blocks, or metal fasteners into the hopper!** Hard objects will score the precision die surfaces or damage the mechanical feeding mechanism.
* **Air Supply Requirements**: Ensure the workspace is supplied with clean, dry compressed air regulated to a steady **6 Bar**, with secure hose couplings.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Safety Verification

1. Place the machine on a level, solid surface and level the non-slip vibration-dampening feet.
2. Verify that the electrical supply is properly grounded (configurable for 110V/220V/380V), and confirm the pneumatic line is pressurized to the 6 Bar operating threshold.
3. Check that the mold retaining hardware, wire-mesh belt tension, and scrapers are properly seated and secured.
4. Run an unloaded test cycle for 15–30 seconds to verify smooth die stroke action, correct ejection timing, proper belt rotation, and the absence of abnormal mechanical rubbing.

### 3. Standardized Patty Forming Operating Guide

1. Open the main pneumatic valve, turn on the control panel power, and initiate the automated cycle.
2. Load the prepared meat mixture into the 30L hopper (maintain the fill level above 1/3 capacity to provide consistent downward head pressure).
3. Inspect the first few finished patties for shape and thickness; fine-tune the thickness adjustment slide (adjustable within 8–20 mm) as necessary to meet weight specifications.
4. As formed patties emerge continuously from the conveyor, catch them on transfer trays, route them directly to automated battering/breading lines, or interleave them with wax paper for packaging.
5. **Safety Red Line**: **Never insert hands, fingers, or rigid utensils into the bottom of the hopper or the reciprocating path of the mold during operation!** In case of feeding issues or blockages, hit the emergency stop button, cut electrical power, and bleed air pressure before clearing.

### 4. Post-Shift Sanitation & Maintenance

* **Lockout/Tagout Safety**: Before any cleaning or maintenance, **shut off the main power switch, disconnect the electrical plug, and vent the pneumatic supply line**.
* **Component Teardown & Washdown**: Loosen the hopper quick-release fasteners to remove the 30L hopper, mixing paddle, forming die assembly, and conveyor drip trays. Wash with warm water and a neutral food-grade detergent to remove residual oils and meat debris.
* **Electrical Protection**: Avoid aiming high-pressure water streams directly at the control panel, electrical enclosure joints, or motor vents.
* **Drying & Lubrication**: Dry all metal assemblies thoroughly with a clean lint-free cloth after washing, and **apply a thin layer of food-grade white mineral oil or vegetable oil to cylinder guide rails and sliding mold faces** to prevent oxidation and ensure smooth motion on the next shift.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Can the patty size and shape be customized? Can it produce chicken nuggets or square patties?**
  * **A**: Yes. Patty shape and dimensions are determined entirely by the interchangeable mold. The machine accommodates custom dies ranging from 28 to 120 mm in diameter, including standard round, square, rounded rectangular, oval, triangular nuggets, and custom novelty profiles. With quick-release clamps, the entire die slide can be swapped in 5 to 10 minutes.
* **Q: Do patties tend to stick to the mold cavity during high-speed compression?**
  * **A**: No. The unit utilizes food-grade non-stick polymer mold materials paired with a synchronized 6 Bar pneumatic blow-off system. When the die indexes directly over the discharge station, a timed pulse of air releases the patty cleanly onto the wire-mesh belt, leaving smooth edges without tearing or distortion.
* **Q: Is an external air compressor required? What are the air supply requirements?**
  * **A**: Yes, the demolding stroke relies on a pneumatic system, requiring connection to a commercial air compressor capable of maintaining a steady working pressure of at least 6 Bar. It is recommended to install an air filter-regulator-lubricator (FRL unit) with water separation at the air inlet to supply clean, dry air, maximizing valve and cylinder longevity.
