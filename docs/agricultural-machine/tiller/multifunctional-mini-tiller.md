## III. Operation Workflow
1.  **Inspection & Preparation**: Check fuel (two-stroke models require mixed fuel; four-stroke models require separate gasoline and engine oil) and fluid levels, ensuring all components are secure and blades are free of wrapped debris.
2.  **Starting the Engine**: Open the fuel valve, pull out the choke, and pull the hand-pull recoil starter to ignite the gasoline engine. Return the choke to the normal position after warm-up.
3.  **Transportation**: Use the rear or front auxiliary wheels and handles to push the machine to the working plot.
4.  **Tilling Operation**: Firmly grip the clutch/throttle handle, guide the mini tiller forward smoothly, and let the rotating blades cut into the soil layer for tilling or loosening.
5.  **Post-Operation Maintenance**: Turn off the engine, wait for the machine to cool down, clear any grass roots or mud wrapped around the tilding shaft, and store it in a dry, ventilated place.
