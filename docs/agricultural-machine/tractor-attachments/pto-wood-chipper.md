## II. Key Advantages
*   **Hydraulic Feeding System**: This is the greatest advantage of this machine. Using hydraulic rollers to automatically pull branches into the crushing chamber, operators do not need to push the material manually, making it both effortless and safe.
*   **360° Swivel Discharge Chute**: The tall "chimney" can rotate 360 degrees, allowing you to precisely control the direction of the wood chips and spray them directly into truck beds or designated storage points.
*   **Wide Hopper**: The feed hopper is spacious, capable of accommodating branched tree limbs and reducing the workload of pre-pruning.
*   **High Efficiency**: Typically equipped with a heavy-duty flywheel and high-strength blades inside, capable of processing larger diameter timber (usually up to 6 inches or 8 inches, depending on the model).
*   **Sustainability**: Transforms bulky branch piles into compact mulch, which can be used to prevent weed growth, fertilize soil, or serve as raw materials for paper-making and fuel.
*   **Easy Maintenance**: This structure typically allows quick opening of the housing to replace blades or clear internal blockages.

---

## III. Operation Workflow
*   **Equipment Connection**: Mount the wood chipper to the rear of the tractor using the three-point hitch, and securely install the PTO drive shaft and hydraulic hoses.
*   **Safety Inspection**: Inspect the feed hopper, blades, flywheel, and hydraulic system to ensure no internal debris remains and safety guards are properly installed.
*   **Startup & Speed Regulation**: Start the tractor and engage the PTO (typically 540r/min or 1000r/min), adjusting the speed of the hydraulic feed roller.
*   **Feeding & Crushing**: Feed pruned branches or timber into the wide hopper, where the hydraulic system automatically pulls the wood into the crushing chamber for powerful slicing.
*   **Discharge & Shutdown**: Accurately spray wood chips into collection vehicles or designated areas via the 360° swivel discharge chute; after operation, disengage feeding, stop the PTO, and then turn off the tractor.
