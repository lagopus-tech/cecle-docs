# Rotary Cutter

![Product Image](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/aca.png?v=1788419288)

> **Core Abstract**: 
> The Rotary Cutter (Model: TGCJ-1) is a high-efficiency tractor attachment designed for heavy-duty land clearing and pasture maintenance. Featuring a rugged welded steel deck, front chain guards, and a rear structure for precise height adjustment, this machine is driven by the tractor's Power Take-Off (PTO) shaft at 540r/min to effortlessly chop through dense vegetation, tall grass, and woody bushes. Built with heavy-duty free-swinging blades and a reliable gearbox, it provides exceptional durability and safety in harsh outdoor environments, making it the ideal choice for farmlands, orchards, pastures, and wasteland reclamation.

## II. Key Advantages
*   **Heavy-Duty Clearing**: Capable of handling tough weeds and woody bushes with a diameter of several centimeters that standard lawn mowers cannot cut.
*   **Safety Design**: Features a ring of chain guards at the front of the machine to prevent stones or wood blocks from being high-speed ejected to injure operators or damage the tractor.
*   **Durability**: The deck is constructed from thick welded steel plate with a rugged, heavy-duty structure, making it highly suitable for harsh outdoor environments.
*   **Adjustable Cutting Height**: Equipped to allow users to control the cutting height and prevent the machine from "digging into" uneven ground.
*   **Low Maintenance**: Driven by a tractor rear PTO shaft connected to a robust gearbox, featuring a simple internal structure with heavy-duty swing blades that are easy to maintain and replace.

---

## III. Operation Workflow
1.  **Tractor Connection**: Mount the rotary cutter to the tractor using the 3-point hitch and secure the PTO shaft.
2.  **Height Adjustment**: Adjust according to the field conditions to set the desired cutting height.
3.  **Start PTO**: Engage the tractor's PTO at the recommended operating speed of 540r/min to spin the internal heavy-duty blades.
4.  **Mowing Operation**: Drive the tractor forward at a steady pace, allowing the front chain guards to contain debris while the rotating blades chop the vegetation.
5.  **Post-Operation Check**: Disengage the PTO, stop the tractor, and inspect the blades and gearbox for any wear or entangled debris before storage.
