> **Core Summary**:
> The agricultural wheel tractor is a full-feature heavy-duty agricultural machinery designed specifically for small and medium-sized farms, cooperatives, and family farms, offering multiple golden horsepower models such as the WT-504, WT-704, WT-904, WT-1004, and WT-1104. Equipped with a fully-enclosed panoramic cabin, four-wheel drive, and a high-level air intake system, it provides robust power and exceptional terrain adaptability. ## II. Core Advantages
*   **Golden Horsepower & Powerful 4WD**: Offers multiple power ranges with strong performance and good fuel economy. Fitted with deep-lug large-diameter agricultural R-1 tires, it provides outstanding anti-sinking capability and massive traction in heavy soils, wetlands, and slopes, while offering high ground clearance to prevent crop and chassis scraping during inter-row management.
*   **Fully-Enclosed Panoramic Cabin**: Effectively isolates field tilling dust and engine noise. Features curved panoramic glass for a wide field of vision, optional HVAC heating and cooling air conditioning with fans, and a Rollover Protective Structure (ROPS) to eliminate the harsh working conditions of traditional open-air tractors.
*   **High-Level Air Intake & Compact Body Design**: Fitted with an extended high-level intake pipe and pre-filter on the right side of the hood, keeping it away from ground mud and dust raised by tires to significantly extend engine and air filter lifespan. The compact wheelbase and body width ensure high flexibility across both large plain fields and narrow farm paths.
*   **Multi-Purpose Operations**: Equipped with a standard rear 3-point hitch and Power Take-Off (PTO) shaft, supporting almost all common farm implements including rotary tillers, reversible plows, subsoilers, seeders/fertilizer spreaders, sprayers, and agricultural transport trailers.

## III. Operation Workflow
*   **Pre-operation Inspection**: Check fuel levels, engine oil, coolant, and tire pressure, ensuring all control levers (such as the gear shift and PTO levers) are in the neutral position.
*   **Engine Starting**: Sit inside the cabin, insert the key and turn on the power, depress the clutch pedal completely, start the engine, and let it idle to warm up.
*   **Starting and Shifting**: Depress the clutch based on operational requirements, select the appropriate gear (such as 8+8 or 12+12 gear positions), and slowly release the clutch while smoothly applying throttle to move forward.
*   **Implement Attachment & Power Take-Off (PTO)**: Connect the required farm implement (such as a rotary tiller or seeder) via the rear 3-point hitch system, and engage the PTO shaft according to operational needs.
*   **Operation and Completion**: Maintain a steady speed in the field while monitoring instrument panel metrics. After work, lower the farm implement smoothly to the ground, reduce engine speed to idle briefly, and shut down the power.
