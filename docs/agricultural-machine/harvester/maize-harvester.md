## I. Product Overview

* **Category**: Agricultural Machinery / Maize Harvester / Single-Row Walk-Behind Corn Harvesting Equipment.
* **Applications**: Widely used in terraces, hilly and mountainous areas, small irregular agricultural plots, or intercropped plots in orchards where large wheeled four-wheel harvesters cannot enter.
* **Core Function**: Integrates corn ear harvesting and stalk crushing for returning to the field, while centrally collecting the picked corn into a large-capacity hopper to achieve efficient, streamlined harvesting operations.

## II. Core Advantages

1. **Agile & Perfect for Small Plots**: Large wheeled four-wheel harvesters cannot enter terraces, hilly and mountainous terrain, small irregular farm plots, or orchard intercropped areas. In contrast, this walk-behind single-row harvester features a compact body and an extremely small turning radius, allowing a single person to easily push and turn around during operation.
2. **2-in-1 Ear Picking & Stalk Chopping**: Simultaneously harvests corn ears while chopping tall corn stalks and returning them directly to the field, completely eliminating the heavy manual labor of secondary manual stalk cutting and hauling.
3. **Integrated Collection Hopper**: Equipped with a large-capacity grain storage hopper on the side, harvested corn goes directly into the box, saving farmers from the trouble of bending down to pick up corn while harvesting. Once full, simply open the tipping bucket to centrally unload the grain.
4. **Affordable & Fast ROI**: Priced at just a fraction of large combine harvesters (typically several hundred to over a thousand US dollars), it is exceptionally well-suited for small-to-medium farm owners, individual farmers, and agricultural mechanization upgrades in developing countries such as in Africa, Southeast Asia, and Latin America.
5. **High Efficiency & Low Fuel Consumption**: A single machine can harvest 1.5 to 2.5 mu per hour, an efficiency equivalent to the harvesting speed of 10 to 15 skilled adult laborers, with extremely low fuel consumption.
6. **Reliable & Easy Maintenance**: Adopts mature mechanical chain and gear transmissions without complex hydraulic or electronic control systems, allowing farmers to perform self-maintenance and replace wearing parts using simple tools.

## III. Operation Process

1. **Pre-operation Inspection**: Check that all component connections are secure, fuel and oil levels are adequate, and chains, gears, and the header area are free of debris obstructions.
2. **Startup**: Start the diesel engine and warm it up at idle for a few minutes to ensure stable and smooth power output.
3. **Aligning Rows**: Guide the walk-behind machine to align with the corn planting rows and smoothly push it forward into the field.
4. **Simultaneous Harvesting & Crushing**: As the machine moves forward, it automatically completes ear picking while chopping the stalks back into the field.
5. **Unloading & Cleaning**: When the side collection hopper is full, open the tipping bucket to unload the grain; after operation, disconnect the power, clean away residual debris, and perform routine maintenance.
