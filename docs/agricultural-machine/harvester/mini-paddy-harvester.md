## I. Product Overview

* **Category**: Agricultural Machinery / Mini Harvester / Lightweight Crop Harvesting Equipment.
* **Applications**: Widely used in terraces, hilly and mountainous areas, small irregular plots, orchards, and soft mud paddy fields where large combine harvesters cannot enter.
* **Core Function**: Utilizing efficient cutting and mechanical guidance, the system cuts crops and neatly side-windrows them on the ground, facilitating subsequent drying and collection.

## II. Core Advantages

1. **Neat Side-Windrowing**: After the crops are cut, they are not randomly piled up but neatly laid flat on the ground with rice/wheat ears facing the same direction. This facilitates ventilation and drying, makes subsequent manual bundling or threshing easier, and significantly reduces grain-shattering losses.
2. **Compact, Lightweight & Agile**: Featuring a light weight and an extremely small turning radius, the machine offers very flexible operation. It is especially suited for terraces, hilly regions, small irregular plots, and soft paddy fields.
3. **High Cost-Efficiency & Low Fuel Consumption**: Compared to large combine harvesters costing hundreds of thousands, this mini harvester is affordably priced (hundreds to over a thousand US dollars). It features exceptionally low fuel consumption with small gasoline/diesel engines, extremely simple maintenance, and universal spare parts.
4. **Multi-Crop Versatility**: Not only capable of harvesting paddy/rice, it is also widely applicable for soybeans, alfalfa/forage, sesame, reeds, chili peppers, rapeseed, and medicinal herbs/mint.
5. **High Working Efficiency**: A single machine can process 2 to 4 mu per hour, an efficiency equivalent to 15 to 20 skilled manual harvesters, greatly shortening peak harvest times.
6. **Adjustable Stubble Height**: The cutter operates smoothly close to the ground, allowing stubble height to be adjusted according to crop characteristics and agronomic requirements without jamming or damaging roots.

## III. Operation Process

1. **Pre-operation Inspection**: Check that all component connections are secure, fuel and oil levels are adequate, and the cutter and transmission parts are free of debris.
2. **Startup**: Start the gasoline or diesel engine and warm it up at idle for a few minutes to ensure stable power output.
3. **Stubble Adjustment**: Adjust the appropriate stubble height based on the specific crop characteristics and field conditions.
4. **Initiate Harvesting**: Drive or guide the machine smoothly into the field. The cut crops will be neatly discharged to the side via the windrowing device.
5. **Shutdown and Cleaning**: Disconnect power after operation, clean any residual straw from the cutter deck and transmission parts, and perform routine maintenance.
