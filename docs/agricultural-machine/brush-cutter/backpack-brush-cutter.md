## III. Operation Workflow
1.  **Fuel and Oil Preparation**: Confirm the power system according to the purchased model. The 2-stroke requires mixed oil prepared at a standard ratio (e.g., 25:1), while the 4-stroke requires separate additions of pure gasoline and engine oil.
2.  **Backpack and Secure**: Put the machine on both shoulders, adjust the back pad and shock-absorbing spring positions, and fasten the waist fixing belt to ensure the machine's center of gravity fits smoothly against the back.
3.  **Start the Engine**: Open the choke valve, press the fuel primer bulb to draw fuel, pull the recoil starter cord firmly to start the engine, and let it idle to warm up briefly.
4.  **Mowing Operation**: Hold the operating rod tightly with both hands, drive the front cutting tool through the flexible shaft, and perform smooth cutting with a natural swinging motion. Use the circular saw blade to push forward steadily when encountering thick and hard bushes.
5.  **Shutdown and Cleaning**: Gradually reduce the throttle to idle after work, turn off the engine switch, take off the machine, and clean up residual weeds and mud from the cutting tool and body.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q: How should I choose between a 2-stroke and 4-stroke backpack brush cutter?**
A: 2-stroke machines offer high explosive power and simple structure; 4-stroke machines use independent gasoline and engine oil (no mixing required), feature low smoke, eco-friendliness, lower vibration, lower noise, and better fuel economy, making daily use and maintenance much more convenient.

**Q: How thick of a branch or weed can this brush cutter cut?**
A: Equipped with the standard multi-tooth alloy circular saw blade, it can clear normal soft weeds and easily cut through thick reeds, corn stalks, hard weeds, and even finger-thick bushes and tree branches.

**Q: Will it feel very heavy and tiring to carry on the back?**
A: No. The machine adopts an ergonomic backpack design, distributing the weight (8–11 kg) primarily across the back, shoulders, and waist. Both hands only need to control the lightweight operating rod. Combined with shock-absorbing springs, the backpack feel is very light and flexible.
