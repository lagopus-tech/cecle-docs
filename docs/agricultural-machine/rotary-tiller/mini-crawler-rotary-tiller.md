## III. Operating Procedure
1.  **Preparation**: Check engine fuel and oil levels, inspect crawler tension, and ensure the rear-mounted plow is securely attached without looseness.
2.  **Starting**: Open the fuel valve and choke, pull the recoil start rope or use the electric start to ignite the engine, and adjust to normal working speed after warm-up.
3.  **Transporting**: Engage the travel gear and use the crawler chassis to steadily drive the machine to the target plot.
4.  **Operating**: Adjust the entry depth of the rear-mounted plow, control the throttle and steering handles smoothly, and advance evenly to perform deep plowing, soil turning, or trenching.
5.  **Maintenance**: Turn off the engine, allow the machine to cool down completely, thoroughly clean mud and debris from the crawlers and plow, and store in a dry, ventilated area.
