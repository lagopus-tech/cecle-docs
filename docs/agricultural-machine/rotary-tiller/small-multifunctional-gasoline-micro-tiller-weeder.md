## II. Key Advantages

*   **Multifunctional**: By changing different cutter attachments, it achieves multiple operations:

    *   *Inter-row Weeding*: High-speed rotating blades cut off weed root systems and flip them to the surface to dry out.
    *   *Furrowing & Ridging*: Opens drainage or sowing furrows and banks soil up to plant roots.

*   **Easy Operation & Low Maintenance Cost**: Dual-handle design integrating a clutch control lever and throttle switch for good pushing balance; intuitive mechanical structure using a general single-cylinder small engine, making replacement parts easy to buy and keeping maintenance and repair costs low.
*   **High Cost-Performance**: Compared to expensive large and medium-sized agricultural tractors, this type of small agricultural machine is moderately priced (typically a few hundred USD / thousands of RMB), making it exceptionally suitable for family farms, vegetable planting retail growers, and small-area manors.

## III. Operation Workflow
1.  **Inspection & Preparation**: Check engine fuel and oil levels, inspect bolt tightness and cutter attachments to ensure everything is secure and secure.
2.  **Starting the Engine**: Open the fuel valve and choke, pull the recoil starter rope to start the engine, and adjust to normal working speed after warm-up.
3.  **Transportation**: Hold the clutch handle tightly, control the machine to move forward smoothly to the target working plot.
4.  **Tilling Operation**: Select the corresponding cutters according to requirements (tilling, weeding, or furrowing blades), control the throttle and steering handles, and push the machine steadily and uniformly to conduct field work.
5.  **Post-Operation Maintenance**: Shut down the engine, let the body cool down thoroughly, clean off mud and weed residues attached to the body and cutters, and store in a dry and ventilated place.
