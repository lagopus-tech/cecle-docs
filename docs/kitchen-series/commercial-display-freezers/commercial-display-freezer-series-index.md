## 3. Buyer's Selection & Decision Matrix

Choose the model that best matches your venue type, merchandising goals, and operational workflow:

### Choose the Supermarket Display Showcase if:
1. **Raw meat and butcher cuts are your core offering:** You need static direct cooling to keep exposed beef, lamb, and poultry red and juicy without air-drying crusts.
2. **You want a clean, single-tier open retail display:** You operate a butcher shop or supermarket fresh section requiring flat GN pan presentation under clear glass sliding doors.
3. **Daily customer inspection is primary:** You want maximum wide-angle visibility for customers selecting fresh proteins across a standard 60" or 71" counter.

### Choose the Buffet Self-Fetching Well if:
1. **You operate a high-volume self-service restaurant:** Your guests repeatedly open and close the lids to retrieve barbecue skewers, hot pot slices, and dumplings.
2. **You want modular food division:** You need integrated stainless steel divider trays for separating marinated meats, shellfish, and frozen balls cleanly.
3. **Thermal recovery is critical:** You need submerged copper coils that keep internal wells solidly cold even under non-stop dinner rush access.

### Choose the Three-Level Ladder Showcase if:
1. **You need both chilling and freezing in one footprint:** You want to merchandise fresh greens/sauces (2~10°C) alongside frozen meats (0~-15°C) without buying two machines.
2. **Visual presentation is your top priority:** You want a stadium-style 3-tier tiered view where rear items are elevated and never hidden behind front trays.
3. **Energy conservation is essential:** You want a versatile dual-zone workstation that operates on a conservative 300W electrical rating.

### Choose the 4-In-1 Combine Island Freezer if:
1. **You manage a large-format retail supermarket:** You need to merchandise massive volumes of frozen stock in the center aisles of a grocery store.
2. **Floor space requires scalable modular layout:** You want to join curved end-caps and linear centers into freestanding 360° display islands or long parallel runs.
3. **You want to replace power-hungry open air-curtains:** You want to cut retail refrigeration power bills by over 50% while preventing dust and droplet contamination.

## 4. Shared Engineering & Quality Standards

Every model across the CECLE Commercial Display Freezer Series is built to rigorous commercial-grade manufacturing standards:

1. **Non-Drying Direct Cooling / Natural Convection:** Engineered with wrap-around copper evaporator circuits or static convection wells, keeping exposed proteins and delicate frozen goods moist, glossy, and free from freezer burn.
2. **Food-Grade SUS Stainless Steel Construction:** Exterior ledges, customer contact rails, and interior food wells are pressed from high-grade stainless steel for maximum corrosion resistance and effortless chemical sanitation.
3. **High-Clarity Tempered Sliding Glass Lids:** Outfitted with low-emissivity, double-pane tempered glass sliding lids with integrated thermal-break channels to suppress surface fogging and keep products fully visible.
4. **Eco-Friendly High-Efficiency Compressors:** Powered by commercial-grade compressors and green refrigerants (R290 / R134a), delivering rapid thermal pull-down, high energy efficiency ratios, and whisper-quiet operation.
5. **High-Density Cyclopentane Polyurethane Insulation:** Fully injected with high-pressure, zero-ODP polyurethane foam insulation to lock in cold reserves and break thermal bridges.
6. **Turnkey Logistics & Global Warranty:** Supported by heavy-duty export wood-skid packaging, DDP door-to-door ocean delivery (customs & duties cleared), official manufacturer warranties, and lifetime technical video support.
