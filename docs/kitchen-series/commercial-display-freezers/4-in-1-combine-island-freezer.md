## II. Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To ensure reliable, long-term operation and maximum cooling performance, store operations and maintenance personnel must adhere to the following standards:

### 1. Site Preparation & Pre-Installation Requirements (Key Success Factors)

* **Floor Flatness & Alignment**: Modular combination units require a rigid, completely level floor. Level each section precisely using the adjustable base leveling feet before locking modules together and sealing the seams, ensuring smooth operation of the sliding glass lids.
* **Ventilation Clearance**: Never obstruct compressor intake and discharge ventilation louvers with cartons or promotional displays. Maintain at least 6–8 inches (15–20 cm) of unobstructed airflow clearance.
* **Resting Protocol Upon Delivery**: Following logistics transit via ocean container and flatbed truck, **do not plug in or power on the unit immediately!** Keep the unit upright on a level floor for **at least 24 hours** to allow displaced compressor oil to drain back into the sump.

### 2. Pre-Startup Inspection & Splicing Setup

1. Align Front end-cap cabinets with Middle extension units per your floorplan, secure module alignment clamps, and engage base stabilizers.
2. Verify electrical infrastructure: each modular compressor section must connect to its own dedicated, properly rated commercial **grounded wall outlet**. Never daisy-chain with multi-outlet power strips.
3. Slide open the top tempered glass lids, remove internal protective foam, and inspect wire baskets and sliding tracks to ensure clean, frictionless motion.

### 3. Standard Operating Guidelines

1. **Empty-Load Pre-Cooling**: Close all sliding lids, switch on power, and run empty for **2 to 3 hours**. Load frozen products only after the digital display confirms stable deep-freeze storage temperatures.
2. **Respect the Load Line**: Never stack merchandise above the inner wall Load Line mark. Overfilling blocks convective cold air blankets and risks product impact with the sliding lids.
3. **Strictly Prohibit Loading Warm or Ambient Items**: Only pre-frozen goods should be loaded into the cabinet. Introducing large batches of ambient or warm products will cause severe temperature spikes and excessive frost accumulation.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why are 4-in-1 combination island freezers preferred over traditional open air-curtain display cases in supermarkets?**
  * **A:** While open air-curtain cases allow easy reach-in access, their forced air currents rapidly dehydrate frozen pastries, raw meats, and seafood, resulting in surface cracking, discoloration, and significant weight shrinkage. They are also vulnerable to airborne dust and droplet contamination. The CECLE 4-In-1 Combine Island Freezer uses enclosed sliding glass doors with gentle natural convection cooling, preserving product moisture while cutting electrical consumption by over 50%.
* **Q: What is the primary difference between Section A and Section C styles?**
  * **A:** Both versions offer the exact same cooling output, modular splicing capabilities, and refrigeration hardware. The difference lies in exterior aesthetics: Section A features a sleek, rounded modern profile focused on fluid visual continuity, while Section C presents a structured, geometric industrial aesthetic. Retailers can select the profile that best complements their store interior and shelving lines.
* **Q: Does this combination island freezer require periodic manual ice scraping?**
  * **A:** No. The unit features integrated microcomputer cyclic defrosting that manages minor frost buildup automatically. Daily maintenance only requires wiping the interior walls and sliding tracks with a clean, soft cloth during store replenishment windows. Avoid using hard or sharp tools against the interior walls.
