## II. Operational Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To ensure long-term reliability and peak product preservation performance, store managers and retail staff must observe the following operational standards:

### 1. Site Preparation & Pre-Installation Requirements (Key Success Factors)

* **Adequate Ventilation & Clearance**:
  * Although the cabinet is suited for wall-aligned or back-to-back placement, the bottom and side compressor compartments must maintain unhindered ventilation—**never block ventilation louvers with promotional displays or cartons!** Maintain at least 4–6 inches (10–15 cm) of intake and exhaust clearance along louvered vents, and ensure sliding door tracks remain free of bone fragments or meat debris.
* **Pre-Startup Resting Protocol**:
  * After delivery via truck or ocean freight, **do not plug in or start the unit immediately!** Transit vibrations can displace compressor lubricating oil into the refrigerant lines. The cabinet must stand level and upright in its designated spot for **at least 24 hours** to allow oil to fully settle back into the compressor sump before power is connected.

### 2. Pre-Startup Inspection & Workstation Setup

1. Position the stainless steel showcase on a level, solid surface with appropriate aisle clearance, well away from heat sources or direct sunlight. Lock the front wheel caster brakes securely.
2. Verify that input power matches unit ratings (110V/220V lines) and plug into a dedicated, properly **grounded wall outlet**. Never daisy-chain with other high-power commercial appliances via extension cords.
3. Slide open the top tempered glass doors, insert stainless steel display trays or divider grids flat into the bottom liner, and wipe sliding tracks clean to guarantee smooth motion.

### 3. Standard Operating Guidelines

1. **Empty-Load Pre-Cooling**: Close the sliding glass doors, switch on power, and run the empty unit for **1 to 2 hours**. Only load goods once digital display readings stabilize at the target storage temperature.
2. **Organized Loading & Zone Management**: Arrange fresh items systematically inside stainless steel trays or standard food pans. **Core Rule: Never stack products above the maximum Load Line marked on the inner wall**, avoiding contact with sliding doors that could breach insulation seals or lift items out of the effective cooling zone.
3. **Strictly Prohibit Loading Hot Foods**: Cooked deli items or hot prepared foods must never be placed directly inside the display freezer. Cool items to ambient room temperature in prep areas before stocking to prevent sudden cabinet temperature spikes and heavy frost formation.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Is frost formation on the inner walls of direct-cooling supermarket showcases normal? How should defrosting be handled?**
  * **A:** Yes, this is completely normal. Direct-cooling freezers rely on direct thermal conduction across metal walls. During peak shopping hours with frequent door openings, ambient airborne moisture naturally condenses and sublimates into frost on cold surfaces. When the frost layer reaches approximately 5 mm thickness, schedule maintenance during store closing hours: disconnect power, allow frost to melt naturally, drain water via the sanitary drain valve, and wipe clean with a soft towel. Never chip or strike interior walls with sharp metal scrapers.
* **Q: Why is direct cooling preferred over air-cooled (frost-free) showcases for supermarket fresh butcher sections?**
  * **A:** Raw butcher cuts, sliced beef/lamb, and fresh seafood are exceptionally vulnerable to surface moisture evaporation. Air-cooled units employ high air velocities that can dry, darken, and harden exposed meat surfaces within 2 hours. Direct-cooling relies on static convective cooling, consistently preserving the natural bloom, tenderness, and moisture content of fresh cuts while dramatically upgrading visual sales appeal.
* **Q: Do top tempered sliding glass doors fog up and obscure items during peak humid hours?**
  * **A:** This unit utilizes commercial-grade double-layer hollow insulated tempered glass to minimize temperature transfer. During heavy rain or high-humidity days, the built-in thermal break channel around the door frame helps suppress condensation. Under extreme humidity, a simple wipe with a dry cloth immediately restores crystal-clear visibility.
