## II. Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To ensure this commercial direct-cooling buffet showcase operates reliably for years while maintaining peak food presentation quality, staff must strictly follow these operational standards:

### 1. Site Preparation & Pre-Installation Requirements (Critical Success Factors)

* **Adequate Ventilation and Airflow Clearance Are Mandatory**:
  * Although the cabinet can sit flush against customer walkways, the bottom and side compressor compartments require unrestricted air exchange—**NEVER block the ventilation louvers with boxes, carts, or trash bins!** Maintain at least 4–6 inches (10–15 cm) of breathing clearance around the louvers, and keep door tracks clear of food debris.
* **Pre-Startup Resting Requirement**:
  * After delivery, uncrating, and positioning, **DO NOT plug in or turn on the unit immediately!** Road or ocean transit can displace compressor lubricating oil into the refrigeration lines. The unit must rest upright on a level floor for **at least 24 hours** to allow oil to settle back into the compressor sump before power connection.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Setup

1. Roll the showcase into its designated customer buffet line away from direct charcoal grills, open fryers, or hot-air vents, and lock the front caster brakes securely.
2. Verify electrical specifications (110V/220V dedicated circuit) and connect the plug directly to an independent **grounded wall outlet** with adequate circuit breaker capacity.
3. Slide open the tempered glass doors, insert the stainless steel food divider trays or GN pan supports into the cabinet well, and verify that sliding tracks operate smoothly.

### 3. Standard Operating Guidelines

1. **No-Load Pre-Cooling**: Close the glass sliding doors, switch on the power, and let the empty unit run continuously for **1 to 2 hours**. Only load food items once the digital display confirms internal temperatures have dropped stably into the target preservation range.
2. **Scientific Loading & Partition Management**: Place meat cuts and skewers neatly within the stainless steel trays. **Core Principle: Never stack food items above the internal maximum "Load Line" indicator.** Overfilling prevents sliding doors from sealing tightly and places top layers outside the effective cold layer.
3. **Strictly Prohibit Hot Foods**: Freshly fried, boiled, or warm cooked foods must never be placed directly inside. Cool all items to room temperature in prep areas before stocking to prevent cabinet temperature spikes and excessive frost buildup.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Is it normal for frost to accumulate on the interior walls of a direct-cooling freezer? How should it be defrosted?**
  * **A:** Yes, this is completely normal physical behavior. Direct-cooling freezers absorb heat directly through perimeter metallic walls. When restaurant diners open the sliding doors, ambient humidity condenses and forms frost on the cold metal. When frost reaches approximately 5 mm (1/4 inch), simply disconnect power during restaurant closing hours, allow the frost to melt naturally, drain water via the sanitary drain port, and wipe dry with a soft cloth. Never scrape interior walls with sharp metal tools.
* **Q: Why is direct cooling preferred over fan-forced air cooling for buffet barbecue displays?**
  * **A:** Fresh-cut beef, marinated pork, seafood, and raw skewers are extremely vulnerable to surface drying. Air-cooled units generate strong internal airflow that quickly dries out exposed meat, turning it dark, leathery, and unappetizing within hours. Direct cooling uses static radiant cold to keep meats red, glossy, and moist, drastically preserving culinary appeal and texture.
* **Q: Will the top sliding glass doors fog up and obscure food during peak dining rush hours?**
  * **A:** The showcase is fitted with high-efficiency commercial double-pane tempered insulating glass that greatly restricts thermal conduction between inside and outside. During extreme humidity, air-break thermal channels around the frame minimize condensation. Any occasional surface mist can be effortlessly cleared with a dry towel for instant panoramic clarity.
