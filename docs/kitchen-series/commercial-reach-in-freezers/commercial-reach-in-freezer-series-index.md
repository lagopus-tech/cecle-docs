## 3. Buyer's Selection & Decision Matrix

Choose the model that best matches your kitchen layout, workflow, and storage volume:

### Choose the Commercial 27" Single-Door if:
1. **Floor space is tightly restricted:** You need a high-performance commercial freezer that fits seamlessly into a narrow 27-inch gap on your primary kitchen line.
2. **You need dedicated prep line storage:** You want an accessible freezer dedicated to a specific station (e.g., burger patties, pizza prep, or fryer appetizers).
3. **Daily inventory turnover is moderate:** An 18.68 cu. ft. volume satisfies your operational volume without occupying excess room.

### Choose the Commercial 30" Four Half-Door if:
1. **Frequent access is your daily reality:** Kitchen staff open the freezer dozens of times per hour; opening small half-doors keeps cold air in and dramatically lowers compressor wear and power bills.
2. **You prioritize organized categorization:** You need separate, isolated levels for different culinary categories to comply with strict kitchen food safety codes.
3. **You want maximum volume in a slim profile:** You want 28 cu. ft. of vertical storage capacity while keeping cabinet width restricted to just 30 inches.

### Choose the Commercial 54" Double-Door if:
1. **Bulk inventory storage is your priority:** You run a high-volume restaurant or central commissary that receives bulk deliveries and stockpiles large inventories.
2. **You store large sheet pans and containers:** You need wide, continuous shelf surfaces capable of holding full-size sheet pans, oversized bulk meat cuts, and commercial storage boxes.
3. **Kitchen layout permits a 54" width:** You have ample back-of-house space for a full-sized dual-door commercial unit.

## 4. Shared Engineering & Quality Standards

Every freezer in the CECLE Commercial Reach-In Series is built to rigorous commercial standards:

1. **Eco-Friendly R290 Refrigeration:** Utilizing green R290 hydrocarbon refrigerant for ultra-low global warming potential (GWP), rapid pull-down speeds, and outstanding energy efficiency.
2. **100% Frost-Free 3D Fan Cooling:** High-velocity evaporator fans circulate cold air evenly across every shelf tier, completely eliminating manual ice scraping.
3. **Food-Grade SUS Stainless Steel:** Built inside and out with heavy-gauge brushed stainless steel for maximum corrosion resistance, effortless cleaning, and long-term durability.
4. **Ergonomic Door Engineering:** Equipped with magnetic door gaskets, auto-closing mechanisms, and 90° stay-open features for seamless loading and unloading.
5. **Heavy-Duty Mobility:** Factory-equipped with heavy-duty swivel casters (front locking brakes) for easy maneuverability during kitchen deep-cleaning routines.
