## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

### 1. Site Preparation & Setup Requirements

* **Countertop Foundation**: Place the shaker on a flat, solid, and dry bar counter, ensuring all four rubber suction feet firmly adhere to the surface to prevent tipping or sliding.
* **Electrical Verification**: Check the voltage rating on the machine nameplate (110V or 220V) and connect to a dedicated, grounded single-phase electrical outlet.

### 2. Standard Operation & Shaking Workflow

1. **Cup Placement & Clamping**: Place the securely capped shaker cup (filled with tea, syrup, and ice) onto the bottom cradle; turn the top adjustment knob down firmly to lock the lid in place, ensuring a tight fit with zero play.
2. **Setting the Timer**: Rotate the front mechanical timer knob to set the desired shaking duration (standard milk tea/fruit tea is recommended at **10–20 seconds**).
3. **Starting the Cycle**: Press the power switch; the machine will shake at high speed and automatically brake to a halt once the time elapses.
4. **Unloading the Drink**: Loosen the top knob, lift the upper clamp, remove the shaker cup, uncap, seal, and serve.

### 3. Daily Maintenance & Cleaning

1. **Surface Cleaning**: At the end of each business day, wipe down the stainless steel exterior and cup cradles with a damp microfiber cloth to remove spilled liquid and syrup. Never wash or spray the body directly with running water.
2. **Hardware Inspection**: Check cup clamps and base screws weekly to ensure all mechanical fasteners remain securely tightened.
