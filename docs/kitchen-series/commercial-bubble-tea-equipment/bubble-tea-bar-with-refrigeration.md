## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To ensure this bubble tea refrigeration counter operates stably for years and maintains peak performance, store management and operating staff must strictly follow these operational protocols:

### 1. Site Preparation & Pre-Startup Treatment (Critical Success Factor)

* **Ensure Adequate Ventilation and Heat Dissipation Space**:
  * As the counter is often built-in or placed against a wall, pay special attention to keeping the compressor ventilation side free of debris or boxes! Maintain at least 4–6 inches (10–15 cm) of clearance around the vents, otherwise cooling efficiency will drop or the compressor may trigger thermal overload protection.
* **Pre-Startup Static Settlement Requirement**:
  * After the equipment is transported and positioned in the shop, **do NOT plug it in immediately!** It must remain in a horizontal position for **at least 24 hours** to ensure the lubricating oil inside the compressor thoroughly settles back to the bottom housing before connecting power.

### 2. Pre-Startup Inspection & Equipment Setup

1. Position the equipment on a flat, weight-bearing floor, adjusting the leveling feet to ensure it is steady and wobble-free, away from heat sources like ice makers or large commercial ovens.
2. Check the power voltage specification (compatible with 110V/220V lines); it must be plugged into an independent dedicated **grounded electrical outlet**. Sharing power strips with high-power heating appliances is strictly prohibited.
3. Clean the internal cold storage cabin and top topping pans to ensure no packaging residue remains, and check that drawers and door gaskets open and close smoothly.

### 3. Standard Operating Guidelines

1. **No-Load Test Cooling**: Close the cabinet doors and topping covers, plug in the power, turn on the temperature control switch, and run continuously with zero load for **1 to 2 hours**. Only place ingredients inside once the microcomputer digital panel readings stabilize at the preset cold storage temperature.
2. **Scientific Loading & Airflow Management**: Milk, tea bases, and toppings must be neatly organized. **Core Principle: Leave gaps between items—never overpack the cold storage cabin.** Ensure free circulation of cold air to prevent localized high temperatures.
3. **Strictly Prohibit Hot Foods & Unsealed Pungent Items**: Freshly cooked pearls or syrups retaining high residual heat must never be placed directly into the refrigerator or topping wells; they must cool down to room temperature first. All opened ingredients must be capped or sealed to prevent odor transfer and contamination.
