## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

### 1. Site Preparation & Pre-Startup Treatment

* **Placement**: Keep on a stable, dry countertop away from direct water splashes and open flames.
* **Initial Cleaning**: Flush internal tubes and the stainless steel barrel with warm clean water before first-time commercial use.

### 2. Pre-Startup Inspection & Equipment Setup

1. Confirm local voltage compatibility (220V/50Hz) and plug into a grounded outlet.
2. Fill the barrel with filtered syrup or fructose and secure the lid tightly.
3. Run a test cycle into a cup to verify accuracy and clear air bubbles from lines.

### 3. Standard Operating Guidelines

1. **Calibration**: Periodically check output volumes with a standard measuring cylinder.
2. **Liquid Selection**: Use approved liquid sweeteners only; avoid pulpy liquids to prevent clogs.
3. **Cleaning**: Rinse tubes with warm water at the end of each business day to prevent sugar crystallization.
