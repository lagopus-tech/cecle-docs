## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

### 1. Site Preparation & Pre-Startup Treatment

* **Countertop Setup**: Place the unit horizontally on a flat, dry, and vibration-free surface away from direct water sources.
* **Sanitization Flush**: Thoroughly flush the internal tank and fluid lines with warm drinking water prior to first commercial operation.

### 2. Pre-Startup Inspection & Equipment Setup

1. Verify that the power source complies with rated electrical parameters (220V) and connect to a properly grounded outlet.
2. Slowly pour filtered fructose or cane sugar syrup into the 8L tank to prevent air bubble entrapment, then secure the top lid.
3. Turn on the power switch, perform initial flow purging and calibration via the test dispense button.

### 3. Standard Operating Guidelines

1. **Periodic Calibration**: Regularly verify dispensed volumes using a standard measuring cylinder.
2. **Viscosity Suitability**: Formulated for liquid sugars, fructose, and uniform fluid syrups. Highly concentrated honey or seed-bearing syrups must be filtered/diluted before loading.
3. **Daily End-of-Day Cleaning**: Run warm clean water through the dispensing cycle at the end of each shift to prevent sugar crystallization.

  * **A:** Yes. The microcomputer control panel features programmable preset keys that allow you to set specific dispensing volumes for different cup sizes and sweetness requests.
