## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

### 1. Site Preparation & Plumbing Installation

1. **Drain Port Inspection**: Before connecting water, use an adjustable wrench to verify and tighten the drain plug/nut located at the bottom rear of the unit to prevent leaks.
2. **Water Supply Line Flushing**: Connect one end of the corrugated water inlet hose to the tap/purified water source, open the valve, and run water for several seconds to purge line debris and air.
3. **Machine Connection**: Securely fasten the other end of the hose to the water inlet port at the rear of the boiler, then turn on the water supply valve.
4. **Electrical Connection**: Ensure the electrical outlet is properly grounded and matches the rated voltage (110V or 220V).

### 2. Startup Commissioning & Indicator Status

1. **Power On**: Plug the power cord into the wall outlet. The digital display will begin flashing; press the **red power button** on the front control panel to turn on the unit.
2. **Water Inflow Indicators**:
   * **Green light on**: The inlet solenoid valve is open, and water has begun filling the internal reservoir.
   * **Left red light on**: Indicates that the water level is currently low/filling.
3. **Heating Status**: Once water reaches the designated level sensor, the **yellow heating indicator** next to the screen illuminates, the digital display shows real-time temperature, and layered heating starts automatically.
4. **Initial Line Purging**: Once water reaches 98°C–100°C boiling point, pull the red faucet lever to drain the first full tank of water, thoroughly flushing internal lines before regular commercial use.

### 3. Daily Operation & Preventative Maintenance

1. **End-of-Day Shutdown**: Turn off the power switch, shut off the water supply valve, and empty/clean the removable drip tray.
2. **Regular Descaling (Critical)**: Depending on local water hardness, descale the inner tank every 1–2 months using food-grade citric acid descaler to preserve heating element longevity.
3. **Faucet Sanitation**: Wipe the dispenser nozzle daily with a sanitized damp cloth to maintain hygienic beverage dispensing.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: What is a "step-by-step water boiler," and how is it different from traditional boilers?**
  * **A:** Traditional boilers heat an entire water tank all at once; when hot water is drawn, incoming cold water mixes with boiling water, creating half-raw, half-boiled "yin-yang water". The step-by-step YC-10 injects cold water at the bottom and heats it layer by layer. Cold water stays at the base, and only fully boiled water rises to the faucet, ensuring every drop dispensed is 100% freshly boiled.
* **Q: Does the YC-10 support 110V power for North American outlets (US/Canada)?**
  * **A:** Yes. The YC-10 is available in both 110V/60Hz and 220V/50Hz electrical configurations, fully compliant with commercial power standards across North America and worldwide. Please specify your voltage requirement upon ordering.
* **Q: How long does it take to produce boiling water?**
  * **A:** Thanks to the 2.5 kW (3.3 HP) high-power heating element and layered heating system, initial heating takes only a few minutes. During normal operations, heating replenishment is nearly instantaneous, with wait times of only tens of seconds.
