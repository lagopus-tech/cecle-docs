## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

To ensure this desktop square bakery display case operates stably for years and maintains optimal transparent visual effects, store management and operators must strictly adhere to the following operational guidelines:

### 1. Site Preparation & Pre-processing (Key Success Factors)

* **Sufficient Ventilation & Clearance Must Be Ensured**:
  * The display case is often placed on a bar counter. Pay special attention that the compressor cooling louvers on the sides and bottom of the machine must NOT be blocked by menu boards, coffee bean bags, or debris! A ventilation clearance of at least 4-6 inches (10-15 cm) must be maintained around the heat dissipation holes; otherwise, it will cause slow cooling or even compressor overheating and shutdown.
* **Resting Requirements Before Startup**:
  * After the equipment is transported to the store counter by land logistics, **DO NOT power it on immediately!** It must be left standing horizontally for **at least 24 hours** to ensure the lubricating oil inside the compressor thoroughly settles and flows back to the bottom of the unit before plugging into the power supply.

### 2. Pre-Start Checklist & Equipment Setup

1. Place the desktop display case on a flat, well-load-bearing bar counter, ensuring it is stable and does not wobble, away from heat sources like ovens, espresso machines, and direct sunlight from windows.
2. Check the power supply voltage specifications (110V/220V matching lines). It must be plugged into an independent dedicated **grounded wall outlet** that meets the rated power. It is strictly forbidden to share a power strip with high-power appliances like microwaves.
3. Adjust the height of the internal storage shelves and fix them steadily. Wipe clean the dust and debris in the sliding door tracks to ensure smooth sliding without abnormal noise.

### 3. Standard Operating Guidelines

1. **Empty Load Test Cooling**: Close the glass sliding doors, plug in the power, turn on the fridge and LED light switches, and run continuously empty for **1 to 2 hours**. Only after the microcomputer digital panel reading stably drops and reaches the preset target refrigeration temperature can pastries be placed inside.
2. **Scientific Loading & Airflow Management**: Desserts should be neatly arranged on the shelves. **Core Principle: Gaps must be left between products and between products and the glass. Do NOT completely block the air outlets and return air inlets inside the cabinet.** Cold air must be allowed to circulate freely; otherwise, it will lead to high local temperatures and affect overall preservation.
3. **Strictly No Hot Food & Unsealed Strong-Smelling Food**: Freshly baked semi-finished products with residual heat must never be placed directly into the refrigerated case; they must be cooled to room temperature before putting them in. Foods with strong odors should be sealed with plastic wrap to prevent cross-flavoring inside the cabinet and increasing the defrosting load.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why does this dessert display case use air-cooling frost-free technology? Compared to direct cooling, what are the advantages?**
  * **A:** For exquisite French pastries, cream mousses, and breads with fresh fruits, display visual effects and temperature uniformity are crucial. Direct cooling equipment easily frosts on the inner walls and has obvious upper and lower temperature differences; while air cooling technology uses internal micro-hole fans to force cold air circulation, making the temperature in every corner of the cabinet uniform and never frosting. Double-layered hollow anti-fog flat glass combined with air cooling can maintain extremely transparent visibility at all times, making it the ideal choice for high-end bakery displays.
* **Q: Will the outer glass fog up and obscure the cakes during peak business hours or in rainy and high-humidity weather?**
  * **A:** Generally no. This unit uses commercial-grade double-layered hollow insulated tempered glass, which can effectively block the indoor and outdoor temperature difference. If encountering southern winds or extreme high-humidity environments, slight condensation on the glass surface is a natural physical phenomenon. At this time, just ensure the machine's anti-fog function is running (if equipped with heating wires), or wipe gently with a clean dry towel, and the transparent view will be restored instantly.
* **Q: Can the inside of the cabinet be washed directly with water for daily cleaning?**
  * **A:** **Absolutely forbidden!** The interior of the display case is equipped with precision electronic components such as circulation fans, LED lighting tubes, and temperature control probes. For daily cleaning, please unplug the power after closing, use a soft damp cloth with a mild, food-safe detergent to gently wipe down the interior glass and shelves, and then dry completely with a clean towel. Never hose down the interior or use sharp objects, as this can severely damage electrical components like LED lights and circulating fans.
