## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

To ensure this desktop double-tier curved bread display case operates stably for years and maintains optimal clear visibility, store management and operating personnel must strictly adhere to the following operating guidelines:

### 1. Site Preparation & Pre-processing (Key Success Factors)

* **Sufficient Ventilation & Clearance Must Be Ensured**:
  * Display cases are frequently placed on bar counters; pay special attention to ensure the compressor cooling louvers on the sides and bottom are **never blocked** by promotional signs, coffee bean bags, or debris! Maintain at least 4-6 inches (10-15 cm) of clearance around air intake/exhaust ventilation paths to prevent slow cooling or compressor overheating/shutdown.
* **Resting Requirements Before Startup**:
  * After the equipment is transported to the store counter by ground shipping, **do not power it on immediately!** It must remain upright and level for **at least 24 hours** to allow internal compressor lubricating oil to thoroughly settle and flow back to the base before connecting to electricity.

### 2. Pre-Start Checklist & Equipment Setup

1. Position the desktop curved display case on a flat, sturdy, load-bearing counter, ensuring it is secure and wobble-free, away from heat sources like ovens or espresso machines, and out of direct sunlight from windows.
2. Verify the power supply voltage specification (matching 110V/220V lines) and plug it into an independent, dedicated **grounded wall outlet** matching rated power requirements. Sharing power strips with high-wattage appliances like microwaves is strictly prohibited.
3. Adjust the height of the internal double-tier shelves, secure them stably, and wipe clean any dust or debris inside the curved sliding door tracks to guarantee smooth movement without strange noises.

### 3. Standard Operating Guidelines

1. **Empty Load Test Cooling**: Close the double curved glass sliding doors, connect power, turn on the cabinet and LED light switches, and run empty continuously for **1 to 2 hours**. Only after the microcomputer digital panel readout drops steadily and hits the target preset refrigeration temperature should pastries be loaded.
2. **Scientific Loading & Airflow Management**: Desserts should be neatly arranged across both display tiers. **Core Principle: Leave gaps between items and between items and the glass. Do NOT completely block internal air supply and return vents.** Cold air must circulate freely; otherwise, localized warm spots will form and compromise preservation quality.
3. **Strictly No Hot Foods or Unsealed Pungent Items**: Freshly baked goods holding residual warmth must never be placed straight into the refrigerated cabinet—they must cool completely to room temperature beforehand. Foods with strong odors must be sealed with plastic wrap to prevent cross-contamination and minimize frosting loads.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why does this double-tier curved dessert case use air-cooling frost-free technology? What are the advantages over direct cooling?**
  * **A:** For exquisite French pastries, cream mousses, and double-tier cakes, presentation clarity and temperature uniformity are critical. Direct cooling units easily frost on internal walls and produce significant temperature variances between upper and lower tiers; whereas air-cooling technology uses internal micro-hole fans to enforce forced-air circulation, keeping every corner of both tiers uniform and permanently frost-free. Double-layer hollow anti-fog curved glass paired with air cooling maintains wide-angle optical clarity at all times, making it the supreme choice for high-end bakery displays.
* **Q: During peak business hours or in rainy, high-humidity weather, will the outer curved glass fog up and obscure the cakes?**
  * **A:** Generally no. This unit adopts commercial-grade double-layer hollow insulated curved tempered glass designed to effectively isolate indoor/outdoor temperature differentials. In the event of extreme high-humidity conditions (like southern humid seasons), minor surface condensation is a natural physical phenomenon; simply ensure the built-in anti-fog function is enabled (if equipped with heating wires) or gently wipe with a clean dry microfiber cloth to restore crystal clarity instantly.
* **Q: Can the interior of the cabinet be washed directly with water during daily cleaning?**
  * **A:** **Strictly forbidden!** The interior of the display case houses precision electronic components such as circulation fans, LED light bars, and temperature sensors. For routine maintenance after closing business, always disconnect power first, then use a soft damp cloth soaked in mild food-safe neutral detergent to wipe down inner walls and double shelves, drying thoroughly with a dry cloth afterward. Never use sharp tools to scrape the curved glass or spray water hoses directly onto the cabinet body.
