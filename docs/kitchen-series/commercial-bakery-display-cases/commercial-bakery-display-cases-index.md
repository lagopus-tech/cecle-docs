## 3. Buyer's Selection & Decision Matrix

Choose the model that best matches your venue type, merchandising goals, and operational workflow:

### Choose the Curved Bakery Display Case if:
1. **You want flowing, organic visual appeal:** Your cafe style emphasizes soft lines, and you want a panoramic curved glass facade that draws customer eyes from across the room.
2. **Single-tier pastry presentation is sufficient:** You merchandise standard quantities of fresh tarts, muffins, and croissants on a compact desktop footprint.
3. **Aesthetic harmony is a priority:** You need an elegant showcase that blends seamlessly into artisanal coffee shop environments.

### Choose the Square Glass Display Case if:
1. **Clean, professional geometry fits your brand:** Your store design relies on sharp lines, straight angles, and a modern, clinical, or industrial aesthetic.
2. **You need counter utility above the unit:** You want a flat top surface that can accommodate promotional table tents, menus, or light items.
3. **Unobstructed direct views are essential:** You prefer absolute flat glass avoiding any optical curvature effects.

### Choose the Double-Tier Curved Showcase if:
1. **Vertical space utilization is mandatory:** You need to display twice as many products (cakes on the bottom, smaller pastries on top) within the same counter length.
2. **Multi-size scalability is required:** You want to choose from precise sizing tiers (0.9M up to 1.8M) with custom black, white, or stainless steel finishes.
3. **High-volume turnover demands structured sorting:** You operate a busy bakery where clear tiered separation speeds up customer selection.

### Choose the Double-Tier Marble Showcase if:
1. **Luxury presentation is non-negotiable:** You operate an upscale hotel lounge or premium patisserie where material quality (genuine marble bases) matters as much as the food.
2. **Black or white marble styling integration is needed:** You want to match your existing high-end interior stone finishes precisely.
3. **Dual-tier capacity must meet elite standards:** You require both maximum visual prestige and robust 2°C~8°C frost-free preservation.

## 4. Shared Engineering & Quality Standards

Every model across the CECLE Commercial Bakery Display Case Series is built to rigorous commercial-grade manufacturing standards:

1. **Frost-Free Dynamic Air-Cooling:** Powered by advanced micro-hole forced-air circulation systems, maintaining precise 2°C to 8°C holding temperatures while keeping glass and food surfaces entirely free of frost and condensation.
2. **Food-Grade Construction & Luxury Materials:** Built with heavy-gauge stainless steel frames, high-grade interior components, and optional genuine marble countertops for ultimate corrosion resistance and effortless sanitation.
3. **High-Clarity Double-Layer Hollow Tempered Glass:** Outfitted with low-E, double-pane hollow tempered glass panes featuring anti-fog performance to guarantee absolute optical transparency under varying ambient humidity.
4. **Eco-Friendly High-Efficiency Compressors:** Driven by commercial hermetic compressors and green refrigerants (R290 / R134a), ensuring fast pull-down times, energy efficiency, and low-decibel quiet operation.
5. **High-Density Polyurethane Insulation:** Injected with high-pressure cyclopentane foam insulation to eliminate thermal leakage and stabilize interior cooling efficiency.
6. **Turnkey Logistics & Global Warranty:** Protected by heavy-duty export wood-crate packaging, international DDP ocean freight delivery (customs and duties cleared), manufacturer warranties, and dedicated technical video support.
