## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To ensure this small refrigeration unit operates stably for years and maintains optimal freshness, users and management personnel must strictly adhere to the following operational guidelines:

### 1. Site Preparation & Pre-Startup Treatment (Critical Success Factor)

* **Ensure Adequate Ventilation and Heat Dissipation Space**:
  * When placing the refrigerator on the floor or under a desk, pay special attention to keeping the back and sides clear—never block them completely against a wall! Maintain at least 4–6 inches (10–15 cm) of clearance around the ventilation vents for airflow, otherwise it may cause slow cooling or even compressor overheating shutdown.
* **Pre-Startup Static Settlement Requirement**:
  * After the equipment is delivered to your home or office via ground freight, **do NOT plug it in immediately!** It must remain upright and stationary for **at least 24 hours** to allow the lubricating oil inside the compressor to fully settle back to the bottom housing before connecting power.

### 2. Pre-Startup Inspection & Equipment Setup

1. Place the refrigerator on a flat, weight-bearing floor or stable countertop, ensuring it is leveled and steady without wobbling, away from heat sources like radiators or microwaves and direct sunlight.
2. Check the power voltage specification (compatible with 110V/220V lines); it must be plugged into an independent dedicated **grounded electrical outlet**. Avoid sharing power strips with high-power appliances like microwaves.
3. Adjust the height of the internal shelves to accommodate your commonly used beverage bottles or storage boxes, wipe clean the door seal and interior liner, and ensure the door opens and closes smoothly.

### 3. Standard Operating Guidelines

1. **No-Load Test Cooling**: Close the door tightly, plug in the power, and turn the temperature control knob to a medium setting. Run it continuously with zero load for **1 to 2 hours**. Only place items inside once the internal digital/temperature readout drops and stabilizes at the target refrigeration range.
2. **Scientific Loading & Airflow Management**: Arrange beverages and foods neatly on the shelves. **Core Principle: Leave gaps between items and between items and the interior walls—never overpack the cabinet.** Cold air must be allowed to circulate freely, otherwise localized warm spots will form and impact overall preservation.
3. **Strictly Prohibit Hot Foods & Unsealed Pungent Items**: Semi-finished baked or cooked goods retaining residual heat must never be placed directly into the refrigerator; they must be cooled to room temperature first. Foods with strong odors (or high-hygiene requirements like breast milk) must be tightly sealed with plastic wrap or airtight bags/bottles to prevent interior odor transfer and bacterial growth.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why does this small refrigerator use a compressor instead of cheaper thermoelectric cooling commonly found on the market?**
  * **A:** While thermoelectric cooling offers a compact size, its cooling speed is slow and heavily affected by ambient room temperatures (often failing to reach safe cold storage temperatures in summer). For users needing safe breast milk storage, optimal fresh fruit texture, and properly chilled drinks, only compressor cooling delivers genuinely strong, stable, and deep refrigeration unaffected by hot outdoor weather.
* **Q: Will this refrigerator be loud if placed in a bedroom or next to a quiet office desk?**
  * **A:** No, it will not. This unit is optimized specifically for home and office environments, equipped with a high-quality dampening silent compressor tier-derived from commercial standards. Operating noise is extremely low, comparable to a whisper or white noise level, and will never disturb your work focus or deep nighttime sleep.
* **Q: How should I clean and defrost it during routine use?**
  * **A:** Always unplug the power cord before cleaning. Use a soft, damp cloth with a mild, food-safe detergent to wipe down the interior walls and shelves, then dry water stains with a dry towel. As this is a direct-cooling refrigerator, if a thin layer of frost accumulates inside after prolonged use, unplug the unit and leave the door open for natural defrosting. Never use sharp objects to scrape the interior walls, as this can damage precision cooling lines.
