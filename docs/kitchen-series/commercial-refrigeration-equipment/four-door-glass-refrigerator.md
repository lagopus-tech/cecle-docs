## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To ensure this 4-door glass door display refrigerator runs stably for years and maintains optimal cooling and anti-fog performance, operations staff must strictly follow these operational rules:

### 1. Site Preparation & Pre-Treatment (Critical Success Factor)

* **Adequate Ventilation and Opening Clearance Must Be Ensured**:
  * Despite its compact footprint, its heavy-duty compressor generates significant heat—**never place the back of the unit tight against a wall!** Leave at least 4-6 inches (10-15 cm) of convection clearance at the back and top. Also ensure all four glass doors can open fully past 90 degrees.
* **Static Placement Requirement Before Startup**:
  * After delivery, unboxing, and positioning, **DO NOT plug it in immediately!** Due to transit turbulence, lubricating oil inside the compressor may shift. The unit must remain upright and static for **at least 24 hours** to allow oil to fully return to the compressor base before powering on.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Workstation Setup

1. Push the 4-door glass display refrigerator to a flat, dry floor away from open flames/heat sources (such as stoves or ovens), and lock the front wheel brakes.
2. Check electrical specs to ensure connection to an independent, dedicated **grounded wall outlet** meeting rated standards.
3. Open the glass doors and adjust the heavy-duty epoxy-coated wire shelves into corresponding side-wall slots based on the height of displayed items (e.g., wine bottles, premium ingredient storage containers).

### 3. Standard Operating Guide

1. **No-Load Test Cooldown**: Plug in and turn on the power switch, letting the empty unit run continuously for **1 to 2 hours**. Only load items after observing the panel temperature drop stably into the target set range.
2. **Scientific Placement & Visual Merchandising**: Categorize and neatly arrange drinks, beverages, or premium fresh items on the shelves. **Core Principle: Always leave gaps between items, and between items, the glass doors, and rear air vents.** Never place items tight against the glass or block internal air ducts, as this impedes cold convection and causes localized temperature anomalies or glass condensation.
3. **Hot Food Prohibition**: Boiling soups or freshly cooked hot dishes must never go straight into the display refrigerator. They must cool naturally to room temperature before sealing and entering the cabinet, preventing sudden internal temperature spikes and excessive condensation from clinging to the glass doors.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why do thin layers of water mist occasionally appear on the outer surface of the glass doors during very humid rainy or "Hui Nan" seasons?**
  * **A:** This is a natural physical condensation phenomenon occurring when hot, humid external air encounters ice-cold glass surfaces under extremely high humidity conditions. Although the equipment utilizes hollow anti-fog glass with built-in anti-dew heating designs, minor water droplets may still form during extreme humidity; simply wipe them clean with a clean soft cloth without affecting regular use or food safety.
* **Q: Does a 35 cu. ft. (1000L) large-capacity glass door display refrigerator require periodic manual defrosting?**
  * **A:** Not at all. This model features an advanced **dynamic fan-cooling frost-free system** with a built-in automated cyclic defrost program. It automatically evacuates and evaporates trace moisture generated by the evaporator, eliminating traditional refrigerator heavy manual ice scraping and ensuring long-term clean, efficient front-of-house merchandising.
* **Q: Why does opening one glass door sometimes make the adjacent door feel tightly suctioned shut and hard to pull open?**
  * **A:** This is due to the large internal volume and exceptional sealing of the 4-glass-door display refrigerator. When you swiftly close one door, internal air compression or fan-driven action momentarily creates a slight negative pressure differential, causing the adjacent door to briefly "seal tight" for a few seconds. This is a sign of superior sealing performance; simply wait 2-3 seconds to easily open it again.
