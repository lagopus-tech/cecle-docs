## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To ensure this 3-zone la carte display freezer operates stably for years and maintains optimal cooling performance, operators must strictly adhere to the following operating guidelines:

### 1. Site Preparation & Pre-treatment (Critical Success Factors)

* **Sufficient Heat Dissipation Space is Mandatory**:
  * Although the body design is compact, due to the high heat generation of its multi-system compressor, **it is strictly forbidden to place the back of the machine or the heat vents flush against the wall!** Please leave sufficient convection heat dissipation space around the equipment.
* **Static Placement Requirement Before Powering On**:
  * After the goods are delivered, unpacked, and positioned, **DO NOT power it on immediately!** Due to bumps during long-distance transportation, the lubricating oil in the compressor may have shifted. The equipment must be left to stand still for **at least 24 hours** to allow the oil to completely flow back to the bottom of the compressor before connecting to the power supply.

### 2. Pre-Startup Inspection & Workstation Deployment

1. Push the 3-zone la carte display freezer to a flat, dry floor away from open flames/heat sources, and adjust the leveling feet to secure it.
2. Check the power specifications to ensure it is plugged into an independent, commercial-rated **dedicated wall outlet with a grounding wire**.
3. Clean the internal GN pans or display surfaces in preparation for official use.

### 3. Standard Operating Guidelines

1. **Empty Load Trial Run**: Plug in the power and turn on the switch. Let the empty cabinet run continuously for **1 to 2 hours**. Observe the panel to ensure the temperature of each zone stably drops to the set range before loading ingredients.
2. **Scientific Placement & Categorized Management**: Place ingredients with different requirements (e.g., vegetables, seafood, meats) into their corresponding temperature zones or GN pans. **Core Principle: Gaps must be maintained between food items.** Do not block internal air ducts or over-stack, as this will hinder cold air convection.
3. **Closing Cleaning & Hygiene Maintenance**: After daily operations conclude, properly transfer unused ingredients, and wipe down the display pans and the inner walls of the cabinet to maintain food hygiene.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why can different temperature zones in the 3-zone display cabinet maintain different temperatures simultaneously?**
  * **A:** This equipment utilizes an advanced multi-system independent control or precise air duct throttling technology. Through physical partitions and independent temperature control probes, it achieves three different temperature environments within a single body simultaneously, meeting the optimal preservation critical points for various types of ingredients.
* **Q: Does the display cabinet require regular manual defrosting?**
  * **A:** It depends on the specific model. Some air-cooled models support a fully automatic frost-free system; for direct-cooling models, when the frost thickness on the inner wall exceeds a certain standard, it is recommended to regularly disconnect the power for manual cleaning and defrosting to ensure optimal cooling efficiency.
* **Q: Why is there sometimes slight condensation (water vapor) on the surface of the glass window?**
  * **A:** When the external ambient temperature is high and humidity is high, while the temperature inside the cabinet is low, moisture in the air will naturally condense when it hits the colder glass surface. This is a normal physical phenomenon. Keeping the store environment well-ventilated or wiping it with a dry cloth will suffice.
