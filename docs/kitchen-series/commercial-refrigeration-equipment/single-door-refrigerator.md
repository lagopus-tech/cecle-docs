### 2. Core Functions & Working Principle

The machine operates through a combination of compressor refrigeration and dynamic airflow circulation:

* **Eco-Friendly Thermodynamic Cooling**: The compressor compresses the R290 refrigerant and sends it to the condenser for heat dissipation, after which it expands and absorbs heat in the evaporator, producing a massive amount of cold air.
* **3D Fan Cooling Circulation**: The top high-efficiency fan forces the cold air generated by the evaporator downward. The cold air passes through the mesh shelves, forming a waterfall-like cold airflow, and is then sucked in by the return air vent to form a closed loop.
* **Automatic Temperature Control & Defrosting**: Built-in smart microcomputer temperature control panel monitors the interior temperature (33°F - 39°F) in real-time. When the set temperature is reached, the compressor automatically sleeps, and the built-in heating wire or fan defrosting program periodically intervenes to ensure the evaporator never freezes.

## II. Production Workflow & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To ensure the equipment's long-term stable operation and maintain optimal cooling performance, please strictly follow these operating procedures:

### 1. Site Preparation & Pre-Treatment (Critical Success Factor)

* **Adequate Ventilation & Heat Dissipation Space is Required (Crucial)**:
  * **Never place the machine tightly against a wall or embed it in a fully enclosed cabinet!** The compressor requires sufficient air convection for heat dissipation. Please leave at least 4-6 inches (10-15 cm) of clearance at the back and top of the unit.
* **Resting Requirement Before Powering On**:
  * After receiving, unboxing, and positioning the equipment, **DO NOT plug it in and turn it on immediately!** Bumps during transit may cause the lubricating oil inside the compressor to flow backward. The unit must stand upright and rest for **at least 24 hours**. You may only connect the power after the oil in the refrigeration system has fully returned to the bottom; otherwise, it will easily damage the compressor.

### 2. Pre-Start Inspection & Workstation Setup

1. Push the equipment to a flat, dry, and well-ventilated floor area, and press the front wheel brakes to lock it in place.
2. Check the power cord and ensure it is connected to a dedicated **standard grounded outlet** (avoid sharing power strips with other high-power appliances).
3. Open the cabinet door and, based on daily storage needs (such as the height of GN pans, large soup buckets, or cartons), snap the stainless steel epoxy-coated wire shelves into the slots at the appropriate heights.

### 3. Standard Operating Guide

1. **No-Load Cooldown**: Plug in the power and turn on the machine switch. The microcomputer thermostat will light up. At this time, please **keep the empty cabinet running for 1 to 2 hours** and observe that the temperature display drops to the set range (33°F - 39°F) before loading ingredients.
2. **Standardized Storage**: Organize and place ingredients on the wire shelves. **Note: Leave gaps between food items, and between the food and the inner walls of the cabinet.** Never fully pack the shelves or block the rear air vents, as blocking the cold air circulation will result in localized overheating.
3. **Hot Food Prohibition**: Hot soups or freshly cooked hot food must be cooled to room temperature before being placed in the refrigerator. Otherwise, it will cause a massive amount of condensation instantly, increasing the burden on the compressor.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Q: Why do the side panels and door frame feel warm when the refrigerator is first powered on?**
  * **A:** This is a normal phenomenon. To prevent condensation or water droplets from forming on the edges of the door frame in highly humid environments, the refrigerator's condenser tubing usually runs around the door frame to provide anti-condensation heating. Once the machine enters a stable cooling state, the heat will decrease.
* **Q: Do I need to periodically unplug this refrigerator to defrost it?**
  * **A:** No. This model uses an advanced **dynamic fan cooling system** with a built-in automatic defrost cycle. The refrigerator interior will not build up thick frost like traditional direct-cooling refrigerators, saving you the tedious work of manual ice chipping and cleaning while keeping ingredients dry.
* **Q: Why is the set temperature 33°F, but the temperature displayed on the screen sometimes jumps above 40°F?**
  * **A:** Frequent door openings will cause hot outside air to rush in. The thermostat probe detects the temperature rise, and the screen value will change. As long as you close the door properly, the powerful fan-cooling compressor will quickly pull the temperature back down within a few minutes. Additionally, when the machine enters its automatic defrost cycle, the temperature will also briefly rise slightly, which is a normal operational logic.
