> **Core Abstract**: 
> The Stand-on Electric Pallet Truck is an efficient warehousing solution designed specifically for modern large-scale facilities. Combining electric drive with a stand-on operating design, this model significantly increases travel speed and operator comfort for long-distance material handling. With a sturdy chassis, high load capacity, and flexible foldable protective guards, it transitions seamlessly between wide warehouse aisles and narrow work areas, making it the optimal choice for boosting operational efficiency.

## II. Core Advantages
1. **High-Efficiency Stand-on Design**: Equipped with a foldable platform and protective guardrails so operators can ride while driving. This is much faster than walking alongside the truck, significantly boosting productivity.
2. **Reduced Labor Intensity**: Both driving and lifting are fully motorized. Operators do not need to push or pull heavy loads, greatly reducing physical fatigue.
3. **Safe & Reliable**: Sturdy protective guardrails ensure operator stability and safety, providing all-around protection especially during sharp turns or high-speed travel.
4. **Flexible Operation**: The platform and guardrails can be folded away, allowing the machine to operate flexibly like a standard walkie pallet truck in tight spaces or narrow aisles.
5. **Ideal for Long-Distance Transport**: Specially designed for long-haul transport in large warehouses, ensuring operators stay energetic and efficient throughout their shifts.
6. **High Load Capacity**: This model typically handles heavy loads ranging from 2.0 to 3.0 tons with an exceptionally stable and robust chassis.

---

## III. Operation Workflow
1.  **Pre-Operation Inspection**: Check battery levels, guardrail status, and the emergency stop button.
2.  **Deploy Stand-on Mode**: Unfold the foot platform and guardrails, and have the operator step onto the platform.
3.  **Position & Insert**: Drive the truck smoothly using the control handle to fully insert the forks into the bottom of the pallet.
4.  **Electric Lifting**: Press the lifting button to let the hydraulic system raise the forks smoothly.
5.  **Efficient Transport**: Drive the truck for rapid transport, relying on the safety guardrails for stable travel.
6.  **Lowering & Unloading**: Upon reaching the destination, smoothly lower the forks and back out the truck.
