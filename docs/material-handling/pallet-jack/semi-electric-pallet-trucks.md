> **Core Abstract**:
> Semi-Electric Pallet Trucks are the backbone of modern logistics and warehouse material handling, offering a perfect balance between the economy of manual pallet trucks and the high efficiency of fully electric models. By utilizing an electric drive for movement, these trucks eliminate the need for operators to manually push or pull heavy loads, significantly enhancing ease of transport. With a compact structure, agile maneuverability, and low maintenance requirements, they are the high-cost-performance choice for small-to-medium warehouses, supermarkets, production workshops, and narrow-aisle loading areas seeking to automate their material handling.

## I. Product Overview
Semi-Electric Pallet Trucks are designed for businesses seeking efficient and cost-effective handling operations.
* **Category**: Electric-Assisted / Semi-Electric Pallet Truck.
* **Usage Scenarios**: Widely used in high-turnover warehousing centers, narrow logistics aisles, container loading/unloading, supermarket restocking, and industrial production line logistics.
* **Core Functions**: Electric walking drive (effortless transport), manual hydraulic lifting (precise control), and emergency power-off protection (safety assurance).

## II. Core Advantages
* **High Cost-Performance**: Much more affordable than fully electric forklifts, yet significantly more efficient than manual pallet trucks.
* **Effortless & Efficient**: Electric drive for movement eliminates the need for manual pushing or pulling, drastically reducing labor intensity.
* **Flexible Operation**: Compact body design with a small turning radius, perfect for use in warehouses, containers, or narrow aisles.
* **Simple Maintenance**: Features a simpler structure compared to fully electric models, resulting in lower failure rates and reduced maintenance costs.
* **Long Endurance**: Electric power is used for driving only, leading to slower battery consumption and longer operation times.
* **Safe & Reliable**: Equipped with an emergency power-off switch and a sturdy protective cover to safeguard the operator.

---

## III. Operation Workflow
1.  **Preparation**: Check the battery level, the position of the emergency power-off switch, and the condition of the hydraulic system before operation.
2.  **Lifting**: Insert the forks smoothly under the pallet. Operate the manual handle to lift the forks until the load is securely off the ground.
3.  **Electric Drive**: Use the forward/backward switches on the handle to control the equipment for smooth, electric-powered movement.
4.  **Precise Positioning**: Leverage the small turning radius to navigate the load to the designated location.
5.  **Safe Lowering**: After confirming the position, slowly release the hydraulic valve to lower the forks gently and withdraw them from the pallet.

## V. Sales Suggestions & FAQ

**FAQ**
* **Q: What is the difference between a semi-electric and a fully electric pallet truck?**
    * A: Semi-electric trucks (this model) feature electric drive + manual lift, offering a simpler structure and better price. Fully electric trucks feature electric power for both drive and lift.
* **Q: How is the battery endurance?**
    * A: Because electric power is only used for driving, energy consumption is well-controlled, allowing for longer usage times on a single charge.
* **Q: Are there requirements for the floor surface?**
    * A: It is recommended to use the truck on flat, hard surfaces to achieve optimal walking efficiency.
