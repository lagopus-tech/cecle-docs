# Electric Pallet Jack

![Electric Pallet Jack](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/121.png?v=1787203718)

> **Core Abstract**
> The Electric Pallet Jack (Models: EPT15TQ / EPT15TLI) is a highly efficient solution for modern logistics and warehousing. Featuring a fully electric drive system, it automates both travel and lifting, eliminating the physical strain associated with traditional manual handling. With an ergonomic design and powerful motor output, it smoothly and quickly manages loads up to 1500kg. It is specifically designed for frequent operations in narrow aisles, between warehouse shelves, and inside truck trailers, making it an ideal choice for businesses looking to boost logistics efficiency and improve workplace ergonomics.

---

## I. Product Overview
*   **Category**: Warehouse Material Handling Equipment / Electric Industrial Forklift
*   **Application Scenarios**: Widely used in logistics centers, automated warehouses, supermarket distribution, food processing plants, manufacturing workshops, and loading/unloading operations for transport vehicles.
*   **Core Functions**: Electric-assisted travel drive, electric-hydraulic lifting and lowering, electric braking control, and emergency collision protection.

---

## II. Key Advantages
*   **Increased Efficiency**: With motorized drive for both traveling and lifting, operators can move heavy loads faster, significantly increasing throughput per unit of time.
*   **Reduced Physical Strain & Better Ergonomics**: Designed to eliminate the physical effort required for manual pulling and hydraulic lifting, greatly reducing the risk of lower back and muscle injuries.
*   **Ease of Use**: Integrated control buttons on the handle (forward, reverse, lift, lower, horn) allow even novice operators to master the equipment quickly.
*   **Maneuverability**: Compact structure and small turning radius make it perfectly suited for operation in narrow aisles, between warehouse racks, or inside tight truck containers.
*   **Higher Load Capacity**: The electric drive system ensures smooth starting and steady operation even when handling heavy loads (e.g., 1.5 tons), where manual pallet jacks would struggle.
*   **Enhanced Safety**: Typically equipped with an emergency brake button, anti-collision protection (belly switch), and an electromagnetic braking system, offering superior safety compared to manual models, especially on ramps.
*   **Better Gradeability**: Powerful drive motors enable the pallet jack to climb inclines that are difficult for manual models to handle.

---

## III. Operating Procedure
1.  **Preparation**: Check battery levels, insert the key, press the start button on the handle, and ensure the brake is released.
2.  **Positioning**: Control the handle to move forward and smoothly slide the forks into the pallet base, ensuring center alignment.
3.  **Electric Lifting**: Press the lift button on the handle until the load is cleared from the ground and maintained at a safe height.
4.  **Transporting**: Operate the drive handle or throttle to control the pallet jack and move at a safe speed to the target area.
5.  **Unloading**: Upon reaching the destination, lightly press the lower button to ensure the pallet touches the ground steadily, then reverse to exit the forks.

### FAQ
**Q: What makes electric pallet jacks better than manual ones?**
A: The most significant difference is the reduction in physical effort. Electric pallet jacks are fully motorized for both travel and lifting, drastically reducing operator fatigue and increasing efficiency several times over compared to manual models.

**Q: How is this product maintained?**
A: Maintenance focuses on following proper battery charging/discharging protocols, periodically checking hydraulic oil levels, and inspecting drive wheel wear. The modular design makes servicing very convenient.

**Q: Can it be used on ramps or inclines?**
A: Yes, our models have good gradeability, though we recommend operation within designed slope limits to ensure safety.
