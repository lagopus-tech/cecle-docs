## III. Operation Workflow
1. **Inspection**: Check the exterior of the forks and hydraulic system for any abnormalities and confirm the braking status.
2. **Insertion & Alignment**: Smoothly push the forks into the pallet openings, ensuring the load is centered.
3. **Manual Lifting**: Set the handle lever to the lift position and pump the handle repeatedly until the load is cleared from the ground.
4. **Transport**: Firmly grip the handle and pull or push the jack steadily at a constant speed on flat surfaces.
5. **Lowering & Unloading**: Upon reaching the destination, gently lift the release lever on the handle to slowly lower the forks until the pallet sits securely on the ground.
