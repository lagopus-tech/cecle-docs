## III. Operation Flow
1. **Inspection & Preparation**: Check all components (e.g., hydraulic pump, parrot-beak clamp, wheel assembly) for proper function and ensure the operating area is flat and free of obstacles.
2. **Movement & Positioning**: Push the lifter to position the curved frame around the target drum and align the parrot-beak clamp with the drum's upper rim.
3. **Clamping & Locking**: Operate the foot pedal to engage the hydraulic pump; the parrot-beak clamp will automatically hook and lock onto the top rim of the drum.
4. **Lifting & Transport**: Continue to operate the foot pedal to lift the drum to the desired height before proceeding with transport.
5. **Lowering & Release**: Move the drum to the designated location (e.g., a pallet or scale), slowly open the hydraulic release valve to lower the drum; the clamp will automatically release, completing the task.
6. **Maintenance & Storage**: Store the equipment in a safe area, keep it clean, and perform routine hydraulic fluid top-ups and bolt tightening as needed.
