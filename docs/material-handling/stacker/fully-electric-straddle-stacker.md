## II. Core Advantages
1. **Ultimate Flexibility for Ultra-Narrow Spaces**
   * **Shorter Body Length**: Lacking a standing platform, the overall length of a walkie stacker is shorter than that of stand-on models.
   * **Smaller Turning Radius**: It is the most flexible turning electric stacker available, making it exceptionally well-suited for extremely crowded warehouses, narrow rack aisles, or even inside small van compartments.
2. **Lower Purchasing and Maintenance Costs (Cost-Effective Choice)**
   * **Affordable Pricing**: Under the same load capacity and lift height, walkie stackers feature a simpler structure and are typically much cheaper than stand-on or ride-on models.
   * **Simple Maintenance**: With a compact mechanical structure and relatively centralized electronic components, routine maintenance and service costs are lower, catering to low-to-medium-intensity handling requirements.
3. **Higher Operational Safety (Pedestrian-Friendly)**
   * **Restricted Speed**: The travel speed of a walkie stacker is typically set to match normal walking speed (approx. 4–6 km/h), ensuring high safety in pedestrian-heavy warehouses and minimizing severe collision accidents.
   * **Unobstructed Line of Sight**: Operators stand on the ground, allowing for a clearer view of the surrounding environment and ground conditions (such as obstacles or potholes) compared to riding on the machine.
   * **Emergency Reversal Safety Device**: The handle head is typically equipped with a "belly button switch" (if pressed against the operator, the machine automatically reverses), effectively preventing operators from being crushed in tight spaces.
4. **Extremely Low Operating Threshold**
   * **Quick to Master**: The operation handle is highly intuitive, integrating all functions such as lifting, driving, and the horn. Ordinary employees can operate it proficiently after a few minutes of simple training.
   * **Controllable Fatigue**: Although walking is required, since lifting and driving are completely electric, operators do not need to pull hard or repeatedly pump like manual stackers, significantly reducing physical labor burdens.
5. **Easy to Store and Transfer**
   * **Small Footprint**: When not in use, the equipment occupies minimal warehouse floor space.
   * **Convenient Transport**: Its self-weight is generally lighter than stand-on models, making it easier to transfer across different floors or locations via elevators or small trucks.

## III. Operation Procedure
1. **Inspection and Preparation**: Before startup, check whether the emergency power-off switch is reset, verify the battery level, and confirm there are no obstacles around the forks.
2. **Starting and Driving**: Insert the key, press the power switch, grip the operating handle, and control forward or reverse movement via the stepless speed control button on the handle.
3. **Handling and Stacking**: Smoothly insert the forks into the bottom of the pallet, use the lifting rocker on the handle to control fork height, and ensure the cargo center of gravity remains stable during transit.
4. **Parking and Locking**: After completing operations, lower the forks to the lowest position, park in a safe area, turn off the power, and remove the key.
5. **Charging and Maintenance**: When the battery indicator shows low power, promptly connect the dedicated charger to prevent over-discharge from affecting battery lifespan.
