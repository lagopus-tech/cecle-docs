## II. Key Advantages
1. **Labor-Saving & Efficient Electric Lifting**:
   * Compared to fully manual stackers, it eliminates the need for manual pumping via handles or foot pedals. 
   * By simply flicking the control lever, 1-ton loads can be easily elevated to a height of 1.6 meters, greatly saving physical strength and improving handling efficiency.
2. **High Cost-Effectiveness**:
   * Its price is much lower than fully electric stackers (which feature both electric travel and electric lifting). 
   * Since lifting is the most tiring and hazardous action in material handling, this machine resolves this core issue through electric power, making it ideal for budget-conscious small and medium warehouses looking to boost productivity.
3. **Compact & Maneuverable**:
   * With a compact body size (approx. 1700mm x 800mm) and a small turning radius (1700mm), it is perfectly suited for operations in narrow warehouse aisles, between workshop shelves, or inside delivery truck containers.
4. **Easy & Low-Cost Maintenance**:
   * Equipped with a maintenance-free lead-acid battery, users do not need to add water regularly, saving time and effort. 
   * Compared to complex fully electric vehicles, the electrical system of a semi-electric truck is simpler, resulting in lower failure rates and reduced maintenance costs.
5. **Convenient Smart Charging**:
   * Comes with a full smart charger that automatically stops once fully charged to protect battery life. 
   * It supports household outlet voltages, making charging extremely convenient.
6. **Safety & Stability**:
   * Built with a thickened steel frame structure that is robust and durable, ensuring stability and safety when lifting heavy loads.

## III. Operation Procedure
1. **Inspection & Preparation**: Check battery power, hydraulic systems, control levers, and wheels before use, and ensure the work area is free of obstacles.
2. **Fork Alignment**: Push the stacker to smoothly insert the forks into the bottom of the pallet, ensuring a balanced load center.
3. **Electric Lifting**: Operate the control switch or button to start the built-in motor, steadily raising the goods to the desired height.
4. **Manual Pushing**: Maneuver the stacker smoothly to target shelves or vehicle locations (its tight turning radius is ideal for narrow aisles).
5. **Goods Placement**: After adjusting the position, slowly lower the forks to place the cargo steadily, then withdraw the forks.
6. **Routine Maintenance**: Park the equipment in a designated safe area after operation and recharge promptly using the smart charger.
