## II. Core Advantages
1. **Fork Reach Function**: The forks can extend forward to retrieve loads and retract afterwards, allowing deep access into racking and compatibility with various pallet sizes.
2. **Pallet Versatility**: Unlike regular stackers hindered by bottom "outriggers", reach stackers can lift any type of pallet, including double-sided and block/perimeter-base pallets.
3. **Space Saving / Small Turning Radius**: Specifically designed for narrow aisles; when the forks retract, the vehicle length decreases, enabling highly flexible steering in tight spaces.
4. **Enhanced Stability**: Retracting the load back toward the vehicle's center of gravity ensures much smoother and safer transport.
5. **Precise Operation**: Equipped with high-quality hydraulic systems, offering exceptionally smooth and precise lifting and reaching operations.

---

## III. Operation Workflow
1.  **Startup Inspection**: Check battery levels, the hydraulic system, and emergency braking switches before operating.
2.  **Movement & Alignment**: Drive the equipment smoothly to the front of the rack or pallet.
3.  **Fork Extension**: Control the operating handle to extend the forks forward to the required depth and insert them into the pallet.
4.  **Lifting & Retracting**: Lift the load, verify stability, and retract the forks back into the vehicle body position.
5.  **Smooth Transport**: Transport securely after pulling the load weight back to the center position.
6.  **Positioning & Lowering**: Upon reaching the destination, adjust the height and lower the forks slowly until the pallet rests securely in place.
