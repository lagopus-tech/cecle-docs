The following covers the core features and application scenarios of fully electric, semi-electric, manual, and special-purpose stackers:

### II. Selection Recommendations

1. **Choose based on pallet type**:
   * If your warehouse uses **double-sided pallets, grid-bottom, or stringerless non-standard pallets** with bottom crossbeams, standard stackers cannot insert their forks. You must choose the **Fully Electric Straddle Stacker** or **Semi-Electric Straddle Stacker** whose legs can bypass the pallet structure.
   * If you only use **single-sided pallets** without bottom crossbeams, you can choose standard walkie, stand-on, or fixed-leg semi-electric/manual stackers.

2. **Choose based on work intensity and budget**:
   * **High intensity, long distance, high efficiency**: The fully electric series (**Stand-on Electric Stacker** or **CTD20 Fully Electric Stacker**) is the top choice to significantly reduce physical fatigue.
   * **Limited budget, but wanting to eliminate the most exhausting "lifting" step**: The **Semi-Electric Stacker** is a high-value choice, combining electric lifting with manual pushing to solve the core pain point.
   * **Low frequency, pursuing ultimate cost-effectiveness**: The **Manual Hydraulic Stacker** features a simple structure, zero electricity bills, and extremely low maintenance costs.

3. **Choose based on special materials (e.g., oil drums)**:
   * If you need **360-degree precise rollover and pouring of oil drums (to prevent splashing)**, choose the Manual Oil Drum Stacker.
   * If you only need **fast gripping, handling, and short-distance lifting of oil drums**, choose the Parrot-Beak Drum Lifter.
