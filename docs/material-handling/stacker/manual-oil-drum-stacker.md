## III. Operation Flow
1. **Inspection & Preparation**: Check all components (e.g., hydraulic pump, C-clamp, chains, and worm gear drive) for proper function and ensure the operating area is free of obstacles.
2. **Drum Clamping**: Move the stacker to align the C-clamp with the target drum (steel or plastic) and use the clamping mechanism to secure the drum firmly.
3. **Lifting**: Step on the foot pedal or operate the hydraulic handle to smoothly lift the drum to the desired height.
4. **Rotation & Pouring**: Use the manual worm gear handwheel to precisely control the rotation angle, achieving 360-degree turnover or accurate liquid pouring without splashing.
5. **Lowering & Transfer**: Once pouring is complete, return the drum to the upright position, lower it to the lowest point, and release the clamp to free the drum.
6. **Parking & Storage**: Park the equipment in a designated safe area, keep it clean, and perform routine lubrication and maintenance.
