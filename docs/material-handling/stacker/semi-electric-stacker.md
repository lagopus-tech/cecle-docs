## III. Operation Guide
To ensure safety and equipment longevity, please follow these operating procedures:

1. **Pre-operation Check**:
   * Ensure the battery is fully charged.
   * Inspect the hydraulic system for leaks and check chain tension.
   * Confirm the brakes are functioning correctly for effective parking on inclines or in work areas.
2. **Stacking & Retrieval**:
   * Slowly push the equipment and fully insert the forks into the bottom of the pallet.
   * Ensure the load center is centered to avoid unbalanced loading.
3. **Electric Lifting**:
   * Press the "Up" button on the control panel to activate the motor, which drives the hydraulic pump to lift the load.
   * Release the button once the desired height is reached; the hydraulic system will lock automatically.
4. **Lowering Operation**:
   * Gently turn or press the lowering handle/knob to control the release speed, allowing the load to descend smoothly.
   * Maintain a steady speed during descent; avoid rapid lowering to prevent impact on the load.
5. **Parking Advice**:
   * After completing operations, lower the forks to the lowest position, park in a flat and dry area, and switch off the power.
