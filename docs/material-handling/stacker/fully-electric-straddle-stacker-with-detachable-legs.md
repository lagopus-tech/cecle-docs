## I. Product Overview
The CTD20 is a fully powered electric stacker designed specifically for heavy-duty material handling. Its core highlight lies in its innovative **removable straddle leg** design. The outriggers can be flexibly adjusted or disassembled according to pallet sizes, which not only improves handling stability but also greatly enhances compatibility with different pallet specifications (especially double-faced closed pallets), making it an ideal choice for pursuing warehousing high efficiency and transportation economy.

---

## II. Core Advantages
1. **Massive Logistics Transportation Cost Savings**: By packaging the body and fork legs separately, the overall outer box volume (CBM) is significantly reduced, drastically lowering unit transportation costs for international ocean and land freight.
2. **Strong Pallet Compatibility**: The straddle leg design can span across both sides of a pallet, enabling it to handle not only standard pallets but also **double-faced closed pallets** with bottom crossbeams.
3. **High Stability and Strong Load Capacity**: Tailored for 2-ton load capacities and 3-meter lift heights, the wide-base design provides exceptional lateral support to prevent tipping during high-altitude lifting.
4. **Easier Maintenance and Replacement**: Featuring a modular design, if a leg suffers collision damage, only the local component needs to be replaced without requiring welding repairs on the main frame.
5. **Simple and Efficient Assembly**: Utilizing high-strength industrial bolts, customers do not need professional welding skills and can quickly complete on-site assembly using basic tools.

---

## III. Operation Procedure
1. **Inspection and Preparation**: Before startup, check whether the emergency power-off switch is reset, verify the battery level, and confirm that there are no obstacles around the forks.
2. **Starting and Driving**: Insert the key, press the power switch, grip the operating handle, and control forward or reverse movement via the stepless speed control button on the handle.
3. **Handling and Stacking**: Smoothly insert the forks into the bottom of the pallet, use the lifting rocker on the handle to control the height, and ensure the cargo center of gravity remains stable during travel.
4. **Parking and Locking**: After completing operations, lower the forks to the lowest position, park in a safe area, turn off the power, and remove the key.
5. **Charging and Maintenance**: When the battery indicator shows low power, promptly connect the dedicated charger to prevent over-discharge.

## V. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q: How is the stability of the bolted connection under a 2-ton load?**
A: We use high-strength industrial-grade shear bolts for fixation, combined with a channel-steel slot design to ensure that the legs and the main body are connected firmly, fully meeting the strength requirements for a 2-ton full-load operation.

**Q: Is daily maintenance complicated after removing the legs?**
A: Very simple. The design is modular by intent; disassembling a few fastening bolts allows for easy leg replacement without requiring professional welding skills.

**Q: Is this design more freight-efficient than regular forklifts?**
A: Yes. By detaching the legs, you can significantly reduce shipping volume (CBM). For cross-border ocean shipping or long-distance transport, freight savings can reach up to 30%–50%.
