## II. Core Advantages
1. **Exceptional Pallet Versatility (Core Advantage)**:
   * **Compatible with "Double-Sided Pallets" or "Closed Pallets"**: Ordinary stackers have base legs directly under the forks, restricting them to single-sided pallets (pallets without bottom crossboards). In contrast, widened straddle legs bypass the sides of the pallet, allowing it to easily handle double-sided pallets, block pallets, or stringer pallets.
   * **Unrestricted by Pallet Bottom Structures**: Regardless of the pallet's bottom design, as long as the widened legs can straddle it, normal operation is guaranteed.
2. **Better Operational Stability**:
   * **Wider Base, Steadier Center of Gravity**: The widened support legs increase the contact area (support surface) between the vehicle and the ground. When goods are lifted to high positions, the straddle leg design effectively prevents lateral shaking or tipping, ensuring higher safety.
   * **Suitable for Oversized Goods**: For non-standard goods exceeding standard pallet widths, the straddle legs offer superior load balance.
3. **Flexible Adjustability**:
   * **Adjustable Width**: The fork and leg widths of this vehicle are typically adjustable (such as within a certain range or fixed via bolts). This means a single unit can adapt to different pallet sizes and narrow racking aisles with extreme flexibility.
4. **Convenience Brought by "Removability"**:
   * **Easy Transportation and Storage**: Large equipment incurs high logistics costs; the removable design significantly reduces shipping volume and cuts freight expenses. When out of use for long periods or moving across floors, disassembly makes it much easier to fit into elevators or narrow doorways.
   * **Convenient Post-Maintenance**: If the straddle legs suffer collision deformation, they can be individually disassembled and replaced or straightened without requiring factory returns or massive cutting and welding.
5. **Protection for Racks and Goods**:
   * **Collision Prevention**: When loading/unloading near storage racks, the support legs on both sides reduce the risk of direct front-end impacts with rack upright bases. Adjusting the width also aids in precise rack slot alignment.
6. **Broader Application Scenarios**:
   * It serves versatile roles ranging from a standard stacker to a specialized handler for oversized and high-center-of-gravity items. For businesses renting warehouses with chaotic pallet assortments, this equipment is an "all-in-one" choice.

## III. Operation Workflow
1. **Inspection & Preparation**: Check all equipment components (such as the hydraulic system, battery level, chains, and adjustable straddle leg bolts) for secure and normal conditions, ensuring the operating area is free of obstacles.
2. **Adjusting Straddle Legs & Fork Width**: Depending on the target pallet dimensions (e.g., double-sided or non-standard large pallets), manually adjust the spacing of the widened straddle legs and forks, ensuring they are firmly locked in place.
3. **Fork Insertion**: Slowly push the equipment so that the widened straddle legs clear both sides of the pallet, inserting the forks completely and steadily into the bottom of the pallet.
4. **Lifting Operation**: Press the "Up" button on the control panel; the high-power motor activates to drive the hydraulic system, smoothly lifting the goods to the desired height.
5. **Lowering & Transfer**: After moving the goods to the target storage location, slowly operate the lowering handle/button to lower the goods smoothly, then back out the stacker.
6. **Parking & Storage**: Upon completing operations, lower the forks to their lowest position, turn off the power switch, and park the equipment in a designated safe, dry area.

## V. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Why is this stacker called a "straddle legs" stacker? How does it differ from regular stackers?**
A: Regular stackers have base legs located directly beneath the forks, limiting them to single-sided pallets without bottom crossboards. Straddle stackers feature wider leg spans that bypass the sides of the pallet, perfectly accommodating double-sided, block, stringer, and other complex pallet configurations.

**Q: Can the fork and leg widths of this truck be adjusted as needed?**
A: Yes. The equipment supports a width-adjustable design, allowing flexible adaptation to varying pallet sizes and narrow rack aisles, greatly enhancing warehousing operational flexibility.

**Q: What are the practical benefits of the removable design?**
A: The removable design not only significantly reduces logistics volume and freight costs during long-distance shipping, but also provides massive convenience when moving across floors or navigating narrow doorways and elevators. Additionally, if the straddle legs experience bump damage, they can be replaced independently without factory returns.

**Q: Is this stacker complicated to operate? Can regular staff use it right away?**
A: Operation is very straightforward. The core lifting process is controlled via electric buttons, saving time and physical effort. Regular employees can operate it skillfully and safely after a brief safety training session.
