## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages (产品概述与核心优势)

The QYMD Series Short Path Distillation system operates by minimizing the distance vapor must travel before condensing, ensuring high-purity separation at significantly lower boiling points under high vacuum conditions. Its core advantages include:

* **High Purity & Non-Destructive Separation**: The system enables distillation at much lower temperatures. This perfectly preserves heat-sensitive compounds, such as active botanical oils and crude oil extracts, without oxidation or thermal degradation.
* **Continuous Automated Operation**: Equipped with an automated continuous feeding and discharging system, allowing for seamless pilot-scale production without the need to constantly break the vacuum seal.
* **Deep Vacuum Capability**: Features an advanced diffusion pump system capable of reaching an ultimate vacuum of 0.1Pa, drastically lowering the boiling point of heavy fractions.
* **Premium Construction & Visibility**: Features fully polished stainless steel construction with clear visual inspection ports, ensuring maximum durability, easy cleaning, and real-time monitoring of the distillation process.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What materials are most suitable for processing with the QYMD series distillation equipment?**
**A:** It is highly favored for the purification and separation of high-boiling-point, heat-sensitive, and easily oxidizable materials. It is widely used in botanical extraction (such as crude oil and essential oil purification), pharmaceuticals, and fine chemicals, as it extracts pure compounds without degrading their active ingredients.

**Q: Can this equipment run continuously, or is it batch-only?**
**A:** The QYMD-60D and QYMD-80D models are designed with automatic continuous feeding and discharging mechanisms. This allows for continuous processing (up to 4 kg/hr on the 80D model) without needing to stop the machine to empty or refill tanks, greatly improving production efficiency.

**Q: What vacuum level can this system achieve, and why is it important?**
**A:** The system is equipped with a high-performance diffusion pump setup that can achieve a deep vacuum level of 0.1Pa. This extreme vacuum environment drastically lowers the boiling point of the target materials, ensuring that heat-sensitive compounds can be safely evaporated and separated at much lower temperatures.
