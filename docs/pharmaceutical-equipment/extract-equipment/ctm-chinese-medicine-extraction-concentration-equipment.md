## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages (产品概述与核心优势)

The CTM Series is meticulously engineered for modern herbal and pharmaceutical processing. It acts as a comprehensive solution for boiling and extraction processes. Its core advantages include:

* **Multi-Purpose Processing**: Capable of handling standard water/alcohol boiling and extraction, as well as the efficient recovery of alcohol and precise separation of volatile essential oils.
* **Fully Sealed & Recyclable System**: The entire extraction process operates within a completely sealed environment. This closed-loop system prevents the loss of active ingredients and allows for the simultaneous recovery of organic solvents from the waste residue.
* **Comprehensive Accessory Integration**: In addition to the main extraction and concentration tanks, the equipment is fully outfitted with a foam catcher, a cooler, and an oil-water separator to guarantee processing purity.
* **Sanitary Stainless Steel Construction**: All components that come into direct contact with the drug or raw materials are manufactured strictly from high-quality stainless steel, complying with stringent industry hygiene standards.
