# GK-25 Dry Granulator

![](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/jimeng-2026-11-14-6587-_1000__1000_....png?v=1794624275)

The GK-25 Dry Granulator is a pharmaceutical and chemical equipment that utilizes a mechanical extrusion granulation process to directly and physically press dry powder or fine crystalline materials containing a certain amount of crystal water into flakes and form granules. It is highly suitable for laboratory R&D, new drug sampling, pilot scale-up, and small-batch continuous production in the pharmaceutical, food, chemical, and new material industries. Its core advantage lies in adopting the "pure dry method" roller compaction technology, requiring no liquid binders throughout the entire process, which perfectly protects the active ingredients of heat-sensitive and moisture-degradable materials. This model has a production capacity of 1-5 Kg/h, and the finished particle size is continuously adjustable from 0.3-5mm. All material contact parts of the machine are made of high-quality food/pharmaceutical-grade stainless steel, fully complying with strict GMP production standards, making it the ideal core equipment for waterless granulation processes.

## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The GK-25 Dry Granulator completely subverts the traditional wet granulation process. Based on the technological principle of mechanical extrusion granulation, it extrudes formulated dry powder materials into dense flakes, which then pass through a granulation system and a screen to produce uniform granules of the required size. Its core advantages include:

* **Waterless Pure Dry Process**: The granulation process requires no addition of wetting agents, eliminating tedious traditional steps such as mixing, making soft materials, and drying. This not only significantly saves production time and drying energy consumption but also fundamentally guarantees the stability of heat-sensitive drugs.

* **High-Precision Operating Parameter Control**: The equipment's roller speed (0-18 rpm), screw speed (0-61 rpm), granulation speed (0-600 rpm), and roller pressure (0-7 Mpa) can all be steplessly adjusted, accurately matching the forming characteristics of different dry powder materials.

* **High Yield & Uniform Particle Size**: The produced granules feature high density and good flowability. The finished particle size can be flexibly adjusted between 0.3-5mm as needed, which can be directly used in subsequent capsule filling or tableting processes.

* **GMP Compliance & Minimalist Design**: The machine features a compact structure and weighs 198kg. The fully enclosed granulation cabin design effectively prevents dust flying and cross-contamination. Disassembly, daily cleaning, and maintenance are extremely simplified.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is the working principle of the dry granulator?**
**A:** It is mainly based on the process principle of mechanical extrusion granulation. It directly extrudes formulated dry powder or fine crystalline materials containing a certain amount of crystal water into flakes, which are then passed through the granulation system and made into granules of the required size through a screen. No moisture or binders need to be added throughout the process.

**Q: What is the capacity and applicable scenario of the GK-25 model?**
**A:** The GK-25 has a designed capacity of 1-5 Kg/h and features a compact overall size (550×500×1180mm), making it a typical laboratory and pilot-scale equipment. It is highly suitable for scientific research institutes, university pharmacy laboratories, and pharmaceutical factories for new drug R&D, formula sampling, and small-batch trial production.

**Q: Can the size of the finished granules produced by the machine be adjusted?**
**A:** Yes, it can be freely adjusted. By replacing the built-in screens with different pore sizes and adjusting the granulation speed (0-600 rpm) on the control panel, you can easily and precisely control the finished particle size within the range of 0.3mm to 5mm, satisfying the requirements of different terminal dosage forms.
