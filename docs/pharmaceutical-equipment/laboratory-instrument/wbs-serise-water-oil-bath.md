## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages (产品概述与核心优势)

The WBS Series Constant Temperature Bath is an indispensable laboratory instrument that combines safety, precision, and durability. It provides uniform heating for various reaction vessels, rotary evaporators, and glass reactors. Its core advantages include:

*   **Dual-Purpose Versatility (Water/Oil)**: Engineered to handle a wide temperature range, making it suitable for standard water bath heating (up to 100°C) and high-temperature oil bath heating (up to 250°C) without changing equipment.
*   **Intelligent PID Digital Control**: Features an advanced PID automated temperature controller that provides rapid heating, minimizes temperature overshoot, and maintains a strict control accuracy of ±1°C. 
*   **Safe Handwheel Lifting Mechanism**: Designed with a smooth, manual handwheel lifting system. This allows operators to easily and safely adjust the height of the heated bath relative to the glass reaction flasks, minimizing the risk of burns or glassware breakage.
*   **Premium Stainless Steel & Explosion-Proof Features**: The inner heating pot is stamped and formed from high-quality stainless steel, ensuring excellent corrosion resistance and easy cleaning. Select configurations can feature explosion-proof electric heating tubes for hazardous environments.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can this bath be used for both water and oil heating?**
**A:** Yes, the WBS series is a dual-purpose system. For temperatures below 100°C, it can be safely used as a water bath. For temperatures ranging up to 180°C or 250°C (depending on the model), it functions as a highly efficient oil bath.

**Q: What is the main advantage of the handwheel lifting mechanism?**
**A:** The handwheel lift allows the operator to manually raise or lower the hot bath rather than moving the delicate and often heavy glass reaction setup. This significantly improves operational safety, preventing accidental burns or the breakage of expensive laboratory glassware.

**Q: How precise is the temperature control during long-term reactions?**
**A:** Extremely precise. The equipment utilizes an intelligent PID (Proportional-Integral-Derivative) control method, which continuously monitors and adjusts the heating power to maintain a strict temperature control accuracy of ±1°C, even during prolonged continuous operation.
