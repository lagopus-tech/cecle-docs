# SHZ-D(III) Series Anti-Corrosion Circulating Water Vacuum Pump Product Overview

![](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/jimeng-2026-09-20-9238-image_1000__1000_....png?v=1789893842)

The SHZ-D(III) Series Anti-Corrosion Circulating Water Multi-Purpose Vacuum Pump is a device that uses circulating water as its working fluid and generates negative pressure through fluid jet action for vacuum evacuation. This machine is best suited for industries such as pharmaceutical, chemical, biological, and materials science, and is commonly used to support 2L to 5L rotary evaporators or glass reactors for distillation and concentration. Its core advantages include a high-strength anti-corrosion material body, flexible dual-tap or quad-tap configurations, and a maximum vacuum level of up to 0.098 Mpa. While providing stable vacuum, it also circulates cooling water to reaction devices, making it an indispensable high-efficiency and energy-saving equipment.

## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The SHZ-D(III) Series Circulating Water Vacuum Pump is a fundamental supporting equipment in modern chemical and pharmaceutical laboratories. It ingeniously combines the functions of a water pump and a vacuum pump. Its core advantages include:

* **Multi-Function Dual Efficiency**: It can both provide stable vacuum negative pressure for reaction systems and supply circulating cooling water to the condensers of rotary evaporators or glass reactors, offering multiple functions in a single unit.

* **Premium Anti-Corrosion Material**: The body is manufactured from high-strength anti-corrosion materials, effectively resisting corrosion from various chemical solvent gases and significantly extending the equipment's service life in harsh chemical environments.

* **Flexible Suction Configuration**: Offers two configuration options: dual-gauge dual-tap and quad-gauge quad-tap. Suction ports can operate independently or in parallel, easily meeting the needs of simultaneous vacuum evacuation for single or multiple experimental devices.

* **High-Efficiency Vacuum Performance**: Single-tap suction capacity reaches 10 L/min, with a maximum vacuum level of 0.098 Mpa, providing reliable assurance for concentration and crystallization processes that require constant negative pressure.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What equipment is the circulating water vacuum pump mainly used with?**
**A:** This equipment is mainly used to support 2L to 5L rotary evaporators or double-layer glass reactors, providing the necessary vacuum environment and auxiliary cooling water circulation for these core laboratory devices.

**Q: How can the pump achieve optimal vacuum performance?**
**A:** Based on the characteristics of this fluid jet equipment, when the circulating water temperature in the water tank is maintained at a low level (between 4°C and 10°C), the impact of water's saturated vapor pressure can be minimized to the greatest extent, thereby achieving the best vacuum effect (maximum vacuum level up to 0.098 Mpa).

**Q: What is the difference between the dual-gauge dual-tap and quad-gauge quad-tap models?**
**A:** The dual-gauge dual-tap model is suitable for routine small-scale single-unit experiments and can connect to one reaction device. The quad-gauge quad-tap model (with higher power at 370W) is equipped with four independent suction ports, allowing simultaneous connection and monitoring of multiple experimental devices, significantly improving parallel work efficiency in the laboratory.
