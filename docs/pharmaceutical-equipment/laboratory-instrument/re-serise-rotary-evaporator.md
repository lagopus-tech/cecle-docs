## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages (产品概述与核心优势)

The RE Series Large Rotary Evaporator is engineered for pilot-scale and continuous industrial processing. It operates by rotating a large glass flask under negative pressure, creating a thin film of the solvent for highly efficient, uniform heating. Its core advantages include:

*   **High-Borosilicate Glass Construction**: All glass components (spin bottle, collection bottle, and condenser) are crafted from durable high-borosilicate glass, offering superior thermal shock resistance and inertness to highly corrosive chemicals.
*   **Optimized Condensation Efficiency**: Features a large-area dual-spiral condensing tube (up to 1.5m² on the 50L model) that rapidly converts chemical vapors back into liquid form, achieving impressive water evaporation rates (up to >9L/h).
*   **Precision Flange Sealing**: Utilizes a robust flange sealing mechanism (ø95 to ø125) combined with high-quality PTFE seals to maintain an optimal, stable vacuum state, which is critical for lowering the boiling point of heat-sensitive solvents.
*   **Reliable Speed & Temperature Control**: Equipped with a stable rotary motor offering adjustable host speeds (10-120 r/min or 10-90 r/min depending on model size) and a dedicated 3kw heating bath to ensure consistent evaporation conditions.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What types of industries benefit most from using this large rotary evaporator?**
**A:** This equipment is highly suitable for the pharmaceutical, chemical, and biological sectors, particularly in processes involving plant extraction (like CBD), solvent recovery, concentration of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), and fine chemical synthesis.

**Q: Why is high-borosilicate glass used for the flasks and condenser?**
**A:** High-borosilicate glass provides excellent resistance to thermal shock, meaning it can handle rapid temperature changes without cracking. It is also highly inert, preventing it from reacting with corrosive solvents, acids, or alkalis, which ensures the absolute purity of the recovered material.

**Q: How does the system achieve such high evaporation rates?**
**A:** The high evaporation rate is achieved by combining three factors: a stable vacuum environment that lowers the solvent's boiling point, a rotating flask that spreads the liquid into a thin, continuously heated film, and a massive condensing area (up to 1.5m²) that rapidly cools vapors back into liquid.
