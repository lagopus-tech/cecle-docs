# Industrial Coating Machine Selection Guide and Product Overview (Coating Machine Series)

## I. What is a Coating Machine?

A coating machine is specialized pharmaceutical and food machinery used to uniformly coat the surface of tablets, granules, pills, or candies with powder, syrup, or film coating mediums. Its core purpose is to improve product appearance, mask unpleasant odors, prevent moisture and oxidation, or achieve sustained and controlled release of drugs.

### Core Types and Working Principles
1. **Small/Laboratory Coating Machines (BY & BYC Series)**: These utilize a rotating coating pan to tumble the base materials, combined with hot air drying to cure the coating. They feature a compact structure and intuitive operation, making them suitable for early-stage R&D and small-batch trial production.
2. **Large High-Efficiency Coating Machines (BG Series)**: These adopt a fully enclosed, high-efficiency through-flow coating technology. Materials perform complex trajectory movements within a clean coating drum, and the coating medium is evenly sprayed through an atomizing spray gun. Combined with negative pressure hot air through-flow for extremely fast drying, this series is specially designed for standardized mass production with high capacity and for micro/small materials.

## III. Core Differences Between Different Models

### 1. Technical Differences Between BY and BYC
* **Spray System**: The BYC series is equipped with a dedicated atomizing spray gun and liquid supply pump, capable of achieving micron-level atomization of the coating liquid, resulting in an extremely flat coating and saving coating liquid. The BY series usually relies on manual syrup application, which places higher demands on operator experience.
* **Process Adaptability**: BYC perfectly matches modern high-standard film coating processes. BY demonstrates extremely high cost-effectiveness in traditional sugar coating and polishing processes.

### 2. Outstanding Performance of the Large BG Series
* **Material Adaptability**: The BG series (such as BG-150E) has optimized air ducts and mesh holes specifically for **micro and small materials**, completely solving the problems of fine particle loss or hole blockage during the through-flow coating process.
* **Environmental Protection and Safety**: Fully enclosed negative pressure operation effectively prevents dust leakage. The optional pulse dust removal system can achieve compliant exhaust emission, protecting the health of operators.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions and Technical Explanations (FAQ)

* **Q: How long does it usually take to coat a batch of materials?**
    * **A:** Standard pharmaceutical-grade coating operation is a rigorous process involving multiple cycles of spraying, fluidization, and drying. **The processing time for a single batch requires at least 2 hours or more** to ensure the density and uniform drying of the coating. Claims in the market such as "completing coating in 15 minutes" are unachievable under standard pharmacopoeia and actual compliant production.
* **Q: My materials are very small (e.g., pellets). Which machine is suitable?**
    * **A:** We strongly recommend choosing the customized **BG series**. We will equip you with a coating drum having a special aperture and adjust the hot air through-flow mode to ensure that micro-particles do not leak or stick together.
