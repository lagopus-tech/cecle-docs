# BG Series Coating Machine Selection Guide and Product Overview

![](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/jimeng-2026-08-10-1882-_1000__1000_....png?v=1786327786)

> **Core Abstract**
> The BG series efficient intelligent coating machine is high-efficiency processing equipment specially designed for film and sugar coating of tablets, pills, and candies. It is extremely suitable for high-standard production lines in the pharmaceutical and food industries. The core advantage of this series lies in its PLC human-machine interface intelligent control system that complies with GMP requirements, as well as a constant-temperature hot air drying system that reaches a 100,000-class purification standard. Covering a wide capacity range from 10 kg to 80 kg/time, this equipment comes standard with Japan-imported spray guns, featuring no dust pollution and energy saving. It ensures that the coated finished products have a uniform, solid, and smooth surface, making it a highly cost-effective and ideal choice for modern, high-standard formulation processes.

## I. Product Overview

The BG series coating machine adopts a control system composed of a PLC, which is reasonably designed and flexibly programmed, enabling it to perfectly adapt to the complex requirements of various pharmaceutical processes. Its performance is stable and reliable, fully complying with GMP standards. The equipment is equipped with a streamlined deflector stirrer internally, making the material flip smoothly and effectively avoiding fragmentation problems caused by falling and colliding from high places, thereby significantly improving the yield.

In addition, this series of machines utilizes a constant pressure variable peristaltic pump, which can automatically balance the output slurry and spray volume, thereby stabilizing the atomization effect, preventing spray gun blockage, and saving dressing materials to the greatest extent. The accompanying hot air drying system undergoes primary, intermediate, and advanced three-stage filtration to ensure the hot air cleanliness reaches the 100,000 class. The exhaust system is also specially equipped with shock-proof and automatic dust removal devices, resulting in low operating noise and easy cleaning.
