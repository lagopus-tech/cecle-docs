# BYC Series Coating Machine Selection Guide and Product Overview

![](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/jimeng-2026-08-08-2902.png?v=1786177294)

> **Core Abstract**
> The BYC Series Coating Machine is high-efficiency processing equipment that integrates sugar coating, film coating, and polishing functions, specifically designed for the surface coating of various tablets, pills, and candies. It is highly suitable for high-standard production environments in the pharmaceutical, food, health product, and chemical industries. The core advantage of this series lies in its extremely broad capacity coverage—perfectly matching everything from 2 kg/time laboratory-grade R&D sampling to 250 kg/time large-scale mass production. The entire machine is constructed from high-quality stainless steel and is equipped with a stepless speed-adjustable rotary pot, an independent hot and cold air temperature control system, and a customized spray gun. It provides the most efficient and uniform coating effect, making it a highly cost-effective solution for modern formulation processes.

## I. Product Overview

The BYC Series Coating Machine is primarily used in the pharmaceutical and food industries to provide a uniform protective layer for various tablets, pills, and candies. The equipment is configured with powerful, independent heating and cooling fans. The heating temperature can be flexibly adjusted between 0-100°C according to process requirements, and cold air cooling can also be performed after turning off the heating. This series includes various models such as BYC-300, BYC-400, BYC-600, BYC-1000, and BYC-1500, perfectly adapting to various customized scenarios from small-batch pilot trials to large-scale automated production.

## IV. FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

**Q: What temperature control functions does the BYC series coating machine have?**
**A:** The heating temperature of the machine can be freely adjusted between 0-100°C according to process requirements. After the heating function is turned off, the equipment can introduce cold air through the internal system for cooling. Both heating and cooling functions can be activated at any time, greatly enriching the flexibility of coating operations.

**Q: What accessories are included when the machine is shipped? Is the packaging safe?**
**A:** Before leaving the factory, all equipment will be fully encapsulated in sturdy, industrial-grade fumigation-free wooden cases (Wooden case), which can completely withstand the bumps of long-distance sea freight. The packaging box will be fully equipped with the standard spare parts (spare parts) and dedicated installation tools required for the machine's operation.

**Q: What is the after-sales service and warranty policy for the equipment?**
**A:** The standard warranty period for the entire machine is one year. During the warranty period, as long as any equipment damage occurs under normal operation according to the instruction manual, we will provide free repair services or free replacement of damaged parts, and the customer only needs to bear the shipping cost from China to their local area. After the warranty period expires, we will continue to provide you with lifelong maintenance and technical support services.

**Q: If we need engineer assistance, how are the costs calculated?**
**A:** If you need our engineers to travel locally to provide on-site technical assistance during the installation or debugging phase, the engineers' round-trip travel expenses will be borne by the customer.
