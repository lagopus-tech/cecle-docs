## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The CGN-208L semi-automatic liquid capsule filling machine has made disruptive innovations based on traditional powder capsule filling, specifically designed to overcome the industry challenges of easy liquid leakage and inaccurate filling volumes when processing liquid and semi-fluid materials. Its core advantages include:

* **Extensive Fluid Adaptability**: Whether it is highly flowable oily liquids and regular solutions, or viscous suspensions and pastes, this equipment can achieve precise filling, greatly expanding the boundaries of dosage form development for health supplements and pharmaceuticals.

* **Innovative Horizontal Capsule Locking Technology**: Abandoning the traditional vertical capsule locking method, it adopts an innovative cylinder-driven horizontal locking structure. This design not only saves time and effort but also fundamentally resolves the industry pain point of liquid overflowing or dripping at the moment of capsule locking.

* **Fully Automatic & Efficient Capsule Sowing**: Equipped with a fully automatic capsule sowing mechanism, the sowing speed is extremely fast. This effectively complements the high-precision liquid filling, keeping the comprehensive capacity of a single machine stable at 15,000 capsules/hour.

* **High Precision & Multi-Specification Compatibility**: The filling accuracy is strictly controlled within ±3%. A single machine is compatible with the full range of hard capsules from 000#, 00#, 0# up to 5#, and mold replacement is highly convenient.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Will this machine leak when filling oily liquids or viscous pastes?**
**A:** Absolutely not. The CGN-208L features a specially developed cylinder-driven "innovative horizontal locking" structure for liquid filling. This design ensures that the capsule remains under horizontal stress during the closing and locking process, completely avoiding the problem of liquid overflowing due to gravity or squeezing found in traditional vertical locking methods.

**Q: How is the filling accuracy of the machine guaranteed?**
**A:** The equipment utilizes a high-precision liquid metering pumping system to ensure a high degree of consistency in every liquid injection. For common oily liquids, solutions, and suspensions, the filling accuracy can be strictly controlled within the ±3% range, fully complying with Pharmacopoeia and GMP standards.

**Q: Can one machine be used for multiple capsule sizes?**
**A:** Yes. The machine is compatible with a full series of capsule specifications including 000#, 00#, 0#, 1#, 2#, 3#, 4#, and 5#. If you need to produce capsules of different sizes, you only need to additionally purchase and replace the corresponding capsule sowing molds and filling modules, without the need to buy multiple main machines.
