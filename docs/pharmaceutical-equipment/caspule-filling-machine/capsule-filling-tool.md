The Manual Capsule Filling Board is a high-efficiency auxiliary tool designed for laboratories, clinics, pharmacies, and small-to-medium health supplement production. 1. **Blue Plastic**: An extremely cost-effective basic option. The material is lightweight and durable, ideal for cost-sensitive or small-batch dispensing needs.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How do I choose the right hole count based on my single-batch filling volume?**
**A:** Your daily average consumption directly determines the appropriate hole count. If you need to frequently test different formulas or only conduct sampling, we recommend choosing 100 holes; if you are doing fully manual mass production of a fixed product, choosing 400 holes or above can exponentially improve labor efficiency.

**Q: What are the differences between the various materials (blue plastic, clear plastic, acrylic), and how should I choose?**
**A:** If you are pursuing economy and practicality, blue plastic is the first choice; if you need precise control and visual operation, choose clear plastic; if you are seeking a long service life, premium texture, and high strength (especially for 600 holes and above in large sizes), be sure to go with acrylic material.

**Q: What should I pay attention to for the daily cleaning and maintenance of the capsule filling board?**
**A:** To maintain the hygiene and safety of the mold and extend its service life, daily maintenance is very simple. After each use, it is recommended to directly wipe or clean the tool with alcohol. Alcohol evaporates quickly and can effectively remove powder residue and grease. Please note: absolutely avoid using highly corrosive chemical solvents or placing the board in high-temperature environments for boiling, to prevent irreversible deformation or damage to the board.
