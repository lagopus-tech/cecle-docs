## Step 1: Identify Your Production Tier

Choose the tier that best describes your situation:

### Tier 1 — Manual Filling Board
**You are a good fit if you need to:**
- Test and develop new capsule formulations in a laboratory
- Fill capsules by hand at a clinic, pharmacy, or home studio
- Produce small batches (a few hundred to a few thousand capsules per session) with minimal upfront investment
- Maintain full hands-on control over every capsule

**Output range:** Depends entirely on operator speed — typically a few hundred to a few thousand capsules per session.

### Tier 2 — CGN-208D Semi-Automatic Machine
**You are a good fit if you need to:**
- Produce 10,000 to 30,000 capsules per hour consistently
- Switch between multiple product varieties frequently (quick mold change in 5–8 minutes)
- Run a small-to-medium health supplement or pharmaceutical workshop
- Transition from manual to automated production without a massive capital outlay

**Output range:** 10,000 – 30,000 capsules/hour

### Tier 3 — NJP Series Fully Automatic Machine
**You are a good fit if you need to:**
- Produce 12,000 to 150,000 capsules per hour with minimal human intervention
- Run standardized, continuous production lines with consistent output quality
- Achieve ±3.5% filling precision and 99%+ capsule loading qualification rate
- Scale production from pilot to full industrial capacity within a single product series

**Output range:** 12,000 – 150,000 capsules/hour (model-dependent)

**Key Decision Factors:**

## Step 4: Material & Compliance Notes

| Standard                     | Upgrade                       | When You Need the Upgrade                                    |
| :--------------------------- | :---------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| 304 / SUS304 Stainless Steel | 316 / SUS316L Stainless Steel | Filling materials involve strong acids, strong alkalis, or special pharmaceutical preparations; or when the production facility must pass GMP / FDA certification |
| Blue Plastic (manual boards) | Clear Plastic or Acrylic      | Need visual monitoring (clear) or extended service life under heavy use (acrylic) |
