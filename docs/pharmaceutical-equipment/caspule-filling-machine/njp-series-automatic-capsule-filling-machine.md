## 1. What Is a Fully Automatic Capsule Filling Machine?

The NJP Series Fully Automatic Capsule Filling Machine is a high-efficiency pharmaceutical and health supplement filling device specifically tailored for **powder and granular materials**. This equipment utilizes advanced intermittent reciprocating motion to fully automatically complete the entire process of capsule feeding, capsule separation, precision filling, waste blister rejection, locking, and finished product output, precisely and efficiently filling powder materials into hard empty capsules.

### Core Process & Industrial Technical Advantages
1. **Improved Rotating Mold Disc Design**: The internal turntable structure has been comprehensively optimized. The core transmission components directly use **genuine imported Japanese linear bearings**, ensuring that the equipment maintains extremely high precision and an ultra-long service life even during prolonged high-speed operation.
2. **Fully Bottom-Mounted Cam with Fully Enclosed Lubrication**: The workstation cams operate within an exceptionally well-maintained fully enclosed lubrication track. Internal oil lubrication is thorough, completely resolving the pain point of traditional machines where cams are mounted on top and bearings are easily exposed to dry or powder-contaminated environments.
3. **Vacuum Capsule Positioning & Extremely High Yield Rate**: A high-precision vacuum suction capsule positioning mechanism is employed, steadily maintaining the capsule-on loading qualification rate at **99% or above**.
4. **Exquisite Filling Precision**: The filling precision reaches **±3.5%** (far exceeding the ±5% national standard), ensuring consistency in material dosage for every single capsule.
5. **Intelligent Safety Protection**: Equipped with advanced microcomputer/PLC touchscreen operation and stepless variable frequency speed regulation system with real-time digital display. Additionally, comprehensive built-in safety protection devices ensure that when material shortage, capsule depletion, or door opening occurs, the equipment will automatically trigger a pause for protection.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How can I ensure the machine's output matches the overall production line speed? What factors affect actual production capacity?**
**A:** The actual production capacity of the fully automatic filling machine is directly affected by the bulk density and flowability of the powder material, as well as the capsule size (supports sizes 00#–5# and A-E safety capsules). We will strictly calculate the machine operating speed based on your expected per-shift daily output, and recommend reserving 15%–20% capacity margin within allowable process conditions to accommodate future business growth.

**Q: Does the machine's material meet pharmaceutical hygiene compliance standards? What premium customization options are available?**
**A:** Our standard models are manufactured with premium SUS304 stainless steel, with corrosion resistance, rust prevention, and heat resistance fully meeting the filling needs of the vast majority of conventional health supplement and food-grade powders. If the filling materials involve highly corrosive strong acids/strong alkalis, special Western pharmaceutical preparations, or if the workshop must pass stringent GMP/FDA certification, we can customize and upgrade the entire material-contact surface to SUS316L top-grade stainless steel, ensuring compliance with the highest level of hygiene standards.

**Q: What brands of components are used in the machine's core parts? How is the mechanical service life guaranteed?**
**A:** To eliminate transmission failure and positioning deviation, the core linear bearings of the NJP Series are directly imported from Japan. Meanwhile, the upper and lower section sealing structures of the equipment exclusively use imported double-lip sealing rings, providing an unparalleled sealing barrier that effectively prevents dust from being drawn back into the machine and wearing components, extending the equipment's service life to several times that of ordinary models.

**Q: How is the equipment packaged for cross-border shipping? What is the approximate production lead time?**
**A:** All machines undergo anti-rust fluid treatment and heavy-duty stretch film wrapping before leaving the factory, and are finally fully enclosed in sturdy fumigation-free plywood stainless steel frame crates (Plywood Case) to withstand sea salt spray and turbulence. Standard models can complete final assembly, testing, and shipment within as fast as 15 days after drawing confirmation and payment receipt, with support for global freight forwarding coordination.
