> The V series V-type double-arm mixer is a heavy-duty, high-efficiency blending machine engineered for precision mixing of dry powders and granular materials across pharmaceutical, food, chemical, and nutraceutical industries. Featuring an asymmetrical V-shaped cylinder body supported by a dual-arm frame, this machine achieves up to 99.9% mixing uniformity without centrifugal force, material segregation, or dead angles. Offering high charging efficiency up to 80%, the V series spans production capacities from laboratory R&D (V-5) to large-scale industrial manufacturing (V-2000).

## I. Product Overview

### 1. Applications
* **Industry Applications**: Widely used in pharmaceuticals (APIs, pre-mixing for tablet pressing and capsule filling), food processing (nutritional powders, seasonings, additives), chemical engineering, cosmetics, metallurgy, and R&D testing in laboratory environments.
* **Applicable Materials**: Suitable for dry powder and granular materials with varying specific gravities. Ideal for gentle, non-destructive mixing where particle shape preservation and high chemical homogeneity are required.

### 2. Core Advantages
* **Superior Mixing Uniformity (99.9%)**: The asymmetrical V-cylinder continuously splits and combines material streams as it rotates. Materials move in multidimensional paths with zero centrifugal force, preventing stratification, accumulation, or specific gravity segregation.
* **High Charging Efficiency**: Reaches a working loading rate of up to 80% (compared to only ~40% for conventional drum mixers), significantly shortening batch times and maximizing throughput.
* **GMP-Compliant Sanitary Construction**: Manufactured from high-grade SUS304/SUS316L stainless steel. Upper and lower cylinder sections feature arc-shaped transition zones with precision mirror polishing, eliminating sanitary dead ends for rapid cleaning and sterilization.
* **Robust Double-Arm Structure**: Dual heavy-duty side pillars provide optimal mechanical stability for larger capacities, maintaining low operational vibration, reduced noise, and extended mechanical service life.

## III. FAQ

**Q: What is the primary operational difference between a V-type double-arm mixer and a Three-Dimensional motion mixer?**  
**A:** The V-type double-arm mixer utilizes rotational gravity diffusion through asymmetrical cylinder arms to gently tumble and divide dry powders, making it ideal for standard free-flowing granules and powders. A Three-Dimensional (3D) motion mixer utilizes a multi-directional spatial movement (rotation, translation, and inversion) that completely counteracts gravity and centrifugal forces, making 3D mixers preferred for ultra-high density variations or cohesive powders.

**Q: Can internal stirring/forced agitation bars be added to the V-type double-arm mixer?**  
**A:** Yes. For materials containing minor liquid additives, sticky components, or agglomerated lumps, an optional high-speed internal intensifier bar (stirring system) can be installed inside the V-cylinder to break up agglomerates and accelerate mixing efficiency.

**Q: How is the equipment packaged and secured for international ocean freight?**  
**A:** All export equipment is cleaned, wrapped in protective moisture-proof film, and enclosed inside solid, export-grade fumigation-free plywood cases. Standard operation tools and critical spare parts are packed together with the machine to guarantee arrival integrity.

**Q: What after-sales service and technical support are provided post-purchase?**

Furthermore, lifetime remote engineering guidance, video operation support, and factory-direct spare parts supply are maintained for the entire lifecycle of the machine.
