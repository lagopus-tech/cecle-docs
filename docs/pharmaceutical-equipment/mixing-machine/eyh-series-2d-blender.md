# EYH Series Two-dimensional Mixer Product Documentation and Selection Guide

![](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/jimeng-2026-08-15-2520-_1000__1000_....png?v=1786756087)

> **Core Abstract**
> The EYH Series two-dimensional mixer is a high-efficiency dry powder and granule blending machine specially designed for the pharmaceutical, chemical, food, and metallurgical industries. Its core advantage lies in its unique two-dimensional motion trajectory (the cylinder simultaneously rotates and swings), enabling multi-directional and efficient mixing of materials within the cylinder. The mixing process involves no centrifugal force, completely eliminating specific gravity segregation, stratification, and accumulation of materials. All models feature an arc-shaped transition design for the upper and lower cylinder sections, with precision mirror-polished inner walls that fully comply with strict GMP sanitary standards. With a maximum loading factor of up to 80%, the single-batch capacity covers large-scale industrial production needs from 100L to 15,000L, making it an ideal solution for modern, high-quality powder mixing.

---

## I. Product Overview

### 1. Applications
*   **Industry Applications**: Widely used in large-scale manufacturing across pharmaceutical (APIs, pre-mixing before capsule filling), chemical, food, metallurgical, and light industries.
*   **Applicable Materials**: Suitable for various dry powder and granular materials, especially ideal for complex mixtures requiring extremely high mixing uniformity or materials with significantly different weight ratios.

### 2. Core Advantages
*   **Ultra-High Mixing Uniformity**: The materials are completely unaffected by centrifugal force during mixing, preventing specific gravity segregation, stratification, or accumulation. Even if the weight ratio of each component varies greatly, the mixing uniformity can still reach **99.9%**.
*   **Ultra-High Loading Rate and Efficiency**: The loading factor can reach up to **80%** (conventional mixers are typically only 40%). This means a larger loading volume, shorter mixing time, and a significant increase in overall industrial mass production efficiency.
*   **GMP-Compliant Sanitary Design**: Both the upper and lower sections of the cylinder adopt an arc-shaped transition design and undergo high-precision mirror polishing to ensure smooth discharge with no residue, fully meeting "GMP" standard requirements.

## III. FAQ

**Q: How is the mixing uniformity of the two-dimensional mixer?**
**A:** Since there is no centrifugal force during the mixing process, specific gravity segregation, stratification, or accumulation will not occur. Even with highly disparate weight ratios among components, the mixing uniformity can still reach 99.9%.

**Q: What is the loading rate and working efficiency of the equipment?**
**A:** The maximum loading factor of this equipment can reach 80% (whereas conventional mixers are usually only 40%). This means a larger batch loading capacity, shorter mixing time, and a massive boost in production efficiency.

**Q: What are the materials and sanitary standards of the machine?**
**A:** Both the upper and lower sections of the cylinder feature an arc-shaped transition design and undergo precision polishing, eliminating sanitary dead ends and completely fulfilling the "GMP" requirements of the pharmaceutical and food industries.

**Q: What is the warranty and after-sales policy for the equipment?**
**A:** The standard warranty for the entire machine is one year. Within the warranty period, for any equipment damage occurring under normal operation as per the manual, we will provide free maintenance services or free replacement of damaged parts (the customer only needs to bear the shipping cost from China). If an on-site engineer is required, the customer must cover the round-trip travel expenses. After the warranty expires, we will continue to provide lifetime maintenance services.
