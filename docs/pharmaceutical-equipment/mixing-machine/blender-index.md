# Complete Selection Guide and Product Overview for Industrial Powder Mixing Equipment

> **Core Abstract**
> This guide provides a complete selection strategy and product overview for industrial powder mixing equipment, covering our full range of machinery from laboratory R&D blenders to high-capacity industrial mass-production mixers. Laboratory and small-scale mixers (such as the VM series) feature compact structures and gentle diffusion action, making them ideal for laboratory R&D, pharmaceutical pilot trials, and small-batch prototyping. Industrial batch and continuous mixers (such as the WLDH horizontal ribbon, EYH two-dimensional, SYH three-dimensional, and V-series double-arm mixers) are designed for scaled production in the pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, chemical, and food industries, offering high capacity, rapid blending, and exceptional uniformity. Whether you are a startup or a large-scale factory requiring GMP compliance, you will find the optimal powder blending solution here to match your capacity needs, material characteristics, liquid addition requirements, and investment budget.

## I. Mixing Equipment Overview: How to Choose Between the Major Types?

### 1. Core Selection Logic

The core basis for selecting an industrial mixer relies on **production scale, capacity requirements, material flowability, density disparity, and liquid addition needs**:

| Decision Dimension           | Choose Laboratory / Small-Scale Mixers                       | Choose Industrial Mass Production Mixers                     |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Production Scale**         | Lab R&D prototyping, small-scale pilot batches, personal DIY | Scaled, automated continuous production, industrial mass manufacturing |
| **Capacity Needs**           | Small batch, multi-variety flexible production               | High capacity, high-efficiency continuous operation          |
| **Material Characteristics** | Fragile granules, expensive APIs, dry powders with good flowability | Extreme density differentials (1:10000), low-viscosity liquid spraying needs, bulk powders |
| **Investment Budget**        | Limited budget, small footprint                              | Pursuing scaled production, high throughput, and GMP compliance |
| **Typical Scenarios**        | Startup launch, laboratory R&D, pharmacy custom mixing       | Food factories, chemical processing plants, large pharmaceutical powder lines |

### 2. One-Sentence Decision Mantra

> * **"Small batch, multi-variety, R&D trial, fragile granules, limited budget"** → Choose **V-Type Single-Arm Mixer (VM Series)**
> * **"Scaled production, high capacity, liquid addition, extreme density difference, high uniformity"** → Choose **Horizontal Ribbon, 2D, 3D, or Double-Arm Industrial Mixers**

## II. Laboratory and Small-Scale Mixing Series (V-Type Single-Arm VM Series)

### 1. What is a V-Type Single-Arm Mixer?

A V-type single-arm mixer is a compact blending machine featuring an asymmetrical V-shaped mixing cylinder supported by a single cantilever arm. Driven smoothly by its internal mechanical transmission, it relies on gravity-induced diffusion to blend dry powders and granular materials without causing mechanical degradation or particle breakage. Due to its small footprint, ease of cleaning, and gentle mixing action, it is widely used in pharmaceutical research, chemical laboratories, and small-scale custom manufacturing.

| ------------- | --------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **VM-2**      | 2 L             | 1.2 kg               | 0.8 - 1.0 L                | Smallest footprint in the series, ideal for micro-scale R&D and precious API sample preparation. |
| **VM-5**      | 5 L             | 3.0 kg               | 2.0 - 2.5 L                | Standard laboratory testing model for pilot formulation trials. |
| **VM-10**     | 10 L            | 6.0 kg               | 4.0 - 5.0 L                | Enhanced capacity for small batch customization and clinical trial preparation. |
| **VM-20**     | 20 L            | 12.0 kg              | 8.0 - 10.0 L               | **Highest capacity in the single-arm series**, bridging laboratory R&D and small-scale production. |

> 💡 The working volume ratio of a V-type mixer typically ranges from 40% to 60% of total volume to allow optimal free-fall particle diffusion space.

### 3. Core Technical Advantages

* **Gentle Diffusion Mixing**: The asymmetrical V-shape prevents high shear forces, protecting delicate granules and fragile crystal structures from physical damage.

* **Cantilever Design & Stability**: The single-arm structural support ensures unobstructed loading/unloading access while maintaining low operational vibration and noise.

* **Easy Sanitization**: Smooth internal stainless steel walls eliminate dead corners, permitting rapid cleaning between different material batches.

* **Low Energy Consumption**: Simple mechanical transmission requires minimal electrical power, reducing operating costs for research facilities.

### 3. Detailed Core Features of Each Industrial Series

#### V-Type Double-Arm Mixer (V Series)

* **Positioning**: Medium to large-scale dry powder and granule blending.

* **Core Features**: Dual reinforced side-column supports provide exceptional mechanical stability under heavy loads. Reaches a high loading rate of 80% while maintaining a mixing uniformity of up to 99.9% without crushing particles. Ideal for standard pharmaceutical and food production lines.

#### WLDH Horizontal Ribbon Mixer (WLDH Series)

* **Positioning**: Heavy-duty industrial mixing with liquid addition and extreme density variance capabilities.

* **Core Features**: Features a dual-helical ribbon agitator where outer ribbons push material inward and inner ribbons push material outward, creating counter-current convection. Capable of blending materials with density ratios up to 1:10,000 and supporting low-viscosity liquid spraying (e.g., oil or water additives). Blending cycles are exceptionally fast, typically completing within 1 to 10 minutes with minimal residual product.

#### EYH Two-Dimensional Mixer (EYH Series)

* **Positioning**: Ultra-large capacity mass production for complex multi-component formulations.

* **Core Features**: The mixing cylinder undergoes simultaneous rocking and rotating movements in two directions, preventing centrifugal segregation and completely eliminating stratification and particle accumulation. Features arc-shaped internal transitions polished to mirror standards, fully complying with GMP guidelines. Capacities scale up to 15,000 liters for massive industrial outputs.

#### SYH Three-Dimensional Motion Mixer (SYH Series)

* **Positioning**: High-precision multi-axis blending for ultra-demanding homogeneity requirements.

* **Core Features**: Utilizes a unique three-dimensional spatial motion combining translation, rotation, and inversion. Generates pulsing alternating rhythms that effortlessly blend powders with extreme weight disparities, achieving 99.9% uniformity without dead zones.

## IV. Production Line Combination Solutions (From Powder to Finished Tablet)

### 1. Micro Pharmaceutical R&D Line Solution (Paired with Single-Punch Presses)

To help research laboratories and startups establish a complete closed loop from raw powder blending to final solid dosage packaging, we recommend integrating mixing units with tableting and packaging systems:

> **Golden Combination: VM-Series Mixer + TDP Tablet Press + Small Blister Packing Machine**
> 1. **Front-End Blending**: Use the VM-5 or VM-10 V-type mixer to achieve uniform powder mixing without material degradation.
> 2. **Core Pressing**: Transfer the blended powder directly to a TDP-5 or TDP-6 tablet press for precision shaping.
> 3. **Back-End Packaging**: Dock with a compact blister packing machine to seal the finished tablets in protective aluminum-plastic packaging.
>
> **Solution Value**: The entire micro-production line occupies a minimal footprint under 5 square meters, offering low capital expenditure and high operational flexibility.

### 2. Fully Automatic Industrial Line Solution (Paired with Rotary Systems)

For high-throughput industrial facilities requiring continuous mass production, mixing systems integrate seamlessly into fully enclosed automated processing lines:

> **Vacuum Feeder ➔ WLDH / 3D Industrial Mixer ➔ ZP-Series Rotary Tablet Press ➔ Deduster ➔ Polisher ➔ Metal Detector ➔ Blister Packing Machine**
> * **Enclosed Feeding**: A vacuum feeder draws raw ingredients into the mixer hopper without dust emission.
> * **High-Efficiency Blending**: The WLDH horizontal ribbon mixer or SYH 3D mixer ensures uniform bulk formulation.
> * **Continuous Tableting**: Transferred to a ZP27 high-speed rotary tablet press for high-capacity molding (up to 97,200 pcs/h).
> * **Quality Control & Packaging**: Processed through automated dedusters, polishers, and metal detectors before final blister sealing.
>
> **Suitable Scenarios**: Large-batch GMP cleanrooms for pharmaceutical and nutraceutical manufacturers.
