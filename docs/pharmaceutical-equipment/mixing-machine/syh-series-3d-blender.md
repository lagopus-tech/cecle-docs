# SYH Series Three-dimensional Mixer Product Documentation and Selection Guide

> **Core Abstract**
> The SYH Series Three-dimensional Mixer is highly efficient multi-directional powder and granule mixing equipment, specially designed for high-standard production lines in the pharmaceutical, chemical, and food industries. Its core advantage lies in its unique three-dimensional movement trajectory of rotation and revolution, allowing materials to achieve a mixing uniformity of up to 99.9% without the influence of centrifugal force, completely eliminating the phenomenon of specific gravity segregation. The inner walls of all equipment in this series are precision-polished to meet GMP standards, and the loading coefficient can reach up to 80%. With a capacity ranging from 1L for laboratory use to 1500L for industrial mass production, it is an excellent solution for modern mixing processes.

---

## I. Product Overview

### 1. Applications
*   **Industry Applications**: Widely used in pharmaceutical (APIs, Chinese medicine powder), chemical, food (protein powder, coffee powder), metallurgical, light industry, and scientific research institutions.
*   **Applicable Materials**: Various dry powder and granular materials, especially suitable for materials with a **significant difference in weight ratio** or those **requiring extremely high mixing uniformity**.

### 2. Core Advantages
*   **Ultimate Mixing Uniformity**: Materials are not affected by centrifugal force during mixing, resulting in no specific gravity segregation, stratification, or accumulation, and the mixing uniformity reaches **99.9%**.
*   **Ultra-High Working Efficiency**: The loading coefficient can reach up to **80%** (compared to only 40% for ordinary mixers), which means higher output and shorter mixing time under the same volume.
*   **No Dead Corner Sanitary Design**: Both the upper and lower cylinders adopt an arc-shaped transition design and are precisely polished. The discharging is clean with no residue, fully meeting "GMP" standard requirements.

## III. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How is the mixing uniformity of the three-dimensional mixer?**
**A:** Since there is no centrifugal force acting on the materials during the mixing process, specific gravity segregation, stratification, or accumulation will not occur. Even if the weight ratio of each component varies greatly, the mixing uniformity can still reach 99.9%.

**Q: What is the loading rate and working efficiency of the equipment?**
**A:** The maximum loading coefficient of this equipment can reach 80% (while ordinary mixers are usually only 40%), which means higher output, shorter mixing time, and significantly improved efficiency under the same volume.

**Q: What is the packaging standard for international export orders?**
**A:** All export equipment is uniformly packed in export-specific fumigation-free plywood cases. Standard spare parts and special tools required for machine operation will be included in the packaging.

**Q: What is the warranty and after-sales policy of the equipment?**
**A:** The standard warranty period for the whole machine is one year. During the warranty period, as long as any equipment damage occurs within the normal operation scope of the manual, we will provide free repair services or free replacement of damaged parts (the customer only needs to bear the shipping cost from China). After the warranty period expires, we will continue to provide lifelong maintenance services.
