## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The ZKS Series Electric Vacuum Feeder uses a high-efficiency motor to generate high vacuum levels, drawing materials from silos or containers via negative pressure suction to the target equipment. Its core advantages include:

* **Powerful & Stable Conveying**: Employs an industrial-grade high-quality vortex air pump that delivers continuous, stable, and powerful vacuum suction. Compared to pneumatic equipment, it performs exceptionally well when handling high-density materials, long-distance conveying, and high-lift elevation.

* **High Adaptability & Independent Operation**: Can operate independently without the need for a large factory air compressor, solving the pain point of insufficient or unstable compressed air supply in some workshops. Simply connect to power to achieve efficient feeding.

* **Dust-Free & GMP Compliant**: The entire system operates under sealed vacuum conditions, completely eliminating dust leakage and environmental contamination. Both the inner and outer walls of the equipment undergo precision polishing treatment with no hygiene dead corners, fully complying with GMP standards in pharmaceutical, food, and other industries.

* **Smart Control & Easy Cleaning**: Equipped with an automated control panel that allows precise setting of suction and discharge timing. The equipment features quick-connect clamp fittings, making disassembly, assembly, and routine cleaning and maintenance extremely convenient.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is the difference between the ZKS Electric Vacuum Feeder and the QVC Pneumatic type? How should I choose?**
**A:** The main difference lies in the power source. The QVC series relies on the factory's compressed air (pneumatic) to generate vacuum, featuring a lighter structure, and is suitable for environments with extremely high explosion-proof requirements and small to medium-volume material conveying. The ZKS series uses a high-frequency motor (electric) as its power source, providing greater suction power and longer conveying distances, making it suitable for workshops lacking high-volume air sources or scenarios requiring large-batch and heavy-material continuous conveying.

**Q: What types of materials is the ZKS series suitable for conveying?**
**A:** It is very suitable for conveying various powders (such as pharmaceutical powder, flour, chemical powders), granular materials (such as capsules, tablets, plastic pellets), and powder-granule mixtures. The powerful negative pressure suction can easily handle high-density and bridge-prone materials, and it is widely used in pharmaceutical, food processing, and chemical production lines.

**Q: Does the equipment's filter clog easily? Is cleaning convenient?**
**A:** The equipment is equipped with an efficient pulse reverse-blow system that automatically performs reverse pulse cleaning of the filter during each discharge cycle, effectively preventing dust blockage and ensuring stable vacuum levels during continuous operation. The entire machine features a quick-disconnect clamp design, making manual disassembly and cleaning of the filter element and chamber extremely convenient and fast.
