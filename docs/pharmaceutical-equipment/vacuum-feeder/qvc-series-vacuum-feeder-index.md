# QVC Series Pneumatic Vacuum Feeder — Solid Dosage Production Line Integration Application & Selection Guide

The QVC Series Pneumatic Vacuum Feeder effectively addresses the pain points of dust dispersion, material damage, and low manual labor efficiency in solid dosage production lines through its enclosed pneumatic vacuum flexible conveying technology. Integrating the vacuum feeding system with fully automatic capsule filling machines, mixers, rotary tablet presses, and back-end counting and packaging lines (such as SPN/JF/YL Series), the core selection logic lies in **seamless upstream and downstream capacity matching, material characteristics (such as fragility), and cleanroom/space constraints in the workshop**.

---

## I. Core Equipment Application Scenarios & Selection Logic

When customers procure vacuum feeders for different main equipment, there are significant differences in key consideration criteria and market logic:

### 1. Fully Automatic Capsule Filling Machine (Empty Capsule & Powder Feeding)

* **Market Pain Points**: Empty capsules are extremely susceptible to being crushed or scratched by traditional mechanical gripping methods; powder loading processes are prone to dust contamination.
* **Selection Logic**: The QVC Series features strong yet gentle suction (Strong & Gentle Conveying). By adjusting the vacuum level, non-destructive conveying can be achieved, completely avoiding mechanical damage to fragile empty capsules. The enclosed system only comes into contact with the vacuum suction cup, meeting the requirements of high-standard cleanroom environments. Typically, the **QVC-2 or QVC-3** is selected to match the continuous throughput requirements of medium-to-large capsule filling machines.

### 2. Mixer (Front-End Bulk Powder Feeding)

* **Market Pain Points**: Regardless of whether the front-end is the VM Series single-arm mixer or the WLDH/EYH industrial mixer, large-volume powder feeding is highly prone to severe dust cross-contamination.
* **Selection Logic**: The feeder must possess extremely high conveying capacity and absolute airtightness. The vacuum feeder can rapidly grab and release materials, significantly improving pre-mixing preparation efficiency. For bulk high-efficiency mixing (WLDH/EYH mixers), customers will inevitably procure the **QVC-5 (6,000 kg/h) or QVC-6 (9,000 kg/h)** to meet the enormous industrial-grade throughput requirements.

### 3. Rotary Tablet Press (Continuous Uninterrupted Feeding)

* **Market Pain Points**: The tablet press hopper is positioned at a considerable height. Traditional manual feeding not only involves high labor intensity but also makes it difficult to maintain a constant powder level inside the hopper, affecting tablet weight variation.
* **Selection Logic**: The QVC Series is completely unaffected by gravity, capable of lifting materials to a specific height at the tablet press hopper before horizontal or downward transfer. Its extremely fast response time ensures that the high-speed rotary tablet press does not experience material shortages during continuous tablet output. Medium-to-large tablet presses typically match the **QVC-3 or QVC-4 (1,500 - 3,000 kg/h)**.

## III. Market Sales & Technical Engagement Strategy Recommendations

When presenting to customers at different tiers, the QVC Vacuum Feeder should be positioned as an essential accessory for "improving the yield rate and compliance of main equipment," rather than an independent material handling tool:

1. **For Pharmaceutical/Food Factories, Emphasize "Non-Destructive & Dust-Free"**: Highlight its enclosed operation and purely pneumatic design, which effectively eliminates dust dispersion and cross-contamination, fully meeting the stringent hygiene standards of the food and pharmaceutical industries.

2. **For Engineering Procurement Portals, Emphasize "High Integration"**: The full range from QVC-1 to QVC-6 covers capacities from 180 kg/h to 9,000 kg/h. Equipment is shipped with sturdy plywood cases and standard installation tools. During the warranty period, free parts replacement is provided for non-human-caused damages (customer bears shipping costs), significantly reducing integrators' after-sales concerns and maintenance costs.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What types of materials is the QVC Series Vacuum Feeder suitable for conveying?**
**A:** The QVC Series features powerful suction capability, enabling it to grasp materials of various shapes and sizes. It is particularly suitable for fragile and irregularly shaped materials. The vacuum suction process is very gentle, causing absolutely no mechanical damage to materials. It is widely applied in the food, electronics, pharmaceutical, and automotive industries.

**Q: What is the conveying capacity of this equipment? Can it meet large-scale production demands?**
**A:** The series offers a complete range of models (from QVC-1 to QVC-6), with conveying capacities covering 180 kg/h to 9,000 kg/h. The extremely fast response speed significantly improves production efficiency, fully meeting the operational requirements of everything from small-scale trials to industrial mass production.

**Q: Is the vacuum feeder easy to clean and maintain?**
**A:** Very convenient. The equipment features an extremely simple and reliable structure, making it easy to operate and maintain on a daily basis. During the conveying process, materials are in an enclosed state and only come into contact with the vacuum suction cup and related components, ensuring an absolutely clean and hygienic production process and effectively preventing dust and cross-contamination.
