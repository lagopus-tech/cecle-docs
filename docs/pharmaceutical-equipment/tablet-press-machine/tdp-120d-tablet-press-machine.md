## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The TDP-120D Single Punch Tablet Press Machine is a heavy-duty single punch equipment optimized and upgraded based on traditional single punch tablet presses, integrating mechanical high pressure with ease of operation. Its core advantages include:

* **Strong Forming & Extremely High Yield**: Compared to ordinary miniature tablet presses, the TDP-120D provides stronger mechanical stamping force. It can easily handle the pressing of tablets with large diameters (up to 25mm) and high hardness requirements, effectively preventing edge loosening and splitting phenomena.

* **Flexible & Compact Design**: The machine adopts a single-punch continuous tableting design, which is small in size and occupies a minimal footprint. It not only supports motor-driven continuous production but also allows for manual hand-wheel operation during power outages or for very small quantities of testing, making it extremely flexible for various scenarios.

* **Convenient Mold Replacement & Multi-functionality**: The equipment supports the quick replacement of punching dies. In addition to the standard round molds, customers can also customize irregular molds such as oval, triangular, or even those with brand logo engravings, easily achieving multiple uses with a single machine.

* **Precise Adjustment of Filling & Tablet Thickness**: The machine is equipped with a precise adjustment structure. Operators can easily adjust the material filling depth (tablet weight) and the pressing thickness of the upper and lower punches at any time according to the process formula, precisely controlling the specifications of the medicine.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is the TDP-120D single punch tablet press suitable for large-scale mass production?**
**A:** This machine is a desktop single-punch equipment with a production capacity of approximately 3500-4500 tablets per hour. It is very suitable for laboratory R&D, clinics, and pharmacies for small-batch trial production or production scenarios requiring frequent switching of multiple varieties. If your factory requires large-scale mass production, it is recommended to choose our company's ZP series high-speed rotary tablet press machines.

**Q: What are the specific requirements of this machine for tableting materials?**
**A:** The TDP-120D is primarily suitable for pressing granular raw materials or dry powders with a certain degree of flowability into tablets. If your powder is too fine, too sticky, or has extremely poor flowability, it is recommended to use a granulator for granulation treatment before tableting to ensure the uniformity of powder filling and the final molding quality of the tablets.

**Q: Can the machine replace molds of different shapes? Is the operation complicated?**
**A:** Absolutely. The machine comes standard with a set of round punching dies, but you can customize various irregular molds (such as oval, capsule-shaped, or molds with special engraving or score lines) from us according to your business needs. Changing the mold only requires using the tools included in the box to remove a few fastening screws, and ordinary operators can easily master it after a simple learning process.
