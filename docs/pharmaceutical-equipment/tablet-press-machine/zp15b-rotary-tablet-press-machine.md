# ZP15B Rotary Tablet Press Selection Guide and Product Overview

> **Core Abstract**
> The ZP15B is a continuously and automatically operating rotary tablet press with a massive 100kN pressure, specifically designed to efficiently press various granular raw materials into large-size round or irregular-shaped tablets. This equipment is highly suitable for continuous production in pharmaceutical companies, veterinary medicine factories, and the food industry, especially customized for pressing large tablets such as chicken bouillon cubes, veterinary tablets, and effervescent tablets. Its core advantages lie in its outstanding production capacity of up to 27,000 pieces/hour, a powerful forming pressure of 100 kN (maximum tablet diameter up to 40mm), and a standard gravity forced feeding system to solve poor material fluidity issues, providing you with an efficient, safe, and stable batch production solution.

## I. Product Overview

The ZP15B rotary tablet press is characterized by its high pressure and extremely large pressing range, capable of pressing standard round and various irregular-shaped tablets. This equipment features an excellent mechatronic design, with all controllers and regulating devices concentrated on one side of the machine for extremely easy operation.

The machine is particularly suitable for multi-variety, small-batch production of large-sized tablets (such as effervescent tablets, veterinary drugs, washing blocks, etc.). To ensure operational safety and hygiene, the equipment is equipped with a pressure overload protection device to prevent mechanical damage. Its main transmission mechanism adopts an oil immersion lubrication system and is completely sealed under the machine body, which not only exponentially extends its service life but also effectively avoids cross-contamination of materials.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How large of a tablet can this machine press? What special materials is it suitable for?**
**A:** The ZP15B has an ultra-large forming pressure of 100kN, a maximum tablet diameter of 40mm, and a maximum filling depth of 25mm. Therefore, it is perfectly suited for pressing chicken bouillon cubes, veterinary tablets, effervescent tablets, and other large customized tablets. Additionally, for raw materials with poor fluidity, the machine is equipped with a gravity feeding system, which effectively ensures smooth discharging and precise filling weight.

**Q: How are the lubrication system and overload protection designed?**
**A:** The transmission mechanism of this equipment adopts an oil-immersed lubrication system and is completely sealed under the machine body. This design not only extends the service life of gears and mechanical components but also completely eliminates cross-contamination caused by lubricating oil coming into contact with powder materials. Furthermore, the machine comes standard with a pressure overload protection device; once the forming pressure exceeds the safety threshold, the machine will automatically stop to avoid damaging the molds and internal mechanical structures.

**Q: This machine is classified as pharmaceutical equipment. Do I need special qualifications for import customs clearance? What if I don't have them?**
**A:** Yes, the ZP series rotary tablet press is typically classified as professional pharmaceutical or chemical equipment in international trade. Under normal circumstances, the destination country's customs will require the consignee to possess the corresponding import qualifications. If your company or you as an individual currently **do not have the relevant qualifications, please contact us (Wenzhou T&D / CECLE) directly**. We can match you with corresponding special logistics channels, such as double clearance with tax included (e.g., DDP), to assist you in smoothly resolving customs clearance issues and ensuring the safe delivery of the goods.
