## III. Purchasing Process and International Logistics Customs Clearance Notice (FAQ & Shipping)

In foreign trade equipment procurement and cross-border transportation, customs clearance and logistics timeliness are the most concerning issues for customers. Relying on our rich export experience, we provide you with the following guarantees and answers to frequently asked questions:

- **Q: How do you ensure the machine's voltage matches our local power supply?**
  - **A:** Industrial and civil voltages vary across different countries and regions. Usually, the US market defaults to a single-phase 110V voltage; while the UK, Australia, and EU regions use a single-phase 220V voltage. We will strictly configure the corresponding motor according to the requirements of your country before the machine leaves the factory.

- **Q: What is your strategy for customs declaration to avoid clearance delays?**
  - **A:** Since customs in many countries (especially US Customs) strictly review equipment containing the words "pills, tablets", which easily causes customs clearance difficulties and extended delivery times, we uniformly declare the customs product name as "mini punching machine". This strategy can effectively promote smooth customs clearance, speed up the process, and ensure the equipment arrives at the destination as soon as possible.

- **Q: Can you help optimize import tariffs, especially for the US market?**
  - **A:** Yes. Considering that the US market will incur an import tariff of about 25% for such equipment, we can assist in reasonably optimizing customs declaration documents, thereby saving you considerable customs clearance tax costs.

- **Q: How is the machine packaged for international shipping, and are spare parts included?**
  - **A:** All equipment is equipped with a full set of spare parts and dedicated tools before leaving the factory. The machines are uniformly packaged in sturdy wooden cases to ensure they are protected from collision damage during long-distance ocean shipping or international express.
