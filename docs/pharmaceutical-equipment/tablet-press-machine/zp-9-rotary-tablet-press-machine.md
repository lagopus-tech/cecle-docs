## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is the number of molds installed on the machine turret fixed?**
**A:** No, it is not fixed. The actual number of molds installed on a rotary tablet press mainly depends on the size of your customized tablets (i.e., punches and dies). Simply put: **the larger the customized mold size, the fewer the number of molds that can be accommodated on the turret; conversely, the smaller the mold size, the more molds can be installed**. The "9 sets" in the technical parameter table is a standard configuration reference for this model at regular medium sizes.

**Q: This machine is classified as pharmaceutical equipment. Do I need special qualifications for import customs clearance? What if I don't have them?**
**A:** Yes, the ZP series rotary tablet press is classified as professional pharmaceutical equipment in international trade. Under normal circumstances, the destination country's customs will require the consignee to have corresponding import qualifications for medical or pharmaceutical equipment. If your company or you as an individual currently **do not possess the relevant import qualifications, please contact our sales representatives directly**. We have extensive experience in cross-border transportation of large machinery and can match you with and provide corresponding special logistics channels, such as double clearance with tax included (e.g., DDP), to help you smoothly solve customs clearance difficulties and ensure the goods are safely delivered to your door.
