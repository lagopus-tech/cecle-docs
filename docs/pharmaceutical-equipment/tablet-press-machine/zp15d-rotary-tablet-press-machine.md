> **Core Abstract**
> The ZP17 is a continuously operating rotary tablet press with 60kN forming pressure, specifically designed to efficiently compress various granular raw materials into round or irregularly shaped tablets. This equipment is ideally suited for multi-variety, small-batch production scenarios. Its core advantages include an outstanding production capacity of up to 30,600 tablets/hour and excellent material adaptability, with a standard gravity feeding system that effectively addresses the issue of poor raw material flowability. The entire transmission system features bottom-sealed lubrication, ensuring high-standard production hygiene requirements, making it an ideal choice for small and medium-sized enterprises in the pharmaceutical, food, and chemical industries for efficient tablet production.

## I. Product Overview

The ZP17 rotary tablet press is characterized by its high pressure and wide compression range, capable of easily handling the compression of both standard round and various irregularly shaped tablets. The equipment excels in mechatronic design, with all controllers and adjustment devices concentrated on one side of the machine body, making operation extremely simple and intuitive.

This model is particularly suitable for multi-variety, small-batch production tasks, and supports customized compression of various tablet products such as chicken bouillon cubes, veterinary tablets, and effervescent tablets. To ensure operational safety and hygiene, the ZP17 is equipped with an over-pressure protection device to effectively prevent mechanical damage. Additionally, its main transmission mechanism uses oil-immersed lubrication and is fully sealed beneath the machine body, which not only significantly extends service life but also fundamentally prevents cross-contamination of materials.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What does the model number (e.g., ZP17) of this machine mean? Is the number of mold installations fixed?**
**A:** The model number of the machine (e.g., ZP17) primarily relates to the standard number of mold installations on the turntable (i.e., 17 sets of molds as standard). However, it is important to emphasize that **the actual number of mold installations is not absolutely fixed — it directly depends on the size of your customized tablet (punch die)**: the larger the customized mold size, the fewer installations the turntable can accommodate; conversely, the smaller the mold size, the more installations are possible.

**Q: How large of a tablet can this machine press? What special materials can it be used for?**
**A:** The ZP17 has a forming pressure of 60kN, with a maximum tablet diameter of 20mm and a maximum filling depth of 15mm. It can not only press standard round and irregularly shaped tablets but can also be customized to press large tablet products such as chicken bouillon cubes, veterinary tablets, and effervescent tablets according to customer requirements. Additionally, for raw materials with poor flowability, the machine is equipped with a gravity feeding system that effectively resolves issues of poor material discharge and uneven filling weight.

**Q: How are the lubrication system and overload protection designed?**
**A:** The transmission mechanism of this equipment uses an oil-immersed lubrication system that is fully sealed beneath the machine body. This design not only significantly extends the service life of gears and mechanical components but also completely eliminates cross-contamination caused by lubricating oil coming into contact with powdered materials. Furthermore, the machine is equipped with a standard over-pressure protection device that automatically shuts down the machine once the forming pressure exceeds the safety threshold, protecting the machine from damage.
