# Selection Guide and Product Overview for CQZ Series Pill Making Machine

The CQZ Series Pill Making Machine is a highly efficient automated pill forming equipment specifically designed for Chinese medicine pharmacies, clinics, laboratories, and small-to-medium health food factories. It can continuously extrude mixed Chinese medicine powder and binders into strips, automatically cut them, and roll them into uniform-sized honey pills, water pills, or concentrated pills. As a premium forming equipment from Wenzhou Tongda Packaging Machinery Factory (CECLE), its core advantages lie in great production flexibility (supporting 3-12mm pill diameter customization), a wide capacity range (up to 80KG/hour), and premium stainless steel construction for all material contact parts. The entire machine has a compact structure, is easy to clean, and fully complies with strict GMP hygiene standards, making it the ideal core equipment for realizing the modern and standardized production of traditional Chinese medicine preparations.

## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The CQZ series pill making machine integrates strip extrusion, cutting, and rolling into one unit, greatly reducing the labor intensity of manual pill making and improving the yield and roundness of the pills. Its core advantages include:

*   **Multi-Dosage Form Adaptability**: It can easily produce water pills, honey pills, water-honey pills, concentrated pills, and various health food pills. By simply adjusting the moisture and viscosity of the medicinal dough, it can adapt to different production formulas.
*   **Diverse & Customizable Molds**: It supports various pill sizes from 3mm to 12mm. Customers can customize and quickly replace the extrusion nozzles and pill rolling cutter rollers according to actual needs, achieving multiple specifications with one machine and flexible production.
*   **Variable Frequency Speed Regulation & High Yield**: Certain advanced models (such as the CQZ-80G dual-speed version) are equipped with a variable frequency speed regulation system, allowing independent adjustment of the extrusion speed and pill rolling speed. This enables the machine to precisely match the characteristics of different medicinal materials, significantly improving the roundness and qualification rate of the pills.
*   **Full Stainless Steel & GMP Compliance**: The equipment casing and all parts in direct contact with drugs (such as the hopper, propulsion screw, extrusion nozzle, pill rolling cutter roller, etc.) are made of food-grade stainless steel. They are rust-proof, pollution-free, and fully comply with national GMP specifications.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What types of pills can the CQZ series pill making machine produce?**
**A:** This series of machines is mainly used for making Chinese medicine water pills, honey pills, water-honey pills, and concentrated pills. In actual production, you only need to adjust the ratio of medicinal powder to the binder (such as honey or water), control the moisture of the dough, and use the corresponding extrusion and rolling molds to form the pills.

**Q: What is the core difference between the single-speed and dual-speed (variable frequency) models? How should I choose?**
**A:** For the single-speed model, the extrusion and rolling speeds are fixed and synchronized, suitable for regular pill making with fixed formulas and moderate viscosity. The dual-speed (variable frequency) model allows independent adjustment of the "extrusion speed" and "cutting and rolling speed". If your formulas vary frequently or the stickiness is difficult to control, the dual-speed model makes it much easier to find the optimal pill-making rhythm, greatly improving the roundness of the pills.

**Q: Can the size of the pills produced by the machine be changed?**
**A:** Yes. A machine usually comes standard with one size of mold (e.g., the commonly used 6mm or 8mm) when leaving the factory. If you later need to produce pills of other sizes (within the 3-12mm range), you simply need to purchase the corresponding size of extrusion nozzles and pill rolling cutter rollers from us and replace them yourself, without needing to buy a whole new machine.
