# Selection Guide and Product Overview for ZP420 Series High Speed Rotary Tablet Press Machine

> **Core Abstract**
> The ZP420 Series High Speed Rotary Tablet Press Machine is a highly efficient, intelligent continuous tableting assembly line equipment designed specifically for the pharmaceutical, health supplement, and chemical industries. It is highly suitable for medium-to-large pharmaceutical factories and health supplement manufacturers for pressing various dry powders and granules into high-quality round or irregular-shaped tablets. Its core advantages lie in its outstanding production throughput (maximum capacity up to 82,000 tablets/hour) and a powerful main pressure of up to 120 KN. The entire machine casing and material contact surfaces are made of premium all-stainless steel, equipped with high-transparency plexiglass windows. It not only operates stably and is easy to clean and maintain but also fully complies with strict GMP hygiene standards, making it an ideal choice for realizing industrial-scale, high-precision tableting.

## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The design of the ZP420 series high-speed rotary tablet press fully integrates the concepts of high pressure, high capacity, and high hygiene standards. Its core advantages include:

*   **Fully Enclosed Stainless Steel Design & GMP Compliance**: The equipment casing features a fully enclosed stainless steel structure, and the internal working table surface is also made of high-quality stainless steel. This design not only maintains a long-lasting surface gloss but also effectively prevents cross-contamination of materials, fully meeting the requirements of GMP production specifications.
*   **Excellent Visibility & Easy Maintenance**: High-transparency Plexiglass windows are equipped all around, making the entire tableting process clearly visible. The side panels can be fully opened, which greatly facilitates internal cleaning and daily mechanical maintenance after shutdown.
*   **Variable Frequency Speed Regulation & Stable Operation**: The electrical control system adopts an advanced variable frequency speed regulation device, making operation extremely simple. During the high-speed tableting process, the turret rotation is smooth, safe, and precise, effectively ensuring the consistency of tablet weight and hardness.
*   **Powerful Pressure & Wide Applicability**: It provides a maximum tableting pressure of 120 KN, easily handling large-diameter (up to 30mm) tablets, deep filling depths, and block-forming materials with excellent molding effects.
