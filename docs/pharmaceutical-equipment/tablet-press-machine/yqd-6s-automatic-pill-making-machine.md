## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The YQD-6S is a modern, multi-functional pill making machine specifically developed to elevate the quality of Chinese medicine preparations. It not only retains the highly efficient rolling capabilities of traditional pill making machines but also incorporates an integrated polishing process. Its core advantages include:

* **Integrated Pill Making & Polishing Design**: It breaks the cumbersome traditional process where pills need to be transferred separately for polishing after being formed. After completing the pill rolling, this machine can directly perform a tumbling and polishing treatment in the polishing tank, making the finished pills look rounder, fuller, and brighter in color, significantly improving the product's market appeal.

* **304 Medical-Grade Stainless Steel Construction**: Safety is the baseline for pharmaceutical equipment. The casing of the YQD-6S and all internal forming molds and hoppers in contact with medicinal materials are made of 304 medical-grade stainless steel. This completely eliminates the hidden danger of rust and fully complies with national GMP hygiene specifications, making the produced pills safe to use.

* **Powerful and Stable Power Output**: The entire machine is equipped with a high-quality 870-watt (870W) motor, which can provide a stable and continuous torque output under conventional 220V voltage. Even hard or viscous medicinal dough can be easily extruded and formed.

* **Compact Body & High Integration**: The machine weighs only 100kg, and its dimensions are controlled at 90×60×70cm. The overall design is compact and exquisite, requiring no excessive workshop area, making it highly suitable for laboratories or clinic operation rooms with strict space requirements.
