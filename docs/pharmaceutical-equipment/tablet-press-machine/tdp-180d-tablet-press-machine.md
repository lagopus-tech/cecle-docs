# TDP-180D Single Punch Tablet Press Machine

![](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0010/4982/1242/files/jimeng-2026-11-07-4888-image_1000__1000_....png?v=1794021003)

The TDP-180D Single Punch Tablet Press Machine is a high-pressure, heavy-duty single punch tableting equipment designed specifically for pressing granular raw materials into shapes. It is widely applicable for trial production of new products and small-batch continuous production in pharmaceutical factories, chemical plants, food factories, hospitals, scientific research institutions, and laboratories. As a heavy-duty tablet press with outstanding load-bearing capacity, its core advantage lies in its tableting pressure of up to 180KN, easily pressing ultra-large round, irregular, annular, and special-shaped tablets with a diameter of up to 70mm and a thickness of up to 25mm. The equipment is equipped with a powerful 5.5KW motor, maintaining a stable capacity of 1800 pcs/hour. The pressure, filling depth, and speed can be flexibly adjusted, making it the ideal core equipment to meet the tableting requirements of Chinese and Western medicine tablets and various special formed products.

## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The TDP-180D single punch tablet press machine has undergone a significant upgrade in load-bearing capacity based on traditional single punch tablet presses, specifically overcoming the tableting challenges of large-sized and hard-to-form materials. Its core advantages include:

* **Ultra-Strong Pressure & Large Tablet Forming Capacity**: The machine provides a tableting pressure of up to 180KN, capable of pressing large tablets with a maximum diameter of 70mm and a thickness of 25mm. Its extremely deep filling capacity (up to 60mm) gives it an irreplaceable advantage in forming special large-capacity products.

* **Flexible & Diverse Mold Adaptability**: It not only supports conventional round tablets but, by replacing them with special molds, can also easily press irregular, annular, and other special-shaped products. One machine for multiple uses, greatly expanding the boundaries of product dosage form development.

* **Excellent Performance & Easy Operation**: The machine features a scientific design, relatively compact size, and reasonable weight distribution (main machine weighs 850kg). Operation is extremely convenient. Core parameters such as tableting pressure, material filling depth, and machine running speed can be steplessly adjusted according to actual process requirements.

* **Wide Industry Adaptability**: It is not only suitable for pressing various Chinese and Western medicine tablets in the pharmaceutical industry but also perfectly matches the pressing requirements of similar products in other industries, such as chemical blocks, large food blocks, and even powder metallurgy, making it highly popular among users.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What size products is the TDP-180D single punch tablet press most suitable for pressing?**
**A:** The load-bearing capacity of this machine is very outstanding. The maximum tablet diameter can reach 70mm, the maximum tablet thickness can reach 25mm, and the maximum filling depth can reach 60mm. It is very suitable for pressing ultra-large round tablets, fertilizer blocks, large food blocks, or annular and irregular pieces that conventional machines cannot handle.

**Q: Can the pressure, tablet thickness, and filling volume of the machine be adjusted?**
**A:** Absolutely. The machine is designed with full consideration for operational flexibility. Its 180KN tableting pressure, material filling depth (which determines tablet weight), and running speed can all be conveniently and precisely adjusted manually according to material characteristics and process requirements.

**Q: Can this machine press shapes other than round?**
**A:** Yes. Although the machine is called a tablet press, by replacing it with your exclusively customized punching molds, it can press out irregular, annular, products with logo engraving, or other special geometric shapes, making it widely applicable in multiple industries such as pharmaceuticals, chemicals, and food.
