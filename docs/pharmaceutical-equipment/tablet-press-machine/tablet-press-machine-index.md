# Complete Selection Guide and Product Overview for Tablet Press Machines

> **Core Abstract**
> This guide provides a complete selection strategy and product overview for tablet press machines, covering our full range of equipment from single-punch to rotary presses. Single-punch tablet presses (such as the TDP series) feature a compact structure and minimal material loss, making them ideal for laboratory R&D, clinics, and small-scale prototyping. Rotary tablet presses (such as the ZP series, including ZP-9, ZP17, etc.) are designed for automated continuous production in the pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, chemical, and food industries, offering high capacity and stability. Whether you are a startup or a large-scale factory requiring GMP compliance, you will find the optimal tablet forming solution here to match your capacity needs, material characteristics, and investment budget.

---

## I. Tablet Press Overview: How to Choose Between the Two Major Types?

### 1. Core Selection Logic

The core basis for selecting a tablet press relies on **production scale, capacity requirements, and product type**:

| Decision Dimension           | Choose Single-Punch Tablet Press                             | Choose Rotary Tablet Press                                   |
| :--------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Production Scale**         | Lab R&D prototyping, small-scale production, clinics/pharmacies, personal DIY | Scaled, automated continuous production, industrial mass production |
| **Capacity Needs**           | Small batch, multi-variety flexible production               | High capacity, single-variety high-efficiency continuous operation |
| **Material Characteristics** | Expensive API (near-zero loss), multi-category trial production | Regular pharmaceutical / food / daily chemical materials     |
| **Investment Budget**        | Limited budget, small footprint                              | Pursuing scaled production and GMP compliance                |
| **Typical Scenarios**        | Startup launch, pharmacy customization, lab R&D prototyping  | Food factories, pharmaceutical plants, daily chemical mass production lines |

### 2. One-Sentence Decision Mantra

> - **"Small batch, multi-variety, R&D trial, limited budget"** → Choose **Single-Punch Tablet Press**
> - **"Scaled production, high capacity, continuous operation, GMP compliant"** → Choose **Rotary Tablet Press**

## II. Single-Punch Tablet Press Series (Suitable for R&D and Small-Scale Production)

### 1. What is a Single-Punch Tablet Press?

A single-punch tablet press is a continuous tablet-pressing machine with a compact structure and intuitive operation. Using a single pair of punches and dies, it precisely presses granular or powder materials into round or irregularly shaped tablets. Due to its exceptionally high cost-effectiveness and small footprint, it is widely used in pharmaceutical pilot scale-up, laboratory R&D prototyping, clinic pharmacies, and small-scale custom nutraceutical production.

### 2. Core Types and Drive Modes

| Drive Type                          | Description                                                  |
| :---------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pure Manual Type**                | Relies entirely on a hand crank for power; simplified structure, can be operated anywhere without electricity. |
| **Pure Electric Type**              | Driven by a motor for automatic continuous feeding and pressing, significantly reducing manual labor and improving production efficiency. |
| **Integrated Manual/Electric Type** | Features a dual power system. Can run fully automatically when powered; in environments without electricity or **during the mold installation and debugging phase**, it smoothly switches to manual mode, greatly enhancing operational safety and flexibility. |

### 3. Core Models Detailed Parameter Selection Table

| Product Model |   Drive Mode   | Max Pressure | Core Features and Positioning                                |
| :------------ | :------------: | :----------: | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **TDP-0**     |  Pure Manual   |  **15 KN**   | Smallest footprint, extremely lightweight, suitable for micro-scale prototyping and personal DIY. |
| **TDP-1**     | Pure Electric  |  **15 KN**   | Basic electrification for automatic feeding and pressing; an automated alternative for laboratories. |
| **TDP-1.5**   | **Integrated** |  **15 KN**   | Classic entry-level model, balancing efficiency and debugging convenience; high cost-effectiveness for small-scale production. |
| **TDHP-5**    | Pure Electric  |  **50 KN**   | Reinforced electric structure providing stronger pressing force, suitable for powders that are harder to form. |
| **TDP-5**     | **Integrated** |  **50 KN**   | Industrial standard pressure, a best-seller in foreign trade, perfectly adapts to most common tablets. |
| **TDP-6**     | **Integrated** |  **60 KN**   | **Highest pressure in the series**, specifically designed for large-sized tablets or high-density, extremely hard-to-press special materials. |

> 💡 The maximum pressure of a tablet press directly determines the hardness of the finished tablet and the range of materials that can be pressed.

### 4. Core Technical Advantages

- **Near-Zero Material Loss**: Ideal for R&D of expensive APIs. Compared to large rotary presses, a single-punch machine requires only a minimal amount of material (just a few grams) for trial pressing, completely eliminating waste.
- **Extremely Easy Mold Replacement**: Supports customization of round, irregular shapes, and punches with brand logos/engravings. A standard operator can complete mold assembly and disassembly within 15 minutes, making it highly suitable for "multi-variety, small-batch" flexible production rhythms.
- **Precisely Adjustable Filling Depth and Pressure**: Users can accurately adjust tablet thickness, hardness, and filling depth via mechanical scales, ensuring every batch meets weight requirements.
- **Low Noise and Low Maintenance Cost**: Transparent and simple mechanical transmission structure with an extremely low failure rate. Daily maintenance requires only basic cleaning and lubrication; no specialized mechanical/electrical engineers are needed.

### 5. Quick Memo for Single-Punch Selection

| Requirement Scenario                                         | Recommended Model                                            |
| :----------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- |

| Focus on mold debugging safety and power outage prevention   | Highly recommend **Integrated Manual/Electric** (1.5 / 5 / 6 series) |

## III. Rotary Tablet Press Series (Suitable for Scaled Mass Production)

### 1. What is a Rotary Tablet Press?

A rotary tablet press is the core equipment for achieving **scaled, automated continuous production** in the pharmaceutical, food, daily chemical, and chemical industries. Through the high-speed rotational movement of multiple sets of dies on a turret, it automatically fills and precisely presses granular or powder materials into tablets. Compared to single-punch presses, rotary models offer immense capacity advantages, superior pressure stability, and a fully enclosed production environment that complies with GMP standards.

### 2. Core Production Directions

| Production Direction                            | Description                                                  |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Standardized Single-Layer Tablet Production** | Primarily for milk tablets, effervescent tablets, vitamin pills, and herbal medicine tablets. Features variable frequency speed control and a precise pressure control system to ensure highly consistent weight, thickness, and hardness for every tablet. |
| **Customized Multi-Layer / Irregular Tablets**  | Specifically designed for two-color/three-color dishwasher tablets, multi-effect laundry pods/blocks, and compound pharmaceutical tablets. Supports independent filling and multiple continuous pressings of various materials in layers, perfectly handling complex, special process requirements. |

- **Dual / Pre-Pressure Design**: Equipped with independent pre-pressure rollers to exhaust air from the material powder before the main pressure forming, effectively preventing tablet capping or lamination, greatly improving the yield rate.
- **High-Precision Dosing Control System**: Utilizes precision filling cams and a forced feeder (optional) to ensure that even materials with poor flowability are uniformly filled, keeping tablet weight variations within an extremely narrow range.
- **Fully Compliant with GMP Hygiene Standards**: Material contact parts are made of high-quality SUS304 / 316L stainless steel. The pressing chamber features a fully enclosed transparent acrylic window design, effectively preventing cross-contamination while being easy to observe and clean without blind spots.
- **Long Lifespan and Extremely Low Noise**: The main transmission system is completely sealed in the oil tank below the machine body, utilizing immersion lubrication. This significantly reduces noise during high-load operations and exponentially extends the lifespan of gears and mechanical components.
- **Standard Pressure Overload Protection**: If the forming pressure exceeds the safety threshold, the machine automatically stops to protect the molds and internal mechanical structures from damage.

> **Golden Combination: TDP Tablet Press + Small Blister Packing Machine**
>
> 1. **Front-End Pressing**: Use the TDP-5 to quickly press mixed powders into standard tablets.

> **Solution Value**: The entire "micro-line" footprint is under 5 square meters, with an extremely low investment cost. Blister packaging effectively protects tablets from moisture and oxidation, significantly enhancing the product's shelf life and commercial appearance.

## VI. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is the standard mold quantity on the machine fixed?**
**A:** No, it is not fixed. The actual number of molds installed on a rotary tablet press primarily depends on the size of the tablets (i.e., punch dies) you are customizing. Simply put: the larger the customized mold size, the fewer the number of molds the turret can accommodate; conversely, the smaller the mold size, the more you can install. The mold quantities listed in the technical parameter tables (such as 9 sets for the ZP-9, 17 sets for the ZP17) are all standard configuration references under standard, medium sizes.

**Q: These machines belong to pharmaceutical equipment. Do I need special qualifications for import customs clearance? What if I don't have them?**
**A:** Yes, the ZP series rotary tablet presses are classified as professional pharmaceutical equipment in international trade. Generally, destination customs will require the consignee to have corresponding import qualifications for medical or pharmaceutical equipment. If you or your company currently do not possess the relevant import qualifications, please contact our sales representatives directly. We have extensive experience in cross-border transportation of heavy machinery and can match and provide corresponding dedicated logistics channels, such as double-clearance tax-inclusive (e.g., DDP) services, to help you smoothly resolve customs clearance issues and ensure the safe delivery of your goods.
