# Selection Guide and Product Overview for CQZ-3/30 Fully Automatic Pill Making Machine

The CQZ-3/30 Fully Automatic Pill Making Machine is an industrial-grade, highly efficient automated pill forming equipment built specifically for the mass production of Chinese medicines and health supplements. It is most suitable for medium-to-large pharmaceutical factories, health food processing plants, and hospital preparation rooms. It can automatically extrude mixed medicinal powder and binders into strips, cut them, and roll them at high speed into round, uniform pills of various types (such as large honey pills, water-honey pills, concentrated pills, etc.). As the flagship mass production model of Wenzhou Tongda Packaging Machinery Factory (CECLE is the registered trademark of this factory), its core advantages lie in its extremely wide pill size coverage (supporting 3-30mm diameter customization) and outstanding continuous mass production capacity (maximum capacity up to 120KG/hour). The entire machine adopts an independent multi-motor drive architecture (total power 4450W), and all material contact parts are made of premium stainless steel, making it sturdy, durable, and fully compliant with strict GMP production standards.

## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The CQZ-3/30 Fully Automatic Pill Making Machine is the core assembly line equipment for transforming traditional Chinese medicine preparations into modern, large-scale production. It perfectly integrates the strip extrusion and pill rolling processes, greatly reducing manual intervention and improving the pill yield. Its core advantages include:

* **Ultra-Wide Specifications & Large Capacity**: It supports the production of everything from tiny 3mm water pills to large 30mm honey pills. With its powerful output, its production capacity reaches 10-120KG/hour, easily meeting the delivery requirements of industrial-grade large-batch orders.
* **Independent Multi-Motor Drive System**: Unlike ordinary single-motor equipment, this model is equipped with independent motors: a speed control motor (0.75KW), an extrusion motor (2.2KW), and a pill making motor (1.5KW). The power for each process does not interfere with the others, and the strong torque can adapt to medicinal materials of various viscosities.
* **Excellent Pill Roundness**: Through variable frequency speed control, operators can precisely match the extrusion speed with the cutting and rolling speed based on the moisture characteristics of the medicinal dough, optimizing the roundness and qualification rate of the finished products.
* **GMP Compliance & Easy-to-Clean Design**: The equipment casing and all direct drug-contact parts—such as the hopper, screw propeller, extrusion nozzle, and cutter roller—are manufactured from food and sanitary-grade stainless steel, ensuring they are anti-corrosion and rust-proof. The scientific structural design of the whole machine makes daily disassembly, cleaning, and maintenance extremely convenient.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How many molds are included with the CQZ-3/30 Fully Automatic Pill Making Machine when it leaves the factory? What if I want to make pills of different sizes?**
**A:** When the machine leaves the factory, it will come **standard with 1 size** of forming mold according to your requirements (choose any size within the 3-30mm range). If you need to make pills of other sizes in subsequent production, you only need to separately purchase the corresponding size of the extrusion nozzle and pill rolling cutter roller assembly from us for replacement, without needing to buy a whole new machine.

**Q: The machine is equipped with three motors (speed control, extrusion, pill making). What are the benefits of this design?**
**A:** The independent multi-motor design is a core hallmark of industrial-grade mass production equipment. The extrusion motor is exclusively responsible for high-intensity extrusion of sticky medicinal materials; the pill making motor is specifically responsible for high-speed cutting and rolling; and the speed control motor is used to coordinate the rhythm of both. This design guarantees extremely strong power output, preventing the machine from jamming due to overly hard or sticky materials, which greatly extends the equipment's lifespan and improves pill quality.

**Q: Is this machine suitable for use in a small clinic?**
**A:** This model weighs 310KG and has a maximum capacity of 120KG/hour, classifying it as medium-to-large production equipment. It is suitable for Chinese medicine factories or large hospital preparation rooms with mass production needs. If you are a small clinic or a small laboratory, we recommend choosing a lightweight model with a smaller footprint and matching capacity (such as the CQZ-80G series).
