This equipment is ideally suited for large-batch, continuous production scenarios in pharmaceutical companies, health product factories, and the chemical industry. Its core advantages include outstanding ultra-high production capacity (up to 97,200 tablets/hour), stable 100kN high pressure operation, and GMP-compliant full stainless steel exterior material with anti-contamination isolation design, providing you with an efficient, safe, and strictly hygienic standardized large-scale tablet production solution.

## I. Product Overview

The ZP27 high-speed rotary tablet press is characterized by its ultra-high pressure, high-speed operation, and excellent stability, capable of easily handling the large-scale compression of both standard round and various irregularly shaped tablets. The equipment features a fully enclosed shell made of premium stainless steel, with internal surfaces specially treated for corrosion resistance, maintaining lasting surface gloss and effectively preventing cross-contamination, fully complying with GMP standards in the pharmaceutical industry.

This model is particularly suitable for large-batch, high-efficiency production tasks. It is equipped with a transparent protective cover, allowing operators to clearly observe the machine's operating status at all times. The side panels can be fully opened for easy internal cleaning and maintenance. To ensure operational safety, the ZP27 is equipped with a sensitive over-pressure protection device that automatically shuts down the machine once the forming pressure exceeds the safety threshold, preventing mechanical damage. Meanwhile, its main transmission system is a fully enclosed independent component housed in an oil tank beneath the machine body. The oil-bath lubrication method provides fast heat dissipation and extreme wear resistance, fundamentally eliminating the risk of lubricating oil contaminating powdered materials.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What does the model number (e.g., ZP27) of this machine mean? Is the number of mold installations fixed?**
**A:** The model number of the machine (e.g., ZP27) primarily relates to the standard number of mold installations on the turntable (i.e., 27 sets of molds as standard). However, it is important to emphasize that **the actual number of mold installations is not absolutely fixed — it directly depends on the size of your customized tablet (punch die)**: the larger the customized mold size, the fewer installations the turntable can accommodate; conversely, the smaller the mold size, the more installations are possible.

**Q: How large of a tablet can this high-speed machine press? What is the actual production capacity?**
**A:** The ZP27 has a powerful forming pressure of 100kN, with a maximum tablet diameter of 25mm and a maximum filling depth of 15mm. It is ideally suited for pressing effervescent tablets, large-sized Chinese and Western medicine tablets, and health product tablets. Thanks to its high-speed rotation and dual-press design (turntable maximum speed up to 30 r/min), its maximum production capacity can reach an impressive 97,200 tablets/hour, making it an ideal choice for large-scale continuous mass production.

**Q: Does the material and transmission lubrication system of this machine comply with pharmaceutical hygiene standards?**
**A:** Fully compliant. The ZP27's exterior is made of fully enclosed stainless steel material, and all components in the tablet pressing chamber that come into contact with materials are treated with anti-corrosion and anti-rust treatment, fully complying with GMP standards. Its core transmission mechanism uses an independent oil-immersed lubrication system that is fully sealed within the oil tank at the bottom of the machine body. This design not only significantly extends the service life of high-load components such as gears but also completely eliminates the risk of lubricating oil splashing into the tablet pressing chamber and causing cross-contamination.
