> **Core Abstract**
> The GZPT Series High Speed Rotary Tablet Press Machine (including models GZPT-51, 65, and 83) is a dual-discharge, dual-pressure high-end fully automatic tablet press equipment designed specifically for large-scale continuous production. It is highly suitable for large pharmaceutical factories, health supplement factories, food, and fine chemical industries, specifically used to efficiently press various powders and granules into round or irregular-shaped tablets. As a representative of cutting-edge pharmaceutical machinery, its core advantage lies in its extreme industrial-grade production capacity (up to 796,800 tablets/hour) and its extremely stable mechanical-grade pressure system (100KN main pressure combined with 16KN pre-pressure). The punches of the equipment are made of high-quality 6CrW2Si alloy tool steel, possessing both extremely high hardness and toughness. The entire machine fully complies with strict GMP production standards, making it the ideal core assembly line equipment for realizing mass-produced, high-precision tableting.

## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The GZPT series high-speed tablet press is a cutting-edge mass production equipment that has been deeply improved and optimized based on classic models. It adopts a dual-side discharge structural design, capable of completing two full tableting cycles in one revolution of the turret, directly doubling the output. Its core advantages include:

* **Dual Discharge & Extreme Capacity**: It adopts a highly efficient dual-pressure and dual-discharge structure, with a maximum rotation speed of up to 80 r/min. Even for the GZPT-51 model with the fewest dies, its maximum capacity reaches 489,600 tablets/hour, while the GZPT-83 approaches 800,000 tablets/hour, completely solving mass production bottlenecks.
* **Dual Guarantee of Pre-pressure & Main Pressure**: The machine is equipped with a stable mechanical-grade pressure system, including a 16 KN pre-pressure and a 100 KN main pressure. The pre-pressure system can effectively expel excess air from the powder, greatly reducing the risk of tablet splitting or loosening under high-speed operation, ensuring excellent hardness and a high yield rate for the finished products.
* **High-Standard Wear-Resistant Alloy Dies**: Both the upper and lower punches are uniformly made of 6CrW2Si alloy tool steel. This material has an excellent balance of hardness and toughness, able to withstand high-frequency, high-intensity tableting impacts for a long time without easy wear, significantly extending the service life of the molds.
* **Intelligent Operation & Protection**: Integrated with high-end electrical control components from internationally renowned brands such as Omron and Weidmuller, the equipment runs smoothly and steadily. It features functions like automatic shutdown protection, ensuring safe operation and extremely stable performance.
