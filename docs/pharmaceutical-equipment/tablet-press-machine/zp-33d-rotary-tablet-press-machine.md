The entire machine operates stably and meets the high-standard requirements of modern industrial GMP production.

## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The ZP series rotary tablet press machine is the core forming equipment on solid dosage production lines. It not only inherits the stability of traditional tablet presses but also achieves breakthroughs in tablet weight precision and the ability to customize irregular tablets. Its core advantages include:

* **Precise Tablet Weight Control System**: Relying on the precise filling tracks and fine-tuning devices inside the machine, the equipment can extremely accurately control the volume of material filled into the die hole each time. This purely mechanical physical limit effectively guarantees the uniform consistency of the weight of each discharged tablet, significantly increasing the pass rate of finished products.

* **Outstanding Irregular Tablet Customization Ability**: Multi-purpose in one machine is the biggest feature of this series. By replacing different high-hardness punches and dies, the same machine can not only press standard round tablets but also easily press irregular tablets such as ovals and triangles, or even customized tablets with complex brand LOGOs, text, or score lines.

* **Ultra-High Capacity & Strong Pressure**: Taking the ZP-35D as an example, its maximum tableting pressure can reach 100KN, which can handle powder materials that are difficult to form. At the same time, its high-speed rotary mechanism allows the maximum production capacity to soar to 126,000 pcs/hour, completely breaking through mass production bottlenecks.

* **Wide Industry Adaptability**: With a scientific design and strong load-bearing capacity, the machine is not only a standard configuration for Chinese and Western medicine tablet production but also widely active in cross-disciplinary industries such as health supplements, food (like milk tablets, candies), chemical blocks, and even electronic component forming.
