# JF-24A 24-Channel Automatic Electronic Counting Machine

It is highly suitable for large and medium-sized pharmaceutical factories and health food processing enterprises, used to accurately count and automatically bottle various capsules, tablets, pills, and irregular granules. Its core advantage lies in being equipped with 24 advanced independent photoelectric detection channels, ensuring an outstanding filling accuracy rate of over 99.5%, and achieving an efficient mass production capacity of 20-90 bottles/minute. The machine casing and material contact surfaces are all made of high-quality food-grade stainless steel, making it sturdy, durable, and easy to disassemble and clean. ## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The JF-24A 24-Channel Electronic Counting Machine is a critical node on modern solid dosage packaging lines, designed to completely replace manual counting and solve industry pain points such as the slow speed and high error rates of traditional counting machines. Its core advantages include:

* **24-Channel High-Speed Detection & Ultra-High Precision**: The machine is equipped with 24 independent discharging and photoelectric detection channels, capable of 360-degree blind-spot-free scanning of high-speed falling granules. The comprehensive counting accuracy rate remains stable at ≥99.5%, effectively preventing quality incidents caused by extra or missing granules.

* **Extremely Wide Material & Packaging Adaptability**: It is not only perfectly compatible with conventional tablets, pills, and capsules but also accurately identifies plain tablets, sugar-coated tablets, and other irregular granules. The system supports a wide-range stepless setting from 2 to 9999 pieces, meeting various filling needs from small trial bottles to large family-sized buckets.

* **Intelligent Interaction & Efficient Capacity**: Centralized control is achieved through a smart touch screen for intuitive operation. While ensuring high precision, the capacity of a single machine can reach 20-90 bottles/minute (depending on material properties, bottle type, and single-bottle filling volume), easily integrating into high-speed packaging assembly lines.

* **GMP Compliance & Easy Maintenance Structure**: The entire machine is made of food and pharmaceutical-grade stainless steel. The internal discharging tracks feature a scientific design that minimizes static electricity and dust accumulation. Daily line changeovers, cleaning, and maintenance are extremely convenient.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can this 24-channel counting machine count capsules and tablets of different sizes at the same time?**
**A:** Yes. The machine has extremely strong self-adaptability to granular materials. Whether it is a 00# capsule or an irregular plain tablet or sugar-coated tablet, you only need to adjust the corresponding counting parameters in the touch screen system and make simple micro-adjustments to the vibrating discharging tracks to achieve compatible production, eliminating the need for cumbersome mold replacements.

**Q: What is the counting accuracy rate of the machine? How does it handle a short count?**
**A:** The JF-24A is equipped with 24 advanced independent photoelectric detection channels, achieving a comprehensive counting accuracy rate of ≥99.5%. In addition, the machine is usually linked with a downstream weight checkweigher. Once the system detects an abnormal discharge or a missing/extra granule in a bottle, the machine will trigger an alarm or automatically reject the non-compliant product at the downstream end, ensuring the finished product is 100% qualified.

**Q: Can the quantity filled in a single bottle be set freely?**
**A:** Yes, it is very flexible. The equipment's counting range can be freely set anywhere from 2 to 9999 pieces. You simply input the target quantity on the human-machine interactive touch screen, and the machine will automatically perform precise filling according to the set quantity.
