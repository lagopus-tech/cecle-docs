It is exceptionally suitable for precise counting and bottling of tablets and capsules in small-to-medium pharmaceutical enterprises, pharmacies, clinics, and laboratories. As a representative product of Wenzhou Tongda Packaging Machinery Factory (CECLE is the registered trademark of this factory), the core advantages of this series lie in its outstanding counting capacity (up to 3,500 tablets/min), rotary precision counting, and dual-bottle simultaneous filling design (YL-4). The entire machine is constructed from full stainless steel, equipped with a built-in dust extraction/collection system and intelligent photoelectric eye protection, fully compliant with GMP standards, ensuring clean and highly accurate counting. It is an ideal choice for enhancing bottling and packaging efficiency.

## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The YL Series Fully Automatic Capsule/Tablet Counter Machine employs an advanced rotary counting and vibratory feeding design, delivering high-speed and exceptionally stable operation. The machine features a user-friendly human-machine interface, making it extremely easy to operate — ordinary employees can become proficient without any complex training.

* **Intelligent & Precise Counting**: Equipped with a built-in special photoelectric protection device (electric eye), ensuring every single tablet or capsule is accurately recorded. The counting setting range can be freely adjusted between 0 and 9,999 pieces.
* **Dual-Channel / Single-Channel Flexible Selection**: The YL-2 adopts a single-bottle alternating counting design; the YL-4 features an innovative dual-bottle simultaneous counting design, capable of counting two bottles at once, significantly improving the speed of two-handed bottle handling and overall production efficiency.
* **GMP Standards & High-Efficiency Dust Removal**: The entire machine is constructed from premium stainless steel, meeting strict GMP hygiene standards. An integrated dust extraction device inside the equipment effectively prevents pharmaceutical powder and dust from interfering with the photoelectric eye and mechanical drive components.
* **Stepless Speed Regulation & Vibratory Feeding**: The vibration frequency of the storage hopper can be steplessly adjusted according to material characteristics, and the rotation and counting speed can also be steplessly regulated to match the operator's actual bottle placement rhythm.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What are the main differences between the YL-2 and YL-4, and how should I choose?**
**A:** The YL-2 is a single-channel counter that counts one bottle at a time, with a capacity of 1,000-1,800 tablets/min. The YL-4 features a dual-channel design that can simultaneously count two bottles at once, doubling the capacity to 2,000-3,500 tablets/min. If your production line requires higher bottling speeds, the YL-4 is the recommended choice.

**Q: How does the YL Series ensure counting accuracy and prevent dust interference?**
**A:** The machine is equipped with a special photoelectric eye protection device, ensuring every single tablet/capsule is precisely detected. Meanwhile, an integrated dust extraction device inside the machine effectively removes pharmaceutical powder and dust generated during the conveying process, preventing dust from obscuring the photoelectric eye or affecting operation.
