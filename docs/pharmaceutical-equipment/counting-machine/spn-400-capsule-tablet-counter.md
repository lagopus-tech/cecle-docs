> The SPN-400 Capsule Counter Machine is a compact, lightweight, and highly accurate semi-automatic counting and filling device. It is exceptionally suitable for small-to-medium pharmaceutical enterprises, hospital pharmacy departments, health supplement, and food industries requiring precise counting and packaging of bottled, bagged, or canned products. As a representative lightweight equipment of Wenzhou Tongda Packaging Machinery Factory (CECLE is the registered trademark of this factory), the core advantages of this model lie in its flexible production capacity (up to 12-26 bottles/min), premium stainless steel contact surfaces with materials, and standard intelligent photoelectric protection with a built-in dust extraction system. While ensuring absolute counting accuracy, it significantly saves production space and procurement costs.

## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The SPN-400 Counter Machine employs an advanced design combining rotary counting with vibratory feeding, delivering fast and exceptionally stable operation. The design philosophy fully considers space constraints and human-machine collaboration in actual production, making the machine extremely easy to operate — employees can get started without any special training.

* **Compact, Lightweight & Wide Applicability**: The machine features a compact footprint and light weight, making it exceptionally suitable for production environments with limited space. It is an ideal dedicated counting device for bottled, bagged, and canned products across relevant industries. All material contact parts are constructed from premium stainless steel, meeting hygiene standards.
* **Intelligent & Precise Counting**: Equipped with a built-in special photoelectric protection device (electric eye), ensuring every single capsule is accurately recorded, effectively eliminating counting errors.
* **Stepless Speed Regulation & Vibratory Feeding**: Featuring a vibratory feeding design, the vibration frequency of the storage hopper can be adjusted according to the conveying needs of specific granules, and the rotation and counting speed can be steplessly regulated to match the operator's bottle placement rhythm.
* **Built-in Dust Extraction & Automatic Alternating**: The machine is internally fitted with a high-efficiency dust extraction device to prevent dust from affecting the machine's internal components and the accuracy of the electric eye. The machine supports single-bottle counting mode — once one bottle is accurately counted, it automatically switches to the next, facilitating manual alternating bottle handling.
