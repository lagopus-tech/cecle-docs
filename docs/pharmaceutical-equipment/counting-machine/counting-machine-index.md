## IV. Complete Solid Dosage Packaging Line Integration Solutions

To help customers establish a complete production loop from raw powder to finished bottled products, the counter machines can seamlessly integrate with front-end and back-end automated equipment:

### 1. Micro / Laboratory Compounding Solution
> **Process Flow: VM Series Single-Arm Mixer → Small Single-Punch Tablet Press → SPN-400 Semi-Automatic Counter**
> * **Front-End Preparation**: Utilizing the VM Series' gentle mixing characteristics for high-quality, non-destructive mixing of fragile granules or expensive active pharmaceutical ingredients.
> * **Precision Filling**: After tablet compression, the SPN-400 is used for semi-automatic precision counting and bottling at a speed of 12-26 bottles/min.
> * **Solution Value**: The entire system has an extremely small footprint, making it ideal for laboratory R&D at research institutions and micro-scale production at startup enterprises.

### 2. Industrial-Grade Fully Automatic High-Speed Bottling Production Line Solution
> **Process Flow: WLDH/EYH Industrial Mixer → High-Speed Rotary Tablet Press → YL-4 Dual-Channel Counter → Back-End Automatic Packaging Line**
> * **Bulk High-Efficiency Mixing**: Using the WLDH horizontal ribbon mixer or EYH two-dimensional mixer to achieve highly efficient and uniform mixing of large-volume powders.
> * **High-Speed Counting & Bottling**: After continuous tablet output from the press, the YL-4's dual-channel design achieves a maximum capacity of 3,500 tablets/min for dual-bottle synchronous automatic counting, completely resolving the bottleneck where front-end production capacity is extremely high but back-end packaging cannot keep up.
> * **Solution Value**: Designed specifically for large-scale production, with smooth transitions between upper and lower cylinder bodies, fully compliant with GMP standards, ensuring mass production efficiency and regulatory compliance.

## VI. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Will pharmaceutical powder dust affect the counting accuracy of the counter machine? How is this resolved?**
**A:** It can have some impact, but our equipment has perfectly solved this problem. The SPN-400, JF-8, and YL Series are all equipped with built-in high-efficiency dust extraction and collection devices that can remove dust in real time as materials fall. Combined with the special photoelectric protection electric eye, this ensures absolutely accurate counting during extended operation.

**Q: How do you adjust the machine according to the size and shape of the material (tablets or capsules)?**
**A:** All machines employ a vibratory feeding design. The vibration frequency of the storage hopper can be steplessly adjusted according to the conveying needs of specific granules, and the rotation and counting speed can also be steplessly regulated to match the rhythm of manual or automatic bottle placement.
