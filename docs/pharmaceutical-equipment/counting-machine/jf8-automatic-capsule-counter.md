## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The JF-8 Capsule Counter Machine employs an advanced rotary counting design, delivering not only fast operation but also exceptional stability. The design philosophy fully considers human-machine collaboration in actual production, making the machine extremely easy to operate — employees can get started without any special training.

* **Intelligent & Precise Counting**: Equipped with a built-in special photoelectric protection device (electric eye), ensuring every single capsule is accurately recorded. The filling quantity can be freely set between 2 and 9,999 capsules according to actual requirements.
* **GMP Standards & Dust Removal System**: The entire machine is manufactured from stainless steel, meeting strict GMP production requirements. To prevent dust from affecting the machine's internal components and the accuracy of the electric eye, the equipment is internally fitted with a high-efficiency dust extraction device.
* **Stepless Speed Regulation & Vibratory Feeding**: Featuring a vibratory feeding design, the vibration frequency of the storage hopper can be adjusted according to the conveying needs of specific granules, and the rotation and counting speed can be steplessly regulated to match the operator's bottle placement rhythm.
* **Automatic Alternating Bottling**: The machine supports single-bottle counting mode. After one bottle is accurately filled, it automatically switches to begin counting for the next bottle. This design greatly facilitates manual bottle handling and significantly enhances packaging efficiency.
