## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

Manufactured with industry-leading technology, the GDSL-12 Electronic Automatic Counting Machine is a critical automated node on modern solid dosage packaging lines. It is designed to completely replace traditional manual counting or inefficient mechanical counting. Its core advantages include:

* **Efficient Multi-Channel Parallel Processing**: The machine is equipped with 12 independent vibration discharging tracks. Through multi-channel collaborative work, the equipment can reach a maximum speed of 7,200 pieces/minute when processing regular-sized round tablets (e.g., 10mm in diameter), significantly increasing the overall throughput of the packaging assembly line.

* **Extensive Material & Packaging Adaptability**: The system possesses extremely strong compatibility for different types of solid preparations. Whether it is conventional pills, sugar-coated tablets, plain tablets, or soft and hard capsules with smooth surfaces and other irregularly shaped pills, it can discharge them smoothly and count them accurately. It is also compatible with a variety of packaging bottles ranging from 40 to 240mm in height.

* **High-Quality Precision Manufacturing & High-Standard Hygiene**: The entire machine has an exquisite external size and a simple, clear internal structure. The casing and all material contact parts are made of high-quality stainless steel. This not only provides a beautiful appearance but also thoroughly eliminates sanitary dead corners, making disassembly, cleaning, and maintenance extremely easy.

* **Flexible & Precise Filling Volume Control**: Operators can freely set the filling volume per bottle between 1 and 1,000 pieces according to production needs, satisfying various filling requirements from small sample trial packs to large-capacity family packs.
