# DSJ-8C Visual Imaging Counting Machine

The DSJ-8C Visual Imaging Counting Machine is a new generation of fully automatic, high-speed granule counting and bottling equipment based on high-definition CCD vision technology and advanced image algorithms. It is highly suitable for the pharmaceutical manufacturing, health food, and high-end dietary supplement industries, specifically designed for ultra-high-precision, non-destructive counting of various tablets, pills, hard/soft capsules, and irregular solid granules. Its core advantage lies in breaking through the limitations of traditional infrared sensors by utilizing dynamic area array scanning with a pixel precision of up to 0.12mm, enabling it to accurately identify and reject overlapping double tablets and tiny fragments. With a maximum counting speed of up to 4000 pieces/minute, the machine comes standard with a fully automatic fill-light and anti-dust system, full-process image traceability, and a tool-less quick disassembly design, making it the pinnacle core equipment for building a digitized, high-standard GMP packaging assembly line.

## I. Product Overview & Core Advantages

The DSJ-8C completely overturns the technical bottlenecks of traditional infrared photoelectric counting machines. It adopts industrial-grade CCD camera backlight imaging technology, endowing the machine with "true vision" to achieve 3D contour analysis and ultra-high-precision counting of pills. Its core performance features include:

* **Ultra-High Resolution & Precise Rejection**: Each channel possesses approximately 250 imaging detection points (compared to only 10-15 in traditional infrared), achieving a detection accuracy of 0.12mm. By calculating the projected area of the falling pills, its precision in identifying "overlapping double tablets" is over 10 times that of infrared sensors, and it can accurately reject tiny fragments as small as one-third the size of a whole pill.

* **Intelligent Anti-Dust & Automatic Fill Light**: It adopts self-developed, high-brightness backlight imaging technology. The system program can automatically and dynamically increase the light source brightness as dust accumulates. Furthermore, the CCD camera is physically located away from the dust generation area, fundamentally solving the problem of inaccurate counting in high-dust environments.

* **Full-Process Image Traceability & Smart IoT**: Built with a quad-core processor and a 32G memory card high-speed recording system, coupled with a precise clock, it can store the historical projection image data of pills from the past year. The machine supports WIFI hotspot connections, enabling remote online upgrades and fault diagnosis for the HMI software, CCD sensors, and main control board.

* **Minimalist Operation & Humanized Design**: The powerful Windows-based Human-Machine Interface (HMI) not only displays real-time camera imaging but also embeds complete video tutorials for installation and debugging. The equipment features an electric lifting function, dual-axis cylinder control for bottle in/out, a recovery trough for dropped materials, and a fully modular tool-less disassembly design, making maintenance extremely convenient.

## IV. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is the biggest difference between a visual imaging counting machine and a traditional infrared photoelectric counting machine?**
**A:** Traditional infrared photoelectric sensors only have 10-15 detection points per channel and can only "see" whether something drops or not; whereas the CCD vision system of the DSJ-8C has 250 imaging points per channel with an accuracy of 0.12mm. It can "clearly see" the projected shape and area of the falling pills in real-time just like a human eye. Therefore, it can extremely accurately identify overlapping double tablets or fragments that are only one-third the size and automatically reject them.

**Q: When producing Chinese medicine tablets that easily generate dust, will the dust block the lens and cause inaccurate counting?**
**A:** No. To address the high-dust issue, we adopted two core technologies: first, the physical location of the CCD camera is kept away from the dust generation area; second, the machine is equipped with an automatic fill-light algorithm. As dust in the pill dropping channel increases, the system automatically increases the brightness of the backlight to penetrate the dust, ensuring the imaging is always clear and completely solving the problem of dust interference.

**Q: The vision system sounds very complex. Will it be difficult for workers to operate and debug on a daily basis?**
**A:** Operation is extremely simple. The system software runs on a universal Windows platform, offering an intuitive interface and built-in complete video operation tutorials. Whether it's adjusting the electric lift, changing bottle types, or handling alarm faults, there are detailed graphic and video guides on the screen. Additionally, as long as the machine is connected to a WIFI hotspot, our factory technicians can remotely help you upgrade or diagnose parameters.
